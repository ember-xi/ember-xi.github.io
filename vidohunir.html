Vidohunir

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Staff weapon skill

  • Skill level: N/A
  • Description: Lowers target's magic defense. Duration of effect varies with TP. Laevateinn: Aftermath effect varies with TP.
  • Available only after completing the Unlocking a Myth (Black Mage) quest.

Properties

  • Element: Darkness
  • Skillchain properties: Fragmentation/Distortion
  • Modifiers: INT: 30% Magic Attack Bonus: 30%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.75 1.75 1.75

Notes

  • Reduces enemy's magic defense by 10%. Deals Dark magical damage.
  • Can open and close a Light skillchain with a Fusion attribute weaponskill.
  • Can open Darkness, but not close. This is due to Fragmentation having priority, which chains with Gravitation.
