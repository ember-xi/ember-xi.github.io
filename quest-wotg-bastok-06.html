Zenith availability

The audited server base explicitly marks the Fire in the Hole battle in Ruhotz Silvermines as unimplemented. The scenes and follow-up handlers exist, but this quest cannot be completed normally through that base alone. Battle and post-battle steps below document the intended route for future implementation.

Before you begin

  • Complete B-05: Storm on the Horizon.
  • Reach WotG 8: In the Name of the Father.
  • Silvermine Key for the reference battle; bring healing and crowd control for an escort fight.

Starting point

Zone boundary from North Gustaberg [S] · Bastok Markets [S]

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Bastok Markets [S] from North Gustaberg [S]. Finish both opening scenes to obtain the Silvermine Key.
  2. Examine the Stonehoused Adit near the northern edge of E-9 in North Gustaberg [S] for a scene. In the complete reference route, examine it again to enter Fire in the Hole.
  3. Inside Ruhotz Silvermines, prepare the party before speaking to Adelheid to start her escort. Keep her alive as successive Quadav groups converge on her.
  4. Use area damage to take enemies off Adelheid, then control surplus enemies with sleep while healing her. Watch for later waves arriving behind the first group; do not advance or rest while attackers still threaten her.
  5. At the final turret, defeat the remaining Quadav. The objective ends when Adelheid reaches the turret; attacking the turret is not the goal.
  6. After victory and the exit scene, enter Bastok Markets [S] from North Gustaberg [S]. Speak to Gentle Tiger at H-6 twice to receive both final scenes and the reward.

Battle and progress notes

  • Reference retry: obtain another Silvermine Key from Solitary Ant at J-9 in North Gustaberg [S]. Retail references require a new game day and zoning; the incomplete native replacement handler does not establish that wait.
  • This is an escort objective. A level-75 party needs control and healing as well as damage; no live solo or Trust compatibility is claimed.

Completion and reward

Intended: Republican Silver Medal; Mythril Musketeer No. 6 title.

What this opens

  • The Bastok route’s requirement for continuing WotG 8: In the Name of the Father
  • B-07: Quelling the Storm, after reaching Crossroads of Time
