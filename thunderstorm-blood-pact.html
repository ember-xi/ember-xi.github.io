Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Ramuh deals lightning damage to target.

Technicals

Avatar: Ramuh (Lightning)

Blood Pact: Rage

Cost: 182 MP

Level: 75 (Merits)

Description

Effect Duration: Instantaneous

Battle Application: Magic Burst

  • Can MB on a Light skillchain

Damage Type: Magic

Additional Notes: Additional merits spent will increase Thunderstorm TP Bonus by 40.

  • TP bonus was increased to 40 in the June 6, 2007 version update.
  • Avatar's TP DOES affect this ability (Although TP is not required, it makes the attack more powerful).

Each level upgrade is in fact a 60% TP bonus, despite what the Merit Description says. Each <pettp> is worth ~1.66 Damage. Each Avatar Magical Attack Merit adds ~16.6 damage consistent through out TP range. Blood Pact Damage appears to 'cap' at 300% including 'Bonus' TP. Source http://ffxi.allakhazam.com/db/jobs.html?fjob=15;mid=121237575685153382;num=31;page=1Enilanerda 07:39, 25 June 2008 (UTC)
