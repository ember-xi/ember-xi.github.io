Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Your true appearance is veiled with an illusion. The illusion cannot be maintained for long periods of time.

The afflicted player receives a costume and a heavy DoT (22HP/tick), and cannot be aggroed by normal sight mobs (true sight will aggro). Movement speed is also slightly decreased.

How to remove the effect

  • Select it from the status bar.

How the effect is inflicted/gained

  • Clicking on jars of Viscous Liquid in Mamook and choosing to try some.

Notes

  • Does not activate the movement speed increase on the Pitchfork +1.
  • Effect of the heavy DoT can be mitigated using the spell Stoneskin.
