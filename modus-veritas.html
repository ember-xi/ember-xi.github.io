Modus Veritas

From Zenith XI Wiki

Scholar level 65.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. How to use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Unlocked by reaching the listed Scholar level.

How to use

Land a Helix spell on the enemy, then use this ability on that enemy. It increases the Helix damage per tick while shortening the remaining duration.

It needs an active Helix effect and can fail. It is not an ordinary direct-damage spell; save it for a useful Helix already on the target.
