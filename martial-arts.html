Martial Arts

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Speeds up Hand-to-Hand attacks.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Martial Arts is a Monk and Puppetmaster's natural form of damage over time.
    • Base Hand-to-hand delay without this trait is set at 480.
    • The initial trait (Martial Arts I) reduces this base delay to 400.
    • Each subsequent upgrade of this trait reduces delay by an additional 20 points.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Wrestler's Mantle

Martial Arts I

  • Obtained: Monk Level 1
  • Obtained: Puppetmaster Level 25
  • Lowers base Hand-to-Hand delay to 400.

Martial Arts II

  • Obtained: Monk Level 16
  • Obtained: Puppetmaster Level 50
  • Lowers base Hand-to-Hand delay to 380.

Martial Arts III

  • Obtained: Monk Level 31
  • Obtained: Puppetmaster Level 75
  • Lowers base Hand-to-Hand delay to 360.

Martial Arts IV

  • Obtained: Monk Level 46
  • Lowers base Hand-to-Hand delay to 340.

Martial Arts V

  • Obtained: Monk Level 61
  • Lowers base Hand-to-Hand delay to 320.

Martial Arts VI

  • Obtained: Monk Level 75
  • Lowers base Hand-to-Hand delay to 300.
