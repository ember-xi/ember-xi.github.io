Walkthrough

  • Thunder Hawk wants you to rescue two merchants who were kidnapped by Quadav and being held at the jail in Beadeaux.
  • To get into the jail cell, you first need to get a Quadav Charm which drops from De'Vyu Headhunter.
  • Once you get the item, take it to the jail door at (I-8) and trigger a cut scene with the merchants Serafin and Evrard. They will give you the Trader's Sack (key item) to return to Thunder Hawk.
  • Return to Thunder Hawk to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Thunder Hawk (Mayor's Residence, Selbina)
Summary:

Rescue two merchants from captivity. They should be somewhere in the Quadav camp of Beadeaux.
