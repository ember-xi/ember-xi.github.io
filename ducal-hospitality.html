Walkthrough

  • Taillegeas, (I-7)in the Dining Hall, will tell you that Nag'molada has a guest who requested strange items and will ask for 3-5 items.
  • Trade the items to Taillegeas to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Taillegeas (Dining Hall, Ru'Lude Gardens)
Summary:

Taillegeas has asked you to collect several items desired by guests of the duchy.
