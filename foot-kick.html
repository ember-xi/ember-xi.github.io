Deals critical damage. Chance of critical hit varies with TP.

Description

Spell cost: 5 MP
Ecosystem: Beasts
Type: Physical (Slashing)
Blue Magic Points: 2
Stat Bonus: AGI+1
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 1

Timers

Casting Time: 0.5 seconds
Recast Time: 6.5 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Foot Kick as part of a combo to create Lizard Killer.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Lizard Killer combo.

  • Claw Cyclone
  • Ram Charge

Notes

  • Slashing damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: STR: 10%, DEX: 10%.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Skillchain Element(s): Wind (can open Scission or Gravitation; can close Detonation)
