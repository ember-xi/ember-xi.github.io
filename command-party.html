Command/party

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Aliases: /p
Usage: /party [message]
Adjusting Chat Mode

/chatmode party
/cm p

Description:

Sends a message to party/alliance members.
In order to receive a /party message, the player
must not be currently changing zones.

Example: /party Is everyone ready?
