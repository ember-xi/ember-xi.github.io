Vellum

From Zenith XI Wiki

Material required for Scholar unlock and AF3 retry transcripts.

For A Little Knowledge, bring 12 sheets. Buy them from the Auction House, or start the quest and use Tucker's exchange in Crawlers' Nest (S).

The full Tucker route is 144 Rolanberries, traded as three batches of 48 for four Vellum each. See his page before exchanging; the reviewed base limits the number of trades.
