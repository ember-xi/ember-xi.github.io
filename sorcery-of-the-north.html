Sorcery of the North

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Eperdur - Northern San d'Oria (M-7)
  Requirements:
   San d'Oria Reputation 4
  Items Needed:   Fei'Yin Chest Key
  Title:   Pilgrim to Vahzl
  Repeatable:   No
  Reward:   Scroll of Teleport-Vahzl

  Previous Quest:   Healing the Land
  Next Quest:   None

Walkthrough

  • Must zone in between this quest and Healing the Land.
  • After talking to Eperdur, head to Fei'Yin to the rooms at (J-8/9/10).
  • Kill monsters until you get a Fei'Yin Chest Key. Shadow, Ore Golem, and Underworld Bats drop the key, and this is a very rare drop even with TH2.
  • Open a treasure chest and you get the Fei'Yin Magic Tome.
  • Bring it back to Eperdur to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Eperdur (Cathedral, Northern San d'Oria)
Summary:

An ancient tome of magic is lost. Journey to Fei'Yin and find another to replace it.
