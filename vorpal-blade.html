Vorpal Blade

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Sword weapon skill

  • Skill level: 200 (Paladin, Blue Mage, Warrior, and Dark Knight exclusive.)
  • Delivers a four-hit attack. Chance of critical varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Scission/Impaction
  • Modifiers: STR:30%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.00 1.00 1.00

Levels

  • Vorpal Blade is only natively available to Paladins, Blue Mages, Warriors, and Dark Knights. While other jobs can obtain the required 200 Sword skill, they must use one of the aforementioned jobs as their support job in order to gain access to Vorpal Blade.
