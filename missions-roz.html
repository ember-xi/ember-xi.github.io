Before you begin

  • Finish your nation’s Mission 5-2 (the Shadow Lord) and reach Rank 6; have access to the Rise of the Zilart areas.
  • If you changed nationality, the native Norg trigger checks Rank 6 in your current nation.

For the shortest story route to Tu’Lia, use Sky Access. Ark Angels is a later objective, not the entry requirement.

Branches and related quests

RouteWalkthroughRequirements
Q1Storms of FateFinish CoP Dawn’s post-battle epilogue and reward sequence; the pinned script checks Dawn Status 8. Reach Riverne - Site #B01; take a Giant Scale for its displacement route.
Q2Shadows of the DepartedComplete Storms of Fate, including the palace return scene. View both Awakening epilogues (Norg and the Neptune’s Spire door). Wait until the next Vana’diel day after finishing Storms of Fate, as required by the pinned native script.
Q3Apocalypse NighComplete Shadows of the Departed; leave Ru’Lude Gardens and wait until the next Vana’diel day. Have access to Sea, the Grand Palace of Hu’Xzoi, the Garden of Ru’Hmet and the Empyreal Paradox from CoP. Keep an inventory slot free for your earring.
Q4Divine MightReach Ark Angels and examine the shrine Blank Target for the mission scene. Accept Divine Might separately at the Blank Target before fighting; bring one Ark Pentasphere for the group.
