Zoreen

From Zenith XI Wiki

Regional merchant for Valdeaunia.

Location

Port Windurst — M-7, Air Travel Agency arrivals.

Trades while Windurst controls Valdeaunia. Sells Frost Turnip and Sage. Availability follows Conquest control; check the current regional map if the shop is unavailable.
