Notes

  • These weapons spawn exclusively in Dynamis - Xarcabard and drop an item used for the final Relic Weapon upgrade.
  • When aggroed, each Animated Weapon will spawn several Satellite weapons to assist it. These summons are also immune to Sleep but are susceptible to Lullaby. The summons will cast buffs and cures upon the main Animated Weapon.
  • After a short time of fighting, the Animated Weapon will "become bored" in emotes, and cast Warp upon itself. If it finishes casting, it will despawn without dropping anything.
  • The weapons spawn when you have defeated the 15 Demon Notorious Monsters in Dynamis - Xarcabard (you get a message after killing the last NM that says "The prison of souls has set free its captive spirits!").
  • When the Animated Weapon warps or dies, its summons will die.
