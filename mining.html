Mining

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Hobby in which various items can be harvested by using a pickaxe at a mining point. In order to harvest, all you need to do is use your pickaxe in the item list on the Mining Point. You can set the following macro in order to speed up the process.

/item Pickaxe <t>

Pickaxes can break with use, although the Field Tunica Set or Worker Tunica Set can minimize such breakages.

Eden Specific Note: If you can't trade pickaxes to a mining spot, try talking to an NPC in the area or logging out entirely. The bug is usually triggered by interacting with a mining point at the same time you or another player trades a pickaxe to the mining point

Custom content warning: Ores DO STACK on Eden, in era they did not. However, some miners may wish to bring some crystals along with them to turn their ores into ingots, providing they have the necessary crafting skills.
