Zenith availability

Reference route: the native quest defines story flags and an assumed completion handler, but the inspected Everbloom Hollow tree contains only doomvoid. The Face of the Future instance is absent from that pinned base; completion is not confirmed.

Before you begin

  • Complete Chasing Shadows; this quest is accepted automatically.
  • Unlock the Batallia Downs Cavernous Maw from its past side before the final scene.

Starting point

Bostillette / arrival scene · Batallia Downs

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. From present-day Batallia Downs, walk into present-day Jugner Forest. Examine Metallic Hodgepodge near the F-9/G-9 border.
  2. Enter Ghelsba Outpost for the preparation scene. Obtain Orcish infiltration kit; Clandestine Marking at I-10 supplies replacements when needed.
  3. Reach Yughott Grotto map 1 and examine Scrape Mark at G-9 for the scene, then again to enter Face of the Future.
  4. Defeat Fangmonger Colossus while keeping allied Excenmille, Maxcimille and Bostillette alive. Removing Tombstones weakens the Colossus; losing an ally fails the encounter.
  5. After the battle and return scenes, examine the unlocked Cavernous Maw at Batallia Downs H-5. Finish the closing scenes and receive the Griffon Ring in the past.

Battle and progress notes

  • This route deliberately returns to present-day zones. The reference battlefield lasts 30 minutes. Keep inventory space for the ring.

Completion and reward

Griffon Ring; title: Fangmonger Forestaller.

What this opens

  • Adieu, Lilisette progression
  • San d’Oria’s Crystal War story conclusion
