Virus

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Viruses are debilitating status effects that attack the body from within, ebbing life away and limiting your ability to heal. Viruses come in two types, and all abilities or spells that refer to viruses refer to these two effects:

  • Disease
  • Plague

Virus is also a Black Magic spell that inflicts the latter, cast by certain NMs, most notably the Mycophile.
