Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Increases party members' resistance against Arcana.
  • Obtained: Dark Knight Level 5
  • Recast Time: 10:00
  • Duration: 1:00

Notes/Description

  • When active, gives all party members' a chance to intimidate Arcana into skipping an attack.
  • If Chaos Sollerets or Chaos Sollerets +1 are equipped, duration of this ability increases to 1:30.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Arcane Circle" <me>
