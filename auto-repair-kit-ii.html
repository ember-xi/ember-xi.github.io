Regen amount is 1 hp + (1 hit point per Light Maneuver) per tick. You must perform at least one Light Maneuver for the regen to start. The Max HP increase is passive, and is not affected by maneuvers. Auto-Repair Kit and Auto-Repair Kit II can be equipped simultaneously, giving a total regen of 1 hp + (2 hp per Light Maneuver) per tick.
