Summoner Avatar Costs

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is how many MP per tick (approx. 3 seconds) that it costs a Summoner to keep a Summon, or Avatar out. The cost varies depending on the level of the Summoner and which avatar is being summoned. There are some items that will reduce avatar perpetuation cost.
