Firespit

Deals fire damage to an enemy.

Description

Spell cost: 121 MP
Ecosystem: Beastmen
Type: Magical (Fire)
Blue Magic Points: 5
Stat Bonus: STR+3
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 68

Timers

Casting Time: 6.5 seconds
Recast Time: 42.75 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Firespit as part of a combo to create Conserve MP.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Conserve MP combo.

  • Chaotic Eye
  • Zephyr Mantle
  • Frost Breath

Notes

  • Primarily modified by INT, INT: 20% and MND: 20% secondary modifiers.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Liquefaction, Fusion, and Light.

How to Obtain

You can only learn this from Mamool Ja mages (WHM and BLM). A good place to learn is from the Mamool Ja in the Wajaom Woodlands near Mamook or just inside Mamook. Was also learned from Mamool Ja Mimiker (BLU) in Mamook.

  • You can learn it from the Assault mission Imperial Agent Rescue.

Acquired From

Mob Family: Mamool Ja
Minimum Blue Mage Level To Acquire: 59 with Magus Jubbah equipped, 62 without Magus Jubbah
