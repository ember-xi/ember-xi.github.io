Before you begin

Be allied with Bastok, have Rank 2, and finish the preceding required story steps. Raise Rank Points if the mission is not offered.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission and speak with Cid in Metalworks (H-8).
  2. Trade a synthesis crystal to a functioning crag Telepoint, such as the northern Telepoint at the Crag of Dem, to obtain a Faded Crystal.
  3. Trade the Faded Crystal to Cid for the C. L. Report.
  4. Speak with Ayame in the Metalworks Cannonry (K-7) to finish.

Important notes

  • The Telepoint and Dimensional Portal are different targets.

Walkthrough

  • This mission requires a certain level of Rank Bar. Complete some repeatable missions or trade 3 crystals to a Conquest Overseer. Talk to a Bastok Gate Guard to receive the mission.
  • Talk to Cid (H-8) in the Metalworks.
  • Trade a crystal to the Telepoint at the Crag of Dem (or Crag of Holla or Crag of Mea). It will become a Faded Crystal.
    • The easiest Crag to walk to from Bastok is the Crag of Dem (I-7) in Konschtat Highlands, which can be reached via western North Gustaberg at (E-6). The three Crags can be warped to via the Teleport-Dem, Teleport-Holla, and Teleport-Mea spells, but only if you possess the Dem Gate Crystal, Holla Gate Crystal, or Mea Gate Crystal respectively.
      • It is highly recommended to check the Telepoint to obtain a Dem Gate Crystal.
  • Trade the Faded Crystal to Cid to obtain the C. L. Report.
  • Optional: Talk to Naji (J-8).
  • Talk to Ayame (K-7) inside the Cannonry in Metalworks to complete the mission. You get additional dialogue if you spoke to Naji before Ayame.

Game Description

Mission Orders
Find a section of the strange structure on the Konschtat Highlands that drains the energy from crystals. Bring a faded crystal back to Chief Engineer Cid for analysis.
