Command/translate

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Please Note: This may not be functional on Eden.

Aliases: None
Usage: /translate "[item]" [subcommand]
Description:

Translates item names into the
specified language. Specify two
languages to translate from one
to another.
Results may not immediately appear
due to server communication issues.

Subcommands:
j Japanese
e English
g German
f French
Example:

/translate "Fire Crystal" eg

Displays the German translation of fire crystal and adds the term to your tab dictionary.

Notes

  • This text command currently functions for item names only.
  • Translated terms will be automatically added to the auto-translate dictionary under the category “Items”.
  • You may first need to press the SPACE and then TAB keys before the term is added to the dictionary.
  • You must use the abbreviated form of the item name exactly as it appears in inventory lists, including punctuation. For example, Mercenary Major Charm must be spelled "Mrc.Mjr. Charm" with only one space character.
  • Items added in this way will be temporary and are not likely to stay in the dictionary after you log out.
  • The term to be translated can be marked with either single or double quotation marks.
