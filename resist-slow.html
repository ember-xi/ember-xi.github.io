Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Increases resistance against slow.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Each upgrade of this trait further increases character's resistance.
    • This trait greatly shortens the duration of slow type status effects.
