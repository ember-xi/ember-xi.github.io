Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

Walkthrough

  1. Return to the Inconspicuous Door at H-8 and examine it.
  2. Finish the Rent-a-Room scene to receive the invitation and advance to Joy! Summoned to a Fabulous Fete.
