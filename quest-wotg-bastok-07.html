Zenith availability

This quest’s dialogue and progression are present in the audited base, but its Bastok storyline prerequisites include unfinished battles. A complete reference route is provided; progression on Zenith needs those earlier battles implemented and tested.

Before you begin

  • Complete B-06: Fire in the Hole.
  • Reach WotG 15: Crossroads of Time.
  • Bring one Goblin Mess Tin, one Goblin Mushpot, and one Twinkle Powder.

Starting point

Zone boundary from North Gustaberg [S] · Bastok Markets [S]

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Bastok Markets [S] from North Gustaberg [S] for the opening scene, then speak with Gentle Tiger at H-6.
  2. Speak with all three witnesses in Bastok Markets [S]: Paul at H-5, Biggorf at H-10, and Wilhelmina at I-9. Their order does not matter.
  3. Return to Gentle Tiger. Then speak with Blatherix at F-8.
  4. Trade Blatherix one Goblin Mess Tin, one Goblin Mushpot, and one Twinkle Powder together in a single trade.
  5. Speak to Gentle Tiger again, then examine the ??? just east of the bridge at F-6 in Vunkerl Inlet [S].
  6. Return to Blatherix for the Goblin Belt and completion.

Battle and progress notes

  • If Blatherix does not request the items, check that all three witnesses and the following Gentle Tiger scene were completed.
  • Goblin Mushpots can be bought from Pawkrix in Lower Jeuno, H-10. Upih Khachla in northern Windurst Waters, H-9, sells Twinkle Powder. Goblin Mess Tins are beastman drops and may be available on the Auction House.
  • The native opening scene requires the North Gustaberg [S] border; it has no additional game-day check.

Completion and reward

Goblin Belt.

What this opens

  • B-08: Honor Under Fire
