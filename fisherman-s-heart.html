Walkthrough

  • Katsunaga, found at the port area of Mhaura on a separate pier from the Selbina-Mhaura Ferry, says he can tell you all about your fishing history if you bring him a Gugru Tuna.
  • Trade him a Gugru Tuna and he will tell your the total number of times you have cast your line, the number of times you actually caught a fish, your heaviest fish caught(measured in ponzes), and your longest fish ever caught (measured in lommes).
