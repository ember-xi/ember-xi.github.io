Zoraal Ja's Pkuucha

From Zenith XI Wiki

A level-75 Colibri NM in Wajaom Woodlands with an additional Beastmaster opponent during the fight.

Contents
  1. Spawn and battle
  2. Notable loot
  3. Sources

Spawn and battle

  1. Defeat Lesser Colibri near I-9 in Wajaom Woodlands to seek its lottery spawn.
  2. Damage Pkuucha until Percipient Zoraal Ja appears. The bird recovers its HP when its master arrives.
  3. Defeat Percipient Zoraal Ja first: he heals the bird, and the bird cannot be finished while he remains alive.
  4. Resume attacking the bird. Paralyze, Slow and Bind are useful; do not depend on Sleep, Silence or Gravity.

Notable loot

Zoraal Ja's Axe and Fowling Earring.
