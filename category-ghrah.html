General Information

  • System: Luminions
  • Element: Like Elementals and Empty, Ghrahs have an elemental alignment indicated by the color of their glowing core. In the images to the right, the "Ball" form is Fire elemental, the "Bird" is Wind, and the "Spider" is Ice.
  • Weaknesses: Ghrahs are weak to the element strong to theirs; see Element.
  • Immune: Ghrahs are immune to their element and highly resistant to the element weak to theirs.
  • Other: Ghrahs appear in 4 different forms: Ball (BLM), Bird (THF), Spider (WAR), and Humanoid (PLD). Ghrahs will still cast magic in any form, however.
  • Behavior: Most Ghrahs alternate between Ball and one other form particular to that Ghrah every 60-75 seconds. In the "Ball" form, Ghrahs are not aggressive, but will link. When not in Ball form, Ghrahs aggro by True Sound, but do not link.

Special Attacks

Special Abilities Promathia Areas
Actinic Burst: 10' AoE Flash
Core Meltdown: AoE self-destruct damage. Used rarely.
Hexidiscs: 6-strike physical damage Cone Attack. Used only in Ball form.
Damnation Drive: Cone Attack 2-strike physical damage. Causes Stun and Knockback if damage is dealt. Used only in Bird form.
Sickle Slash: Single-target extreme physical damage which ignores shadows. Used only in Spider form.
Vorpal Blade: Single-target 4-strike physical damage which ignores shadows. Used only in Humanoid form.
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
