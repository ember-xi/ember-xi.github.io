Blade of Darkness

Blade of Darkness
Starting NPCGumbah — Bastok Mines (J-7), upper floor of the house across the bridge
Required FameNone
Level RestrictionLevel 30
Items NeededChaosbringer (given during the quest)
TitleNone
RepeatableNo
DescriptionZeid, the only master of the dark knight's way, has been seen in the Palborough Mines. Seek him out and he may take you as an apprentice.
Rewards
  • Ability to become a Dark Knight
← Previous questNext quest →
——
EmberXI changes
  • The blade needs 50 kills instead of 100.
  • Kills by your Trusts count while the Chaosbringer is equipped.

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Gumbah in Bastok Mines (J-7). Keep talking until you get the cutscene about Zeid.
    • The great sword weapon skill quest (Inheritance) takes priority over this one, so check your quest log before leaving.
  • Go to Palborough Mines (from Port Bastok → North Gustaberg, enter at K-3) and climb to the third floor.
  • Board the boat at (H-8) and pull the lever. You ride to Zeruhn Mines, watch a cutscene with Zeid and receive the Chaosbringer, a level 1 great sword.
    • Keep one free inventory slot before boarding. Anyone can pull the lever.
    • If you drop the Chaosbringer, ride the boat again for another one.
  • Defeat 50 enemies with the Chaosbringer equipped. Any enemy counts, even Too Weak ones.
    • The finishing blow must be a normal or critical hit with the Chaosbringer. Weapon skills and abilities do not count.
    • Kills made by your Trusts while you wield the Chaosbringer also count.
    • A counter message tells you your progress after each kill.
  • With the Chaosbringer in your inventory (it does not need to be equipped), zone into Beadeaux from Pashhow Marshlands (L-11) for the final cutscene. Any job can do this.
