Garuda

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Avatar Information

The queen of avians, said to soar through the heavens on immeasurable wings. Garuda aids the summoner with her command over air and wind.

Summoners can acquire the ability to summon the avatar Garuda by defeating Garuda Prime in either the Trial Size Trial by Wind quest at level 20, or the Trial by Wind quest at a much higher level (65+).
