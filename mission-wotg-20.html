Before you begin

  • Complete Betrayal at Beaucedine.

Starting point

Raustigne · Southern San d'Oria [S] · I-7

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Raustigne at (I-7) and finish the scene.
  2. Begin gathering signatures around Southern San d'Oria [S] for Proof of Valor.

Battle and progress notes

  • The pinned core has an incomplete main-story battle at mission 14; later story handlers do not establish that this chapter is currently reachable on Zenith.
