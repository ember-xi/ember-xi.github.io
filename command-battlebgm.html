Command/battlebgm

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Aliases: None
Usage: /battlebgm
Description:

Toggle the condition for battle music playback single and party battle mode.

Subcommands:
On (1): Battle music on only when you yourself enter battle mode.
Off (2): Battle music on whenever any party member enters battle mode. (Battle music will play if an enemy is claimed by a player or party member.)
