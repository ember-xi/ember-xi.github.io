General Information

  • System: Birds
  • Weaknesses: Ice, Lightning, Piercing
  • Immune:
  • Resistances: High resistance to Dark based spells and effects
  • Special: Bat Triplets that Link will link with Giant Bats

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Jet Stream: Three-hit TP attack
Sonic Boom: AoE Attack Down effect
Turbulence: AoE Wind Damage
Slipstream: AoE Accuracy Down
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
