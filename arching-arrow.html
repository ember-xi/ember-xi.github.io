Arching Arrow

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Archery weapon skill

  • Skill level: 225
  • Delivers a single-hit attack. Chance of critical varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Fusion
  • Modifiers: STR:16%; AGI:25%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
3.50 3.50 3.50


Levels

  • Arching Arrow is only available to Rangers. While other jobs can obtain the required 225 Archery skill, they will be unable to use Arching Arrow.
  • Archery skill level 225 is obtainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
Job Rating Level
Ranger A- 66
Samurai C+ 74
