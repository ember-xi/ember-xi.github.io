Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Frog Kick: - Single target TP attack.
Silence Gas - Cone Attack Silence + Damage
Dark Spore - Cone Attack Blind + Damage
Spore - Single target Paralyze
Numbshroom - Single target Paralyze + Damage
Queasyshroom - Single target poison (3dmg/tick) + Damage
Shakeshroom - Single target Disease + Damage
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.

Note: The "-shroom" attacks are used in the following order: Queasyshroom, Numbshroom, Shakeshroom. Each attack will consume one mushroom on the funguar's cap. There are three total.
