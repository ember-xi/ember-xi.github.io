Sell

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Before selling an item, it's a good idea to view the item's price history and use it as a guide to setting your asking price.

When you have decided on an asking price, select Sell and a list of the items currently in your inventory appears. Select the item you want to sell and enter your asking price. For stackable items, select a full stack to sell a stack and select a single or a partial stack to sell a single. The Auction House lets you know what the fee will be and asks you to confirm the sale.
