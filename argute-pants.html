Argute Pants

From Zenith XI Wiki

Legs · SCH level 73 · Relic armor.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Upgrade
  3. Sources

DEF 27; HP +15; MP +15; INT +5; Dark Magic skill +7; Enmity -2.

How to obtain

Obtain the base piece from Dynamis - Bastok or Dynamis - Qufim. Follow Dynamis for entry requirements and the zone guide for enemy routes. Drops are not guaranteed.

Upgrade

Trade the base piece, Argute Pants -1, Ether Leather and 28 1 Byne Bill to Sagheera for Argute Pants +1.
