Zenith availability

Reference walkthrough: the pinned server core contains story scenes but leaves the Prelude to a Storm instance in Ghoyu's Reverie unfinished or unconnected. This is not confirmed playable on Zenith.

Before you begin

  • Complete The Battle of Xarcabard.
  • The Magelight signal flare is obtained during this mission from Rally Point: Green in Xarcabard [S].

Starting point

Rally Point: Green · Xarcabard [S] · G-9

Walkthrough

  1. Examine Rally Point: Green (G-9) to receive the Magelight signal flare. Check the Spell-worked Snow just southeast to enter Ghoyu's Reverie.
  2. Speak with Pesoso to begin the operation. Defeat the beastman forces throughout the battlefield; area damage is useful against the numerous enemies.
  3. When all enemies are defeated, speak with Pesoso again to finish. If entry must be repeated, return to Rally Point: Green for another flare.

Battle and progress notes

  • The pinned core has an incomplete main-story battle at mission 14; later story handlers do not establish that this chapter is currently reachable on Zenith.

Completion and reward

An Elixir, Vile Elixir, or Vile Elixir +1 according to performance in the reference version.
