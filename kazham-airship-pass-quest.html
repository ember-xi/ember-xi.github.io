Walkthrough

  • You have 2 options when you first talk to him, pay 148,000g or do a quest.
  • The quest requires you to collect a Giddeus Chest Key, Ghelsba Chest Key, and Palborough Chest Key.
  • Giddeus Chest Key drops from Yagudo Priest, Yagudo Votary, and Yagudo Theologist in Giddeus.
  • Ghelsba Chest Key drops from Orcish Fighter, Orcish Serjeant, and Orcish Cursemaker in Yughott Grotto or Fort Ghelsba.
  • Palborough Chest Key drops from Old Quadav, Brass Quadav, Copper Quadav, Mine Scorpion and Scimitar Scorpion in Palborough Mines.
  • Once all three chest keys are obtained, trade them to Guddal for a key item.


Notes

  • You can solo each key as level 25 having a Warrior, Monk, or any Damage Dealer job. Soloing as 25 has a downfall, which means you have to find a safe area first. They will aggro you and shouldn't try battling with links. Each mob you battle for the key will consume most of your HP. Last is traveling place to place by running and riding chocobos without a level 36 White Mage meaning your time may take a toll, in other words it will take you longer to finish.
    • A mage job soloing is more difficult or likely death.
      • White Mage - lack the damage but have healing magic which is slow battles.
      • Black Mage - lack the defense and healing magic but have magic damage.
  • Best way to collect these keys safely is bringing a level 40-75 friend or bring more 25s along who need the keys too.
