Before you begin

  • Complete Cavernous Maws.
  • To finish this mission: complete one introductory national chain through Claws of the Griffon, Fires of Discontent, or The Tigress Strikes.

Starting point

Allied recruitment NPC · Garlaige Citadel [S]

Walkthrough

  1. Obtain a recommendation: Randecque in Garlaige Citadel [S] (I-6) for San d'Oria; Turbulent Storm in The Eldieme Necropolis [S] (J-9) for Bastok; or Kalsu-Kalasu in Crawlers' Nest [S] (L-8) for Windurst.
  2. Join one past army: Steamed Rams (San d'Oria), The Fighting Fourth (Bastok), or Snake on the Plains (Windurst). Past allegiance is separate from your present nation.
  3. Finish one introductory story pair: Gifts of the Griffon → Claws of the Griffon; Better Part of Valor → Fires of Discontent; or The Tigress Stirs → The Tigress Strikes.
  4. Touch a Jeuno-area maw in Batallia Downs [S], Rolanberry Fields [S], or Sauromugue Champaign [S]. An already activated present counterpart also works. Watch Cait Sith's scene.

Battle and progress notes

  • One qualifying national chain is sufficient. You do not need to complete all three.

Completion and reward

Lightsworm; Cait Sith story begins.
