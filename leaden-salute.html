Leaden Salute

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Marksmanship weapon skill

  • Skill level: N/A
  • Description: Deals darkness elemental damage. Damage varies with TP. Death Penalty: Aftermath effect varies with TP.
    • Ignores Magic Shield, Invincible, and Perfect Dodge
  • Available only after completing the Unlocking a Myth (Corsair) quest.

Properties

  • Element: Darkness
  • Skillchain Properties: File:Gravitation-Icon.gifGravitation/File:Transfixion-Icon.gifTransfixion
  • Modifiers: AGI:30%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
4.0 4.25 4.75

Notes

Damage Formula appears to be:

  • 2 * ( AGI - EnemyINT ) + [ fTP * ( 75 + 2 + [ .83 * [ .3 * AGI ] ] ) ]
  • Magic Attack Bonus increases damage dealt.
  • Not affected by weapon base damage.
  • Damage is enhanced by the use of Dark Staff or Pluto's Staff.
