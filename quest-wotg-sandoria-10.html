Zenith availability

Reference route: native Forbidding Portal entry and victory handling explicitly await implementation of the quest instance. No matching Ghoyu’s Reverie instance exists in the inspected pinned tree; this is an unresolved progression blocker.

Before you begin

  • Complete Songbirds in a Snowstorm.
  • Receive Vial of military prism powder before entering the battlefield.

Starting point

Animal Spoor · Xarcabard (S) · J-9

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Examine Animal Spoor at J-9 in Xarcabard (S), near the Beaucedine entrance.
  2. Examine Wheel Rut in the southern I-9 dead-end tunnel. Return to Animal Spoor to receive the military prism powder.
  3. Examine Forbidding Portal at northwest I-7 for the scene, then again to enter Blood of Heroes.
  4. Keep allied Excenmille alive. Defeat Kingslayer Doggvdegg to win; the field also contains Gherrmoga, two Bloodwing Deathrainers and four Bloodwing Maimers. Control the additional enemies while protecting Excenmille.
  5. After the return scene, examine Wheel Rut at I-9 to complete the quest.

Battle and progress notes

  • Reference retry: obtain another powder from Animal Spoor on the next Vana’diel day. Excenmille’s death fails the battle.

Completion and reward

Ram Staff; title: House Aurchiat Retainer.

What this opens

  • Fate in Haze progression
  • Chasing Shadows after Darkness Descends
