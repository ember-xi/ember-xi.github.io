Some equipment and monsters change behavior depending on the Time of Day, and some quests can only be completed at certain hours in Vana'diel.

Game Term Hours Affects
Daytime 06:00-18:00 Arco De Velocidad, Brisingamen (HQ), Daylight Dagger, Fenrir items, Garden Bangles (HQ), Horror Head II, Koga Hakama, Koga Hatsuburi, Koga Kyahan, Koga Tekko, Nocturnal mobs go to sleep, Sunlight Pole, Vampire Cloak
Nighttime 18:00-06:00 Brisingamen (HQ), Diurnal mobs go to sleep, Fenrir items, Giant Bats spawn in outdoor areas, Horror Head, Ninja Artifact Armor, Koga Tekko, Nightmare Sword, Schwarz Axt, Shiranui, Vampire Boots, Vampire Cloak, Vampire Earring, Vampire Mask
Dusk to dawn 17:00-07:00 Koga Hakama +1, Koga Hatsuburi +1, Koga Kyahan +1, Koga Tekko +1, Ninja Hakama +1, Ninja Kyahan +1
? 04:00-06:00
Dawn 06:00-07:00
? 07:00-17:00
Dusk 17:00-18:00 Verification Needed Lovers in the Dusk
Evening 18:00-20:00
Dead of Night 20:00-04:00 Undead spawn in outdoor areas

In-game dawn and dusk are different from their real-life counterparts in that the in-game ones are considered part of daytime rather than nighttime. (According to Wikipedia, dusk refers to a time shortly after sunset.) --Srduvarno 04:46, 25 August 2008 (UTC)
