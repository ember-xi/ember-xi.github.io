Zhuu Buxu the Silent

From Zenith XI Wiki

Two era versions share this Ninja Yagudo name: a low-level mission target in Giddeus and a stronger NM in Castle Oztroja (S).

  2. Castle Oztroja (S)
  3. Sources

Giddeus: Parana Shield

  1. In Giddeus, drop to the lower map through the hole near G-8, then head north toward G/H-7 on map 2.
  2. Defeat the level-16 NM and collect Parana Shield. The shield is used in Journey Abroad when Windurst is the first foreign nation visited.
  3. Keep HP topped up for Mijin Gakure. If absent, wait for its timed respawn; classic guides list ten minutes, while later retail reduced this to five.

Castle Oztroja (S)

The level-80–84 version is a lottery from Yagudo Sentinels near H-10 on the first map. Possible rewards are Sangoma Lappa and Yagudo Kote. Pull it clear of the hallway to avoid additional Yagudo; prepare for Mijin Gakure.
