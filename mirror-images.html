Mirror Images

Walkthrough

  • Speak to Luto Mewrilah for a cutscene.
  • Head to Qu'Bia Arena in Fei'Yin for a solo level 50 BCNM with a 15 minute time limit.
  • Defeat the mob.
  • Speak to Luto Mewrilah for a cutscene. Must have Signal Pearl on you.
  • Click on the first door on the left inside Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno for final cutscene.
Strategy tips
  • The BCNM enemy is Vassago, a Demon Dark Knight with a Scythe; he casts Drain, Aspir, and Absorb spells, and uses Demon TP attacks.
  • He also does Blighted Gloom, a very strong Darkness attack, which ignores shadows.
  • If this is directed at you, you can easily run out of range to avoid damage; from his "Mirror of Blighted Gloom...I command thee" message to when he actually uses it is 11 seconds. Run away when he uses it.
  • If Your NPC is targeted, however, Blighted Gloom will be countered with Blessed Radiance (so Stalwart Shield works well).
  • Blessed Radiance is a very powerful counter ability. It will take Vassago down about 25-30%.
  • He is reportedly very susceptible to Paralyze. Silence and Slow will also stick.
  • His melee attacks are very strong, dealing around 150-200 damage.
  • Doing this BC on Lightday is recommended.
  • If you get KO or NPC is KO, you are ejected from the battlefield.
  • Reraise earrings suggested after entering battlefield.
  • The NPC and Vassago will not do any action until you approach them.
  • You have just 15 minutes to defeat Vassago.
Blessed Radiance Special Ability
  • After you have completed this quest your NPC will sometimes use Blessed Radiance when one or both of your HP gauges are in the red.
