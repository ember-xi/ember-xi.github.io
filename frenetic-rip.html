Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Delivers a threefold attack. Damage varies with TP.

Description

Spell cost: 61 MP
Ecosystem: Demon
Type: Physical (Blunt)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: INT+1
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 63

Timers

Casting Time: 0.5 seconds
Recast Time: 28.5 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Frenetic Rip as part of a combo to create Accuracy Bonus.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Accuracy Bonus combo.

  • Dimensional Death
  • Disseverment

Notes

  • Blunt damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: STR: 20%, DEX: 20%.
  • Cap@900dmg Verification Needed

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Skillchain Element: Ice (can open Impaction, Compression, or Fragmentation; can close Induration).

How to Obtain

Imps will only use this move after having their horn broken. The horn can be broken by either using a multi strike weapon skill, for example, Vorpal Blade, Penta Thrust, Rampage, Blade: Ku, and Asuran Fists, or randomly from a critical melee attack while standing behind or to the side of the monster.
