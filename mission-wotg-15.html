Before you begin

  • Complete A Nation on the Brink.
  • For the gated story scene: complete Bonds That Never Die, Honor Under Fire, or The Forbidden Path.

Starting point

Zone entrance · Southern San d'Oria [S]

Walkthrough

  1. Complete one national pair: Quelling the Storm → Honor Under Fire; The Price of Valor → Bonds That Never Die; or The Long March North → The Forbidden Path.
  2. Walk into Southern San d'Oria [S] from East Ronfaure [S] to trigger the next main-story scene.

Battle and progress notes

  • The pinned core has an incomplete main-story battle at mission 14; later story handlers do not establish that this chapter is currently reachable on Zenith.

Completion and reward

Story progression.
