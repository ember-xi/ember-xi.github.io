Valor Armor Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

All races in full Paladin Relic Armor.]] The pinnacle of Paladin Armor, this set surpasses that of Gallant armor in almost every way. Giving the Paladin a more invincible stature than before, this incredibly rare set signifies that the wearer has met the many trials of being a holy knight and exceeded all expectations.
