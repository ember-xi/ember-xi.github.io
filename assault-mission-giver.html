Assault Mission Giver

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

An Assault Mission Giver is an NPC who offers various Assault missions for a specific area. Signing up for each mission will cost one Imperial Army I.D. Tag. Advanced missions require a particular Mercenary Rank, before they will be offered.
Accumulated Assault Points are area-specific, and can be spent by buying equipment from the corresponding Assault Mission Giver.

Location: Commissions Agency, Aht Urhgan Whitegate (L-9/10)

List of NPCs and their areas

  • Bhoy Yhupplo: Ilrusi Atoll
  • Famad: Lebros Cavern
  • Isdebaaq: Mamool Ja Training Grounds
  • Lageegee: Periqia
  • Yahsra: Leujaoam Sanctum
  • Sorrowful Sage: Nyzul Isle

For further information, see the Assault entry, or talk with the NPCs.
