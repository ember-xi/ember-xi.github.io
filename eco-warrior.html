Eco-Warrior

Eco-Warrior
TypeRepeatable quest — every two days
FameLevel 1 in any nation
Level cap20 (Buffs from the NPC inside the cave)
TrustsThey enter with you at the level 20 cap
Rewards10,000 gil · Page from the Dragon Chronicles (4,500–9,000 XP) · Empress Band (once) · Title
EmberXI vs retailEvery two days instead of weekly; Trusts instead of an alliance; no Eeko-Weeko escort; the NPC tells you whose turn it is

A quest that repeats every two days, rotating between the three nations. You enter a cave at the level 20 cap with your Trusts, spawn the NM at the ???, kill it and return for the reward. The best regular XP on the server.

Mechanics: The rotation is fixed and then repeats. If you talk to the NPC of a nation that is not next, it tells you where to go. After every win: two days of waiting.
San d'Oria → Bastok → Windurst → San d'Oria

General Rules

  1. The level 20 cap must be active for the NM to spawn and the key item to count. If you die, return to the NPC inside the cave to get the cap again (Reraise keeps it).
  2. Buffs are removed by the cap; cast Protect and Shell after the buff from the NPC.
  3. If you leave the zone before touching the second ???, come back and touch it again — no new fight.
  4. The reward is given by the nation NPC after you talk to the cave NPC a second time.
Mechanics: The NM spawns hating whoever touched the ???. Let the tank (Trion / Valaineral) touch it.

San d'Oria — Norejaie

  1. Talk to Norejaie in Southern San d'Oria (K-6), Lion Springs Tavern.
  2. Go to Ordelle's Caves via the Survival Guide. Talk to Rojaireaut (G-3) for the buff.
  3. East across the pond and the long corridor, exit B (I-6) to the first map.
  4. From B walk to G-8 then the narrow tunnel east: the ??? is on a rock in the small chamber (G-9).
  5. The NM Necroplasm (Slime) spawns. Kill it, touch the ??? again, return to Rojaireaut then Norejaie.

Bastok — Raifa

  1. Talk to Raifa in Bastok Markets (H-9).
  2. Go to Gusgen Mines. Talk to Chalzeim near the entrance for the buff.
  3. Follow the tunnels to the ??? at H-8. The NM Fungus Flytrap spawns.
  4. Kill it, touch the ??? again, return to Chalzeim then Raifa.

Windurst — Lumomo

  1. Talk to Lumomo in Windurst Woods (J-10).
  2. Go to Maze of Shakhrami. Talk to Ahko Mhalijikhari near the entrance for the buff.
  3. Go to the ??? at F-6 (second map). The NM Chocobo Leech spawns.
  4. Kill it, touch the ??? again, return to Ahko Mhalijikhari then Lumomo.

Rewards in Detail

  1. 10,000 gil.
  2. Page from the Dragon Chronicles — use it on any job: 4,500–9,000 XP.
  3. Empress Band — XP +50% ring (7 charges, recharges itself) — given once.
  4. Title: Eco-Warrior of the nation.
