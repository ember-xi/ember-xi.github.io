What to do

  1. Accept Eco-Warrior from one of the three contacts above. Only one nation’s Eco-Warrior can be active at a time.
  2. Enter that nation’s battle area and speak to its helper: Rojaireaut in Ordelle’s Caves, Degga in Gusgen Mines, or Ahko Mhalijikhari in the Maze of Shakhrami. Apply the ointment for Zenith’s level-25 restriction.
  3. Examine the quest’s ??? to start the encounter. Defeat every required monster while under the quest restriction.
  4. Examine the same ??? again for the key item. Returning to the dungeon helper first is optional; the helper can remove the restriction.
  5. Return to the original contact for your reward. Keep inventory space for Dragon Chronicles. If it cannot be delivered, your quest and key item remain ready to turn in.

Rewards each completion

RewardHow it works
10,000 gilBase quest reward; the server’s existing quest-gil rate still applies.
Dragon ChroniclesThe original EXP scroll. Use it from your inventory.
One saved EXP bonusActivate at your Eco-Warrior quest giver → Activate EXP bonus. Grants +75% EXP, up to 10,000 extra EXP, with a 24-hour effect duration. This is an earning bonus, not an immediate 10,000 EXP award.

Saved bonuses stay on your character until activated. If Dedication or Commitment is already active, use it first: activation preserves the existing effect and retains the saved Eco bonus. Each successful activation spends one saved bonus.

Weekly rotation

  • Complete one Eco-Warrior per Conquest week across all three nations. The next reward opens at the next server Conquest tally.
  • Finish all three nations before repeating a nation. Choose their order freely; finishing the third nation opens a fresh circuit for a later week.
  • Your original quest giver handles acceptance and completion; saved bonuses appear there when available.
  • A quest accepted before this update can still be finished. Older completed quests do not retroactively fill the new circuit or grant bonus credits. An existing weekly lock is retained.

Completion sound

The original Eco-Warrior completion scene already invokes the native quest fanfare. This package keeps that scene. A new fanfare for Zenith’s custom quests is not included: playback through this server’s Lua API has not been verified.

Version and references

Uses the existing Eco-Warrior rules with NPC conversations. Activate saved EXP bonuses at Norejaie, Raifa or Lumomo. Offline quest, reward, menu and reversible-installation checks passed; Windows installation and in-game play have not yet been confirmed.

  • Horizon Eco-Warrior: inspiration for the weekly three-nation circuit and reward increase.
  • Horizon’s EXP-bonus reward: reference for the 75%, 24-hour, 10,000-extra-EXP limits. Zenith uses saved activation at Norejaie, Raifa or Lumomo instead of adding Horizon’s custom item.
  • Zenith Eco-Warrior v1.0 source and tests; existing level cap 25 and native encounters preserved.

Saved EXP bonus

After earning a bonus, speak to Norejaie, Raifa or Lumomo. Choose Activate EXP bonus when ready; Other business opens the original Eco-Warrior interaction. No bonus is consumed merely by opening the menu.
