A Healer’s Promise

From Zenith XI Wiki

Help Apururu gather supplies and earn a permanent healer companion.

Requirements

  • Main-job level 30 or higher when accepting and trading.
  • A Trust permit from any starting city. A Windurst-specific permit is not required.
  • 3 Honey and 3 Distilled Water.
  • No Unity membership or Rhapsodies mission completion required.

Starting NPC and map

Go to the Manustery in Windurst Woods (H-9). Apururu is behind the work counter inside the building.

Walkthrough

You can tick off steps below. Checkmarks are saved in this browser only; they do not change your character’s progress.

  1. Collect 3 Honey and 3 Distilled Water. Wije Tiren in Windurst Woods (I-8) sells Distilled Water.

  2. Speak to Apururu. A Healer’s Promise (Lv.30) opens the description; choose Accept the supply request to begin.

  3. Trade exactly 3 Honey + 3 Distilled Water together. Do not include gil or additional items.

  4. You learn Apururu (UC) directly. Open Magic → Trust and summon her under the usual Zenith Trust rules.

Reward

Apururu (UC), learned permanently once per character. No Cipher or ongoing Unity points are needed. “UC” is part of the spell’s name; the quest does not enroll you in Unity. Level 30 is the acquisition requirement; afterward, you can use her with other jobs.

Notes

  • Other matters opens Apururu’s original dialogue and quests.
  • If the materials were accepted but the spell was not delivered, select A Healer’s Promise: Progress to collect the pending reward without trading again.
  • If you already know Apururu (UC), you do not need to repeat the request.
  • This uses the existing Apururu behavior; it does not change Kupipi’s AI.
