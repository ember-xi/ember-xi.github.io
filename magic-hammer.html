Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Steals an amount of enemy's MP equal to damage dealt. Ineffective against undead.

Description

Spell cost: 40 MP
Ecosystem: Beastmen
Type: Magical (Light)
Blue Magic Points: 4
Stat Bonus: MP-5, MND+2
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 74

Timers

Casting Time: 4 seconds
Recast Time: 180 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Magic Hammer as part of a combo to create Magic Attack Bonus.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Magic Attack Bonus combo.

  • Sound Blast
  • Cursed Sphere
  • Eyes On Me
  • Memento Mori
  • Heat Breath
  • Reactor Cool

Notes

  • Primarily modified by MND: 30% secondary modifier.
  • Can only drain MP from targets that have MP, and cannot drain more MP than target has.
  • Affected by Magic Attack Bonus.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Transfixion, Fusion, and Light.

How to Obtain

Acquired From

Mob Family: Poroggo
Minimum Blue Mage Level To Acquire: 65 with Magus Jubbah equipped, 68 without Magus Jubbah
Name Level Zone
Poroggo 75-77 Mamook
