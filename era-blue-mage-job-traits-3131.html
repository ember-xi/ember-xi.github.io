Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Upon setting certain combinations of Blue Magic, a Blue Mage can acquire certain Job Traits. Many of these Job Traits have multiple valid spell combinations. Equipping more than 1 combo set will earn you a higher tier of that Job Trait for Clear Mind, but nothing else.

Auto Refresh

The points system for Auto Refresh has nothing to do with Blue Magic set points (the ones you use to equip spells) - it's specifically about obtaining Auto Refresh.

Different spells weigh more/less toward triggering Auto Refresh when equipped, which is where the points system came from. You can have Auto Refresh by any combination of the spells listed below as long as the sum of their Auto Refresh points add up to 8 points or higher (based on that list, not on set points).
