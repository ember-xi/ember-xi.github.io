Hauberk +1 Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

An upgraded heavy version of the Haubergeon Set, the Thick Chainmail +1 set offers increased attack and accuracy for melee damage, while sacrificing evasion and defense on each piece. The body piece is an improvement over the Haubergeon by decreasing the evasion penalty and removing the Agility penalty.
