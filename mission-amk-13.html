Zenith availability

The route has been checked against references and available code; completion on Zenith has not yet been play-tested.

Before you begin

  • Complete AMK 12.
  • Map of the Northlands.
  • Sneak/Invisibility consumables for the level-1 timed gauntlet.

Starting point

Shadowy Pillar · Castle Zvahl Baileys

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

Walkthrough

  1. Examine the Shadowy Pillar near Switchstix. Travel to Beaucedine Glacier and examine the Lonely Evergreen at G-7, then speak to the Goblin Grenadier near H-8/H-9 with a Map of the Northlands.
  2. Inspect the six marked Pips and note their elements. Connect horizontally or vertically adjacent pips whose elements are consecutive weekdays: Fire → Earth → Water → Wind → Ice → Lightning → Light → Dark → Fire. The lines form a digit. Report that digit to the Grenadier; take the Pocket mogbomb to the Lonely Evergreen for the Trivia challenge kupon.
  3. In Xarcabard, examine Option One (G-8/H-8), Option Two (G-9) or Option Three (G-8). Answer three questions correctly, using your character's displayed statistics when requested, to obtain the Gauntlet challenge kupon.
  4. Return to the Baileys Shadowy Pillar for the level-1 gauntlet. Reach the Flame of Fate by the Keep approach within eight minutes for the Festival souvenir kupon. Your initial stealth can expire sooner; bring Sneak/Invisibility items.
  5. In Castle Zvahl Keep, examine the Ominous Pillar, then search the Craggy Pillars along the teleporter route for the Slacker Moogle. Obtain the Mega Bonanza kupon.
  6. Continue to the Throne Room and examine its door for the final introductory scene.

Completion and reward

Mega Bonanza kupon and final battlefield access.
