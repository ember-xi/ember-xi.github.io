Walkthrough

  • Talk to Bluffnix in order to trigger the quest and see if you can actually get it.
  • Trade the Cermet Chunk, Goshenite, Silk Cloth, and Darksteel Ingot to him (these items can all be found at the AH and/or crafted).
  • He will upgrade your Gobbiebag from 45 spaces to 50!

Game Description

Client: Bluffnix (Muckvix's Junk Shop, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

Bluffnix will make your pack as big as a Gobbiebag if you bring him the following: a cermet chunk, a darksteel ingot, a square of silk cloth, and a goshenite.
