Before you start

SCH 50+; Downward Helix complete; choose Pants from Loussaire.

Steps

  1. Find Indescript Markings among Slugs in Pashhow Marshlands (S) at (K-5), (J-6), (I-6), (I-7) or (G-6) for Slug Mucus. The active spot moves after collection.
  2. Check the markings on the fire at Vunkerl Inlet (S) (D-11), near the Jugner Forest (S) entrance, for Djinn Ember. Historical guides recommend 16:00–06:00; weather-related reports conflict, so use a visible marker rather than assuming it must be absent.
  3. Return to Loussaire on Scholar with inventory space. Markings remove Sneak; watch the surrounding Djinn.

Reward

Scholar's Pants (SCH 56).
