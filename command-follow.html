Command/follow

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Aliases: None
Usage: /follow
Description: Automatically follows the targeted object.

Move in the opposite direction to cancel.

Example: /follow <stpc>

Notes

/follow will not work on Invisible players; instead, /lockon and /automove can be used together to achieve the same effect.
As with /target, you may specify a character name (/follow "[name]"). The person must, of course, be within targetable range in order for this to work.
