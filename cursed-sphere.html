Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Deals water damage to enemies within area of effect.

Description

Spell cost: 36 MP
Ecosystem: Vermin
Type: Magical (Water)
Blue Magic Points: 2
Stat Bonus: MND+1
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 18

Timers

Casting Time: 3 seconds
Recast Time: 19.5 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Cursed Sphere as part of a combo to create Magic Attack Bonus.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Magic Attack Bonus combo.

  • Sound Blast
  • Eyes On Me
  • Memento Mori
  • Heat Breath
  • Reactor Cool
  • Magic Hammer

Notes

  • Primarily modified by INT, with an INT: 30% secondary modifier.
  • Area of effect is centered around target.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Reverberation, Distortion, and Darkness.
