Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Blink is a beneficial status effect that provides shadowy simulacra to absorb enemy attacks until it wears off or is removed. Benefitting characters gain a number of shadow images that absorb normal attacks, most spells, and some monster abilities. Actions directed at a player with Blink may either hit the player himself or be absorbed by an image. Once an image absorbs an attack, it is spent.

How to remove the effect

  • Knocking away images with attacks, spells, and abilities will remove the images.
  • AoE spells will remove all shadows.

How the effect is inflicted/gained

Spells

  • White Magic - Blink
  • Blue Magic - Zephyr Mantle
  • Blood Pact - Garuda - Aerial Armor

Equipment

  • Blink Band

Automaton Attachments

  • Replicator
