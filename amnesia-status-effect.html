Description

File:Amnesia (Status Effect).png Amnesia is a harmful status effect that makes the player unable to use Job Abilities, Weapon Skills or Pet Commands until it wears off or is removed.

How to remove the effect

  • No known method.

How the effect is inflicted

Monster Abilities

  • Imp - Abrasive Tantara
  • Lamiae - Hypnotic Sway
  • Qiqirn - Kibosh
  • Promathia - Winds of Oblivion
