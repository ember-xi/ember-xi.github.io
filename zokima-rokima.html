Zokima-Rokima

From Zenith XI Wiki

Windurst mission gate guard.

Location

Windurst Walls — H-7, by Heavens Tower.

Speak with him for available Windurst Missions. Accepting your first Windurst mission from him grants the title Heavens Tower Gatehouse Recruit.
