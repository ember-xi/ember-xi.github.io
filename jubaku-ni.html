Jubaku: Ni

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Uses the ninja tool, "Jusatsu". Paralyzes an enemy.

Other Information

Note
The spell "Jubaku: Ni" is not available for players to learn within the FFXI world, even though many high level NIN mobs/NPCs are able to cast it. This spell is comparable to Paralyze II in strength. Verification Needed
