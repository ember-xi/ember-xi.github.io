Mercy Stroke

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Dagger weapon skill

  • Skill level: N/A
  • Description: Temporarily improves critical hit rate.
  • Available only when equipped with the Relic Weapons Batardeau (Dynamis use only) or Mandau.
    • These Relic Weapons are only available to Red Mages, Thieves, and Bards. So only they can use this Weapon Skill.
  • Poison seems to be 10 HP/3 seconds.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Darkness / Gravitation
  • Modifiers: STR:60%
