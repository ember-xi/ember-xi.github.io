Subfamily: Eruca

With the release of the Treasures of Aht Urhgan expansion, the crawler family has been expanded to include a slightly different variety of Crawler, the Eruca. Eruca exhibit the same basic behavior as other members of the Crawler family, with some differences:

  • Eruca have a very different physical appearance than the standard crawlers found in non-Aht Urhgan zones, being black and red as opposed to the standard yellow and black seen in the image above.
  • Eruca are also diurnal; they sleep during the night and are active during the daytime.
  • Eruca use the special ability Incinerate, as noted above.
  • Incinerate is a Breath Attack, which does fire damage in a frontal cone, based on the Eruca's HP. This damage can be reduced by the spells like Barfira.
  • Fire-based weather (volcanic eruptions) grant the Eruca TP regen, making them an even more dangerous foe.

Subfamily: Defoliator

These green-skinned caterpillars can only be found in Aydeewa Subterrane, and share the diurnal behavior of the Eruca, but not their attacks..
