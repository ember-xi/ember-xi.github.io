Dark Legacy

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is the first Dark Knight Artifact Armor quest - otherwise known as AF1.

  Start NPC:   Raibaht - Metalworks (G-8)
  Requirements:
   Dark Knight 40+
  Items Needed:   Yagudo Cherry
  Title:   None
  Repeatable:   Yes, after AF is complete.
  Reward:   Raven Scythe

  Previous Quest:   Blade of Darkness
  Next Quest:   Dark Puppet

Walkthrough

  • Raibaht, on the 2nd floor of Metalworks in Cid's Laboratory, will send you to talk with Mighty Fist in the Dark Steel Forge (first room on the left as you enter the Metalworks), who will give you the Letter from the Darksteel Forge (key item).
  • Now go see Cochal-Monchal in the West wing of the Opistery in Northern Windurst Waters at F-8.
  • Get a Yagudo Cherry from the Auction House or the Kolshushu Regional Vendor
  • Head to Giddeus.
    • Follow the path along the left (West) side of the map and head to (H-7). Drop down to the lower level and continue heading South to (H-14).
    • Make sure to not drop down off the cliff after you exit the cave.
  • Clear the area and trade the cherry to the ??? to spawn Vaa Huja the Erudite.
    • Vaa Huja the Erudite can be soloed by most jobs 55-60+.
  • Kill Vaa Huja the Erudite, check the ??? again to receive the key item Dark Steel Formula.
  • Return to the Metalworks and talk to Mighty Fist and then Raibaht to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Raibaht (Cid's Lab, Metalworks)
Summary:

You have been asked to find and bring back a book pertaining to the Galka's darksteel formula.
