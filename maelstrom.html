Deals water damage to enemies within range. Additional effect: STR Down.

Description

Spell cost: 162 MP
Ecosystem: Aquans
Type: Magical (Water)
Blue Magic Points: 5
Stat Bonus: Mind +2
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 61

Timers

Casting Time: 6 seconds
Recast Time: 39 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Maelstrom as part of a combo to create Clear Mind.

Pair with one of the following spells to create the Clear Mind combo.

  • Poison Breath
  • Soporific
  • Venom Shell
  • Awful Eye
  • Filamented Hold
  • Feather Tickle
  • Sandspray
  • Warm-Up
  • Lowing
  • Mind Blast

Notice, the effect stacks for each pair on this group. Each additional pair grants the next tier of Clear Mind.

Notes

  • Primarily modified by INT, INT: 30% and MND: 10% secondary modifiers.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Reverberation, Distortion, and Darkness.
