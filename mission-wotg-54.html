Before you begin

  • Complete A Token of Troth.
  • A free inventory slot.

Starting point

Veridical Conflux · Grauberg [S] · F-5

Era note: Later WotG chapter, released after the level cap began rising above 75. Included for story continuity; this guide does not claim a tested level-75 balance.

Walkthrough

  1. Leave at least one inventory slot free and examine the Veridical Conflux at (F-5).
  2. Choose one augment from each of the two displayed groups, review the combination, and confirm to receive the augmented Moonshade Earring.
  3. The first group offers Accuracy, Attack, Ranged Accuracy, Ranged Attack, Magic Accuracy or Magic Attack Bonus +4, or HP/MP +25. The second group includes TP Bonus, Refresh, Regain and other situational effects; read the options before committing.

Battle and progress notes

  • This is the closing mission-log entry. Its presence in the current log after the reward does not itself mean the story is unfinished.
  • The pinned core has an incomplete main-story battle at mission 14; later story handlers do not establish that this chapter is currently reachable on Zenith.

Completion and reward

Augmented Moonshade Earring.
