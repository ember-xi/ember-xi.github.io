Abyss Armor Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

All races in full Abyss Armor.]] An armor set from the Dynamis zones, which the player may be rewarded with for defeating the mobs within. The armor is in the same style as the lower level Dark Knight AF, however coloring differences (most noticeably the distinct purple highlights) makes the set instantly distinguishable from it's lower level counterpart.

The set as a whole leans towards the oft neglected magic side of the Drk job, with enhancements to Enfeebling Magic Skill and Dark Magic Skill, along with Magic Accuracy and Magic Attack Bonus enhancements. The Melee side of the job is not forgotten, however, with pieces enhancing both Accuracy and Attack.


Number of Pieces: 6
