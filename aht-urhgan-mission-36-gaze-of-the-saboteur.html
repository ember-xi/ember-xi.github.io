Walkthrough

Talacca Cove

  • Watch the cutscenes that occur when the battle of the last mission is over.

Hazhalm Testing Grounds

  • Exit Talacco Cove via the cave to the south and go to the Hazhalm Testing Grounds via Caedarva Mire.
  • Zone in for a cutscene.
  • Check the gate at the end of the tunnel for another cutscene.
  • You will receive a new key item: Luminian Dagger.


Game Description

Gessho believes Nashmeira's life may be in danger. Hurry to the Hazhalm Testing Grounds to protect her from the conniving Flit.
