Walkthrough

  • Moreno-Toeno, a teacher in the Aurastery, will reward you if you bring him a Two-Leaf Mandragora Bud and a Bird Feather.

Game Description

Client: Moreno-Toeno (Aurastery, Windurst Waters)
Summary:

Moreno-Toeno would like you to collect one bird feather and one two-leaf mandragora bud to be used as teaching materials in the next magic experiment at the School of Magic.
