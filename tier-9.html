Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Beastmen tend to be grouped into tiers of 10 levels each, with certain kinds of beastmen within those tiers.

Tier 9 is for beastmen whose levels range from 80 and above. These are mostly seen in strongholds in the Near East areas. Platinum Beastcoins can be stolen from the majority of these beastmen.
