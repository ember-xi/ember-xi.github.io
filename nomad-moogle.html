Notes

  • With the exception of the one in Ru'Lude Gardens, the various Nomad Moogles allow the player to change jobs, check their Mog House, and receive items and money sent to them from the Auction House via the Delivery Box. Nomad Moogles allow you to access your Mog Locker after selecting the option with Fubruhn to increase Mog Locker availability.
    • The Nomad Moogle in Ru'Lude Gardens, on the other hand, will offer information about the Merit Point system.
