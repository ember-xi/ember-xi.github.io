Musical Score - 1st Page

Description:A scroll covered in Moblin letters and pictographs. You cannot read the Moblin words, but the pictographs have been drawn to be understandable to Trolls. The score seems to represent a rhythm of some sort.

Used In BCNM:Halvung Invasion

Contains Zone(s):Halvung

Obtained:Troll Ironworker, Troll Gemologist, Troll Combatant

Notes:This key item will be lost on zoning or logging out.
