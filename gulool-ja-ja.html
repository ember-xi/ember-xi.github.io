  • They are "The Autarch" of the Mamool Ja Savages.

Mamook Version

  • Defeating this NM inside Mamook prevents them from appearing at the next level 5 or higher Mamool Ja Besieged battle.
  • When Gulool Ja Ja is defeated the next Mamool Ja Besieged will be reduced to level 1. Verification Needed
  • Confirmed to be behind the Mahogany Door on map 3, opened with Mamook Tanscale Key, Mamook Blackscale Key and Mamook Silverscale Key
  • As Ninja it uses Ninjutsu
  • Special attacks:
    • Vorpal Wheel: single-target damage, ignores Utsusemi
      • Will use it on anyone who casts magic on them.
      • As it's health decreases, the damage from Vorpal Wheel seems to increase and can easily one shot any mage who casts on them, even with Stoneskin.
      • If you're in range to cast on Gulool Ja Ja, you're in Vorpal Wheel range.
    • Tyrranic Blare: AoE damage, ignores Utsusemi
    • Miasma: AoE damage + Slow + Poison + Disease
    • Rushing Slash: single-target, 4-hit
    • Decussate: Heavy AoE damage, absorbed by Utsusemi, used at <=25% health
      • Damage may go as high as ~800-1300
      • With decent defense damage can be lowered to ~250-450
  • Inside Mamook, they are assisted by 2 Mamool Ja Palatines (THF) and 2 Mamool Ja Chamberlains (WHM).
    • Mamool Ja Palatine and Mamool Ja Chamberlain will respawn 10 seconds after being killed.
    • Mamool Ja Palatine and Mamool Ja Chamberlain do not share hate with Gulool Ja Ja and can easily be dragged away to a side room.
  • Uses Mijin Gakure at low health (~3%)
  • Clicking on the Mahogany Door again warps you to Jade Sepulcher
  • On being defeated, they say:
Gulool Ja Ja (One of the heads): Dead, am I? And you?
(Other head): Dead as well, I am.
(First head): Then finished we are...
