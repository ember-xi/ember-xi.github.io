Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Bio is a harmful status effect that erodes the player's skin, making him/her lose focus until it wears off or is removed. Afflicted characters experience dark damage over time and reduced attack. The amount of DoT damage Bio inflicts is determined by the tier of Bio cast and the caster's Dark Magic Skill (players) or level (mobs). Damage dealt by the bio effect will wake characters under the effect of sleep.

Attack Down

Bio: 5%
Bio II: 10%
Bio III: 15%

How to remove the effect

  • The White Magic spell Erase will remove this effect
  • A Dia effect of a higher tier will replace the Bio effect with itself.
  • Going in and out of your Mog House.

How the effect is inflicted

Spells

  • Black Magic - Bio, Bio II, Bio III

Other Information

Dia / Bio Priority
Dia and Bio do not stack.
Dia -> Bio -> Dia II -> Bio II -> Dia III -> Bio III
Example: If Dia is cast, and Bio is cast afterwards, Dia will be overwritten. If Dia II is then cast, Bio will be overwritten. If Bio II is cast, Dia II will be overwritten.
Note: This is not the case for player characters who have been hit with both spells. This only applies to spells cast by PCs onto monsters.
