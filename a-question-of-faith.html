   Bastok Reputation 4 at the most

  Previous Quest:   Out of the Depths
  Next Quest:   Return to the Depths

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Ayame to activate the quest.
  • Next, go to Bastok Mines and talk to Virnage at I-5 (in the Bats Lair Inn) to receive the Dawn Talisman key item.
  • Next head out to Oldton Movalpolos to speak with Rakorok at K-10. When you click on Rakorok, the NM Bugallug will immediately appear. Defeat Bugallug.
  • Killable by: 3 characters level 61-63. Confirmed kill with the following setup: 63 Paladin, 63 Red Mage, 61 Red Mage
  • Confirmed Kill with a solo Level 66 Beastmaster with FunguarFamiliar
  • Confirmed kill with a solo Level 67 Puppetmaster with Soulsoother Head/Stormwaker Frame Automaton
  • Confirm kill with a 63 RDM/BLM. only had to use stoneskin once. got aggro by 2 moblins near the end of fight but that wasnt a problem.
  • Talk to Rakorok again, and get a cut-scene.
  • Go back to Bastok Mines and speak with Virnage again to get your reward.

Game Description

Client: Ayame (Cannonry, Metalworks)
Summary:

A holy tome was among the items you retrieved from Movalpolos. A friar known as Virnage is asking for the help of an adventurer to investigate this matter further.
