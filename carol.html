Carol

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Carols are songs sung by a Bard to increase the resistance to elemental spells.

36: Light Carol
38: Earth Carol
40: Water Carol
42: Wind Carol
44: Fire Carol
46: Ice Carol
48: Lightning Carol
50: Dark Carol

Effect

Capped at +40 resistance. Each +1 on an instrument adds +5 resistance.

+17 with Singing Skill 105-111 on a subjob (Light Carol only).
+18 with Singing Skill 113-114.
