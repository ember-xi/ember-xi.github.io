Moghancement: Fishing Skill

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description
"Moogle-powered assistance from your Mog House. Increases your fishing skill."
Notes
This increases your Fishing Skill by 1.

The log will say you have mog enhancement Fishing. However it does not state whether you have the Fishing Skill enhancement or Fishing for Items enhancement. The only way to tell which fishing enhancement you have is to check your key items.

See Moghancements for more information.

It is obtained through these Furnishings:
  • Fishing Hole Map
  • The Big One
