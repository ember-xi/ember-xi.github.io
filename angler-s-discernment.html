Angler's Discernment

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Angler's Discernment is the innate ability for skilled fishers to see what fish is on their hook. The activation rate of Discernment is effected by the difference in you fishing skill and the skill cap of the fish you are targeting. Your modified fishing skill can be four levels lower than the skill cap of the fish in order for Discernment to activate. Furniture enhancements, and fishing skill+ gear will all count towards your skill for Discernment. It also increases the chance of gaining critical stamina drain chances / Gold Arrows. <be>

Uncomfirmed: Activation may also have some other factor like salt/freshwater, small/large fish, zone, moon phase, and how many fish types can be caught in the zone
