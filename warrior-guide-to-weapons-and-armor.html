Beginning Note

This guide is made to help you on choosing gear while leveling Warrior on Eden. It caters towards new players, omitting some niche but useful items that may not be the easiest to obtain or use. Eden tends to focus on certain level ranges, so I put extra emphasis on items in important level ranges, and also don't mention items higher than level 67 as people just don't EXP in that level range.

Bolded items are items I find to be very important to get.

Last Updated: 2-5-25.

Weapons

Axe:

  • 1: Bronze Axe - You can't use a Great Axe till level 5, so just use something that bonks.

Great Axe:

  • 5: Butterfly Axe - You want a Gaxe as soon as you can get it. Try to grab it from the NPC in Bastok Mines for 700g~.
  • 8: Inferno Axe
  • 12: Greataxe
  • 16: Moth Axe
  • 20: Neckchopper - Very strong, but expensive for a level 20 item.
    • 25: Plantbane - Alternative to Neckchopper.
  • 30: Centurion's Axe - You'll want to own one of these for 30 cap missions and BCNMs.
  • 31: Twicer - Coolest Gaxe alive, when it is used it will give you 30 seconds of 100% Double Attack rate. Using this and pairing it with Mighty Strike does insane DPS.
    • 31: Voulge - Small upgrade over Centurion's Axe.
  • 34: Heavy Axe - You'll probably EXP in Zi'tah a lot, this is your BIS axe for that zone, it's also very cheap.
  • 37: Horror Voulge
    • 39: Huge Moth Axe - Cheap alternative to Horror Voulge.
  • 42: Raifu
  • 46: Eisentaenzer - NM drop, honestly you probably won't be EXPing in the 40s so maybe not worth the time getting.
  • 49: Gigant Axe - Cheap, big upgrade for 50 cap stuff.
  • 54: Executioner - You shouldn't use Gaxe in this level range, so I'd recommend skipping this.
  • 56: Schwarz Axt - You shouldn't use Gaxe in this level range, so I'd recommend skipping this.
  • 59: Heavy Darksteel Axe - 60 cap legend.
  • 62: Demon's Axe
  • 64: Colossal Axe

Polearm:

The primary use for Polearm is at level 53-60, Penta Thrust just outclasses any Great Axe weaponskill for this level range, once you get Raging Rush you stop using Polearm.

  • 53: Thundercloud
  • 55: Spark Fork
  • 57: Partisan

Grips:

  • 30: Brass Grip
  • 55: Mythril Grip
    • 55: Sword Strap - Probably don't use this, it has the chance to lower your DPS if it makes you gain not enough TP per swing.
  • 60: Pole Grip - BIS forever, expensive.
    • 65: Claymore Grip - Good starter alternative to Pole Grip and its almost free.

Ammo

  • 1: Orphic Egg - Pretty good if you can activate it.
  • 1: Happy Egg/Fortune Egg - Event only but good.
  • 30: RSE Sachet
  • 58: Tiphia Sting
  • 60: Bibiki Seashell - If you don't want to buy a Tiphia Sting this is questable and has defensive stats.
  • 63: Bomb Core

Crossbow:

Crossbows tend to be by far your highest damaging way to attack until at the very least level 40, maybe even into the 50s. The HQ crossbows have vastly improved stats, so get them if you can afford them.

  • 1: Light Crossbow - Crossbow will carry you 1-10, you don't even need to draw your weapon.
  • 10: Legionnaire's Crossbow
  • 12: Crossbow - The most overpowered weapons in the dunes, get an HQ if you can.
  • 25: Power crossbow
  • 30: Zamburak - This will carry you through promyvions and beyond, get an HQ.
  • 37: Rikonodo - Very rare and expensive, can skip.
  • 40: Thug's Zamburak - Probably the last crossbow you'll use as you stop shooting Crossbows not long after.

Crossbow Bolts:

  • 1: Bronze Bolt
  • 10: Blind Bolt
  • 15: Acid Bolt
  • 25: Venom Bolt
    • 25: Bloody Bolt - For soloing and healing.
  • 30: Holy Bolt - Turbo busted additional effect damage at 30 and will be for a while.

Armor

Something worth mentioning for Armor is you generally can ignore items that are just "Defense" stat upgrades, they have almost no value even in the best circumstances. Do not get hung up on upgrading your level 1 Bronze Cap to a level 7 Leather Bandana, it just is not worth the gil or time spent. Focus purely on items that provide beneficial stats like Accuracy, Strength, Dexterity, or Attack.

Head

  • 1: Bronze Cap
    • 7: Leather Bandana - Skippable defense upgrade.
  • 10: Royal Footmans Bandana - This has AGI on it which translates into Ranged Accuracy, you will use this till 24+.
  • 24: Empress Hairpin - Very good ra/ex item that is worth camping
    • 30: Centurion's Visor - DEX+1 is good if you can't get a Hairpin.
  • 41: Voyager Sallet - You will use this to 60+ if you have it.
    • 43: Valkyrie's Mask or Walkure Mask - Good option if you don't have a Voyager Sallet.
    • 56: Fighter's Mask - Has some DEX on it, really not bad but not better than many of the earlier helms.
  • 59: Sipahi Turban - Good for weaponskilling.
  • 60: Patroclus's Helm - Cool ra/ex item that isn't super easy to get but is good if you have it.

Body

  • 1: Bronze Harness or RSE Starter Body
    • 7: Leather Vest - Skippable defense upgrade.
    • 10: Scale Mail - Skippable defense upgrade.
    • 16: Bone Harness - Skippable defense upgrade.
    • 17: Lizard Jerkin - Skippable defense upgrade.
    • 21: Beetle Harness - Skippable defense upgrade.
    • 24: Chainmail - Skippable defense upgrade.
  • 29: Eisenbrust - Has some AGI for shooting the crossbow.
    • 30: Centurion's Scale Mail - Still a skippable defense upgrade but its cheap and easy to get for 30 caps.
  • 33: RSE Bodies
  • 40: Royal Squire's Chainmail - Holy moly it's an item with useful stats!
  • 45: Brigadine - HQ is pretty cool but expensive.
  • 45: Luisant Haubert - Wildly expensive and I don't recomend using your gil on it, but it is very good.
  • 55: Jaridah Peti - First good body, and you'll want it if you level at Leypoint.
  • 57: Scorpion Harness - Good for the last few levels of Leypoint and an affordable body to use till you buy a Haubergeon.
  • 59: Haubergeon - Huge item you'll use all the way to 75 and beyond.

Hands

  • 1: Bronze Mittens or RSE Starter Hands
    • 7: Leather Gloves - Skippable defense upgrade.
  • 10: Royal Footmans Gloves or Legionnaire's Mittens - They have offensive stats, will last you till 30.
  • 27: RSE if applicable.
  • 30: Royal Squire's Mufflers - You'll use these for a while.
    • 40: Iron Musketeer's Gauntlets - Great for macroing in for ranged attack.
  • 45: Luisant Moufles - Expensive, not worth it, but good.
  • 50: Spiked Finger Gauntlets - Wildly expensive and in general rare on Eden, but this item is very overpowered for its level.
  • 50: Royal Knight's Mufflers - The alternative to Spiked Finger Gauntlets if you don't want to drop 1m+ on leveling hands.
    • 54: Fighter's Mufflers - Free armor that has good strength on it, probably not better than RK Mufflers, but free.
    • 55: Jaridah Bazubands - Basically the same thing as Royal Knight's Mufflers

Legs

  • 1: Bronze Subligar or RSE Starter Legs
    • 7: Leather Trousers - Skippable defense upgrade.
    • 10: Scale Cuisses - Skippable defense upgrade.
    • 16: Bone Subligar - Skippable defense upgrade.
    • 17: Lizard Trousers - Skippable defense upgrade.
    • 20: Royal Footmans Trousers - Skippable defense upgrade.
  • 25: Bastokan Subligar - This is gonna last you a long time, I'd recommend investing in the Republic Subligar.
  • 55: Royal Knights Breeches - You'll use this till 75.
    • 58: Fighter's Cuisses - Free alternative to Royal Knight's Breeches.

Feet

  • 1: Bronze Leggings or RSE Starter Feet
    • 7: Leather Highboots - Skippable defense upgrade.
    • 10: Royal Footmans Boots - Skippable defense upgrade.
    • 16: Bone Leggings - Skippable defense upgrade.
    • 21: Beetle Leggings - Skippable defense upgrade.
  • 7: Leaping Boots or Bounding Boots - Hard to get but there is little else that has relevant stats until 75.
    • 29: RSE if applicable.
    • 30: Centurion's Greaves or Royal Squires Sollerets - AGI good for shooting if you don't have Leapers.
  • 40: Iron Musketeers Sabatons - Great for ranged attack macroing.
  • 52: Fighter's Calligae - Free and pretty good, you can ride these to 75.
  • 62: RSE if applicable.

Waist

  • 1: Moogle Belts - Event only belts that you can only get during Easter.
  • 7: Leather Belt
  • 15: Warrior's Belt
  • 18: Brave Belt - I wouldn't recommend getting the NQ version of this, it goes stat neutral.
  • 40: Tilt Belt - Very cheap, very good, and you will use for any 40 cap thing so keep it.
  • 48: Life Belt - You'll never sell this belt, it's very good even at 75.
    • 50: Swift Belt - Great belt at 75, not so great for leveling.
    • 55: Speed Belt - Best belt at 75, not so great for leveling.
    • 60: Potent Belt - Alternative to Life Belt, pricey and mostly only good for WS.

Back

  • 4: Rabbit Mantle
  • 12: Travelers Mantle
  • 24: Nomads Mantle - AGI helps with Ranged Accuracy and there's truly nothing else till 47.
  • 47: Jaguar Mantle - First cape that actually does anything, but it's expensive so I'd skip.
  • 55: Royal Army Mantle or Republican Army Mantle
  • 61: Amemet Mantle - You'll use it till 75 and maybe even at 75.

Neck

  • 7: Wing Pendant
  • 21: Fang Necklace - Invest in a Spike Necklace, you'll be using this probably till 75 if you are new.
  • 33: Peacock Charm or Peacock Amulet - Extremely good even at 75, but also very hard to get.
  • 55: Royal Guard's Collar
  • 56: Merman's Gorget - Skip it if you can't afford the HQ.
  • 60: Chivalrous Chain - Affordable alternative to Peacock Charm.

Ring

  • 1: Copper Ring - Skippable but if you want to fill the slot.
  • 1: Starter Nation Rings
  • 14: Mighty Ring - Very powerful rings for the level, you'll use these till 30+ at least but they do have consumable charges.
  • 14: Amethyst Ring - If you can't afford to use Mighty Rings the DEX+1 or DEX+2 rings are the choice.
  • 30: Rajas Ring - Not for new players.
  • 34: Venerer Ring
  • 35: Jaeger Ring
  • 39: Blitz Ring - This ring is is BiS at 75, but probably isn't worth using before then.
  • 40: Woodsman Ring or Sniper's Ring - Take the time to farm 2 of these up, you'll use them till and at 75.
  • 57: Toreador's Ring

Earring

  • 1: Cassie Earring - Expensive and advanced level stuff.
  • 10: Optical Earring
  • 16: Bone Earring - Think about investing in +1s of these as you may use them a lot.
  • 21: Beetle Earring - Buy +1s of these, you can ride these to 75 to be honest.
  • 45: Tortoise Earring - Skip if you have Beetle+1s.
  • 55: Fang Earring
  • 58: Assault Earring - BiS even at 75 but very expensive.
  • 61: Minuet Earring - Very good if you have a Bard in your party.
  • 63: Coral Earring
