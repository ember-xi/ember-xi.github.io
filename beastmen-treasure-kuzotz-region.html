Walkthrough

  • Peddlestox only appears on Earthsday at 0:00 at (L-7) in Western Altepa Desert.
  • She will ask you to bring her four items. They are all Rare/Ex. Note: This quest will not appear in your quest log.
    • Xhifhut Strings drop from Antican Aedilis in Quicksand Caves.
    • Xhifhut Body drops from:
      • Antican Signifer in Quicksand Caves
      • Antican Lanista in Western Altepa Desert
    • Xhifhut Bow drops from:
      • Antican Hoplomachus in Western Altepa Desert
      • Antican Veles in Eastern Altepa Desert
    • Xhifhut Head drops from Hastatus XI-XII in Quicksand Caves.
  • When you bring the items back to Peddlestox, she will tell you the location of your Treasure Box. The location is different every time.
  • The ??? will appear in the location between 20:00 and 4:00.
  • Use a Pickaxe on the ??? and you will randomly receive a treasure item, but not necessarily the item he said you could get. Possible treasure includes 3-5 of the following and 4,000 to 10,000 gil:
