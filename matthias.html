Matthias

From Zenith XI Wiki

Crafts three pieces of Dancer artifact armor in Bastok Markets.

Contents
  1. Unlock the service
  2. What to bring
  3. Steps
  4. Sources

Unlock the service

Finish The Road to Divadom, then speak to Olgald in Upper Jeuno (G-7). Keep Dancer as your main job when speaking to Olgald and using Matthias’s service.

What to bring

RewardLevelTrade one of each
Dancer's Bangles52Karakul Cloth, Rainbow Cloth, Rainbow Velvet
Dancer's Tiara54Imperial Silk Cloth, Silver Brocade, Wolf Felt
Dancer's Toe Shoes56Wamoura Cloth, Moblinweave, Gold Brocade

Steps

  1. Find Matthias on the bridge near the music shop in Bastok Markets (J-9).
  2. Choose one armor piece. Trade all three listed materials for that piece.
  3. Change zones and return after the next Vana’diel day to collect it.
  4. Order the next piece after collection. Only one order can be active, and a chosen order cannot be cancelled.

These are material commissions, not treasure-coffer drops.

Zenith availability: this is the original quest route. Completion on the current Zenith server has not been confirmed; a wiki entry does not install the quest.
