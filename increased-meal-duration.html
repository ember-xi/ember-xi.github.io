Increased Meal Duration

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Increased Meal Duration can be bought, for 100 Imperial Standing, when getting Sanction from an Al Zahbi/Aht Urhgan Whitegate Gate Guard. When the Astral Candescence has been safe in Al Zahbi, for a while, this effect will double the length of time that a food's effect stays active.

After being so enchanted, any food effect will remain active for double its normal length. A food effect active before receiving Increased Meal Duration is not affected in any way.

Example Results:

  • A Meat Mithkabob, normally a 30-minute food, lasts for one hour.
  • A Meat Chiefkabob, normally a 1-hour food, lasts for two hours.
  • A Roast Mutton, normally a 3-hour food, lasts for six hours.

It should be noted that Increased Meal Duration doesn't offer any resistance to anything that would remove a food effect, such as the Colibri Snatch Morsel ability.
