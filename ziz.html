Ziz

From Zenith XI Wiki

Cockatrices found in Mamook, Nyzul Isle and the original Zhayolm Remnants.

Locations and rewards

  • Mamook: level 76–78 regular monsters. Drops include Giant Bird Feather and Ziz Meat. The group near I-9 can produce Zizzy Zillah.
  • Nyzul Isle: appear as normal floor enemies; Giant Bird Feather and Ziz Meat are listed.
  • Zhayolm Remnants: the original Salvage version can drop Praecipitatio Cell. Follow the floor route for cell distribution.

They detect by sight. Bring petrification removal for Cockatrice attacks and pull clear of nearby monsters.
