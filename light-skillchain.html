Light (Skillchain)

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Light is a Level 3 Skillchain (or Renkei), composed of the Fire, Light, Lightning and Wind Elements.

Formation

Weapon Skills, Blood Pacts, or Blue Magic w/ Chain Affinity with the proper attributes must be used in sequence to create Light:

  • Fusion → Fragmentation
  • Light → Light
