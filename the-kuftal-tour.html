Walkthrough

  • Talk to Datta. He will ask you to participate in a tour of Kuftal Tunnel.
  • Make a party of six members all lower than level 40.
    • There is no level cap, so your actual job level must be level 40 or below.
    • You can avoid aggro by renting a Chocobo to get there, and, once inside the tunnel, the only mobs around will be Lizards who do not aggro.
  • Travel to Kuftal Tunnel and examine the ??? that is just inside the entrance to get a cutscene with Hawk Nose.
  • Then return to Datta to get another cutscene and finish the quest.

Game Description

Client: Datta (Rabao)
Summary:

You have been asked to participate in a tour planned by Datta. Travel to the cliff inside the entrance to the Kuftal Tunnel with a party of six people of level forty or less, then meet with Datta's partner, Hawk Nose.
