Long-Bowed Chariot

From Zenith XI Wiki

The final boss on floor 5 of the original Bhaflau Remnants.

Contents
  1. Reach and defeat
  2. Armor drops
  3. Sources

Reach and defeat

  1. Follow the Bhaflau Remnants route. On floor 4, defeat all ten Archaic Gear before they escape to weaken the boss’s Homing Missile and remove its enmity-reset effect.
  2. Reach floor 5. Avoid standing directly in front of the boss, where Homing Missile can be used; recover HP and control enmity promptly after a missile.
  3. Prepare for strong physical hits and multiple attacks. Stun resistance builds quickly, so prioritize dangerous moves.
  4. Victory grants Comet Charioteer for An Imperial Heist.

Armor drops

Bodb's Crown, Bodb's Robe, Ea's Dastanas, Freyr's Ledelsens, Phobos's Mask, Phobos's Cuirass, Tsukikazu Haidate. These are level-25 components for the original Salvage armor upgrades; they are not the finished level-75 pieces.
