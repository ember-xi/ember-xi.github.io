Silence Gas

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Emits a noxious cloud in a fan-shaped area of effect, dealing Wind damage to all targets. Additional effect: silence

Description

  • Family: Funguar
  • Type: BreathImage source
  • Can be dispelled: N/A
  • Utsusemi/Blink absorb: Ignores shadows
  • Range: Unknown cone
  • Notes: Can be very deadly from higher level funguars. Damage varies depending on HP.
    • Appears to be directly affected by Magic Accuracy and Magic Attack.
