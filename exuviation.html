Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Restores HP and removes one detrimental magic effect.

Description

Spell cost: 40 MP
Ecosystem: Vermin
Type: Magical (Fire)
Blue Magic Points: 4
Stat Bonus: HP+5 MP+5 CHR+1
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 75

Timers

Casting Time: 3 seconds
Recast Time: 60 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Exuviation as part of a combo to create Resist Sleep.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Resist Sleep combo.

  • Pollen
  • Wild Carrot
  • Magic Fruit
  • Yawn

Notes

  • Self-target only.
  • Heal cap arround ~85 HP
  • Can only remove effects that can also be removed via Erase.
  • Being afflicted with one or more status ailments does not affect the amount of HP restored, unlike the mob version.
  • Generates an oddly high amount of enmity.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: n/a
