Promotion: Second Lieutenant

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Start NPC: Naja Salaheem - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-10) upstairs
Requirements:
 Acquire 25 Mercenary Rank points as Chief Sergent.
Items Needed: 3x Imperial Gold Pieces
6x Imperial Mythril Pieces
Title: Second Lieutenant
Repeatable: No
Reward: SL Wildcat Badge

Previous Quest: Promotion: Chief Sergeant
Next Quest: Promotion: First Lieutenant

Walkthrough

  • Begin the quest by walking into the Salaheem's Sentinels office.
  • After a cutscene, trade three Imperial Gold Pieces to Abquhbah for another cutscene. You will receive the key item Officer Academy Manual.
  • Trade Abquhbah two Imperial Mythril Pieces to initiate the Infiltration trial.
    • Three scenes with stationary Chocobo chicks will appear for a few seconds each.
  1. After the first scene, you will be asked what species were present and how many of them there were.
  2. After the second scene, you will be asked twice how many Chocobos were facing in certain directions.
  3. After the third scene, you will be asked how many Chocobos were present. There will be many more Chocobos here than in the first two scenes.
  • A wrong answer does not result in an immediate failure (Failing the third scene will result with a "wrong" response and a new picture.)
  • Trade Abquhbah two Imperial Mythril Pieces to initiate the Platoon Combat trial.
    • Trade Abquhbah a piece of equipment from Aht Urhgan beastmen. Confirmed accepted equipment include:
      • Macuahuitl -1
      • Mamool Ja Collar
      • Mamool Ja Helmet
      • Januwiyah -1
      • Tariqah -1
      • Troll Pauldron
      • Troll Vambrace
      • Lamian Armlet
      • Lamian Kaman -1
      • Qutrub Gorget
  • Trade Abquhbah two Imperial Mythril Pieces to initiate the Survival Training trial.
    • You will be asked a series of eight questions regarding details from the previous Promotion quests. You only need to answer seven of the eight questions correctly to pass.
    • The answers are (German answers in round brackets):
  1. Fulfill requests without fail. (...Wünsche der Generaldirektorin Naja mit äußerster Hingabe!)
  2. Bigger than a marid. (Größer als ein Marid.)
  3. Mythralline Wellspring. (Mythrallin-Quelle)
  4. A detective agency. (Schlangenauge-Detektivagentur.)
  5. Empyrean Incense. (Der Weihrauch des vollkommenen Glücks.)
  6. Warjal. (Warjal.)
  7. Blackrust. (Schwarzer Rost.)
  8. He tripped over his right foot. (Er stolperte über sein rechtes Bein.)
  • After the third trial you receive the SL Wildcat Badge and the quest is complete.
  • If you fail any of the trials, you will be required to pay the 2 Imperial Mythril Piece fee again to retry


Game Description

Client: Naja Salaheem (Salaheem's Sentinels)
Summary:

To earn your position in the upper ranks of Salaheem's Sentinels, you have enrolled in the Salaheem Officer Academy.
Be the best that you can be!
