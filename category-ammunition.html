This is a category at the Auction House that contains:

  • Arrows for Archery
  • Bolts and bullets for Marksmanship
  • Thrown projectiles for Throwing.
  • Equippable ammunition for additional effects on certain types of weapons.
  • Non-expendable items that give the user certain status changes when equiped on the ammo slot
