Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Restores HP for party members within area of effect.

Description

Spell cost: 55 MP
Ecosystem: Beasts
Type: Magical (Wind)
Blue Magic Points: 4
Stat Bonus: CHR+2, HP+10
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 16

Timers

Casting Time: 4.5 seconds
Recast Time: 15 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Healing Breeze as part of a combo to create Auto Regen.

Can be paired with the following spell to create Auto Regen combo.

  • Sheep Song

Notes

  • Self-target only with area of effect centered around caster and a range of 10'.
  • Can be used with Divine Seal to cure for more than the typical amount.
  • Amount healed increases quickly with MND (e.g. 60 with no +MND, 81 with +11 MND) and Healing Magic skill and to a lesser extent with VIT.
  • Affected by Light Staff/Apollo's Staff.
  • Healing Breeze from Elvaan WHM75/BLU cures for ~226 HP with Light Staff.
  • Is NOT affected by Wind Staff/Auster's Staff.
  • Soft cap is around 180 HP, (198 with staff), beyond that it takes a lot of MND to raise the amount healed at all.
  • Dhalmel's in Buburimu Peninsula seem to use Healing Breeze more frequently than Dhalmel's in Tahrongi Canyon.
