Walkthrough

  • Talk to Sobane again. She and her husband need your help confirming a threat to a local aristocrat, but the only ones who may know about it are the tight lipped Tonberries in Carpenters' Landing.
  • Travel to South Landing in Carpenters' Landing. Either take the barge from the entrance in Northern San d'Oria, or take a chocobo to the entrance at (J-8) in Jugner Forest.
  • Speak with the elvaan Anguenet standing next to the Tonberry at the docks. He knows a way to speak with them, but he won't do it for free. He wants some Attohwa Ginseng.
    • Attohwa Ginseng drops from Gallinippers, Ogreflies and Monarch Ogreflies in Attohwa Chasm.
  • Anguenet will give you the Key Item: Tonberry Blackboard which will allow you to communicate with the Tonberry Riche on the ferry, who can randomly appear on any route.
    • Purchase a Barge Ticket or Barge Multi-ticket and board a barge headed for Central Landing via Emfea Way.
    • Talk to Riche, the Tonberry on board. Ask him all the questions (5); at the end he'll take the key item. Be sure to do this before the ride is over, which is approximately 7 minutes.
  • Go to Fei'Yin I-6 on map 1 and kill Shadows there for the Treasury Gold.
  • Take the Treasury Gold to Davoi and trade it to the ??? at J-11, which spawns a Hematic Cyst NM.
  • Kill the Hematic Cyst and select the ??? again for a cutscene.
  • A group of characters who are doing this quest can all get the cut-scene by killing just one Hematic Cyst, from one Treasury Gold.
  • Return to Southern San d'Oria and talk to Sobane to complete the quest.

Adventurer's Notes

  • Attohwa Ginseng seemed to be a very rare drop with Gallinippers. The drop rate was nearly 100% on the higher level Monarch Ogreflies when subbing Thief. The Monarch Ogreflies were easily killed by two L75 characters.

Game Description

Client: Sobane (House on Pikeman's Way, Southern San d'Oria)
Summary:According to Sobane's husband Ondieumais, the count who owns the Carpenters' Landing territory is being threatened. Find a way to communicate with the Tonberries of Carpenters' Landing and discover who is making these threats.
