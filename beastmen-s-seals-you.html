Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

If your reading this you are here for one of two reasons, either you don't know what a Beastmen's seal is or you are looking how you can get more of them. Hopefully the categories below can be of some use to you.

What are they?

Beastmen's seals are items that allow you to take on some challenging battles known as BCNMs. The point to taking on these battles are the valuable rewards that can drop from these fights, for example Peacock Charm, Refresh and Utsusemi: Ni all drop from BCNMs as well as many other powerful items.

How do I use them?

In order to use them you will need to visit a npc in Port Jeuno by the name of Shami, he is standing in (H-6) on the map, he is tucked away in a corner on the main level.

You can trade to him all of your seals and he will keep them in storage for you which will nicely save you some inventory space, if you talk with him you will be able to exchange seals for orbs you will need 20, 30, 40, 50 or 60 seals to receive an orb for more details on exchanging use the BCNM page.

How do I get more of them?

When you start doing BCNMs you may find yourself very quickly burning through all of your seals pursuing that one elusive item that just won't drop for you! So here are some tips to maximising your seals gained.

  • One of the best times to gain lots of seals is leveling from 1 to 12, as mobs are plentiful die easily and you can solo these levels so no need to share your seals with party members.
  • When a Beastmen's seal drops there is a hidden 5 minute lockout before another can even try to drop, you can however reset this timer by either zoning out of an area or simply logging out and back in.
  • At higher levels use a strong solo class to fight with mobs that are Easy Prey. Preferably kill mobs that also drop something worth farming or a NM.
