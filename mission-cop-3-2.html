Before you begin

  • Complete CoP 3-1.

Starting point

Door to Tenshodo headquarters · Lower Jeuno · J-8

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno and inspect the back door leading into Tenshodo headquarters.
  2. Go to Ru'Lude Gardens and approach the palace stairs toward the Audience Chamber for the next scene.

Battle and progress notes

  • Follow the story door interaction; this mission does not require joining the Tenshodo first.

Completion and reward

Story progression.
