Information

  • There were two Versions of the paper: edition 1 and edition 2.
  • The newspaper was home to various articles, stories and detailed information on various people, places, and events in Vanadiel.
  • Characters that work for the paper had a above their heads.
  • In recent updates for edition 2 an advertising section had been added so people can advertise various planned events and linkshells etc.
  • A new issue of the newspaper was produced monthly.
  • The Vana'diel Tribune was suspended in February 2006.
