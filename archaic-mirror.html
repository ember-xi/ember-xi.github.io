Information

An Archaic Mirror found within a Beastmen lair such as Mamook is a targettable structure that you can destroy. The amount of Archaic Mirrors in a particular lair will increase the rate of the residents' training. Ie. the more Archaic Mirrors an area has, the faster the Beastmen in that area "level up" for Besieged and progress from "Training" to "Preparing" and finally to "Advancing".

On attacking one of these structures, they give off a siren-type noise. Defending beastmen in the area around that mirror will attack. Regardless of how far away from the Mirror they are, they will always walk to it and upon seeing it being attacked, link to whoever has hate. Clear the beastmen guarding the mirror before attacking the mirror. When defeated, they will always drop an Archaic Mirror (Item).

You can view how many Archaic Mirrors and Prisoners an area has, as well as the current status of that area's troops, by going to the in-game menu and clicking "Region Info" then "Besieged".

1 mirror will respawn every 3 game days, except for the three situations:

  • mirror is destroyed while the beastmen army in that lair is in a preparing stage
  • beastmen army in that lair is "defending"
  • beastmen army in that lair is "attacking"
