Charged Ammunition

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Certain items which are equippable in the Ammunition slot on the character cannot be thrown. These items instead serve as ammunition to enhance the currently equipped Melee Weapon.

If the appropriate weapon and Charged Ammunition pair is equipped, the effect listed below will happen occasionally during battle. When the effect occurs, one unit of the Charged Ammunition will be consumed.
