Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Delivers a threefold attack. Accuracy varies with TP.

Description

Spell cost: 16 MP
Ecosystem: Arcana
Type: Physical (Blunt)
Blue Magic Points: 2
Stat Bonus: STR+1
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 18

Timers

Casting Time: 0.5 seconds
Recast Time: 11.75 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Bludgeon as part of a combo to create Undead Killer.

Can be paired with the following spell to create Undead Killer combo.

  • Smite of Rage

Notes

  • Hand-to-Hand based damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: CHR: 30%.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Skillchain Element(s): Fire (can open Scission or Fusion; can close Liquefaction)

How to Obtain

Acquired From

Mob Family: Cardian
Minimum Blue Mage Level To Acquire: 8
Name Level Zone
Two of Batons/Coins/Cups/Swords 1-5 Outer Horutoto Ruins
Three of Batons/Coins/Cups/Swords 5-9 Outer Horutoto Ruins
Four of Batons/Coins/Cups/Swords 10-14 Outer Horutoto Ruins
Five of Batons/Coins/Cups/Swords 15-19 Outer Horutoto Ruins


Several of these cardians can be found in Outer Horutoto Ruins by entering the F-11 tower in West Sarutabaruta.
