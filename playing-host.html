Notes

  • Venessa in Ru'Lude Gardens (H-10) will offer you the first three ENMs in Promyvion if you have completed the first cut-scenes to start the Chains of Promathia missions.
  • If you select the 'Spire of Mea' she will give you the Censer of Animus key item.
  • Upon entering the Spire of Mea, you will have the option of entering the ENM battle at the battlefield.
  • May spawn at least one Seether, but only the Envier NM must be defeated to win this ENM.
  • Hysteroanimas are suggested, this boss uses a very potent large range spin attack called Carousel which does around 300 damage to everyone.
