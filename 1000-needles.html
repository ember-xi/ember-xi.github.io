Shoots multiple needles at enemies within range.

Description

Spell cost: 350 MP
Ecosystem: Plantoid
Type: Magical (Light)
Blue Magic Points: 5
Stat Bonus: VIT+3, AGI+3
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 62

Timers

Casting Time: 12 seconds
Recast Time: 120 seconds

Combines With...

You can use 1000 Needles as part of a combo to create Beast Killer.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Beast Killer combo.

  • Sprout Smack
  • Wild Oats

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Bursts on Light; affects accuracy only.
