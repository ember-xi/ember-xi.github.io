Before you begin

Be allied with Bastok, have Rank 6, and finish the preceding required story steps. Raise Rank Points if the mission is not offered.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission and speak with Naji. Visit Gilgamesh in Norg until he briefs you about Frag Rocks.
  2. Prepare Adaman Ore. Enter Ifrit’s Cauldron from Yhoator Jungle (I-5) and reach the lava ??? on map 1 (H-7).
  3. Trade the ore to summon Magma and Salamander. Defeat Magma and obtain a Frag Rock; prepare for the other enemy as well.
  4. Trade your Frag Rock to Gilgamesh, then return to Naji.

Important notes

  • The Norg introductory Zilart scene may be needed to access Gilgamesh’s room. Each player needs a Frag Rock.
