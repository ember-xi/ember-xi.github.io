Crystal Clusters

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A Cluster is an item that, when used, will shatter into a stack of 12 crystals of its respective element. Elementals are guaranteed to drop at least one when defeated, and may drop up to three. A handful of synthesis recipes also use Crystal Clusters as a synthing ingredient.

Possible Crystal Clusters:

  • Light Cluster
  • Dark Cluster
  • Fire Cluster
  • Ice Cluster
  • Wind Cluster
  • Earth Cluster
  • Lightning Cluster
  • Water Cluster
