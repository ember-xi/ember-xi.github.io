Zircon Quadav

From Zenith XI Wiki

Black Mage Quadav found in present-day and past areas.

Locations and useful drops

AreaLevelUseful information
Rolanberry Fields30–36Gil; Silver Beastcoin can be stolen.
Beadeaux34–38Silver Engraving, Wool Hat and Wool Cuffs are listed drops.
Pashhow Marshlands (S)71–74Higher-level past-area version.

These are normal spawns that detect sound and link. Interrupt or silence their spells when possible. One upper-floor Beadeaux spawn is the placeholder for Zo'Khu Blackcloud.
