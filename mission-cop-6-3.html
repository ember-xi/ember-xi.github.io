Before you begin

  • Complete CoP 6-2: A Place to Return.

Starting point

Pherimociel · Ru'Lude Gardens · G-6

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Pherimociel in the palace guard room (G-6).
  2. Examine the Audience Chamber door upstairs in the palace (H-6).
  3. Speak to Mathilde in Selbina's Weavers' Guild (H-9). This finishes 6-3 in the native mission script.
  4. Continue to Cid in the Metalworks (H-8) to begin 6-4 before travelling to Sealion's Den.

Progress to the next Chains of Promathia mission.

Walkthrough

  • Head to Ru'Lude Gardens and talk to Pherimociel at G-6 for a cutscene.
  • Head to Ru'Lude Gardens and check the Audience Chamber door upstairs at H-6 for a cutscene.
  • Head to Selbina and talk to Mathilde at H-9, inside of the Weavers' Guild, for a cutscene.
  • Head to Metalworks and talk to Cid at H-8 for a cutscene.

Game Description

Mission Orders
Esha'ntarl has returned to the Grand Duke Palace and is preparing the Jeuno Armada for its attack on Bahamut. At the same time, Prishe and Ulmia have finally solved the mystery that has been haunting them for the past twenty years.
