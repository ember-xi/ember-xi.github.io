  Previous Quest:   None

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Nogelle; she needs 3 Lufet Salt for her bean surprise and hints you might get it from river crabs.
  • Obtain the Lufet Salt by defeating River Crabs in West Ronfaure at the Knightwell. You cannot buy it as it is an Exclusive item.
  • Trade the salt to Nogelle to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Nogelle (Rusty Anchor Pub, Port San d'Oria)
Summary:

Fetch three chunks of salt from Lufet's Lake. Beans at the Rusty Anchor are famous for their special salty taste.

Game Script

SPOILER WARNING: Details about a quest, mission or other Final Fantasy XI in-game storyline follow.

Port San d'Oria

Nogelle: Welcome travelers! Come on in! Try the best beans in town! Try our famous bean cuisine, satisfaction guaranteed! You know... Our beans really need some Lufet Salt. It's the secret to our bean stew. Thing is, we're running short, and once we're out we'd lose our customers! You think you could bring three chunks? What do you say?


Not today.

Nogelle: Well that's too bad. If you find any, though, you know where to bring them! Remember, you can get Lufet Salt from river crabs!


Sure.

Nogelle: Thank you! You can find Lufet Salt from river crabs. But watch out for the pincers.


Quest Completion

Nogelle: Yes! This should give our beans a bang! Here, this is for you. If you happen to find more, bring them by. I'll buy them off you in bunches of three.
