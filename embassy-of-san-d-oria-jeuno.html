Embassy of San d'Oria (Jeuno)

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Grouped around the fountain in Quadrangle, at the foot of the stairs leading to the Palace, are the Embassies of the three Nations. The Embassy of San d'Oria is located on the western edge of the Quadrangle.

Location: Ru'Lude Gardens ( G - 9 )

Building type: Embassy
