Igqira Armor Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A set of armor crafted from natural materials such as bones, tusks, leathers, and skins. The Igqira set, most visibly recognized for the intricate tattoo design revealed underneath the armor, is wearable by Black Mages only, and enhances many of their most potent skills and bonuses, including Magic Attack Bonus and Elemental Magic skill. Every piece except the huaraches also has the potential to afflict an opponent with Curse if it strikes the wearer. The rate of Curse being inflicted stacks with additional pieces of Igqira and is superior if it is HQ.

Number of Pieces: 5

Cost to store: Cannot store

This set has a non-storable High Quality Version.
