Notes:

  • Title Obtained: The Vivisector
  • Lottery Spawn from the Demonic Rose around G-5/H-6 near Dragon's Aery roughly every 16-24 hrs.
  • Special Attacks: Meikyo Shisui, Draw In, Bad Breath, Sweet Breath, Impale, Vampiric Lash.
  • Breath attacks seem to be 50' AoE instead of the usual Cone Attack.
  • Echo Drops/Remedy and Poison Potion/Venom Potion are required to counteract Vivian's Special Attacks.
  • Killable with 3 party members. Ninja, Bard, and White Mage or Red Mage.
