Before you begin

  • Complete the RoZ and CoP closing sequences, including Apocalypse Nigh’s reward selection.

Starting point

Gilgamesh · Norg

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Finish Apocalypse Nigh, speak to Aldo, and complete Gilgamesh’s reward conversation in Norg after the required wait.
  2. Choose the quest earring with inventory space available. The native ending handlers advance both Awakening and Dawn to The Last Verse.
  3. No further battle or collection task belongs to The Last Verse; it is the completed storyline’s final mission entry.

Battle and progress notes

  • This finale is shared by Rise of the Zilart and Chains of Promathia. The earring is Apocalypse Nigh’s reward, not a second reward from this entry.

Completion and reward

Final RoZ/CoP story-log entry; no additional reward.
