Before you begin

  • Complete B-01: Better Part of Valor and leave/re-enter Bastok Markets [S].

Starting point

Engelhart · Bastok Markets [S] · K-10

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Engelhart at K-10 to begin, then speak to Pagdako at H-9 in Bastok Markets [S].
  2. Travel to present-day Metalworks and speak with Iron Eater at J-8 near the President’s Office. This step is in the present, not Bastok’s past.
  3. Return to Engelhart in Bastok Markets [S].
  4. In Grauberg [S], enter the cave near the H-5/I-5 border and examine the ??? inside, at the northwestern corner of I-6.
  5. Report to Engelhart, then speak with Gentle Tiger at H-6 outside the Metalworks entrance.
  6. Speak to Engelhart once more to complete the quest.

Battle and progress notes

  • If Iron Eater has another active quest’s dialogue, speak to him again until the requested scene occurs.
  • The audited handler has no additional game-day timer for this quest. The preceding quest requests zoning.

Completion and reward

10,000 gil.

What this opens

  • The Bastok route’s requirement for finishing WotG 2: Back to the Beginning
  • B-03: Light in the Darkness, after reaching Cait Sith
