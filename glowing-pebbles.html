Glowing Pebbles

From Zenith XI Wiki

Targetable quest location beside Lake Mechieume in Jugner Forest (S), at (I-5).

Contents
  1. Where to find it
  2. What to do
  3. Sources

Where to find it

Use the past version of Jugner Forest. Follow the eastern shore of Lake Mechieume at (I-5), approaching from the northeast near the Batallia Downs (S) route. An unmarked fence prevents a direct approach from some directions. Cycle nearby targets: the location has a name but does not visibly glow.

What to do

  • Lakeside Minuet: after the San d’Oria performance and return visit to Rhea Myuliah, examine the pebbles to receive the Stardust Pebble key item. Return to Laila.
  • The Road to Divadom: after the Jugner Forest (S) entry scene, examine the pebbles on Dancer. Bring and trade one Yagudo Glue, then return to Laila for the tights.
