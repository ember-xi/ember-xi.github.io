Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 1: Land of Sacred Serpents.
  • Supplies Package key item.

Starting point

Nareema · Caedarva Mire · K-9

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Choose one of the five Immortal sentries. A practical first route is the boat from Whitegate to Nashmau, then the eastern exit into Caedarva Mire.
  2. Use Sneak and Invisible and head east to Azouph Isle Staging Point at K-9. Speak with Nareema inside and hand over the Supplies Package through her dialogue.
  3. Use the Runic Portal inside the staging point to register it and return to Whitegate.
  4. Report to Naja Salaheem at I-10 to join the mercenaries.

Battle and progress notes

  • Only one delivery is required. The other accepted sentries are Nahshib at Dvucca Isle (Caedarva Mire I-9, second area), Waudeen at Halvung (Mount Zhayolm K-6), Meyaada at Ilrusi Atoll (Arrapago Reef G-5), and Daswil at Mamool Ja (Bhaflau Thickets H-11, second area).
  • A Runic Portal must be activated at its destination before normal return travel can use it.

Completion and reward

150 Imperial Standing; PSC Wildcat Badge; Private Second Class title.

What this opens

  • Mog Locker access
  • Sanction access
  • ToAU 3: President Salaheem
