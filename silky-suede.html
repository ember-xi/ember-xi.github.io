Silky Suede

From Zenith XI Wiki

Crafted leather used for Dancer’s Tights +1 and Dancer’s Toe Shoes +1.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Dancer use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Buy the material from the Auction House, or make one using a Wind Crystal, one Garnet and one Buffalo Leather. The reviewed server recipe caps at Leathercraft 69; older item lists sometimes label it 70.

Dancer use

Save one for Dancer's Tights +1 or one for Dancer's Toe Shoes +1. Each upgrade also requires the matching base artifact piece, Filet Lace, Gold Stud and the listed Ancient Beastcoins. See Dancer Artifact and Relic Upgrades before trading to Sagheera.
