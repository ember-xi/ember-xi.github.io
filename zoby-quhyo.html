Zoby Quhyo

From Zenith XI Wiki

Regional merchant for the Elshimo Lowlands.

Location

Port Bastok — J-7.

Trades while Bastok controls Elshimo Lowlands. Stock includes Kazham Peppers, Kazham Pineapple, Mithran Tomato, Black Pepper, Ogre Pumpkin, Kukuru Bean, Phalaenopsis.
