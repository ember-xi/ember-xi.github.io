Keeneared Steffi

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Familiar summoned by using the Beastmaster's Call Beast ability.

Familiar Jug Level Available Pet Level Cap Ready abilities
Keeneared Steffi (HQ Rabbit) Famous Carrot Broth 43 55
  • Whirl Claws - AOE damage (1 Charge)
  • Dust Cloud - Single target Blind (1 Charge)
  • Foot Kick - Single target damage (1 Charge)
When comparing Steffi vs. Funguar, Steffi falls way short in damage and TP moves, but is MUCH cheaper, making her better used for soloing or when you just don't "need" amazing damage from your pet.
