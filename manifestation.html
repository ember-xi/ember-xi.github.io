Manifestation

From Zenith XI Wiki

SCH 40 · Dark Arts stratagem.

Use

  1. Activate Dark Arts.
  2. Spreads the next eligible black enfeebling spell to enemies around its target, with increased MP and recast costs. Check nearby enemies before using area Sleep or Dispel.

Uses one stratagem charge. Charges are shared between the two Arts.

See Scholar Stratagems for charge recovery and Scholar Spell List for spell access.
