Max HP

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This page was only first created October 2008 on the retail wikipedia, which is after our era cut-off date. This information is likely to be accurate but please double check (ask in-game/discord etc) for cofirmation.

Merit Point Enhancement

  • Job: N/A
  • Limits: 8 levels (Merit Point Progression: 1>2>3>4>5>5>5>5 = 30 points total)
    • Note that this is also the group limit for the HP/MP Merit Points category.
  • Description: Each level of this enhancement increases your base HP by 10 per level, to a maximum bonus of +80 base HP at level 8.
    • This enhancement applies to all jobs, even if they are not 75- However the number of earned levels actually applied depends on your job level as shown in the chart below:
Job Levels Maximum HP Upgrade level applied
1-9 0
10-19 1 (+10 HP)
20-29 2 (+20 HP)
30-39 3 (+30 HP)
40-49 4 (+40 HP
50-54 5 (+50 HP
55-59 6 (+60 HP)
60-64 7 (+70 HP)
65-75 8 (+80 HP)
