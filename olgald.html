Olgald

From Zenith XI Wiki

The contact who introduces the crafted Dancer artifact armor orders.

Contents
  1. Location
  2. Unlock armor commissions
  3. Sources

Location

Upper Jeuno (G-7), beside Laila.

Unlock armor commissions

  1. Complete The Road to Divadom.
  2. Set Dancer as your main job and speak with Olgald.
  3. Visit Matthias in Bastok Markets and select the armor piece. Trade the requested materials, then collect it after the next Vana’diel day begins.

This route makes Dancer's Tiara, Dancer's Bangles and Dancer's Toe Shoes. Open the item or Dancer page for its material list. Commission one piece at a time.
