Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Shantotto at her Windurst Walls residence at K-7.
  2. Finish the scene in which she requests the reagent from Outer Horutoto Ruins. Confirm An Errand! The Professor's Price is active before farming Cardians.
