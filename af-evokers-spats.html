The second Summoner artifact piece. Koru-Moru's old classmates have had their memories tampered with, and the trail ends at a Protocrystal in Fei'Yin.

Walkthrough

  1. As Summoner, examine the House of the Hero in Windurst Walls (G-4) to get Carbuncle's Tear. If you just finished The Puppet Master (or any Carbuncle quest), zone first.
  2. Talk to Koru-Moru in Windurst Walls (E-7). He asks for 4 Astragalos — trade them to him if you already have them.
  3. Talk to Fuepepe at the Aurastery, Windurst Waters (L-6).
  4. Talk to Furakku-Norakku at the Optistery, Windurst Waters (G-8).
  5. Talk to Shantotto at her home in Windurst Walls (K-7) until she mentions Sunny-Pabonny.
  6. Talk to Gulmama in Northern San d'Oria (E-7) — the same Tarutaru who gives the Tuning Fork of Ice. He gives you the Ice Pendulum.
  7. Go to the Cloister of Frost in Fei'Yin (I-5). Fei'Yin Home Point #2 is right outside it.
  8. Buff outside, summon your Trusts, then trade the Ice Pendulum to the Ice Protocrystal and enter after the cutscene.
  9. Kill the six Dryads.
  10. Return to Koru-Moru in Windurst Walls (E-7) for the Evoker's Spats. If you skipped Fuepepe or Furakku-Norakku, talk to them first or he will not hand it over.
  11. The Ice Pendulum can be dropped once the fight is done.

The Fight

  • Six Dryads, Sapling type. They attack as soon as you walk up to them at the top of the ramp.
  • Buffs cast outside carry in, except Protect, Shell, Regen and Refresh.
  • A BLM or /BLM can Sleepga them with Elemental Seal; otherwise pull them down the ramp and take them one at a time.
  • You do not have to be Summoner for the fight — only to start the quest.
  • The exit drops you on the first floor around (K-6); without a warp you need Sneak and Invisible to reach Home Point #1 at (K-8).

EmberXI changes

  • Trusts can be summoned inside the battlefield.
  • No Fame requirement anywhere in the quest.
