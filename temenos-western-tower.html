Temenos - Western Tower

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


  • Temenos - Western Tower is a subsection of the Temenos zone in the Limbus region.
  • It is reached via the Teleport-Holla crag entrance to Al'Taieu.
  • Monsters on the first six floors drop an average of two chests per floor; you can open them at any time.
    • Over the six floors, on average there will be four Time (15 minutes), four Restore and four Items chests; there can be more or less.
    • Luck plays a very important role in this Limbus area; depending on Time chest drops you can finish with 45 minutes left or run out of time halfway.
    • Chest drops on these six floors seem to be affected by moon phase and Treasure Hunter.
    • The seventh floor just has the final Items chest.
  • A party of 3 strong DD/NIN, 1 BRD, 1 RDM, 1 BLM can complete this area pretty consistently.
    • Use 2-hours at will to speed up killing as much as possible; there are no bosses, and there will be plenty of Restore chests.
  • The gate to the next level opens with the first kill.
  • Typical Ancient Beastcoin yield: 55
  • Reward for completion: Emerald Chip

First Floor

  • Enemies: Enhanced Tiger x9 (Tigers)
    • ~5/9 drop an Ancient Beastcoin.
    • Linking (sight & sound)
    • Barparalyzra and Barblizzara for Roar
  • Items chest has 5-7 Ancient Beastcoins and 1 AF+1 item (BRD, RDM, SAM, SMN, WAR, ?)


Second Floor

  • Enemies: Enhanced Mandragora x9 (Mandragora)
    • ~4/9 drop an Ancient Beastcoin.
    • Linking (sound)
    • Mages stand well back and/or use Poison Potions to avoid Dream Flower.
  • Items chest has 5-6 Ancient Beastcoins and 1-2 AF+1 items (BRD, RDM, DRK, SAM, BST, WHM, ?)
  • Mandies in the north section wander around randomly and can be single pulled.
  • Mandies by the exit in the southeast march back and forth all together and can be easily avoided.


Third Floor

  • Enemies: Enhanced Beetle x6 (Beetles)
    • Linking (sight)
    • ~4/6 drop an Ancient Beastcoin.
    • Around 9000 HP
  • Items chest has 4-7 Ancient Beastcoins and 1-2 AF+1 items (BRD, SAM, RDM, WAR, BST, WHM, SMN, NIN, DRK, ?)


Fourth Floor

  • Enemies: Enhanced Lizard x9 (Lizards)
    • Linking (sound)
    • ~7/9 drop an Ancient Beastcoin.
    • Barfira for Fireball
  • Items chest has 4-6 Ancient Beastcoins and 1-2 AF+1 items (SAM, BST, WAR, RDM, MNK, DRK, SMN, NIN, ?)


Fifth Floor

  • Enemies: Enhanced Slime x6 (Slimes)
    • Linking (sound)
    • ~2/6 drop an Ancient Beastcoin.
    • Barwatera might help with Fluid Spread
    • Items chest has 6-8 Ancient Beastcoins and 1-2 AF+1 items (WAR, BST, WHM, SMN, MNK, RDM, BRD, NIN. ?)
  • A small, melee-heavy group may want to kill the first slime and then skip to the next level.
  • Be warned, if you train the slimes, they will not move if you die.


Sixth Floor

  • Enemies: Enhanced Pugil x6 (Pugils)
    • Linking (sound), can be Carby pulled.
    • ~3/6 drop an Ancient Beastcoin.
    • Barwatera for Water Ball
  • Items chest has 6-7 Ancient Beastcoins and 1-2 AF+1 item (SMN, WAR, WHM, BRD, NIN, MNK, RDM, DRK, ?)


Seventh Floor

  • Enemies: Enhanced Vulture x6 (Birds)
    • When pulled all 6 link, unless Carby pulled.
    • ~4/6 drop an Ancient Beastcoin.
    • Defeat them all to spawn the final chest, with the Emerald Chip sometimes Metal Chip, 6-7 Ancient Beastcoins and 1 AF+1 item (BRD, SAM, WAR, SMN, RDM, ?)
