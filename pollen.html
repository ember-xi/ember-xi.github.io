Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Restores HP.

Description

Spell cost: 8 MP
Ecosystem: Vermin
Type: Magical (Light)
Blue Magic Points: 1
Stat Bonus: CHR+1, HP+5
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 1

Timers

Casting Time: 2 seconds
Recast Time: 5 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Pollen as part of a combo to create Resist Sleep.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Resist Sleep combo.

  • Wild Carrot
  • Magic Fruit
  • Yawn
  • Exuviation

Notes

  • Self-target only.
  • Can be used with Divine Seal to cure for more than the typical amount.
  • Amount healed increases with MND.
  • Seems to cap at 35 HP healed.
  • Affected by Light Staff/Apollo's Staff.
