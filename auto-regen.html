Auto Regen

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Gradually restores HP.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • While active, this trait restores 1 point of HP per tick.
    • Gained by equipping the Blue Mage spells Sheep Song and Healing Breeze.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

None

Auto Regen

  • Obtained: White Mage Level 25
  • Obtained: Blue Mage Level 16
