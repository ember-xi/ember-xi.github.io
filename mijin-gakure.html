Mijin Gakure

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Sacrifices user's life to deal damage to an enemy.
  • Obtained: Ninja Level 1
  • Recast Time: 2:00:00
  • Duration: Instant

Notes/Description

  • Mijin Gakure is a Ninja's two hour special.
  • The damage dealt by this ability is based on user's current HP.
  • The player will fall in battle when this ability is used, however no experience points are lost nor any gained unless raised before the monster's death.
  • Commonly used to easily return to one's Home Point.
  • Correct use in EXP party is debatable, greater HP will result in a far more damaging attack, however you will be unable to aid in the fight after use.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Mijin Gakure" <t>
  • Since Mijin Gakure is a two hour special, some people prefer not to make a macro for it to prevent unintentional activation.

Additional Information

  • Monsters that use the same ability do not fall in battle and will continue to fight until defeated normally.
  • Damage from Mijin Gakure is reduced by -Magic Damage Taken gear and Shell, but not by Magic Defense Bonus.
