Melee Attire Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

An armor set from the Dynamis zones, which the player may be rewarded with for defeating the monsters within. The body and leg pieces are a deep maroon color with black trim, while the gloves, shoulders, waist, boots and crown bear complex designs in shades of black and gray. Despite sharing the same basic design as the Temple set, the color change makes confusing the two very difficult.

The set provides a well rounded boost to the Mnk's combat abilities, along with a significant boost to the Mnk's already impressive HP pool and some enhancements to the Mnk abilities Counterstance, Chakra, Subtle Blow, and Kick Attack traits. Combat skills are not neglected either, with an increase in Guarding Skill. To top it off, recovery is aided with an increase to HP recovered while resting.
