Mission 6-2 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 6-1 Leaute's Last Wishes.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission from a Gate Guard and talk to Perfaumand.
  2. Enter King Ranperre's Tomb, go to the lower floor and examine the Tombstone. Three NMs appear: Corrupted Soffeil / Ulbrig / Yorgos.
  3. Kill them and examine the Tombstone again for the Ancient San d'Orian Book.
  4. Return to a Gate Guard for Rank 7.
Mechanics: Three level ~55 NMs at once: Sleepga from Shantotto, then kill them one by one. The lower floor has undead at night.
