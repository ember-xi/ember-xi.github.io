Cosmo-Cleanse

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description: A somewhat doubtful alchemy
 product of the Near East.

Used In Quest(s): Limbus: Temenos, Apollyon

Obtained: Sagheera in Port Jeuno

Notes:

Cosmo-Cleanse is a Key Item available every 72 Earth hours for 30,000 gil from Sagheera, in Port Jeuno.

A Cosmo-Cleanse is required to enter Limbus, along with either a Red Card, Black Card, or White Card depending on which area of Temenos or Apollyon you are entering. The Cosmo-Cleanse and appropriate card are expended when you enter the actual battlegrounds from the lobby of your chosen area.

Each participant in Limbus must possess his/her own set of key items to be allowed entry.
