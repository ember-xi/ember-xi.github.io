Divine Veil

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: When used after Divine Seal, an ailment-removing spell's effect area will increase from single target to small radius.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • This trait does not affect spells' targetability.
    • This trait does not work with the spell Raise.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • None

Divine Veil

  • Obtained: White Mage Level 50
