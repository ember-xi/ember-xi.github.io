Statistics

Wyrmal Abjuration: Head
The inscription reads: "In accordance with the ancient wyrmal pact, may the bane be lifted from thy forbidden artifact." This prayer is necessary in purifying a cursed mask.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Other Uses

Guild Points Value: None

Used in Quests:

Abjuration: Crimson Mask
Abjuration: Blood Mask


NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
