Statistics

Page from Balrahn's Reflections
A page taken from the autobiography
of legendary hero, Balrahn. Contains
invaluable information on the arts of
war and governing.

Lv. 60 All Jobs

Stackable: Not Stackable

Other Uses

Used in Quests: None

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

Experience Scroll: Grants 200-500 EXP

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Einherjar

  • Grimgerde's Chamber
  • Rossweisse's Chamber

Notes:

  • The inventory name for this item is "Hero's Reflections", however it appears in the log and on the image titled as "Page from Balrahn's Reflections".
