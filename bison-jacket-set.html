Bison Jacket Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

An eye-catching mixture of earthen colors and rustic tweed patterns, this armor set invokes images of certain old Native-American cultures. Its shamanistic look is further enhanced by the presence of bold polygonal shapes woven up the legs and beaded, feathered accents adorning the headdress. The high-quality version of this set is the Brave's Jacket Set.

Armour Set Performance

Though beautiful to wear in town, most of this set is not very useful in a full-time sense. Most of it consists of equipment you would swap in for Reward or Tame, then swap out again afterwards. The body is a good and standard body piece to last a BST until obtaining Kirin's Osode, but apart from that, the other gear is optional, or for looks.
