Crab Familiar

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Familiar summoned by using the Beastmaster's Call Beast ability.


Familiar Jug Level Available Pet Level Cap Ready abilities
Crab Familiar (Crab) Fish Broth 23 55
  • Metallic Body - Crab gains a Stoneskin effect (1 Charge)
  • Bubble Shower - Crab deals AOE water damage and STR down (1 Charge)
  • Bubble Curtain - Crab gains a shell effect (3 Charges)
  • Scissor Guard - Crab gains Defense Up effect (2 Charges)
  • Big Scissors - Single-hit TP attack (1 Charge)
