Promotion: Sergeant

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Start NPC: Naja Salaheem - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-10) upstairs
Requirements:
 Acquire 25 Mercenary Rank points as Corporal.
Items Needed: Sutlac
Title: Sergeant
Repeatable: No
Reward: S Wildcat Badge

Previous Quest: Promotion: Corporal
Next Quest: Promotion: Sergeant Major

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Naja.
  • When given three options of where to start searching, choose the top option, "Balrahn Way".
  • Go to central area of Balrahn Way in Whitegate for a cutscene; again, choose the top option, Ask where he was. You will be directed to Nashmau.
  • Purchase a Sutlac from Yafaaf (J-12) in the Shararat Teahouse and then ride the boat to Nashmau.
  • Upon arriving in Nashmau, exit the dock area for a cutscene with Totoroon. You can also use the Runic Portal to Caedarva Mire, run to Nashmau, and head to H-9 for the cutscene. If you choose one of the bad answers in the event you have to wait for the next Vana'diel day to begin before you can restart the event.
  • Trade Totoroon the Sutlac and he will direct you to Caedarva Mire.
  • Take the east exit from Nashmau and investigate the ??? on the east coast of the pond at I-10 in Caedarva Mire for a cutscene.
  • Return to Naja for your rank.

Game Description

Client: Naja Salaheem (Salaheem's Sentinels, Aht Urhgan Whitegate)
Summary:

The perfumier Liliroon has come to Salaheem's Sentinels in search of help, believing it to be a famous detective agency. President Naja wants you to help collect her lucrative reward.
