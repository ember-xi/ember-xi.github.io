Zenith Pumps

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-73 feet equipment for WHM, BLM, RDM, BRD and SMN. DEF 20, INT +2, CHR +2; converts 50 HP to MP.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Result
  3. Sources

How to obtain

  1. Obtain Aquarian Abjuration: Feet; original sources include King Behemoth or Fafnir.
  2. Obtain Cursed Pumps through crafting or the Auction House.
  3. Trade the abjuration and cursed item together to Alphollon C Meriard inside the cathedral in Northern San d'Oria.

Result

The uncursed armor is Rare/Exclusive. Use the linked cursed-item page for its crafting recipe.
