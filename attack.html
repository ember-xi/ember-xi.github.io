Attack

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

EDEN USES 4 STR = 3 ATTK FOR 2H

Stat that is compared to the target's Defense to determine the minimum and maximum amount of damage for melee attacks. The formula of your Attack seems to be like the ranged attack formula, keeping in mind that 2 STR equals 1 Attack for 1 Handed Weapons and 1 STR equals 1 Attack on 2 Handed Weapons.

Formula:

1 Handed Attack = {( 8 + Combat Skill + STR/2 + (+attack from gear) + (+attack trait/ability/minuet)) * berserk effect (if available)} * (1+ percentage increase from food )
2 Handed Attack = {( 8 + Combat Skill + STR + (+attack from gear) + (+attack trait/ability/minuet)) * berserk effect (if available)} * (1+ percentage increase from food )
