Before you begin

  • Her Memories is the current Wings of the Goddess mission.
  • The San d'Oria past-allegiance branch of Her Memories.

Starting point

Mainchelite · Southern San d'Oria [S] · I-9

Era note: September 2010 WotG memory chapter, released after the original level-75 cap.

Walkthrough

  1. While allied with the Iron Rams, enter Southern San d'Oria [S] from East Ronfaure [S] for the memory scene. Speak with Mainchelite at (I-9).
  2. Walk back into East Ronfaure [S] for another scene.
  3. Examine the three Steamed Rams ??? locations: (H-8), the dirt hollow northeast of the fort; northwest (I-8), beneath the waterfall; and northwest (J-7), beside the tree.
  4. Return to Mainchelite for the fragment.

Completion and reward

Large memory fragment IV.
