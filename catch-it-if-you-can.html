Catch It If You Can!

  Requirements:
   Muted, Baned, or Plagued.
  Items Needed:   None.

  Repeatable:   Yes.

  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   None

Walkthrough

  • Get a rare or remarkable status ailment: Mute, Bane, or Plague. These are added effect from hits from specific mobs. The normal status ailments Silence, Curse, or Diseased will not work.
  • Mute is given by Eight of Cups in the Outer Horutoto Ruins - Dahlia Tower (F-11) in West Sarutabaruta.
  • Eight of Cups inflicts Mute through an Additional Effect by it's weapon. This will have a difficult time inflicting a high level player.
  • Bane is given by Lost Soul mages in the Eldieme Necropolis.
  • Plague is given by Bloodsuckers in the Toraimarai Canal.
  • Buffalo in Uleguerand Range can also give you this status effect, however its time effecting the player is limited (see talk page).
  • Simply speak with Ohruru when you're afflicted with any of the status ailments.
  • She will give you 1,000gil for Mute, 1,200gil for Bane, or 1,500gil for Plague

Game Description

Client: Ohruru (Orastery, Port Windurst)
Summary:

If you ever receive a rare or remarkable status ailment, rush to show it to Ohruru.
