Evoker's Horn

Evoker's Horn

StartHouse of the Hero — Windurst Walls (G-4), as Summoner main job
RequirementClass Reunion completed · Summoner 50+
Key ItemsLightning Pendulum · Daze-breaker Charm · Wind Pendulum
BattlefieldsCloister of Storms — The Boyahda Tree · Cloister of Gales — Cape Teriggan (F-5)

RewardEvoker's Horn
TitleParagon of Summoner Excellence

The third artifact quest, and the longest: two battlefields, two Pendulums and four trips back to Koru-Moru.

Walkthrough

  1. As Summoner main job, examine the House of the Hero in Windurst Walls (G-4) to hear Carbuncle's cry for help. Zone first if you just finished another Carbuncle quest.
  2. Talk to Koru-Moru in Windurst Walls (E-7). The Rhinostery researcher he mentions is Ripapa.
  3. Talk to Ripapa in Mhaura (I-9), on the upper level just east of the Porter Moogle, for the Lightning Pendulum.
  4. Go to the Cloister of Storms in The Boyahda Tree. Boyahda Tree Home Point #1 is right outside it. Buff outside, summon your Trusts, trade the Lightning Pendulum to the Lightning Protocrystal and enter.
  5. Kill the Lightning Gremlin and the Thunder Gremlin.
  6. Back to Koru-Moru — he gives you the Daze-breaker Charm.
  7. Talk to Agado-Pugado in Rabao (G-9) for the Wind Pendulum.
  8. Go to the Cloister of Gales: Western Altepa Desert → Kuftal Tunnel (enter at H-4, cross to the north exit) → Cape Teriggan, the Cloister is at (F-5). Cape Teriggan Home Point #1 is outside it.
  9. Trade the Wind Pendulum to the Wind Protocrystal and beat Ogmios.
  10. Return to Koru-Moru in Windurst Walls (E-7) for the cutscene and the Evoker's Horn.
  11. Both Pendulums can be dropped once the fights are done.

The Fight

  • Gremlins: the Lightning Gremlin is a Warrior whose hits drain TP; the Thunder Gremlin is a Red Mage whose hits drain MP. Both resist Sleep and Repose. They hit hard and drain 1–40 per hit, and Stoneskin absorbs the damage but not the drain — a shadow tank is better. Kill the Thunder Gremlin first so your avatar keeps its MP.
  • Ogmios: a Manticore that spams Silencega and Dispelga — bring Echo Drops. Keep mages back, away from his AoE TP moves (Riddle in particular).
  • Buffs cast outside do not wear off when you enter either battlefield.
  • If you lose a fight you need a new Pendulum from the same NPC to try again.

EmberXI changes

  • Trusts can be summoned inside both battlefields.
  • No Fame requirement anywhere in the quest.
  • Note: on retail the Lightning Pendulum can be blocked while Trial by Lightning is active. If Ripapa will not hand it over, finish Ramuh's trial first.
