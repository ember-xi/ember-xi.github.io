Armored Chariot

From Zenith XI Wiki

The boss on floor 7 of the original Arrapago Remnants.

Contents
  1. Reach and defeat
  2. Armor drops
  3. Sources

Reach and defeat

  1. Enter the original Arrapago Remnants Salvage route and follow the floor guide to the seventh floor. Allocate cells so your tank and healer regain their essential equipment and abilities.
  2. Prepare for Mortal Revolution: area damage with Stun. Keep Utsusemi shadows available where possible and keep support out of unnecessary area attacks.
  3. Defeat the boss and collect its armor components. Use An Imperial Heist to track the required original Salvage boss clears.

Armor drops

Bodb's Pigaches, Ea's Doublet, Ea's Tiara, Freyr's Trousers, Phobos's Sabatons, Tsukikazu Gote. These are level-25 components for the original Salvage armor upgrades; they are not the finished level-75 pieces.
