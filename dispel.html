Dispel

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Dispel refers to a spell or ability that removes one or more beneficial status effects from a target. There numerous ways an effect can be dispelled, be it a spell, blood pact, job ability, or monster special attack.

The following jobs are able to dispel in one form or another:

Job Level Type Name
Red Mage 32 Spell Dispel
Bard 33 Song Magic Finale
Summoner 32 Fenrir Blood Pact Lunar Roar
Blue Mage 38/46 Spell Blank Gaze/Geist Wall
Corsair 40 Job Ability Dark Shot
Thief 75 Job Trait Aura Steal
