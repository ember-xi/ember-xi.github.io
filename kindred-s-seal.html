Statistics

Kindred's Seal
A rare seal believed to be used
by the Kindred as a checkpoint pass.
It is used by pressing its surface onto
a piece of clay.

Stackable: 99

Other Uses

Used in Quests: Riding on the Clouds

Used for: KSNMs

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Dropped From

  • There is a chance a Kindred's Seal will drop from any mob that is level 50 or higher, and still gives you experience points (i.e. Easy Prey or higher), usually ~5-10% chance of one dropping.
