Zone Map Coordinates

From Zenith XI Wiki

Use the zone map to find a quest contact or route.

Read a map location

  1. Open the named zone page and its map gallery.
  2. Find the lettered column and numbered row. For example, G-7 means column G, row 7.
  3. Check the map number in multi-floor areas. The same grid square can refer to different floors.
  4. Follow the marked entrance or passage to the correct floor. A roaming monster can move within or beyond its listed spawn square.
