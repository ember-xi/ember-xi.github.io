Requirements

Bring 10,000 gil. The reviewed NPC script has no level, fame or mission prerequisite. This unlisted side quest does not appear in the quest log.

Walkthrough

  1. Travel to the north section of Windurst Waters.
  2. Go to the roof of the Optistery at (F-8). Churano-Shurano stands by the telescope.
  3. Speak to Churano-Shurano and accept the offer to buy the astrolabe. Complete the dialogue with at least 10,000 gil. You do not trade an inventory item.
  4. Check Permanent Key Items for Magicked Astrolabe. The purchase deducts 10,000 gil.
  5. In The Eldieme Necropolis, examine a closed intersection gate to open it yourself. Pass through promptly; the reviewed script opens it for eight seconds.

How it helps

Useful for solo travel through Eldieme during In Defiant Challenge and other quests or missions that use its gated rooms. It does not award Ancient Papyrus, complete a mission or bypass other quest requirements.

If you cannot buy it

Check that you have 10,000 gil and do not already own the key item. If you declined the first offer, speak to the NPC again. The key item is permanent and does not occupy a normal inventory slot.
