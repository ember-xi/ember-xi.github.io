Notes:

  • Their attacks have "Additional Effect: Disease."
  • Chigoes spawn whenever a Marid uses a TP skill. They will aggro anyone near to both Sight and Sound.
  • Chigoes also spawn from Aydeewa Diremites in Aydeewa Subterrane.
  • Chigoes also spawn from Wild Karakul and Mosshorn in Caedarva Mire.
  • Additionally in Caedarva Mire Chigoes are only visible as part of the environment (no name markers) until someone walks past. Use Sneak & Invisible to prevent Chigoe aggro in Caedarva Mire. Chigoes may be found around any Wild Karakul and also in the tunnels at the following locations of Caedarva Mire:
    • Northwest corner of (H-8) inside the tunnel.
    • Western exit of the tunnel at (J-7).
  • A Critical Hit or damage dealing job abilities such as Jump, Shield Bash, Weapon Bash, or any AoE Weapon Skills will cause them to instantly flee. You will not receive Experience Points from them unless they are defeated by depleting their HP.
