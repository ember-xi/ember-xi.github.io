Walkthrough

  • Talk to Vola at the Tenshodo HQ beneath Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno, then go through the Door: "Neptune's Spire" to get a cut-scene with Atori-Tutori who will request a Wyrm Beard.
  • Defeat the HNMs Fafnir or Nidhogg in Dragon's Aery until a Wyrm Beard is obtained.
  • This item is Rare/Ex. It is an uncommon drop from Fafnir, and a 100% drop from Nidhogg.
  • Alternatively this uncommon drop can be obtained from the KS99 BCNM Early Bird Catches the Wyrm.
  • Trade the Wyrm Beard to the Door: "Neptune's Spire".
  • Atori-Tutori will request a second item, but will not mention its name. He asks you to discover what it is.
  • Go to Bastok Markets and speak with Degenhard at (I-10). He will tell you that the item is a Behemoth Tongue.
  • This item is also Rare/Ex. It is an uncommon drop from Behemoth or a 100% drop from King Behemoth in Behemoth's Dominion. Both are HNMs.
  • Alternatively this uncommon drop can be obtained from the KS99 BCNM Horns of War.
  • Trade the Behemoth Tongue to the Door: "Neptune's Spire".
  • Atori-Tutori will request a third item. Again, he won't mention its name, and will ask you to find out.
  • Go to Ru'Lude Gardens and speak with Maat at (H-5). He will tell you that the item is an Adamantoise Egg.
  • This item is likewise Rare/Ex. It is a an uncommon drop from Adamantoise or a 100% drop from Aspidochelone in the Valley of Sorrows. Both are HNMs.
  • Alternatively this uncommon drop can be obtained from the KS99 BCNM The Hills Are Alive.
  • Trade the Adamantoise Egg to the Door: "Neptune's Spire".
  • Finally, trade a Brown Belt to the door to complete the quest and receive your reward. (You do not receive the Brown Belt back).

Game Description

Client: Atori-Tutori (Tenshodo, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

If you want information on the black belt, you need to first bring back what Atori-Tutori wants.
