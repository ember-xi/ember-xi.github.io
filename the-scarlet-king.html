Notes

  • He has all the normal Manticore specials including:
  • Greater Sandstorm: A hate-erasing AoE, so watch out for this and have the mages hold off on casting until the tanks can re-establish hate.
  • Unlimited Blood Weapon which constantly gives him HP.
  • He's susceptible to Paralyze, Gravity, and Bind.
  • He swings very quickly and has Triple Attack that goes off often.
  • Blood Weapon seems to activate under 50% HP.
