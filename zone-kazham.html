Description

A fishing village founded some four hundred years ago on the northern tip of Elshimo Island. Mithra form nearly half of Kazham's population and support the village by hunting and fishing. The village is self-governed, and all matters are judged by councils headed by Chieftainness Jakoh Wahcondalo.
