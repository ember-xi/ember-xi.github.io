Electrum Ingot

From Zenith XI Wiki

Goldsmithing material for Scholar's Mortarboard +1.

Buy from Auction House → Materials → Goldsmithing. Standard synthesis: Goldsmithing42, Fire Crystal, Silver Ore ×1 and Gold Ore ×3; yield one ingot.

Use one in Scholar's Mortarboard +1. This is the ordinary recipe; Horizon's custom Terminal Alloy recipe is not assumed to exist on Zenith.
