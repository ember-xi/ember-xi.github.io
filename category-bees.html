Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Bees are common flying monsters found throughout Vana'diel.

General Information

  • System: Vermin
  • Weaknesses: Piercing, Ice
  • Immune:
  • Detection: Sight
  • Tracking: Scent

Special Attacks

Special Abilities Original and Zilart Areas Promathia Areas Aht Urhgan Areas
Final Sting - Single target, Deals damage equal to its remaining HP, but kills the bee
Pollen - A quick self-heal, healing a portion of the bee's HP (high level bees can cure as much as 1000HP+ with this ability)
Sharp Sting - Deals a moderate amount of damage quickly
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
