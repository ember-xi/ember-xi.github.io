Notes

The Pugils in this BCNM are equipped with a ranged bubble attack (that they will use like a regular attack). This attack causes knock-back and makes the Pugils hard to kill with a traditional party of a Tank, a Healer, and a Damage Dealer. This attack is not affected by Perfect Dodge.
