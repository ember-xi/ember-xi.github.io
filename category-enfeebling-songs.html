Enfeebling Songs

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

One of the two classifications of Bard Songs. These are used against enemies in order to debilitate them and make them easier to defeat. Almost universally single-target spells, with Horde Lullaby being the sole exception.
Just as one bard can only maintain two songs on one person; only two enfeebling songs can be maintained on one monster. Verification Needed
