The Rescue Drill (San d'Oria 2-1)

San d'Oria Mission 2-1
MissionThe Rescue Drill
TypeQuest
Start NPCGate Guard (Endracion or Ambrotien in Southern San d'Oria; Grilau in Northern San d'Oria)
LocationLa Theine Plateau → Ordelle's Caves
RequirementsRank 2
Items NeededNone
Rewards
300 Rank points

Mission 2-1 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 1-3 Save the Children.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission from a Gate Guard.
  2. Go to La Theine Plateau and talk to the Royal Knights spread across the zone (Augevinne, Deaufrain, Galaihaurat, Laurisse and others). Each one sends you to the next.
  3. The last knight, Yaucevouchat, gives you the Rescue Training Certificate.
  4. (Optional) Ruillont inside Ordelle's Caves has an extra cutscene.
  5. Return to a Gate Guard and hand in the certificate.
Mechanics: No forced fight. Monsters on the Plateau are level 6-15 if they aggro.
