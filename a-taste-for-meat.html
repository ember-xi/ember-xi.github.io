A Taste For Meat

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Antreneau - Port San d'Oria (G-7)
  Requirements:
   San d'Oria Reputation 1
  Items Needed:   Hare Meat x5
  Title:   Rabbiter
  Repeatable:   No
  Reward:   150g
  Grilled Hare

  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   The Dismayed Customer

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Antreneau, he complains that he's had naught to eat but beans lately, and they just do not satisfy. The cook, Thierride, wishes he could make him some meat, but is fresh out.
  • Speak with Thierride and he will ask you to get him 5 slices of Hare Meat.
  • Obtain 5 slices of Hare Meat and trade them to Thierride to complete the quest.
  • Talk to Antreneau again to get a sample slice of Grilled Hare.
Note
It's a good idea to do this along with The Merchant's Bidding as Wild Rabbit will drop both Rabbit Hide and Hare Meat.

Game Description

Client: Thierride (Rusty Anchor Pub, Port San d'Oria)
Summary:

Fetch five slices of hare meat.
