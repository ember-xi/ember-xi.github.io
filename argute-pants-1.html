Argute Pants -1

From Zenith XI Wiki

Damaged Scholar relic armor; cannot be equipped.

Farm Nightmare enemies in Dynamis - Tavnazia. Use this damaged piece together with Argute Pants, Ether Leather ×1 and 1 Byne Bill ×28 to make Argute Pants +1 at Sagheera.

See Scholar Relic Upgrades for the hand-in order and all five recipes.
