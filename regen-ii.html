Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Gradually restores target's HP.

Description

Spell cost: 36 MP
Spell element: Light
Magic skill: Enhancing Magic
Jobs:

  • White Mage lvl 44

Timers

Casting Time: 4.5 seconds
Recast Time: 16 seconds
Spell Duration: ~60sec

Notes

  • Restores 12HP/tick.
  • Approximately 240 HP total.
  • Cleric's Briault increases this effect to 14HP/tick for a maximum of 280 HP.
  • Merits can boost this effect further (+1 to +5)
  • Cleric's Briault + max merits = 19HP/tick for a maximum of 380 HP.
