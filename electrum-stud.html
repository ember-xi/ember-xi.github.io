Electrum Stud

From Zenith XI Wiki

Apollyon material used for every Scholar artifact+1 piece.

Obtain from Apollyon item chests. One documented route is NE Apollyon floor 1; it is a possible drop, not guaranteed. NW Apollyon and SE Apollyon also have Scholar material pools.

Each Scholar Artifact Upgrades recipe needs one Electrum Stud plus one Brilliantine. These exclusive materials are earned in Limbus rather than bought on the Auction House.
