Toraimarai Turmoil

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Ohbiru-Dohbiru - Windurst Waters South (J-9)
  Requirements:
   Windurst Reputation 6
  Items Needed:   Starmite Shell x3
  Title:   Certified Rhinostery Venturer
  Repeatable:   Yes.
  Reward:   Rhinostery Certificate

  4500 gil

     
  Previous Quest:   Blue Ribbon Blues
  Next Quest:   None.

Walkthrough

  • If you have just finished Blue Ribbon Blues you will have to zone out and back in to get the quest. Also if you're on Windurst Mission 7-2 (Awakening of the Gods) you may have to talk to Ohbiru-Dohbiru twice.
  • Upon accepting the quest, you will receive a Rhinostery Certificate which will provide access to Toraimarai Canal via Windurst Walls(If you've just completed the previous quest, Blue Ribbon Blues, you will need to zone before accepting this quest).
  • Talk to Ohbiru-Dohbiru. The Rhinostery wants you to kill Starmites in Toraimarai Canal. Collect all the Starmite Shells you can gather and bring them back to the Rhinostery.
  • There are 2 paths to the Starmite camp:
    • Starting from Priming Gate at H-3 of Windurst Walls, zone into Toraimarai Canal (Rhinostery Certificate is required for access):
      • At first intersection head east, follow path
      • Down stairs at I-8 & head east
      • Make 2 right turns at I-10
      • Up stairs at I-9
      • Head to G-9
    • Starting from J-7 tower in East Sarutabaruta, zone into Inner Horutoto Ruins:
      • Cracked wall at G-8
      • Magic Gate of Horutoto at E-10
      • Cracked Wall at D-10
      • Sealed Portal at H-9 (Portal Charm or WHM,BLM & RDM required for access)
      • Through false wall at H-8
      • Toraimarai Canal door at H-6 & zone into Toraimarai Canal
      • Cross bridge at H-6
      • Down stairs at H-7 & turn right at intersection
      • Up stairs at H-9
      • Cross bridge at I-8
      • Down stairs at J-9 & head east to 2nd map
      • Up stairs at F-8
      • Down stairs at H-10
      • Up stairs at I-9
      • Head to G-9
  • Ohbiru-Dohbiru will reward you with 4,500 gil for every 3 Starmite Shells that you bring him.

Game Description

Client: Ohbiru-Dohbiru (Rhinostery, Windurst Waters)
Summary: Become a certified Rhinostery adventurer in order to enter the Toraimarai Canal and cull the Starmites that are causing havoc there. Bring back the shells of three Starmites to receive your reward.
