General Information

  • System: Unclassified
  • Weaknesses: Elemental's weakness (i.e. Air Elementals are weak to Ice)
  • Resistance: Elementals are resistant to its own element, and the element it's strong against (i.e. Air Elementals are resistant to Wind and Earth). Only Air and Ice elementals resist silence.
  • High Resistance: Physical attacks. The only melee attacks that will do decent damage to them is Spirits Within or elemental-based weaponskill attacks that correspond to the weakness of the elemental.

Standard Elemental Spawning Behavior

  • Elementals will spawn around set positions on the map in an area when there is Weather in effect of the element matching the Elemental's type.
  • A Weather effect's length is not affected by killing elementals. Elementals may respawn if the weather effect continues.
  • If an Elemental is unclaimed when the weather effect has ended, it will despawn.
  • For elementals used as pets by Summoner mobs, Beastmen races are limited to certain elements:
  • Yagudo use only Wind, Earth, and Fire.
  • Demon use only Dark, Ice, and Thunder.
  • Tonberries use only Fire, Water and Light.
  • Lamiae use only Water, Ice, and Dark.

Special Attacks

Elementals do not have TP moves. They will cast spells based on their element and their spells will depend on their level. Dark Elementals cast Stun.
