Regional Area where only people with Chains of Promathia (CoP) can access after completing Promathia Mission 1-3.

This tiny string of islands off the west coast of Quon was once home to the proud nation of Tavnazia.

Before the Great War, this region was actually a peninsula connected with the mainland. However, a devastating explosion caused by a secret weapon of the beastmen resulted in the disintegration of a large portion of the surrounding landscape.

The explosion also sent the nation's capitol, a city built on the profits of its thriving trade business, into ruin.

The remnants of the beastmen army remain in control of the archipelago, and until now, it was thought that none of the former inhabitants had survived.

Outpost

The outpost of this region is located in Lufaise Meadows at E-8.
