Walkthrough

  • First speak to Ryoma in Norg for a cutscene and then head to Port Bastok. (The rest of the quest can be done on any job.)
  • Kagetora in Warehouse #2, just across from the Airship dock, will send you off to find Ensetsu,located at (I-5) in the first house east of the warehouse.
  • Ensetsu provides additional background and sends you to Eastern Altepa Desert to investigate.
  • Head to the border of H-5/H-6 in Eastern Altepa Desert, clear the area, and click the ??? when your party is set.
  • Two NM spiders named Tsuchigumo will spawn. The spiders are not sleepable.
  • After you kill the Tsuchigumo; re-examine the ???, you will receive a key item called a Trick Box.
  • Return to Norg and speak with Ryoma to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Ryoma (Quay, Norg)
Summary:

Norg has begun investigating an ancient scroll that was rediscovered as of late. Speak with Tenshodo member Kagetora in Bastok for more details.
