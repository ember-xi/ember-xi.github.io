Walkthrough

  • You must wait approximately 8 game days before Rycharde will give you this quest.
  • "Hey, long time no see. Remember the last order I got? That was from the governor herself, and she really liked the dish I made for her! She liked it so much, she said she wants me to prepare all her meals from now on! I don't want to disappointe her, so I need you to bring me exotic ingredients to practice my cooking skills! I heard they're pretty hard to get, but I'd like to start with a puffball. Whaddya say?"
  • Bring Rycharde a puffball.

Game Description

Client: Rycharde (Sailors' Stay, Mhaura)
Summary:

Rycharde wants you to get him an ingredient called a puffball.
