Zenith availability

The audited server base explicitly marks the Honor Under Fire battle in Everbloom Hollow as unimplemented. The scenes and follow-up handlers exist, but this quest cannot be completed normally through that base alone. Battle and post-battle steps below document the intended route for future implementation.

Before you begin

  • Complete B-07: Quelling the Storm.
  • Reach WotG 15: Crossroads of Time.

Starting point

Gentle Tiger · Bastok Markets [S] · H-6

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Gentle Tiger at H-6.
  2. In Vunkerl Inlet [S], examine the ??? just beyond the F-6 bridge, then the Beastman Ensign at J-7.
  3. Return to Gentle Tiger to receive the Flare Grenade key item.
  4. Examine the Beastman Ensign at J-7 again for a scene. In the complete reference route, examine it once more to enter Honor Under Fire in Everbloom Hollow.
  5. Defeat Arch Ahriman and the false allies it creates. When it transforms into Volker, Five Moons, or Nicolaus, defeat that replica and resume damaging the Ahriman when it returns.
  6. Be ready to recover enemy attention after a transformation. Keep healers clear of area attacks, prepare for sleep, and interrupt dangerous spells where possible.
  7. After winning, return to Gentle Tiger for the final scene and Elixir Tank.

Battle and progress notes

  • The reference fight lasts 30 minutes. The Ahriman and Nicolaus replica use strong magic; physical damage and a dedicated healer are useful at level 75.
  • A replacement Flare Grenade comes from Gentle Tiger after a failed attempt in the reference route.
  • Keep the Elixir Tank or an Elixir for The Truth Lies Hid.

Completion and reward

Intended: Elixir Tank.

What this opens

  • The Bastok route’s requirement for continuing WotG 15: Crossroads of Time
  • B-09: Beneath the Mask, after reaching Fate in Haze
