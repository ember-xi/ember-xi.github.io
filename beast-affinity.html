Beast Affinity

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Increases the level of pets summoned with Call Beast.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Additional merit points increase the level of pets summoned with Call Beast by 2. (Max. level: 75)
  • Note: it seems that this only applies to jug pets which are already capped. Uncapped jugs will continue to spawn 0-2 levels below the Beastmaster.

Beast Affinity

  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 75 Merit Group 2
