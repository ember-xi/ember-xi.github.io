Additional level-75 reference

Ancient Elvaan for "Frozen Ground", the Fauregandi region is a desolate, snow covered landscape, where the enormous Beaucedine Glacier encompasses much of the landmass. Few people have passed through the area since the Crystal War, when the Dark Kindred used the region to launch many of their attacks upon the neighboring Batallia Downs and East Ronfaure.

Outpost

The outpost of this region is located in Beaucedine Glacier at H-9.
