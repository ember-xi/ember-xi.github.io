Chamnaet Spring

From Zenith XI Wiki

Target used to fill the Cotton Pouch during Zephyr Fan acquisition.

Steps

  1. Get a Cotton Pouch from Zebada near the Uleguerand Range entrance.
  2. Climb to the western slope. Slide down toward the southern ledge above the bottom and enter its cave.
  3. Trade the pouch to Chamnaet Spring to receive Chamnaet Ice.
  4. Take the ice back to Zebada for Zephyr Fan.

The fan is the entry key item for the Bearclaw Pinnacle ENMs. Open your chosen battlefield guide for its separate entrance route.
