Description

This ferry is actually a cargo ship that carries adventurers across the Bastore Sea from Mhaura (on the continent of Mindartia) to Selbina (on the continent of Quon). The ferry runs three times a day. Though these ferry ships once relied on the ever changing winds to power their sails, the three-vessel fleet now moves with the speed of internal combustion engines, thanks to the technology of Bastok. The Selbina-Mhaura Ferry's main purpose is to transport goods, so the boats are not exactly equipped for moving passengers. However, the ferry features a small shop to provide the bare bones essentials for those who are making the journey.
