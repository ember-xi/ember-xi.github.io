Notes:

  • Fought in the BC for Promathia Mission 8-3.
  • When first aggroed, they all cast a weak spell.
  • The two on the north side (where you enter) are RDM and have 7000 HP.
  • These cast Dispelga, Silencega and Diaga II as well as normal RDM debuffs and Regen.
  • The two on the south side (where you can run away) are BLM and have 6250 HP.
  • These cast Bio III as well as nukes up to Thunder IV and Thundaga III.
  • They use all the normal Zdei special attacks, most of which are absorbed by Utsusemi.
  • Optic Induration hits for about 1500 damage!
  • Apparently there's a long charging animation for this attack, during which the Ix'Zdei will not use normal attacks, but rather gather energy. This process is not interrupted by Stun.
  • Static Filament hits for about 500.
  • This and Optic Induration are cone attacks, so don't stand behind the tank.
  • Decayed Filament hits for about 150 from BLM, 300 from RDM.
  • Reactor Overload hits for about 350.
  • Reactor Cool spikes from a BLM pot do 20 damage per hit; from an RDM pot, 30.
