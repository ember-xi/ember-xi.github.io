BCNM

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Officially called Orb Battles, Burning Circle Notorious Monster (BCNM) events are great ways to have fun and earn powerful items in the world of Vana'diel. These events are accessed by trading a specific BCNM orb to the entrance of a Burning Circle. These orbs can be obtained by trading beastmen seals or kindred seals to Shami (an NPC at H-8 in Port Jeuno).

Not all of these fights have been implemented yet. The BCNM Names in Blue are implemented. Names that are red are not implemented. Names in black have not had a page created yet.
