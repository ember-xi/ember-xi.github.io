Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

These are large doors found in Garlaige Citadel that need to be opened by 4 people. There are a total of 3 Banishing Gates found here, each one a bit more difficult to get through than the last.

Opening

In order to open the Gate, each of the four people need to stand on one of four switches that are found near the Gate. When a person gets on the switch, it will sink down a bit - that's how you know you're actually on it. Once everyone is on their switch a message will appear in your chat box: "The first/second/third Banishing Gate begins to open."

You have about 15 seconds to get through the open Gate before it closes. As long as you start to run towards the door as soon as you see the 'open' message, you should have no problem making it through. It will say "The first/second/thrid Banishing Gate begins to close" when its about to shut.
