Layton

From Zenith XI Wiki

Scholar magic vendor in The Eldieme Necropolis (S).

Find Layton near Erlene in The Eldieme Necropolis (S) (J-8). Buy the Storm and Helix scrolls here, then use each scroll when your Scholar meets its learning level.

Scholar Spell List shows the historical 75 progression. Klimaform is a quest reward from On Sabbatical, not part of the ordinary Storm/Helix purchase route.
