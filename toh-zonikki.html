Notes

The mithra manages Clamming - using a bucket and water to find items. She can sell and upgrade your Clamming kit up to 200 pz.

Beginning - Toh Zonikki: "Welcome to Purrrgonorgo, pardner! How'd ya like to try yer hand at some clammin'? It'll only cost ya a mere 500 gil!"

What is all this clamming? - Toh Zonikki: "What's a clammin'? What arrre you, one of them city folk? Where do you think clams come from, boy, some magical clam factory in the sky? If ya want a clam, you hafta dig it from the sand!
Now if ya want, I can lt ya borrow this clamming kit. Use it, and you'll be swimmin' in shellfish!
Once ya dig 'em up, jus' pop all the clams into the bucket that comes with the kit. That tin beauty'll hold up near 50 ponzes.
When you've had enough clammin' and you'rrre ready to thrrrow in the towel, jus' mosey on over here and I'll wrap yer clams up for the trip back to the mainland. So, whaddaya say?"

Ready to get clammin'? or I was born to clam - Toh Zonikki: "Spoken like a true clammer! Here's yer clamming kit.
Rememberrr, I'm loanin' ya this kit outta the kindness of my heart. Ya don't leavin' this here island untils you've given her bck to me, hear?"

Sells

  • Clamming kit (key item) 500 gil
