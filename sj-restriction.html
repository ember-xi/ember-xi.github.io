Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Description

SJ Restriction is a status effect that temporarily lowers your Subjob level to 0 until it is removed. Affected characters are treated as if they have no subjob at all, making Job Abilities and Magic pertaining to that Subjob completely unusable.

How to remove the effect

  • Locating the ??? at various locations.
  • Completing certain task(s) such as defeating certain NMs like those located in Dynamis - Tavnazia.

How the effect is inflicted

  • Entering Dreamworld Dynamis
  • Certain Quests
  • Fighting either Maat during Shattering Stars, Raubahn during The Beast Within, Shamarhaan during Achieving True Power or Qultada during Breaking the Bonds of Fate.
