Notes:

  • Spawned by trading a San d'Orian Carrot to a Rabbit Footprint in Uleguerand Range during the full moon.
  • The Rabbit Footprint is located at K-11 in the wooded area, and moves around every 5-10 seconds between various spots in that locale.
  • The Rabbit Footprint respawns 3 to 15 minutes after the NM is killed randomly.
  • Note that the Black Coney also spawns this way, but during the new moon.
  • Around 8500HP
  • Special Attacks: Wild Carrot (TP-based special attack that cures the NM for over 1000HP), goes off at 300% TP for most of the fight but goes off faster at 100% TP when the NM has 20% or less health.
  • Resistant to Paralyze.
  • Susceptible to Sleep and Stun, which can be used to stop the Wild Carrot ability.
  • Killable by: 2 characters at level 75, soloable by 75 BLM/RDM Sleep/Nuke technique.
  • Does not link with the Polar Hares around the spawn point.
