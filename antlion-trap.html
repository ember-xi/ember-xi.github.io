Statistics

Antlion Trap
Originally designed by the Antica, this
powerful trap, made from an antlion
jaw, is extremely effective in capturing
all types of antlions.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Other Uses

Guild Points Value: None

Used in Quests: None

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

Used to Spawn: Alastor Antlion
