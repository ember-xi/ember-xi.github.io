Pianissimo

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Limits area of effect of next song to a single target.
  • Obtained: Bard Level 45
  • Recast Time: 0:00:15
  • Duration: 1 Song or 0:01:00, whichever occurs first.

Notes

  • Increases song casting range to 20.0
  • If the target already has two songs active, Pianissimo will replace the song with the shortest duration remaining.
  • Has no effect on single target songs.
  • Does not work with any Enfeebling Songs. (Effect will not be lost)
    • Horde Lullaby cannot be used with Pianissimo in order to make it only land on one foe. It will still AoE.
  • Effect will remain until a player enhancing song is cast. (AoE)
