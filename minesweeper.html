  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   None

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Gerbaum; he complains about the monsters in Zeruhn Mines and asks you to clear them out and bring back Zeruhn Soot as proof.
  • Obtain three Zeruhn Soot and trade them to Gerbaum to complete the quest.
Note
This quest can be repeated, without zoning in between.

It takes around 15 stacks of soot to reach Fame Level 2 with Bastok

Game Description

Client: Gerbaum (Work Area, Bastok Mines)
Summary:

Rid the Zeruhn Mines of a few monsters to keep them off the miners' backs. Bring three pinches of Zeruhn soot to Gerbaum as proof of your success.
