Statistics

Turbo Animator
All Races
An improved version of the animator.
Transmits commands at a faster rate
than the standard model.
Lv. 40 PUP

Allows the use of maneuvers.

Hidden Effect

  • DEX +2

Other Uses

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Quest

The Wayward Automaton

Notes

Please note, the Animator and Turbo Animator do not affect the recast time of a maneuver. An animator is required to use maneuver commands. The recast time for a maneuver when the Puppetmaster has either the Animator or the Turbo Animator equipped remains ten seconds

This quest is easily soloed by a level 75. However, it is best to have a healing job near by, as a level 40 Puppetmaster will draw hate as soon as the Caedarva Toad is spawned
