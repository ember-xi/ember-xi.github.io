Leujaoam Assault Orders

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description
A document that authorizes you to take part in a Leujaoam Sanctum Assault mission.
Obtained from Quest/Mission
Obtained when you accept one of the Assault Missions for Leujaoam Sanctum:
  1. Leujaoam Cleansing
  2. Orichalcum Survey
  3. Escort Professor Clavauert
