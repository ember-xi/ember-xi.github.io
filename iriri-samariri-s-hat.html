Statistics

Iriri Samariri's hat
The perfectly form-fit hat of Poroggo
Iriri Samariri. Tailor-made to an
amphibious head, it cannot and will not
adorn the typical adventurer noggin.

Other Uses

Resale Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.
ZNM: Trade to Sanraku to obtain an Apple Green Seal.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
