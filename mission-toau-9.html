Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 8: A Mercenary Life.
  • One Imperial Silver Piece for the normal ruins entrance.

Starting point

Kamih Mapokhalam · Bhaflau Thickets · F-6

Walkthrough

  1. Leave Whitegate through Al Zahbi and travel into Bhaflau Thickets to Kamih Mapokhalam at F-6.
  2. Trade one Imperial Silver Piece for entry into Alzadaal Undersea Ruins.
  3. Inside, head east toward the Gilded Doors at H-8. Approach the doorway to trigger the scene with Ghatsad.
  4. Finish the scene to obtain the Astral Compass.

Battle and progress notes

  • Optional: Kamih also sells the Map of Alzadaal Ruins for three Imperial Mythril Pieces. To register Nyzul Isle afterward, take the north teleporter, then the west teleporter; use a Runic Portal in the central chamber to return to Whitegate.

Completion and reward

Astral Compass key item.

What this opens

  • ToAU 10: Astral Waves
