Ember Trials

Ember Trials
WhereDiorama Abdhaljs-Ghelsba (from Halvard)
FormatFive waves + a boss; death costs one wave, never the run
TiersEmbers 65 · Flames 70 · Ashes 75 · Depth of Eclipse 78
RewardsTreasure chest: AF2 piece (chance rises with each miss), 2–4 Alexandrite, EXP
TrustsAllowed — the first wave waits a few seconds so you can summon

A wave arena. Clear four full waves, an elite guard and the boss. Every monster is a real orc, demon or warden borrowed from the era zones, set to the tier's level.

Tiers

TierMin job levelWave levelsBossMonsters
Trial of Embers (65)6060–6566Orcs of Monastic Cavern
Trial of Flames (70)6565–7071Demons of Castle Zvahl
Trial of Ashes (75)7070–7576Wardens of Ru'Aun
Depth of Eclipse (78)75 + Ashes cleared78–8384Shades of Apollyon and the Zvahl court

Rules

  1. Waves enter one monster at a time (4 seconds apart). Anything left standing idle is pulled in a few seconds later.
  2. If you die, the wave resets and you go back one wave. Dying to the boss only restarts the boss.
  3. At the end a treasure chest appears: open it for the AF2 piece roll, Alexandrite and EXP.
  4. You leave beside Halvard.
Mechanics: AF2 pity: every chest without an AF2 piece adds +10% to the next roll for your job. Depth of Eclipse mobs are above the cap (78–84): bring full Trusts and Reraise.
