Notes

  • All have the Spider special attacks and Additional Effect: Poison. Poison hits for 50 HP/tic.
  • Anasi casts Paralyga, Poisonga II and Slowga.
  • The Son of Anansi spawn after Anansi is defeated. Spawns occur every few seconds so it takes a little while before all the Sons can be sleepga'd together. You'll know all Sons are out when Anansi's body disappears.
  • The Son of Anansi are susceptible to Sleep, Lullaby, and Charm.
  • Anasi is susceptible to bind, paralyze and gravity but gains some resistance to gravity over time.
