Notes:

  • Spawned by trading 3 Bomb Queen Ash and 1 Bomb Queen Core to the ??? at I-10.
  • The ??? will respawn 15 minutes after the Bomb Queen dies.
  • About 50,000 HP.
  • Summons 3 bomb NMs which explode and respawn every 10 seconds: Bomb Bastard, Bomb Prince, and Bomb Princess.
    • Bomb Prince and Bomb Princess have about 1000 HP. Two Fire III will usually kill them.
    • Bomb Bastard is rarely seen, and is reported to be much tougher than the other two.
  • Special Attacks: Draw In, Enfire, Burn, Flare, Firaga III, Fire IV, Blaze Spikes
  • The Bomb Queen and her summons are immune to Stun, but susceptible to all other enfeebles.
  • Barfira and Fire Carol strongly recommended for everyone in melee range since Firaga III cannot be stunned.
  • Item Drops: Bomb Queen Ring drops 100% of the time, Avengers about 20%.
  • Killable by: 16 characters level 72+, 9 characters level 75.
