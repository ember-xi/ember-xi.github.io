RoE Trust Walkthrough

From Zenith XI Wiki

Earn five permanent companions through the beginner RoE objectives, one Cipher at a time.

Before you begin

  1. Reach main-job level 5 and complete a starting-city Trust quest. Finish the return dialogue that awards the Trust permit.
  2. Open Quests → Records of Eminence → Objective List → Tutorial → Basics. Activate First Step Forward, then speak to Rolandienne, Isakoth or Fhelm Jobeizat to complete it.
  3. Keep a free inventory slot for each Cipher. Activate Call Forth an Alter Ego before summoning a Trust in an outdoor area such as West Ronfaure. An already learned starting companion, such as Excenmille, works.

The six objectives award five Ciphers. They are one-time objectives; acquiring these spells does not spend Sparks. The reviewed reward definitions give 100 Sparks and 300 EXP per objective for steps 1–5, and 100 Sparks and 500 EXP for step 6, before any applicable reward processing.

After receiving each Cipher

  1. Return to a Trust representative listed below. Trade the Cipher by itself and finish the dialogue to learn the spell permanently.
  2. Select the next named objective under Tutorial → Basics. Learning the spell is separate from completing its summoning objective.
  3. Go outside the city, target yourself and cast the named spell from Magic → Trust. If that companion is already present, dismiss it and summon it again after activating the objective. Make room in your party first.
  4. Check the completion message and collect the next Cipher. Repeat until Alter Ego: Joachim is complete.

A completed Trust permit from any starting city qualifies for Cipher exchange. The Sparks NPC and the Trust representative are different NPCs. San d’Oria’s first Trust walkthrough explains the permit step.

If a step does not complete

  • Check that the exact objective is active before you summon. A previous summon does not count for a newly activated objective.
  • Check Magic → Trust and your inventory: a Cipher in your bag still needs to be traded; a learned spell needs to be summoned for the named objective.
  • If the next objective is unavailable, finish its predecessor and check your completed objectives.
  • Free inventory space if an item reward is pending, then check the RoE objective again. Do not discard a Cipher to try to restart the chain.
  • Use a permitted outdoor area and an available party slot. Summoning rules still apply.

How this fits Zenith

  • The beginner chain is allowed by Zenith’s reviewed RoE policy. The source audit confirms that policy; it does not prove that every step has been completed on a fresh character.
  • Sparks Essentials sells a separate set: Qultada, King of Hearts, Cid, Gilgamesh and Ferreous Coffin. Those purchases are not rewards from this beginner chain.
  • A Healer’s Promise remains the Zenith quest for Apururu. Collecting Trust spells does not unlock extra party slots; Trust Journeys handles the fourth and fifth companions.
  • Retail’s Always Stand on 117 objective awards Koru-Moru and requires average item level 117. Objective 1049 is absent from Zenith’s allowed RoE list, so that retail acquisition route is unavailable in this level-75 profile.
