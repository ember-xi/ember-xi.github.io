—The Moonlit Path

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Gulmama in Northern San d'Oria (E-7). He gives you the Tuning fork of ice.
    • Under 65 he gives the mini fork instead — trade it back to him and he teleports you to the Cloister.
  • Talk to Gulmama again while you hold the fork. You are sent straight to the Cloister of Frost.
    • The Cloister is in Fei'Yin at (I-5). Fei'Yin Home Point #2 is outside it.
  • Summon your Trusts and your avatar, then examine the Ice Protocrystal and pick Trial by Ice.
  • Defeat Shiva Prime.
    • Ice damage heals her: no Blizzard spells, no ice weapon skills, no weapons with additional ice damage.
    • She uses Double Slap, Rush, Frost Armor and Blizzard IV, and at about 50% HP her Astral Flow pact Diamond Dust — heavy AoE ice damage.
    • Best day: Firesday. Avoid Iceday. Keep Barblizzara up.
    • Bring Ifrit: fire beats ice, and Flaming Crush hits hardest.
  • You receive the Whisper of Frost.
    • Going for Fenrir? Keep it and do not take a reward yet — see The Moonlit Path.
  • Return to Gulmama and choose your reward.
    • Repeatable once a day (after midnight JST).
    • At SMN 75 with Shiva already learned, winning teaches Heavenly Strike, her level 75 Blood Pact.

EmberXI changes

  • No Fame requirement.
  • Trusts can be summoned inside the battlefield; the Prime fight needs level 65+.
  • Talking to Gulmama while you hold the fork teleports you to the Cloister.
  • Repeatable once a day.
  • Under 65, summoners get a solo trial-size fight (level 20 cap, Carbuncle only).
