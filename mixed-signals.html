Walkthrough

  • After your NPC is level 56, (With Signal Pearl in your inventory) talk to Ratoto in Upper Jeuno (I-10).
  • Talk to Luto Mewrilah for another cut scene.
  • Talk to Raimbroy in Southern San d'Oria (E-8).
  • Talk to Luto Mewrilah.
  • Click the ??? in Qulun Dome. This is the same ??? as the Beadeaux Limit Break 3 quest. (Whence Blows the Wind)
  • Talk to Luto Mewrilah.

Game Description

Client: Luto Mewrilah (Artisan Bridge, Upper Jeuno)
Summary:

Your adventuring fellow is acting strangely. Talk to Luto to see what you can find out.

Food Rewards and Effects

  • The specific food reward you receive corresponds to the face-type of your NPC.
  • Possible foods are: Homemade Stew (Vit +1), Homemade Salad (MP +10), Homemade Steak (Str +1), Homemade Cheese (HP +10, Def +40), Homemade Risotto (Mnd +1), Homemade Bread (Agi +1), Homemade Herbal Tea (Chr +1), Homemade Rice Ball (Dex +1), Homemade Gelato (Int +1)
  • All foods are 30 minute duration.
