Skadi's Cuirie Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


A set of armor crafted from various incredibly rare and expensive items, as well as a total of 50 Imperial Gold Pieces. The Skadi Leather Armor set is wearable by Thiefs, Beastmasters, Rangers, and Corsairs only.

Number of Pieces: 5

Cost to store: Unknown. Storing may not be possible.
