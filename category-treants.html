A treant in Jugner Forest.General Information

  • System: Plantoids
  • Weaknesses: Dark, Fire
  • Immune:
  • Behavior: Detect on Sound, monsters of the Sapling family may link with it.

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Pinecone Bomb: Sleep and Damage
Leafstorm: 15' AOE Damage, absorbed by Utsusemi
Drill Branch: Single target damage and Blind
Entangle: Single target Bind, ignores Utsusemi
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
