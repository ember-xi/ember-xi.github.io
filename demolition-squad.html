Notes

  • All have the Gigas special attacks.
  • Nephiyl MoatFiller uses Charm.
  • Nephiyl Pinnacletosser has Eagle Eye Shot.
  • Nephiyl Rampartbreacher has Mighty Strikes.
  • Nephiyl Keepcollapser has Hundred Fists.
  • All are susceptible to Gravity and Bind, and all except Nephiyl Moatfiller are susceptible to Lullaby.
  • Generally very resistant to sleep. blm could only land sleepga with Elemental Seal
  • Plan on having a rdm kiter for the sleep immune bst.
  • Light Shot slept them easily
