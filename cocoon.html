Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Enhances defense.

Description

Spell cost: 10 MP
Ecosystem: Vermin
Type: Magical (Earth)
Blue Magic Points: 1
Stat Bonus: VIT+3
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 8

Timers

Casting Time: 1.75 seconds
Recast Time: 60 seconds
Duration: 90 seconds

Notes

  • Self-target only.
  • Increases defense by 50%.
  • Can overwrite AND be overwritten by most Defense Down effects, including Sonic Wave used by Dhalmel's, Frightful Roar used by Taurus, and acid bolts. Will not overwrite Fomor defense down.
