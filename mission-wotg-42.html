Before you begin

  • Complete Her Memories.
  • Carry Large memory fragments I-IV collected during Her Memories.

Starting point

Cavernous Maw · Batallia Downs [S]

Era note: Later WotG chapter, released after the level cap began rising above 75. Included for story continuity; this guide does not claim a tested level-75 balance.

Walkthrough

  1. Examine one of the six Jeuno-area maws (Batallia, Rolanberry, or Sauromugue, in either era) after collecting the four large fragments.
  2. Let the linked scenes and automatic transfers finish. In the pinned handler, you visit Grauberg during the scene, then return to a Jeuno-area maw; completion is recorded on that return.
  3. Travel to Grauberg [S] for Pillar of Hope after the mission log updates.

Battle and progress notes

  • The pinned core has an incomplete main-story battle at mission 14; later story handlers do not establish that this chapter is currently reachable on Zenith.

Completion and reward

Story progression.
