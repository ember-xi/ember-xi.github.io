Astral Covenant
Description:
Placing this sheet of parchment on the altar of the Shrouded Maw is said to open a portal to a parallel dimension.
Used In Quest(s):
ENM: Test Your Mite
Obtained:
Trading Florid Stone to Ghebi Damomohe.
Notes:
Need to be on or above Chains of Promathia Chapter 3-5 to receive this Key Item.
