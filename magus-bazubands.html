Other Uses

Used in Quests: None

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

Synthesis Recipes

  • Platinum Sheet
  • Velvet Cloth
  • Karakul Leather
  • Venom Potion
  • 2 x Imperial Mythril Piece

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Quests

  • After receiving the first cut scene in Transformations, speak with Lathuya and you will be able to have the item crafted by Kushdeel.
  • You must zone and wait until Japanese midnight after trading the synthesis items to Lathuya to receive your reward.
  • You must wait past the next Japanese midnight before ordering another piece of equipment from Lathuya.
