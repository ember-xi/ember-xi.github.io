Like a Shining Subligar

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Heiji - Norg I-7
  Requirements:
   Tenshodo Reputation 3
  Items Needed:   Rusty Subligar x 10
  Title:   Looks Sublime in a Subligar
  Repeatable:   No
  Reward:   Scroll of Kurayami: Ichi
     
  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   None

Walkthrough

  • Trade 10 Rusty Subligar to Heiji, who is situated at the top of the ramp, just to the left of the large staircase in the main room.
  • He will give you the scroll of Kurayami: Ichi

Game Description

Client: Heiji (Norg)
Summary:

Ten rusty subligaria are needed for the training of young ninjas. Heiji will accept them one at a time.
