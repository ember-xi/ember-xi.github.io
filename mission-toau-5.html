Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 4: Knight of Gold.
  • Raillefal's Letter key item.

Starting point

Halver · Chateau d'Oraguille · I-9

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Go to Northern San d'Oria, enter Chateau d'Oraguille, and present Raillefal's Letter to Halver.
  2. If the castle guard will not admit you, speak with Guilerme at Northern San d'Oria I-7 and accept his help. He arranges the mission audience and returns you outside afterward.
  3. Finish the audience; the letter is handed over and the mission advances.

Battle and progress notes

  • A high nation rank is not an additional mission prerequisite: the Guilerme route exists for characters without normal castle access.

Completion and reward

No item reward.

What this opens

  • ToAU 6: Easterly Winds
