Before you begin

Be allied with Bastok, have Rank 7, and finish the preceding required story steps. Raise Rank Points if the mission is not offered.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission and speak with Karst, then Hilda at the Steaming Sheep in Port Bastok (E-6).
  2. Reach Waughroon Shrine through Palborough Mines and enter On My Way.
  3. Defeat Sa’Nha Soulsaver, Go’Bha Slaughterer, Ku’Jhu Graniteskin and Da’Shu Knightslayer. Crowd control helps; separate the White Mage to keep Benediction from waking the group.
  4. Return to Karst after obtaining the Letter from Werei.
  5. Speak with Gumbah in Bastok Mines (J-7) for the closing scene.

Walkthrough

  • Receive the mission and talk to the President. Next talk to Hilda in the "Steaming Sheep" restaurant in Port Bastok.
  • Go to Palborough Mines and into Waughroon Shrine for a 6 person uncapped BCNM fight against:
  • Go'Bha Slaughterer (Dark Knight)
  • Ku'Jhu Graniteskin (Warrior)
  • Da'Shu Knightslayer (Red Mage)
  • Sa'Nha Soulsaver (White Mage)
  • It is recommended that you kill them in the order listed above.
  • Soloable by 75RDM/37BLM (takes around 17-20 minutes.)
  • If you use Sleepga and go for the White Mage first, he can use Benediction or Curaga to wake the others. (This can be avoided by bringing it to the entrance and killing it there.)
  • Only Bastokans (or a player who has finished this Mission, regardless of current Allegiance) can enter the BCNM. On that note 3 people at 75DRG/SAM, 75THF/NIN 75BLM/WHM Can do it fine with no problem. A 75WHM/37NIN 75BLU/37NIN and 75SNM/37WHM can do it fine also. Easily duoable by 75 BLU/NIN and 75 RDM/BLM in about 10 minutes.
  • Return to Bastok and report to the President.
  • Take the letter to Gumbah in Bastok Mines.
  • You can take the letter to Gumbah before talking to the President to obtain rank 8 first, the order doesn't matter.
