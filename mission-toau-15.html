Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 14: The Dolphin Crest.
  • Ephramadian Gold Coin for every entrant; consumed on entry.

Starting point

Cutter · Arrapago Reef · H-8

Walkthrough

  1. Carry an Ephramadian Gold Coin. For a missing coin, revisit the headstone in Caedarva Mire E-10.
  2. Travel from Dvucca Isle Staging Point west to the Arrapago Reef entrance at Caedarva Mire F/G-9. In the reef, go north to the Cutter at H-8 on the southwestern ship map.
  3. If Dvucca is unregistered, leave Nashmau west, enter Alzadaal Undersea Ruins from Caedarva Mire D-11 with an Imperial Silver Piece, take the teleporter and exit to the Dvucca-side area.
  4. Approach the Cutter for its scene, then examine it and choose The Black Coffin.
  5. Defeat five Ashu Talif Crew, then fight the Captain and four reinforcements. Reduce the Captain to 20% HP within 30 minutes.
  6. Allow the automatic exit scenes in Arrapago Reef and Nashmau to finish.

Battle and progress notes

  • Gessho assists. Control the spare crew while concentrating damage. A failed entry/battle needs another coin.

Completion and reward

No item reward.

What this opens

  • ToAU 16: Ghosts of the Past
