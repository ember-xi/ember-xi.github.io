Additional level-75 reference


Accessible via an underground burrow from Mamook to the western coast of the Bhaflau Thickets, this tomb is a sacred site of the Mamool Ja Savages. You must use the Ebony Door to do battle.

This area is only accessible if the Treasures of Aht Urhgan expansion is installed and The Road to Aht Urhgan quest is completed.
