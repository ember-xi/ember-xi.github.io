Walkthrough

  • Portaure wants you to help him smuggle some goods into Jeuno.
  • Take the Airship to Jeuno (you must do this, warping will not work as you need to pass through customs)
  • It is possible to warp to Jeuno or use some other means to get there without taking the airship, but then you must enter the San d'Oria airship dock area and attempt to exit again, which still takes you through customs like it normally would. In short, passing through customs is required to complete the quest.
  • If you pass through customs untroubled, continue with the walk-through.
  • If customs stops you, they will revoke your Airship pass for a short while (any number of game days), and you must return to Portaure if you wish to try again. The chance of this happening is random.
  • Take the goods to Yin Pocanakhu in Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno. She will tell you to talk to Portaure.
  • Return to Port San d'Oria however you like (Warp, Airship, etc.).
  • Talk again with Portaure to receive your reward.

Game Description

Client: Portaure (Cargo Room B, Port San d'Oria)
Summary:

The Brugaire Consortium wants you to smuggle a package onto the airship and take it to the Tenshodo in Jeuno.
