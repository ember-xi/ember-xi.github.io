Notes:

  • Lottery Spawn from the 2 Cactuar on the Top Side of (F-11) and Top Left side of (G-11).
    • The two Cactuar show on Wide Scan after three Desert Beetle and before a Tulwar Scorpion about half way down the list. The NM would occupy the slot directly before the Tulwar Scorpion while it is up.
  • Respawn Time is 1 to 6 hours (Earth time).
  • Has around 5500HP.
  • Known defeated by a party of three Level 60 characters. Level 60 DRG/RDM, 60 NIN/WAR, and 56BST/WHM using Scorpions and Beetles in the area. Also known soloable by a 55 BST/WHM, 65 RDM/BLM, 75 RDM/BLM, 75 BLM/RDM, 75 SMN/WHM, 75 PLD/THF, 75WHM/NIN.
  • Uses the two-hour Job Ability Hundred Fists along with 1000 Needles.
  • There is a chance that Cactuar Cantautor will not use its two-hour.
  • Resists Sleep, Sleep II, Sleepga, Sleepga II. Susceptible to Bind and Gravity.
