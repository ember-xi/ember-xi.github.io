Zephyr Fan

From Zenith XI Wiki

Key item used to enter the original Bearclaw Pinnacle ENMs.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

  1. Speak with Zebada near the entrance of Uleguerand Range to receive a Cotton Pouch.
  2. Climb the mountain, descend the western slope and land on the southern ledge near its base. Enter the cave.
  3. Trade the pouch to Chamnaet Spring for Chamnaet Ice.
  4. Return the ice to Zebada to receive the fan.

Use

Choose the route for Brothers, Follow the White Rabbit, Holy Cow, When Hell Freezes Over. Each battlefield page identifies its entrance; the entrances are not interchangeable.
