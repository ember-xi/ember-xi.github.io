Walkthrough

  • Speak with Zacc near the fountain in Bastok Markets and (with enough fame) watch a cutscene about his missing wife Alisa.
  • Talk to Malene (Alisa's mother) in house next to Sororo's Scribe, who will tell you that Alisa has been dead for a while.
  • Talk to Enu (Alisa's daughter), who is next to Zacc, and watch another cutscene, where she will ask you to find a Fallen Star which can only be found inside a huge tree in a jungle far south of Bastok. This logs the quest in your quest log.
  • Bring a hatchet to Yuhtunga Jungle and use it on a Logging Point to receive the Rare/Ex Fallen Star.
  • Return to Bastok Markets and trade the fallen star to Enu on a clear starry night for your reward.

Game Description

Client: Enu (Firewater Circle, Bastok Markets)
Summary:

Fallen stars are said to grant any wish. Enu wants you to bring one back from a southern jungle. She thinks it might be hidden inside a tree.
