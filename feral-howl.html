Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Terrorizes the target.
  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 75
  • Recast Time: 15 min
  • Duration: 1~5 seconds

Notes/Description

  • Each additional merit point decreases recast by 2:30.
  • Can add up to 2 additional merit points bringing recast to 10:00.
  • Can miss.

Macro Syntax

/ja "Feral Howl" <stnpc>
