Trail Markings

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

These special targets are found in Bastok Mines, Ru'Lude Gardens, Southern San d'Oria, Windurst Walls, Beaucedine Glacier, and Xarcabard. When a Timeless Hourglass is traded to them, they will allow yourself and up to 63 other players to enter the Dynamis areas Dynamis - Bastok, Dynamis - Jeuno, Dynamis - San d'Oria, Dynamis - Windurst, Dynamis - Beaucedine, and Dynamis - Xarcabard respectively.

  • You must have received the Key Item Vial of Shrouded Sand to enter Dynamis - Bastok, Dynamis - Jeuno, Dynamis - San d'Oria, or Dynamis - Windurst.
  • You must have successfully completed Dynamis - Bastok, Dynamis - Jeuno, Dynamis - San d'Oria, and Dynamis - Windurst and received a cutscene at each of the Trail Markings for those areas after completing them to enter Dynamis - Beaucedine.
  • You must have successfully completed Dynamis - Beaucedine and received a cutscene at the Trail Markings for that area after the completion to enter Dynamis - Xarcabard.

Entering each of these Dynamis areas for the first time will cause you to receive a cutscene, involving the story of the Dynamis areas. These cutscenes are only shown once, though they may be viewed again through that area's Goblin Footprint or Past Event Watcher.
