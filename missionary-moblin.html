Walkthrough

  • Talk to Koblakiq and select 'Terrified' in answer to her question.
  • Obtain a Soiled Letter from the Orcish Footsoldiers in either Davoi or Castle Zvahl Baileys.
    • In Davoi, 26 total Orcish Footsoldiers are found throughout the zone. Here are some positions where at least 1 can be found if not more:
      • (E-6), (E-7), (F-6) x4, (F-7) x2 in the wider area, (F-7) in the little path, (F-8), (G-7), (H-9), (H-10), (I-10)
  • Return to Oldton Movalpolos and trade the Soiled Letter to Koblakiq to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Koblakiq (Oldton Movalpolos)
Summary:

Koblakiq has stopped receiving letters from her brother Zeelozok after he went to speak with the Orcish overlord in Davoi. Koblakiq wants you to go there and search for him.
