Walkthrough

  • Talk to Anguenet and take the fourth option (Newtpool) for him to talk about the Muddy Siredon. (Note: On Eden 09/29/2022 I did not receive this dialog.)
  • Trade Lourdaude a Muddy Siredon and then 100g to complete the quest.
  • Muddy Siredon can be fished up on the Carpenters' Landing Barge with a Fly Lure or Lufaise Fly in the Newtpool (needs confirmation), in any fishable body of water in Carpenters' Landing.
Note
  • The Blackened Muddy Siredon is helpful in defeating the Mithran Trackers during Promathia Mission 5-3.

Game Description

Client: Anguenet (Carpenters' Landing)
Summary: If you give a "Muddy Siredon" to Lourdaude, the tonberry that stands beside Anguenet, he will make you one of the Mithra's favorite foods.

Note: Lourdaude only requires 100 gil the first time you complete the quest. Afterwards you need only trade him the fish to obtain the Blackened Muddy Siredon.
