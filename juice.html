Juice

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Juice is a term used to describe food items which restore MP over time (i.e., grant Refresh). Juice will not stack with the Red Mage spell Refresh, but juice does stack with the Bard spells Mage's Ballad and Mage's Ballad II. The effects of juice will also stack with equipment and job traits that grant the Refresh effect, such as Vermillion Cloak or the Summoner, Paladin, and Blue Mage jobs.

Some juices will also grant the Regen effect.
