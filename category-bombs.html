Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Self-Destruct: AoE Fire damage based on Bomb's remaining HP and suicide. Easily stunnable.
Berserk: Greatly reduces Defense, but improves Attack considerably.
Heat Wave: Severe Burn
Vulcanian Impact: Single-target magic attack. Ignores shadows.
Hellstorm: Ignores shadows and does AoE fire damage based on the monster's HP.
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
