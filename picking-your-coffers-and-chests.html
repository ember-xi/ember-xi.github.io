Notes:
  • These only spawn every-once-in-a-while when a Thief fails to pick a Coffer. By no means do they spawn every time a pick is failed, but there is a chance one might.
  • The Coffer Key drop is 100% guaranteed.
  • Has the abilities:
Draw In: Immediately pulls the player to the Mimic, making it nearly impossible to run away.
Death Trap: An AoE Stun and Poison (10 damage per tick) that cancels Utsusemi.
  • They're typically about the same level as the mobs that drop coffer keys in that zone.
  • See also: Picking your Coffers and Chests
