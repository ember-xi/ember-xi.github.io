Walkthrough

  • Talk with Honoi-Gomoi again after zoning. He wants you to disassemble the cardian Joker for the S.O.B.s.
  • Speak with Kohlo-Lakolo who will tell you where the Cardian has been hiding (the House of the Hero in Windurst Walls).
  • At this point, you can have one of two storylines.
    • If you have not completed Windurst Mission 8-2:
      • When you show up at the House of the Hero, the cardian Joker is there. It is confused about what is its purpose. It then asks you to go to Toraimarai Canal and search for an answer in its place.
      • Go there, get a coffer key, find a treasure coffer, and open it to get the Joker Card.
      • Return to the House of the Hero. Joker will take the card from you, and ask that you do not disassemble it. When you agree, Joker will give you the 8000 gil reward.
        • If you complete Windurst Mission 8-2 before going back to the House of the Hero, Apururu will appear instead and buy the card from you for 8000 gil.
    • If you have completed all the Windurst Missions:
      • When you show up at the House of the Hero, Apururu is there. She asks you to go to Toraimarai Canal.
      • Go there, get a coffer key, find a treasure coffer, and open it to get the Joker Card.
      • Go see Apururu at the Manustery in Windurst Woods. She will take the Joker Card from you, and give you the 8000 gil reward.
  • Finally, talk to Honoi-Gomoi to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Honoi-Gomoi
Summary: You have been asked to take apart the Joker Cardian and steal its Mana Orb. But ... Where, o where has the Joker gone? O where, o where can he be?
Additional Information: If you have completed the Windurst missions, you access an alternate storyline for this quest. Instead of Joker, Apururu is at the House of the Hero and you must return the Joker Card to her at the Manustery.
