Virtue Stone Pouch

Statistics

Virtue stone pouch
This small velvet pouch is used to
carry large quantities of virtue stones.

Stackable: 12

Note: When a pouch is used you obtain one stack of Virtue Stones.

Other Uses

Used in Quest: None

Resale Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Quest

Trade any one of the following items to Meret in Tavnazian Safehold:

  • Aern Organ
  • Euvhi Organ
  • Hpemde Organ
  • Phuabo Organ
  • Xzomit Organ
  • Yovra Organ
  • Luminion Chip
  • Luminian Tissue

For each of these items you turn in, you will receive a Virtue Stone Pouch. You must complete her friend Yurim's quest, In the Name of Science, before Meret will accept any trades. Note: you can only turn in one item at a time; for example if you have 12 Luminion Chips, you can not give her all of them at once.
