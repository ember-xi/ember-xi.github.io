Notes

Before the outbreak of the Crystal War, Iru-Kuiru was one of the ministers for the Rhinostery, along with Yow Rabntah. They were sent along with Mythril Musketeers Ulrich, Raogrimm, and Cornelia to investigate the northlands in cooperation with Francmage of San d'Oria. Some time after returning from the expedition he was mysteriously murdered.
