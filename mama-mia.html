Walkthrough

  • Speak to Mamaulabion, to start the quest.
  • He wants you to obtain for him super-duper rare items that can only be obtained from the high level Avatar battles. What he needs is the following:
    • Egil's Torch which can be obtained from the quest Trial by Fire.
    • Desert Light which can be obtained from the quest Trial by Earth.
    • Eye of Nept which can be obtained from the quest Trial by Water.
    • Bubbly Water which can be obtained from the quest Trial by Wind.
    • Rust 'B' Gone which can be obtained from the quest Trial by Ice.
    • Elder Branch which can be obtained from the quest Trial by Lightning.
    • Ancients' Key which can be obtained from the quest The Moonlit Path.
  • The items can be traded as you receive them. Mamaulabion will hold onto all of them until he receives the seventh.
  • If you return with all 7, you cannot trade all at once. They must be traded one at a time. If you try to put all 7 in and submit, he will just take one and you will get the other 6 back.
  • You will receive the Evoker's Ring at or after 12:00 am Japanese Standard Time the following Earth day from your final item turn-in.

Game Description

Client: Mamaulabion (Near the Auction House, Norg)
Summary:

Mamaulabion would like you to bring him any "thuper-dee-duper, overflowing with thpethial power itemth" you happen to come across in your travels. However, he was not able to provide you with any details on their whereabouts.
