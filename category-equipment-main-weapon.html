Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

What can I equip?

  • One-handed or two-handed Weapons.
  • If you equip a two-handed weapon, you can only equip a Grip in the Sub Slot.
  • If you equip a one-handed weapon, you can equip a Shield or, with Dual Wield Job Trait, equip a second one-handed weapon.

TP

Changing equipment in this slot will result in TP reset.

Macro Syntax

In order to change equipment in this slot, use the following syntax:

/equip main "<Item Name, as written in Inventory>"

Notes

Unequipping items from this slot will not result in the removal of Shields but will result in the removal of One-handed weapons in the Sub Slot.
