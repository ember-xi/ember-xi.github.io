Walkthrough

  • Kill Savanna_Rarabs in West Sarutabaruta until you receive a Torn Epistle.
  • Koru-Moru will smell the scent of his love in the letter you are carrying and asks for a rough black stone.
  • Kill crawlers until one drops a meteorite and then bring it back to Koru-Moru and he will give you a reward.
  • Koru-Moru will not give you the quest until you have a Torn Epistle in your inventory.
  • You must not have the quest Class Reunion active in order to activate this quest.

The meteorite is dropped by Crawlers around Starfall Hillock in West Sarutabaruta.

Note: You can also trade him the Torn Epistle for 50 gil.

Game Description

Client: Koru-Moru (Manor, Windurst Walls)

Koru-Moru wants you to find him a rough black stone, for some spaced-out reason.
