Increases Elemental Weapon Skill Damage

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

It is reported that a fTP + 0.05 bonus is applied to every WS (whether it's elemental or physical) whose Skillchain attributes match the current weekday, and the bonus may ocassionally kick in on a non matching weekday too.
