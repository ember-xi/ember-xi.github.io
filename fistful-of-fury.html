Walkthrough

  • Collect the three items required by Vola (Tenshodo HQ in Lower Jeuno).
  • Get a Dodo Skin (Rare/Ex) from an NM named Deadly Dodo (Lv39 - Lv40) in Sauromugue Champaign.
  • Get a Nue Fang (Rare/Ex) from an NM named Nue (Lv41 - Lv42) in Beaucedine Glacier.
  • Get a Morbolger Vine (Rare/Ex) from the NM named Morbolger (Lv42 - Lv44) in Ordelle's Caves.
  • Alternatively, the Morbolger Vine may be obtained from the Uncapped 6-member KS30 BCNM Contaminated Colosseum, the Nue Fang may be obtained from the BCNM50 Eye of the Tiger, and the Dodo Skin may be obtained from the Uncapped 6-member KS30 BCNM Moa Constrictors.
  • Trade these items to Vola to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Vola (Tenshodo, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

To receive your brown belt, find a nue fang, a molbolger vine, and a dodo skin. Only then will you be worthy.
