Migratory Hippogryph

From Zenith XI Wiki

Quest-spawned Hippogryph for the Dancer artifact weapon quest.

Spawn and reward

  1. Start The Unfinished Waltz as Dancer level 40 or higher and finish the opening conversations with Laila and Rhea Myuliah.
  2. On Dancer, find the ??? among three nearby trees in Witchfire Glen, Grauberg (S), at (F-5).
  3. Examine it for a scene, then examine it again without leaving the zone to spawn Migratory Hippogryph. Bring help if needed.
  4. Defeat the monster and examine ??? again for The Essence of Dance key item. The item comes from the target after the battle, rather than the treasure pool.
  5. Read the key item in your inventory, then return to Laila on Dancer for War Hoop.
