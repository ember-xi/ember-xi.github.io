Zenith availability

Reference route: the pinned native quest explicitly awaits its Ghoyu’s Reverie instance implementation. The inspected tree contains only the doomvoid instance for that zone. Battlefield entry/completion is not established for the reviewed Zenith base; this is a known progression gap, not a confirmed playable guide.

Before you begin

  • Complete Perils of the Griffon.
  • Receive the Number eight shelter key from Diordinne.

Starting point

Diordinne · Garlaige Citadel (S) · I-6, map 1

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Diordinne at I-6 on Garlaige Citadel (S) map 1 and finish the scene to receive the shelter key.
  2. Reach the Wooden Crates at H-9 on map 2. Examine them for the preparation scene, then have the party leader use them to enter the quest battlefield.
  3. In Ghoyu’s Reverie, follow the map 15 route south toward H-10, using the western walls after the first ramp. Destroy the Orcish Turret within 30 minutes; clearing every enemy is unnecessary.
  4. Watch the return scene. Speak to Rholont in Southern San d’Oria (S) at E-7 between 18:00 and 23:59 Vana’diel time to finish.

Battle and progress notes

  • Poison devices make status cures useful. The reference retry route is to speak to Diordinne after failure and return after the next Vana’diel day for a replacement key.

Completion and reward

Fullmetal Bullet.

What this opens

  • In the Name of the Father progression
  • The Price of Valor after A Nation on the Brink
