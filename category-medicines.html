Medicines are similar to foods in that they can modify a players statistics and status effects. Some medicines can cure you of different Status Effects, some can replenish some of your HP, some can boost a stat for a while, and a number of other things. A few may grant the Medicated status effect, prevent you from using similar medicines until it wears off.
