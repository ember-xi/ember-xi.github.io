Linkpearl

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A linkpearl is created from a linkshell or pearlsack and when equipped allows the person to see and join in discussion with a linkshell community. Your linkpearl may be changed remotely by the linkshell owner to a pearlsack, which will give you the ability to make multiple linkpearls for other players.
