Statistics

Rancor Den coffer key [1][2] A key that opens a treasure coffer in
the Rancor Den.

Type: Coffer Key

Stackable: Not Stackable

Other Uses

Used in Quest: The Road to Aht Urhgan

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

Possible Treasure

  • 8,000 - 14,000 gil
  • Map of the Den of Rancor**
  • Aquamarine
  • Moonstone
  • Sunstone
    • Automatically received if you do not already have the map.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account. [6][7]
