Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 29: Puppet in Peril.

Starting point

Cutter · Arrapago Reef · H-8

Walkthrough

  1. Travel to Dvucca Isle Staging Point and head west to the Arrapago Reef entrance at Caedarva Mire F/G-9.
  2. Watch the scene on entering the reef. If arriving through another entrance, the scene still flags the mission step.
  3. Go north to the Cutter at H-8 on the southwestern ship map and approach it for another scene.
  4. Finish the scene to obtain the Periqia Assault Area Entry Permit.

Completion and reward

Periqia Assault Area Entry Permit key item.

What this opens

  • ToAU 31: Shades of Vengeance
