Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


File:Bad Breath.jpg

Deals earth damage that inflicts multiple status ailments on enemies within a fan-shaped area originating from the caster.

Description

Spell cost: 212 MP
Ecosystem: Plantoids
Type: Magical (Earth)
Blue Magic Points: 5
Stat Bonus: INT+2, MND+2
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 61
  • Cone Attack that inflicts many debilitating status effects, including Slow, Poison, Silence, Paralyze, Weight, Bind and Blind.

Timers

Casting Time: 8.75 seconds
Recast Time: 120 seconds

Notes

  • The formula for this spell appears to be (Current HP / 8) + (your level / 3). For example, if you are level 61 and have 1000 HP, your damage would be 125+20.3, or 145. This is subject to Resists, however. Using it against Beasts or Magic Bursting it will not boost damage; it will only affect resist rates.
  • Damage is NOT affected by Magic Attack Bonus.
  • Ignores Magic Defense Bonus, but is subject to direct magic resistance (from Proto-Omega for example).

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Scission, Gravitation, Darkness
