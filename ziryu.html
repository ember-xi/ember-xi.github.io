Ziryu

From Zenith XI Wiki

Worm adds summoned during the Ouryu battlefield encounters.

Contents
  1. Encounter
  2. Handle the adds
  3. Listed drops
  4. Sources

Encounter

Four Ziryu accompany Ouryu in Ouryu Cometh. They can also appear when Ouryu is summoned during The Wyrmking Descends. These are battlefield adds, rather than a normal Riverne camp.

Handle the adds

They cast dangerous earth magic, including Stonega III and Quake. Assign control or interruption while the main group handles Ouryu. Beastmasters can Charm them.

Listed drops

Worm Mulch; the Riverne A01 listing also includes Lugworm Sand. Follow the main battlefield page for entry items and victory rewards.
