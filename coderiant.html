Coderiant

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Adventurer's Assistant NPC

Location: Southern San d'Oria (F-8)

Notes

Offers Directions Around Town:

Are you lost? I know this neighborhood well. Which way are you going?

"Left."

  • There on Pikeman's Way you can find a grocer, sundries and the Tanners' Guild. At the end lies the mansion of Count Caffaule.

"Right."

  • Go up those stairs for Watchdog Alley. Homes of townsfolk line the street.

Involved in Quests

  • Lost Chick
