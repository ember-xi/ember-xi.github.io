Long-Armed Chariot

From Zenith XI Wiki

The final boss on floor 5 of the original Silver Sea Remnants.

Contents
  1. Reach and defeat
  2. Armor drops
  3. Sources

Reach and defeat

  1. Enter Silver Sea Remnants and follow its Salvage floor route to the fifth floor.
  2. Watch for Brainjack: it Charms a target and adds damage over time, often in repeated sequences. When the boss turns toward you, swap to a harmless weapon if possible.
  3. Keep Regen on tanks. Uncharmed players should control charmed allies with Bind or Gravity and avoid being hit by them.
  4. Win for Moon Charioteer, one of the titles needed for An Imperial Heist.

Armor drops

Bodb's Cuffs, Ea's Brais, Freyr's Jerkin, Freyr's Mask, Phobos's Gauntlets, Tsukikazu Sune-Ate. These are level-25 components for the original Salvage armor upgrades; they are not the finished level-75 pieces.
