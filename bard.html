Job Overview


Available after completing the quest Path of the Bard. A bright tune to soothe a party's wounds... A brisk march to raise a party's spirits... A demoralizing lamentation to weaken a party's foes... The bards of Vana'diel use music as their weapon.

 
A bard uses songs as its main advantage to boost the party's stats. Bards have many songs but only one non-meritable Job Ability.
