Outpost

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Border guards are assigned to these wilderness posts to oversee conquest efforts and provide support for adventurers. Some of these outposts are constructed with simple guardhouses and defensive walls. As long as they are not under beastman control, the outposts will be staffed by a regional Conquest Overseer and an Outpost Vendor.
