Pantin Attire +1 Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A high-quality version of the Pantin Attire set. It is said by Sagheera that the advanced Relic Armor was made during the Great War to resist the Kindred advances. However it's still unclear as to why the Puppetmasters of the Aht Urgan Empire would've had such fantastic clothes made for a war not of their lands. However, for a small price, Sagheera has offered to restore your Relic Armor to it's original beauty and power...
