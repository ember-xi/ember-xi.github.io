Before you begin

  • Complete Cait in the Woods.
  • Carry the Ronfaure dawndrop awarded by Cait in the Woods.

Starting point

Glowing blank targets · Jugner Forest [S] · I-6

Era note: Later WotG chapter, released after the level cap began rising above 75. Included for story continuity; this guide does not claim a tested level-75 balance.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. The Ronfaure dawndrop is already obtained. Collect the other seven by examining their glowing blank targets in any order.
  2. Visit Jugner Forest [S] (I-6), across the river beside the familiar stump; La Vaule [S] southeast (K-9), beside the house; and Southern San d'Oria [S] (K-6), outside Lion Springs.
  3. Visit Beaucedine Glacier [S] (H-9), down the ramp from the Campaign tower; Xarcabard [S] (F-8), beside Rally Point: Red; and Throne Room [S], in the central walkway.
  4. Enter Walk of Echoes through Grauberg [S] (F-5) and examine its target south of the conflux. The eighth dawndrop combines the set into Primal glow.
  5. If Walk of Echoes was your last stop, leave to Grauberg before starting Maiden of the Dusk.

Battle and progress notes

  • The pinned core has an incomplete main-story battle at mission 14; later story handlers do not establish that this chapter is currently reachable on Zenith.

Completion and reward

Primal glow.
