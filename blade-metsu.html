Blade: Metsu

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Katana weapon skill

  • Skill level: N/A
  • Description: Additional effect: Paralysis
  • Hidden effect: temporarily enhances Subtle Blow effect.
  • This weapon skill is only available with the stage 5 relic Katana Kikoku or within Dynamis with the stage 4 Yoshimitsu.
  • One hit weapon skill, despite non single-hit animation.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Darkness / Fragmentation
  • Modifiers: DEX:60%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
3.00 3.00 3.00

Translation

"Metsu" means more than the other Katana Weapon Skills. The kanji for the WS in Japanese is 生者必滅, which means "All living things must eventually die". In short it could also mean "Return to nothing". In the english version, it is shortened from "shou ja hitsu metsu" to simply "Blade: Metsu".

Blade: Metsu shares its name with Final Fantasy VII's Yuffie Kisaragi's second level three limit break, "Doom of the Living" or "生者必滅."Source
