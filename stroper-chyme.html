Notes:

  • Two spawns on Map 1, one on each of the southern raised areas that you drop to from Map 2.
  • Two spawns on Map 3:
    • One to the north, usually with the Goliath Beetles in I-6 but sometimes far to the north.
    • One to the south, usually in the hall around H-9 but sometimes in the Napalm room.
  • In order to get to these locations you have to drop down from the upper level drop point in Ordelle's Caves.
  • Killable by: Solo at level 45 by certain jobs, although since it is a Slime type it will be very difficult at this level alone.
  • Stroper Chyme are not Notorious Monsters and can therefore be charmed.
