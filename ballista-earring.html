Ballista Earring
Description:
Wear this earring during a Ballista
match, and you will be able to gain
aid from your nation's Pursuivant.
For novice players only.
Obtained:
Pursuivant at any Ballista Battlfield or by borrowing from the Pursuivant in Ru’Lude Gardens (players without a Ballista licence).
Notes:
Wear this earring during a Ballista match, and you will be able to gain aid from your nation's Pursuviant. Also when in possession of this item, the player will receive information about the next Ballista match and the entry registration time when entering San d’Oria, Bastok, Windurst, or Jeuno from one day prior to the match (Vana’diel time) until the end of the entry period.
