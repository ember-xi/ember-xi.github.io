Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

File:Dedication (Status Effect).png Dedication is a beneficial status effect that makes you benefit more from your experiences until it wears off. Benefitting characters receive a boost in EXP.

How to remove the effect

  • Reaching the EXP bonus limit will remove this effect. (See the item that gave you this effect, for details.)

How the effect is gained

Items

  • Anniversary Ring
  • Chariot Band
  • Empress Band
