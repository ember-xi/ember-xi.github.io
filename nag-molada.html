Notes

Nag'molada is a diplomat for the Duchy of Jeuno, and a member of the Armathrwn Society. In the time of the Great War, he was employed by the Archduke as a special envoy to the other three nations, and assisted in the formation of the Allied Forces of Altana. He has an exceptional grasp of strategy and is highly skilled in negotiations.
