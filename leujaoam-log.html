Leujaoam Log

From Zenith XI Wiki

Leujaoam Observation Log for the Mythic Assault record.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. How to fill it
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Receive this record from Paparoon when starting Duties, Tasks, and Deeds.

How to fill it

Complete all ten normal Leujaoam Sanctum Assaults with the record in your inventory. At the end of each successful mission, trade it to the Rune of Release before leaving. Repeat any missing mission, then hand the completed record to Paparoon.
