Fear of the Dark

From Zenith XI Wiki

An original San d’Oria quest to help Secodiand by collecting bat wings.

Quest NameFear of the Dark
Start NPCSecodiand · Northern San d’Oria (E-6). Fear of the Dark.
Required levelOriginal quest
ObjectiveTrade 2 Bat Wings together. Repeatable.
Reward200 gil base + San d’Oria fame
Status: Original quest documented from source; Zenith play test unconfirmed. Details

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Secodiand in Northern San d’Oria (E-6) and agree to help.
  2. Obtain 2 Bat Wings. Bat Fangs are a different item.
  3. Trade both wings together to Secodiand and finish the dialogue.
  4. Return with another pair if you wish to repeat the delivery.

Reward

200 gil and San d’Oria fame under the original quest rules.
