Tachi: Rana

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Great Katana weapon skill

  • In order to obtain Tachi: Rana, Unlocking a Myth (Samurai) must be completed.
  • Delivers a three-fold attack. Accuracy varies with TP.
  • Aftermath effect varies with TP. See Kogarasumaru.
  • Will stack with Sneak Attack (first hit only).

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Gravitation / Induration
  • Modifiers: STR:35%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.000 1.000 1.000

Notes

This is a Mythic Weaponskill, see also Mythic Weapons.
