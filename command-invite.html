Command/invite

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Aliases: /inv
Usage: /invite [subcommand]
Description:

Turns the party flag on/off.

Subcommands:
on Turn flag on.
off Turn flag off.

Toggles on and off when no subcommand
is specified.

Cannot be used with an automatic party.

Example: /invite on

Notes

/invite will toggle your seek party flag making others aware of whether you are looking for a party.
It is common to use the party flag in conjunction with a search comment (see /seacom) in order to convey what kind of party one is seeking.
This command will not allow you to automatically join parties; instead, it will require a party leader to send you an invitation to join his/her party.
If you log off with this flag on, it will still be on when you log back in.
