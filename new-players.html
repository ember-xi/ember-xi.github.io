New Players

Welcome. If you are new to Final Fantasy XI, this page collects everything you need to start on EmberXI: a family server in the level 75 era, played solo with Trusts (an NPC party that fights for you) or together with the family.

Starting Out

When you create your character you choose three things: your race, your job and your nation.

  • Jobs — six standard jobs from the start; the rest unlock by quest after level 30
  • Nations — San d'Oria, Bastok, Windurst
  • Races — Hume, Elvaan, Tarutaru, Mithra, Galka

Server Rules

  • Level cap is 75. No item or spell above 75 exists (Auction House and drops).
  • Trusts are allowed everywhere, even inside Battlefields: 3 from the start, a 4th at level 30 and a 5th at level 50.
  • Dying inside missions and battlefields does not cost XP.
  • The Auction House stocks itself with era items and charges no fees.
  • XP is ×2 and Skill-ups are ×3 to make solo play comfortable.
  • All Home Points are open from the start.

Exploring Vana'diel

A huge world that grew with every expansion. EmberXI runs the first three: Rise of the Zilart, Chains of Promathia and Treasures of Aht Urhgan.

  • Areas — coming soon

Leveling Up

On EmberXI you level solo with Trusts: call a tank, a healer and a mage, then fight monsters that check as Even Match or Tough. Play the first 3 levels alone, then use the full party.

  • EXP Guide (1–75) — where to level at every stage
  • Trusts — how your NPC party works

What to Do

There is always something to do: the nation and expansion story, quests, Avatars for Summoners, and Eco-Warrior every two days.
