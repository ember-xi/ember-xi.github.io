Walkthrough

  • Talk to Brygid at K-9 in Bastok Markets. She advises you to wear a Bronze Subligar and a Robe.
  • Obtain and equip these items. This combination of equipment only can be worn by: BLU, BRD, DRK, MNK, RDM, or THF.
  • Once you are wearing both items, speak with her to receive the Gloves to complete the set.

Game Description

Client: Brygid (Gold Street, Bastok Markets)
Summary:

Brygid, the "stylist extraordinaire," gave you some fashion advice: she wants you to come back wearing a robe and a bronze subligar.
