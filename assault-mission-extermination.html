Walkthrough

Kill everything.

  • It is possible an NM will pop when you defeat an enemy of that type. (After killing one Slime, Undead Slime popped)
    • 1 NM will always spawn per run
    • It must also be defeated in order to win.
    • It spawns where an enemy just despawned
  • If melee DD fight the slimes, they should sub NIN to avoid Fluid Spread.
    • Or you can send a BLM or two to kill them while the rest of the party goes the other way.
    • The Carrion Slimes do not have MP, so Aspir cannot be used. They also appear to be resistant to Bind.
    • Be careful when sending BLMs, wait for every slime to disappear before pulling a second one, otherwise the NM could spawn and the BLMs can wipe against two.
