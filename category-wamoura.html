Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


General Information

  • System: Vermin
  • Weaknesses:
  • Immune: Fire
  • All wamoura detect by spellcasting and true-sound.
  • Wamouracampa (larval wamoura) will link by sound with adult wamoura and vice versa.

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Erratic Flutter: AoE fire damage, plus high % Haste on itself. Can be Dispelled.
Exuviation: Full Erase. Restores HP based on amount of debuffs it Erased from itself.
Proboscis: Cone Attack MP Drain, deals damage equal to MP drained and removes one random buff.
Magma Fan: Cone Attack fire damage
Erosion Dust: AoE Dia effect, can be Erased. Removes all shadows.
Fire Break: Single-target fire damage? Notorious Monsters only.
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.

Other

  • Pop when certain larval wamoura in Mount Zhayolm remain alive for an extended period of time.
  • When adult Wamoura in Mount Zhayolm de-spawn, they are replaced by a normal Wamoura Prince. This is only true for Wamoura that spawned from wamouracampa. Verification Needed
  • Adult wamoura have greater than normal movement speed and can be Aspir-ed.
