Notes

Apururu is current Minister of Windurst's Manustery, the Ministry of Windurst that is in charge of the creation, training, and maintenance of all Cardians. She is also the daughter of former Manustery Minister Zonpa-Zippa and the sister of current Orastery Minister Ajido-Marujido.

She is constantly at odds with her brother over his recklessness, fearing that he may be getting in over his head. Her words, however, usually fall upon deaf ears.
