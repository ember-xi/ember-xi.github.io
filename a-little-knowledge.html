Before you start

Any job level 30+; access to the past via Cavernous Maw. Bring 12 Vellum. Finish on BLM, RDM, SMN or BLU with its special ability ready.

Steps

  1. Talk to Erlene in The Eldieme Necropolis (S) (J-8, first map), reached from Batallia Downs (S) (J-10). Accept the quest.
  2. Trade Erlene all 12 sheets of Vellum. You can buy them from the Auction House or obtain them through Tucker.
  3. As BLM, RDM, SMN or BLU, activate Manafont, Chainspell, Astral Flow or Azure Lore respectively. Talk to Erlene while that effect is active.
  4. Finish the scene, then change to Scholar at a Mog House. Next equipment quest: On Sabbatical at SCH 40.

Reward

Unlocks Scholar; Grimoire key item; Schultz Scholar title.
