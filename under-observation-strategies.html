BLM x3

Party Setup

  • BLM
  • BLM
  • BLM

Recommended subjob: WHM

Supplies

  • Melon Pie (or Melon Pie +1)
  • 1 Yagudo Drink per fight
  • A reraise item (Reraise Earring, Scroll of Instant Reraise, etc.)

The Fight

You have 15 minutes to clear this BCNM, which means if you are there for drops and not to set records you have plenty of time to stop, buff up and rest to regain your MP. After entering the BCNM each Black Mage should use a Melon Pie, then cast Blink and Aquaveil. If you want, have one of the BLMs cast Protectra and Shellra, then wait for everyone to rest to get their MP back. Note: If you plan on sharing a reraise earring or similar item, pass it around before you cast Blink/Aquaveil, the wait time for everyone to get reraise up off the same item will be long enough for Blink/Aquaveil to wear off before you actually get to start casting.

Approach the arena at the top of the hill when everyone is ready. Have all 3 party members stand on the very edge of the hallway leading into the circular area where the mobs are. One BLM should put Barfira up, however if you forget it's not a big deal. It is important to get as close to the edge as possible without causing aggro, because if one or two of the Black Mages are out of range when they attempt to start casting, the one who was successful has a very low chance of surviving which can ultimatly lead to a wipe. When you are within casting range, have all 3 BLM turn their backs to the Hecteyes, this will prevent them from being able to hit you with AoE Petrify. You can not avoid Breakga, only Hex Eye and Petro Gaze. Whatever you do don't look them in the eye. Everyone should target the Sobbing Eyes and have one BLM call a time to start casting. Open the volley of spells with Elemental Seal + Stonega II. While Stonega is casting, start spamming your Thundaga or Blizzaga macro until you are able to cast it, repeat for your next -ga spell (whichever of the two you didn't use). By the time each BLM has 3 spells off, the 2 Compound Eyes should be dead, and the Sobbing Eyes should be around 50% or less. Just have each BLM start their 3 spell volley again (Stonega II, Bliz, Thunder). The Sobbing Eyes should drop dead after this second round, or be close enough that one or two more casts will kill it. Note: During casting, each BLM should use a Yagudo drink well before they need it, this should keep your MP high enough through the whole fight.

This entire process should take you no more than 5 or 6 minutes, including the time it took you to buff/rest at the start.

Alternative BLMx3 Strategy

If you happen to have an "unusually raced blm" (ie: Galka or Elvaan) with high HP/def, then you can have him tank. Designate one of the other two BLMs as healer for the tank. Then make sure the tank nukes first, to get the extra initial hate of "first damage". This can give you a win, without even using yagudo drinks (if you happen to have a Taru blm in the party, anyway) This method was successful 2/2 times, even with the Elvaan tank(me) only having Thundaga instead of Stonega II. No risk, easy win. Just make sure that you all face away, to avoid paralyze, and start with Blink+Aquaveil, and there's no problem. We did use melon pies. Just no drinks. (Note: It might be that the tank casting the smaller-faster -ga spells instead of Stonega II,helped keep hate. haven't tested otherwise]

One slight improvement on this strategy,is to make sure someone who can take the hits from the monsters to start the first cast (i.e. not a tarutaru,preferrably elvaan or galka),as a tarutaru can get torn apart without proper gear,and may spell defeat.

NIN x2 + RDM

Party Setup

  • NIN/BLM or NIN/RDM (or BLU/NIN)
  • NIN/BLM or NIN/RDM (or BLU/NIN)
  • RDM (Using /BLM or /WHM or /NIN) (or WHM/BLM)

The NINs should have BLM15+ or RDM20+, since Magic Attack Bonus will help out with the damage. NIN/BLM has better INT and Elemental Seal for sure landing of Jubaku: Ichi or Drain, while NIN/RDM has Barfire, Shell, Fast Cast, Resist Petrify and Cure II.

For this strategy, BLU/NIN using Yagudo Drinks can substitute for NIN. Alternatively WHM/BLM can be used in place of RDM, the loss of Gravity is not so big a deal.

I would like to point out that NIN/RDM is noticeably faster and more efficient than NIN/BLM. Also, since you're /RDM, you can save time AND the kiters MP by keeping barfire up on yourself at all times and virtually eliminate having breakga landing on more than one player at a time. Not to mention having extra cures available to pull hate or save a life can pretty much guarantee the win.

  • A simple Pineapple Juice will be plenty of refresh for the NIN/RDM's but is not needed at all.

Supplies

Required:

  • One stack of each tool for the Ninjas' level 40 elemental Ninjutsu spells. One stack will accommodate approximately 10 runs.
  • At least one stack of Shihei.
  • Yagudo Drinks if substituting with BLU.

Optional:

  • INT+/MP+ food for everyone. (Use something cheap, like Melon Pie.)
  • Yagudo Drink(s) for the RDM.
  • A reraise item (Reraise Earring, Scroll of Instant Reraise, etc.)
  • Jusatsu for Jubaku: Ichi (Paralyze)

The Fight

Summary:

  1. Shell/Protect II for all, Utsusemi up, MP rest.
  2. RDM starts the fight with Gravity on Sobbing Eyes, kites it toward entrance. Use Bind when Gravity is resisted or not up. Keep Stoneskin up.
  3. Each NIN (or BLU) takes a Compound Eyes, use the wheel (or Blue Magic) while facing away.
  4. Once done with both Compound Eyes, catch up with Sobbing Eyes, and attack the same way.
  5. Spread out to form a triangle while fighting Sobbing Eyes if possible, to minimize Breakga's danger.

As soon as everyone enters, go up to the hill before the hecteyes and buff up. Have the RDM cast Shell II on both NINs. Protect II can be helpful as well, but isn't necessary. Make sure everyone knows which mob is the his/her target.

Once the RDM is done buffing and resting MP, cast (Elemental Seal if available) Gravity on the Sobbing Eyes. Right when the RDM does this, both NINs cast a Ninjutsu spell on their respective Compound Eyes. NINs can start with the wheel or a debuff like Jubaku: Ichi. There will be breaks between wheels to (re)enfeeble the critters as well.

The RDM immediately starts running from the Sobbing Eyes, back toward the entrance of the BC. The NINs solo their eyes, making sure to tank them backwards, as the eyes' "Hex Eye" attack is devastating, but can be avoided if the target isn't facing the eye. Using only the level 40 elemental ninjutsu, each NIN should be able to defeat their eye easily within about two minutes.

If a NIN's HP gets low, can use Elemental Seal + Drain on /BLM, or just Cure II on /RDM. In the mean time, the RDM should be constantly casting Gravity (or Bind in an emergency) on the Sobbing Eyes when necessary, avoiding the enemy as much as possible while waiting for the NINs to get done. Blink or Utsusemi would be stripped by AoE, so save recasting for when bound to conserve Stoneskin from Sobbing Eyes' melee hits.

For the RDM kiter, get plenty of space to cast Stoneskin and Aquaveil after you start the pull, usually about halfway down the hill. If you cast Stoneskin at the beginning of the BCNM, it may wear off too early. Stay out of reach but not too far away that you miss when Gravity wears off. Avoid using Bind if possible, because the Sobbing Eyes will be more likely to cast magic like Breakga when bound. Keep your distance but allow it to get within casting range. It will begin casting Bindga or Breakga when first getting within range, which takes a few seconds to cast. When you see the casting animation, continue running and its casting will be interrupted. Also, there will be a small window where the Sobbing Eyes will not attempt to cast magic, use this to your advantage when you get to the bottom of the hill. To turn around and kite back up the hill safely, use the casting interruption technique, but as soon as you see the cast fail and the Sobbing Eyes start moving again, run directly for it and right past it. By the time it readies Hex Eye, you will be well past it and it will have no effect. Get the NINs to report when their Compound Eyes are at about half HP, so you know when to turn around.

With both Compound Eyes defeated, the NINs catch up to the Sobbing Eyes and start double teaming it, using the same method as they used on the Compound Eyes. NIN/BLM can toss out a Elemental Seal Jubaku, and NIN/RDM should put up Barfire to make the fight a bit safer. Try to stand apart, so won't both get hit by Breakga or Firaga. The Sobbing Eyes can be tricky with its -aga spells, so make sure the RDM stands out of range, throwing the NINs some Cures and Regens when necessary. The Sobbing Eyes should go down pretty easily. Feel free to nuke and Convert to speed things up, once NINs have completed a round or two of the wheel.

The entire fight should take no more than 10-12 minutes, even with a full MP rest at the start after full buffs, and without rushing the fights any.


-Key points to remember:-

  • NINs (and BLUs) always face away from the enemy to avoid "Hex Eye".
  • RDM stays out of AOE range as much as possible.
  • Don't neglect Elemental Seal, Barfire, and Drain.
  • If possible you can use a Powder Boots {feet gear}on your rdm which will give you "flee" and use it before casting the 1st elem-gravity which will give you good speed to be able to rest for a good amount of time.


NIN, DD, RDM or SMN

Party Setup

  • NIN/WAR
  • DD/NIN (DRG/NIN and WAR/NIN work well)
  • RDM (Using /BLM or /WHM or /NIN) (or SMN/WHM)

Very useful when you can't find another 40+ NIN with the Ni wheel. The NIN must come /WAR in order to keep hate off the melee.

SMN is very useful for this fight as it can kite much easily than RDM and Carbuncle can give added magic defense.


Supplies

Required:

  • One stack of each tool for the Ninjas' level 40 elemental Ninjutsu spells. General NIN spells such as Jutsatsu (Paralyze) and Kurayami (Blind) well also work very well. One stack will accommodate approximately 10 runs.
  • At least one stack of Shihei.
  • About 2 Hi-Potions per run for the DD, more if your DD sucks or is generally squishy.

Optional:

  • INT+ food for the NIN
  • Attack+ or Defense food for the DD
  • Yagudo Drink(s) if your kiter is SMN.


The Fight

Summary:

  1. Buffs: Shell/Protect for all, NIN uses Yonin and gets Utsusemi up, MP rest.
  2. RDM starts the fight with Bio or Gravity on Sobbing Eyes, kites it toward entrance. Use Bind when Gravity is resisted or not up. Keep Stoneskin up. If SMN is kiting, after Shell/Prot use Carby's Shining Ruby, rest to full, and use Carby to kite the Sobbing Eyes, keeping stoneskin blink up etc. Run far enough away form the Sobbing Eyes so that you can resummon Carby when it dies, and keep repeating until NIN and DD have killed the other eyes.
  3. The NIN will provoke one Compound Eyes, and use the Ni wheel to nuke it while facing away.
  4. DD gets hate on other Compound Eyes via Jump, Provoke, etc. DO NOT ENGAGE. Face AWAY from the Eye you get hate on, and run to the NIN to hit the NIN's Eye from the back.
  5. Once the NIN's eye is killed, DD stays still until NIN gets hate on other eye, facing away as usual. DD goes to the back of that eye and attacks.
  6. Once both eyes are done, NIN and DD rest to full and kiter brings Sobbing Eyes back to arena.


As soon as everyone enters, go up to the hill before the hecteyes and buff up with both Protect and Shell. Make sure everyone knows which mob is his/her target.

Once the kiter is done buffing and resting MP, they will get hate on the Sobbing Eyes. NIN provokes one Compound Eyes and tanks backwards using provoke and ni spells. DD gets hate on other Compound Eyes via Jump or Provoke. DO NOT ENGAGE. Face AWAY from the Eye you get hate on, and run to the NIN to hit the NIN's Eye from the back, like a THF. Thus both the NIN and the DD will be killing the same eye, while the DD lets the other eye beat on him.

With the arrows indicating the direction you are facing, it will be:
WALL << NIN << COMPOUND EYE << DD << COMPOUND EYE

The kiter immediately starts running from the Sobbing Eyes, back toward the entrance of the BC. The NINs backwards tanks the Eye and DD attacks it from the back to avoid Hex Eye. At any point if the DD takes hate, turn around IMMEDIATELY (from BOTH eyes) to avoid getting paralyzed. For this reason it is highly recommended that the DD fight with the target lock OFF. Because the other eye is beating on the DD, the DD must use Hi-potions and utsusemi shadows to keep themselves alive. The Kiter should NOT heal the DD since the DD is not maintaining hate on the other eye. The first eye will go down quite fast with two people killing it at the same time. Once the first eye is defeated, NIN provoke/gets hate on the DD's eye:

WALL << NIN << COMPOUND EYE << DD

At this point, the kiter may toss cures to the DD and NIN as needed.


For the kiter, get plenty of space to cast Stoneskin and Aquaveil after you start the pull, usually about halfway down the hill. If you cast Stoneskin at the beginning of the BCNM, it may wear off too early. Avoid using Bind if possible, because the Sobbing Eyes will be more likely to cast magic like Breakga when bound. Keep your distance but allow it to get within casting range. It will begin casting Bindga or Breakga when first getting within range, which takes a few seconds to cast. When you see the casting animation, continue running and its casting will be interrupted. Also, there will be a small window where the Sobbing Eyes will not attempt to cast magic, use this to your advantage when you get to the bottom of the hill. To turn around and kite back up the hill safely, use the casting interruption technique, but as soon as you see the cast fail and the Sobbing Eyes start moving again, run directly towards and past it. By the time it readies Hex Eye, you will be well past it and it will have no effect.


With both Compound Eyes defeated, the NIN and DD catch up to the Sobbing Eyes and start whacking away with NIN backwards tanking, using the same method as they used on the Compound Eyes. Barfire to make the fight a bit safer. Try to stand apart, so won't both get hit by Breakga or Firaga. The Sobbing Eyes can be tricky with its -aga spells, so make sure the Kiter/Healer stands out of range and doesn't pull hate.


-Key points to remember:-

  • NIN and DD must always face away from the enemy to avoid "Hex Eye".
  • Kiter stays out of AOE range as much as possible.
  • Don't neglect Elemental Seal, Barfire, and Drain.
  • DD must be able to keep themselves alive with hi-pots and shadows.



BLU x2 + RDM

Similar to the NINx2 + RDM strategy, this setup capitalizes on BLU's ability to tank backwards to avoid paralysis. Due to a reliance on MP instead of ninja tools, this can be slightly cheaper. BLU also has the added advantage of self-cures for emergencies, as well as a Stun spell. No food or drink is necessary, but melee foods for the BLU's may help speed up the kills. Cheap food such as crab sushi or meat jerky are favorites. If you feel uncomfortable without refresh, bring a yagudo drink for emergencies, but once you get the hang of this strategy, you'll find they're not needed. Both BLUs sub ninja for shadows and need to equip Head Butt, Bludgeon, Cocoon and Wild Carrot*. The RDM can sub whatever he wants, but I prefer /BLM for more INT and Elemental Seal.

Strategy:

  • Enter the battlefield, BLU cast shadows*, RDM casts Shell II on everyone, and Stoneskin on himself. RDM rests to full. Other buffs can be cast as well, but are unnecessary and just waste time*.
  • Each BLU targets a separate Compound Eyes and draws weapons at the edge of the battlefield. The RDM casts Gravity on the Sobbing Eyes and kites him away from the main arena. BLU's swing once at the Compounds as they go by and grab their attention*.
  • Hold 'em and Fold 'em: Each BLU faces away from his mob to tank it backwards. Spam Head Butt and Bludgeon as recast allows. If you need to, cast Wild Carrot to cure yourselves (be sure to cast on the other BLU if he's having trouble). With proper timing in recasting your shadows and stunning the more hurtful spells, you should each be able to finish off your mobs before you run out of MP. (Some like to throw in Screwdriver as a spell here, but Bludgeon is more MP efficient and deals about the same damage). Once both Compound Eyes are dead, rest your MP to full. Make sure to rest off to the side to avoid any -ga spells when the RDM bring the main one back.
  • Let's Go Fly a Kite: The RDM will have the task of keeping the Sobbing Eyes occupied until the BLU's are finished killing and healing their MP. After the initial Gravity, run back down the path to the first set of torches and wait for the Sobbing Eyes to catch up. He will stop to cast a spell (bindga or breakga), so just keep moving down the path out of spell range. If you know you're spell timing right, you can dart back within range after it's spell fails to cast Bind or reapply Gravity as needed.
    • If you're running out of room, you're going to have to go back up the path. This is usually where the Sobbing Eyes has the best opportunity to land its spells on you. Dart in as a spell fails and Bind him. Run around him and watch for his next spell. As he starts casting either Bindga or Breakga, stop and cast Barblizzard or Barstone as appropriate (you won't have enough time to get out of range). This can significantly shorten the duration of bind or petrify or cause you to resist outright.
    • If you're bound, cast Barstone immediately (he will be following up with Breakga next) and then reapply Gravity or Bind if needed. If he petrifies you, you're going to have to wait it out with your stoneskin and remaining HP, so the longer he's bound out of melee range, the better. Once your petrify wears off, make sure he's got either Bind or Gravity on him and run for it. Once you're out of spell casting range, just drag him back to the arena slowly as before.
  • The Grand Finale: As you come back into the arena, move to the opposite side of the BLUs. Wait for the Sobbing Eyes to come back in and cast a spell on you. BLU's should stay out of range, and you should Barspell yourself appropriately. Just after he casts, BLU's should rush in. Use Bludgeon to kill this guy backwards, as before. Save Head Butt to stun his -ga spells. RDM can back off at this point and use Regen, cures and nukes to make the killing go faster, being careful not to pull hate.

Strategy Pros:

  • Other than shihei and Yagudo drink, no money need be spent on this strategy.
  • Stun makes Bindga and Breakga much less dangerous to the damage dealers.
  • DDs have the ability to heal themselves in a pinch.
  • RDM, with it's ability to use Barspells as well as Gravity, Bind and other survival tools, makes an ideal kiter for this fight. I have yet to have a single Bind or Gravity be resisted by the Sobbing Eyes.
  • DDs have extremely fast damage output.

Strategy Cons:

  • Relies heavily on the soloing skills of the Blue mages and the kiting ability of the Red mage.
  • Accidental paralysis at any point during the initial killing of the Compound Eyes can spell doom for the fight, so be extra careful to tank backwards.

BST x3

Party Setup

  • BST/WHM
  • BST/WHM
  • BST/NIN

Supplies

Required:

  • Funguar or Tiger Jug pet
  • some Shihei for the BST/NIN.

Optional:

  • Au Lait's for everyone.
  • Hi-Potions

The Fight

As soon as everyone enters, Call Beast, buff up and rest to regain MP.

The BST/NIN should send his jugpet to fight Sobbing Eyes and quickly hit Heel. This should aggro all three mobs. The other BSTs should attack one of the Compound Eyes while the BST/NIN kites the other two around the zone. When the first Compound Eyes dies, the BST/WHMs should pick off the other Compound Eyes. If the BST/NIN gets low in HP, he should pop a potion or 2 or let one of the BST/WHM run to heal him. The kiter should be careful to stay out of range of the Sobbing Eyes's AOE petrify. Once the Compound Eyes are taken care of, everyone should finish off the Sobbing Eyes. Now make a mad dash to the Armoury crate because you should be running out of time.

  • BST/WHM or RDM (Sub either BLM for Elemental Seal or go WHM)

Supplies

  • Yagudo Drink > For the BLU (or RDM). Bring however many according to how many runs are being done.

The Fight

This is a little different from the other set-ups, although really a fusion if anything. Short and simple. BST is kiting, using their jugpet (preferably the tiger pet) to take the main Hecteyes away from the lesser ones that both the NIN and BLU will be tanking. Like the NIN and BLU strategies mentioned in the above strategies, you're back will be turned towards the Hecteyes.

NIN > Spam Utsusemi as well as ninjutsu.

BLU > Spam utsusemi and blue spells, preferably Screwdriver and Head Butt (for when spells are being casted by the Hecteyes, such as Fire II and Firaga). Use a Yagudo Drink before doing so, so you get an MP regen while tanking.

  • Note: Screwdriver may have a water animation, but it is a physical spell, Bludgeon is the better choice, if Blue Magic skill aswell as Weapon skill is capped, it will do far more damage at a fraction of the MP. Not only that but since it gives 30% mob TP, it will cause the weapons to gain TP faster causing them to spend more time uselessly spamming hex eye.

BST > While kiting, heal from far away while your pet tanks. Cure when necessary, kite, MP rest.. it's not as complicated as it sounds, although it might take time to get used to for new BST players. If by chance either tank is paralyzed, the BST can assist in Paralyna-ing the members.

Almost usually, the BLU will kill his/her hecteye before the NIN will, and in this case (depending on MP remaining) will either MP rest or assist the NIN~ which then both should full rest before taking on the bigger hecteye once the second lesser one has been downed.

From there, the method used earlier won't be changing much as the NIN and BLU will still be attacking using ninjutsu/blue magic, while the BST and pet assist in attacking.

If no BST is present, the RDM would kite by using the same strategy as shown in stragety #2.

BLU + NIN + SMN

Party Setup

  • BLU/NIN
  • NIN/BLM
  • SMN/WHM

Supplies

  • Yagudo Drink > For the BLU and SMN. Bring two per person per run. This strategy is not a speedy strategy by any means, and the first one will be well worn off before you're finished.
  • Hi-Ether x3 and Hi-Potion +3 x3 > For the BLU and SMN. These are not necessary but good for recovering after killing the two peripheral Hecteyes.

The Fight

This strategy has won us 95% of our runs, it just takes good stress management and you must realize you'll be cutting it close (HP-wise) every time. Of course, start with buffs like every other fight. Make sure the BLU has Cocoon, Metallic Body and all of Head Butt, Screwdriver and Bludgeon equipped. Make sure everyone has Shellra, Utsusemi (and Blink on the SMN), and Barfire on.

After each pulling their eyes, and the SMN pulling the Sobbing Eyes, the BLU and NIN should backwards-tank the two Hecteyes as usual while the SMN keeps the Sobbing Eyes busy with Carbuncle. The BLU should kill his eyes a bit quicker than the NIN if he's spamming Head Butt Bludgeon and Screwdriver.

Once the BLU kills his, he can help the NIN finish off his and then heal for MP. The SMN should be running away from the Sobbing Eyes every time Carbuncle engages, and recasting when Carbuncle is KOed. Once the BLU is healed, the BLU and NIN can both engage the Sobbing Eyes.

Please note that if the SMN is bound and ends up under attack from the Sobbing Eyes before the BLU is ready, the NIN should go ahead and tank it while the SMN cures him. Once the BLU is ready, he should go ahead and engage the Sobbing Eyes, facing him for a quicker win. The SMN will have Paralyna ready for the inevitable Hex Eye.

Although this seems risky, if you fail to engage the mob quickly enough you run the risk of running out of time. BLU should be able to do great damage with his spells, using Hi-Potion +3s as needed. Fast Blade should do around 130 damage as well. This fight is very easy with this strategy and usually lasts about eleven minutes.

BLU/NIN (or /WAR), BST/WHM Duo

Very easy if you are prepared. The BLU should have yag drinks, a tavnazian taco, and 1 remedy if you're scared of being paralyzed (not necessary). The BST should have pet foods, and can use DD food and/or drinks for MP, but these aren't necessary either.

Have the BST buff at the start (Protect/Shell/Barfira) and use a Tiger Familiar jug. The BLU should face away from the eyes in case they open with Hex Eye. The BST sends the Tiger Familiar after the main eye, moves out of agro range (50+ yalms) and heels his pet, kiting the eyes to the entrance and back. The BLU should pull with ranged attack (or provoke) and cast spells facing away from the lesser eye.

Sprout Smack, Bludgeon, Head Butt and Screwdriver are all the DD spells you need, saving Chain Affinity for Screwdriver. A taco and cocoon will ensure that the lesser eyes will barely break your stoneskin from Metallic Body, if at all. Repeat with the other eye, resting as needed, and have the BST, tiger and BLU converge on the main eye. Let the BLU build a little hate so that the BST can dd without worrying about Hex Eye. As always, face away from the mob while casting.

Use yag drinks as necessary, and try to heal to full before the big eye. Catharsis can draw the fight out needlessly if you aren't prepared. I personally always bring 1 remedy just in case. We clear consistently in about 12:30 with this strat. Good luck! --Eremes 03:08, February 20, 2010 (UTC)
