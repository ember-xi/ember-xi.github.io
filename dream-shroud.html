Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Diabolos enhances magic attack and magic defense of party members within area of effect.

Technicals

Avatar: Diabolos (Dark)

Blood Pact: Ward

Cost: 121 MP

Level: 56

Description

Effect Duration: 3:00

Battle Application: N/A

Damage Type: N/A

Additional Notes: adds a MAB/MDB bonus based on the ingame time, 10% MAB at midnight(23:00-1:00) to near 1%-0% at noon (11:00-13:00), the MDB is effected in a similar way, but no known testing has been done to get exact numbers, see discussion.
