Sangoma Lappa

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-74 leg armor: DEF 35, MP +12, INT +6 and Evasion +3.

Contents
  1. Who can equip it
  2. How to obtain
  3. Sources

Who can equip it

MNK / WHM / BLM / RDM / THF / BST / BRD / RNG / NIN / SMN / BLU / PUP / DNC / SCH.

How to obtain

  1. Travel to Castle Oztroja (S). Hunt the past-area version of Zhuu Buxu the Silent, a lottery from Yagudo Sentinels near H-10 on the first map.
  2. Defeat the NM for a chance at the drop. The low-level Giddeus version drops Parana Shield instead.
  3. Alternatively, check the Auction House under Armor → Legs. Availability depends on sellers.
