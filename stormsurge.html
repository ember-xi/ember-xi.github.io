Stormsurge

From Zenith XI Wiki

Scholar Group 2 merit trait at level 75.

Purchase Stormsurge with merits. Storm spells you cast then add a bonus to an attribute associated with their element. More upgrades increase the bonus.

StormAttribute
FirestormSTR
SandstormVIT
RainstormMND
WindstormAGI
HailstormINT
ThunderstormDEX
AurorastormCHR
VoidstormAll seven attributes at reduced potency

Cast the relevant Storm on the party member who benefits. You still need its scroll and required casting level.
