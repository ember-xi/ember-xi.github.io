Walkthrough

NOTE: Wait 2 Vana'diel days and zone to start this quest after finishing the previous quest.

  • Speak with Shantotto multiple times before she starts talking about prison, and how it is all your fault. Choose the options "Cry in despair" and "No way! Not on your life!". (in case you don't get these options from Shantotto just rezone).
  • She tells you to bring her two Bomb Arms, a Revival Tree Root and a Lock of Hiwon's Hair.
  • Speak with Hiwon-Biwon again. He'll become physically ill on your feet, and you take this opportunity to snag a lock of his hair.
  • After you've collected the rest of the items, trade them to Shantotto and she de-curses Hiwon-Biwon and gives you your "reward".
  • Upon activating the quest, your title becomes "Tarutaru Murder Suspect". When you complete the quest, it becomes "Hexer Vexer".

Game Description

Client: Shantotto (Manor, Windurst Walls)
Summary:

The dancing spell Shantotto cast on her bothersome Peeping Tom turned out to be a deadly curse. To remove the curse, she'll need a revival tree root, two bomb arms, and some hair from the victim. Hurry, before it's too late!
