Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Beastmen tend to be grouped into tiers of 10 levels each, with certain kinds of beastmen within those tiers.

Tier 7 is for beastmen whose levels range from 61 to 70. Many beastmen of this tier are common sources of Coffer Keys and of their job's Testimony. Gold Beastcoins can be stolen from the majority of these beastmen.
