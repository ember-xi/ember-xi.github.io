Spiral Hell

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Scythe weapon skill

  • Skill level: 240
  • In order to obtain Spiral Hell, the quest Souls in Shadow must be completed.
  • Delivers a single-hit attack. Damage varies with TP.


Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Distortion/Scission
  • Modifiers: STR:50% ; INT:50%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.375 1.875 3.625

Levels

  • While Dark Knights may obtain skill level 240 earlier than level 71, Spiral Hell must be quested once base skill level 240 (without merits) and job level 71 is reached (requires the ability to wield Scythe of Trials).
  • Scythe skill level 240 is obtainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
Job Rating Level
Dark Knight A+ 68
Warrior B+ 72
Beastmaster B- 75
