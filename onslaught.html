Onslaught

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Axe weapon skill

  • Skill Level: N/A
  • Description: Lowers target's accuracy. Additional effect: temporarily increases Attack.
  • Available only when equipped with the Relic Weapons Ogre Killer (Dynamis use only) or Guttler.
    • These Relic Weapons are only available to Beastmasters, so only they can use this Weapon Skill.
  • One hit attack despite of non single-hit animation
  • The attack increase seems to be 10%.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Continuing Darkness , Gravitation
  • Modifiers: DEX:60%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
2.75 2.75 2.75
