Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Game Description

"Enemies are less likely to detect you by smell."

Description

Deodorize is a beneficial status effect that prevents monsters from tracking you by smell until it wears off or is removed. Scent-tracking monsters will cease pursuit of Benefitting characters once they're out of the mob's detection range for 30~ seconds.

How to remove the effect

  • This effect can be manually cancelled.
  • Effect is lost by taking an offensive action against a monster
  • Effect is lost by recieving an offensive action from a monster

How the effect is gained

Spells

  • White Magic - Deodorize

Items

  • Deodorizer

Other

Please Note: The following information is accurate for Eden as of 8/29/2026.

  • Rainy Weather or Squalls grants a perma-Deodorize effect for the duration of the storm, but this does not actually grant the Deodorize status symbol.
  • Double snow weather also deodorizes a player the same way as Rainy Weather.
  • Pursued players are granted a deodorize effect by running across or standing in valid bodies of water, but the effect is instant, similar to Hide.
