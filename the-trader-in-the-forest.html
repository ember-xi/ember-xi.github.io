Walkthrough

  • Speak with Abeaule, who will explain he needs someone to go out and fill an order he has. If you accept, he will give you a Supplies Order (speak with him again if you lose it).
  • Trade this Supplies Order to Phairet in the tower at I-11 in West Ronfaure and he will give you the Batagreens.
  • Trade the Batagreens to Abeaule and you will receive your reward.

Game Description

Client: Abeaule (Phoenix Perch Inn, Northern San d'Oria)
Summary:

Purchase some batagreens from Phairet. Find him in the tower on the southern fringes of West Ronfaure.
