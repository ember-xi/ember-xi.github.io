Statistics

Ice maker
An ice-based automaton attachment.

Functionality:
  • Converts the energy of an Ice
    Maneuver into magic attack power
    when casting magic spells
    However, this adds burden to Ice
    Maneuvers
Stackable: 12
This is an Automaton Attachment:
Automaton Effect Req. Capacity
Converts the energy of an ice maneuver into magic attack when casting elemental magic spells.
The maneuvers will only be consumed if the spell hits (not absorbed by images). 		Template:Element
  • For other ice attachments, all ice maneuvers bonuses are still counted for the cast, even though the ice maneuvers are removed.
  • In the event of a complete resist, such as being absorbed by a shadow, active ice maneuvers will not be removed upon casting the resisted elemental spell.
  • Magic attack bonuses from other attachments will stack, as the bonus is calculated after all other MAB boosts are factored in. The damage increase is similar to that of the Elemental Staves. (Note the difference between +20% Magic damage and +20 MAB.)

Example: With one ice maneuver up and a loudspeaker equipped, you have +10 MAB from the loudspeaker, bringing your MAB multiplier to 1.1. When a nuke is used, the ice maker's damage bonus gives you 1.1 * 150% = 1.65 from the loudspeaker and ice maker.

Maneuvers Magic damage bonus

Ice x 1 150%
Ice x 2 175%
Ice x 3 200%
