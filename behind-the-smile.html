Walkthrough

Tavnazia

  • Talk to Enaremand at J-7 on the upper level in the Tavnazian Safehold for a cut scene.

Mhaura

  • Head all the way to Mhaura to talk with Fyi Chalmwoh at G-8 (in the Goldsmiths' Shop) for another cut scene.

Carpenters' Landing

  • Next you want to head out to Carpenters' Landing to fight an Orc. Zone into Carpenters' Landing from Jugner Forest at J-8.
  • Go to the western part of I-10 where you'll find a ???. When you touch it Bullheaded Grosvez will spawn.
  • After you've defeated Bullheaded Grosvez touch the ??? again to receive the key item Red Oil.

Back to Tavnazia

  • Go back to the Tavnazian Safehold and talk to Enaremand again for your Mannequin Pumps.

Game Description

Client: Enaremand (Third floor, Tavnazian Safehold)
Summary:

One last material is required to complete Enaremand's final masterpiece. Perhaps the mannequin craftswoman in Mhaura has some clues to where you may be able to find it.
