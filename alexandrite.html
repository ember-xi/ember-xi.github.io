Alexandrite

From Zenith XI Wiki

Salvage currency collected for a level-75 Mythic weapon.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Defeat Archaic machinery in original Salvage, or use Cotton/Linen coin purses from its treasure. Alexandrite can also be traded between players.

ContainerAlexandrite per use
Cotton Coin Purse5–20
Linen Coin Purse50–99

Use

Trade a total of 30,000 to Paparoon during Duties, Tasks, and Deeds. Level-99 Salvage II and later retail campaigns are outside this era route.
