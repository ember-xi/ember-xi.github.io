Alexandrite

Piece of Alexandrite
TypeCurrency item — it stacks, so you can carry plenty
Earned fromAmbuscade · Nine Teeth · The Henge
Spent onArtifact +1 · Relic armor +1 · Mythic weapons · forge pieces
Check your balanceTalk to Teodor in Port Jeuno

Alexandrite is the stone currency of EmberXI. You earn it from repeatable content and spend it on armour and on the Mythic ladder.

Where to get it

SourceAlexandrite
Ambuscade — Intense4 per win
Ambuscade — Normal2 per win
Hollow of Nine Teeth5 on your first clear, 3 after that
The Henge3 per clear, +5 when a set is completed, +2 on your first clear
Ambuscade win milestones (Halvard)5 stones at 5 wins · 10 at 30 wins · 20 at 60 wins

Ambuscade rotates weekly and is the steady source. The two dungeons pay more per run, but you clear them less often.

Milestones: Halvard pays the win milestones. Once you pass one, talk to him and he hands the stones over.

Where to spend it

What you getWhoCost
Artifact armour +1Brandt4
Relic armour +1 (AF2+1)Brandt9
Mythic weapon (whole ladder)Teodor150, over five steps
One forge piece (Homam / Nashira / Salvage)Halvard20

Notes

  • Brandt takes the base piece and the stones in one trade.
  • Teodor tells you what the next Mythic step costs when you talk to him.
  • The forge is the slow road to a set. The dungeon coffers are the fast one — they drop the pieces directly.
  • Teodor and Brandt stand in Port Jeuno. Halvard is still at the fountain in Lower Jeuno.
