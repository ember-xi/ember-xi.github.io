Notes:

  • Spawn from trading a Smoky Flask to the ??? near (I-8) of Halvung.
  • Uses Fire based spells, such as Firaga III, Fire IV, Burn, Blaze Spikes, Enfire.
  • Grows in size up to 4 times in response to large spike damage (i.e. WS and large nukes).
  • Is immune to all forms of stun, including those from spells, weapons and weapon skills.
  • In order to arrive at this camp you must pass by many True Sight monsters and past no less than 2 Archaic Mirror and their NM Mirror Guards.
  • Killable by: 2-6 characters level 75. Can be duoed by skilled players consisting of NIN or THF and RDM, or RDM and BLM.
