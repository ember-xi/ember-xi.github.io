Walkthrough

  • Elysia asks you to recover a hairpin she thinks she may have dropped while shopping.
    • She can be found as you enter the circular room with the bridge from the entrance, by following the path to the right and entering the second hall on your right.
  • After speaking with Elysia, procede to the top floor of Tavnazian Safehold.
    • To get to the top floor, retun to the large circlular room with the bridge. One side of the bridge has ramps going up and down. Take the ramp up.
  • On the top floor, go right. Procede clockwise around the circle until you pass an open door and then come to a second open door in the southeast of the room. Go through the second open door.
  • Proceed down the hall through the door. At the end of this hall, take the ramp up on your left.
  • Go right at the top of the ramp. Behind the far pillar is a ???.
  • Examine the ??? on the ground south of Korbi-Marobi, behind the stone column. You will obtain the Key Item Alabaster Hairpin.
  • Return to Elysia and speak with her. You are asked to relay a message to Pradiulot.
  • Speak with Pradiulot at the west end of the altar/bridge room at (H-9) to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Elysia (G-10) in Tavnazian Safehold

Find and bring back the hairpin that Elysia dropped while doing her shopping.
