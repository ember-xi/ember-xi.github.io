Guild Point

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Abbreviation: GP

These are points needed to buy certain guild-related items. After speaking to a Guildworker's Union Representative and signing up with a Guild, you can trade requested items of your craft to the Guild Representative for Guild Points. The amount of points you can earn each day varies from day to day.

See Guildworker's Union for more information. See Guild Points/Items for all items.
