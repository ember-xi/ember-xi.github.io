Description

Surrounding Heavens Tower in Windurst Walls are five manors belonging to the retired former Ministers of Windurst. Karaha-Baruha, the former Optistery Minister, once lived in this manor until his death during the Crystal War. This house is (mostly) inaccessible to adventurers, but some of Karuha's property still resides in the building....

Location: Windurst Walls (G-3)

Shop type: House
