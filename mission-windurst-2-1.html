Before you begin

Be allied with Windurst, have Rank 2, and finish the preceding required story steps. Raise Rank Points if the mission is not offered.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission. Speak with Tosuka-Porika at the Optistery in northern Windurst Waters (G-8), then Nanaa Mihgo in Windurst Woods (J-3).
  2. Enter the Maze of Shakhrami from Tahrongi Canyon (K-5). Take the upper-map (G-6) passage to the lower map and search Fossil Rocks for Lapis Coral.
  3. Return to Nanaa Mihgo for the Hideout Key.
  4. Enter Lily Tower in East Sarutabaruta (J-7). Follow Inner Horutoto’s cracked wall (G-9) into Beetles Burrow and examine the Mahogany Door at (G-8).
  5. Visit the House of the Hero in Windurst Walls (G-3), then return to Tosuka-Porika.

Walkthrough


Windurst

  • The mission will not be available until after you amass a certain number of Rank Points by either trading a single stack of crystals of any type to the War Warlock (character with the initials W.W. after their name) located at the city gates, by killing monsters while Signet is applied to your character, or a combination of the two.
  • After accepting the Mission, talk to Tosuka-Porika at G-8 in the Optistery in the northern portion of Windurst Waters. Then go outside the Optistery and down the stairs. Take the warp from Tonana (G-8) to Windurst Woods and go see Nanaa Mihgo at J-3.
Note: It is recommended that you talk to Hariga-Origa and start the Glyph Hanger quest for the Map of the Horutoto Ruins, if you have not done so already.
  • Purchase some Prism Powders and Silent Oils or have Sneak and Invisible available for the next part. It is recommended to obtain an Instant Warp scroll as well, provided your Home Point is in Windurst.

The Maze

  • Continue to the Maze of Shakhrami, which can be found at the very Northeast corner of Tahrongi Canyon (K-5).
  • Once inside, go to H-5 on the lower maze map (map 2). There are four Fossil Rocks in this room. There are also two Fossil Rocks in the large room on the same map at I-8.
  • Examine a Fossil Rock and get key item Lapis Coral.
    • There are 5 Fossil Rocks to examine, one will give you the Lapis Coral. There are several aggressive mobs on the way to this room, Goblins which detect by sight and Maze Scorpions and Ghouls which detect by sound. Once in fossil room, the active mobs all detect on sound only. You'll need to cancel your Invisible to examine the Fossil Rock, but you can keep Sneak.
      • EDIT: There are actually 14 Fossil Rocks throughout the zone. Confirmed via GM when I couldn't find the key item with the above info. They're all on the same map, just a lot of walking. You do not need to touch all of the Fossil Rocks listed, once you get the key item Lapis Coral you can move on.
        • Enter Maze of Shakhrami from Tahrongi Canyon, go to G-6 and pass to map 2 at D. In this room there are four Fossil Rocks at H-5.
        • From previous room, go south to H-6 and pass to different area of map 1 at E. Go east to J-6 and drop down, then south to J-7 and pass through G onto lower part of map 2. Follow the path and at I-7 there are two Fossil Rocks here.
        • Keep following the path to the next big room. There are five Fossil Rocks in this room, spanning G-7 and G-8.
        • The previous big room extends west, then there is a path south. There is one Fossil Rock at the end of this path at F-8.
        • Go back to the previous big room and head south, follow the path at G-9 around. There is another drop down here that leads into another big room. The final two Fossil Rocks are in this room at I-8.
  • Once you have the key item Lapis Coral, head back to Nanaa Mihgo in Windurst Woods (J-3) to receive key item Hideout key.

Horutoto Ruins

  • Head to the Lily Tower in East Sarutabaruta at J-7.
  • Go to the northwest corner of the ring-shaped room at G-8 and into the room with the Ancient Magical Gizmo. There is a cracked wall here; go through the cracked wall and then proceed to the Mahogany Door.
    • NOTE: Walking through the cracked wall will take you to a new map of Beetle's Burrow. The cracked wall at G-8 will lead to the new map at K-7. Go Southwest - there are two paths, but you can take either of them, as they both converge at I-9. At I-9, take the West tunnel at the 3-way intersection. Follow the tunnel Northwest - again, take either path, as both converge at your target, the Mahogany Door.
  • After the cut scene, head to the House of the Hero in Windurst Walls (G-3). After the cut scene, go back to Tosuka-Porika to finish the mission.


Game Description


Mission Orders
There are reports that the Cat Burglar has her paws on a "Book of the Gods," a restricted book that was lost twenty years ago. Assist the Opistery in investigating the reports, and attempt to retrieve the priceless, but dangerous, tome.
