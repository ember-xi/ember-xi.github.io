Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Enhances defense for party members within an area of effect.

Description

Spell cost: 84 MP
Spell element: Light
Magic skill: Enhancing
Jobs:

  • Level 75 White Mage (via Merits)

Timers

Casting Time: 7 seconds
Recast Time: 20 seconds


How to Obtain

Obtained: White Mage Merit Point Group 2

Other Information

Bonus
Increases Defense by 60 points.
Each additional Merit upgrade increases the Defense Bonus granted by 2.
