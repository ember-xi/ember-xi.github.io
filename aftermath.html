Aftermath

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Aftermath is a beneficial status effect applied upon using one of the weapon/job specific weapon skills with a Relic Weapon (or Stage 4 Weapon in Dynamis areas) or a Mythic Weapon equipped.
The Aftermath icon appears for the duration of the effect and does stack with spells and other effects.
The Aftermath effects are as follows:
