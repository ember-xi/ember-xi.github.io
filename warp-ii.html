Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Transports target party member to their home point.

Description

Spell cost: 150 MP
Spell element: Dark
Magic skill: Enhancing Magic
Jobs:

  • Black Mage Level 40

Timers

Casting Time: 5 seconds
Recast Time: 10 seconds

How to Obtain

Auction House Category: Scrolls > Black Magic (
 
 )

Quest

  • Curses, Foiled A-Golem!?

Notes

  • Occasionally abbreviated D2 due to the Japanese word for Warp, Dejyon (de-zone)
  • Cannot be used on Alliance members or other players outside of your party.
  • The player receiving the effects of the spell will be cast to their own home point (not the home point of the caster).
  • When cast on a Beastmaster with a charmed pet, the caster will be aggroed by pet.
