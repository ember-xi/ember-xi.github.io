Royal Footman's Vest Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A set of armor with the same appearance as the Leather Vest Set. This set, however, originates from San d'Oria, requiring Conquest Points from rank 1 and 2 San d'Orians. It is equippable by most common front line jobs and offers small bonuses to vitality, agility, attack, and various elements in addition to its defense. There is a crafted upgrade to this set in the form of the San d'Orian Vest Set and an HQ crafted upgrade in the form of the Kingdom Vest Set.
