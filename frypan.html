Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Delivers an area attack. Additional effect: "Stun." Accuracy varies with TP.

Description

Spell cost: 65 MP
Ecosystem: Beastmen
Type: Physical (Blunt)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: DEX+2
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 63

Timers

Casting Time: 1 seconds
Recast Time: 26 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Frypan as part of a combo to create Max HP Boost.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Max HP Boost combo.

  • Flying Hip Press
  • Body Slam

Notes

  • Blunt damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: STR: 20%, MND: 20%.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Skillchain Element(s): Lightning (can open Liquefaction or Detonation; can close Impaction or Fusion)
