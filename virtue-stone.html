Virtue Stone

Statistics

Virtue stone
All Races
Virtue
Lv. 73 All Jobs

 Stackable: 99

Other Uses

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

Charged Ammunition: Can enhance damage of weapons that use virtue stones.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

NPC

  • Trade any one of the following items to Meret in Tavnazian Safehold:
  • Aern Organ
  • Euvhi Organ
  • Hpemde Organ
  • Phuabo Organ
  • Xzomit Organ
  • Yovra Organ
  • Luminion Chip
  • Luminian Tissue
For each of these items you turn in, you will receive a Virtue Stone Pouch. You must complete her friend Yurim's quest, In the Name of Science, before Meret will accept any trades.
