A Clock Most Delicate

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Galmut - Upper Jeuno (G-7)
  Requirements:
   Jeuno Reputation 5
  Items Needed:   Clock Tower Oil (key item)
  Title:   Professional Loafer
  Repeatable:   No
  Reward:   1200 gil
  Engineer's Gloves

  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   Deal with Tenshodo and (separately)
  Save the Clock Tower

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Collet at G-7 to gain access to Galmut's house.
  • Click on Galmut's door to start the quest.
  • Complete the quest Deal with Tenshodo.
  • Click on Galmut's door to trade him the Clock Tower Oil and complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Galmut (House, Upper Jeuno)
Summary:

Galmut is running out of oil for the clock tower. The Tenshodo might have some in stock.
