Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Grants a bonus to Third Eye when using two-handed weapons.
  • Obtained: Samurai Level 35
  • Recast Time: 1:00
  • Duration: 5:00

Notes/Description

  • While Seigan is in effect, the recast time for Third Eye is halved.
    • Third Eye may anticipate or counter an attack without being consumed.
  • Hasso and Seigan do not stack.
  • Like Weapon Bash or Hasso, this Job Ability requires a two-handed weapon.
  • Ninjutsu, song, and magic casting and recast times are increased by 50% while Seigan is up.
  • Seigan works in conjunction with Third Eye to act in a similar manner to the Utsusemi series of Ninjutsu. A monster's attack can either be evaded (anticipated) or counter-attacked. Each time a Samurai evades or counters with Seigan/Third Eye, there will be a chance that Third Eye will cancel. The longer Third Eye has been active, the higher chance of cancellation.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Seigan" <me>
