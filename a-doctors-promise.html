Requirements

Main level 60+ and a Trust permit from any starting city.

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Monberaux · Upper Jeuno (G-10). Inside the infirmary. Finish his original introduction if it is offered first. Accept A Doctor’s Promise.
  2. Go to Batallia Downs (H-6), sapling field. Summon your Trusts before examining ???. Choose Begin challenge.
  3. Defeat Cratebreaker within 15 minutes. It scales with your level, minimum 62. Stay alive, within 50 yalms and on the same main job used to start the fight.
  4. Return to Monberaux · Upper Jeuno (G-10). Inside the infirmary. Collect your Trust reward.

Reward

Permanent Trust spell: Monberaux. Learned directly, once per character. After acquisition, the companion can be used on your other jobs under normal Trust rules.

Retry

Losing does not erase acceptance. Return to the same ??? once the previous encounter clears and try again.
