Walkthrough

  • Trade Odasel a Chameleon Diamond to complete the quest.
  • Chameleon Diamonds will drop from all of the Lufaise Meadows Gigas

Game Description

Client: Odasel (Gems by Kshama, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

Odasel wants to find out which of the three beauties in the gem store has feelings for him. According to a passing Elvaan girl, a jewel known as the "Chameleon Diamond" could help solve his dilemma...
