This quest does not show up on your quest log.

Walkthrough

  • Nenne can be found in the third house in Watchdog Alley.
  • Speak with her and you will learn of her illness.
  • On the top floor of the first house in Watchdog Alley, there is a diary. Read all three pages of the diary to receive a hint on how to cure this disease. If you do not get the option to read all 3 pages, you may not have enough fame.
  • Speak with Amaura in the second house in Watchdog Alley. She knows how to make a medicine for the cough, but needs Thyme Moss to brew it.
  • Thyme Moss (key item) can be found by clicking on the ??? at (F-9) in Davoi, the Orc stronghold. To get there you must enter the river at (H-8) and travel west through the river to get to the land at (D-9).
  • Return to Amaura and she will give you the Cough Syrup (key item).
  • Take the syrup to Nenne, who will give you a Scroll of Treasure (key item) as a reward.
  • The map will lead you to a signpost at (H-6) in East Ronfaure. Click on this to receive 3000g.

Game Description

Client: Amaura (Watchdog Alley, Southern San d'Oria)
Summary:

Bring Amaura some thyme moss, and she'll concoct a brew with curative powers.
