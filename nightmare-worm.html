Notes
  • Spawns randomly at 1 of 5 locations in the 2nd floor of Dynamis - Tavnazia; remains underground until it aggros.
  • Killing this monster and Nightmare Antlion re-enables support jobs in Dynamis - Tavnazia.
  • Special Moves:
    • Earth-based Magic: Stonega IV, Break, Stone V, Quake, Rasp, and Stoneskin
    • Draw-In: Always used before a TP move; draws in the person with the most hate and anyone in his alliance regardless of their position in the zone.
    • Sound Vacuum: AoE Silence
    • Tremors: Heavy AoE damage and DEX down
