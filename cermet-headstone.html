Cermet Headstone

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

These headstones are used during Zilart Mission 5: Headstone Pilgrimage, as well as a few simple quests that can be done after starting the mission. There are 7 headstones in all - one for each element, except dark:

  • Fire: Yuhtunga Jungle L-6 (through Ifrit's Cauldron)
  • Earth: Western Altepa Desert H-9 (through Quicksand Caves)
  • Water: La Theine Plateau G-11 (through Ordelle's Caves)
  • Wind: Cape Teriggan H-5
  • Ice: Cloister of Frost
  • Lightning: Behemoth's Dominion G-9
  • Light: The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah I-7

All 7 are involved in: Zilart Mission 5: Headstone Pilgrimage. The Wind headstone is used in Wandering Souls. Fire is used in Wrath of the Opo-opos. And Light is used in Soul Searching.
