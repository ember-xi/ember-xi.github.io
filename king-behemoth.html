Notes:

  • After 3 days from his last spawn, King Behemoth becomes a Lottery Spawn from Behemoths.
  • His regular attacks have an additional effect of Stun.
  • Is usually kited around the open part of the zone.
  • Either Pixie Earring or Defending Ring will drop 100% of the time; Defending Ring being the much rarer drop.
  • King Behemoth will rage after 60 minutes.
  • Special Attacks: Meteor
