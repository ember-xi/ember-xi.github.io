Final Heaven

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Hand-to-Hand Weapon Skill

  • Skill Level: N/A
  • Description: Additional Effect: Temporarily enhances Subtle Blow effect.
  • +10 Subtle Blow for a short duration after using the weapon skill.
  • Available only when equipped with the Relic Weapons Caestus (Dynamis use only) or Spharai.
  • Since these Relic Weapons are only available to Monks, only Monks may use this Weapon Skill.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Light / Fusion
  • Modifiers: VIT:60%

Notes

Final Heaven seems to be a popular final move with Hand-to-Hand users in the Final Fantasy Series; Zell from FFVIII uses this as his final Limit Break, as does Tifa from FFVII.
