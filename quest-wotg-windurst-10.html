Zenith availability

Reference walkthrough. Completion is unconfirmed in the reviewed Zenith base: no dedicated quest handler or matching progression hook was found in the inspected zone/NPC files. A missing interaction may reflect unfinished content.

Before you begin

  • Complete Sins of the Mothers and wait until the next Vana’diel day.
  • Arrange present-day Magicite access: Crest of Davoi, Silver bell, Coruscant rosary and Black matinee necklace from the nation Magicite route.

Starting point

Zone-entry cutscene from West Sarutabaruta [S] · Windurst Waters [S]

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Windurst Waters [S] from West Sarutabaruta [S]. Collect the three shards at the present-day Magicites.
  2. Optistone: pass Davoi’s Wall of Dark Arts (G-7) into Monastic Cavern and examine the Magicite.
  3. Aurastone: take Beadeaux’s H-7 tunnel into Qulun Dome at I-7. Pass the sealed door using the bell, rosary and necklace, then examine the Magicite.
  4. Orastone: in Castle Oztroja, operate the I-8 lever and avoid the falling-floor trap. Continue through G-7’s courtyard and I-7’s northern corridor, then light the H-9 torch to pass the Brass Door into the Altar Room. Examine its Magicite.
  5. Speak to Velda-Galda (K-9) in Windurst Waters [S], then exit into West Sarutabaruta [S] for another scene.
  6. Examine the Windurstian Bulwark (J-8) for a scene. Prepare, then examine it again to start the defense.
  7. Defeat the successive Yagudo Parivir, Giganotaur, Kindred Incantor and Blurry Eyes waves. Romaa Mihgo and at least one Protective Ward must survive; prioritize enemies attacking them.
  8. After victory, examine the bulwark again before zoning. Enter Windurst Waters [S] for the reward.

Battle and progress notes

  • Leaving before the post-battle bulwark scene can force a repeat.
  • After failure, leave and re-enter West Sarutabaruta [S] before retrying.

Completion and reward

Cobra Staff; Moon’s Companion title.

What this opens

  • Windurst route prerequisite for continuing Fate in Haze.
  • Manifest Destiny, after Darkness Descends.
