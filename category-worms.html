General Information

  • System: Amorphs
  • Job: Black Mage
  • Can cast any earth based spell (e.g. Stoneskin, Rasp, Stonega) and Bind.
  • Weaknesses: Wind, Light
  • Resistances: Dark
  • Immune:

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Full-Force Blow: Single target Knockback
Tremors: AOE earth damage and DEX Down
Gastric Bomb: Single target damage and Attack Down. Long range
Sound Vacuum: Single target Silence
Sandspin: AOE earth damage and Accuracy Down
MP Absorption: Single target MP Drain
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
