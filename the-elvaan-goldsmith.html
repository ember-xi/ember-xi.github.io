The Elvaan Goldsmith

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Michea - Bastok Markets F-10
  Requirements:
   Bastok Reputation < (less than) 2
  Items Needed:   Copper Ingot x 1
  Title:   None
  Repeatable:   Yes, see below
  Reward:   180 gil
     
  Previous Quest:   None.
  Next Quest:   Father Figure

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Michea; she's a goldsmith, but not in the guild, and she needs a copper ingot for her work. She can't get it from the guild since she's not a member.
  • Obtain a Copper Ingot and trade it to her to complete the quest.

Notes

  • The quest is repeatable, but capped at Bastok reputation 2. You can zone and repeat it. If you have completed The Elvaan Goldsmith and have attained Bastok reputation 2, speaking to Michea begins Father Figure.

Game Description

Client: Michea (House, Bastok Markets)
Summary:

Help Michea, a free goldsmith, complete her order by bringing her a copper ingot.
