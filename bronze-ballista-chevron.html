Bronze Ballista Chevron

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description
"Presented only to those who have displayed excellence on the Ballista battlefield. Bronze Ballista chevron must be returned to a Herald after set period indicated in official Ballista rulebook."
Notes
You will get this key item when you are 4th - 10th at Ballista Ranking. With this chevron you are the owner of the title Bronze Balli☆star for one earth week.
