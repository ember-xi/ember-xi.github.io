Zoredonite Ring

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-56 ring for all jobs: MP +20 and INT +1.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Trading
  3. Sources

How to obtain

  1. Ride the Manaclipper.
  2. Defeat Zoredonite when it appears on deck. The ring is a possible drop.

Trading

Rare/Exclusive: obtain your own drop; it cannot be purchased on the Auction House.
