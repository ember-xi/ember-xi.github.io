Statistics

Tarut card: the Fool
Tarutaru use these cards for
divination.
When upright, the Fool symbolizes
"freedom." When reversed, "escape."

Stackable: 12

Other Uses

Used in Quest: Collect Tarut Cards

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs

How to Obtain

Cannot be sold on the Auction House or delivered, but can be traded and bazaared.
 

Quests

  • Collect Tarut Cards
