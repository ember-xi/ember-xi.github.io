Notes:

  • Spawns for Promathia Mission 1-3 The Mothercrystals
  • This is the final boss for Promyvion - Holla
  • Uses the following attacks:
Shadow Spread: Sleepga/Cursega/Blindga; Poison Potions highly recommended to counter this. Oddly, if everyone around it is asleep, it doesn't seem to attack for around 10 seconds or so.
Negative Whirl: Medium-High AoE damage, strips shadows?
Winds of Promyvion: Erases a single debuff from itself
Trinary Tap: Absorbs up to 3 buffs from a single target
Trinary Absorption: Absorbs a large amount of HP (~200-300) from a single target; cannot be blocked with shadows. Spams around 20%; you must have Hysteroanima to counter this.
