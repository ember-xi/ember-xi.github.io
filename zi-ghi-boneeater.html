Zi'Ghi Boneeater

From Zenith XI Wiki

A Paladin Quadav notorious monster in Palborough Mines, around level 15–16.

Contents
  1. Find and defeat
  2. Notable loot
  3. Sources

Find and defeat

  1. Reach the third floor of Palborough Mines and the east side of the H-shaped room around H-7/H-8.
  2. Defeat the Veteran Quadav in the Onyx/Veteran/Greater Quadav group. This is a lottery spawn; killing the placeholder does not guarantee the NM. The traditional minimum window is one hour after the previous defeat.
  3. Expect Banish and Invincible. Save physical weapon skills until Invincible ends; isolate it from nearby Quadav.

Notable loot

Braveheart and Coarse Gauntlets.
