Walkthrough

  1. Obtain the Silver Bell from Aldo during Magicite. Speak to Sattal-Mansal in the same headquarters; his conversation accepts both Beadeaux requests.
  2. Defeat De’Vyu Headhunter on the upper level of Beadeaux around (I-9) and collect one Quadav Charm.
  3. Return to Sattal-Mansal and trade only the Quadav Charm for the Coruscant Rosary. Complete the second request as well before travelling to the sealed Magicite door.

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Sattal-Mansal (Lower Jeuno) after you talked to the Tenshodo leader while you are on the Magicite mission.
  • He will asked you for a Quadav Charm and in return he will give you a Coruscant Rosary.
  • The Quadav Charm is found in Beadeaux dropped off De'Vyu Headhunter.

Game Description

Client: Sattal-Mansal (Tenshodo, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

Bring back a Quadav charm and Sattal-Mansal will trade a coruscant rosary for it. Only then can you enter the depths of Beadeaux.
