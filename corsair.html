Job Overview

Eden-specific rule (not a Zenith adjustment):

No Distance Correction on ranged attacks.

Available after completing the Luck of the Draw quest. Descendants of the dauntles pirates that once scourged the seas of the Near East, Corsairs rely on the “Hexagun,” a multi-barreled revolver, and elaborate luck-based abilities to alter the stakes of battle.
