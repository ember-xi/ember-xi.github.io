Dalzakk

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: NPC

Location: Cut scenes only

Notes

  • Foster father to Michea and student to Oggbi.
  • If you begin the game in Bastok he gives you your Adventurer Coupon and directs you to your first choice of tasks (serving Bastok, helping people, etc...), pointing you to The Elvaan Goldsmith quest if you choose to help people.
  • Hints that the 3-1 Bastok mission may be a test for something more important when it's over.
  • See also: Great Figures of the Crystal War, Vol. II

Involved in Missions

  • Bastok Mission 1-3: Fetichism
  • Bastok Mission 3-1: The Four Musketeers
