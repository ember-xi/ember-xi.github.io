Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 11: Imperial Schemes.
  • One vial of Jody's Acid for each character progressing.

Starting point

Pyopyoroon · Nashmau · H-7

Walkthrough

  1. Obtain one vial of Jody's Acid from Ameretat in Bhaflau Thickets or Wajaom Woodlands, or obtain the tradable item from another player/Auction House.
  2. Take the ship from Whitegate to Nashmau and speak with Pyopyoroon at H-7.
  3. After his opening scene, trade exactly one vial of Jody's Acid and finish the conversation.

Completion and reward

Vial of Spectral Scent key item.

What this opens

  • ToAU 13: Lost Kingdom
