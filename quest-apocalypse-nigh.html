Before you begin

  • Complete Shadows of the Departed; leave Ru’Lude Gardens and wait until the next Vana’diel day.
  • Have access to Sea, the Grand Palace of Hu’Xzoi, the Garden of Ru’Hmet and the Empyreal Paradox from CoP.
  • Keep an inventory slot free for your earring.

Starting point

Esha’ntarl (palace entrance scene) · Ru'Lude Gardens · H-6

Era note: The reviewed Zenith modules list enabled custom/zenith only. The pinned base scripts therefore supply the waits described here; optional era modules that restore JST-midnight waits were not enabled in that snapshot. Live installation may differ.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Re-enter Ru’Lude Gardens and approach the palace audience area for Esha’ntarl’s new scene.
  2. Enter Sealion’s Den for Prishe’s scene. Then examine the Gate of the Gods in the Grand Palace of Hu’Xzoi, leading into The Garden of Ru’Hmet; the native quest advances at this door interaction.
  3. Reach the Empyreal Paradox. Examine Transcendental Radiance for the scene, then again to select Apocalypse Nigh.
  4. Defeat Eald’narche and Kam’lanaut together. A common approach is controlling Kam’lanaut while concentrating damage on Eald’narche; avoid matching Kam’lanaut’s elemental blade with elemental damage.
  5. Finish the victory scene and speak directly to Aldo in Lower Jeuno’s Tenshodo.
  6. After the next Vana’diel day following the victory, speak to Gilgamesh in Norg and select Static, Magnetic, Hollow or Ethereal Earring. If an earlier visit asked you to return later, leave and re-enter Norg.

Battle and progress notes

  • The pinned native battlefield allows Trusts, up to six participants and 30 minutes, with the server maximum level as its cap. This is a code check, not a completed Zenith solo test.
  • The pinned Aldo step checks current-nation Rank 5 or higher; Horizon documents Rank 6. The native wait is a Vana’diel day rather than Horizon’s JP midnight.
  • The earring is a choice of one. This conversation also advances the two expansion logs to The Last Verse.

Completion and reward

One of Static Earring, Magnetic Earring, Hollow Earring or Ethereal Earring; title: Breaker of the Chains.

What this opens

  • The Last Verse in both RoZ and CoP

Walkthrough

After completing Shadows of the Departed, you must wait until after midnight in Japan to activate this quest.
  • Enter the palace in Ru'Lude Gardens for another cutscene with Esha'ntarl. You must zone out/in after completing Shadows of the Departed.
  • Zone into Sealion's Den for a cutscene.
  • Zone into The Garden of Ru'Hmet for a cutscene.
  • Go to the Empyreal Paradox and check the Transcendental Radiance for a cutscene.
  • Check the Transcendental Radiance again to enter the BC against Eald'narche and Kam'lanaut (6 people, 30 min).
  • Because this is considered a quest, Experience Points ARE lost in this BC (similar to Divine Might)

Battle

  • Kam'lanaut is a tougher version of the boss from Zilart Mission 8.
    • He casts various debuff spells: Silencega, Slowga (overwrites Haste), Graviga, Dispelga
    • He is Susceptible to Gravity, but it is very difficult to land without Elemental Seal.
    • Special attacks:
      • Light Blade: Single-target high damage. Damage is reduced by kiting.
      • Earth Blade, Water Blade, Wind Blade, Fire Blade, Frost Blade, Lightning Blade: En-spell effects (~60 damage per hit).
        • When this effect is on, nukes of the matching element heal him.
      • Great Wheel: AoE damage (~350) and Hate Reset, wipes Utsusemi.
    • When given TP Kam'lanaut can triple attack TP attacks in a row giving very little breathing space for healers, meaning he can do 3 Great Wheels in a row killing any Melee in range
  • Eald'narche is a tougher version of the stage 2 boss from Zilart Mission 16.
    • He teleports around the arena.
    • He is highly evasive. Sushi and/or Madrigal strongly recommended.
    • He casts Bindga, Sleepga II, and all tier III -ga nukes.
    • He is very strong vs. magical damage.
    • Special attacks:
      • Vortex: AoE low damage (~150) and Terror
      • Stellar Burst: AoE low damage (~100) and Silence
      • Omega Javelin: Single target low damage (~150) and Petrification
  • Strategy tips: Kite Kam'lanaut initially and go all-out to kill Eald'narche.
    • Stunning Eald'narche's nukes is key. A RDM/DRK can chainspell stun Eald'narche while someone kites Kam'lanaut and the rest of the team focuses on Eald'narche. Stunners should stay back to avoid Terror. A BLU is an extremely good choice as well because Headbutt has a much shorter recast (be sure to use the Magus Jubbah, as well as accuracy equipment and sushi, to make sure the stun effect from Headbutt lands). BLU spells can also be spammed to quickly take down Eald'Narche.
    • Erase Slowga from a NIN tank ASAP.

Afterwards

  • Talk to Aldo in Tenshodo guild for a cutscene. If you have switched nations, you must be Rank 5 or higher to receive this cutscene.
  • Wait until Japan midnight and talk to Gilgamesh in Norg for a cutscene to complete the quest.

Rewards

Upon talking to Gilgamesh, you are given your choice of four earrings as a reward. You can only choose one of the following and they are lvl 72 All Jobs.

Name Stats
Ethereal Earring HP +15, Attack +5, Evasion +5, Converts 3% of Damage Taken to MP
Hollow Earring DEX +2, Accuracy +3, Ranged Accuracy +3, Sword Enhancement Spell Damage +3
Magnetic Earring MP +20, "Conserve MP" +5, Spell Interruption Rate Down 8%, MP Recovered While Healing +1
Static Earring MND +2, Magic Defense Bonus +2, Bonus Damage Added To Magic Burst

Repeating the Quest

It is possible for this quest to be repeated. Note that the earring previously acquired from this quest must not be in your possession to proceed (You must throw your first earring out in order to get a different one).

  • Target the ??? in the Empyreal Paradox and erase all memories of the quests "Shadows of the Departed" and "Apocalypse Nigh." Complete these quests again to obtain one of the earrings listed above.

The next incarnation of the Keeper of the Apocalypse is attempting to cross over into Vana'diel. Sing the Lay of the Immortals before the fifth mothercrystal and put an end to this threat once and for all!
