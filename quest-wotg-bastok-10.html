Zenith availability

The audited server base explicitly marks the What Price Loyalty battle in Everbloom Hollow as unimplemented. The scenes and follow-up handlers exist, but this quest cannot be completed normally through that base alone. Battle and post-battle steps below document the intended route for future implementation.

Before you begin

  • Complete B-09: Beneath the Mask.
  • Reach WotG 26: Fate in Haze.

Starting point

Gentle Tiger · Bastok Markets [S] · H-6

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Gentle Tiger at H-6 to receive the Sack of Victuals.
  2. Deliver it by speaking to Roderich at E-11 in North Gustaberg [S]. Return to Gentle Tiger for the following scene.
  3. Enter Xarcabard [S] to receive the Commander’s Endorsement.
  4. Examine the Forbidding Portal in the northwest of I-7 for a scene. In the complete reference route, examine it again to enter What Price Loyalty in Everbloom Hollow.
  5. Defeat Klara. Establish a tank quickly, keep her enfeebled, and reserve healing for her sword attacks. Later in the fight, Temblor Blade adds area petrification and disrupts enemy attention; keep a healer able to use Stona outside the area.
  6. After victory, finish the Xarcabard [S] exit scene and return to Gentle Tiger in Bastok Markets [S] for the Fourth Staff.

Battle and progress notes

  • Reference retry: Gentle Tiger replaces a lost Commander’s Endorsement without an added game-day wait.
  • This battle is a 30-minute reference encounter. Current retail level-99 and Trust shortcuts are not a verified plan for Zenith’s level-75 setting.

Completion and reward

Intended: Fourth Staff.

What this opens

  • The Bastok route’s requirement for continuing WotG 26: Fate in Haze
  • B-11: The Truth Lies Hid, after reaching Adieu, Lilisette
