Camp Travel Rules

From Zenith XI Wiki

Zenith Atlas provides free camp travel from level 10 through 75.

Contents
  1. Before travelling
  2. Training
  3. Known hazards

Before travelling

  • Choose Recommended for my level or Browse levels 10-75. You must meet the minimum level of the selected camp.
  • Travel is blocked during combat or aggro.
  • You receive one minute of Sneak, Invisible and Deodorize on arrival. These do not protect against every detection method.
  • Valkurm routes 14–20 and 20–24 use the native outpost teleport point after Travel and Quest Clarity v1.0. Qufim retains its existing staging arrival. Higher camps may require walking from the staging book.
  • Sky requires The Gate of the Gods access and a previous visit. Atlas does not bypass the story.
  • Return to a starting city takes you to one of the three city Guides for free.

Training

Select the stated page at the original Field Manual or Grounds Tome. Atlas does not start training automatically. Use Check before fighting; not every target awards EXP all the way to the top of a suggested range.

Known hazards

Avoid Qufim giants and night undead, Garlaige floor holes, Guivre in Kuftal, and level-90+ areas in Crawlers’ Nest. Manticores near the Cape Teriggan book are too strong for the lower end of its camp range.
