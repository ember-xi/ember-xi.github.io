Iron Chainmail Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A nice looking set of armor for the heavier melee and tanking types. Consists mainly of chainmail over padding, with shoulder pads and strips around the waist for extra protection and to obscure gaps between mail. Overall color scheme is blue mixed with grey. For information on how to obtain this armor set, click the name of each piece to view their page. The high quality version of this set is the Iron Chainmail +1 Set.
