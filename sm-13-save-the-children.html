Save the Children (San d'Oria 1-3)

← 1-2 Bat Hunt · San d'Oria Missions · 2-1 The Rescue Drill →

San d'Oria Mission 1-3
MissionSave the Children
TypeRepeatable
Start NPCGate Guard (Endracion or Ambrotien in Southern San d'Oria; Grilau in Northern San d'Oria)
LocationGhelsba Outpost
RequirementsRank 1 · 1-2 complete
Items NeededNone
RewardsRank 2 · 1,000 gil · Title: Fodderchief Flayer

Mission 1-3 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 1-2 Bat Hunt.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission from a Gate Guard.
  2. Go to Ghelsba Outpost (from West Ronfaure). Kill Orcs until you get the Orcish Hut Key (key item, drops from Orcish Fodder / Grappler).
  3. Examine the Hut Door at the hut (H-8). Cutscene.
  4. Return to a Gate Guard for Rank 2.
Mechanics: The key is a chance drop from any Orc in the Outpost. Orcs are level 8-12; three Trusts are enough.
