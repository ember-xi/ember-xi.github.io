Before you begin

  • Complete ACP 5.
  • Sneak/Invisibility supplies and a route through both floors recommended.

Starting point

Seed Afterglows · Fei'Yin

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

Walkthrough

  1. Search both floors of Fei'Yin for the nine differently colored Seed Afterglows. Examine all nine within 30 minutes; their positions can move, so use the route maps and check nearby rooms.
  2. The required colors are red, orange, yellow, green, cerulean, blue, golden, silver and white. Keep track of colors received in your key items.
  3. At the reward choice, select the Mark of Seed to continue the story. The Azure key is an alternative reward and does not replace the battlefield entry item.
  4. If the timer expires, leave Fei'Yin and re-enter to restart the search. Take the Mark of Seed to the Burning Circle in Qu'Bia Arena.

Completion and reward

Mark of Seed for the next battlefield.
