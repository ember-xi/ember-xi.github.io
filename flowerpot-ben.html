Flowerpot Ben

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Familiar summoned by using the Beastmaster's Call Beast ability.

Familiar Jug Level Available Pet Level Cap Ready abilities
Flowerpot Ben (HQ Mandragora) Rich Humus 51 63
  • Head Butt: - Single target damage with Knockback (1 Charge)
  • Scream: - AoE effect MND down (1 Charge)
  • Dream Flower: - AoE Sleep (2 Charges)
  • Wild Oats - Single target VIT down (1 Charge)
  • Leaf Dagger - Single target damage, possible Poison (1 Charge)


Cultural Background

Both Flowerpot Bill and Flowerpot Ben are references to Bill and Ben, The Flower Pot Men, a classic BBC children's television program from the 1950s and 1960s.
