Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Grants a powerful resistance to enfeebling magic.
  • Obtained: Paladin Level 75
  • Recast Time: 20:00
  • Duration: 1:00

Notes/Description

  • This Job Ability can only be learned by using 3 Merit Points in category two.
  • Grants one minute of enfeeble resistance.
  • Does not grant resistance to the elemental resistance down effects of ninjutsu spells.
  • Also blocks status ailments from monster abilities (e.g. Radiant Breath will not silence and slow)
  • Initially, the recast timer is at 20:00. Every Merit Point upgrade will reduce the recast timer by 2:30.
  • Blocks all status effects except for Terror and Death (it will, however, block the Doom status effect which can be dealt by the Mortal Ray weaponskill from mobs in the Tauri family). This includes any enfeeble that can be removed by any -na spell (such as Poison, Paralyze and Silence), and any enfeeble that can be removed by the White Mage spell Erase. It has also been known to block Charm (Status Effect) from monsters such as Vrtra and Jailer of Justice.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Fealty" <me>
