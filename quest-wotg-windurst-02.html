Before you begin

  • Complete The Tigress Stirs.

Starting point

Dhea Prandoleh · Windurst Waters [S] · H-10

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Dhea Prandoleh.
  2. Enter Fort Karugo-Narugo [S] and speak to Rotih Moalghett (I-8, inner/Fivespires map). She stands on the upper ledge east of the central Spiritwell. Enter the fort through Cavalry Gate (F-8), or lower the magical barrier using the Phosphorous Ward (I-9).
  3. On the outside map, examine the ??? at I-9 for a cutscene. Prepare, then examine it again to spawn War Lynx.
  4. Defeat War Lynx and examine the ??? again before leaving.
  5. Return to Dhea Prandoleh for completion.

Battle and progress notes

  • The reviewed handler requires you to change zones after completing this quest.

Completion and reward

Star Globe; Ajido-Marujido’s Minder title.

What this opens

  • Windurst route prerequisite for finishing Back to the Beginning and reaching Cait Sith.
  • Knot Quite There, after Back to the Beginning.
