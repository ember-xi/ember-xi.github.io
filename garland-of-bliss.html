Garland of Bliss

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Staff weapon skill

  • Skill level: N/A
  • Description: Lowers target's defense. Duration of effect varies with TP. Nirvana: Aftermath effect varies with TP.
    • Reduces enemy's defense by 12.5%.
    • Defense down duration:
      • 100%TP : 60 seconds
      • 200%TP : 90 seconds
      • 300%TP : 120 seconds
  • Available only after completing the Unlocking a Myth (Summoner) quest.

Properties

  • Element: Light
  • Skillchain Properties: File:Fusion-Icon.gifFusion/File:Reverberation-Icon.gifReverberation
  • Modifiers: MND: 40%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
2.00 2.00 2.00

Notes

  • Damage is increased by INT, Magic Attack Bonus, and WS modifiers.
  • Magic Attack Bonus modifier is direct % modifier on base damage. Magic Attack Bonus + 7% items will modify WS damage by 7%.
