Other Uses

Used in Quests: Relic Bow: Yoichinoyumi
Used to Make: Wolver Bow

Speak to Switchstix in Castle Zvahl Baileys at I-8. Trade him the weapon and the Behemoth Horn, Lancewood Lumber & Carbon Fiber.

Speak to him again and he will ask for the payment fee. Trade him M. Silverpiece x 4. After this, wait 1 RL day and speak to him again to receive the Wolver Bow.


NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
