Pearlsack

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A Pearlsack holds Linkshell Pearls.

Only the Linkshell holder can upgrade members' Pearls to Pearlsacks. A member of a Linkshell with a pearlsack is commonly called a "sackholder."

A Pearlsack holder can:

  • Create Linkpearls, in order to recruit new members.
  • Break other players' Linkpearls, with /breaklinkshell.
  • Change the Linkshell welcome message with /lsmes, as long as the Linkshell holder has not set "/lsmes level lh".
