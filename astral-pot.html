Astral Pot

Statistics

Astral Pot
All Races
Enchantment:
Enhances avatar magical attack
Lv. 61 SMN

<20/20 0:30/[30:00, 0:30]>

  • Has no correlation to TP. Can be tested yourself by using Nether Blast.
  • Seems to be purely Magic Attack Bonus and does stack with Dream Shroud.
  • Targets the avatar, not yourself, so no status effect icon will appear.
  • Must have an avatar summoned in order to be used.
  • The effect lasts the duration the avatar remains out. Once the avatar is released, the effect will wear off.
  • There may be a limit to the length of the buff (about 10 minutes).Verification Needed
