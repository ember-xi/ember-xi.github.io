Notes

  • Venessa in Ru'Lude Gardens (H-10) will offer you the first three ENMs in Promyvion if you have completed the first cut-scenes to start the Chains of Promathia missions.
  • If you select the 'Spire of Holla' she will give you the Censer of Abandonment key item.
  • Upon entering the Spire of Holla, you will have the option of entering the ENM battle at the battlefield.
  • The Cogitator NM must be defeated to win this ENM. It seems to have approximately 2000 HP.
  • The Cogitator NM is the only enemy present at the beginning of the battle, and subsequently spawns the Weeper NMs as its health decreases.
    • It seems able to spawn an unlimited number of Weepers, but only a maximum of 3 at a time will be present.
  • Anima work in this ENM just as they do in the Promyvion boss fights.
  • It is possible for more than one of the same type of Cluster to drop.
