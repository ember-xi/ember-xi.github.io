Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Delivers an area attack. Additional effect: "Stun." Damage varies with TP.

Description

Spell cost: 77 MP
Ecosystem: Beastmen
Type: Physical (Blunt)
Blue Magic Points: 4
Stat Bonus: MND+2
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 69

Timers

Casting Time: 1 seconds
Recast Time: 28.5 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Tail Slap as part of a combo to create Store TP.

Can be paired with the following spell to create Store TP combo.

  • Sickle Slash

Notes

  • Blunt damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: STR: 20%, VIT: 50%.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Skillchain Element: Water (can open Impaction and Induration; can close Reverberation and Fragmentation).

How to Obtain

Can only be learned from Merrow type monsters.

Acquired From

Mob Family: Lamia
Minimum Blue Mage Level To Acquire: 60 with Magus Jubbah equipped, 63 without Magus Jubbah
