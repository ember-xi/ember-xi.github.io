Airship Pass for Kazham

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description: This pass allows you to ride the airship between Kazham and Jeuno.

Obtained: Trade a Giddeus Chest Key, a Palborough Chest Key, and a Ghelsba Chest Key, or pay 148,000g to Guddal in Port Jeuno.

Notes: Needed to board the Airship traveling between Kazham and Jeuno.
