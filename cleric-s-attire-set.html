Cleric's Attire Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

An armor set from the Dynamis zones, which the player may be rewarded with for defeating the enemies within. The Cleric's set looks similar to the regular Healer's Set, except for a more elaborate design on the front, and a tan coloring on the back, however unlike most other AFv2's the color differences between each set are not as readily apparent.

The armor boasts an overall high boost to the wearer's MP pool, along with a large increase to MND and an added Refresh effect. Bonuses to Healing Magic Skill, Enhancing Magic Skill, and the spell Regen, along with an increase to the potency of Elemental Resistance spells and a resistance to Silence all further help the Whm to keep their comrades alive and healthy in the most desperate of situations, with enhanced MP recovery reducing downtime. Negative Enmity traits on the pieces help keep hate away from the Whm should they overcure. An Enfeebling Magic Skill enhancement is also present, as well as a bonus to the Banish spell when used against Undead mobs.
