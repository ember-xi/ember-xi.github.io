Additional level-75 reference


The Ashu Talif is a famous pirate ship, originally believed by The Empire of Aht Urhgan to have been long destroyed.

It is also a battlefield for a couple of Assault missions linked to quests. This area is only accessible if the Treasures of Aht Urhgan expansion is installed and The Road to Aht Urhgan quest is completed. You cannot obtain mission orders from the commissions agency for The Ashu Talif.

A cage of Cutter Fireflies is given to all participating mercenaries who enter. They can be used to return to the cutter found in the Arrapago Reef.
