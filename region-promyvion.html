Emptiness-an insidious phenomenon that has begun to gnaw away at the land. This menace, first disregarded as a distant threat, is drawing heroes of every nation into a race to uncover its true nature.

Is this "emptiness" a tear in the fabric of Vana'diel? Or is it the birth of an entirely new world?
