Pop Conditions

  • Pops between E-11 and F-9 on Map 2 of Arrapago Reef
    • The map location seems to be incorrectly circled on the Notorious Monster Map of Arrapago Reef. These coordinates (between E-11 and F-9) are correct.
  • Pops randomly when a character dies in the appropriate area.
  • Lamia No.19 will spawn directly on top of the defeated player's corpse, if unclaimed will despawn 1 minute later.
  • Pop will be accompanied by a chat message saying "You feel an eerie sense of forboding." You can receive this message upon dying anywhere in the Reef; however, Lamia No.19 will only pop along with it if the character dies in the right area. This message will only occur when Lamia No.19's pop window is open.
  • Seems to have a repop time of between 1 and 3 hours.

Notes

  • Lamia No.19 summons 2 Lamia's Skeletons when it is aggroed. They are both warrior types. They do not repop once they are killed.
  • Lamia No.19 casts from a distance. It will only melee if the person with hate moves in to melee range.
  • Lamia No.19 is susceptible sleep from RDM. Verification Needed
  • Killed fairly effortlessly by 6 people if pets are killed first. Kite, hold or sleep NM and take the pets out one by one.
  • SMN using Titan Bloodpact Mountain Buster will take out a pet in 3-4 hits. More than one SMN is ideal.
  • BST or BRD/BST can charm nearby leeches and hold the NM with little effort.
