Statistics

Giant scale
This faintly glowing scale is thought
to have been taken from the body of
a colossal wyrm.

Other Uses

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

Notes: Used to enter certain Spatial Displacements in Riverne - Site #A01 & Riverne - Site #B01.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
