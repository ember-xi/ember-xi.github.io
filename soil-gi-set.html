Soil Gi Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Another oriental gi set with the same appearance as the original Kenpogi, but this time it has other bonuses. Additional earth and lightning resistance provides more protection from elemental damage of those types as well as increased resistance to slow and stun effects. It is crafted largely of linen and grass. The high quality version of this set is the Soil Gi +1 Set.

Number of Pieces: 5

Cost to store: 400 gil (Soil gi claim slip)
