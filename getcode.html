!getcode

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Typing !getcode in-game generates a 7 digit code which can be used to activate a new content ID. Anyone can use this command provided they meet the minimum requirements which are:

  • 5 Days Playtime
  • Level 15 or higher

These codes are typically distributed amongst private messages on Eden's discord to prevent malicious use of them. Players are encouraged to distribute these codes to others who may wish to join the server.
