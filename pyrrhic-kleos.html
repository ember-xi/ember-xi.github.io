Pyrrhic Kleos

From Zenith XI Wiki

Dancer’s Mythic dagger weapon skill: a four-hit physical attack.

Contents
  1. How to unlock
  2. Use
  3. Sources

How to unlock

Complete Unlocking a Myth (Dancer) using Main Gauche. You can then use it as Dancer with a suitable dagger; owning Terpsichore is not required for the permanent unlock.

Use

Skillchain properties: Distortion and Scission. When used with the awakened Terpsichore, it also activates the weapon’s Aftermath. Exact damage modifiers follow Zenith’s installed weapon-skill rules, so modern retail multipliers are not assumed here.
