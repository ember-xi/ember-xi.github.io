Magic Shield

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This effect provides 100% resistance to all spells, including Dispel and Magic Finale. Both single-target and -ga spells are resisted.

It does not protect against magic damage from non-spell sources, such as Astral Flow or Skillchains.

Damage Over Time spells that are already on the target before it gains this effect continue to do damage until they wear off normally.

This effect can be caused by:

  • Magic Barrier (Ahriman TP ability)
  • Spectral Barrier (Magic Pot TP ability)
  • Certain Notorious Monsters, including Omega.

Players can gain this effect by using:

  • Fool's Drink
  • Spiritual Incense
  • Rampart
