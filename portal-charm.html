Portal Charm

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description: A key that allows you to enter the Sealed Portal beneath the central tower of the Horutoto Ruins by yourself.

Obtained: Only obtainable after completion of Windurst Mission 3-2: Written in the Stars.
