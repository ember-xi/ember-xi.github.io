Before you begin

  • Complete Return to Delkfutt’s Tower.

Starting point

Oaken Door · Norg · K-8

Walkthrough

  1. Return to Norg and examine the Oaken Door at K-8.
  2. Watch the conversation about the ruins north of The Sanctuary of Zi’Tah. Confirm that The Temple of Desolation is active.

Completion and reward

Story progression; no separate item reward.
