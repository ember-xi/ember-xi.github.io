How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Quests

  • After flagging the Against All Odds quest, speak with Leleroon, and choose the Gants.
  • Head to D-7 in the southern map of Windurst Waters. Check the front door of the old miser's house for a Cutscene.
  • He will ask you for the following materials:
    • Gold Thread
    • Karakul Leather
    • Red Grass Cloth
    • Wamoura Silk
  • After trading those items to the door, you are asked for 4 Imperial Mythril Pieces as a fee.
  • Wait until Midnight JST and check the door again to obtain the Gants.
  • (Note: You must first complete your current COR AF item with Leleroon, before you can start the next one. I.E: You have to finish making your Gants before you can start the Corsair's Bottes Quest.)
