Gold Stud

From Zenith XI Wiki

Exclusive Apollyon material required for every Dancer artifact +1 upgrade.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. How to use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Open Limbus item chests in Apollyon. One verified source is the fifth-floor reward chest in NW Apollyon. Clear the route and open its item reward chest; Gold Stud is a possible drop rather than a guaranteed reward. Other Apollyon routes can also award it.

How to use

Every Dancer AF +1 upgrade takes one Gold Stud together with one Filet Lace, the base artifact piece, its crafted material and Ancient Beastcoins. Follow Dancer Artifact and Relic Upgrades for each recipe and Sagheera’s hand-in procedure. It is an exclusive material and is not available on the Auction House.
