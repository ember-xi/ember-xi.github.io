Before you start

Scholar level 50+; Downward Helix complete; zone and wait for the next Vana'diel day.

Steps

  1. Speak to Erlene in The Eldieme Necropolis (S) (J-8) on Scholar.
  2. Check Indescript Markings at Pashhow Marshlands (S) (E-11) for the Unaddressed Sealed Letter.
  3. Return to Erlene for a scene. Read the letter in Temporary Key Items, then speak again for a Porting Magic Transcript.
  4. Enter the cave opposite the waterfall on the (E-10)/(F-10) boundary in Grauberg (S). Check the sparkling light, then the Indescript Markings.
  5. The party leader must have the active quest and entry key item. The retail battlefield in Ruhotz Silvermines allows up to six players and 30 minutes; you may fight on another job.
  6. Fight Ulbrecht until he concedes at roughly 20% HP. Save defensive resources for his Tabula Rasa around half HP. Return to Erlene for the headpiece.
  7. After a failed attempt, trade one Vellum to Erlene for a replacement transcript.

Reward

Scholar's Mortarboard; Summa Cum Laude title.

Zenith availability

Retail walkthrough supplied for reference. The reviewed base revision does not contain a complete Seeing Blood-red / Ulbrecht instance handler. Completion and Trust entry on Zenith are not confirmed; do not spend retry materials until the encounter is verified.
