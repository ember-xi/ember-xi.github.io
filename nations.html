Nations

You start in one of three nations. The choice does not change your character's power; it changes the story (missions) and which jobs get free starting armor. You can change allegiance later at Rank 1.

NationRacesStarting gear jobsHome PointsNotes
San d'OriaElvaanWAR · MNK · WHM · BLM · RDM · THFSouthern / Northern / PortKingdom of knights. Start in Ronfaure and Ghelsba. Shiva quest in Northern San d'Oria.
BastokHume · GalkaWAR · MNK · WHM · BLM · RDM · THFMarkets / Mines / PortRepublic of mines. Start in Gustaberg and Palborough. Titan quest from Ferrol in Port Bastok.
WindurstTarutaru · MithraWAR · MNK · WHM · BLM · RDM · THFWoods / Waters / Walls / PortFederation of mages. Start in Sarutabaruta and Giddeus. Fenrir at the Full Moon Fountain.
Mechanics: The job you pick at character creation gets its nation's starting armor for free. Rank 1 → 10 comes from nation missions; Rank 5 gives the Airship Pass.
