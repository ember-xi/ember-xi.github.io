Nations

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

There are a number of different nations in Vana'diel. As a player, you can align yourself with one of the following:

  1. The Kingdom of San d'Oria
  2. The Republic of Bastok
  3. The Federation of Windurst

In addition to the three nations above, there are two additional nations/nation-states which can be visited in Vana'diel.

  1. The Grand Duchy of Jeuno
  2. The Empire of Aht Urghan
