Blade Bash

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Deliver an attack that can stun the target and occasionally cause Plague.
  • Obtained: Samurai Level 75
  • Recast Time: 15:00 (-2:30 with each additional merit)
  • Duration: Instant

Notes/Description

  • As of the 10/19/06 update, Blade Bash is no longer a Ranged Ability. Its range is now the same as Weapon Bash and Shield Bash.
  • Blade Bash does not deal damage and you will not gain TP by using it.
  • Stun and Plague activation rate is 90-100%Verification Needed
  • Each additional merit lowers its recast by 2:30, with a maximum of 3 merits for a 10:00 recast time.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Blade Bash" <t>
