Zenith availability

Reference walkthrough: the pinned server core contains story scenes but leaves the Dungeons and Dancers maze instance in Everbloom Hollow unfinished or unconnected. This is not confirmed playable on Zenith.

Before you begin

  • Complete A Sanguinary Prelude.
  • Carry the Aroma bug awarded by A Sanguinary Prelude.

Starting point

Regal Pawprints · Beaucedine Glacier [S] · G-9

Walkthrough

  1. Examine the high-ground pawprints at (G-9), west of the Campaign tower, for a scene; examine again to enter Everbloom Hollow with the Aroma bug.
  2. Head northwest, keeping the right wall. Use Ratstail Explosives on walls at (H/I-9) and (H-8). Kill the nearby Goblin Reaver and check its dropped ??? repeatedly to refill explosives.
  3. Open the southeast wall (I-9), go northeast to (J-7), inspect the dead end for the Firesand explanation, then inspect again to place Firesand.
  4. Return through (H-8) and activate the green switch at (H/I-7). Return to (J-7) and pass into the second map carrying at least two explosives.
  5. Open the southeast wall (G-8/9), then the east wall (I-9). Continue southeast to the blue exit ??? before the 30-minute limit.

Battle and progress notes

  • On failure, request another Aroma bug at Naoi's pawprints (H-10).
  • The pinned core has an incomplete main-story battle at mission 14; later story handlers do not establish that this chapter is currently reachable on Zenith.

Completion and reward

Umbra bug.
