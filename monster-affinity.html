Monster Affinity

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Were you looking for Beast Affinity?

Monster Affinity is a hidden trait gained by wearing certain pieces of equipment. It causes the wearer to react to intimidation effects as if he is a member of a particular Monster Family. This means that when a player attempts an action on monsters or players with an appropriate Killer effect, there is a chance they will be intimidated. The following pieces of equipment are known to give monster affinity:

  • Crimson Scale Mail Set/Blood Scale Mail Set (Dragon Affinity)
