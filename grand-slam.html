Grand Slam

Delivers an area attack. Damage varies with TP.

Description

Ecosystem: Beastmen
Type: Physical (Blunt)
Blue Magic Points: 2
Stat Bonus: INT+1

Timers

Casting Time: 1 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Grand Slam as part of a combo to create Defense Bonus.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Defense Bonus combo.

  • Terror Touch
  • Saline Coat
  • Vertical Cleave
