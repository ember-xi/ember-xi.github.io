Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Dagger weapon skill

  • Skill level: 125 (Thief, Red Mage, Bard, Ranger, and Ninja exclusive.)
  • This weapon skill is ranged and can be used from a distance (Up to 15'), much like Mistral Axe.
  • Delivers an area of effect wind elemental attack. Damage varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: Wind
  • Skillchain properties: Detonation/Impaction
  • Modifiers: DEX:30% ; INT:25%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.00 2.375 2.875

Levels

  • Cyclone is only natively available to Thieves, Red Mages, Bards, Rangers, and Ninjas. While other jobs can obtain the required 125 Dagger skill, they must use one of the aforementioned jobs as their support job in order to gain access to Cyclone.
