Notes:

  • Spawns with the pirates, on the ferry route from Mhaura to Selbina; only spawns during the Pirate event on the ferry when the middle pirate during the cut-scene is wearing a Vermillion Cloak.
  • Can spawn on any boat ride with pirate event including during the day time.
  • Killable by: 4+ characters level 70+, 2 characters level 75, solo by a WHM/NIN at level 75
  • Special Attacks: High damage AOE; casts Ice Spikes, Waterga III, Thunder III, Thundaga II, Stun, Flood, Freeze, and Burst.
