Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Puts all enemies within range to sleep.

Description

Spell cost: 38 MP
Ecosystem: Plantoids
Type: Magical (Dark)
Blue Magic Points: 4
Stat Bonus: HP-5, MP+5
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 24

Timers

Casting Time: 3 seconds
Recast Time: 90 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Soporific as part of a combo to create Clear Mind.

Pair with one of the following spells to create the Clear Mind combo.

  • Poison Breath
  • Venom Shell
  • Awful Eye
  • Filamented Hold
  • Maelstrom
  • Feather Tickle
  • Sandspray
  • Warm-Up
  • Lowing
  • Mind Blast


Note- Unlike other job traits, for each pair of spells you add from this list, you gain the next tier of Clear Mind, up to a maximum of Clear Mind IV (Clear Mind V is not attainable).

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Compression, Gravitation, and Darkness.
