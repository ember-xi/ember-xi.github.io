Before you begin

  • Complete CoP 5-2.
  • All three branch completion flags, not just one selected dialogue path.

Starting point

Cid · Metalworks · H-8

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Cid. Complete all three branch guides: Tenzen's Pursuit of Paradise, Ulmia's Where Messengers Gather, and Louverance's Past Sins.
  2. You may choose any order or progress branches together. Choosing a name in Cid's dialogue does not lock the other branches.
  3. Report to Cid at the end of each branch. Finishing the last branch and its Cid scene advances to 6-1.

Required paths

  • 5-3T — The Pursuit of Paradise — Tenzen’s Path
  • 5-3U — Where Messengers Gather — Ulmia’s Path
  • 5-3L — Past Sins — Louverance’s Path

Battle and progress notes

  • Shopping checklist: a Gold Key per character for Louverance; Snow Lilies for the convenient Mine Shaft transport; optional Blackened Muddy Siredon and Shu’Meyo Salt for Ulmia’s battles.

Completion and reward

Story progression.

Walkthrough

Note
This is a long mission with two dungeons, an uncapped NM, and three BC fights.
  • Head to the Metalworks and talk to Cid (H-8) to get started.
  • It doesn't matter what path you choose when you talk to Cid; you don't have to start that fork first.
  • You can be working on more than one section at a time.
  • Complete all three paths below to complete the mission.

Game Description

Mission Orders
To break the Wyrmking's pact, you must locate the Keeper of the Apocalypse as quickly as possible. To do that you must first learn more of Al'Taieu, as well as find Prishe and the mysterious boy.
