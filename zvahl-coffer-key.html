Zvahl Coffer Key

From Zenith XI Wiki

Opens a treasure coffer in Castle Zvahl Baileys.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Defeat Abyssal Demon, Arch Demon, Blood Demon, Doom Demon, Ahriman in Castle Zvahl Baileys. These are the higher-level demon and eye enemies, not the low-level chest-key targets.

Use

Trade the key to a Treasure Coffer. Rewards depend on active quests and job conditions. Uses include Fighter's Cuisses, Rogue's Vest, Borghertz's Harmonious Hands and Under Oath. Activate the relevant artifact quest and follow its instructions before opening the coffer.
