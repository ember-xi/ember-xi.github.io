Lanolin Cube

Statistics

Lanolin cube
The Goblins make these cubes out of
fat harvested from sheep wool.
It is the main ingredient in Goblin soap.

Stackable: 12

Other Uses

Used in Quests: Candle-making

NPC Sell Price: 680~697 gil

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
