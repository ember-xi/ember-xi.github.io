Statistics

Earthen Hakutaku Eye
An earthen eyeball of the legendary
beast, the Hakutaku. It will supposedly
revive when mixed with blood.

Other Uses

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

Used in Recipes

  • Hakutaku Eye Cluster - Alchemy (84)

How to Obtain

Cannot be sold on the Auction House or delivered, but can be traded and bazaared.
