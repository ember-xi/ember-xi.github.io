Jettons

Zandjarl will convert Imperial currency into Jettons

  • 1 Imperial bronze piece = 1 Jetton
  • 1 Imperial silver piece = 10 Jettons
  • 1 Imperial mythril piece = 30 Jettons
  • 1 Imperial gold piece = 200 Jettons

Wares

Zandjarl will allow people without sufficient Jetton to view his wares in that category.

  • 12 blank soul plates

  • Dartorgor's Coif
