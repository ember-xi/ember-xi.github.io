Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Increases maximum Hit Points.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • The increase in HP from Max HP boost is the same when monk is set as support job.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Askar Korazin Set (Set: Max HP Boost) 10%
  • Puppetry Tobe (Automaton: Max HP Boost) 1.50%
  • Adaman Sainti (Automaton: Max HP Boost) 0.75%
  • Gem Sainti (Automaton: Max HP Boost) 1.50%
