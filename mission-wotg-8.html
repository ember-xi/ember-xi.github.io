Before you begin

  • Complete Purple, The New Black.
  • For the gated story scene: complete In a Haze of Glory, Fire in the Hole, or A Feast for Gnats.

Starting point

Lion Springs Tavern door · Southern San d'Oria [S] · K-6

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Complete one next national pair: Storm on the Horizon → Fire in the Hole (Bastok); Perils of the Griffon → In a Haze of Glory (San d'Oria); or When One Man Is Not Enough → A Feast for Gnats (Windurst).
  2. Examine the Lion Springs Tavern door at (K-6) in Southern San d'Oria [S] to continue the main story.
