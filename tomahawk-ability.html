Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Tomahawk has an identical name. If an internal link incorrectly referred you to this page, please change the link to point directly to Tomahawk.

Job Ability

  • Expends a throwing tomahawk to inflict a special defense down effect on an enemy.
  • Obtained: Warrior Level 75
  • Recast Time: 3 minutes
  • Duration: 30 seconds.

Notes/Description

  • "Special Defense Down" means that this ability will lower a mob's physical damage resistance, should it have any.
  • Examples of mobs to use this ability on: Slimes, Elementals, Skeletons, etc.
  • If the mob has no physical damage resistance, Tomahawk will have no effect on the mob.
  • Tomahawk has no effect on buffs that increase a mob's defense such as Protect or Cocoon.
  • Consumes one Throwing Tomahawk per use.
  • Tomahawk has no effect on Stoneskin.
  • Tomahawk will generate a considerable amount of enmity on the mob it is used on.
  • Each additional merit upgrade extends the defense down duration by 15 seconds.
  • Currently Unknown/Untested if Tomahawk will affect Phalanx in any way.Verification Needed

Macro Syntax

/ja "Tomahawk" <t>
