Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Diabolos deals dark damage and sleep to enemies within area of effect and gradually reduces their hit points.

Technicals

Avatar: Diabolos (Dark)

Blood Pact: Ward

Cost: 42 MP

Level: 29

Description

Effect Duration: Unknown

Battle Application: Skillchain Verification Needed

Damage Type: Magic

Additional Notes: Unlike other forms of sleep, the gradual HP reduction from this attack will not cause the targets to awaken.

Macro Syntax

  • /pet "Nightmare" <t>

or

  • /ja "Nightmare" <t>
