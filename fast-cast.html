Fast Cast

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Quickens spellcasting.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Reduces both the Casting Time and the Recast Time of the spell.
    • The listed percentages are the reduction in Recast Time; double them to get the Casting Time reductions.
    • Recast Time can be reduced by no more than 50%.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Warlock's Chapeau / Warlock's Chapeau+1 (10%)
  • Warlock's Mantle (2%)
  • Duelist's Tabard / Duelist's Tabard+1 (10%)
  • Rostrum Pumps (3%)
  • Loquacious Earring (2%)
  • Homam Cosciales (5%)
  • Marduk's Jubbah (5%)
  • Marduk's Jubbah Set (10%)
  • Pi Ring (Salvage Only: ???%)

Fast Cast I Cast time -10%, recast -5%

  • Obtained: Red Mage Level 15

Fast Cast II Cast time -15%, recast -7.5%

  • Obtained: Red Mage Level 35

Fast Cast III Cast time -20%, recast -10%

  • Obtained: Red Mage Level 55
