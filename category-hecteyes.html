General Information

  • System: Amorphs
  • Job: Black Mage
  • Weaknesses:
  • Resistances: Blunt (-25% damage), Dark
  • Immune: Dark based sleep spells and additional effects

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Carthasis: Restores HP
Death Ray: Single-target darkness damage
Hex Eye: Gaze Attack paralysis
Petrogaze: Gaze Attack petrification
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
