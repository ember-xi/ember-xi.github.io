Walkthrough

  • Talk to Quelveuiat and agree both times, to start the quest.
  • Head north and exit the Tavnazian Safehold into Lufaise Meadows, taking the south exit at I-6.
  • Cross over the bridge and through the pass, then head south to H-10 where you will find a lookout platform. Clear any Orcs (including the Stonelauncher) from around the platform so they do not link.
  • Click the ??? at the top of the platform to spawn Splinterspine Grukjuk, a Ranger Orc NM.
  • Kill Splinterspine Grukjuk.
  • Return to Tavnazian Safehold and talk to Quelveuiat to complete the quest.
  • The additional portion of the 'after-quest' which allows solo door access in Sacrarium does not exist on Eden.

Game Description

Client: Quelveuiat (Altar of the Dawn, Tavnazia)
Summary:

Your initiation into the Tavnazian order of knights requires you to assess the situation from the lookout platform in the south of Lufaise Meadows.
