Zombie

From Zenith XI Wiki

Night-spawning Skeletons, using Warrior or Black Mage behavior depending on the area.

Contents
  1. Where to find
  2. Drops and fighting
  3. Sources

Where to find

AreaLevel
Jugner Forest16–26
Pashhow Marshlands16–26
Meriphataud Mountains16–26
Buburimu Peninsula18–22
Pashhow Marshlands (S)67–69

They appear between 20:00 and 04:00 Vana’diel time. No spawn item is required.

Drops and fighting

Bone Chip is both a drop and a steal item. Zombies detect sound and low HP. Avoid approaching while injured; deal with the Black Mage version’s spells and use Fire or Light damage when suitable.
