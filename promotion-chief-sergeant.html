Promotion: Chief Sergeant

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Start NPC: Abquhbah - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-10) upstairs
Requirements:
 Acquire 25 Mercenary Rank points as Sergeant Major.
Items Needed:
Title: Chief Sergeant
Repeatable: No
Reward: CS Wildcat Badge
Imperial Mythril Piece
Imperial Gold Piece

Previous Quest: Promotion: Sergeant Major
Next Quest: Promotion: Second Lieutenant

Walkthrough

White Gate:

  • Begin the quest by speaking with Abquhbah for a cutscene.
  • Next speak with the weapon shop owner, Hagakoff, who works a stall at (K-10) for another cutscene.
  • Return to Abquhbah for another cutscene.
  • Return to Hagakoff for yet another cutscene.
    • At this point Hagakoff just repeats "Macici said that we can find scourshroom in the Aydeewa Subterrane."
  • You must now acquire a minimum of 2 scourshroom sprouts.

Aydeewa Subterrane:

  • Enter Aydeewa Subterrane (map 2) from Wajaom Woodlands (I-10).
  • Walk to the large room at (I-9) where you will find a pair of mushroom patches.
  • Check either mushroom patch to harvest a mature scourshroom, then check the other patch to plant that mature scourshroom.
    • There are sound-aggro Defoliators and Diremites in this room. You should only need Sneak when harvesting. There are also chigoe-type mobs here that aggro through Sneak; you may need Invisible when walking through.
  • To ensure you harvest at least two scourshroom sprouts in one night, travel to (E-7) where you will find another pair of mushroom patches in the tunnel. Repeat the harvest/plant process.
    • There is a third pair in the large room near (G-10) on Map 3, but it is not strictly required.
  • Zone and wait until the game day changes; then return to each mushroom patches for harvesting sprouts.
    • It's best to pick during the morning hours, for a higher yield; you will typically get two young scourshrooms when picking between 4:00 and 6:00.
    • You will need to return to Abquhbah before you can pick/plant another mature scourshroom.
      • Note: You are still able to pick/plant another mature scourshroom at (E-7) without having to go back to Abquhbah.
  • Return to Abquhbah with the scourshroom sprouts.

Return to White Gate:

  • Speaking with Abquhbah triggers a cutscene.
    • Ensure you have at least one open inventory space prior to initiating the final cutscene, or you will be required to repeat the cutscene to complete the quest.
    • If Abquhbah says the rust has completely disappeared, then you can choose to finish the quest, though you can also choose to do more cleaning.
  • After the rust is gone and you've chosen to stop cleaning, you will receive your promotion and a bonus of one Imperial Mythril Piece.
    • If you cleaned it two times (the minimum), you will receive an added bonus of one Imperial Mythril Piece.
    • If you cleaned it five times (the maximum), you will receive an added bonus of one Imperial Gold Piece.
