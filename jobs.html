Jobs

Your Main Job is the job that gains experience. A Support Job (sub) is a second job at half your main level; it gives you its abilities, traits and spells up to that level.

The "Standard Jobs" below are available to every new character. The "Advanced Jobs" are unlocked by completing a quest after level 30.

Standard Jobs

There are six standard jobs available to all starting players.

Warrior

Masters of weapons. Warriors stand in front and protect the party. The easiest first job, and the best support job for melee.

Monk

Fighters who use their fists. Fast punches, high HP and the best Hand-to-Hand skill. Very good solo from level 1.

Thief

Sneak Attack, Trick Attack and Treasure Hunter for more drops. Works best with a Trust tank in front.

White Mage

The main healer: Cure, Regen, Erase and Raise. With Trusts on EmberXI a White Mage can play solo too.

Black Mage

The highest magic damage in the game: elemental nukes, Sleep, Warp and Escape. Magic Burst is +35 on EmberXI.

Red Mage

A fighter-mage: sword plus white and black magic, Refresh, Haste and Dispel. The best solo job of the era.

Advanced Jobs

By completing certain quests after reaching level 30, the door to many other jobs opens.

Paladin

The knight with white magic: the best tank of the era. Cure, Provoke, Sentinel and Invincible.

Dark Knight

High damage with Scythe and Great Sword, plus black magic, Absorb spells and Stun. Uses its own HP for power.

Beastmaster

Charms monsters and fights beside them, or calls Jug Pets. The best solo job even without Trusts.

Bard

Songs that speed up the party, restore MP and put enemies to sleep. The backbone of every era party.

Ranger

Ranged damage with Bow and Gun, Barrage and Sharpshot. Needs arrows or bullets from the Auction House.

Samurai

Master of Weapon Skills: fast TP with Meditate and Hasso, and Third Eye to dodge. The king of skillchains.

Ninja

Utsusemi shadows absorb hits; Ninjutsu gives elemental damage and debuffs. The best evasion tank of the era.

Dragoon

Lance and Wyvern. Jump, High Jump and Super Jump for damage and enmity control. The Wyvern heals and fights with you.

Summoner

Makes pacts with Avatars: Carbuncle, then the six, then Fenrir. The avatar fights for you with Blood Pacts. Support, damage and healing.

Blue Mage

Learns monster spells (Blue Magic) by killing monsters after they use the move. Sword plus a flexible spell set.

Corsair

A pirate with a gun and Phantom Rolls: luck-based buffs for the party and Hexagun damage.

Puppetmaster

Controls an Automaton that can be a tank, healer or mage depending on its Attachments.

Dancer

Fights with daggers and turns TP into cures, buffs and debuffs with Waltzes, Sambas and Steps. Heals itself, so it is very good solo.

Scholar

A flexible mage with two stances: Light Arts (white magic) and Dark Arts (black magic), plus Stratagems for faster or cheaper spells.
