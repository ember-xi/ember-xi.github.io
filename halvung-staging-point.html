Halvung Staging Point

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Halvung Staging Point is the Staging Point for Assault missions in Lebros Cavern.
It is located at Mount Zhayolm K-6.

Directions

To get there from Al Zahbi:

  • Exit Al Zahbi to Bhaflau Thickets
  • Enter a tunnel in F-8 for the zone to Halvung.
  • Escape to Mount Zhayolm (L-7).
    • If you don't have Escape, head SW to (K-7) and then north, across the bridge, and south to the zone in (J-7).
  • In Mount Zhayolm, head North to L-6, West to K-7, and finally North to K-6 and the Heavy Iron Gate.
