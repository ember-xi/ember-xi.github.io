Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Weakens defense of enemies within range.

Description

Spell cost: 32 MP
Ecosystem: Demon
Type: Magical (Wind)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: AGI+2
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 50

Timers

Casting Time: 2 seconds
Recast Time: 20 seconds

Notes

  • Enemy defense is weakened by 26/256 (about 10%, similar to Dia II effect). This effect will override defense-modifying effects such as Cocoon (and vice versa, the effect of Defense Down is overwritten by Cocoon) and the effect of Acid Bolts.
  • Area of effect is centered around caster.
  • It may or may not be possible to learn Frightful Roar from the Taurus mobs in Phomiuna Aqueducts, the mechanics of the level cap induce a 30 Blue Magic Skill difference between the skill cap of level 40 (Blue Magic skill capped at 123) and the skill cap of level 50 (Blue magic skill capped at 153), thus not exceeding the restricted 30 level difference of Blue Magic Skill allowed between the current level of skill and the skill cap of the level when the spell may be equipped. No conclusive evidence is available that supports the theory that this spell is possible to learn in the Aqueducts.

Combines With...

You can use Frightful Roar as part of a combo to create Auto Refresh.

Can be paired with some of the following spells to create the Auto Refresh trait. Please refer to the Auto Refresh section of the Blue Mage Job Traits page for details.

  • Stinking Gas
  • Cold Wave
  • Self-Destruct
  • Light of Penance
  • Voracious Trunk
  • Actinic Burst
  • Plasma Charge

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Detonation, Fragmentation, and Light.
