Yoran-Oran's Manor

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Surrounding Heavens Tower in Windurst Walls are five manors belonging to the retired former Ministers of Windurst. Yoran-Oran, the former Rhinostery Minister, lives on the western bank of the swampy area.

Location: Windurst Walls (E-5)

Building Type: House
