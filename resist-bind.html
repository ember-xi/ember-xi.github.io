Resist Bind

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Increases resistance against bind.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Each upgrade of this trait further increases character's resistance.
    • This trait greatly shortens the duration of bind type status effects.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Arete Del Sol
  • Elegant Ribbon
  • Flawless Ribbon
  • Genin Mantle
