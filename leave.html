Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Sets your pet free.
  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 35
  • Recast Time: 10 seconds
  • Duration: N/A

Notes/Description

Obtained at Level 35 by the beastmaster, Leave sends any pet away, aggro-free. It simply tells your pet that its services are no longer required.

Due to a December 2005 update involving the problems with Monster Player Killing (MPK), a released pet will despawn if it is outside of its normal spawn radius.

Macro Syntax

  • /pet "Leave" <me>
