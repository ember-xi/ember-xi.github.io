File:Yawn.jpg

Puts all enemies within range to sleep.

Description

Spell cost: 55 MP
Ecosystem: Birds
Type: Magical (Light)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: CHR+1, HP+5
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 64

Timers

Casting Time: 3 seconds
Recast Time: 60 seconds

Notes

  • Functions as a "gaze" or line of sight attack, meaning mobs must be facing your general direction for the sleep to take effect.

Combines With...

Pollen, Magic Fruit, or Wild Carrot to create Resist Sleep.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Transfixion, Fusion, Light
