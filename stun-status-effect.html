Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

File:Stun (Status Effect).png Stun is a harmful status effect that stuns the player until it wears off or is removed. Afflicted characters will be unable to act for the short duration, and any spell or ability being cast as the stun takes effect is interrupted.

How to remove the effect

  • The White Magic spell Erase will remove this effect, but its duration is usually too short to require others to remove the effect.

How the effect is inflicted
