Before you begin

  • Complete Back to the Beginning.
  • To finish this mission: complete Wrath of the Griffon, Burden of Suspicion, or A Manifest Problem.

Starting point

Zone entrance · Southern San d'Oria [S]

Walkthrough

  1. Advance one past nation: Light in the Darkness → Burden of Suspicion (Bastok); Boy and the Beast → Wrath of the Griffon (San d'Oria); or Knot Quite There → A Manifest Problem (Windurst).
  2. Walk into Southern San d'Oria [S] from East Ronfaure [S] for the mission scene. Teleporting directly into the city does not meet this zone-line trigger.

Battle and progress notes

  • National quests are in Quests → Crystal War, not the main WotG mission list.

Completion and reward

Story progression.
