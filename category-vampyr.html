Vampyr
With only a handful of vampyrs known to exist, these undead are shrouded in mystery. They appear demonic, but they do not exhibit the same behavior as demons. Rather, vampyrs are even more dangerous, with the ability to charm entire parties and doom their prey. A few vampyrs are located in secluded areas of the Near East; some lurk the depths of the Bostaunieux Oubliette; others were known to work for the Shadow Lord in the past.
