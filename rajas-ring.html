Statistics

Rajas Ring
[Ring] All Races
STR +2~5 DEX +2~5
"Store TP" +5
"Subtle Blow" +5
Lv. 30 All Jobs

Notes

  • The Strength and Dexterity stats increase by 1 for every 15 levels, from +2 at level 30 up to +5 at level 75.
Level 30: STR+2, DEX+2.
Level 45: STR+3, DEX+3.
Level 60: STR+4, DEX+4.
Level 75: STR+5, DEX+5.

Other Uses

Guild Points Value: None

Used in Quests: None

Resale Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, bazaared, or delivered.
 

Mission

  • One of three rings that can be chosen as the final reward upon completion of Promathia Mission 8-4 "Dawn". (Choice of Rajas Ring, Sattva Ring, or Tamas Ring.)
