Get your spells

  • Buy Storm and Helix scrolls from Layton beside Erlene in The Eldieme Necropolis (S).
  • Earn Klimaform through On Sabbatical.
  • Other spells use ordinary scrolls. Open each spell page for its NPC, quest or monster source; buying a scroll at the Auction House also works.
  • Zenith may retain later base-server learning levels. Use the in-game scroll requirement if it differs; this historical table is not a claim that every modern adjustment has been disabled.
