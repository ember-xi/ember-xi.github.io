Walkthrough

  • Talk to Mighty Fist.
  • Trade two Darksteel Ore to Mighty Fist to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Mighty Fist (Darksteel Forge, Metalworks)
Summary:

Mighty Fist's forge is running low on darksteel ore, and he has asked you bring him two chunks of it. The Gusgen Mines is known to yield darksteel, but some claim to have dug a few chunks from the Zeruhn Mines.
