Lakeside Minuet

From Zenith XI Wiki

Unlock Dancer. Start with any main job at level 30 or higher.

Contents
  1. Requirements
  2. Steps
  3. Reward
  4. Sources

Requirements

Level 30+, Wings of the Goddess access, and a route into Jugner Forest (S). No item purchase is required.

Steps

  1. Speak to Laila at the dancer group in Upper Jeuno (G-7). Choose “I surely do.”, then “Never!” to accept.
  2. Speak to Rhea Myuliah beside Laila.
  3. Visit Valderotaux inside Lion Springs Tavern, Southern San d'Oria (K-6), and complete the dance conversation.
  4. Return to Rhea Myuliah in Upper Jeuno.
  5. Enter Jugner Forest (S) and examine Glowing Pebbles by Lake Mechieume (I-5). Receive the Stardust Pebble key item. The target name matters; the ground need not visibly glow.
  6. Return to Laila in Upper Jeuno to unlock Dancer.

Reward

Dancer becomes selectable at your Mog House. Title: Troupe Brilioth Dancer.
