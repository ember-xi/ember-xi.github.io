Kaela

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Adventurer's Assistant

Location: Metalworks (I-8)

Notes

Offers Information on the Constellations:

That star is here...no, it's in that constellation there... Hm? What am I doing? Oh, I'm trying to match real stars with the ones on this map, but it's not as easy as it looks. Say, you're an adventurer, aren't you? You must've been to a lot of places, seen many different skies... Would you like to take a look?

"Yes."

  • [Shows a map of the constellations] I knew you'd be interested! This map was drawn by the famous Windurstian astronomer, Professor Lago-Charago! See that constellation to the south? That's Bastok's guardian, Titan! But not many people know that now. It's been a long time since we've celebrated Titan's Eve. The yellow star at its center is Daemdalus, or "Titan's left foot." It used to be a lot brighter in the old days, and soldiers returning from battle followed it back to Bastok. I'm glad to have met someone else who likes looking at the stars. Maybe we can talk about them more sometime.

"No."

  • Oh, I-I'm sorry. Of course you wouldn't be interested in stars... Goodbye!

Involved in Quests

  • Lure of the Wildcat (Bastok)
