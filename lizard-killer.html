Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Gives an edge against lizard enemies.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Occasionally causes lizard type enemies to be intimidated.
    • How often a mob is intimidated is based on Charisma.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Beast Earring
  • Beast Trousers / Beast Trousers +1
  • Flawless Ribbon
  • Panther Mask / Panther Mask +1
  • Tamer's Ring (as a latent effect)
  • Tatami Shield

Food that Enhances this Trait

  • Bison Steak / Marbled Steak
  • Goblin Chocolate / Hobgoblin Chocolate

Lizard Killer

  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 40
  • Obtained: Blue Mage Level 20
