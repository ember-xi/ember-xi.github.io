Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Deals wind damage to enemies within a fan-shaped area originating from the caster. Additional effect: Blindness.

Description

Spell cost: 116
Ecosystem: Demons
Type: Magical (Wind)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: AGI+1
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 54

Timers

Casting Time: 5.25 seconds
Recast Time: 33.75 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Hecatomb Wave as part of a combo to create Max MP Boost.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Max MP Boost combo.

  • Metallic Body
  • Mysterious Light

Notes

  • The formula for this spell appears to be (Current HP / 4) + (your level / 1.5). For example, if you are level 54 and have 1000 HP, your damage would be 250+36, or 286. This is subject to Resists, however. Using it against Dragons or Magic Bursting it does not boost damage; it only affects resist rates.
  • Is NOT affected by Magic Attack Bonus.
  • Ignores Magic Defense Bonus, but is subject to direct magic resistance (from Proto-Omega for example).

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Detonation, Fragmentation, Light
