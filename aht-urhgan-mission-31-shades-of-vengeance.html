Walkthrough

  • Watch the cutscene at the Cutter that triggered the end of the last mission.
  • Once the cutscene is over you will receive the Periqia Assault Area Entry Permit Key Item.
  • Return to the Dvucca Isle Staging Point in Caedarva Mire.
  • Enter Periqia by using the Periqia Assault Area Entry Permit just received.
    • You DO lose exp in this.
    • Uses Periqia Map 6.
    • Even though this is an uncapped fight, like all Assault missions you will lose buffs when entering.
    • Kill 3-4 of the 10 K23H1-LAMIAE (Located at H-9) for the next cutscene to automatically start.
    • They are grouped tightly and link very easily, so SMN or /SMN is useful for Carbuncle pulling.
    • They inflict intense poison with every melee and ranged attack.
    • They have a cone shaped AOE poison attack that does around 80 HP per tic which overwrites the weaker poison of Poison Potions.
    • They are susceptible to sleep effects but are resistant to Lullaby.
    • The Lamia can be sac pulled.
    • Be prepared to Poisona/Antidote yourself/party members after exiting the cutscenes as the intense poisoning will last outside of the assault.
    • If the mission is a failure, your Periqia Assault Area Entry Permit will be exhausted; you can receive another permit from the Immortal Nahshib stationed at the Staging Point. If you fail again after using the second permit, Nahshib will not issue you another permit until the next game day.
    • If you already completed the mission but are helping someone out, you do not require a new permit. The person with the permit has to start the Assault mission, the other party members will be pulled in automatically.
  • As the cutscene begins, you will be on Mission 32.
