Cast Time

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Cast Time is the amount of time, in real seconds, it takes for a spell to be cast or an ability to be used.

Affected By:

  • General:
    • Fast Cast
    • Chainspell
  • Bard songs:
    • Song Spellcasting Time equipment
    • Bard Merit Point Abilities:
      • Nightingale (-50%)
      • Troubadour (+50%)
  • Cure spells:
    • Cure Clogs (-15%)
    • White Mage Merit Points (-20% possible)
