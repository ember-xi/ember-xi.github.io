Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This page has been made for the convenience of being able to choose your Adventuring Fellow's personality. Under "Quest Dialogue" it shows what your Fellow would say between the end of Unlisted Qualities and the beginning of Mirror, Mirror. In the event you do not wish to spoil the cut scenes that follow, do not read the "Quest Dialogue" section. "Let's chat" is a feature at Rendezvous Points after your NPC is available. Clicking on this will have your Fellow say some random stuff, depending on the zone- in this case Jeuno. Outdoors Dialogue consists of what your Fellow says after you have called him or her, either in battle (denoted with parantheses "()") or talking directly (denoted with brackets "{}"). The personality choices for Hume ♀, Elvaan ♀, Tarutaru ♀, and Mithra are Sisterly, Lively, Agreeable, Naive, Serious, and Domineering. The personality choices for Hume ♂, Elvaan ♂, Tarutaru ♂, and Galka are Sullen, Passionate, Calm and Collected, Serious, Childish, and Suave.
