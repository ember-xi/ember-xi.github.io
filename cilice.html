Cilice

From Zenith XI Wiki

Crafted cloth needed to upgrade the Dancer relic body to Etoile Casaque +1.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Dancer use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Buy from the Auction House under Materials → Clothcraft, or craft one with an Earth Crystal, one Wool Thread and two Dhalmel Hair. The reviewed server recipe uses Clothcraft 85. Dhalmel Hair is a separate material; Dhalmel Hide is not a substitute.

Dancer use

Trade one Cilice with Etoile Casaque, Etoile Casaque -1 and 30 Ordelle Bronzepiece to Sagheera in Port Jeuno. Collect Etoile Casaque +1 after the next Vana’diel midnight. See Dancer Artifact and Relic Upgrades for access requirements.
