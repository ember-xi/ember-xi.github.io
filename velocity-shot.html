Velocity Shot

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Increases attack power and speed of ranged attacks, while reducing attack power and speed of melee attacks.
  • This ability can be activated all the time because of the recast and duration time.
  • Obtained: Ranger Level 45
  • Recast Time: 5 Minutes
  • Duration: 5 Minutes
  • Ranged Attack: + 15%
  • Attack: - 15%
  • Attack Delay: +15%
  • Ranged Attack Delay: -15%
