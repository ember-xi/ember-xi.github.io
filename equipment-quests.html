Artifact progression

The Doorman begins the Warrior AF route. Continue with Phara’s original follow-up quests. Other jobs use their own original AF quest lines. These are separate from Zenith’s retired equipment chapters.

Existing equipment

Keep all equipment and augments earned before this update. Retired custom chapters no longer accept objectives or award new items. No AF quest is auto-completed and no replacement reward is granted.
