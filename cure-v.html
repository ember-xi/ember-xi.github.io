Other Information

Formula
(Healing magic skill / 3) + (MND * 1.5) + 430. Note that the effect of Cure potency enhancing items goes into effect after this. For example, with 276 Healing magic skill and 100 MND, a Cure V would do 672 without any Cure potency gear. With a Light Staff (Cure potency +10%), it would do approximately 739. It should also be noted that your numbers can be different on Lightsday/Darksday, as well as anywhere with Light/Darkness weather.
Undead
The Cure line of spells may also be used offensively against Undead mobs.
Enmity
The enmity received from Cure V is about equal to that of Cure III, despite the amount of HP that is recovered.
