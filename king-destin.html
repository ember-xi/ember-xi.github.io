King Destin

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: NPC

Location: Cutscenes

Notes

King Destin is the king of San d'Oria. His wife passed away long ago. He is the father of Prince Trion, Prince Pieuje and Princess Claidie. The King is also first cousins and good friends with Valaineral R Davilles, whom he learned most of his diplomacy and public affair skills from.

Involved in Missions

  • San d'Oria Mission 4-1: Magicite
  • Bastok Mission 4-1: Magicite
  • Windurst Mission 4-1: Magicite
  • San d'Oria Mission 5-2: The Shadow Lord
  • San d'Oria Mission 9-2: The Heir to the Light
