Walkthrough

  • Kurou-Morou asks for a crystal ball in order to tell your fortune.
  • To do so, you need to get an Ahriman Lens and dip it in a pool in the Maze of Shakhrami.
  • The Ahriman Lens is dropped by Ahrimans. This lens is Ex, and cannot be traded or purchased from the Auction House.
  • Once you have the Ahriman Lens, take it to the Maze of Shakhrami. The Rockwell is located at G-5 of the first map when entering from Tahrongi Canyon.
  • Once you reach the Rockwell, trade the Ahriman Lens to it.
  • After midnight Japan time, you can return to withdraw the Divination Sphere by clicking again on the Rockwell.
  • Trade Kurou-Morou the Divination Sphere to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Kurou-Morou (Fortunetellers, Lower Jeuno)

Create your own crystal ball to have your fortune told.
