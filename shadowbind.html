Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

fr:Entrave d'ombre

Job Ability

  • Roots enemy in place.
  • Obtained: Ranger Level 40
  • Recast Time: 5:00
  • Duration: :20, although it can be removed if the target is attacked.

Notes/Description

  • Target gains the effect of Bind.
  • Apart from a few NM's who are immune to Bind, Shadowbind cannot be resisted.
  • Cannot be removed with Erase.
  • If Hunter's Bracers are equipped, the duration of Shadowbind is extended by 25 seconds.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Shadowbind" <t>
