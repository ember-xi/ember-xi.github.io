Fire (Element)

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


File:Fire icon.JPG The element of Fire is strong against Ice, and weak to Water. Associated with Liquefaction, Fusion, and Light skillchains. Governed by the celestial avatar Ifrit.

  • Barblizzard/Barblizzara
  • Barparalyze/Barparalyzra
  • Blastbomb
  • Bomb Toss
  • Burn
  • Enfire
  • Exuviation
  • Fire, Firaga line of spells
  • Flare ancient magic

  • Katon line of spells
  • Lowing
