Telepoint

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Telepoints are simply Crystals used to travel easily around Vana'diel. There are currently six of them around the world; three of them can be found in the mainland whereas the other three are found in the Outlands.

The ones found in the mainlands are outside Crags, while the others are merely in an altar-looking teleportation circle.

Telepoints Across Vana'diel

The following is a list of all six Telepoints:

  • Mainland Telepoints

-Teleport-Holla

-Teleport-Dem

-Teleport-Mea

  • Outland Telepoints

-Teleport-Altep

-Teleport-Yhoat

-Teleport-Vahzl
