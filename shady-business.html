Shady Business

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Talib - Port Bastok (F-6)
  Requirements:
   Tenshodo Reputation 1
  Items Needed:   Zinc Ore x4
  Title:   None
  Repeatable:   Yes
  Reward:   350g

  Previous Quest:   Beauty and the Galka
  Next Quest:   None

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Talib who tells you he wants four more Zinc Ore.
  • Obtain four Zinc Ore and trade them to Talib to complete the quest and receive the gil reward.
Note
This is one of the primary quests used to raise Tenshodo fame, which is required for many quests in Norg. This quest can be repeated by continuing to trade sets of four ore to Talib; zoning is not required. It does not raise Bastok reputation when it is completed, only Tenshodo reputation.

Game Description

Client: Talib (Tenshodo Warehouse, Port Bastok)
Summary:

Talib of the Tenshodo is buying zinc. Bring him four chunks of the ore to receive gil.
