Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Lowers the defense and magical defense of enemies within range.

Description

Spell cost: 48 MP
Ecosystem: Beastmen
Type: Magical (Dark)
Blue Magic Points: 5
Stat Bonus: HP-5, MP+5
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 67

Timers

Casting Time: 3.5 seconds
Recast Time: 60 seconds

Notes

  • Effect is approximately -10% (25/256) Defense and -8 Magic Defense Bonus.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Compression, Gravitation, and Darkness.

How to Obtain

Learn this spell from Troll cannoneers (MNK, RDM, RNG, PUP).

Acquired From

Mob Family: Troll
Minimum Blue Mage Level To Acquire: 58 with Magus Jubbah equipped, 61 without Magus Jubbah
Name Level Zone
Troll Shieldbearer 72-73 Bhaflau Thickets
Troll Sabreur 71-73 Bhaflau Thickets
Troll Surveillant 71-73 Bhaflau Thickets
Woodtroll Monk 72-72 Wajaom Woodlands
Woodtroll Ranger 72-72 Wajaom Woodlands
Troll Stoneworker 71-75 Halvung

Minimum Setup

  • You will need a 75 job that can solo like Ninja or Red Mage to help.
  • Duo with PLD75 and BLU67 on Troll Shieldbearer.
  • 3 level 66 Blue Mage are capable to safely trio the trolls in Wajaom Woodlands.
