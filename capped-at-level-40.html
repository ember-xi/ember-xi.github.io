Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Level Cap is a status effect that temporarily lowers your level until it wears off or is removed. Affected characters are treated as if they were of lower level, only able to use abilities, weapons, and armor appropriate to that level. When level cap status is inflicted upon a player, all of that players positive magical effects are removed.

How to remove the effect

  • Finishing the task that set the cap upon you will remove the effect.
  • Returning to your homepoint will remove the effect.
  • Dying will sometimes remove the effect, usually when it would have worn off with time.

How the effect is inflicted

Other

  • BCNM
  • Certain Quests
  • Certain Missions
  • Entering certain zones.
  • See also Level Cap Guide for a list of content.
