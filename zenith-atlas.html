Field books

Valkurm Dunes: outpost (H-7). Qufim Island: outpost (F-6). For other camps, open the camp guide to see its book’s game-map square and route.

Use the Atlas

  1. Choose Recommended for my level or Browse levels 10–75.
  2. Read the camp’s targets and required level before travelling.
  3. Examine the original Field Manual or Grounds Tome at the destination to activate training. Atlas does not select the page for you.
