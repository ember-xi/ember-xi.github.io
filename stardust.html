Walkthrough

  • Talk to Baldric.
  • Trade a a pinch of Valkurm Sunsand to Baldric to complete the quest.
  • Valkurm Sunsand can be found in Valkurm Dunes after a sandstorm by clicking on the ??? on the overturned ship at H-9 in the Siren Sands, or can be purchased from Auction House Category: Others > Misc.

Game Description

Client: Baldric (Darksteel Forge, Metalworks)
Summary:

Baldric wants you to bring him a pinch of Valkurm Sunsand for one of his experiments. It is said to only appear during dust storms.
