A Furious Finale

From Zenith XI Wiki

Original Dancer route for raising the level limit from 70 to 75.

Contents
  1. Requirements
  2. Steps
  3. Sources

Requirements

Dancer level 66+, the prior level-limit quest completed, and Comeback Queen completed. Zone and wait until the next Vana’diel day.

Steps

  1. Talk to Laila in Upper Jeuno (G-7) on Dancer.
  2. Obtain Dancer's Testimony. Verified examples are Amber Quadav in North Gustaberg (S) and Young Quadav in Grauberg (S).
  3. Trade the testimony to Laila and accept her challenge to travel to Qu'Bia Arena.
  4. Trade the testimony to the Burning Circle. Defeat Laila within the ten-minute battle. Preserve TP for healing; use Quickstep to help land hits.
  5. After the victory, complete the exit scene and check the raised limit.

Do not repeat a level-limit unlock already cleared on another job. Follow Zenith’s current limit-break page if a custom progression route is installed. Retail Rhapsody-based Trust access is outside this 75-era route.

Zenith availability: this is the original quest route. Completion on the current Zenith server has not been confirmed; a wiki entry does not install the quest.
