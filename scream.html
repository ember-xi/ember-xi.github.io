Scream

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Reduces MND of players in area of effect.

Description

  • Family: Mandragora
  • Type: Enfeebling
  • Can be dispelled: N/A
  • Utsusemi/Blink absorb: Ignores shadows
  • Range: 15' radial
  • Notes: In Dynamis - Buburimu, the Nightmare Mandragora's Scream is enhanced to also inflict Terror.
