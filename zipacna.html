Zipacna

From Zenith XI Wiki

A roaming level-83–85 Golem NM in Sky, and a source of Gem of the North.

Contents
  1. Find and defeat
  2. Notable loot
  3. Sources

Find and defeat

  1. Complete The Hall of the Gods access requirements for Sky, then enter Ve'Lugannon Palace through the northern entrances from Ru'Aun Gardens.
  2. Search the northern route between E-3 and K-3, including the attached lower loops. Open and closed doors affect the patrol route.
  3. Zipacna is a timed spawn; classic references give a multi-hour window. It moves quickly and can use a TP attack immediately on being pulled.
  4. Keep support away from Crystal Rain and prepare for repeated Crystal Weapon attacks.

Notable loot

Gem of the North for Genbu, Oblation Abjuration, and Ushikirimaru.
