  Previous Quest:   The Old Monument

Walkthrough

  • This quest is HIDDEN, and cannot be seen in your Quest list until completed.
  • Trade a Parchment to the the Song Runes again and obtain the Poetic Parchment.
  • Head back to Lower Jeuno and trade the Poetic Parchment to Mertaire. Watch cutscene of Mertaire and Lewenhart to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Mertaire (Merry Minstrel Meadhouse, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

Show the desparate bard Mertaire the words to the song.
