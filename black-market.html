Walkthrough

  • Speak to Muzaffar, to start the quest.
  • He wants either four Northern Furs, four pieces of Eastern Pottery or four Southern Mummies.
    • Northern Furs drop from Gigas on Qufim Island and Delkfutt's Tower or may be bought from Auction House Category: Others > Misc.
    • Eastern Pottery drops from Clockwork Pods in Fei'Yin, Clockwork Pods in Garlaige Citadel or Magic Pots in Delkfutt's Tower or may be bought from Auction House Category: Others > Misc.
    • Southern Mummies drop from Liches in Western Altepa Desert or may be bought from Auction House Category: Others > Misc.
  • Trade four of any item to Muzaffar to receive a variable amount of gil.

Notes:

When passing through Customs, there is a chance that you can be caught carrying these items, and your airship pass will be revoked for a short period of time.

Game Description

Client: Muzaffar (Norg)
Summary:

Bring sets of either four northern furs, four pieces of eastern pottery, or four southern mummies for a hefty reward.
