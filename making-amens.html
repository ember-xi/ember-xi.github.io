Walkthrough

  • Minister Ajido-Marujido found out about the replacement wand that Hakkuru-Rinkuru made.
  • Hakkuru-Rinkuru is beside himself with worry, and so Kuroido-Moido in the Orastery asks you to retrieve the original broken wand.
  • The wand however, has been sent to be incinerated in Garlaige Citadel and it is your task to collect the broken wand from the furnace!
  • To get to the Incinerator, you need a Garlaige Key (Rare/Ex), which is dropped by Fallen Evacuee in Garlaige Citadel. You can find them in the smaller rooms on the first map.
  • After getting the key, proceed past the First Banishing Gate at I-9.
  • Next proceed through the Second Banishing Gate which is at G-10.
  • After you get through the 2nd Banishing Gate, go to I-7 on the map to the right to get to the Incinerator Room.
  • Speak with Mashira to get the Broken Wand (key item).
  • Return to Kuroido-Moido and Hakkuru-Rinkuru. The quest cannot be completed if you are currently on Windurst Mission 8-1.


Note:
Consider completing the quest Rubbish Day at the same time.

Game Description

Client: Kuroido-Moido (Orastery, Port Windurst)
Summary:

Hakkuru-Rinkuru's switching of Ajido-Marujido's broken wand has been discovered. It seems that the original wand had some special significance to the minister. Hurry and retrieve the original wand from the furnace in Garlaige Citadel before it's too late.
