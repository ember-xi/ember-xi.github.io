Overview

This is a category at the Auction House that contains grips for two-handed weapon users. Grips cannot be equipped if your primary weapon is not a two-handed weapon. Grips are equipped in your "Sub" slot (the same as for shields and offhand weapons).

  • Note: Not compatible with Hand-to-Hand weapons.


Grips

  • Note: Despite their names, grips can be used for any two-handed weapon type.
