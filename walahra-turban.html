How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Mini-Quest

  • Donate 1,000 Imperial Bronze Pieces to Gajaad at (J-6) in Al Zahbi in support of the city's defenses. Trade him 100 Imperial Bronze Pieces at a time and for every 100 he will give you a Walahra Water. When a total of 1,000 Imperial Bronze Pieces are traded, he will give you the Walahra Turban.
    • If Gajaad says: "I hear that Chochoroon the appraiser has been taken by the beastmen... Oh my...! If he should be forced to talk, then they might come looking for me next time!", he will not accept your trades. You will have to wait until Chochoroon is returned.
    • You can trade 20,000 Imperial Standing to Ugrihd at (G-9) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate for 20 Imperial Gold Pieces. You can then exchange the Imperial Gold Pieces for Imperial Bronze Pieces with Awaheen at (G-9) in Nashmau. The Imperial Gold Pieces will convert to 100 Imperial Mythril Pieces then to 200 Imperial Silver Pieces and finally to 1,000 Imperial Bronze Pieces.
      • A macro such as /item "[coin name]" <t> will make the trading go faster.
    • Alternatively to the above, you can just buy 1,000 Imperial Bronze Pieces from Ugrihd. It costs exactly the same, and you don't have to go to Nashmau. If you do the exchange method above, note that you can only trade one coin at a time (320 trades). You waste a significant amount of time on this, even with the macro.
    • If you throw the Walahra Turban away, you can get a new one by donating another 1,000 Imperial Bronze Pieces to Gajaad.
