How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Quests

  • After flagging the Against All Odds quest, speak with Leleroon, and choose the Frac.
  • Head to Port San d'Oria and speak with Raqtibahl in the Rusty Anchor Pub.
  • He will ask you for the following materials:
    • Gold Chain
    • Velvet Cloth
    • Red Grass Cloth
    • Sailcloth
  • After trading him those items, he will ask for 1 Imperial Gold Piece as a fee.
  • Wait until Midnight JST and speak with Raqtibahl again to obtain your Frac.
  • (Note: You must first complete your current COR AF item with Leleroon, before you can start the next one. I.E: You have to finish making your frac before you can start the Corsair's Bottes Quest.)
