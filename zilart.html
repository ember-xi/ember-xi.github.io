Rise of the Zilart (ZM1–ZM17)

Rise of the Zilart
RequirementRank 6 in any nation
GoalOpen Sky (Ru'Aun Gardens) at ZM13 and finish the story
EmberXITrusts inside every BCNM; Home Points open (Norg, Kazham, Rabao, Sky) so there is no long walking

The Zilart missions from ZM1 to ZM17: they open Sky at ZM13 and end with the fight against Eald'narche.

MissionZoneStepsEmberXI notes
ZM1 — The New FrontierNorgAfter Rank 6, enter Norg (Home Point on EmberXI). Story cutscene at the entrance; you get the Norg map.No fight.
ZM2 — Welcome t'NorgNorgTalk to Gilgamesh inside the cave (L-8).No fight.
ZM3 — Kazham's ChieftainessKazhamTalk to Jakoh Wahcondalo (her house, J-9). You get the Sacrificial Chamber Key.No fight; Kazham by Home Point.
ZM4 — The Temple of UggalepihTemple of Uggalepih → Den of Rancor1) Uggalepih Key from a Tonberry Cutter near the entrance; open the painting and take the Paintbrush of Souls. 2) Collect Unlit Lanterns from Tonberry Maledictors / Imprecators (one is enough, four is faster). 3) Reset Tonberry hate at the secret door (F-11) before entering. 4) Enter the Den of Rancor, light the lanterns in room E-5 on map 2, and the door opens for three Tonberry NMs.Everyone's Grudge is the danger: do not kill many Tonberries before the end, and let the tank open the secret door. Trusts fight with you.
ZM5 — Headstone Pilgrimage6 headstones + Sacrificial ChamberCollect the Fragments from the Headstones: Ifrit's Cauldron (Fire), Den of Rancor (Ice), Cape Teriggan (Wind), Western Altepa (Earth), Behemoth's Dominion (Lightning), Yuhtunga Jungle (Water) — some spawn an NM when touched. The last one, Light, is the BCNM "The Sacrificial Chamber" in Yhoator Jungle (Yagudo Avatar + Yagudo) with the ZM3 key.Sleep the small Yagudo with Shantotto and start with them before the Avatar.
ZM6 — Through the Quicksand CavesQuicksand Caves → Chamber of OraclesEnter from Western Altepa and cross to the last Headstone at the Ancient Vessel → BCNM "Chamber of Oracles": 3 Antica NMs.The sand drops you to the lower floor; hug the wall. Sleep two and kill one at a time.
ZM7 — The Chamber of OraclesLower Jeuno / NorgCutscenes: talk to Aldo in Lower Jeuno, then Gilgamesh.No fight.
ZM8 — Return to Delkfutt's TowerUpper Delkfutt's Tower → Stellar FulcrumClimb the tower (Qufim by Home Point) to the top floor → BCNM "Stellar Fulcrum": Kam'lanaut.He casts Silence and hits hard: bring Echo Drops and keep the tank's back away from the edge.
ZM9 — Ro'MaeveLower Jeuno / NorgTalk to Aldo, then Gilgamesh.No fight.
ZM10 — The Temple of DesolationRo'Maeve → Hall of the GodsEnter Ro'Maeve (from the Sanctuary of Zi'Tah) → Hall of the Gods at H-5 → examine the gate twice.By day the Weapons sleep; Sneak and Invisible for safety.
ZM11 — The Hall of the GodsNorg → RabaoTalk to Gilgamesh, then Maryoh Comyuja at the windmill (Rabao G-7).No fight.
ZM12 — The Mithra and the CrystalQuicksand CavesFrom Western Altepa (D-12 → C-11) enter Quicksand Caves → K-8 → door at G-8 → drop into the hole → ??? at the pillar spawns an NM (Doll) → after the kill touch the ??? again for the key item → back to Maryoh → then the Hall of the Gods gate.The NM casts; Silence it first.
ZM13 — The Gate of the GodsHall of the GodsTouch the gate: cutscene, and Sky (Ru'Aun Gardens) opens.From here you can enter Sky and start our Sky page.
ZM14 — Ark AngelsRu'Aun Gardens — 5 ShrinesFive BCNMs (Hume, Elvaan, Tarutaru, Mithra, Galka), each in its shrine, or all five at once in Divine Might.The hardest are EV (Elvaan — Spirits Within) and GK (Galka — Meikyo Shisui and high damage). Trusts fight with you.
ZM15 — The Sealed ShrineRu'Aun → Shrine of Ru'Avitau → NorgEnter the Shrine of Ru'Avitau from Ru'Aun H-9 (the mission log updates) → talk to Gilgamesh → return and enter Ru'Avitau again.No forced fight.
ZM16 — The Celestial NexusShrine of Ru'Avitau → Celestial NexusWalk to the end of the Shrine → BCNM "Celestial Nexus": Eald'narche (two phases).Phase two uses Sleepga: Poison from Monberaux protects you. Kill only Eald'narche.
ZM17 — AwakeningNorgFinal cutscene with Gilgamesh. Zilart is complete.All of Sky is open, and you can start CoP.
Mechanics: Zilart fights have no level cap: bring all five Trusts (tank, healer, BRD, BLM, DD).
