Walahra Temple

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

The Walahra Temple stands at the eastern end of Aht Urhgan Whitegate (J7/K8). Inside stands the Gordeus, an artifact constructed by Walahra and which forms the basis of the religion's philosophy.

In the FFXI storylines, an adventurer may learn about Walahra and the Gordeus in Aht Urhgan Mission 4: Knight of Gold and the missions that follow.
