Orange Ballista Chevron

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description
"Presented only to those monks who have displayed excellence on the Ballista battlefield. Orange Ballista chevron must be returned to a Herald after set period indicated in official Ballista rulebook."
Notes
You will get this key item when you are first at Ballista Ranking at the category: Monk. With this chevron you are the owner of the title Striking★Star for one earth week.
