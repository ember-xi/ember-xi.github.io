Walkthrough

  • Go to Riverne - Site #A01 and head towards Monarch Linn. On the last island (the one with all the Cloud Hippos) don't go to the gate that zones to the small island before Monarch Linn; instead, go a little further to the other distortion. On the last island, there is a Stone Monument at C-7; select that for a cutscene. At the end you will obtain the Keyitem Ornamented Scroll.
  • Go to Bastok Markets and talk with Reinberta for a cutscene.
  • Go to Northern San d'Oria and talk with Mevreauche for a cutscene.
  • Return to Riverne - Site #A01 and head back to the Stone Monument. Select it to spawn the Arcane Phantasm NM.
  • Defeat the Arcane Phantasm, a Fomor Bard NM.
  • Select the Stone Monument again for a cutscene to complete the quest.
Notes
Each time you go to the Stone Monument, you will need two copies of the giant scale that drop from the wyverns in Riverne. A total of four are needed to complete the quest, since it has to be visited twice.
