Walkthrough

  • Radeivepart asks you to bring him a Flame Degen.
  • A Flame Degen can be bought at AH, drops from Napalm, or can be crafted (Alchemy).

Game Description

Client: Radeivepart (Peace Monument, Ru'Lude Gardens)
Summary:

Prove you are worthy to receive the map of Castle Zvahl by bringing a flame degen back to Radeivepart.
