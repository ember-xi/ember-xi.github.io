Zenith availability

The route has been checked against references and available code; completion on Zenith has not yet been play-tested.

Before you begin

  • Complete ASA 4.
  • Soultrapper and Blank Soul Plates.
  • Ability to reach the assigned Sea Serpent Grotto targets.

Starting point

Andrause · Norg · I-8

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Andrause at I-8 and record the three monster names he assigns. These targets vary between characters.
  2. Obtain a Soultrapper camera and Blank Soul Plates, equip them in the appropriate ranged/ammunition slots and travel into Sea Serpent Grotto.
  3. Use the Soultrapper on living monsters with the requested names to create Soul Plates. Take additional photographs if necessary; ordinary monster drops are not the requested evidence.
  4. Return to Andrause and trade the three matching Soul Plates individually. After he accepts the full set, finish his dialogue and receive the Black book.

Battle and progress notes

  • Confirm the item names if using Andrause's supply menu: the reviewed source has an inconsistent item constant in its plate-purchase branch.

Completion and reward

Black book.
