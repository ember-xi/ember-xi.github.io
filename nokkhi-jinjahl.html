Nokkhi Jinjahl's job is to make quivers, bullet pouches and card cases. Trade her 99 arrows, bolts, bullets or cards and one Carnation to receive a quiver, pouch or case respectively. You can trade more than one set at a time, just be sure to give her one Carnation for each stack of 99 arrows, bolts, bullets or cards. She doesn't quiver all types of Ammunition, only the ones listed below:
