Mog House Management Union

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


The Mog House Management Union (or MHMU, for short) is an organization of Moogles that sponsor the various special events that are held annually in Vana'diel--and they also safeguard adventurers' items, whenever they are away.

Very little is known about this exclusive organization.
