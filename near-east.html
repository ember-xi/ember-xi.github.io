Near East

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Name used in several ocasions on the game to describe the areas around Aradjiah continent. As examples there is Wilhelm in Mhaura and several others NPCs involved in quests across Vana'diel. The Near East is accessible in the Treasures of Aht Urhgan expansion.
