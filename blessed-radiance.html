Blessed Radiance

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


  Start NPC:   Luto Mewrilah - Upper Jeuno (G-8)
  Requirements:
   Sufficient Adventuring Fellow Bond
  Items Needed:   Glimmering Mica
  Lunascent Log
  Mistroot
  Title:   None
  Repeatable:   No
  Reward:   Strengthens the bonds between you and your adventuring fellow.
  (Increases max Adventuring Fellow Bond to 90).

  Previous Quest:   Blighted Gloom
  Next Quest:   Mirror Images

Walkthrough

  • Speak to Luto Mewrilah for a cutscene.
  • Head to Neptune's Spire and click on the first door to the left for a cutscene where you will be asked to find Glimmering Mica, Lunascent Log, and Mistroot to take to "Mirror Pond" in Beaucedine Glacier.
  • Mistroot (Key Item) is harvested in Giddeus with a sickle.
  • Lunascent Log (Key Item) is logged in Jugner Forest with a hatchet.
  • Glimmering Mica (Key Item) is excavated in Maze of Shakhrami with a pickaxe.
  • Note: Your NPC must be present and close to the spot when trying to obtain the above key items. If it's too far away you will simply do the action you normally would without NPC, therefore not getting key item. You can call your NPC with either a Signal Pearl or a Tactics Pearl. By now you should have high enough bond to have your NPC with you long enough to gather all 3 key items in a single use of either Signal or Tactic pearl (Be sure to have maps ready with harversting logging and excavating points indicated).
  • Note: Be careful not to get on a chocobo if your NPC is already out, as it will make him or her disappear. The points in Jugner and Shakhrami aren't far, but the run to Giddeus might require you to take a chocobo.
  • Note: You can Teleport with your NPC.
  • After you have collected all 3 key items, head to Beaucedine Glacier for a cutscene.
  • You may receive this cutscene directly after visiting the Neptune's Spire, but you would still have to collect the key items in order to advance the quest.
  • Go to the small lake in the upper left corner of J-7 (near Fei'Yin) and click the "Mirror Pond" for a cutscene.
  • Note, there are 2 "Mirror Pond"s, you want the northern one located at (J-7), not the one located at (G-10).
  • Head to "Neptune's Spire" and click on the first door to the left for another long cutscene.
  • Speak to Luto Mewrilah for a cutscene.

Game Description

Client: Luto Mewrilah (Artisan Bridge, Upper Jeuno)
Summary:

Palometa still lies in cursed slumber. You must find a way to save her!
