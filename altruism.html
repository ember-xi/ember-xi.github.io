Altruism

From Zenith XI Wiki

SCH 75 merit · Light Arts stratagem.

Use

  1. Activate Light Arts.
  2. Improves accuracy for the next white spell. Unlock it with Scholar Group 2 merits.

Historical level 75 merit stratagems use two charges. Later retail changed this cost; check Zenith's in-game behavior if different.

See Scholar Stratagems for charge recovery and Scholar Spell List for spell access.
