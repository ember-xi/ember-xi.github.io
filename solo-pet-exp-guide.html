>>>>Advice

>>Work in progress<<

This Guide is geared toward a Black Mage with a Ninja sub, however this doesn't mean it isn't possible for other job's or duo's to make good experience points using this guide.

To start I would strongly recommend that you level BLM to 20 with Valkurm Dunes parties, then level up to 24 with a BLM burn (5BLM burns are pretty nice!) and only start trying to solo once you have ninja as a sub job and access to Utsusemi: Ichi.

There is also a degree of risk with these xp camps multiple resisted spells, bad pulls, links and resisted sleep spells can easily result in deaths, please bear this in mind as BLM burns by comparison are far easier.

Be sure to macro in Escape and Warp to save yourself when things go bad and be aware of your MP, it's not great when you try to warp/escape and you don't have enough MP.

Most camps are quicker and safer as a duo with only a tiny exp trade-off, so if you find yourself heading to a camp and someone is already there I recommend teaming up!

Level 20 - 25

Zone: Lower Delkfutt's Tower - Target: Gigas's Bat

Camp Advice

Right as you enter Delkfutt's Tower the Giant Sentry's that spawn these bats will be directly north and west of the very first crossroads.

The Giant sentry north of your position is much safer to pull than any on the West side, as there may only be 1 or 2 gigas on the way north if any at all.

On the West side however there is a gigas that patrols right up to the entrance and an aggressive bat that can also go right to the west entrance not to mention the giant sentry in the south part of the west room is almost impossible to pull without linking some bats on the way out.

Advantages

  • Very close to a zone edge
  • 2 - 3 spawns means it's great for chains

Disadvantages

  • There are bats that will link on the west side
  • Pulling a Giant Lobber can be fatal even with the very close zone edge
  • You will be typically under level 24 so without shadows a PL is strongly recommended
  • The Giant Sentry's share spawns with other gigas, so if they aren't up or you accidently pull one they can respawn as other gigas and will need to be despawned at zone until they respawn as a Sentry again
  • Anything killing the Gigas you need will result in a 15+ minute wait to respawn

Level 24-26

Zone: Lower Delkfutt's Tower - Target: Gigas's Bats

Camp Advice

To get to this camp you will need a Delkfutt Key in order to access the area with the mobs.

Enter Lower Delkfutt's Tower Head West at the crossroads keep heading around the map not going up the staircase, go until you reach a door trade the key to it and head inside keep going along the path until the room opens up to a large room with lots of Gigas (the room where you more or less finish Rank 3 missions) this is here you will find your mobs.

This camp is high risk, I would recommend opting for Bee's in Yhoator Jungle even if you won't be able to get decent chain kills.

This camp could start lower but under level 24 without shadows come with big risks.

Advantages

  • There are 2 Gigas Butcher so there is possibility for chains
  • Isn't far from the Qufim outpost provided you have the key
  • If you have a PL hold the Beastmasters you will be able to chain nice kills off 2 spawners

Disadvantages

  • The place is full of Gigas that walk all over the place
  • There are bats that will link dotted around
  • During thunder weather which feels almost infinite here there will but Thunder elementals that will aggro magic
  • This is a challenging high risk camp
  • Sneak and Invisible are needed to reach the camp

Level 24-30

Zone: Yhoator Jungle - Target: Goblin's Bee

Camp Advice

To get to the camp you can either come from one of the Outposts, Teleport-Yhoat or Chocobo from Kazham.

Refer to the map below to find the camp it is the corner at G-7.

If you are sat right in the corner that aren't any dangers to you at all here, however keep in mind back behind the tree to the west there is another goblin, it shouldn't really be a threat but worth keeping in mind.

Advantages

  • Nothing special honestly
  • The camp is extremely safe
  • If you aggro the goblin it will always respawn and if it is killed it will respawn in 5 minutes

Disadvantages

  • Only 1 mob spawning pets so chains aren't getting very high
  • Not super accessible
  • If you aggro the Goblin Pathfinder you are depending on landing a sleep spell and logging out otherwise its a death

Level 27-34

Zone: Attohwa Chasm - Target: Goblin's Gallinipper

Camp Advice

To get to camp the closest access is Teleport-Mea from there you need to head through Maze of Shakhrami into Attohwa Chasm.

Head to the east part of the Attohwa map then immdiately north until you see the Goblin Pathfinder with his pet fly keep going till you pass the 3 trees this is where you will be camping.

The goblin's patrolling around won't ever pass the 3 trees so make sure you are just far enough on the other side of them that they wont see you while resting.

Keep in mind however further north there will be a Attohwa Coeurl roaming around so you need to be careful not to be too close to either side.

Advantages

  • The camp is safer than it feels just be aware of distancing
  • Once you have Water II the mob often dies in 2 casts of this spell
  • There is almost always a way to pull the pet at this camp so less time spent waiting for the goblin to turn around

Disadvantages

  • Only one mob spawning so hard to get over Chain 4
  • Quite out of the way without chocobo access, Sneak and Invisible are needed, as /nin at this level you will need Silent Oil to get to camp
  • There is a Gallinipper very close to the camp that can link onto your pulls

Level 31-36

Zone: Beaucedine Glacier - Target: Gigas's Tiger

Camp Advice

This camp is a real pain to get to but you have access to Tonko invis at this point which does help once you can get to Beaucedine Glacier but getting through Ranguemont Pass without sneak at this level isn't happening so make sure to bring some Silent oils.

If you come from Xarcabard the walk can be done without sneak with some risk but is do-able and if you are lucky enough to have the OP warp direct to Beaucedine Glacier then that Tonko invis and a short walk to camp are super nice.

Make sure to wait for the Rime Gigas to stop moving before pulling, it is very possible to pull the tiger from almost any position the gigas stops in by running around either left or right to get a good angle then pull it back into the rocky area for safety.

Since a recent patch the one Gigas in this area is now a slotted position, if killed it can respawn as 1 of 3 options the Rime Gigas, a Sleet Gigas or a Cold Gigas.

Be aware of the Tundra Tiger on your left when in the camp when you run around left to pull.

Advantages

  • The camp is very safe just nestle into the rocks
  • Can easily pull as there are good angles and rarely need to wait for movement
  • Very short easy walk from outpost only need Invisible

Disadvantages

  • Only one mob spawning so hard to get over Chain 4
  • Very out of the way, Sneak and Invisible are needed, as /nin at this level you will need Silent Oil to get to camp, without outpost
  • There is a Tundra Tiger to the North East that can aggro when you do a left side pull
  • The tiger has pretty consistent double attacks and hits hard

Level 33-40

Zone: The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah - Target: Goblin's Leech

Camp Advice

Just a short walk north from the Outpost to reach the camp, Sneak and Invis is however recommended to reach the camp easily.

This is one of the best camps for chaining kills as there are 3 Goblin Traders in a small area, honestly i would recommend teaming up with a small group of BLM to get some rapid chain kills.

This camp lasts until 40 as Solo even without chains rapid killing these mobs is still pretty good while the risk of the leeches killing you gets much lower, beyond 40 the exp just starts to drop off as well as no longer gaining elemental magic skills.

Advantages

  • Super close to the outpost
  • 3 mobs to pick from
  • Very viable for chains

Disadvantages

  • One of the goblins can wander pretty close to where the camp is so mind your positioning
  • At night Lesser Gaylas spawn all over the place so mind the sound aggro
  • Starting this camp at level 33 can be risky, perhaps safer to start at 34 or 35
  • Water II the most powerful spell at this point (until Aero II) tends to wiff a lot vs Leeches
