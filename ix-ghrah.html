Notes:

  • Final boss for Promathia Mission 8-2.
  • About 9000 HP.
  • Changes between the four Ghrah forms at will, and uses the appropriate 2-hour of its current form at about 40% HP.
  • In ball form it is a Black Mage, uses Manafont, and then spams the Tier III -ga nuke of its element (or Sleepga II if it is Dark elemental).
  • In bird form it is a Thief, has extremely high evasion, and uses Perfect Dodge.
  • A MNK75 with good equipment but no sushi had 32% accuracy on the bird form, vs 67% on the ball form.
  • In spider form it is a Warrior, and uses Mighty Strikes.
  • In human form it is a Paladin, and uses Invincible.
  • Resistant to silence, susceptible to slow, blind, gravity, paralyze.
