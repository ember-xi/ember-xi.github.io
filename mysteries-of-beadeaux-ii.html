Walkthrough

  1. Accept the two Beadeaux requests from Sattal-Mansal after obtaining Aldo’s Silver Bell.
  2. Defeat Go’Bhu Gascon on Beadeaux’s upper level around (F-6) and collect one Quadav Augury Shell.
  3. Return to Sattal-Mansal and trade only the shell for the Black Matinee Necklace. Your second required key item is the Coruscant Rosary from Mysteries of Beadeaux I.

Walkthrough

  • After you have spoken with Aldo for the mission Magicite, speak with Sattal-Mansal (Lower Jeuno).
  • He will give you a Black Matinee Necklace in return for a Quadav Augury Shell.
  • The Quadav Augury Shell drops from Go'Bhu Gascon, who is found in Beadeaux.
  • The Quadav Augury Shell can also be sold from player bazaars.
  • Trade the shell to Sattal-Mansal for your reward.

Game Description

Client: Sattal-Mansal (Tenshodo, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

Bring back a Quadav augury shell and Sattal-Mansal will trade a black matinee necklace for it. Only then can you enter the depths of Beadeaux.
