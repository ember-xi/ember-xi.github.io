Navigating the Unfriendly Seas

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Start NPC: ??? - Arrapago Reef (H-10), CS NPC Zweeha
Requirements:
 Corsair 50+, Equipped for All Occasions completed
Items Needed: Hydrogauge
Title: None
Repeatable: Yes, after erasing AF memory
Reward: Corsair's Culotte

Previous Quest: Equipped for All Occasions
Next Quest: Against All Odds

Walkthrough

1) After completing Corsair AF1 Quest, return to Arrapago Reef's ??? as Corsair 50+.

2) Examine the ??? for a cutscene. You will be told you need a Hydrogauge.

3) Return to Aht Urhgan Whitegate, and hitch a ride on the boat to Nashmau. While on the boat, purchase a fastwater fishing rod and lugworm. Begin fishing from the boat until you fish up a Hydrogauge.

  • This does not require any Fishing skill level.
  • It is also possible to fish up Hydrogauge from Al Zhabi, Whitegate, and Nashmau using a Tarutaru Fishing Rod and a Little Worm.
  • Hamsi likes to bite on Lugworm. To avoid catching actual fish, Little Worm an Rogue Rig are better choices.
  • A Composite Fishing Rod with a Minnow can also fish up a Hydrogauge.

4) After fishing up your Hydrogauge, head to Nashmau and trade your Hydrogague to Leleroon at (G-7).

5) Next, head to the Leypoint in Wajaom Woodlands at (G-8), and trade the Hydrogauge to the Leypoint. You will receive the message "You set the hydrogague in the glowing trench." (Note: You don't need to trade this as a Corsair, so the Leypoint can be conveniently warped to by any 60+ job using an Olduum Ring.)

6) Wait until the following day (after midnight Japan time) and re-examine the Leypoint.

7) Next, return to Zweeha by once again examining Arrapago Reef's ???. (Note: You don't need to check this as Corsair.)
