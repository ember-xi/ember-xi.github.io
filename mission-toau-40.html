Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 39: Allied Rumblings.
  • Next Vana'diel day after ToAU 39; change areas.

Starting point

Pherimociel · Ru'Lude Gardens · G-6

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Change areas after the council and wait for the next Vana'diel day.
  2. Speak with Pherimociel at G-6 and agree that you are ready to depart.
  3. Allow the linked Wajaom Woodlands scenes to finish. You are moved automatically to the Leypoint and the mission advances after the final scene.

Battle and progress notes

  • If you asked about the airship too early and saw the not-ready scene, change areas again before returning to Pherimociel. Declining the ready departure can be corrected by talking to him again.

Completion and reward

Endymion Paratrooper title.

What this opens

  • ToAU 41: Light of Judgment
