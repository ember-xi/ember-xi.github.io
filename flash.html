Notes

  • Severely reduces accuracy compared to Blind, the effect weakens with duration.
  • While it may be partially resisted, it cannot be fully resisted, unless the target is immune.
  • Lasts up to 15 seconds if completely unresisted.
  • Stacks with Blind.
  • Acquires a significant amount of Enmity.
  • See also Flash (Status Effect).
  • Automatons with a Flashbulb equipped can use a non-magic version of Flash, which can not be reflected or absorbed by shadows.
