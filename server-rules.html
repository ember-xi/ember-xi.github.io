Server profile

SystemZenith rules
Level capMaximum level 75. Complete First Limit Break at 50; automatic increases at 55, 60 and 65; final job trial at 70.
ContentA level 75 profile through Wings of the Goddess, plus the three level-75 scenario add-ons. Content restrictions are enabled. Abyssea, Adoulin and later content are outside this profile. Not every story has been tested.
ExperienceThe server profile advertises 2× EXP. This is not a measured multiplier for every reward type. RoE EXP and Sparks use a 1.0 multiplier in the RoE package.
TrustsStart with three companions. Two Zenith quests unlock a fourth and fifth without requiring Rhapsodies missions.
EquipmentJob-specific starting gear and WAR / THF reward quests. Other equipment comes from ordinary drops, vendors, crafting and the Auction House.
RoE & SparksRoE supports training and progression. Sparks Essentials offers supplies and Trust Ciphers instead of equipment.
FameFame Access waives reviewed quest reputation thresholds. Story, level and item requirements remain.
ProgressZenith quest progress survives job changes, zoning and logout. Once-per-character rewards do not reset on login.

About this guide

This wiki documents the Zenith packages and confirmed play reports. It does not connect to the live server. Implementation status distinguishes tested features, prepared packages and future plans.

Inventory convenience

Main Inventory: 80 slots after Adventures v1.1 is installed. Bag errands are removed from the custom goal guide.
