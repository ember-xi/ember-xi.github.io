Before you start

Scholar75; Treasures of Aht Urhgan access; Elder Staff and personal Runic Disc.

Steps

  1. Equip Elder Staff as your main weapon. Talk to Zalsuhm in Muckvix's Junk Shop, Lower Jeuno (H-9), and accept his offer.
  2. Build weapon-skill points while Elder Staff remains equipped, fighting experience-yielding enemies. Weapon skills and closing skillchains earn points.
  3. Historical requirement depends on your own saved Nyzul progress: clearing floor 100 reduces it to 250 points; no progress requires 16000. Completing someone else's floor 100 run does not automatically give your disc that progress.
  4. When Omniscience appears in the weapon-skill list with Elder Staff equipped, trade the staff to Zalsuhm to finish the unlock.

Reward

Omniscience permanently usable as Scholar with an equipped staff.

Zenith availability

Retail reduced every floor tier to 250 points in July 2014. Zenith's exact floor/point rule must be checked in its active implementation; this guide labels the historical rule rather than assuming the later reduction.
