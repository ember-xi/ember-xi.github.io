Zenith availability

Reference walkthrough. Completion is unconfirmed in the reviewed Zenith base: no dedicated quest handler or matching progression hook was found in the inspected zone/NPC files. A missing interaction may reflect unfinished content.

Before you begin

  • Complete A Feast for Gnats and A Nation on the Brink; reach Crossroads of Time.
  • Bring several Pickaxes.

Starting point

Zone-entry cutscene · Windurst Waters [S]

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Windurst Waters [S]. In Southern San d’Oria [S], speak to Raustigne (I-7), then Hauberliond (H-9).
  2. Check the New-turned Earth in the area Hauberliond assigns: East Ronfaure [S] J-11, La Vaule [S] K-9, or Jugner Forest [S] J-5. Receive the Darkfire cinder.
  3. Speak to Striking Snake in the present-day Metalworks gunpowder room (H-7).
  4. Use Pickaxes at North Gustaberg [S] mining points until you obtain Liquid quicksilver and Gelid sulfur.
  5. Return to Striking Snake. Wait until the next Vana’diel day, change zones, and speak again for Beastbane bullets.
  6. Speak to Rotih Moalghett in Fort Karugo-Narugo [S] (I-8, inner map). Prepare before examining the Warding Door at E/F-7: the battle starts immediately.
  7. Defeat Count Furfur, two Count Murmurs and two Tartalos. Recheck the door and collect the reward before leaving.

Battle and progress notes

  • Hauberliond repeats the assigned location if needed.
  • Keep a free inventory slot; leaving without collecting the earring can require repeating the fight.

Completion and reward

Stellar Earring.

What this opens

  • The Forbidden Path
