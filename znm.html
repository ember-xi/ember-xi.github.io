ZNM

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Zeni Notorious Monster (ZNM) events are spawned by trading a pop item to ??? around Vana'diel. The pop items are obtained from Sanraku at (E-8) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate in exchange for Zeni. When spawned, the pop item will disappear and the monster can be fought like any other mob in Vana'diel. A full alliance of 18 can engage these ZNMs only using one pop item. This is a normal monster battle, not a battlefield instance. After a maintenance, the number of Zeni for a NM pop item is set to max; however, it can go higher than the max listed. Price seems to vary based on seals you have currently and supply and demand on pop items.
