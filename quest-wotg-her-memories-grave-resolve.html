Before you begin

  • Her Memories is the current Wings of the Goddess mission.
  • Start Her Memories: Homecoming Queen.
  • One Lilac.

Starting point

Halver · Chateau d'Oraguille · I-9

Era note: September 2010 WotG memory chapter, released after the original level-75 cap.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Halver (I-9) for the scene.
  2. Reach the Weathered Gravestone in present-day Batallia Downs (I-10). Examine it first, then trade one Lilac to finish the memory. The native handler requires the examination before the trade.

Completion and reward

A tiny memory fragment toward Large memory fragment I.
