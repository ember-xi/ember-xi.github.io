Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Bane is a harmful status effect that inflicts a number of bad effects until it wears off or is removed. Afflicted characters have both their maximum HP and MP reduced by a percentage relative to the strength of the monster's ability of their maximum HP and MP, respectively, and gain the effect of weight.

How to remove the effect

  • Cursna and Holy Water does remove this effect.

How the effect is inflicted

Spells

  • None

Equipment

  • None

Monster Abilities

  • Lost Soul - Eldieme Necropolis

This status effect is involved in the quest: Catch It If You Can!
