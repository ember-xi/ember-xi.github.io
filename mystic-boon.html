Mystic Boon

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Club weapon skill

  • Skill level: N/A
  • Description: Converts damage dealt to own MP. Damage varies with TP. Yagrush: Aftermath effect varies with TP.
  • Available only after completing the Unlocking a Myth (White Mage) quest.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: None
  • Modifiers: STR:30% MND:50%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.0 1.5 2.0

Notes

The word comes from the Latin for good. Therefore the meaning of the word is closer originally to a blessing, gift, good thing that happens than it is to a prayer or a request.
