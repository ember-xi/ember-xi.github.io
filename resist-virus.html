Resist Virus

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Increases resistance against viruses.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Each upgrade of this trait further increases character's resistance.
    • Virus type status effects include Disease and Plague.
    • This trait greatly shortens the duration of virus type status effects.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Amood / Amood +1
  • Elegant Ribbon
  • Flawless Ribbon
  • Hale Ring
  • Jambiya / Jambiya +1
  • Scintillant Ring
  • Shining Ring
