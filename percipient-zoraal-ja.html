Percipient Zoraal Ja

From Zenith XI Wiki

A Beastmaster Mamool Ja who joins the Zoraal Ja’s Pkuucha fight in Wajaom Woodlands.

Contents
  1. Spawn and battle order
  2. Where the loot comes from
  3. Sources

Spawn and battle order

  1. Find Zoraal Ja's Pkuucha through the Lesser Colibri lottery near I-9 in Wajaom Woodlands.
  2. Damage the bird until Percipient Zoraal Ja appears; the bird’s HP recovers when he arrives.
  3. Defeat Percipient first. His Reward-style ability repeatedly restores the bird, preventing you from finishing it while he remains active.
  4. Control the bird with Bind if needed, then finish it after the Mamool Ja dies.

Where the loot comes from

Zoraal Ja's Axe and Fowling Earring drop from the bird, not Percipient. There is no separate pop item for him.
