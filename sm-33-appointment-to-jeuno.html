RequirementsRank 3 · 3-2 complete
Items NeededNone
RewardsRank 4 · 5,000 gil

Mission 3-3 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 3-2 The Crystal Spring.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Halver for the Letter to the Ambassador.
  2. Go to Ru'Lude Gardens (Jeuno) and talk to the ambassador Nelcabrit at the embassy.
  3. Enter Lower Delkfutt's Tower (from Qufim Island) and get the Delkfutt Key from the ??? near the entrance.
  4. Return to Nelcabrit, then Halver, for Rank 4.
Mechanics: Giants in the tower are level 25-35; the key is close to the entrance. Bring Trusts.
