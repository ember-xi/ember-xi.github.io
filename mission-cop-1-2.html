Before you begin

  • Complete CoP 1-1.

Starting point

Pherimociel · Ru'Lude Gardens · G-6

Era note: Historical reference cap: 30. Native battlefields use the server MAX_LEVEL instead. The referenced native battlefield permits Trusts; optional era modules or Zenith overrides can change entry rules. This is code review, not a completed Zenith battlefield test.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Pherimociel in the palace guard room; repeat if another storyline takes priority. Speak to Harith at H-5 for his explanation of Emptiness.
  2. Choose one crag: Holla in La Theine Plateau, Dem in Konschtat Highlands, or Mea in Tahrongi Canyon. Examine its Shattered Telepoint and then the Large Apparatus inside the Hall of Transference.
  3. Enter that Promyvion. Defeat Memory Receptacles and use the resulting Memory Streams to climb. If no stream appears, search another receptacle on the same floor.
  4. On floor four, enter the corresponding Spire and win Ancient Flames Beckon. Holla has Wreaker, Dem Progenerator, and Mea Delver. The first victory advances your log to 1-3; finish the other two there.

Battle and progress notes

  • Entering a Promyvion alone does not complete this mission. If switching an unfinished Promyvion, accept the prompt to seal its memories.
  • Anima made by Harith are optional boss weakening items; they are not keys required for entry.

Walkthrough

  • Speak to Pherimociel at the Grand Duchy's Palace in Ru'Lude Gardens (G-6) to trigger a cutscene and start this mission. Note: During the cutscene, the guards will talk of an alchemist.
  • The alchemist named Harith is waiting in the Duke's House out the back entrance (behind the large staircase H-5) of the Grand Duchy's Palace. Talk to Harith and he will mumble something about Emptiness and Promyvion.
  • You should now be able to enter Promyvion - Mea, Promyvion - Holla, and Promyvion - Dem.
    • Head to any one of the three crags in either Tahrongi Canyon, Konschtat Highlands or La Theine Plateau. Examine the Shattered Telepoint to trigger a cutscene and enter Hall of Transference.
    • You will need to complete all 3 Promyvions in order to finish missions 1-2 and 1-3. When you finish your first Promyvion (order doesn't matter), your mission log will change to 1-3. All of the strategy you will need for the Promyvions are on the 1-3 page.


Notes:

  • Harith will turn Recollections (dropped from certain empty & Purchased from AH.) into an anima (that are used to weaken the empty boss in various ways).
  • To obtain this service you must first view the cutscene you get by entering any Promyvion and using the machines inside. You then must return to him without erasing your memory. You do not have to beat one Promyvion before you can get Animas. You need only to remember you have been to a Promyvion area in order to converse with Harith.
