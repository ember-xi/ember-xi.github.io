Warrior's Armor Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

An armor set from the Dynamis zones which the player may be rewarded with for defeating the mobs within. The armor is in the same style as the lower level Warrior AF; however, it's distinct blue coloring makes mistaking the two highly unlikely.

The bonuses given by this armor reflect the Warrior's dual nature as both Damage Dealer and Tank with significant increases to Attack, Str and Dex enhancing its role as a melee, and increased Defense, HP, Vit, Agi, Enmity and Parrying helping the War to tank, along with a reduction to Opponent's Critical Hit Rate. Job Abilities and Traits also benefit from these pieces with enhancements to the Berserk, Aggressor and Warcry abilities, and the Double Attack trait.


Number of Pieces: 6


Cost to store: 2000g (excluding Warrior's Stone)
