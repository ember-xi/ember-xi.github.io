Notes:

  • Spawned by trading a Monkey Wine to a ??? on the Wajaom Woodlands Islet.
    • Islet is accessed from (H-13) in Wajaom Woodlands after purchasing Sicklemoon Salt (key item) for 500 Zeni from Sanraku.
    • Sicklemoon Salt is needed by everyone wishing to access the Islet.
    • The Sicklemoon Salt will be removed from your key item list after one use.
  • Spawns at 50% HP with only 1 head, and heavy Auto Regen. Quickly regenerates a second head restoring 25% of its life, and then a third head restoring another 25% HP.
    • Auto Regen seems to disappear once all three heads have been restored.
  • Susceptible to Slow, Paralyze, Poison, Elegy, and Blind.
  • Immune to Stun.
  • Has ability to Draw In, which is then immediately followed by a TP move. (Including single target Serpentine Tail, single target 2000+ damage.)
  • Uses special Bulwark abilities that last for 45 seconds and grants either physical or magical immunity.
    • Pyric Bulwark: Immune to melee attacks.
    • Polar Bulwark: Immune to magic attacks.
    • After using either Pyric Bulwark or Polar Blast, it will immediately use Nerve Gas.
      • Nerve Gas: AoE Curse and 20hp/tick poison. As soon as mages see the use of a bulwark, they should be prepared to quickly cursna, cure, and poisona tanks.
    • Trembling: 5' AoE 0-500 DMG Attack with additional effect of Dispel (10' range). Blinkable, wipes shadows (Just fought this today and trembling was hitting people for 200 up to 1,000 damage, it's not weak.)
    • Barofield: 5' AoE 50-500 DMG Attack with additional effect of Gravity.
  • Does not have regain.
  • Unlike the normal Hydra, black magic works very well against this, and is highly recommended.
  • Getting the Monkey Wine from Sanraku requires a Charcoal Grey Seal, a Deep Purple Seal and a Chestnut-colored Seal.
  • Uses the 2hr move Mighty Strikes.
  • Can drop multiple Enkidu's Harnesses.
  • It is possible through the dropping of crystals to exceed the 10 item limit. In this case the first item in the treasure pool will be allocated to someone randomly in the alliance. This will always be Hydra Meat.
  • Is immune to magic based non-elemental attacks (Chi Blast, Spirits Within, etc).
  • Risk:
    • Seen Tinnin perform Pyric and Polar TP attacks back-to-back. 1600 HP PLD felled from full HP.
