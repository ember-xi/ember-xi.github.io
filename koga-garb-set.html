Koga Garb Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

An armor set from the Dynamis zones, which the player may be rewarded with for defeating the enemies within. The set is a striking red and tan color, a significant change from the subdued blacks and grays of the Ninja Garb, despite both sets sharing a similar model.

The set is unusual in that the precise bonuses granted by it change depending on the time of day. The Ninja can count on significant increases to HP, Attack and Accuracy, Ranged Attack and Accuracy, Vit, Ninjutsu Skill, a small Evasion boost and a 4% Haste bonus 24/7, along with permanent enhancements to Dual Wield, Ninjutsu damage and Ninja Tool Expertise (as long as the appropriate pieces are equipped). These bonuses alone are quite significant, but come nighttime the Ninja has even more waiting for them. An almighty boost of 12 Str, 7 Dex, 10 Evasion, 10 Parrying, and 4% more haste on top of what has already been given them. All in all a highly desirable set.
