Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Additional effect: Poison. Duration of effect varies with TP.

Description

Spell cost: 20 MP
Ecosystem: Plantoids
Type: Physical (Piercing)
Blue Magic Points: 2
Stat Bonus: HP-5, MP+5
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 8

Timers

Casting Time: 2 seconds
Recast Time: 15 seconds

Notes

  • Range 14.7'
  • Calculated as a ranged attack with piercing damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: INT: 20%.
  • Poisoned effect is 3 HP/tick.
  • Additional effect of poison is not guaranteed even if the spell connects.
  • Will wipe all Utsusemi Shadows

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Skillchain Element(s): Dark (can open Transfixion or Detonation; can close Compression or Gravitation)
