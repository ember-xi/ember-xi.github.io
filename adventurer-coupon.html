From Zenith XI Wiki

Trade your starter coupon for a once-per-character Zenith welcome gift.

Walkthrough

  1. Prepare 12 empty inventory slots. Find Matildie in Northern San d’Oria (J-8).
  2. Trade exactly one Adventurer Cpn. Do not add gil or other items.
  3. Confirm the trade to receive the reward. Visiting another assistant does not grant a second gift.
  4. If delivery is interrupted, make space and speak to the assistant to collect the remaining reward. A second coupon is not required.

Rewards

  • 3,000 gil.
  • 12 Meat Jerky for physical jobs, or 12 Apple Pies for magical/support jobs, according to the package’s classification at turn-in.
  • 5 Potions and 5 Ethers.
  • Mandragora Lantern: Mog House furnishing with its original properties.

The earlier Delivery Box compensation was a separate correction for a recorded case. It is not an additional gift for every character.
