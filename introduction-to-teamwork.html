   San d'Oria Reputation 2
  Any Job at Level 10 or higher
  Items Needed:   None

  Repeatable:   No

  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   Intermediate Teamwork

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Vilatroire in the Watchtower, accessed from Northern San d'Oria (C-7).
  • Form a party of 6 people from the same nation and at least level 10, then talk to him again to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Vilatroire (Watchtower, Northern San d'Oria)
Summary:

In order to form a cohesive party, gather adventurers of the same allegiance.
