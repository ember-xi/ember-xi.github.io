Statistics

Quicksand coffer key
A key that opens a treasure chest in
the Quicksand Caves.

Type: Coffer Key

Stackable: Not Stackable

Other Uses

Used in Quest: The Road to Aht Urhgan

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

Possible Treasure

  • 6,490 - 11,880 gil
  • Map of the Quicksand Caves**
  • Myochin Haidate*
  • Drachen Greaves*
  • Painite
  • Chrysoberyl
  • Sunstone
  • Fluorite
  • Aquamarine

* Only available if you have the necessary quest active.
** Automatically received if you do not already have the map. Quest items take priority.

Treasure Maps

Coffers on the Quicksand Caves' first map Coffers on the Quicksand Caves' second map

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
