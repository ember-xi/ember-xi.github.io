Notes

  • Both use Cockatrice special attacks; Contagion Transfer, a special single attack that absorbs up to 8 positive AND negative effects (including Food, Job Ability effects, and petrification, but 2 hours are not absorbed); and Contagion, a very wide aoe attack that transfers all negative effects from the Giant Moa to the players within range.
  • Both Giant Moa have Additional Effect: TP Absorption.
  • Critical Hit and TP Absorption go off fairly often.
  • Both Giant Moa are susceptible to Bind, Gravity, and Sleep. They’re immune to Charm. They build resistances to spells very quickly, and Sleep does not last long. Lullaby is untested.
  • The left Giant Moa has an AoE Gravity, Dia, and Poison Effect, it's highly recommended to bring Antidotes; the Poison does roughly 30HP/tick.
  • The right Giant Moa has an AoE Gravity, Bio, and Paralyze Effect, it's highly recommended to be ready to paralyna; this can be a make or breaker.
