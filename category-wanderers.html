Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A species of Empty, the memories of the souls that lie the mothercrystals. The Empty consist of monsters inside Promyvion and can be considered fairly powerful with stronger weapon skills than a normal enemy. These are the most common Empty and are sometimes found in packs.

Drifting aimlessly through the void of Promyvion, wanderers are a species of the Empty that resemble large floating brains. Contrary to their appearance, however, their behavior shows no hint of intelligence. In their endless hunt for memories, other species of the Empty often prey upon the relatively small but abundant wanderers.
