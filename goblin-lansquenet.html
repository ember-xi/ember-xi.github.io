Notes:

  • Spawn during Besieged.
  • Start off as friendly NPCs, but, as mercenaries, may be persuaded to join the other side. When this happens, they'll return to their spawn point, nod their head side to side as if thinking, and turn into an enemy monster. This happens when the Imperial Defense value is severely lowered during a Besieged invasion that started with enough value to have the Goblins present in the first place.Verification Needed
  • Can use Goblin and Moblin specials, including Bomb Toss and the Shower attacks.
