Walkthrough

Talk to Maudaal in E-9 of Aht Urhgan Whitegate to start this quest. You will receive a lengthy cutscene upon doing so.

After, you are directed to head to the "secret entrance" to Wajaom Woodlands, G-10/H-10 of Aht Urhgan. Upon zoning, you receive another cutscene.

At this point, it is recommended you either use a chocobo whistle or return to town to get a chocobo, as you will be heading for Mamook. Also, have a warp item ready if there is no BLM in party, if you do not wish to run back from Mamook.

Reach Mamook by entering the cave at D-12 on the Wajaom Woodlands map. Once you've entered, you will find a "Toad's Footprint," click it for another cutscene.

You are directed to a small lake inside Mamook. Use the viscous liquid to turn yourself into a Beastman to avoid aggro from the 2 Poroggos nearby (Tough to 75). You will find more tracks in the lower hallway of G-7/H-7. Click these once for a cutscene. Click them again for the fight.

Once you've clicked for the fight, you will spawn a Poroggo Casanova and 4 smaller Poroggos. They are unable to be slept, even with Elemental Seal. The other Poroggos have horrible accuracy, but the Casanova may cast sleepga. He also uses "Frog Song" which turns you into a frog and causes "Charm" status. The Casanova likes to cast Freeze which is easily avoided or interrupted.

This fight can be done by 3 level 75 players. A tank, a healer, and a damage dealer. It is safer to bring more as "Frog Song" will charm allies, making the odds harder to win.

  • Confirmed soloable by 75 DRG/Mage.
  • Confirmed solo by 75 MNK/NIN. *Confirmed solo by a 75 NIN/WAR with evasion gear.
  • Confirmed solo by 75 SMN/WHM using Ramuh's Thunderspark for initial aggro followed by Chaotic Strike until death.

After winning, click the tracks again for yet another cutscene. Afterwards, you may warp back to Whitegate.

Talk to Maudaal again for a final cutscene, and your prize, the Chanoix's Gorget.
