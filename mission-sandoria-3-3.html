Before you begin

Be allied with San d'Oria, have Rank 3, and finish the preceding required story steps. Raise Rank Points if the mission is not offered.

Accept the mission before following its route. If the guard does not offer it, finish your current mission and any closing scenes, then raise Rank Points with crystals at a Conquest guard if required.

Walkthrough

Get the letter

  1. Accept Appointment to Jeuno, speak to Halver (I-9), and examine the Great Hall door in Chateau d’Oraguille. Receive the Letter to the Ambassador.
  2. Go to the San d’Oria embassy in Ru’Lude Gardens and speak to Nelcabrit at (G-9).

Reach the imprisoned ambassador

  1. Enter Lower Delkfutt’s Tower from Qufim Island (F-6). If this is your first ascent, follow the tower’s stairs and teleporters through the middle and upper tower to floor 10.
  2. Defeat Porphyrion on floor 10 and collect a physical Delkfutt Key. Make sure every player who needs one receives a key.
  3. Use the key at the tower’s elevator route and descend to the basement. At the San d’Oria Cermet Door in Lower Delkfutt’s Tower basement (M-8), trade the physical key for the rescue scene. If you already hold the permanent Delkfutt Key (key item), examine the door instead.

Report in Jeuno

  1. Return to the San d’Oria embassy and examine the ambassador’s office door at Ru’Lude Gardens (H-10). Receive Rank 4 and 5,000 gil.

Important notes

  • The embassy doors in Delkfutt’s basement differ by nation. For this mission use the San d’Oria door at (M-8).

Completion and reward

Rank 4; 5,000 gil

Walkthrough

  • Upon receiving the mission, report to Halver. He will direct you to speak to King Destin (check the Door:Great Hall for a cut scene) to receive the key item: Letter to the Ambassador. The King tells you to report the embassy in Ru'Lude Gardens. Report to the embassy in Jeuno and you will get a cutscene. Now it is time to make this trek up the Delkfutt's Tower.
  • Head to Delkfutt's Tower in Qufim, where you will need to climb up to the tenth floor and fight the NM giant Porphyrion.
  • It is suggested that you have the Map of Qufim Island, which can be purchased in Lower Jeuno and the Map of Delkfutt's Tower, which is obtained from the quest The Antique Collector.
  • There are 10 floors in the Tower divided in 3 parts linked by teleport. Here are the locations of the stairs/teleports:
  • 1st Floor: E-6 (NW)
  • 2nd Floor: I-9 (SE, deadend at H-9, so go east first)
  • 3rd Floor: G-6 (or J-8, but I think that might be one way only)
  • 4th Floor: I-6 (NE, deadends at G-7, H-9, J-9)
  • 5th Floor: H-9 (S, deadend at I-10, so go west first)
  • 6th Floor: J-10 (SE)
  • 7th Floor: F-6 (NW)
  • 8th Floor: (1st time): I-6 (NE)
  • 9th Floor: (1st time): I-9 (SE, this sends you back to 8th floor)
  • 8th Floor: (2nd time): F-9 -> 9th Floor
  • 9th Floor: (2nd time): F-6 -> 10th Floor
  • Once you are on the 10th Floor you fight Porphyrion at H-8 for Delkfutt Key.
  • Porphyrion will drop 6 Delkfutt keys each and is a 10 minute respawn.
  • After everyone has a Delkfutt Key, use elevator at H-8 to return to the first floor.
  • Head to the tunnel to the east, take the stairs down to the basement and trade the Delkfutt Key to the cermet door at M-8 for a cutscene with the San d'Orian ambassador.
  • Return to the San d'Orian Embassy in Ru'Lude Gardens to receive your reward.

Notes

It is suggested that you start the quest Tenshodo Membership after you get back from the tower. This will make it much easier when you go to do mission 4-1.
