Map Marker

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

These NPC's will mark the city maps with the locations of various NPCs important to adventurers. With the exception of Hugo, map markers will mark locations in all zones of their particular city. The Map Markers tend to be found on the outsides of the Mog House exits.

Map Markers

Name Location
Hugo Aht Urhgan Whitegate (F-10)
Zelala Windurst Waters, North (L-10)
Rumoie Southern San d'Oria (M-5)
Gwinar Port Bastok (J-12)
Nuria Ru'Lude Gardens (I-10)
Fariska Lokhmi Eastern Adoulin (G-9)

Markers will locate the following NPCs within a city:

  • Melody Minstrels
  • Traveling Bards
  • Weather Reporters
  • Armor Depositories
  • Item Depositories
  • Reputation NPCs
  • Immigration
  • Teleportation
  • Pursuivants
  • Gold World Pass NPC's
  • Homepoints

In addition to Eastern and Western Adoulin NPCs, Fariska Lokhmi will also mark Bivouac waypoints you've activated in:

  • Ceizak Battlegrounds
  • Yahse Hunting Grounds
  • Foret de Hennetiel
  • Morimar Basalt Fields
