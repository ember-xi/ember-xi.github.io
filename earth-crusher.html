Earth Crusher

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Staff weapon skill

  • Skill level: 70
  • Delivers an area of effect earth elemental attack. Damage varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: Earth
  • Skillchain properties: Detonation/Impaction
  • Modifiers: INT:30%; STR:30%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.00 2.3125 3.625

Levels

  • Earth Crusher is only natively available to Paladins, Warriors, Monks, and White Mages. While other jobs can obtain the required 70 Staff skill, they must use one of the aforementioned jobs as their support job in order to gain access to Earth Crusher.
  • Staff skill level 70 is obtainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
