Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Gives a party member certain amount of damage resistance.

Description

Spell cost: 42 MP
Spell element: Light
Magic skill: Enhancing Magic
Jobs:

  • Red Mage lvl 75

Merit Info:

  • Lasts 120 seconds upon first unlocking.
  • Each additional merit increases damage shield effect by 3 and increases duration by 30s up to a maximum of 180s.
  • Damage prevented Formula for this spell is [(enhancing magic/25) +1 + (3 x MeritLv)]
  • Adding more enhancing magic is useless unless you can break another 25 skill tier (see examples below)
  • Max casting range is 25' which is useful for casting upon kiting tanks.

Timers

Casting Time: 3 seconds
Recast Time: 10 seconds
