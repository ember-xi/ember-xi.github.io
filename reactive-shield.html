Statistics

Reactive shield
A fire-based automaton attachment.

Stackable: 12

Note: This is an Automaton Attachment:

Automaton Effect Req. Capacity
 Use "Blaze Spikes".  Fire x 2

The Blaze Spikes effect must be activated, unlike the Volt Gun's Enthunder effect. It is similar to Shock Absorber and Flashbulb in that it activates like a job ability and has a recast timer. A Fire Maneuver must be active in order for the automaton to use the attachment. The spike damage is dependent on the automaton's INT and Magic Attack Bonuses. The spike effect activates on every hit, like standard Blaze Spikes. The recast timer is 65 seconds.
