Zenith availability

The route has been checked against references and available code; completion on Zenith has not yet been play-tested.

Before you begin

  • Complete AMK 1.

Starting point

Moogle · Mog House

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Collect an Orcish Armor Plate from Orcs in Yughott Grotto, a Quadav Backscale from Quadav in Palborough Mines and Yagudo Caulk from Yagudo in Giddeus.
  2. Return to your home-nation Mog House and trade all three items to the Moogle together. Finish the scene before travelling to Jeuno.

Battle and progress notes

  • The White coral key is a repeat-route reward; the first story delivery advances the mission.
