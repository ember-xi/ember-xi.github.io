Brandt & Teodor (AF+1, Relic, Mythic)

Brandt & Teodor
WhereLower Jeuno, the fountain row — Brandt and Teodor stand side by side, a few steps from Halvard and the Forge Stone
BrandtArtifact +1: AF piece + 3 Alexandrite → the +1 piece. Also removes curses for gil.
Teodor — RelicWeapon of your stage + 200 in Dynamis coin → one step up
Teodor — Mythic150 Alexandrite over five steps → your job's Mythic (level 75)
CurrencyPiece of Alexandrite · Dynamis coins (bronze / silver / gold)

Two counters in Lower Jeuno handle AF+1, Relic and Mythic upgrades. Everything is paid from your bag; there are no hidden counters.

Artifact +1 — Brandt

Trade any artifact armor piece together with Piece of Alexandrite ×3. Brandt hands the +1 version back at once. All 75 pieces of the 15 classic jobs are covered.

Relic weapons — Teodor

  1. Get the base relic weapon from the Dynamis cities.
  2. Trade the weapon of your current stage with exactly 200 in Dynamis coin value (bronze 1, silver 100, gold 10,000).
  3. The weapon climbs one stage. Repeat until the final stage (whole ladder = 1,500 coin).
