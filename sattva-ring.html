Statistics

Sattva Ring
[Ring] All Races
HP +15~30 VIT +2~5 AGI +2~5
Enmity +3
Lv. 30 All Jobs

Notes

  • The Vitality and Agility stats increase by 1 for every 15 levels, from +2 at level 30 up to +5 at level 75.
  • The HP stat increases by 1 for every 3 levels, from +15 at level 30 to +30 at level 75.
Level 30: VIT+2, AGI+2, HP+15.
Level 45: VIT+3, AGI+3, HP+20.
Level 60: VIT+4, AGI+4, HP+25.
Level 75: VIT+5, AGI+5, HP+30.

Other Uses

Guild Points Value: None

Used in Quests: None

Resale Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Missions

  • One of three rings that can be chosen as the final reward upon completion of Promathia Mission 8-4 "Dawn". (Choice of Rajas Ring, Sattva Ring, or Tamas Ring.)
