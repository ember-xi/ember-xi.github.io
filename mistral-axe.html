Mistral Axe

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Axe weapon skill

  • Skill level: 225 (Beastmasters and Warriors only.)
  • Delivers a single-hit ranged attack. Damage varies with TP.
  • Despite being considered a ranged attack, it does stack with Trick Attack.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Fusion
  • Modifiers: STR:50%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
2.50 3.00 3.50

Notes

Renkei
Fusion (can make level 3 skillchain Light)
Description
Damage varies with TP.
Animation
You throw your axe at the mob. When it connects, there is a blue and white swirl as a black portal appears in front of the mob. Your axe flies into the portal, then out again, and you catch the axe.

Levels

  • Mistral Axe is only available to Warriors and Beastmasters. While other jobs can obtain the required 225 Axe skill, they will be unable to use Mistral Axe.
  • Axe skill level 225 is obtainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
Job Rating Level
Warrior A- 66
Beastmaster A- 66
Dark Knight B- 71
Ranger B- 71
