Shaded Cruse

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description: A golden pot that appears to be of great value. Wazozo has kept it hidden well, claiming it as her prized treasure. However, it was recently shattered into several pieces.

Used In Quest(s): In Search of the Truth

Notes: This is now listed among Permanent Key Items as of the May 2011 update. This Key Item was formerly listed as a Temporary Key Item, but nevertheless remained in the player's inventory even after completing In Search of the Truth.
