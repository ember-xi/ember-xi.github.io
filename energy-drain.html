Energy Drain

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Dagger weapon skill

  • Skill level: 175 (Thief, Red Mage, Bard, Ranger, and Ninja exclusive.)
  • Drains enemy's MP. Amount drained varies with TP.
  • As this weapon skill does not deal damage, it will not wake a sleeping mob.

Properties

  • Element: Darkness
  • Modifiers: Unknown
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
0.00 0.00 0.00

Levels

  • Energy Drain is only natively available to Thieves, Red Mages, Bards, Rangers, and Ninjas. While other jobs can obtain the required 175 Dagger skill, they must use one of the aforementioned jobs as their support job in order to gain access to Energy Drain.
