Zenith Crown +1

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-73 head equipment for WHM, BLM, RDM, BRD and SMN. DEF 31, INT +4, MND +4; enhanced Resist Silence; converts 55 HP to MP.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Result
  3. Sources

How to obtain

  1. Obtain Aquarian Abjuration: Head; original sources include Byakko.
  2. Obtain Cursed Crown -1 through crafting or the Auction House.
  3. Trade the abjuration and cursed item together to Alphollon C Meriard inside the cathedral in Northern San d'Oria.

Result

The uncursed armor is Rare/Exclusive. The high-quality result requires the -1 cursed item; an ordinary cursed item produces the ordinary armor.
