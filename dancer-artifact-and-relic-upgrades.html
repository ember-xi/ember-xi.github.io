Before starting

Unlock Sea by reaching Garden of Antiquity in Chains of Promathia. Visit Sagheera in Port Jeuno (I-8).

Artifact +1: level 74

Each recipe needs its matching base AF piece, one Filet Lace, one Gold Stud, the crafted material and the listed coins.

RewardCrafted materialAncient Beastcoins
Dancer's Tiara +1Gold Ingot30
Dancer's Casaque +1Scarlet Linen35
Dancer's Bangles +1Gold Ingot25
Dancer's Tights +1Silky Suede25
Dancer's Toe Shoes +1Silky Suede20
  1. Collect the armor and materials. Filet Lace comes from Temenos; Gold Stud from Apollyon. Ancient Beastcoins are Limbus currency.
  2. Trade the required Ancient Beastcoins to Sagheera first to add them to your stored balance.
  3. Trade the base piece, Filet Lace, Gold Stud and crafted material together. Sagheera deducts the recipe’s coins.
  4. Return after the next Vana’diel midnight and collect the finished piece before placing another order.

Relic +1: level 75

Trade the base relic piece, its matching -1 item, the material below and 30 Ordelle Bronzepiece together. Collect after the next Vana’diel midnight.
