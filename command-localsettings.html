Command/localsettings

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Aliases: none
Usage: /localsettings [subcommand] [number]
Description:

Changes various game settings.

Subcommands:
Shadows high/normal/off Toggle PC shadow display between high, normal, and off.
charanum [number] (25-50) Change number of displayed characters
distance [number] (0-10) Change draw distance
Footeffect on/off Turn footsteps sound effects on and off.
This command is not available on the Playstation 2 or the Xbox 360.
Example: /localsettings distance 10
