Walkthrough

  • Talk to Benita; she's having problems cleaning her chimney but says her mother-in-law has a secret for doing it easily.
  • Talk to the mother-in-law, Tete, to find out about Bomb Ash.
  • Trade a Bomb Ash to Benita to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Benita (House, Port Bastok)
Summary:

The chimney in Benita's house is clogged with soot. Her mother-in-law, who's gone outside with Benita's daughter, knows the perfect item for clearing chimneys. Go consult the elderly woman, and find the item.
