Before you begin

  • Access to Toraimarai Canal through the Three Mage Gate route or the Rhinostery Certificate entrance.

??? sites · Toraimarai Canal

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Toraimarai Canal and examine the sap ??? sites. Bring at least one elemental sap to the next fight; collecting all eight is recommended at level 75 because each weakens the matching avatar attack.
  2. Map 1 search areas: Dark F-8; Earth J-8/H-7; Water H-7/J-9; Fire J-9; Lightning H-7.
  3. Map 2 search areas: Fire F-10/J-8; Ice I-8/H-7; Light G-8; Lightning G-10/G-11; Wind H-9/J-9. Some elements have alternative spawn points, so inspect the listed alternatives if one is absent.
  4. Continue to Full Moon Fountain and examine the Moon Spiral for the scene that advances the mission.

Completion and reward

Elemental sap key items for the next battle.
