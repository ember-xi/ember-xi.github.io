Walkthrough

  • Naiko-Paneiko, in the "Tarutaru Times" offices at C-11 South Windurst Waters, wants you to track down several reporters and get their story.
  • Speak to the following:
  • Kyume-Romeh - Timbers Tavern in Windurst Waters
  • Yujuju - By the Air Travel Agency in Port Windurst
  • Hiwon-Biwon - Next to Shantotto's in Windurst Walls
  • Umumu - By the Dhalmel farm in Windurst Woods
  • Do not worry if you turned in the "Scoops" before checking up on Umumu's lead. Just proceed to next part.
  • Go to Inner Horutoto Ruins (Lilly Tower) through the tower at J-7 in East Sarutabaruta.
  • Go to G-8 and click on the cracked wall and follow the tunnels around to G-7/G-8 on the map on the right.
  • Follow the tunnel south and west (off the map) to the Mahagony door. Click on the door, watch a cutscene and return to Naiko-Paneiko to complete the quest.

If you get "You do not have COP installed on your account"

  • Talk to door to get red text saying COP isn't installed
  • Talk to door again and it will say "The door is firmly closed"
  • Now return to Naiko-Paneiko

Game Description

Client: Naiko-Paneiko (Tarutaru Times, Windurst Waters)
Summary:

The editor wants you to gather news articles from the reporters dispatched to each of the four districts of Windurst. In addition, he wants you to confirm the validity of the scoop reported by the reporter in Windurst Woods.
