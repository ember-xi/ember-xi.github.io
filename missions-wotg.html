Before you begin

  • Wings of the Goddess content enabled.

WotG alternates between its main story and a past nation’s quest chain. A qualifying chain from one nation is sufficient at the main-story gates; do not complete all three unless you want their separate stories.

The complete expansion is indexed here, including later chapters released after the original level-75 period. Known missing battle implementations are identified on their pages; the full campaign is not confirmed completable on Zenith.

San d’Oria [S] quest order

QuestWalkthroughRequirements
S-00Steamed RamsAccess the Crystal War through Cavernous Maws. For a first enlistment, obtain the Red recommendation letter from Randecque at Garlaige Citadel (S), I-6 on map 2. Do not have another nation’s recruitment quest currently active.
S-01Gifts of the GriffonFinish Steamed Rams, The Fighting Fourth, or Snake on the Plains. Have inventory space for seven Plumes d’Or.
S-02Claws of the GriffonComplete Gifts of the Griffon and wait until the next Vana’diel day.
S-03Boy and the BeastComplete Claws of the Griffon and the Back to the Beginning mission; Cait Sith should be active or later.
S-04Wrath of the GriffonComplete Boy and the Beast.
S-05Perils of the GriffonComplete Wrath of the Griffon and WotG Mission 7, Purple, The New Black. After Wrath, leave the zone and wait until the next Vana’diel day.
S-06In a Haze of GloryComplete Perils of the Griffon. Receive the Number eight shelter key from Diordinne.
S-07The Price of ValorComplete In a Haze of Glory, leave the zone and wait until the next Vana’diel day. For the intended story order, finish A Nation on the Brink and reach Crossroads of Time. Bring multiple Hatchets for logging.
S-08Bonds That Never DieComplete The Price of Valor. Bring Hatchets and leave inventory space for the quest’s supplied hatchet.
S-09Songbirds in a SnowstormComplete Bonds That Never Die, leave the zone and wait until the next Vana’diel day. Complete The Will of the World so Fate in Haze is active or later. Bring a fishing rod, a Flint Stone and inventory room for bait.
S-10Blood of HeroesComplete Songbirds in a Snowstorm. Receive Vial of military prism powder before entering the battlefield.
S-11Chasing ShadowsComplete Blood of Heroes and wait until the next Vana’diel day. For the intended story order, complete Darkness Descends and reach Adieu, Lilisette. Keep an inventory slot free for the reward.
S-12Face of the FutureComplete Chasing Shadows; this quest is accepted automatically. Unlock the Batallia Downs Cavernous Maw from its past side before the final scene.

B-04Burden of SuspicionComplete B-03: Light in the Darkness. Wait until the next Vana’diel day and change zones after that completion.
B-05Storm on the HorizonComplete B-04: Burden of Suspicion and collect the Elixir from Blatherix. After collecting it, wait until the next Vana’diel day and leave Bastok Markets [S].
B-06Fire in the HoleComplete B-05: Storm on the Horizon. Reach WotG 8: In the Name of the Father. Silvermine Key for the reference battle; bring healing and crowd control for an escort fight.
B-07Quelling the StormComplete B-06: Fire in the Hole. Reach WotG 15: Crossroads of Time. Bring one Goblin Mess Tin, one Goblin Mushpot, and one Twinkle Powder.
B-08Honor Under FireComplete B-07: Quelling the Storm. Reach WotG 15: Crossroads of Time.
B-09Beneath the MaskComplete B-08: Honor Under Fire. Reach WotG 26: Fate in Haze. Bring one Beeswax and one Red Textile Dye.
B-10What Price LoyaltyComplete B-09: Beneath the Mask. Reach WotG 26: Fate in Haze.
B-11The Truth Lies HidComplete B-10: What Price Loyalty. Reach WotG 38: Adieu, Lilisette. Bring one Elixir.
B-12Bonds of MythrilComplete B-11: The Truth Lies Hid. Reach WotG 38: Adieu, Lilisette. Keep the Sanctus Rosary and prepare a party for the open-area monster and final reference battle.
