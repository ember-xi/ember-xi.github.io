Notes
  • About 85,000 HP.
  • Uses normal buffalo TP moves.
    • Snort doesn't show up as a TP move in the log; it just shows a bunch of "Himinrjot hits <Player>" at the same time.
      • This Snort-like attack does conal damage and knockback and ignores Utsusemi.
      • If everyone stands against the wall, the knockback has no effect (but more people will get hit).
  • Hate is very random. Either the Snort move does hate reset, or it just randomly switches targets every few seconds. Even someone AFK away from the battle can get hate.
  • Very high Defense. Lowest when attacking from the back, highest when attacking from the front. Dia III + Angon recommended.
  • Elemental Magic damage is very effective. Thunder works pretty well, at least with Threnody on. Blizzard is mostly resisted.
  • Easily kited, as it stops to do its Snort attack.
