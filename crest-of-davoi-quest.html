Walkthrough

  1. Advance Magicite through the embassy, Audience Chamber and Aldo until you have the Silver Bell.
  2. Speak to Baudin in Upper Jeuno (G-8) and agree to obtain the requested meat.
  3. Get one Coeurl Meat from a documented monster source or an actual Auction House listing. Trade it to Baudin to receive the Crest of Davoi.
  4. In Davoi (G-7), examine the Wall of Dark Arts. Your crest opens the route into Monastic Cavern and its Magicite.
