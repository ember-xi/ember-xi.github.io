Other Uses

Resale Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

  • You can use a chocobo whistle to call your chocobo in the field to use as a mount if you registered it with a VCS member.
  • You cannot call your chocobo if you have hate from a mob.
  • You cannot call your chocobo while in the Shadowreign areas.
  • You cannot call your chocobo while Level Sync is active.
  • To recharge your whistle, trade it to a VCS member. You will have to pay 400 Gil for each refilled charge. (Total of 10,000gil, if recharging a completely empty whistle.)
  • Trade a Whistle Coupon and an old chocobo whistle to a VCS Trainer to obtain a new whistle.
  • If you have completed the chocobo whistle quest, have a whistle and retired your chocobo, when you raise a new chocobo and register to call it you will not have to repeat the quest, your whistle will automatically call your new chocobo.
  • If you have dropped your whistle, you will be required to pay 20,000g to get a new one from your VCS trainer.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
