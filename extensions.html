Addons, Plugins & DAT Packs

From Zenith XI Wiki

The Extensions list reflects files actually installed in Ashita.

Addons and plugins

  1. Use search and filters to find an extension. Choose Refresh after adding files.
  2. Turn each desired extension on or off and choose Save.
  3. Launch the game again to apply the saved load list.

DAT packs with XIPivot

  1. Close the game. From Tools, choose Install XIPivot.
  2. Place packs under polplugins/DATs/PackName/ROM/... while preserving the original ROM folder structure.
  3. Enable the packs and set their priority. The highest enabled pack wins if files conflict.
  4. Save and restart the game.

DAT packs have a separate list and priority order. They do not replace the original game files. No map or texture packs are bundled automatically. Extensions loaded by another script may be outside this list’s switches.
