Mission acquisition and use

Defeat Zhuu Buxu the Silent in Giddeus, map 2 around (H-7). On San d’Oria’s Windurst-first route, obtain two shields and trade both to Mourices in Windurst Woods (F-10), after delivering the separate Shield Offering to Uu Zhoumo.

Used in: 2-3 — Journey Abroad
