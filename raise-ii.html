Notes

Restores 75% of lost Experience Points. If target player is level 50 or below, Raise II only restores 50% of the lost Experience Points (like Raise) but still raises with 25% HP.

  • e.g. If a character KOs and loses 800 xp, he will regain 600 xp upon being raised
