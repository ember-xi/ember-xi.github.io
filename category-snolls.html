Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Hypothermal Combustion: AoE Ice damage based on Snoll's remaining HP and suicide. Easily stunnable.
Freeze Rush: Single target ice based attack
Cold Wave: AoE Frost with DoT for ~700 HP total
Berserk: Berserk effect
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
