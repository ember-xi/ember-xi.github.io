Walkthrough

  1. Reach main-job level 5 or higher.
  2. Speak to Gondebaud in Southern San d’Oria (L-6). Agree to participate and receive the Red institute card.
  3. Speak to Excenmille in Northern San d’Oria (D-9) to learn his Trust spell.
  4. For your first Trust quest, go to West Ronfaure or East Ronfaure. Outside combat, target yourself and cast Magic → Trust → Excenmille.
  5. Return to Excenmille and speak again to receive the San d’Oria Trust permit. Learning the spell alone does not finish this step.

Already have another permit?

After completing another city’s Trust quest, the San d’Oria path can skip the first summoning trial and award its permit through the appropriate completion dialogue.
