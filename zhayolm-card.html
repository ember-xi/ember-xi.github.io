Zhayolm Card

From Zenith XI Wiki

Salvage drop used to spawn Don Poroggo in a different Remnants area.

Contents
  1. Obtain it
  2. Use it
  3. Sources

Obtain it

Defeat archaic machinery in the original Zhayolm Remnants. Confirmed sources include First Rampart, Second Rampart and Fourth Rampart.

Use it

  1. Take the card into the original Silver Sea Remnants. Choose a northern second-floor teleporter to reach the north section of floor three.
  2. Defeat the Devilet there to reveal the Slot.
  3. Trade the Zhayolm Card to the Slot to spawn Don Poroggo.
  4. Defeat it for the level-15 Salvage body components Enyo's Breastplate and Nemain's Robe.
