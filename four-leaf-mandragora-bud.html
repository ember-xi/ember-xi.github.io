Statistics

Four-Leaf Mandragora Bud
The Tarutaru prize this extremely rare
four-leaved mandragora sprout,
said to bring good luck.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Food Effects: (1 hours, All Races)

  • Agility +3
  • Vitality -5
Note
  • There have been persistent rumors that this food also adds a Treasure Hunter Job Trait when eaten, or enhances the trait if one is active. To date, the existence of this effect has yet to be verified through experimentation. Several tests have shown that at the very least, it does not seem to affect Treasure Hunter II. A party of three hunting an exclusive drop used one each, one after the other and received no noticeable increase in rates

Other Uses

Used in Quests: Mandragora-Mad

NPC Sell Price: ~25 gil

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
