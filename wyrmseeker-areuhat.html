Wyrmseeker Areuhat

From Zenith XI Wiki

The Nyzul purchase required for the Mythic quest.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Turn in
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Earn 150,000 tokens through Nyzul Isle Investigation. Buy the book from Zasshal in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (K-9) after receiving Paparoon’s assignment.

Turn in

Trade it to Paparoon during Duties, Tasks, and Deeds. Tokens are distinct from normal Assault points and Imperial Standing.
