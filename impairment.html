Impairment

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Impairment blocks the use of any job abilities as well as weapon skills and pet commands. However, it does not affect job traits. The Impairment effect is removed when an Opacus Cell is used.

Item Effect
Pathos: Impairment
Opacus Cell Unlocks job abilities, weapon skills
