Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Lowers chance of being detected by enemies.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Very useful when pulling sound aggro enemies.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Sneaking Boots

Stealth

  • Obtained: Ninja Level 5

Notes

The conditions of how this ability kicks in seems to be random. Although you do have a chance to avoid detection, it's recommended to rely on this as a last resort as there is still a good chance the enemy will aggro you if they can. Remember that walking behind sight aggro mobs avoids aggro and it has been proven that this trait helps that particular function.
