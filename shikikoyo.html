Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Share TP above 100 with a party member.
  • Obtained: Samurai Level 75
  • Recast Time: 15:00 (-2:30 with each addtion merit)
  • Duration: Instant

Notes/Description

  • Any TP you have over 100 is transfered to target party member. This Ability has no effect when you have less than 100TP
  • You do not lose all of your TP using this ability. You will always be left with 100TP
  • Each increase in level lowers recast timer by 2:30. Highest level is three bringing the recast timer to 10:00

Macro Syntax

  • /ja Shikikoyo <stpc>
