Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Steal gil from enemy.
  • Obtained: Thief Level 35
  • Recast Time: 15:00
  • Duration: Instant

Notes/Description

  • Gil can only be stolen from Beastmen and some Notorious Monsters.
  • Usage of this ability will add a small amount of enmity to the user.
  • Usage of this ability will cause a disengaged enemy to engage the user.
  • Usage of this ability in Ballista will allow the user to steal petras.
  • The amount of gil you can mug depends on the amount of gil the monster can drop.
  • Assassin's Bonnet causes the THF to mug a larger percentage of a monster's gil than usual.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Mug" <t>
