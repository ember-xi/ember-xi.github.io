Notes

  • This leech can be distinguished from the other Bloodsuckers in the area by its blue skin.
  • Spawns randomly throughout the third map every 72 hours or 21-24 hours after server maintenance.
  • Appears as the last Bloodsucker on Wide Scan.
  • Has around 3,600 HP
  • Has an Additional Effect of Drain on normal attacks.
  • Can be defeated by a party of 6 level 70 characters; Soloable by Red Mage and Black Mage at level 75.
