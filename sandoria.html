The Kingdom of San d'Oria is the Elvaan capital in the north of the Quon continent, ruled by King Destin. It is the city of knights and the cathedral. Your adventure starts in West Ronfaure and Ghelsba. Missions from Rank 1 to 10 come from the Gate Guards, then from Halver in Chateau d'Oraguille. Rank 5 gives the Airship Pass and Rank 6 unlocks Rise of the Zilart.

Missions

These missions are for players allied with San d'Oria. Most of them need Rank points before you can start the next one. Every mission gives points; if you do not have enough, trade crystals to a Conquest Guard.
