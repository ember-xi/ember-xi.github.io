The Davoi Report (San d'Oria 2-2)

← 2-1 The Rescue Drill · San d'Oria Missions · 2-3 Journey Abroad →

San d'Oria Mission 2-2
MissionThe Davoi Report
TypeRepeatable
Start NPCGate Guard (Endracion or Ambrotien in Southern San d'Oria; Grilau in Northern San d'Oria)
LocationDavoi
RequirementsRank 2 · 2-1 complete
Items NeededNone
Rewards350 Rank points

Mission 2-2 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 2-1 The Rescue Drill.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission from a Gate Guard.
  2. Go to Davoi (west through Jugner Forest). Examine the ! marker to get the Lost Document.
  3. Talk to Zantaviat inside Davoi. He gives you the Temple Knights Davoi Report.
  4. Return to a Gate Guard.
Mechanics: Davoi monsters are level 20-30. If you are lower, use Sneak and Invisible from a Trust (Koru-Moru) or Prism Powder and Silent Oil.
