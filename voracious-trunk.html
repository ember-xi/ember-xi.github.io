Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Steals one beneficial effect from an enemy.

Description

Spell cost: 72 MP
Ecosystem: Beasts
Type: Magical (Wind)
Blue Magic Points: 4
Stat Bonus: AGI+2
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 64

Timers

Casting Time: 10 seconds
Recast Time: 56 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Voracious Trunk as part of a combo to create Auto Refresh.

Can be paired with some of the following spells to create the Auto Refresh trait. Please refer to the Auto Refresh section of the Blue Mage Job Traits page for details.

  • Stinking Gas
  • Cold Wave
  • Frightful Roar
  • Self-Destruct
  • Light of Penance
  • Actinic Burst
  • Plasma Charge

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info
