Scholar Relic Upgrades

From Zenith XI Wiki

Recipes for all five level 75 Argute +1 pieces.

Each recipe uses its matching base Argute armor, the matching damaged -1 piece, one crafted material, and 28 1 Byne Bill. Trade all items together to Sagheera in Port Jeuno (I-8), after unlocking Sea. Collect after the next Vana'diel midnight.

UpgradeDamaged piece areaCrafted material
Argute Mortarboard +1Dynamis - ValkurmPlatinum Ingot
Argute Gown +1Dynamis - TavnaziaScarlet Linen
Argute Bracers +1Dynamis - BuburimuLynx Leather
Argute Pants +1Dynamis - TavnaziaEther Leather
Argute Loafers +1Dynamis - QufimIncombustible Wool

Damaged -1 pieces come from Nightmare enemies in Dreamland Dynamis. They are materials, not wearable armor. Argute Attire Set lists where the original armor drops.
