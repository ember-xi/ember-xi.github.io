Notes:

  • Spawned by trading a Mint Drop to a ??? at (J-6) in Caedarva Mire. Take Azouph Isle Staging Point.
  • (Note: Zoning through Arrapago Reef is not required. The ??? is near the bottom right corner of J-6, nearly on the borderline.)
  • The two imps near the ??? respawn every 5 minutes. Additionally, a Thunder Elemental spawns nearby during thundery weather.
  • Spams -agas tier I - III, and tier II - IV single target nukes. Has a very strong Fast Cast.
  • A single WHM can use Curaga and Curaga II without pulling hate from a PLD and keep an entire party's HP full.
  • Alternatively, AoE Stoneskin from SCH, SMN, or BLU can be used to reduce damage from AoEs.
  • Uses standard Imp Abilities with normal duration.
  • Melee attacks are relatively weak, but Frenetic Rip can deal up to 1,300 damage against melee.
  • Resistant to Enfeebling Magic. Susceptible to Paralyze II, though.
  • Immune to Silence, Gravity and Slow.
  • Builds resistance to Stun, but the BLU spell Head Butt lands very frequently.
  • His horn breaks more often than normal Imps.
  • Fairly low HP and defense.
