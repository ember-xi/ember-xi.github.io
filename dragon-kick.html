Dragon Kick

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  • Skill level: 225 (Monks and Puppetmasters only.)
  • Damage varies with TP.
  • Dragon Kick is not affected by equipment that increases Kick Attacks damage such as Kung Fu Shoes.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Fragmentation
  • Modifiers: STR:50% ; VIT:50%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
2.00 2.75 3.50

Levels

  • Dragon Kick is only available to Monks and Puppetmasters. While other jobs can obtain the required 225 Hand-to-Hand skill through the use of merits and special equipment, they will be unable to use Dragon Kick.
  • Hand-to-Hand skill level 225 is obtainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
Job Rating Level
Monk A+ 65
Puppetmaster B 69
