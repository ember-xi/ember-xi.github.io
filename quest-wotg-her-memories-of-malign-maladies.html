Before you begin

  • Her Memories is the current Wings of the Goddess mission.
  • One Philosopher's Stone.

Starting point

Amaura · Southern San d'Oria · G-6

Era note: September 2010 WotG memory chapter, released after the original level-75 cap.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Amaura (G-6), then Monberaux in Upper Jeuno (G-10).
  2. Examine the Library Book on the eastern wall of the Optistery in northern Windurst Waters (G-7).
  3. Trade one Philosopher's Stone to the Fey Blossoms in Witchfire Glen, Grauberg [S] (F-5).
  4. Leave the zone and wait until the next Vana'diel day. Return to the Fey Blossoms and examine them for the Fey stone.
  5. Report to Raustigne in Southern San d'Oria [S] (I-7) for the fragment.

Completion and reward

Large memory fragment II.
