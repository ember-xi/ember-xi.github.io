Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


This particular class of Mamool Ja mount trained wivres for the purpose of combat. They are skilled in the throwing angons and are decent in the art of white magic. They mostly rely on the wivre for physical power, namely defense.

General Information

  • System (Mamool Ja): Beastmen/Bestiary
  • System (Wivre): Lizards
  • Weaknesses: Wind, Ice
  • Immune:

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Fire Angon: AoE fire damage.
Blazing Angon: AoE fire damage. Only used by Notorious Monsters.Verification Needed
Demoralizing Roar: AoE Attack Down.
Clobber: Uses tail to attack, cone AoE damage.
Batterhorn: Frontal cone damage.
Granite Hide: Parrying / Guard Up.
Boiling Blood: Gains Haste and Berserk effect. Will always show "Haste" effect in log, and never Berserk.
Ranged Attack: Throws an angon at the target. (Normal ranged attack.)
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
