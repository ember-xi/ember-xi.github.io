Incombustible Wool

From Zenith XI Wiki

Clothcraft material, abbreviated Incomb. Wool in the game.

Buy from Auction House → Materials → Clothcraft. One square is required for Argute Loafers +1. Ordinary Wool Cloth is a different item and cannot replace it.

The synthesis uses Clothcraft37 and Cloth Ensorcellment: Earth Crystal, Wool Thread ×3, Fire Anima ×1, Ice Anima ×1 and Dark Anima ×1. A crafter with the required key item can make it.
