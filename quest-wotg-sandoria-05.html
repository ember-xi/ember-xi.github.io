Before you begin

  • Complete Wrath of the Griffon and WotG Mission 7, Purple, The New Black.
  • After Wrath, leave the zone and wait until the next Vana’diel day.

Starting point

Rholont · Southern San d'Oria (S) · E-7

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Rholont for the opening scene. Leave and re-enter the zone, then speak to him for the follow-up.
  2. Speak to Daigraffeaux at I-11, near the chocobo stables. Return to Rholont for the assignment.
  3. Examine the tree-stump ??? at Jugner Forest (S) I-6, east of the river, to obtain the Orcish warmachine body.
  4. Return to Rholont and finish the conversation for the reward.

Battle and progress notes

  • This is a collection/story step; no new NM fight is required.

Completion and reward

Elixir; title: Knight of the Swiftwing Griffin.

What this opens

  • In a Haze of Glory
