Notes:

  • Spawned at the "???" at F-11 by trading Second Virtue, Deed of Moderation, and HQ Xzomit Organ.
  • It can summon up to 6 Qn'xzomits.
    • Like normal Xzomits, they move very fast.
    • They have 2,000~ HP and use Mijin Gakure not too long after being attacked. This is reminiscent of Bomb Queen's Self-Destructing helpers.
    • The Jailer of Justice summons them one at a time, but summons often.
  • The Jailer can charm people for long periods of time--multiple minutes.
  • Charm Lasts 5 full minutes unresisted.
  • The Jailer itself seems rather weak and not too special, but its helpers seem to give people the most trouble.


Dialogue

"Desssireth...thou?

Sssecond...irtue...
