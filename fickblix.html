Notes

A kindly Goblin living in Jeuno, and a friend of Verena. Despite the animosity toward beastmen, he tries to help improve the community, even helping children grow a flower garden.

Involved in Missions

  • San d'Oria Mission 4-1: Magicite
  • Bastok Mission 4-1: Magicite
  • Windurst Mission 4-1: Magicite










Image source SPOILER WARNING: Details about the Final Fantasy XI in-game storyline follow.Image source

Tragically, Fickblix meets his demise when he attempts to speak with the Yagudo at Castle Oztroja. The player and Verena find him dying near the Magicite deposits.
