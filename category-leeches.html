General Information

  • System: Amorphs

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Suction - Single target damage. Sometimes stuns target
Brain Drain - Single target darknessVerification Needed damage and [[Attribute Down|INT down.
Regeneration - Leech gains regen effect
Drainkiss: - Single target HP drain
MP Drainkiss - Single target MP drain
TP Drainkiss: - Single target TP drain
Sand Breath - Cone Attack damage plus blindness
Acid Mist: - high AoE damage, 50% attack down effect.
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
