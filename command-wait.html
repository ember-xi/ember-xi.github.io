Command/wait

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Aliases: None
Usage: /wait [wait time]
<wait [wait time]>
Description: A macro command for adjusting the time between commands.

A wait time of 1 would equal one second.
The maximum wait time allowed is 60.
If the wait time amount is over 60, it will be counted as 0.

Example: /wait 30

/ma "Protect" <t> <wait 5>
