Wind

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

File:Wind icon.JPG The element of Wind is strong against Earth and weak to Ice. In-game, wind is represented by the color green.

Wind is associated with movement and Agility; many Thief abilities have some basis in wind, for example.

Associated with Detonation, Fragmentation, and Light skillchain. Governed by the celestial avatar Garuda.
