Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Dagger weapon skill

  • Skill level: 100 (Thief, Red Mage, Bard, Ranger, Corsair, and Ninja exclusive.)
  • Deals double damage and Poisons target. Duration of poison varies with TP.
  • Despite the animation showing two swings, this is a single-hit weapon skill.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Scission
  • Modifiers: None
  • Viper Bites damage is calculated by doubling your current attack for 1 blow.
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.00 1.00 1.00

Common Skillchain Usage

Viper Bite is the game's primary closer of Distortion from levels 33 to 59. Coupled with Sneak Attack and Trick Attack, a Thief's Viper Bite damage makes it the optimal choice for large Distortion chains.

Levels

  • Viper Bite is only natively available to Thieves, Red Mages, Bards, Rangers, Corsairs, and Ninjas. While other jobs can obtain the required 100 Dagger skill, they must use one of the aforementioned jobs as their support job in order to gain access to Viper Bite.
