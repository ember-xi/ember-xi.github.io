Guarding Skill

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is a Combat Skill that Monks and Puppetmasters have. It decreases the amount of damage that the player takes from a melee hit when it activates, similarly to a Shield Trigger.

Guard only works when the player is using a Hand-to-Hand weapon, or no weapon at all.

The higher your Guarding Skill, the better the chance that it will activate.
