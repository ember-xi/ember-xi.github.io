"Knights of Round"
Lv. 75 RDM / PLD

Damage Per Second: 12.62
TP Per Hit: 62

Additional effect: Damage proportionate to current HP

  • Deals damage equal to 25% of the wielder's HP. This is affected by modifiers which increase and decrease damage.
  • The additional damage does not add to enmity.


Hidden Effect

  • Attacks will occasionally do 2.5 times normal damage.

Other Uses

Resale Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
