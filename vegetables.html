Vegetables can be classified into two different types, based on the effect they give you upon use. The first type is Veggie Snacks, which all last 30-60 minutes and stackable to 12. The second type is Salads and Sautees, which are all 180+ minutes and non-stackable. Majority of all vegetable dishes offer Agility and Ranged Accuracy, jobs that benefit the most are Corsairs, Rangers, and Thieves. Uniquely though, all mushroom-base dishes offer Mind, that are most beneficial to White Mages.
