Statistics

One Hundred Byne Bill
This ancient Bastokan currency dates
back to C.E. 857. It commemorates the
opening of the ferry route between
Mhaura and Selbina. A machine cog and
a Hume technician are portrayed.

Stackable: 99

Other Uses

Used in Quest: Relic Weapons

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be sold on the Auction House or delivered, but can be traded and bazaared.
