Domain Invasion

Domain Invasion
WhereEscha - Zi'Tah, Escha - Ru'Aun, Reisenjima
StartZolgrip, Lower Jeuno (beside Halvard)
TypeOpen-world boss battle, three-zone circuit
FormatThree waves of heralds, then the dragon
AlliesSix adventurers fight at your side
TrustsAllowed
DeathNo XP loss inside
Rewards
Domain Points, Alexandrite, and one weapon from the Domain Coffer after every three zones

A dragon is loose in one of the three Eschan lands. Go there, hold the domain against its heralds, and bring the dragon down. The domain moves to the next land after each victory: Zi'Tah, then Ru'Aun, then Reisenjima. Clear all three and the Domain Coffer holds a weapon.

How to enter

StepWhat to do
1Talk to Zolgrip in Lower Jeuno, next to Halvard. He tells you where the domain rages now.
2Talk to him again within 15 seconds and he sends you straight to the battle.
3You arrive inside the arena with the Elvorseal. Summon your Trusts — the first wave comes one minute later.
4You can also walk in: talk to Affi (Escha - Zi'Tah, F-10), Dremi (Escha - Ru'Aun, H-11) or Shiftrix (Reisenjima, F-12) and ask to be taken to the battle.

The battle

ZoneHeraldsDragonHP

Escha - Ru'AunNaga Raja's LamiaNaga Raja45,000
ReisenjimaQuetzalcoatl's SibilusQuetzalcoatl40,000
Any zone (rare)—Mireu80,000

Three waves of heralds come first: 3, then 4, then 5. When the last wave falls the dragon descends. Heralds are level 72–74; the dragons are level 78. Mireu takes the dragon's place about once in twenty battles and must be beaten within one hour.

In Reisenjima the fight takes place in the sealed arena at Ethereal Ingress #7, outside the normal map.

Six adventurers stand with you in the arena. They attack every herald and the dragon on their own, buff the party, and cannot be attacked by you. An ally that falls returns to the fight 45 seconds later at full strength.

Rewards

RewardWhen
Domain Points10–15 per dragon, ×1.5 on your first battle of the day
AlexandriteFrom the coffer after every dragon (3 pieces; 10 for Mireu)
One weaponFrom the coffer after clearing all three zones in a row

A Domain Coffer appears in the arena when the dragon falls. Open it for your share; you are returned to Zolgrip in Lower Jeuno twenty seconds later.

Domain Coffer weapons
Joyeuse · Hagun · Mercurial Kris · Kraken Club · Ridill · Blau Dolch · Speed Belt · Peacock Charm

Each prize comes once. When you own them all, the coffer gives 5 Alexandrite instead.

Zurim — spending Domain Points

Zurim
WhereNorg (I-7), beside Oseem under the wooden footbridge
Talks to youOnce you hold at least 10 Domain Points
Daily limit80 Domain Points a day (Mireu's points do not count)

Zurim keeps the tally of your Domain Points and trades them for goods. Talk to him and pick from the list.

PointsItemNotes
101 AlexandriteSame stone the Domain Coffer gives
405 Dynamis currencyChoose One Byne Bill, Ordelle Bronzepiece or Tukuku Whiteshell — for Teodor's relic ladder
801 AbjurationChoose the set, then the slot. Aquarian (Zenith), Dryadic (Shura), Earthen (Koenig / Adaman), Martial (Hecatomb), Neptunal (Crimson / Blood), Wyrmal (Dalmatica)

An Abjuration is not armor by itself. Buy the matching Cursed piece (Auction House, Others → Cursed Items, or a crafter), then trade both to Alphollon C Meriard in the Cathedral of Northern San d'Oria (L-6, Manuscript Room). He lifts the curse and hands you the finished piece.

Mechanics: The next invasion begins 5 minutes after the last one ends, in the next zone of the circuit. There is no time limit except against Mireu. Deaths inside the domain cost no experience. Everyone in the zone who joins the fight shares the reward.
