Dragon Harness Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This Thief-specific set adds significant bonuses to DEX, AGI, Attack, and Enmity, all very important in the Thief's trademark SATA-enhanced weaponskill. The "Subtle Blow" bonuses ensure that the attacks not only do massive damage but also do not add significant TP to the enemy. This set, crafted from materials only scavenged from wyrms and their brethren, is highly prized and most effective. The high-quality set is even more effective, and naturally significantly more expensive.
