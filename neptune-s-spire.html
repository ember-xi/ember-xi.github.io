Description

On the outside, Neptune's Spire is a small shop in Lower Jeuno that offers small rooms to rent. But in reality its the secret headquarters of the Tenshodo Guild. These thieves and smugglers bring goods from the Far East into Jeuno, under the noses of the diligent Ducal Guards. To become a member of this secretive guild, one must learn to read between the lines...

Location: Lower Jeuno (I-7)

Shop type: Tenshodo Guild Headquarters
