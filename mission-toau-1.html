Before you begin

  • Boarding Permit key item; Treasures of Aht Urhgan content enabled.

Starting point

Salaheem's Sentinels · Aht Urhgan Whitegate · I-10

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Obtain the Boarding Permit through The Road to Aht Urhgan in Lower Jeuno.
  2. Travel to Mhaura and board the mercenary transport bound for Al Zahbi. It arrives in Aht Urhgan Whitegate.
  3. Enter Salaheem's Sentinels at I-10 and walk toward Naja Salaheem. The scene starts as you enter her office area.
  4. Finish the scene and check your temporary key items for the Supplies Package.

Battle and progress notes

  • The opening mission has no separate job-level or nation-rank check in the pinned server script. Travel through hostile territory comes next.

Completion and reward

Supplies Package key item.

What this opens

  • ToAU 2: Immortal Sentries
