Walkthrough

  • Talk to Nivorajean at (I-9) on the main floor of the Tavnazian Safehold to receive the quest.
  • Head to the Sacrarium.
  • Obtain a Sacrarium Chest Key.
  • Locate a Treasure Chest, but, before you open it, note the <pos> where it is and write it down.
  • Open the Treasure Chest to obtain the Piece of Ripped Floorplans (key item).
  • Head back to Nivorajean who will ask you where you found the floorplans. He will first show you the map on which the treasure chests are found, and then give you a listing of locations from which you must choose the <pos> where your treasure chest was. You may choose to take another look at the map, by selecting "Think a little longer." but you cannot cancel the NPC dialog unless you forcibly disconnect (and you will presumably keep the Piece of Ripped Floorplans key item)
  • After one game-day, if you chose the correct <pos>, he will reward you with the Map of the Sacrarium.

Game Description

Client: Nivorajean (Second floor, Tavnazian Safehold)
Summary:

To complete a map of the Sacrarium, Nivorajean needs you to find a piece of the old floorplans.
