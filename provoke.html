Provoke

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Goads an enemy into attacking you.
  • Obtained: Warrior Level 5
  • Recast Time: 0:30
  • Duration: Instant

Notes/Description

  • Upon usage, a large amount of enmity is added to the user.
  • Amount of enmity accrued with this ability increases as the user's level increases.
  • Provoke is one of the most efficient and effective means of generating and maintaining hate in a party.
  • In Ballista and Brenner, this ability temporarily makes you the opponent's target and prevents them from switching targets (which also prevents the opponent from using any self-cast abilities or spells).
  • A Puppetmaster's Automaton can use this ability by having a Strobe equipped and a Fire Maneuver in place.
  • Provoke is NOT affected by Charisma.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Provoke" <t>
  • /ja "Provoke" <bt>
  • /ja "Provoke" <stnpc>
