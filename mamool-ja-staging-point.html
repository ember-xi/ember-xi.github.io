Mamool Ja Staging Point

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Mamool Ja Staging Point is the Staging Point for Assault missions in Mamool Ja Training Grounds.
It is located at Bhaflau Thickets (2nd map) H-11.

Directions

To get there from Al Zahbi:

  • Rent a chocobo in Al Zahbi, or use the Aht Urhgan Whitegate exit to Wajaom Woodlands at H-10. It is the little tunnel on the left side of the south square where the ship from Mhaura docks.
  • Travel west, then south until D-12 and enter Mamook.
  • In the first room, take the south exit down the ramp, and continue south off the ledge.
  • Cross the room to the other side, and go up the ramp at the border of H-9 and I-9.
  • Head west across the small bridge, then turn south to enter the cave at H-9. You will see lots of spiders.
  • From there, it is mostly west, but you have to do a n-w-s-w round-the-blockage dance to get to G-10 which leads to Bhaflau Thickets.
    • The above line was confusing for me. This is what I did:
      • At H-10 go north, then west. At the next bend you have to go north again, but don't fall down the drop. Go west instead. Follow the bend south, then east. Again, don't fall down the ledge. Go south and follow the bound around to the zone to Bhaflau Thickets. This essentially is, when you enter the cave at H-10, you're facing west. Then the bends are as follows, without falling off ledges: N-W-N-W-S-E-S-W-Zone. Use the map below if needed.
  • You will be on the southern half of the second map. Head south to H-11.
  • Enter Imperial Compound through the Heavy Iron Gate.
