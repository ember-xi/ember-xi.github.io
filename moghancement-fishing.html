Moghancement: Fishing

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description
"Moogle-powered assistance from your Mog House. Increases the chances of finding items when fishing."
Notes
This makes fishing up items more likely - notice that this doesn't affect your chances to fish up fish! The stronger this moghancement is, the greater your chances of fishing up items are. See Moghancements for more information.
It is obtained through these Furnishings:
  • 3-Drawer Almirah
  • 6-Drawer Almirah
  • 9-Drawer Almirah
  • Arcane Flowerpot
  • Armoire
  • Armor Box
  • Cabinet
  • Cartonnier
  • Ceramic Flowerpot
  • Chest
  • Coffer
  • Commode
  • Earthen Flowerpot
  • Kadomatsu
  • Porcelain Flowerpot
  • Rusty Bucket
  • Wooden Flowerpot
