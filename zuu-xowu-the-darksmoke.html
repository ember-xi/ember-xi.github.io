Zuu Xowu the Darksmoke

From Zenith XI Wiki

The Black Mage Yagudo in the level-60 Divine Punishers BCNM.

Contents
  1. How to encounter
  2. Battle and rewards
  3. Sources

How to encounter

Enter Divine Punishers at Balga's Dais with a Moon Orb. The original encounter allows six players and lasts 30 minutes.

Battle and rewards

Zuu Xowu can use Manafont once. Control the other Yagudo and interrupt dangerous magic as your party’s strategy allows. Rewards belong to the battlefield’s Armoury Crate; use the Divine Punishers loot table. This page covers the original BCNM, not the later high-tier Divine Punishers II.
