Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Blinds an enemy, lowers its accuracy.

Description

Spell cost: 31 MP
Spell element: Dark
Magic skill: Enfeebling Magic
Jobs:

  • Red Mage lvl 75


Merit Info:

  • Each additional merit increases blind effect by 1 and magic accuracy by 2.

Timers

Casting Time: 3 seconds
Recast Time: 20 seconds

Other Information

  • Blind II will overwrite Ninjutsu spell Kurayami: Ichi and Blind.
  • Blind II and Kurayami: Ni do not overwrite one another regardless of the level it's merited.

How to Obtain

Obtained Merit Point Group 2

Notes

  • Blind II (Amount physical accuracy is decreased)
  • Lowest value at -80 player INT compared to enemy MND: 15
  • Highest value at +120 player INT compared to enemy MND: 90
