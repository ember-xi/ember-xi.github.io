Fisherman's Tunica Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A set of armor designed from the ground up to give fishermen an advantage when working. Because of this, the bonus to fishing skill is noticable, but the set grants no other stats, except very poor bonuses in defense. Though this may be a moot point to many fisherman, those seeking to fish in areas where they be attacked either by enemies in the area or by monsters they reel in may wish to upgrade to the stronger Angler's Tunica Set.
