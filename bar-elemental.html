Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Bar Elemental is a beneficial status effect that surrounds a player with an element until it wears off or is removed. Benefitting characters gain resistance to a specific element, reducing damage and effect duration of spells and effects of that element.

How to remove the effect

  • This effect can be dispelled.
  • This effect can be removed manually.
  • Only one Bar Element spell can be cast at one time, and any new Bar Element effect will replace the previous one.

How the effect is inflicted/gained

  • White Mage Party-Buffs
    • Barstonra
    • Barwatera
    • Baraera
    • Barfira
    • Barblizzara
    • Barthundra
  • Red Mage Self-Buffs
    • Barstone
    • Barwater
    • Baraero
    • Barfire
    • Barblizzard
    • Barthunder

Enhanced By

Gear

  • Cleric's Pantaloons enhances resistance by +20
  • Cleric's Pantaloons +1 enhances resistance by +22

Merit Points

  • White Mage Group 1 Job-Specific Merit Points (+2 potency of Bar spells, +2 Magic Defense Bonus per upgrade)
