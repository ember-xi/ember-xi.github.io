Purple Ballista Chevron

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description
"Presented only to those dragoons who have displayed excellence on the Ballista battlefield. Purple Ballista chevron must be returned to a Herald after set period indicated in official Ballista rulebook."
Notes
You will get this key item when you are first at Ballista Ranking at the category: Dragoon.
With this chevron you are the owner of the title Spearing★Star for one earth week.


For further information on chevron and Ballista Ranking see Ballista.
