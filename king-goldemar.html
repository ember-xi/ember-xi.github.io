Notes:

  • Spawns for Assault Mission - The Price is Right
  • Extremely resistant to debuffs and stun
  • Can cast Sleepga and tier-3 -ga spells
  • Hellsnap: AoE Stun.
  • Hellclap: ?
  • Cackle: AoE Magic Attack down, Magic Acc down, and Magic Def down.
  • Thundris Shriek: Terror and AoE Damage (300-400)
  • Necrobane: AoE Paralyze
  • Necropurge: AoE Curse
  • King Goldemar summons some items when he is aggroed. They will hover above and cannot be attacked until they are called down one at a time by the King.
  • Living Staves - Casts Black Mage spells (including tier-3 -ga spells)
  • Cursed Axe - Melee damage (Constant Mighty Strikes)
  • Bloody Daggers - Melee damage (Constant Hundred Fists)
  • Magic Shields - Shield Bash (Does not use Regular attacks, Higher defense/hp)
  • Demonic Rod - Casts White Mage spells (including Cure V)
  • Will intimidate players more as he summons his weapons. At the end of the battle, he will intimidate more often than not.
