Mighty Strikes

From Zenith XI Wiki
Contents
  1. Job Ability
  2. Notes/Description
  3. Macro Syntax

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Turns all melee attacks into critical hits.
  • Obtained: Warrior Level 1
  • Recast Time: 2:00:00
  • Duration: 0:00:45

Notes/Description

  • Mighty Strikes is a Warrior's two hour special.
  • When active, all attacks made by the user result in critical hits.
  • This ability has no effect on accuracy, as attacks made while Mighty Strikes is active may still miss.
  • Also affects physical weapon skills used while ability is active.
  • Use of this ability with multi-hit weapon skills can produce extremely high damage (Example: Sturmwind, Raging Rush, Rampage).

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Mighty Strikes" <me>
  • Since Mighty Strikes is a two hour special, some people prefer not to make a macro for it to prevent unintentional activation.
