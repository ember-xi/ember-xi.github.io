Zenith availability

Reference walkthrough: the pinned server core contains story scenes but leaves the Distorter of Time battle in Ruhotz Silvermines unfinished or unconnected. This is not confirmed playable on Zenith.

Before you begin

  • Complete Dungeons and Dancers.
  • Carry the Umbra bug awarded by Dungeons and Dancers.

Starting point

Regal Pawprints · Beaucedine Glacier [S] · H-7

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Reach the lower ledge in the southeast of (H-7); a hole near the Campaign arrival plateau provides access.
  2. Examine the Regal Pawprints between the rocks with the Umbra bug to enter Ruhotz Silvermines.
  3. Defeat Cait Sith Ceithir while protecting Lilisette. Atomos periodically drains attributes and transfers them to Ceithir; keep healing available through these weakened periods.
  4. Finish the victory scene. After failure, a replacement Umbra bug comes from Naoi's pawprints (H-10), subject to the Vana'diel-day retry restriction.

Battle and progress notes

  • The pinned core has an incomplete main-story battle at mission 14; later story handlers do not establish that this chapter is currently reachable on Zenith.
