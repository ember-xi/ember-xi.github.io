Notes:

  • Lottery spawn from a Birdtrap placeholder.
    • The placeholder can be located on widescan, near the bottom. There will be a Sabertooth Tiger, a Birdtrap, and a Diving Beetle, followed by more Birdtraps. The Birdtrap between the Tiger and the Diving Beetle is Orctrap's placeholder. Lightning weather is prevalent in the area and a Thunder Elemental may also be present between the Sabertooth Tiger and the Diving Beetle.
    • The Beetle may not always appear, if you do not see the above setup, look for the Pugils at the bottom of the list. The placeholder is the first of 3 Birdtraps above this, underneath the Tiger.
  • Respawn is approximately every 1-3 hours, but has been known to take as long as 24 hours.
  • Killable by: Solo at level 50. Soloable as lvl40 BST.
