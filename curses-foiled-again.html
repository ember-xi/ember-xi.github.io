Walkthrough

  • You need to bring Shantotto a Bomb Ash and two Bone Chips. Both drop in Inner Horutoto Ruins -- the ash from balloons and the bone chips from skeletons.

Game Description

Client: Shantotto (Manor, Windurst Walls)
Summary:

Shantotto is being bothered by an unseen Peeping Tom. To get revenge, she wants you to gather her two bone chips and some bomb ash.
