Prelude

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Prelude is a type of Enhancing Song used by Bards. It increases the Ranged Accuracy stat for the targeted party member.

Two songs give this effect. Different tiers can be combined.

Notes

  • This is one of the four types of Enhancing Songs that has no Area of Effect. The others are Etude, Foe Sirvente, and Adventurer's Dirge.
  • Prelude does not improve melee accuracy. Madrigal may be used for this purpose.


Songs are learned by Bards only, at the following levels:

31: Hunter's Prelude
71: Archer's Prelude
