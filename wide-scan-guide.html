Wide Scan Guide

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Scans the area for monsters, revealing them on the map.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Each upgrade of this trait increases the area of the scan.
    • In town a player's wide scan appears to be enhanced, showing more of the map than it would show in an outside area or dungeon.
      • i.e. Wide Scan I would become Wide Scan II, Wide Scan II would become III, etc.
      • It is possible Square Enix designed Wide Scan this way to benefit players searching for NPCs in town.
      • A player can go to a town's Mog House and select the sub job BST01 or RNG01 and still benefit during quests from the higher level Wide Scan without having to level the job.
    • The upgrades are unofficial and are not reflected in your abilities menu.
    • See the Wide Scan Guide for additional information.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • None

Wide Scan I

  • Range: 50'
  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 1
    • Approx 1/2 map grid square

Wide Scan II

  • Range: 150'
  • Obtained: Ranger Level 1
  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 20
    • Approx 2 map grid squares

Wide Scan III

  • Obtained: Ranger Level 20
  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 40

Wide Scan IV

  • Obtained: Ranger Level 40
  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 60

Wide Scan V

  • Obtained: Ranger Level 60
