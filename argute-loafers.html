Argute Loafers

From Zenith XI Wiki

Feet · SCH level 72 · Relic armor.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Upgrade
  3. Sources

DEF 13; MP +20; Healing Magic skill +7; weather enhances Celerity and Alacrity.

How to obtain

Obtain the base piece from Dynamis - Windurst or Dynamis - Buburimu. Follow Dynamis for entry requirements and the zone guide for enemy routes. Drops are not guaranteed.

Upgrade

Trade the base piece, Argute Loafers -1, Incombustible Wool and 28 1 Byne Bill to Sagheera for Argute Loafers +1.
