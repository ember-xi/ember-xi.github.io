Ferrol

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Quest Giver

Location: Port Bastok (I-7)

Notes

Will warp main-job Summoners to Cloister of Tremors if traded the Mini Tuning Fork of Earth while Trial Size Trial by Earth is open. Most SMNs never complete this quest so that a permanent warp to Cloister of Tremors is available. He will also provide a new mini fork at any time if the old one was dropped.

Starts Quests

  • Trial Size Trial by Earth
