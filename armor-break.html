Armor Break

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Great Axe weapon skill

  • Skill level: 100
  • Lowers enemy's defense. Duration of effect varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: Wind
  • Modifiers: STR:20% ; VIT:20%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.00 1.00 1.00
  • Lowers defense by 25% File:Wind icon.JPG
  • Land rate of the additional effects use Combat Skill as a 1:1 analogue to Magic Accuracy.
  • Has three resist states, full duration, half duration, and full resist.
  • Does not stack with: Acid Bolt, Angon, Shell Crusher, Frightful Roar, Armor Break.

Levels

  • Great Axe skill level 100 is obtainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
