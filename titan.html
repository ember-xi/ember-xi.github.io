Titan

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Avatar Information

A legendary giant said to bear the world in its arms. Aids the summoner by shifting earth and rocks with its enormous strength.

Summoners can acquire the ability to summon Titan by defeating Titan Prime in either the Trial Size Trial by Earth quest at level 20, or the Trial by Earth quest at a much higher level (65+).
