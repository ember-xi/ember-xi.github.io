Notes:

  • When pulled, the Animated Staff spawns 4 Satellite Staves which assist it during the fight.
  • After a short period of time has passed, the Animated Staff will cast Warp and despawn. Its Warp can be interrupted.
    • From its dialogue, it appears to like magic damage more than melee damage. This may help you kill it before it attempts to Warp.
  • The Animated Staff uses the weapon skill Gate of Tartarus.
  • The Animated Staff can cast Sleepga and Breakga, as well as other earth elemental spells.
  • The Satellite Staves can cast Stone III and Cure IV.
  • The Satellite Staves despawn when the Animated Staff is defeated.
  • Celestial Fragment drops 100% of the time if the 15 Demon Notorious Monsters in Dynamis - Xarcabard have already been defeated and it will not drop the item if they have not all been defeated.
  • Immune to: Stun, Sleep, Lullaby
