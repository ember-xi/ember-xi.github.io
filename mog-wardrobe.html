Mog Wardrobe

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

The Mog Wardrobe is an inventory area for weapons and equipment. They are 80 slots, and can be equipped directly from them. Freeing up your inventory for food, meds, and farmed drops! Talking to your moogle will unlock available slots.

They can be accessed with the /wardrobe, /wardrobe2, /wardrobe3, and /wardrobe4 commands

Wardrobe 1 unlocks at Rank 3
Wardrobe 2 unlocks at Rank 6
Wardrobe 3 unlocks after clearing Apocalypse Nigh
