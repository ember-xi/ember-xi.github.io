Before you begin

  • Complete The New Frontier.

Starting point

Oaken Door · Norg · K-8

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Go upstairs in Norg and follow the corridor to the Oaken Door at K-8.
  2. Examine the door, agree to open it, and watch the meeting with Gilgamesh. Check that Kazham’s Chieftainess is now active.

Completion and reward

Story progression; no separate item reward.
