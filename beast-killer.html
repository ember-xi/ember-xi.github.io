Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Gives an edge against beast enemies.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Occasionally causes beast type enemies to be intimidated.
    • Blue Mage gains this trait by equipping Sprout Smack and Wild Oats.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Plantoid Earring
  • Byakko's Axe
  • Koenig Shield
  • Kaiser Shield
  • General's Shield

Food that Enhances this Trait

  • Rogue Rice Ball
  • Red Hot Cracker
  • Spicy Cracker

Beast Killer

  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 70
  • Obtained: Blue Mage Level 4
