Defense Bonus

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Increases defense against physical attacks.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Each upgrade of this trait raises a character's defense.
    • Repeats of this trait do not stack. For example, a Level 20 Paladin/Warrior will not receive two Defense Bonus I traits.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • None

Defense Bonus I

  • Obtained: Warrior Level 10
  • Obtained: Paladin Level 10
  • Obtained: Blue Mage Level 40
  • Total Defense Up: +10

Defense Bonus II

  • Obtained: Paladin Level 30
  • Total Defense Up: +22

Defense Bonus III

  • Obtained: Paladin Level 50
  • Total Defense Up: +35

Defense Bonus IV

  • Obtained: Paladin Level 70
  • Total Defense Up: +48
