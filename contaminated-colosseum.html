Notes

  • Evil Oscar uses [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Morbol Morbol] special attacks and Extremely Bad Breath that causes instant death. From experience it appears to be a radial [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/AoE AoE] attack, but it only has a range of approximately [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Distance 10'].
  • At certain time intervals, the chat log will display the message "Evil Oscar begins filling his lungs with the foul air around him..."; once this message has been displayed for the third time, Evil Oscar will immediately start to ready Extremely Bad Breath if its target is within range. If its target is not within range, it will wait until it is before readying the attack.
  • The time between warnings for Extremely Bad Breath shorten as the fight progresses.
  • It is possible to outrun Extremely Bad Breath before it occurs, as it has a long readying time. However, even if its intended target runs out of range, it can still hit others.
  • Evil Oscar's physical attacks have an added effect of [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Weight Weight]. The effect is extremely potent and will make escape from Extremely Bad Breath almost impossible if you are targeted by it. [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Utsusemi Utsusemi] is highly recommended for anyone at risk of receiving physical blows.
  • His [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Impale Impale] Tp move does Bind, unlike normal Morbols.
  • It is susceptible to [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gravity Gravity] and [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Bind Bind], but will gradually gain resistance to both effects with repeated use.
  • It is susceptible to [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/DoT DoT] spells such as: [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Dia Dia]/[./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Bio Bio], [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Poison Poison], Elemental Enfeebles, [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Helix Helixes], and [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Requiem Requiem].
  • It is immune to [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Sleep Sleep] and [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Stun Stun].
  • It has about 10,000-12,500 HP.
