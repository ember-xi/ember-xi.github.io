Ultrasonics

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Reduces evasion of targets in area of effect.

Description

  • Family: Giant Bat
  • Type: Magical(Ice)
  • Can be dispelled: N/A
  • Utsusemi/Blink absorb: Ignores shadows
  • Range: Unknown radial
  • Notes: Effect potency is -25 evasion. Barblizzard reduces effect's accuracy and duration. Shiva, Ifrit are immune to this effect.
  • Removal: Erase
