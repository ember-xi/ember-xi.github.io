Curilla

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Quest Giver NPC

Location: Chateau d'Oraguille (I-9) Temple Knight Qtrs

Notes

Curilla V Mecru is the leader of the Temple Knights and bravest warrior within the military, and receives all the benefits of it - especially being a woman. The side of her face has been scarred due to an accident while sparring with Prince Trion. While understanding it wasn't on purpose, she still hides her face for shame and believing herself to be ugly. However, there is talk that a member of the royal family has seen past that, to her real beauty.
