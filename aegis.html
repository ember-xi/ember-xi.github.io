Statistics

Aegis
All Races
DEF: 40
Enhances "Shield Bash" effect
Magic Damage Taken -25%
Lv. 75 PLD

Enhances Shield Bash Effect:

  • Shield Bash damage +200. The additional effect damage has a chance to crit for x2 or x3 the normal Shield Bash damage.

Hidden Effect

  • Has an extremely high chance of blocking, 45%-60%+ chance to block depending to the level of monster. 75% damage will be reduced during blocking

Other Uses

Used in Quest: None

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Quest

  • Relic Shield: Aegis
Note that this is the fifth, and final, stage of the Relic Shield: Aegis.
