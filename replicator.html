Statistics

Replicator
A Wind-based automaton attachment.

Stackable: 12

Note: This is an Automaton Attachment:

Automaton Effect Req. Capacity
 When HP is low, converts all wind maneuvers into Shadow Images.  Wind x 1
  • The attachment gives blink shadows (not utsusemi).
  • A Wind Maneuver must be active to use this attachment.
  • Triggers at 75% HP with Damage Gauge equipped.
  • Triggers at 50% HP without Damage Gauge.
  • One minute recast time.
  • Shadows have a five minute duration.
  • Cannot be recast until all shadows are worn or their timer has expired.

1 Wind Maneuver = 2 Shadows
2 Wind Maneuver = 3 Shadows
3 Wind Maneuver = 4 Shadows
