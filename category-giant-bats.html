General Information

  • System: Birds
  • Weaknesses: Wind, Light, Piercing
  • Immune:
  • Resistances: High resistance to Dark based spells and effects.
  • Special: Giant Bats that Link will also link with Bat Trios.

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Blood Drain: Single target Drain attack. Based on TP.
Ultrasonics: AoE Evasion Down
Marrow Drain: Single target Aspir attack. Based on TP.
Subsonics: AoE Defense Down
Supersonics: AoE Accuracy Down.
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
