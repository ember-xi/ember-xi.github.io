Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Refueling: Haste
Sling Bomb: Single-target damage (1 shadow)
Formation Attack: Single-target damage (1 shadow)
Self-Destruct: 10' AoE Fire damage, destroys only one of the three bombs in the cluster if HP is over 20%; if below 20%, there is a chance that all bombs remaining will Self-Destruct. Will not use this attack if above 50% HP.
Circle of Flames: 10' AoE physical damage and Weight, blocked by Utsusemi
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
