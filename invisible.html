Notes

  • One still leaves visible foot prints in sand/dust or ripples in water.
  • The following actions will not remove Invisible:
  • The use of Trick Attack.
  • The use of any "fireworks" related item.
  • The use of crystals while performing a synthesis.
  • The use of the Beastmaster/Summoner/Puppetmaster Pet Commands Fight/Assault/Deploy.
  • The use of an Empress Band.
  • Invisible drops when a spell cast completes, an attack is made, or you are directly attacked (being caught in an AoE ability if not the main target will not remove invisible.)
  • Unlike Sneak, Invisible will not need to be dropped before recasting. The act of casting the spell again will automatically remove the old instance and replace it with the new instance of the spell. Therefore, it is possible to cast Invisible in front of a sight aggressive based Monsters without being detected.
  • Use of this spell can attract the attention of sight and magic aggressive based monsters such as Bombs so its use is suggested with care in certain areas. Magic aggressive monsters will aggro whenever a spell is started, the spell does not have to complete.
  • It is not possible to interact with anything in the world (for example, speaking to an NPC or opening a door) while invisible, the effect must be manually removed first. Take care not to drop the effect in front of anything that aggros by sight.
