Zhayolm Fireflies

From Zenith XI Wiki

Temporary escape item supplied inside Zhayolm Remnants.

Contents
  1. How to obtain and use
  2. Before entering
  3. Sources

How to obtain and use

  1. Enter Zhayolm Remnants for Salvage. Each participant receives the temporary fireflies on entry.
  2. Use them when ready to leave the area. They return you to the Zhayolm entrance section of Alzadaal Undersea Ruins.

Before entering

The original event requires level 65+, the relevant Treasures of Aht Urhgan story progress, and a Remnants Permit. See Salvage for entry and equipment-restriction details.
