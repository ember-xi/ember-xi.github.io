Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 15: The Black Coffin.

Starting point

Naja Salaheem · Aht Urhgan Whitegate · I-10

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Return from Nashmau to Whitegate.
  2. Speak with Naja Salaheem and finish the report and clothing discussion.

Battle and progress notes

  • Your clothes alter the conversation, but this particular mission does not require the palace outfit. The next mission does.

Completion and reward

No item reward.

What this opens

  • ToAU 17: Guests of the Empire
