Metatron Torment

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Great Axe Weapon Skill

  • Skill Level: N/A
  • Description: Lowers target's defense. Additional effect: temporarily lowers damage taken from enemies.
  • Available only when equipped with the Relic Weapons Abaddon Killer (Dynamis use only) or Bravura.
  • Since these Relic Weapons are only available to Warriors, only Warriors may use this Weapon Skill.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Light / Fusion
  • Modifiers: STR:60%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
1000 TP 2000 TP 3000 TP
2.75 2.75 2.75

Notes

  • Lowers defense by 18.75%File:Wind icon.JPG
    • Land rate of the additional effects use Combat Skill as a 1:1 analogue to Magic Accuracy.
    • Has three resist states, full duration, half duration, and full resist.
    • Does not stack with: Acid Bolt, Angon, Shell Crusher, Frightful Roar, Armor Break.
  • Damage Taken Aftermath is -20%.
    • Aftermath lasts 20 seconds at 1000 TP, 40 seconds at 2000 TP, and 60 seconds at 3000 TP.
