Seraph Strike

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Club weapon skill

  • Skill level: 40
  • Deals light elemental damage to enemy. Damage varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain: Impaction
  • Modifiers: MND:30%; STR:30%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.00 2.00 3.00

Levels

  • Seraph Strike is only natively available to Paladins, White Mages, Warriors, Dark Knights, Blue Mages and Samurai. While other jobs can obtain the required 40 Club skill, they must use one of the aforementioned jobs as their support job in order to gain access to Seraph Strike.
