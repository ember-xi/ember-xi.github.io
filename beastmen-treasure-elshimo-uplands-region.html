Walkthrough

  • Peddlestox only appears on Lightningsday at 0:00 at (E-8) in Yhoator Jungle.
  • Peddlestox will question why you are in a jungle without a map if you don't have the Elshimo Islands map. She won't offer further dialogue without it.
  • She will ask you to bring her four items. They are all Rare/Ex. Note: This quest will not appear in your quest log.
    • Rancor Mantle drop from Tonberry Cutter in Temple of Uggalepih.
    • Rancor Globe drops from Tonberry Harrier in Temple of Uggalepih.
    • Rancor Tank drops from Tonberry Hexer in Yhoator Jungle.
    • Rancor Handle drops from Bisque-heeled Sunberry in Yhoator Jungle.
  • When you bring the items back to Peddlestox, she will tell you the location of your Treasure Box. The location is different every time.
  • The ??? will appear in the location between 20:00 and 4:00.
  • Use a Pickaxe on the ??? and you will randomly receive a treasure item, but not necessarily the item she said you could get. Possible treasure includes 3-5 of the following and 4000 to 5000 gil:
