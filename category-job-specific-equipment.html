Job Specific Equipment

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

JSE is gear that is specifically designed for each job. While there are certain items which can be used by only a limited number of jobs, sometimes as few as two, JSE is what it says, job specific, and therefore does not include these pieces. Popular JSE pieces are:

  • Artifact Armor
  • Artifact Armor +1
  • Relic Armor
  • Relic Armor +1
  • Relic Weapons
  • Mythic Weapons - Not yet implemented.
