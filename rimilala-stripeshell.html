Statistics

Rimilala Stripeshell
This ancient Windurstian currency,
valuing 10,000 mumus, dates back to
C.E. 719. The engraving commemorates
the 500 year anniversary of Star
Sibyl Rimilala's discovery of magic.

Stackable: 99

Other Uses

Used in Quest: Relic Weapons

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be sold on the Auction House or delivered, but can be traded and bazaared.
