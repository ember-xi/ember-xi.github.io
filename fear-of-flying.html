Walkthrough

  • Talk to Kurando to accept the quest.
  • Go to Rolanberry Fields and hunt the Silk Caterpillar - it spawns every 11 minutes in the area of K-7, when the Airship flies by overhead. Kill it to get the Silkworm Egg.
  • Go back to Kurando and trade him the Silkworm Egg to get your reward.

Game Description

Client: Kurando (Docks, Port Bastok)
Summary:

Kurando claims airships are dangerous - that every time one passes over the Rolanberry Fields, a horrible beast appears. He needs you to bring back whatever the beast drops as proof to back his claims.
