Notes

  • Has all the normal Adamantoise abilities including:
  • Auto Regen
  • Flood
  • Silencega
  • Slowga
  • Waterga IV
  • Susceptible to Silence, Gravity and Bind.
  • Unlike almost every other worthwhile mob, is highly susceptible to Requiem, which seems to have a dramatic effect on its regen.
  • When he goes into his shell, you can barely do any damage to him; however, continually doing damage will result in him coming back out of his shell (similar behavior seen in Aspidochelone).
