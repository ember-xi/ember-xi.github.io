Walkthrough

Note
You can have three people total in the fight and once you trade everyone will be capped at level 20.
  • Obtain a Pirate's Chart from Zaldon from the Inside the Belly quest.
  • Trade the Pirate's Chart to a ??? in Valkurm Dunes on the Whitebone Sands (F-9) and immediately start fishing. You will fish up Tarutaru Snares and/or Mithra Snares.
  • A mysterious tarutaru will appear and start talking about fishing. About 15 seconds after you trade the chart, the tarutaru will die, and three NMs will appear and automatically be aggroed to whoever traded their chart:
  • Heike Crab (Crab)
  • Houu the Shoalwader (Sahagin)
  • Beach Monk (Sea Monk)
  • All three have Hundred Fists which they will use at half life. Use the Tarutaru Snares and/or Mithra Snares to impair the NMs and prevent them from using Hundred Fists.
  • Kill all three to have a Barnacled Box appear with your loot.
  • Open the Barnacled Box and you will receive four items. Barnacled Box can only be opened by a party member with level restriction active. It appears that the Albatross Ring drops 100% of the time.
  • If your party is defeated by the 3NMs, you will lose your Pirate's Chart and must obtain another one before trying the fight again. Players without the level cap restriction cannot interact with you in any way, even if in party, even to raise.
  • There's a limit of around 10 minutes in this fight. If you can't kill all three NMs within the time limit the message "Too much time has passed. The monster has lost interest." will appear and all remaining NMs will despawn.
