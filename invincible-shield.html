Invincible Shield

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Conflict Director

Location: Metalworks (G-8), Outside Cid's Lab

Notes

  • A renowned Bastokan hero due to his rank of Legatus of the 3rd Division during the Crystal War.
  • Is in charge of Ballista concerns for the Republic and will give you a Ballista Earring (Key Item)

Involved in Quests

  • Lure of the Wildcat (Bastok)
  • Getting a Ballista License
