Zhayolm Apkallu

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-70–74 Apkallu on Zazalda Island in Mount Zhayolm.

Contents
  1. Where and rewards
  2. Battle notes
  3. Sources

Where and rewards

Look around the L-6 area of Mount Zhayolm. Normal drops include Apkallu Egg and Apkallu Feather.

Battle notes

  • Repeated kills raise shared Apkallu hostility in the zone. Their resistance, aggression and tendency to link can increase.
  • Turn your character away when Yawn or Frigid Shuffle begins to avoid the gaze effect.
  • At low HP they may run toward another Apkallu. Bind or Gravity can prevent a chase and extra links.
  • Blue Mages can use Apkallu for Yawn learning; see the spell page for learning requirements.
