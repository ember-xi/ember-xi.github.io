Ground Strike

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Great Sword weapon skill

  • Skill level: 250
  • In order to obtain Ground Strike, the quest Inheritance must be completed.
  • Delivers a single-hit attack. Damage varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Fragmentation/Distortion
  • Modifiers: STR:50% ; INT:50%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.50 1.75 3.00
  • Increased attack power.

Levels

  • Great Sword skill level 250 is obtainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
Job Rating Level
Dark Knight A- 72
Warrior B+ 74
Paladin B 75
