Large Apparatus

The Large Apparatus is found within the Hall of Transference.

Hall of Transference, Dem
Hall of Transference, Holla
Hall of Transference, Mea

The Large Apparatus is used for the following:

• granting access into a Promyvion zone for the first time (left apparatus)
• sealing your memories of a Promyvion zone (left apparatus)
• transportation to Ru'Aun Gardens (right apparatus)
