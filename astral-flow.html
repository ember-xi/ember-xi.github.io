Astral Flow

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Allows Avatars to use their full power.
  • Obtained: Summoner Level 1
  • Recast Time: 2:00:00
  • Duration: 0:03:00

Notes/Description

  • Astral Flow is a Summoner's two hour special.
  • While active, Avatar Perpetuation costs are reduced to zero.
  • While active, each Avatar gains the ability to perform their special Blood Pacts:
    • Carbuncle - Searing Light - AOE Light Damage
    • Garuda - Aerial Blast - AOE Wind Damage
    • Ifrit - Inferno - AOE Fire Damage
    • Shiva - Diamond Dust - AOE Ice Damage
    • Leviathan - Tidal Wave - AOE Water Damage
    • Titan - Earthen Fury - AOE Earth Damage
    • Ramuh - Judgement Bolt - AOE Lightning Damage
    • Fenrir - Howling Moon - AOE Darkness Damage
    • Diabolos - Ruinous Omen - AOE Darkness Damage (Note: The amount of damage of this Blood Pact will be a random percent of the targets HP.)
  • Upon usage of any of the above Blood Pacts, all of the user's MP is consumed.
    • When using the Blood Pact, the user must have MP = their current level X 2. Otherwise it will fizzle and no damage will be done.
  • The Blood Pact activated by Astral Flow can be resisted.
  • Abilities used during Astral Flow can be used to perform Magic Bursts.
  • While Active, Elemental Spirits will cast magic far more quickly, AI's use of more powerful spells will be increased.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Astral Flow" <me>
  • /pet "Blood Pact Name" <t>
  • Since Astral Flow is a two hour special, some people prefer not to make a macro for it to prevent unintentional activation.
