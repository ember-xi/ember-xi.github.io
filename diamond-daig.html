Notes:

  • Lottery Spawn from the Helm Beetle, during a Sandstorm (double earth weather). Will depop when the Sandstorm disappears.
  • Located at: Enter Quicksand Caves from Western Altepa Desert at J-9; go to the next map at D-11; found at H-8 on that map.
  • Spawns in the hallway, unlike the Helm Beetle placeholder which spawns in the 6 adjoining rooms.
  • Very high defense along with a significantly large amount of hp.
  • Does not cast spells nor uses a 2 hour ability.
  • Double Attacks around 80-90% of the time.
  • Killable by: 5 characters level 64, 2 characters level 70+, 1 level 75
  • Drops around 1200 Gil
