Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Improves defense against magic spells.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Each +1 to from gear or traits increases the MDB factor in Calculating Magic Damage by 0.01 from its base of 1.00
    • As a result, it reduces magic damage by a bit less than 1%.
  • Further Notes:
    • Repeats of this trait do not stack. For example, a Level 25 RDM/WHM will not receive two Magic Defense Bonus I traits.
