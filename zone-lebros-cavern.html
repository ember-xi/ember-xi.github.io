Additional level-75 reference

An expansive cavern on the eastern side of the Halvung Territory. Filled with lava and other dangers common to the volcanic region the cavern has the been the site of many battle between the Imperial Army and the Troll Mercenaries.

This area is an Assault area. This area is only accessible if the Treasures of Aht Urhgan expansion is installed, The Road to Aht Urhgan quest is completed, and Aht Urhgan Mission 3: President Salaheem is completed.

A cage of Zhayolm Fireflies is given to all participating mercenaries who enter. They can be used to return to the Halvung Staging Point.
