Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

File:Gravity (Status Effect).png Weight is a harmful status effect that increases the force of gravity on the player until it wears off or is removed. Afflicted characters experience reduced movement speed and evasion.

How to remove the effect

  • The White Magic spell Erase removes this effect.
  • Use of Flee will remove some but not all variations of this effect.

How the effect is inflicted

Spells

  • Black Magic - Gravity
  • Black Magic - Graviga (only usable by monsters, usually NMs)
  • Blue Magic - Magnetite Cloud
  • Blue Magic - Mysterious Light
  • Summoning Magic - Tail Whip (Leviathan)
  • Summoning Magic - Somnolence (Diabolos)

Monster Abilities

  • Antica - Magnetite Cloud
  • Cravers - Murk
  • Dynamis Statues - Lead Breath
  • Magic Pots - Mysterious Light
  • Morbols - Bad Breath
  • Phuabo - Tidal Dive
  • (Vanguard) Quadav - Wrath of Gu'Dha
  • Xzomit - Mantle Pierce
  • Yovra - Concussive Oscillation

Environmental Effect

  • Possible random effect gained from the swamps in Caedarva Mire

Weapons

  • Magnet Knife
