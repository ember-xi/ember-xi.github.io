Zoraal Ja's Axe

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-74 axe for WAR, DRK, BST and RNG: DMG 46, delay 276; Reward healing +10%.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Defeat Zoraal Ja's Pkuucha in Wajaom Woodlands for a chance at this axe. It can also be bought under Auction House → Weapons → Axes.

Use

Equip it when using Reward to improve the HP restored to your pet.
