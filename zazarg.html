Notes

Zazarg is one of 5 generals commanding the defense of Al Zahbi during Besieged. He holds the title of Stoneserpent General and is a Galka Monk.

  • He must be in Al Zahbi in order to complete the quest Fist of the People. If he is held prisoner, the quest cannot be completed until he is rescued.
  • If he is not held prisoner at the beginning of a Besieged, everybody will receive 1 Giant's Drink.
  • Originally Praefectus of the Republican Legion's Fourth Division's Seventh Cohors during the Crystal War.
