The Old Monument

The Old Monument
Starting NPCMertaire — Lower Jeuno (I-8), Merry Minstrel Meadhouse
Required FameNone
Level RestrictionLevel 30
Items NeededParchment
TitleNone
RepeatableNo
DescriptionMertaire has lost his songs. The runes on an old monument in Buburimu Peninsula may hold them.
Rewards
  • Poetic Parchment (traded from the Song Runes)
← Previous questNext quest →
—A Minstrel in Despair

Walkthrough

  • In the Merry Minstrel Meadhouse in Lower Jeuno (I-8) talk to Mertaire, then Bki Tbujhja, then Tuh Almobankha.
  • Buy a Parchment (Auction House, Leathercrafting materials).
  • Travel to Buburimu Peninsula. Go down to the beach at (G-9), head west along the water through the cave, and the Song Runes are on the far beach at (F-9).
    • Bogys walk the beach at night.
  • Examine the Song Runes for a cutscene, then trade the Parchment to the runes to receive the Poetic Parchment.
