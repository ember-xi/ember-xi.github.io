Walkthrough

Note
Bring a Parchment along to complete A Minstrel in Despair while you are in Buburimu Peninsula.
  • This quest is HIDDEN, and cannot be seen in your Quest list until completed.
  • If you're not already on a high level job then change to a level 30 job to start this quest.
  • Mertaire (I-8, Merry Minstrel Meadhouse in Lower Jeuno), will lament on a lost love.
  • Talk to Bki Tbujhja, behind the counter in the same room, who talks about the Song Runes in Buburimu Peninsula and how it would cheer up the broken-hearted.
  • Head to Buburimu Peninsula and on the western edge of the Khoonta Dunes (beach) there is a hidden path in the shallows (G-10) that takes you to (F/G-9).
  • Click the Song Runes, on the west side up from the beach for a cutscene with Lewenhart to complete the quest. Don't forget to trade the Parchment to the Song Runes before you leave!!
