Three Mage Gate

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This impressive door is found deep in the Inner Horutoto Ruins. There are only two ways to get past this door:

  • Have a Red Mage, White Mage, and Black Mage stand on the corresponding colored circles.
  • Have someone who has the Portal Charm open it.

You can obtain the Portal Charm after completing Windurst Mission 3-2: Written in the Stars.

You will need access through this gate for:

Windurst Mission 3-2: Written in the Stars
Windurst Mission 8-2: The Jester who'd be King
Black Mage AF3: The Root of the Problem
Anything involving Toraimarai Canal (although this area can be accessed directly from Windurst Walls by anyone possessing a Rhinostery Certificate).
