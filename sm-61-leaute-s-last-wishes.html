Mission 6-1 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 5-2 The Shadow Lord.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Halver. You receive a Piece of Paper.
  2. Go to Western Altepa Desert and examine the Dreamrose. The NM Sabotender Enamorado appears.
  3. Kill it and examine the flower again for the Dreamrose key item.
  4. Return to Halver.
Mechanics: The Sabotender NM is around level 55. 1,000 Needles deals 1000 damage split between everyone near it, so keep the Trusts close to you.
