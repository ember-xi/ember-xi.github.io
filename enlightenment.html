Enlightenment

From Zenith XI Wiki

Scholar level 75 merit.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. How to use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Reach Scholar75, enable merits and purchase Enlightenment in Scholar Group 2.

How to use

Unlock the ability in Scholar Group 2 merits. Activate it, then cast the needed learned spell that is normally locked behind an Addendum.

It provides access for the next spell; it does not permanently change your Arts or teach a missing scroll.
