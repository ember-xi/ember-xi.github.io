Notes:

  • How To Spawn
  • Head into the center section and then north at the top of I-9 on the first map. This will take you onto the second map, the one with the other exit to Rolanberry Fields. If you look on this map you will see a small southern niche that you want to go to at H-9.
  • Once you make it to the niche, face west and you'll see a ??? that spawns the Dreadbug when selected. The Dreadbug is basically an overgrown Soldier Crawler, with about 40% more hitpoints and damage than a Soldier in Crawler's Nest. It has approx. 5000 HP.
  • The Dreadbug will drop 1-4 copies of the Giant Shell Bug.
  • Dreadbug likes to buff often so having a dispeller is recommended.
  • Dreadbug can be spawned by the person with the quest open once every Vana'diel day.
  • Used in Quest: A Boy's Dream
