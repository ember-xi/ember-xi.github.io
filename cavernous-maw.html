Cavernous Maw

From Zenith XI Wiki

A Wings of the Goddess portal between the present and Shadowreign era.

First trip to the past

  1. Use the maw in Batallia Downs (H-5) or Rolanberry Fields (H-6). A third starting maw is on the raised ledge in Sauromugue Champaign reached through Garlaige Citadel.
  2. Examine the maw to start Wings of the Goddess and receive the Pure White Feather. Your first arrival can be in Batallia Downs (S), Rolanberry Fields (S) or Sauromugue Champaign (S), regardless of which entrance you used.
  3. Before leaving the past, use a convenient maw from its past side to unlock that present-day entrance. Use this method to unlock the other routes as you explore.

Maws unlocked from the past can then be used for return trips. This page covers Wings of the Goddess travel.
