Starting point

??? · Qufim Island · G-6

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

Walkthrough

  1. Prepare beside the same G-6 ??? with the participating party nearby, then examine it again to start the confrontation.
  2. Defeat all 30 Seed Mandragoras within the 30-minute field battle. Keep the fight inside the encounter area and do not zone away.
  3. After victory, examine the ??? again for the concluding scene. Confirm that Those Who Lurk in Shadows (I) is active.

Battle and progress notes

  • If the fight fails, recollect the three Goblin NM seedspall key items before trying again.
  • The reference confrontation dismisses Trusts on entry; Zenith behavior is not confirmed.

Completion and reward

Amber key and story progression.
