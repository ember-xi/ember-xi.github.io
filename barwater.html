Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Increases your resistance against water.

Description

Spell cost: 6
Spell element:Lightning
Magic skill:Enhancing Magic
Jobs:

  • Red Mage 9

Amount of the increase equals (Enhancing magic skill / 5) + 40.

Timers

Casting Time: 0.5 seconds
Recast Time: 10 seconds

Other Information

Note
This spell does not make players immune to water damage. This spell only increases the chance of a player resisting a water based spell. water based status effects will have a greater chance of having their duration reduced, and water based damage effects will have a greater chance of doing less damage. This spell's effectiveness, like any other barspell, is wholely dependant on your Enhancing Magic skill. Low enhancing magic will produce little to no results in increasing your resistances.
