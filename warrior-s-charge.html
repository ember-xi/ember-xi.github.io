Warrior's Charge

From Zenith XI Wiki

  2. Notes/Description
  3. Macro Syntax

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Will double your next attack.
  • Obtained: Warrior Level 75 (Merit)
  • Recast Time: 15 minutes
  • Duration: Next attack or 1 minute

Notes/Description

  • Stacks with weapon skills but seems to only affect the first hit.
  • Stacks with Mighty Strikes.
  • Stacks with Sneak Attack.
  • This job ability turns your next melee attack into a Double Attack, just as if the Warrior job trait Double Attack had been activated normally.
  • Each additional merit upgrade lowers recast time by 2 minutes and 30 seconds.

Macro Syntax

/ja "Warrior's Charge" <me>
