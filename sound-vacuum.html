Sound Vacuum

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Silences opponents in a fan-shaped area of effect.

Description

  • Family: Cockatrice, Worm
  • Type: Enfeebling
  • Can be dispelled: N/A
  • Utsusemi/Blink absorb: Ignores shadows
  • Range: Unknown cone
  • Notes: Worm version is single target rather than conical (except for Nightmare Worm). The Nightmare Cockatrice inflicts Mute with this ability.
