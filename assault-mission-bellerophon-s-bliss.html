Walkthrough

  • Defeat Martial Maestro Megomak.
  • Will link with Khimaira 14X when either of them is pulled.
  • While he is alive, he can command Khimaira 14X to do any of his TP move any time he wants.
  • Has very little HP.
  • Will Warp out of the battlefield when his HP is about 20% .
  • Once Martial Maestro Megomak is gone, all that is left to do is to defeat Khimaira 14X.
  • Once Khimaira 14X is defeated the Rune of Release will unlock in the back of the battlefield.

Notes

  • Most of Khimaira 14X's moves are Thunder based.
  • If you have a White Mage have them try to keep Barthundra up at all times.
  • If you have a Bard you may want them to have Lightning Carol up after Khimaira 14X has 25% and starts using Fulmination.
