Before you begin

  • Wings of the Goddess content enabled.

Starting point

Cavernous Maw · Rolanberry Fields · H-6

Walkthrough

  1. Examine a starting maw in Rolanberry Fields (H-6), Batallia Downs (H-5), or Sauromugue Champaign (J-10). Rolanberry is a straightforward first route from Jeuno.
  2. Finish the scene to receive the Pure white feather and enter a Shadowreign area. Check which past zone you arrived in before leaving.
  3. Use other maws from their past side to activate their present-day entrances. If you leave before opening alternatives, return through the first starting maw that admitted you.

Completion and reward

Pure white feather; access to the past.
