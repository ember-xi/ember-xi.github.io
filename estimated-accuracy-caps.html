Estimated Accuracy Caps

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This page is an attempt to link necessary accuracy totals with specific mobs or events, in order to facilitate better gearing. It's important to remember this is estimated accuracy caps, many of these will not be proven by using mob stats and math, instead they will be ballparked using parser data. Given we generally don't know mob stats on Eden, this is likely the best course of action for the time being.
