Scholar

From Zenith XI Wiki

SCH is a Wings of the Goddess job that switches between healing/support and black magic through Arts and stratagems.

Unlock and progression

Unlock with A Little Knowledge at level 30. Begin On Sabbatical at SCH 40; continue Downward Helix at SCH 50 for artifact armor and Loussaire commissions.

Weapons

The first AF quest gives Klimaform instead of a weapon. Scholar can use Staff, Club and Dagger; Elemental Staves are common magic tools at 51. Its level 75 Mythic staff is Tupsimati, associated with Omniscience. Standard 75 Claustrum is for BLM/SMN, not Scholar.

Quick use

  1. For healing, select Light Arts and Addendum: White when status-removal or Reraise spells are needed.
  2. For damage, select Dark Arts. Use Addendum: Black for Sleep, Dispel and tierIV nukes.
  3. Match a Storm to the element you plan to cast; use Klimaform when available. Save charges for spells that matter.
  4. Charge Sublimation between MP demands, then activate it again when you need the stored MP.

This page follows historical 75 progression. Zenith-specific custom changes should be read from its dedicated job-balance notes; a level 75 cap alone does not prove every later base-game spell adjustment is disabled.
