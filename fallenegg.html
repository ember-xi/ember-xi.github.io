FallenEgg

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Walkthrough

  • Fallen Egg appears under various trees in La Theine Plateau.
  • See map for Fallen Egg locations.
  • Nihniknoovi can spawn from any of the Fallen Egg's dotted around the map, as specified in the map.
    • Note: the spawn locations shown on the map are the forest areas in which the egg may appear, not the exact spawn points; be sure to examine the complete forest around each spawn location before moving on to the next point.
  • Once one is located, and is examined it can do the following;
  1. Spawn NM; Nihniknoovi
  2. Find item; Bird Egg
  3. Give you a message; "There is a broken egg on the ground here./ Perhaps there is a nest in the boughs of this tree."
  • The FallenEgg will disappear immediately after being examined and will respawn elsewhere in 5 minutes.


Nihniknoovi drops:
Van Pendant
  • Nihniknoovi can be solo'd at level 25
