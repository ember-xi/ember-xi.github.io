Radar

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Located in the lower left of the screen, the Compass/Radar will show the direction that you are traveling and other characters nearby as little blips or dots.

Using Ninja as your main or sub job causes the radar to expand both in size and range.

Dot Color Character type
Red Enemies (with some jobs, see table below)
Purple Party members
Yellow Friendly NPCs and Pets
Blue Other players
Green Static NPCs

Arrows around the outside of the radar show the direction and name of NPCs (hostile and friendly) you are tracking by use of Wide Scan or enemies that have "Call for Help" tagged onto them.

Arrow Color Character type
Teal Wide Scan Tracking (<scan>)
Red Enemies with the "Call for Help" tag (<ht>)

Which jobs (main or sub) see red dots on the radar, and which don't:

Mob radar? Jobs
Yes Beastmaster, Ninja, Thief, Ranger, Summoner, Blue Mage
No Warrior, Monk, Bard, White Mage, Black Mage, Red Mage, Dragoon, Paladin, Dark Knight, Corsair, Puppetmaster
