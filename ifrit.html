Ifrit

Ifrit — Trial by Fire

RequirementPrime: level 65+. Trial-Size: Summoner under 65 with Carbuncle. No Fame needed.

BattlefieldCloister of Flames — deep in Ifrit's Cauldron. 30 minutes, no level cap, up to 6 players, Trusts allowed

Best day / AvoidWatersday / Firesday

RewardsIfrit (pact) · Ifrit's Blade · Fire Belt · Fire Ring · Egil's Torch · 10,000 gil

Ifrit waits in the Cloister of Flames at the bottom of Ifrit's Cauldron, north of Kazham. Two ways to win his pact: the Trial-Size fight under 65, or the Prime fight from 65 that also gives the Whisper of Flames for Fenrir.

Under 65: Trial-Size Trial by Fire

  1. Talk to Ronta-Onta in Kazham (J-9) as SMN. You get the Mini Tuning Fork of Fire.
  2. Trade the Mini Fork back to Ronta-Onta. You are teleported to the Cloister of Flames.
  3. Trade the Mini Fork to the Fire Protocrystal and enter.
  4. Summon Carbuncle, keep him in front, stay back and heal him, use the Blood Pact when it is ready.
  5. Talk to the crystal after the win. Ifrit is yours. You keep the Mini Fork.
Trial-Size rules: solo, level 20 cap, Carbuncle only, 15 minutes. Buffs cast outside are kept. Best day: Watersday. Never on Firesday.

65 and up: Trial by Fire (Prime)

  1. Talk to Ronta-Onta in Kazham (J-9). You get the Tuning Fork of Fire.
  2. Talk to Ronta-Onta again while you hold the fork. You are sent straight to the Cloister of Flames.
  3. Summon your Trusts and your avatar, then examine the Fire Protocrystal and pick Trial by Fire.
  4. Defeat Ifrit Prime. You receive the Whisper of Flames.
  5. Going for Fenrir? Keep the Whisper and do not return to Ronta-Onta yet. Otherwise return to him and choose your reward.

Walking to the Cloister of Flames

  • Enter Ifrit's Cauldron from Yhoator Jungle at (I-5) and follow the right-hand wall (north-west).
  • A flame spout blocks the path: wait for it to die down (up to 15 minutes) or trade it an Ice Cluster.
  • Follow the path west; where it splits keep west through the cave to the end, turn south and drop to the lower level (map 2, I-8).
  • Head back north to the two sets of posts at (H-7) and walk off the edge between them. Only one path from there: follow it and take the first left into the Cloister of Flames.
  • Ifrit's Cauldron Home Point #1 is on the way — take it for later runs.

The Fight

  • Ifrit Prime uses Burning Strike, Crimson Howl, Fire IV and Meteor Strike. Fire IV and Meteor Strike go straight through Utsusemi shadows.
  • At about 50% HP he uses Inferno, his Astral Flow pact: heavy AoE fire damage. Keep everyone topped up before that point.
  • Fire damage heals him: no Fire spells, no fire weapon skills, no weapons with additional fire damage.
  • Very long magic-detection range — buff outside the crystal, then enter.
  • The arena is a long path up a hill with a switchback: mages can stand above the fight and still reach it.
EmberXI solo tips: bring Leviathan (water beats fire; Spinning Dive). Keep Barfira up, let the tank Trust hold Ifrit on the lower path, and stand up the slope with the healer.

Rewards

One per win. The Whisper is used up when you take a reward; win again (once a day) for the next.

Prime Pact (level 75)

Win this Prime fight as SMN 75 with Ifrit already learned and the message === The Prime Pact === appears: you learn Meteor Strike (the level 75 Blood Pact).

EmberXI changes

  • Ronta-Onta also runs the Trial-Size fight for summoners under 65 (mini fork, trade to teleport).

  • Trusts allowed in the Prime battlefield; Prime requires level 65+.
  • Repeatable once a day.
  • Prime Pact at SMN 75 (see above).
