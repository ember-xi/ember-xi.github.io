Description

Spell cost: 12 MP
Ecosystem: Beastmen
Type: Physical (Piercing)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: CHR+2, HP+5
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 12

Timers

Casting Time: 0.5 seconds

Notes

  • 14.7' range
  • Calculated as a ranged attack and piercing damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: AGI: 30%.
  • Additional effect of poison is not guaranteed.

Combines With...

You can use Feather Storm as part of a combo to create Rapid Shot.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Rapid Shot combo.

  • Jet Stream
  • Hydro Shot
