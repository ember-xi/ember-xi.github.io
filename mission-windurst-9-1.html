Before you begin

Be allied with Windurst, have Rank 9, and finish the preceding required story steps. Raise Rank Points if the mission is not offered.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission and speak with Apururu. Enter Heavens Tower, visit the Vestal Chamber, then return to Apururu.
  2. Obtain Goobbue Humus from Old Goobbue in the Boyahda Tree or Goobbue Gardener in the Sanctuary of Zi’Tah.
  3. Trade the humus to the lone Mandragora Warden in the Boyahda Tree’s western (F-4) area for the Letter from Zonpa-Zippa.
  4. Return to Apururu.
  5. Enter Full Moon Fountain from Toraimarai Canal (G-7) for the final scene.

Important notes

  • Use the solitary Mandragora Warden, not the nearby pair.

Walkthrough

  • Receive the mission and talk to the NPCs in this order.
  • Apururu at the Manustery.
  • Zone into Heavens Tower to get a small cutscene, then head up to the top of the tower.
  • Star Sibyl in Heavens Tower.
  • Apururu at the Manustery, again.
  • Yoran-Oran in Windurst Walls. (not required to do the Mission)
  • Head to Boyahda Tree and kill Elder Goobbues or Old Goobbues to receive the item Goobbue Humus (Rare/Ex).
  • After getting the item head to the first map of the Boyahda Tree and head to E-4 and you should see a Mandragora NPC called a Mandragora Warden. These Mandragora Warden are found in two different places. One is by itself and the other location has two of them. Talk to them until you get a message stating that the Mandragora Warden wants something from you.
  • Trade the humus to this NPC and watch a long cutscene.
  • After the cutscene, head back to Apururu, watch another cutscene, and then head to the Full Moon Fountain to complete this mission.

Game Description

Mission Orders
The Orastery Minister has awoken and the Bearer of Darkness once again terrorizes Heavens Tower. At the request of the Manustery you are to speak with a person imprisoned within Boyahda Tree and discover the truth behind the doll of the dead.
