Walkthrough

  • Watch the cutscene for an explanation of the ruins.

Bhaflau Thickets Map 1

  • Travel/Chocobo to Bhaflau Thickets (F-6) and speak to NPC Kamih Mapokhalam.
    • She also says that if you trade her 3 Imperial Mythril Pieces she will give you a map of the Alzadaal Undersea Ruins. Trade her these first for the map.
    • She tells you that in order to enter the Alzadaal Undersea Ruins zone you must trade trade her 1 Imperial Silver Piece.
      • Note: If you forget to trade the 3 imperial pieces you can exit back out to Bhaflau Thickets without having to pay to re enter as she is inside the tunnel with you.

Alzadaal Undersea Ruins Map 7

  • Approach the Gilded Doors (H-8). Mission 10 is started as soon as the cutscene begins.

NOTE: There are no monsters in this section of Alzadaal Undersea Ruins.


Side Note: You can get the sixth staging point at this time also. After paying the Imperial Silver Piece to get in an after the cutscene, you can travel Alzadaal Undersea Ruins to the Nyzul Staging Point and return to Whitegate for Mission 10. Refer to [1] as a map.

Game Description

Mission Orders
You have been requested to enter the Alzadaal Undersea Ruins from Bhaflau Thickets and patrol the interior.
