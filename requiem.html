Requiem

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Did you want Assault Mission - Requiem?

Requiem is a type of Enfeebling Song used by Bards. It inflicts Damage Over Time on an enemy, similar to a Poison effect.

Notes

  • Requiem is Light element, meaning it tends to succeed more against creatures of darkness, or other targets susceptible to Light element.
  • It is an Enfeebling Song, meaning that only one Requiem effect can be in place on a target at any given time. Additional Requiems on one target will not combine.
  • Fun fact: Currently, Requiem has the distinction of being the only spell family with a 6th tier that players are able to use. Spell families that take second place with 5 usable tiers are Cure, Paeon, Protectra, and Shellra.
