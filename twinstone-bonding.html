Walkthrough

  • Kill the Hill Lizards in Meriphataud Mountains until they drop a Twinstone Earring.
  • Bring it back to Gioh Ajihri at K-5 for a reward.

Mass Turn-in Tip

  • To start set your home point at Port, Walls or Waters Mog Houses. (not Woods to enforce a zone between hand-ins)
  • Run to the NPC
  • Talk to the NPC
  • Start casting Warp
  • Immediately trade while Warp is casting, with the macro "/item "Twinstone Earring" <t>" and skip quickly through the dialogue.
  • After Warp goes off zone into MH, exit into Woods and repeat

Game Description

Client: Gioh Ajihri (Mithra Groves, Windurst Woods)
Summary: Twenty years ago, there was a great battle between the Mithra and the Yagudo in the Meriphataud Mountains. Gioh wants you to collect the twinstone earrings that belonged to the Mithra warriors who fell there.
