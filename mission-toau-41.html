Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 40: Unraveling Reason.

Starting point

Rodin-Comidin · Aht Urhgan Whitegate · I-7

Walkthrough

  1. Return to Whitegate and find Rodin-Comidin on the upper level outside the Automaton Workshop.
  2. Speak with him and accept the assignment to receive the Nyzul Isle Route.

Battle and progress notes

  • No additional day wait is imposed before Path of Darkness.

Completion and reward

Nyzul Isle Route key item.

What this opens

  • ToAU 42: Path of Darkness
