How to obtain

Possible drop from notorious monsters in Nyzul Isle Investigation. The floor-100 HNM has a guaranteed job-based weapon drop tied to the Runic Disc holder’s job; use Dancer for Main Gauche.

Two uses

GoalQuest
Pyrrhic Kleos with any suitable daggerUnlocking a Myth (Dancer)
Terpsichore level-75 Mythic weaponMythic Weapon Acquisition at 75

Do the weapon-skill unlock before handing the dagger to Abquhbah if you want to use the same copy for both routes.
