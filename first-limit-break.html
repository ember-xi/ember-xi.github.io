Accept

At level 50 with your limit still at 50, speak to Maat in Ru’Lude Gardens (H-5). Accept In Defiant Challenge through his original dialogue.

Drop assistance

Original drops remain random. After the third eligible kill of a reviewed monster of that type in the listed zone, its missing item is granted directly, once. Stay alive, in the same zone and within 50 yalms with valid kill credit. Trusts can help. Existing counters remain.

Full inventory

Free a slot, then speak to Maat, change zones or log in again to retry delivery. Discarding a guaranteed item does not grant a second guaranteed copy.

Trade and reward

  1. Put 1 Exoray Mold, 1 Bomb Coal and 1 Ancient Papyrus in your main inventory.
  2. Trade exactly those three items together to Maat and complete his original event.
  3. Reward: level limit 55 and the Horizon Breaker title. The three items are consumed.

Solo access: Eldieme gates

Before entering gated rooms, buy Magicked Astrolabe from Churano-Shurano at Windurst Waters North (F-8), on the Optistery roof, for 10,000 gil. Step-by-step directions. This is an optional access aid, not a required turn-in item.

Solo access: Banishing Gates

Obtain Pouch of Weighted Stones from ??? at Map 1 (G-8) to open the three gates yourself. Full directions, requirements and map · Gate locations.

Zenith availability: This target is hidden in the reviewed Zenith setup. Weighted Stones v1.0 corrects its availability; installation and an in-game check are pending. No level or prior quest unlocks it.
