Walkthrough

  • These quests are HIDDEN, and cannot be seen in your Quest list until completed.
  • Go to the following quest and complete it first: The Old Monument
  • Go back to Lower Jeuno and talk to Bki Tbujhja again. She mentions another set of Song Runes in Valkurm Dunes.
  • Head to the Secret Beach at far west of Valkurm Dunes (C-6). Drop out onto the hidden beach and head south east to the bottom right of (B-7).
  • Click on the Song Rune for another cutscene to complete the quest.

Client: Lewenhart (Song Runes, Valkurm Dunes)

You have taken the first step. The path of the bard lies before you.
