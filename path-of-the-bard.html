RepeatableNo
DescriptionYou have taken the first step. The path of the bard lies before you.

  • Ability to become a Bard

A Minstrel in Despair—

Walkthrough

  • Optional: talk to Mataligeat, then Mertaire again.
  • Talk to Bki Tbujhja again. She tells you of a second set of Song Runes in Valkurm Dunes.
    • Do not buy another Parchment — it is not needed here.
  • Go to the hidden beach at the far west of Valkurm Dunes through the tunnel at (C-6). On the beach head southeast to the bottom right of (B-7).
  • Examine the Song Runes for the final cutscene. Bard is unlocked.
    • This quest does not show in your log until it is complete.
