Notes

  • All have the Sheep special attacks. All are susceptible to Gravity, and partially resistant to Bind.
  • The Sheep's melee attacks cause knockback.
  • The drops are a belt, a ring, a Mahogany Log/Mythril Ingot and a Manticore Hide
  • All are highly resistant to Ice elemental magic (including Freeze and Bind).
