Walkthrough

  • To start this wait until after Japanese Midnight after you finished Aht Urhgan Mission 3: President Salaheem.
  • Talk to Naja Salaheem for a cutscene.
  • Talk to Cacaroon (G-11) for a cutscene.
  • Trade Cacaroon either an Imperial Bronze Piece or 1000 gil for another cutscene with Raillefal.
  • Enter the Walahra Temple (K-8) for a cutscene with Nadeey and Raillefal.
  • Head over to the Shararat Teahouse (K-12), 2nd floor for another cutscene.
    • In the dialogue boxes, choose The Empire, Walahra Temple, and Salaheem's Sentinels each time you are asked what you have learned.
    • After selecting all 3 items, Raillefal's secret will appear. Click this choice.
    • Of the third list, choose any option in the list.
    • Of the fourth list, choose any option in the list to receive Raillefal's letter and complete the mission.

Game Description

Mission Orders
Bring Raillefal back to Salaheem's Sentinels and help teach him the proper attitude for a mercenary.

Notes

  • If the quest Two Horn the Savage is active, just go back and talk to Naja Salaheem then return to Cacaroon again.
