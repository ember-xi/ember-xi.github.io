Klimaform

From Zenith XI Wiki

SCH 46 · Dark magic · Scholar artifact-quest reward.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Complete On Sabbatical, available at SCH 40 after unlocking Scholar. Its reward is the Klimaform scroll; learn it at SCH 46.

Use

  1. Give yourself weather matching the element you plan to cast, using a Storm spell when needed.
  2. Cast Klimaform on yourself.
  3. Cast spells of the matching element while its effect lasts for improved magic accuracy.

The spell depends on matching weather. It is not a damage spell and is not an AF weapon.
