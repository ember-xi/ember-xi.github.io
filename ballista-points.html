Ballista Points

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

These points can be earned by participating official Ballista matches.

You can spend points either for key items (vouchers) that can be exchanged for temporary items for a Ballista match, or for teleporting to and from Ballista matches..

For further information read the topics at Ballista.
