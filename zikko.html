Zikko

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-80 imp notorious monster in Caedarva Mire.

Contents
  1. Find and fight
  2. Drops
  3. Sources

Find and fight

Search the marsh pools between Alzadaal Undersea Ruins and Dvucca Isle Staging Point in Caedarva Mire. It may appear when a player crossing the marsh receives Weight; it is not a normal placeholder camp. The reported respawn delay is about two hours.

Prepare for damaging area Silence and Amnesia attacks, shadow removal, Manafont and Sleepga II.

Drops

Templar Hammer and Immortal's Sash are possible drops.
