Zenith adjustments

Dual Wield

The first tier provides a 10% delay reduction. Equip one weapon in Main and the other in Sub; carrying both in inventory is not enough. The change applies to existing and new characters.

With /THF, the support job must reach level 20, normally requiring main-job level 40. Job Balance v1.0.1 adds Dual Wield II at level 40; it requires the new package.

Other abilities

Treasure Hunter remains unchanged. The new Ambush recast benefit is described below.

Job reference

Job Overview


Specializing in covert actions, Thieves (THF) aim for the perfect opportunity to attack the enemy from behind. Using Sneak Attack and Trick Attack, thieves can inflict massive amounts of damage while 'tricking' the enemy into thinking it was someone else. This helps to control the party's hate levels, and trick enemies into thinking that the party's tank is the primary threat.


Thieves also have many tools at their disposal to get out of sticky situations in the game. Not only do they possess the highest Evasion rating in the game, but they also have abilities such as Flee and Hide, which allows them to escape most dangers unharmed. Additional abilities such as Steal, Gilfinder, and Treasure Hunter, as well as their unique ability to pick Treasure Chests and Treasure Coffers with their thief's tools, makes the Thief the optimal choice for acquiring gil and items.
