Demon Killer

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Gives an edge against demon enemies.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Occasionally causes demon type enemies to be intimidated.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Dragon Earring
  • Demon Slayer / Devil Slayer
  • Staurobow / Staurobow +1

Demon Killer

  • Obtained: Samurai Level 40
