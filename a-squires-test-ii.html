Walkthrough

  • Talk to Balasiel in Southern San d'Oria (F-7).
  • Optional: talk to Chanpau in Southern San d'Oria (E-7) and Morjean in Northern San d'Oria (L-7, Cathedral manuscript room).
    • Morjean will not talk about this quest while you are between his step and the Wyvern Egg step of The Holy Crest (Dragoon quest). It does not block the quest.
  • Bring Sneak and Invisible (or the items), and Warp or Escape for a quick exit.
  • Go to La Theine Plateau and enter the ravine at (F-6). Past Equesobillot, follow the left wall to the Ordelle's Caves entrance at (F-7).
  • Inside Ordelle's Caves follow the right wall to the Map 1 transition at (G-4). Go down the steps to the large room at (I-5). Take the south exit and follow the path to Map 2 at (G-6).
    • From the other entrance (La Theine H-7) you start at the north end of Map 1: go to the room at (I-5), take the south exit and climb the steps to Map 2 at (H-6).
  • Continue to the large room at (H-6) on Map 2.
    • Under level 40 keep Sneak up: Shriekers, Hognosed Bats and Jellies in this room aggro to sound.
  • Touch the ??? in the pool of water on your left, then quickly touch the second ??? near the centre of the room to receive the key item Stalactite Dew.
    • No key item means you missed a step or waited too long between the two ???.
    • A Will-o'-the-Wisp may stand near the pool; it aggros to sight.
  • Return to Balasiel. If you already meet the level, A Knight's Test can be started without zoning.
