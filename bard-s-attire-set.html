Bard's Attire Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

An armor set from the Dynamis zones, which the player may be rewarded with for defeating the monsters within. The set has an identical model to the Choral set but with a texture upgrade. The Bard's Set is a light purple, instead of the patchwork brown of the Bard's quested AF, and has a gold trim on the headpiece and along the arms. A fur trim on the jacket completes the look.

The set naturally has enhancements to those attributes and skills most useful to a Bard, namely Charisma, and enhancements to Singing, Wind Instrument, and String Instrument skills, along with a negative Enmity bonus. The set also has bonuses to aspects most often associated with that of a Damage Dealing class, namely Attack, Accuracy, Parrying and Evasion. Of particular note is the addition of Military Parade and Courtly Measure, which provide significant attribute boosts/penalties depending on whether the Bard has their weapon drawn on an enemy or not.
