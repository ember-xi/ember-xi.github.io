Zenith availability

The route has been checked against references and available code; completion on Zenith has not yet been play-tested.

Before you begin

  • Complete AMK 7.
  • Sahagin Key access for the Ornamented Door.
  • Sneak and a return/escape method recommended.

Starting point

Shady Sconce · Sea Serpent Grotto · hidden room beyond C-8

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

Walkthrough

  1. Prepare a Sahagin Key before taking the one-way Ornamented Door route. One party member can open it for the group.
  2. From Sea Serpent Grotto C-8, pass through both Cracked Walls into the hidden room. Examine the Shady Sconce at the back; check again to finish the Moogle scene.
  3. Go to the Ornamented Door at J-11 and use the Sahagin Key. Beyond it, follow the bridge to the Waterfall Basin at H-6 and examine the basin, not the nearby ???.
  4. Return to the Inconspicuous Door in Upper Jeuno H-8 for the scene completing this mission.

Battle and progress notes

  • The Sahagin Key is consumed when opening the door; avoid accidentally leaving the required section before examining the basin.
