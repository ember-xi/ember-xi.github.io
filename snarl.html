Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Pet Command

  • Transfers hate to your pet. Only works on pets invoked with the "Call Beast" ability.
  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 45
  • Recast Time: 30 seconds
  • Duration: N/A

Notes/Description

Obtained at Level 45 by the beastmaster, Snarl transfers all hate generated by you to your pet.

  • Enmity will not be transferred if you did not originally attack an aggressive monster (i.e. a monster linked with the monster you attacked).

Macro Syntax

  • /pet "Snarl" <me>
