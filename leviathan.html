Leviathan

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Avatar Information

A great serpent said to rule over the rivers and the seas. Leviathan aids the summoner with its command over waves and water.

Summoners can acquire the ability to summon the avatar Leviathan by defeating Leviathan Prime in either the Trial Size Trial by Water quest at level 20, or the Trial by Water quest at a much higher level (65+).
