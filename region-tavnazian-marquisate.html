Tavnazian Marquisate

From Zenith XI Wiki

Cities

  • Tavnazian Safehold
Additional level-75 reference

After the devastating destruction of Tavnazia by the Orcish Hosts during the Crystal War, the Tavnazian Marquisate has been forced to relocate to a remote cave hidden away on the Tavnazian Archipelago.

Despite the Tavnazian Safehold being its only area, the Tavnazian Marquisate is considered a region, unlike the Chocobo Circuit, an isolated area that is considered to have no region.
