Goblin Digger

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job: Warrior
Family: Goblin
Crystal: Fire
Weak to: Light

Has Vermin Killer trait. Seen intimidating a MayFlyFamiliar.

Special actions:
Can sometimes be seen to "bury" items instead of dropping them after battle. This is a special action related to Chocobo Digging - see the quest Orlando's Antiques for details.
