Sheep Leather Missive

From Zenith XI Wiki

The trigger item for Yuhtunga Jungle Garrison.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. How to use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Defeat Brook Sahagin (levels 41–48) or Riparian Sahagin (levels 44–48) in Sea Serpent Grotto. The missive is a possible drop, not a guaranteed reward.

How to use

Prepare for the level-40 Garrison event, then trade the missive to the eligible Conquest overseer at the Yuhtunga Jungle outpost (G-11). Check Garrison’s entry conditions before trading; this starts a defense battle. The item cannot be bought through the Auction House.
