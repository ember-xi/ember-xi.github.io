Command/blacklist

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Aliases: /blist
Usage: /blacklist [subcommand] [PC_name]
Description:

Views the list of PCs on your chat filter
blacklist. This is different from the
Friend List menu's Ignore List.

Subcommands:
list Display your blacklist.
add Add a PC to your blacklist.
delete Delete a PC from your blacklist.

Displays your blacklist when no subcommand
is specified.

Example: /blacklist add <stpc>

Notes

Players On Your Own Blacklist:

You will not see any chat (including emotes) or receive party or alliance invitations.
You cannot send messages via /tell to PCs on your blacklist, but you can send party and alliance invitations to them.

Players that Have You Blacklisted:

They will not receive your party or alliance invitations.
They will not be able to see your chat. (including emotes)

Other Pertinent Information:

There is a hard limit of 300 entries per character on your blacklist.
You cannot add someone to your blacklist unless they are currently logged into the game.
