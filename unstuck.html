!unstuck

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A player can use this command by simply typing !unstuck in the chat window. When used, the player will receive a 20 minute terror debuff where they'll be unable to move. At the end of the 20 minutes, they will be warped to the start of the area that they are currently in.

This command is typically used by players who find themselves stuck outside of a map and have no other way to escape. The 20 minute terror debuff is present to prevent this being abused.

If this command does not work, players should then proceed to put a GM ticket in.
