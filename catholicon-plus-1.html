Statistics

Catholicon +1 Temporary
This powerful elixir is known for its
ability to cure most status ailments.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Other Uses

Ballista: See Notes

Resale Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

Notes
  • Erases up to any 7 Verification Needed negative status effects on either the user or a party member.
    • Seems to erase anything that can be removed by Erase or -na spells, with the exception of Curse.
      • Curse can be obtained during Ballista if an opponent has any equipment, such as an Ancient Sword, which inflicts Curse. See talk page for more details.
  • In Ballista, this can be used on anyone on the same side as the user.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
