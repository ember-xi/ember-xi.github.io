The Holy Crest

The Holy Crest
Starting NPCArnau — Northern San d'Oria (L-7), Cathedral
Required FameNone
Level RestrictionLevel 30
Items NeededPickaxe, Wyvern Egg, key item: Dragon Curse Remedy
TitleNone
RepeatableNo
DescriptionExcavate the wyvern egg that lies beneath the Maze of Shakhrami and protect it from the dragonslayers.
RequirementsAccess to Chateau d'Oraguille (San d'Oria Rank 2, or mission 2-3 started for Bastok and Windurst)
Rewards
  • A wyvern of your own
  • Ability to become a Dragoon
← Previous questNext quest →
——

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Ceraulian in Cargo Room A, Port San d'Oria.
  • Talk to Novalmauge in Bostaunieux Oubliette (under Chateau d'Oraguille).
    • If the scythe weapon skill quest Souls in Shadow is active you must finish it first. Pick the dialogue options carefully.
  • Talk to Morjean in the manuscript room of the Cathedral, Northern San d'Oria (L-7). Oiheaurese asks you to bring back an egg.
  • Buy a Pickaxe and go to the Maze of Shakhrami (enter from Tahrongi Canyon K-5). Trade the Pickaxe to an excavation point (first floor: E-7, F-7, G-7, I-8, I-10, K-7 or L-8) until you dig up a Wyvern Egg.
  • Return to Morjean with the egg for a cutscene. Yachemidot asks you to take it to the Meriphataud Mountains.
  • In Meriphataud Mountains find the ??? at the eastern edge of Drogaroga's Spine (K-8) and trade the Wyvern Egg to it.
  • Talk to Rahal in the Royal Knights' Quarters, Chateau d'Oraguille (left as you enter) for the key item Dragon Curse Remedy.
    • If Lure of the Wildcat is active he scolds you first — talk to him again.
  • Go to the hut at (F-10/G-10) in Ghelsba Outpost and open the door to enter the battlefield.
    • Six players, no level cap, buffs stay on. Only players on this step (or who finished it) may enter.
    • Defeat Cyranuce M Cutauleon, a wyvern around level 40.
  • Zone once and Dragoon appears in your job list.
