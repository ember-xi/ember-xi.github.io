Icarus Wing
When used, these man-made wings
instantly raise TP. Due to muscle
strain, repeated use is prohibited.

Stackable: 12

  • Grants user 100% TP upon usage.
  • Takes 3 seconds to use.
  • Gives Medicated effect for 2 hours.
