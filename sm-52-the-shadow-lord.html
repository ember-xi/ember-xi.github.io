The Shadow Lord (San d'Oria 5-2)

San d'Oria Mission 5-2
MissionThe Shadow Lord
TypeBCNM
Start NPCHalver — Chateau d'Oraguille
LocationCastle Zvahl Baileys → Castle Zvahl Keep → Throne Room
RequirementsRank 5 · 5-1 complete
Items NeededNone
RewardsRank 6 · 20,000 gil

Mission 5-2 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 5-1 The Ruins of Fei'Yin.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Halver.
  2. Cross Castle Zvahl Baileys and the Keep to the Throne Room. Enter the Battlefield against the Shadow Lord (two phases).
  3. Take the Shadow Fragment and return to Halver for Rank 6. This also unlocks Rise of the Zilart.
Mechanics: In phase two the Shadow Lord switches between immunity to physical and magic damage. Bring a BLM Trust and a melee Trust. Use Reraise before entering.
