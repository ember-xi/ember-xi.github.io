Dream Flower

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Puts all enemies within range to sleep.

Description

  • Family: Mandragora
  • Type: Enfeebling
  • Can be dispelled: N/A
  • Utsusemi/Blink absorb: Ignores shadows
  • Range: 15' radial
  • Notes: In Dynamis - Buburimu, the Nightmare Mandragora's Dream Flower is enhanced to undisrupted by damage similar to Nightmare.
