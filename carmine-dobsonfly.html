Notes:

  • Spawns on the island at G-11 approximately every 21-24 hours.
  • 10 copies of this NM spawn at once, and are located on an island near each other. When one NM is attacked all the rest will eventually link, regardless of how close they are to the attacked NM, or how it was pulled.
  • All the Dobsonflies are susceptible to: Lullaby, Sleep, Silence
  • Item Drops: A Dobson Bandanna drops 100% of the time from the dobsonflies. Also, a Jaeger Ring drops around 30% of the time; a Voyager Sallet drops around 10% of the time.
  • Around 1300-1700HP each.
  • Special Attacks: Casts Slowga (over-writes Haste) and Aero II, Haste on itself; Somersault (physical damage on target); Venom (AOE poison); Debilitating Drone (AOE stat down)
  • Killable by: 12 characters at level 40 (level capped area), with 3+ Black Mage or Bard for crowd control; it can be done with less, using three Summoners with Astral Flow for fast kills. For best results a party of 5 summoners and a thief all with 2 hours ready is used, with thief using perfect dodge and pulling the dobsonflies and all the summoners using Shiva's Diamond Dust for an instant Chain 9.
