Notes:

  • Spawned by trading a Merrow No. 11 Molting to a ??? at the northeast corner of (I-10[[File:{{#section:Arrapago Reef/Maps|Map 2}}|512px]]<span data-ws="23"error">Expression error: Unrecognized punctuation character "{".px; z-index:15; width:32px; height:32px; background:

    • Ilrusi Atoll Staging Point, which is on Map 2, is the fastest, safest way to to the spawn point.
    • There are a few Imps along the way from Ilrusi Atoll Staging Point, but otherwise there are no True Sight mobs.
  • Does an attack similar to jump that stuns. It can be done multiple times in a row. can kill mages with 1 or 2 strikes. Utsusemi may be helpful.
  • May use the jump move up to five times in a row.
  • Immune to Stun.
  • Lamia No.19 may spawn in the cave, down the ramp, if someone dies during the fight. this can happen often.
  • May have some sort of hate reset, especially with dagger out and using the jumping attack.
  • Death is not required, low HP will cause Lamia No.19 spawn.
  • Spams an AoE Drain move at about 20% HP that does 200-300 damage. Unblest Jambiya possibly prevented by breaking sword with critical hits.
  • Uses Blood Weapon.
