Zenith availability

The route has been checked against references and available code; completion on Zenith has not yet been play-tested.

Before you begin

  • Main job level 10 or higher.
  • A Shantotto Ascension must be enabled on the server.

Starting point

Area entry · Windurst Walls

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. With your main job at level 10 or higher, enter Windurst Walls on foot from Windurst Waters or Windurst Woods.
  2. Watch the opening scenes and confirm Burgeoning Dread is active. Coming directly from your Mog House is not the required entrance trigger.

Completion and reward

Story progression.
