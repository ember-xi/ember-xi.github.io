Devilet

From Zenith XI Wiki

Imp enemies with route-opening roles in the original Silver Sea Remnants.

Contents
  1. Third-floor north route
  2. Second-floor southeast route
  3. Sources

Third-floor north route

  1. Use a northern teleporter from floor two of Silver Sea Remnants.
  2. Defeat the fast-moving Devilet in the northern room. It repeatedly disengages and resumes its patrol as it loses HP; pursue and defeat it.
  3. Its defeat unlocks progression and reveals the Slot. Trade Zhayolm Card there to spawn Don Poroggo.

Second-floor southeast route

Four Devilets must be defeated to open that wing’s teleporter. Do not confuse these with the separately named Deadpan Devilet on the northwest route.
