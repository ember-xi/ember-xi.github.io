Lynx Leather

From Zenith XI Wiki

Leathercraft material used in Scholar artifact and relic upgrades.

Buy from Auction House → Materials → Leathercraft. Standard synthesis: Leathercraft71, Dark Crystal, Lynx Hide ×1, Windurstian Tea Leaves ×1 and Distilled Water ×1; yield one leather.

Farm Lynx Hide from Lynxes in Meriphataud Mountains (S) or purchase the hide. Use the finished leather for Scholar's Gown +1, Scholar's Bracers +1 or Argute Bracers +1.
