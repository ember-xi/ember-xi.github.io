Zephyr Mantle

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-65 Blue Magic that creates four Blink-like shadows on the caster.

Contents
  1. Learn it
  2. Spell details
  3. Sources

Learn it

  1. Fight Puk in Wajaom Woodlands or Mamook, or Sea Puk in Mamook or Bhaflau Thickets.
  2. Let the monster use Zephyr Mantle, then defeat it while eligible to learn Blue Magic. Learning is not guaranteed.

Spell details

31 MP; 2 Blue Magic set points; AGI +2 when set. Casting time: 7 seconds. Recast: 60 seconds. Maximum duration: 5 minutes. Pair with a suitable spell such as Chaotic Eye for Conserve MP. These are Blink-style shadows, not guaranteed Utsusemi absorption.
