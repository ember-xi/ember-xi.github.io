Were you looking for the general concept of Magic Accuracy?

Merit Point Enhancement

  • Category: Job-specific - Group 1 (Blue Mage)
  • Limits: 5 Levels (Merit Point progression: 1>2>3>4>5 = 15 points total)
  • Description: Each level of this enhancement provides +2 Magic Accuracy to magical Blue Magic spells per upgrade, up to a maximum of +10 magic accuracy.
