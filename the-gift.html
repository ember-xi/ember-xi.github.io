Walkthrough

  • You need to finish Under the Sea and have begun The Sand Charm to get this quest.
  • Talk to Oswald. He asks you to get him a Danceshroom.
    • Danceshrooms drop off Shriekers (Ordelle's Caves), Myxomycetes (Sanctuary of Zi'Tah), Killer Mushrooms (Crawlers' Nest), Exorays (Crawlers' Nest), and Death Caps.
    • Danceshrooms can be bought from the regional vendor for Movalpolos in each of the three major cities.
    • Danceshrooms can also be obtained from the BCNM Toadal Recall, Chocobo Digging in Yuhtunga Jungle, Harvesting in Yuhtunga Jungle and Yhoator Jungle, and Gardening.
