Before you begin

  • Complete CoP 2-3.

Starting point

Walnut Door · Tavnazian Safehold · K-7, upper floor

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Inspect the Walnut Door at K-7 upstairs and finish the scene with Prishe to recover the Mysterious Amulet.
  2. Leave the Safehold for Misareaux Coast. Examine the Dilapidated Gate at I-11 for Ulmia's scene.
  3. Return to the Safehold and approach its central bridge for the final scene.

Completion and reward

Mysterious Amulet returned.

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Justinius (J-6 on the upper floor, to end CoP 2-3)
  • Go to the Walnut Door at K-7 for a cutscene, you will receive the "Mysterious Amulet" Key Item.
  • Head out to the Misareaux Coast at I-11 and check the Dilapidated Gate for a cutscene.
  • Return to the Safehold and walk towards the Bridge for a final cutscene.
