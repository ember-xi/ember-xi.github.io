Before you begin

  • Win Through the Quicksand Caves; retain all eight elemental fragments.

Starting point

Eight elemental pedestals · Chamber of Oracles

Walkthrough

  1. In the room reached after the Antican battle, examine each of the eight pedestals: Darkness, Earth, Fire, Ice, Light, Lightning, Water and Wind.
  2. Finish the cutscene after placing the last fragment. If interrupted, examine a pedestal again to resume it.
  3. Check for the Prismatic Fragment and Return to Delkfutt’s Tower in your mission log before departing.

Battle and progress notes

  • This step requires no additional fight. The native completion scene removes the eight fragments and awards the Prismatic Fragment.

Completion and reward

Prismatic Fragment; title: Lightweaver.

What this opens

  • Return to Delkfutt’s Tower

Walkthrough

  • After winning the BCNM in Zilart Mission 6, you will be put into a room with a large device.
  • Touch each of the 8 parts of this device to put your shards into it.
  • After each shard is put into the device, you will be presented with a cutscene. This is Zilart Mission 7.
