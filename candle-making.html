Walkthrough

  • Rouliette agrees to make you a candle if you can get her a Lanolin Cube.
  • Lanolin Cubes drop from Battering Rams in La Theine Plateau.
  • Trade the Lanolin Cube to Rouliette to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client
Rouliette (Temple of the Goddess, Upper Jeuno)
Summary
Bring a lanolin cube to Rouliette so she can make you a candle.
