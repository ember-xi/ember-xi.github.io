Shanana

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: NPC

Location: Windurst Waters, North (G-11)

Notes

Once in several hundred years, a great wizard is born out of the Tarutaru in Windurst. This wizard is so powerful, he is called the Mastermind of the Millennium.

Decades ago, a child formidable enough to carry that title graduated at the top of his class from the School of Magic. I'm sure you'll hear his name mentioned as the hero who saved Windurst.
