Notes:

  • Spawns at (K-9), on the hill near the tunnel entrance. Spawn specifics are unknown, some reports state that he alternates spawning with Snow Maiden when the Morozkos around (K-9) are left unharmed. Other reports state he appears only if Snow Maiden is left undefeated for an undefined amount of time.
  • Around 12000HP
  • Killable by: 75 BLM, 75 RDM, or 67+ SMN (marathon Carbuncle kiting little over 2 hours)
  • Attempted by a 65 SMN/WHM, after 16 minutes of Carbuncle kiting, only about 10% of its HP was gone. It does not appear to use Hypothermal Combustion.
  • Special Attacks: Additional Effect: Ice Spikes (not dispellable)
  • Purgatory Collar has a higher drop rate than Snow Maiden.
