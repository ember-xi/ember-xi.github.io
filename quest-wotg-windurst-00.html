Before you begin

  • Access to the past through Wings of the Goddess.
  • For first enlistment, obtain a Green recommendation letter. Another nation’s enlistment quest must not be active.

Starting point

Miah Riyuh · Windurst Waters [S] · H-9

Walkthrough

  1. Obtain the Green recommendation letter from Kalsu-Kalasu in Crawlers’ Nest [S] (L-8), if this is your first Campaign enlistment.
  2. Speak to Miah Riyuh and accept the assignment to receive Zonpa-Zippa’s all-purpose putty.
  3. In West Sarutabaruta [S], examine all three Sealed Entrances at F-4, J-8 and F-11. Each consumes part of the putty; the order does not matter.
  4. Return to Miah Riyuh and confirm enlistment. Leave an inventory slot for the first-enlistment shoes.

Battle and progress notes

  • Completing this quest sets your Campaign allegiance to Windurst.
  • For the Windurst story, completing Steamed Rams or The Fighting Fourth also satisfies the initial enlistment requirement; changing allegiance is unnecessary.

Completion and reward

Cobra Unit Mercenary title; first Campaign enlistment also grants Sprinter’s Shoes and a Bronze Ribbon of Service.

What this opens

  • The Tigress Stirs
  • Windurst Campaign enlistment
