Minuet

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Minuet is a type of Enhancing Song used by Bards. It increases Attack and Ranged Attack stats for party members within range.

Four songs give this effect. Different tiers can be combined.

Notes

  • "Minuet Effect" is one of the items within the Bard Group 1 job-specific merit category. Each time this is merited will increase the Attack boost of each Minuet spell cast by 1, up to a maximum of 5. If the Bard fully merits this item and casts two Minuets, players receiving the effects will have 10 more points of Attack than they would otherwise.
  • A player wearing the Minuet Earring can gain the earring's +3 Accuracy effect by having at least one Minuet spell cast on them. The effect lasts for as long as the player maintains Minuet status.

Songs are learned by Bards only, at the following levels:

3: Valor Minuet
23: Valor Minuet II
43: Valor Minuet III
63: Valor Minuet IV
