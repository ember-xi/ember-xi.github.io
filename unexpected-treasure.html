Walkthrough

  • Morunaude is located in the furniture shop.
  • Buy a Cupboard from him and place it in your Mog House.
  • Wait until the next Vana'diel day and talk to your Moogle for a cutscene where your Moogle tells you he found a Small Teacup. (Note: You must zone out of and back into your mog house if you were already there when the day switched over; your Moogle will not say anything out of the ordinary until you do.)
  • Head back to the furniture shop and talk to Morunaude twice, who will eventually tell you about Calovour.
  • Head to the Cathedral in Northern San d'Oria and talk to Calovour, who asks you to fetch a Mistletoe.
  • Trade a Mistletoe to Calovour to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Morunaude (Furniture Store, Northern San d'Oria)
Summary:

The small teacup found in the cupboard sold at this store appears to have royal origins. The owner would doubtlessly be grateful to have it back.
