Shield Skill

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Shield Skill determines your ability to block an Attack with your Shield, negating some or all Damage. The higher your Shield Skill, the more often it will activate (relative to mob level).

To activate Shield skill you must:

  • Have a shield equipped.
