Scholar Artifact Upgrades

From Zenith XI Wiki

Complete recipes for Scholar artifact armor +1.

Unlock Sea / Garden of Antiquity and speak to Sagheera in Port Jeuno (I-8). Every piece requires the original armor, one Electrum Stud from Apollyon, and one Brilliantine from Temenos.

Upgrade (SCH 74)Ancient BeastcoinsAdditional material
Scholar's Mortarboard +120Electrum Ingot ×1
Scholar's Gown +140Lynx Leather ×1
Scholar's Bracers +125Lynx Leather ×1
Scholar's Pants +125Ether Leather ×1
Scholar's Loafers +125Karakul Cloth ×1
  1. Collect the two Limbus materials and the crafted item. The crafted material can be purchased from the Auction House.
  2. Deposit the listed Ancient Beastcoins with Sagheera separately. Then trade the four recipe items together.
  3. Wait until the next Vana'diel midnight and speak to Sagheera to collect. Finish the current order before starting another.

The listed costs follow the reviewed Zenith base: Mortarboard 20, Gown 40, and 25 for each other slot. This avoids conflicting retail-wiki tables.
