Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

File:Petrification (Status Effect).png Petrification is a harmful status effect that turns the player to stone until it wears off or is removed. Afflicted characters cannot move, attack, cast spells, or use abilities.

How to remove the effect

  • The White Magic spell Stona will remove this effect.
  • The negative status effect Terror will overwrite the effect of petrification, replacing it with the Terror effect.

How the effect is inflicted

Spells

  • Break (Enemy-only)
  • Breakga (Enemy-only)

Monster Abilities

  • Ahriman - Level 5 Petrify
  • Antican - Sand Trap
  • Cockatrice - Baleful Gaze
  • Dragon - Petro Eyes
  • Farlarder the Shrewd - Additional effect on Hundred Fists
  • Lizard - Baleful Gaze
  • Ram - Petribreath
  • Troll - Rock Smash
  • Zdei - Optic Induration
