Moghancement: Lightning
Description:
Moogle-powered assistance from your Mog House.
Reduces the chances of losing materials when synthesis attempts utilizing a lightning crystal have failed.
Obtained:
It is obtained through these Furnishings:
  •   6 - Console
  •   4 - Falsiam Vase
  •   2 - Flower Stand
  •   2 - Lightning Lamp
  •   2 - My First Magic Kit
  •   3 - Tableware Set
Notes:
This is helpful when you're leveling a craft and using a lot of Lightning Crystals. When you fail, you will have a greater chance of not losing your materials. The stronger this Moghancement is, the less you will lose overall. See Moghancements for more information.
