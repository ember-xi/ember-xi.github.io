Infiltrate Davoi (San d'Oria 3-1)

San d'Oria Mission 3-1
MissionInfiltrate Davoi
TypeRepeatable
Start NPCGate Guard (Endracion or Ambrotien in Southern San d'Oria; Grilau in Northern San d'Oria)
LocationDavoi
RequirementsRank 3
Items NeededNone
Rewards300 Rank points

Mission 3-1 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 2-3 Journey Abroad.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission from a Gate Guard.
  2. In Davoi, collect the three block codes (North / East / South Block Code) by examining the ! markers in each block.
  3. Talk to Quemaricond (or Zantaviat) inside Davoi for the Royal Knights Davoi Report.
  4. Return to a Gate Guard.
Mechanics: Orcs are level 25-35 with Warlords. Your fourth Trust (level 30) makes this easy.
