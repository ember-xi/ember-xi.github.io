Lago-Charago

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Adventurer's Assistant NPC

Location: Windurst Waters, North (F-8)

Notes

Offers Information on the Constellations:

Whaddaya want-want? Can't ya see I'm busy-busy preparing for my astronomic observations? Hm. I infer somebody wants to see my stellar stellar map, perhaps?

"Stellar!"

  • [Shows a map of the constellations] Humph-humph. Good to see someone has an interest in the stars. Climbing the northern sky is the famous Odin. The black point there is Odin's beloved steer, Sleipnir. And above that--although in the actual night sky it appears below Sleipnir--is the North Star, the prominently bright star that sailors guide their ships by. If you want to further further your interest in the stars, then you're always welcome to visit here and see my stellar stellar map.

"Map-schmap!"

  • Then don't bug-bug me again! People's lives are but a fleeting moment when compared to the lives of the stars. There are so many stars to map, I don't wanna waste a second second!

  • Lure of the Wildcat (Windurst)
