Howling Fist

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Hand-to-Hand weapon skill

  • Skill level: 200 (Monks and Puppetmasters only.)
  • Damage varies with TP.


Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Transfixion / Impaction
  • Modifiers: STR:20% ; VIT:50%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
2.50 2.75 3.00

Levels

  • Howling Fist is only available to Monks and Puppetmasters. While other jobs can obtain the required 200 Hand-to-Hand skill, they will be unable to use Howling Fist.
  • Hand-to-Hand skill level 200 is attainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
Job Rating Level
Monk A+ 60
Puppetmaster B 62
Warrior D 70
Thief E 75
Ninja E 75
