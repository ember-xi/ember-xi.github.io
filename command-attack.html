Command/attack

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Aliases: /a
Usage: /attack [subcommand]
Description:

Turns auto-attack of the selected
target on/off.

Subcommands:
on Turn on auto-attack.
off Turn off auto-attack.

Toggles on and off when no subcommand
is specified.

Example: /attack <stnpc>

Notes

If you enable the "Auto-target during battle" option in the Gameplay section of your Config menu, then your character will automatically lock on to enemies that you /attack.
