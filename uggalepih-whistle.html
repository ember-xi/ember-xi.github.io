Uggalepih Whistle
Tonberries use this whistle to warn
their comrades of danger. It emits a
high-pitched sound that hurts one's
ears at close range.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Other Uses

Used to Spawn: Sacrificial Goblet

Used in Quests: None

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.
