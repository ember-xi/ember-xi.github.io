Imperial Merchant

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Imperial merchants deal in Imperial currency exchange. Either exchanging Imperial Standing (IS) for imperial coin, or exchanging coin for higher and lower denominations (gold to silver, bronze to mythril, etc). There are two NPCs can that assist for these transactions:

NPCs

  • Ugrihd - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (G-9), 2nd Level
  • Will sell Imperial currency in exchange for your Imperial Standing.
  • Awaheen - Nashmau (G-9), 2nd Level
  • Will exchange your Imperial currency into lower or higher denominations.
