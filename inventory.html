How it works

  1. Install Adventures v1.1 with the server stopped. It supports the previous Adventures package and the reviewed server without Adventures installed.
  2. Continue playing with your existing items and quest progress. No trade, fee or GM command is required.

What changes

The update changes main Inventory capacity. It does not replace items, relocate them or enlarge other storage containers. Bag-upgrade reminders are removed from the Adventure guide. Original story and limit-break quests remain.

Current status

The player reported a 50-slot starting bag before this update. The new 80-slot behavior is implemented and tested offline; installation and the in-game capacity display have not yet been confirmed.

Rollback

Rolling back the package restores its prior files while retaining earned rewards, saved activity progress and any inventory capacity already granted. It does not shrink the bag.

Additional level-75 reference

A place used to store items that can be accessed anywhere, at any time. Also known as a Gobbiebag, your inventory is identified as a spacious backpack that you carry with you while you're exploring Vana'diel.

Upgrading

When you start the game it can carry a maximum of 30 items. You can upgrade this amount to 60 through a series of quests:

  • The Gobbiebag Part I : Dhalmel Leather, Steel Ingot, Linen Cloth, Peridot
  • The Gobbiebag Part II : Ram Leather, Mythril Ingot, Wool Cloth, Turquoise
  • The Gobbiebag Part III : Tiger Leather, Gold Ingot, Velvet Cloth, Painite
  • The Gobbiebag Part IV : Cermet Chunk, Darksteel Ingot, Silk Cloth, Goshenite
  • The Gobbiebag Part V : Bugard Leather, Paktong Ingot, Moblinweave, Rhodonite
  • The Gobbiebag Part VI : Balloon Cloth, Shakudo Ingot, High-Quality Eft Skin, Iolite


Additional Inventories

Mog Safe/Mog Safe 2

Players start with full access to Mog Safe and Mog Safe 2. Their storage capactiy can be upgraded via the following quests:

  • Give a Moogle a Break (50 -> 60) : Bronze Bed, Power Bow, Beetle Ring
  • The Moogle's Picnic! (60 -> 70) : Mahogany Bed, Selbina Butter, Shrimp Lure
  • Moogles in the Wild (70 -> 80) : Noble's Bed, Raptor Mantle, Wool Hat


Storage

Storage is obtained by placing items of furniture into their mog house. The storage capacity limit is 80.

Mog Locker

The Mog Locker can be unlocked by speaking to the Aht Urhgan NPC Fubruhn and completing the necessary procedures (Mission 2: Immortal Sentries), players can use their Mog Lockers in all Mog houses and rent-a-rooms in Vana'diel. The Mog Safe 2 is opened by completing the San'doria, Bastok and Windurst Mog House Exit Quests.

Mog Wardrobe

Mog Wardrobes one and two are currently available on Eden. Players can unlock them by fulfilling the following requirements:

  • Mog Wardrobe 1: Completed mission 2-3 for their respective nation.
  • Mog Wardrobe 2: Completed mission 5-2 for their respective nation.


Once a player has met these requirements, they can unlock the corresponding wardrobe by speaking to their moogle.
