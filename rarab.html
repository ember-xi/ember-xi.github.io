Rarab
Rabbits are herbivorous creatures that propel themselves by hopping with their powerful hind legs and tails. They are known for their distinctively long ears and charming eyes. These small, bipedal creatures of Vana'diel can be found in various environments throughout the Quon continent, and can usually be distinguished by their relative strength and, occasionally, their color. The rabbits of the Quon continent, hares, have brown fur with the snowflake-like spots. Lapinion rabbits are found in the East Ulbuka Territory, and can usually be distinguished from other rabbits by their fluffy fur and orange color.
