Before you begin

  • Complete CoP 3-2.
  • Both branch routes, including their NM fights and the Mimeo Jewel mountain climb.

Starting point

City arrival scenes · Northern San d'Oria

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Complete both branch guides: 3-3A Emerald Waters in San d'Oria, and 3-3B Memories of a Maiden in Windurst. Either order works and their progress is tracked separately.
  2. Once both branches are complete, speak to Cid at Metalworks H-8 for the concluding scene.

Required paths

  • 3-3A — Emerald Waters — San d’Oria branch
  • 3-3B — Memories of a Maiden — Windurst branch

Battle and progress notes

  • The fights in these branches are open-world fights, not level-40 BCNM entries. Level 40 is not a guarantee of being able to solo them.

Completion and reward

Story progression.
