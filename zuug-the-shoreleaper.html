Zuug the Shoreleaper

From Zenith XI Wiki

A level-70 Dragoon Sahagin NM in Sea Serpent Grotto, hunted for Narval.

Contents
  1. Where and how
  2. Notable loot
  3. Sources

Where and how

  1. Enter Sea Serpent Grotto and pass the door opened with a Gold Beastcoin.
  2. Search the area around F-10. The Sahagin reference lists a timed spawn after at least two hours.
  3. Prepare for its accompanying Sahagin's Wyvern and nearby Sahagin links.

Notable loot

Narval.
