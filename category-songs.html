These are the the songs that Bards use. Most songs are used to enfeeble enemies or enhance party members.

Songs are unique among classes of magic in that they require no MP or other resources to use. Keep in mind, that bard songs will not activate if the caster is stunned when the casting time ends.

Songs can be used in macros with the /ma or /so commands.
