Notes

  • Aries Attacks very quickly similar to 100 fists effect.
  • Aries uses: Ram special attacks including Severe HP down effect
  • Uses Stoneskin, Paralyga and Petrabreath
  • It is immune to Charm and Stun, but susceptible to Sleep, Gravity and Bind.
  • Regens HP at decent rate. "Aries enters a deep sleep" Presumably, gains TP while sleeping also.
  • Can be taken down with a BLM kite-nuke method.
