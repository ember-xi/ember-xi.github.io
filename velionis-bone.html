Statistics

Velionis' Bone
A shard of blackened bone taken from
the remains of the wicked skeleton,
Velionis.

Other Uses

Resale Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

ZNM: Trade to Sanraku to obtain a Pine Green Seal.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
