The Crimson Trial

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is the first Red Mage Artifact Armor quest - otherwise known as AF1.

  Start NPC:   Sharzalion - Southern San d'Oria (K-6)
  Requirements:
   Red Mage Level 40+
  Items Needed:   Orcish Dry Food
  Title:   None
  Repeatable:   Yes, after AF complete
  Reward:   Fencing Degen
     
  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   Enveloped in Darkness

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Sharzalion at the bar in the Lion Springs Tavern. He wants some Orcish Dry Food.
  • To get this food, you need to get a key to open the Storage Hole which spawns randomly about Davoi.
  • Travel to Davoi and defeat Purpleflash Brukdok around (E-9). He will drop the Davoi Storage Key.
  • Purpleflash Brukdok pops automatically every few minutes (about 5 to 10 min between spawns). If he is not there when you arrive, simply wait a little while. Being a Red Mage is not required for the fight, only to receive the quest.
  • The Storage Hole spawns randomly around Davoi, so just hunt around until you find it. There are flower-shaped patterns on the ground which identify potential spawn positions. Trade the key to it to receive the Orcish Dry Food (key item). The location changes each game day and disappears upon trading the Davoi Storage Key.
  • Some known locations of the Storage Hole are at (G-10), (K-10), (E-10), (F-7), (K-7), (K-9) (I-7) and another at (J-8). There are other locations that can spawn anywhere on the map.
  • Once you have obtained the Orcish Dry Food, talk to Sharzalion to receive your Fencing Degen.

Game Description

Client: Sharzalion (Lion Springs Tavern, Southern San d'Oria)
Summary:

Retrieve some Orcish dried food from a storage hole in Davoi. Sharzalion will let you in on something in return.
