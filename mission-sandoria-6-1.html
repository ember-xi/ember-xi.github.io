Before you begin

Be allied with San d'Oria, have Rank 6, and finish the preceding required story steps. Raise Rank Points if the mission is not offered.

Accept the mission before following its route. If the guard does not offer it, finish your current mission and any closing scenes, then raise Rank Points with crystals at a Conquest guard if required.

Walkthrough

Receive the king’s request

  1. Finish the Great Hall scene after 5-2. Accept Leaute’s Last Wishes from a mission guard, speak to Halver, and examine the Great Hall door.

Find the flower

  1. Travel to Western Altepa Desert (G-7). Prepare your party before examining Dreamrose flowers: this summons Sabotender Enamorado.
  2. Defeat the cactuar. Its 1000 Needles shares damage between eligible nearby targets; avoid facing it alone with insufficient HP.
  3. Examine Dreamrose flowers again after victory to receive the Dreamrose key item.

Deliver and follow the story

  1. Return to Halver. Then approach the Chateau garden around (F-8) for the closing scene and Piece of Paper. Keep the paper for the next mission.

Walkthrough

  • Trading three stacks of crystals will give the necessary rank points for this mission.
  • Upon receiving this mission, talk to Halver who will instruct you to speak to the king.
  • It is important to make sure that when talking to Halver, party members do not get the cut scene for the quest Fit for a Prince. Have each member double-check to make sure the cut scene is the correct one.
  • After a cutscene, get a full alliance of level 60+ and head to Western Altepa Desert to G-7 (the small lake on the west side of Revelation Rock).
  • Earth Staff and other equipment that reduces physical damage is effective against 1000 Needles.
  • Clear out the area and when ready, have a member click on the Dreamrose to spawn Sabotender Enamorado.
  • Sabotender Enamorado is level 63 and has about 15000 HP. Known soloable as 74Rdm/Nin
  • Once Sabotender Enamorado is defeated, everyone in the alliance needs to inspect the Dreamrose to receive a key item.
  • Return to the castle, talk to Halver and head to the garden for a cutscene to finish the mission. You should also receive the key item Piece of Paper.
