Cursed Items
Automaton

This Auction House category contains the cursed armor that is needed to create Abjuration Armor. This armor is not equippable in its cursed stage.

To restore the cursed equipment or food to a usable state, you must bring the cursed item along with its corresponding Abjuration to Alphollon C Meriard in Northern San d'Oria (L-6).
