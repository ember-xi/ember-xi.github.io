Notes:

  • Spawns approximately every 72 hours on the island in J-6 of map 3. Does not spawn immediately after maintenance, can spawn 60 hours or less after maintenance.
  • Evoker's Gages are not a 100% drop. Droprate appears to be very low.
  • Uses the avatar Leviathan (Lamia's Avatar) throughout the battle; uses all of Leviathan's BPs, including Grand Fall. Spring Water does not take effect on the NM. Note: Slowga has a wider radius than a Player's avatar.
  • If Lamia's Avatar is killed the Lamie will resummon it 30-60 seconds later. She will resummon Leviathan immediately for Astral Flow.
  • Her avatar's hate is shared with her; however, Leviathan can be separated but will go back to the target of its master over time. If her avatar is on anyone other than the "tank", it's best to just ignore it and try and have the "tank" pull more hate from the NM.
  • Astral Flow can happen multiple times.
  • Its Astral Flow radius is much larger than a Player Character's Astral Flow radius.
  • Trio'd by 3 SMN with good equips with some difficulty.
  • Trio'd by 2 well equipped SMN and a RDM/BRD with some difficulty.
  • Before the NM (Next to the boat) is a cliff, pull the Lamia to the bottom of the cliff while assaulting from the top. Due to the height difference Astral Flow will not hit you.
  • Her avatar is immune to Bind, Gravity, Sleep, Lullaby, and Stun.
  • Immune to Sleep and Lullaby.
  • Susceptible to Paralyze, Slow, and Blind.
  • Before the NM is a boat with 2 Nix Songstresses (True Sight IT mobs) on it. Be careful not to aggro. Usually best to just sac pull and die on the other side.
  • Defeating her lowers the Undead Swarm's military force by 1.
  • Uses the TP moves Pole Swing(Cone Attack), Tail Slap, Hypnotic Sway(Gaze Attack Amnesia), and Belly Dance (AoE charm)
  • Uses Belly Dance more often when under 20% HP.
