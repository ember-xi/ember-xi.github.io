Walkthrough

  • Mashuu-Ajuu is located on top of the Aurastery in Windurst Waters (North).
  • Mashuu-Ajuu wants you help with his horticultural homework assignment and asks that you use Herb Seeds to grow something that will impress his classmates, such as a Sobbing Fungus.
  • He will give you a bag of Herb Seeds.
  • Plant the seeds in a flower pot and in about 3 days real time you have a plant.
  • You may also just buy a Sobbing Fungus or a Deathball from

Mashuu-Ajuu wants help with this horticultural homework assignment. He asks that you use "Herb Seeds" to grow something that will impress his classmates, such as a "Sobbing Fungus."
