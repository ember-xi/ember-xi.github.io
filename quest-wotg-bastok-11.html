Zenith availability

This quest’s dialogue and progression are present in the audited base, but its Bastok storyline prerequisites include unfinished battles. A complete reference route is provided; progression on Zenith needs those earlier battles implemented and tested.

Before you begin

  • Complete B-10: What Price Loyalty.
  • Reach WotG 38: Adieu, Lilisette.
  • Bring one Elixir.

Starting point

Gentle Tiger · Bastok Markets [S] · H-6

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Gentle Tiger at H-6. Enter Castle Zvahl Baileys [S] for a scene before proceeding to Castle Zvahl Keep [S].
  2. On Keep map 1, find Rikke at G-7 (northwest), Rakke at G-10 (southwest), and Rokke at H-10 (southeast). Listen to the statements following their requests for an Elixir.
  3. Choose the truthful Imp from the day’s statements. If Rikke says Rikke is honest, Rakke says Rikke is lying, and Rokke says Rikke is honest, choose Rakke.
  4. If Rikke says Rokke is lying, Rakke says Rokke is honest, and Rokke says Rikke is lying, choose Rikke.
  5. If Rikke says Rokke is lying, Rakke says Rikke is honest, and Rokke says Rikke is lying, choose Rokke.
  6. Trade the Elixir to the correct Imp. The item is not consumed. If wrong, wait for the next Vana’diel day and read the new statements before trying again.
  7. Continue through the Keep’s teleporters to map 3. Examine the ??? in the southwest room at G-9.
  8. Drop to the central room, use the central teleporter, and examine the Displaced Block for the final scene and Sanctus Rosary.

Battle and progress notes

  • The puzzle changes with the Vana’diel day. Do not use yesterday’s answer after midnight; the native script permits one Elixir attempt each game day.
  • Save the Sanctus Rosary for the final reference battle in Bonds of Mythril.
  • The native quest checks the main mission gate and prior quest completion, without an initial extra day wait.

Completion and reward

Sanctus Rosary.

What this opens
