Walkthrough

  • This quest can not be started if the quest Say It with Flowers has been started. In addition, it still can not be started until the right flower(see details on the page of that quest) has been traded and an Iron Sword has been received as a reward.
  • Talk to Kerutoto in the Rhinostery. She will tell you to go talk to Ohbiru-Dohbiru & Kenapa-Keppa in the "higgledy-piggledy laboratory" next door. Speak with the other two Taru then go back to Kerutoto; she will give you her order. Make sure you talk to the two Taru next door again to get their orders. You will need to talk to Kenapa-Keppa a few times before he tells you what he wants.
  • Kerutoto needs a Grilled Hare.
    • You can buy the Grilled Hare from the Cooking Guild.
  • Kenapa-Keppa needs a Hard-Boiled Egg.
    • You can buy the Hard-Boiled Egg from the Cooking Guild.
  • Ohbiru-Dohbiru needs Windurstian Tea, Tortilla, and clump of Pamtam Kelp.
    • You can buy the Tortilla from Timbre Timbers Tavern or the Cooking Guild.
    • You can buy the Windurstian Tea from Timbre Timbers Tavern.
    • You can buy the Pamtam Kelp from the Fishing Guild.

Game Description

Client: Kerutoto (Rhinostery, Windurst Waters)
Summary:

You've been asked to buy food for the two busy researchers and one freshly-awoken meditator. You'll have to ask each of them what they want... if you can understand them, that is.
