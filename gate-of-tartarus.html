Gate of Tartarus

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Staff weapon skill

  • Skill Level: N/A
  • Description: Lowers target's attack. Additional effect: Refresh
  • Available only when equipped with the Relic Weapons Thyrus (Dynamis use only) or Claustrum.
  • These Relic Weapons are only available to Black Mages and Summoners. As such, only these jobs may use this Weapon Skill.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Darkness / Distortion
  • Modifiers: CHR:60%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
