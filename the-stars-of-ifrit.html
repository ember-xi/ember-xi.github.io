Walkthrough

  • If you have an Airship Pass, Agapito, who is outside the air travel agency in Port Bastok, has a riddle for you to solve and asks if you can retrieve the letter he seeks.
  • "Under a full moon, towards the Stars of Ifrit, a bird delivers a letter to the ship in the sky."
  • In order to obtain the letter of which he speaks you must travel on in the right conditions. In this case, it is on the Jeuno - San d'Oria airship when the moon is full (nightime and moon ≥ 90%).
  • Ride an airship between Jeuno and San d'Oria and look on the rail of the ship for a ???.
  • Click the ??? and you will get a key item, "Carrier Pigeon Letter."
  • Return to Agapito with the letter and he is disgusted to find out it is only a love letter. He gives you 2100 gil and the title "Star of Ifrit."

Game Description

Client: Agapito (Near Air Travel Agency, Port Bastok)
Summary:

Agapito wants you to find out the meaning of the following words: "Under a full moon, toward the stars of Ifrit, a bird delivers a letter to the ship in the sky." Also, try to bring back this "letter" to satisfy Agapito's curiosity.
