Statistics

Metal Chip
This metal chip is shaped like
a tiny hexagon. A strange light
emits from its sparkling center.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Other Uses

Used for: Accessing Central Temenos or CS Apollyon

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Merchant:

Price: 75 Ancient Beastcoins
Name Location Type
Sagheera Port Jeuno (I-8) Special Merchant

Chest/Coffer:

  • Possible loot from the final chests in each of the Areas of Apollyon or Temenos.
