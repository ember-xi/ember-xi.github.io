Cover

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Allows you to protect party members by placing yourself between them and the enemy.
  • Obtained: Paladin Level 35
  • Recast Time: 3:00
  • Duration: Random, between 0:15 and 0:30

Notes/Description

  • While active, all physical attacks directed at the target will instead become directed at the user, as long as the covered target stands directly behind the user.
  • Cover reduces the CE by 10% with each hit the Paladin intercepts and it also increases the Paladin's CE by +200.
  • No enmity is transferred upon usage of this ability.
  • If Gallant Coronet is equipped, user will be able to Cover magical attacks directed to the covered target too.
  • If Valor Surcoat is equipped, 20% of the damage taken while covering a target will be converted to MP.
  • If Save the Queen is equipped, user will have Accuracy +20.
  • Merits increase duration by 4 seconds per upgrade.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Cover" <stpc>
