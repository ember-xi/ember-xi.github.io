Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Damages enemies within area of effect with a twofold attack. Damage varies with TP.

Description

Spell cost: 24 MP
Ecosystem: Beasts
Type: Physical (Slashing)
Blue Magic Points: 2
Stat Bonus: VIT+1
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 20

Timers

Casting Time: 1 seconds
Recast Time: 19.75 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Claw Cyclone as part of a combo to create Lizard Killer.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Lizard Killer combo.

  • Foot Kick
  • Ram Charge

Skillchain Element(s): Earth (can open Liquefaction, Detonation, or Reverberation; can close Scission or Distortion)
