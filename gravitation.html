Gravitation

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Gravitation is a Level 2 Skillchain (or Renkei), composed of Darkness and Earth Elements.

Formation

Weapon skills, Blood Pacts, or Blue Magic w/ Chain Affinity with the proper attributes must be used in sequence to create Gravitation:

  • Detonation ---> Compression
  • Fusion ---> Gravitation
