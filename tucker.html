Tucker

From Zenith XI Wiki

Vellum exchange for the Scholar unlock quest.

Find Tucker in Crawlers' Nest (S) (K-8).

  1. Start A Little Knowledge with Erlene first, then speak to Tucker.
  2. For the full unlock requirement, bring 144 Rolanberry (12 stacks). Make three trades of 48 berries (four stacks) to receive four Vellum each time.
  3. Take the resulting 12 Vellum to Erlene.

The reviewed Zenith base allows three exchanges, up to four Vellum each. Trading only one stack in each exchange will leave you short; use the full quantities above.
