Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


File:Heat Breath.JPG

Deals fire damage to enemies within a fan-shaped area originating from the caster.

Description

Spell cost: 169 MP
Ecosystem: Beasts
Type: Magical (Fire)
Blue Magic Points: 4
Stat Bonus: STR+3
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 71

Timers

Casting Time: 7.5 seconds
Recast Time: 49 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Heat Breath as part of a combo to create Magic Attack Bonus.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Magic Attack Bonus combo.

  • Sound Blast
  • Cursed Sphere
  • Eyes On Me
  • Memento Mori
  • Reactor Cool
  • Magic Hammer

Notes

  • The formula for this spell appears to be (Current HP / 2). For example, if you are level 71 and have 1000 HP, your damage would be 500. This is subject to Resists, however. Using it against Lizards or Magic Bursting it will not boost damage; it will only affect resist rates.
  • Is NOT affected by Magic Attack Bonus.
  • Ignores Magic Defense Bonus, but is subject to direct magic resistance (from Proto-Omega for example).

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Liquefaction, Fusion, Light
