Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Additional effect: Vitality Down. Duration of effect varies on TP.

Description

Spell cost: 9 MP
Ecosystem: Plantoids
Type: Physical (Piercing)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: CHR+1, HP+10
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 4

Timers

Casting Time: 0.5 seconds
Recast Time: 7.25 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Wild Oats as part of a combo to create Beast Killer.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Beast Killer combo.

  • Sprout Smack
  • 1000 Needles

Notes

  • Piercing damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: AGI: 30%.
  • Is NOT calculated as a ranged attack.
  • VIT down is about 13~14 VIT down with Blue Magic Skill 222-227
  • VIT down effect decays over time.
  • Additional effect of VIT down is not guaranteed even if the spell connects.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Skillchain Element(s): Light (can open Compression, Reverberation, or Distortion; can close Transfixion)
