Notes

  • Lottery Spawn from the Mousse (they are named Panna Cotta on Eden) around (H-11) to (I-11). Does have a low spawn rate (~5%/PH?) so can take awhile.
  • Drops around 12,000 gil.
  • Has around 10,000 - 11,500 HP.
  • Resistant to Sleep.
  • Susceptible to Bind, Gravity, and Stun.
  • Resistant to melee damage, takes higher damage from magic attacks than normal monsters.
  • Can be defeated by a party of four characters at level 66; Soloable by most jobs at level 75, with some difficulty.
  • Jelly Ring drops a high percentage of the time, but not every time.
