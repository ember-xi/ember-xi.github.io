Zeni

From Zenith XI Wiki

The currency used to buy Zeni Notorious Monster trigger items from Sanraku.

Earn Zeni: short steps

  1. Speak to Ryo and Sanraku in Aht Urhgan Whitegate at E-8. Ask Ryo which fauna Sanraku currently wants photographed.
  2. Visit Zandjarl at F-7 inside The Colosseum. Exchange Imperial currency for Jettons and buy a Soultrapper plus Blank Soul Plate ammunition.
  3. Equip the camera and blank plates. Approach a suitable living monster and use the Soultrapper from your item menu. A successful picture produces a Soul Plate.
  4. Take pictures close to the monster, from the front, with its HP low when safe. Preferred fauna and better pictures increase the reward.
  5. Trade the completed plates to Sanraku. The classic limit is ten plates per Vana’diel day.
  6. Spend the balance on a trigger, then follow Zeni Notorious Monsters for the tier route.

Exact Zeni yields and prices depend on server settings. This guide uses the era photography method; it does not assume later Rhapsodies bonuses.
