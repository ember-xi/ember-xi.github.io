Command/autotarget

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Aliases: None
Usage: /autotarget [subcommand]
Description:

If auto-target is turned on, when
attacked by more than one monster,
auto-attack will switch to the next
enemy after the current one is defeated.

Subcommands:
on Turn auto-target on.
off Turn auto-target off.

Displays your current mode when no
subcommand is specified.

Example:

Notes

Auto-target will only activate on enemies in your field of vision (from 270 to 90 degrees) that are within a certain range.
Enemies that have attacked or are being attacked by your party/alliance members can be selected by auto-target.
