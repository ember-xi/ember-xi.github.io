Notes

  • All have the Sabotender special attacks.
  • Sabotender Campeon casts Cure V, has Auto-Regen, and runs around regularly inside the BCNM.
  • Sabotender Amante Share Hate somewhat strangely with the Campeon
  • After Sabotender Campeon finishes running around the BCNM he will immediately fire 1000 Needles
  • All of the NMs in this BC are highly, if not totally immune to Sleep.
