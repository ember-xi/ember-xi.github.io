Wizard's Attire Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Black mages use their magic with but one desire - destruction with devastating spells. This black mage set is designed for spell casting, but dabbles in increasing durability in times of danger. The dark blue robe and distinctive petasos are well known and feared throughout Vana'diel. For a fee you can store the set at any Armor Storer.
