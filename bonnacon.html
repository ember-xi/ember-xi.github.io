Notes:

  • Lottery spawn from the Buffalo around D-8.
  • Killable by: Solo by a Ninja at 72, Summoner at 70+ (non-taru summoners may need refresh drinks)
  • Killable by: Solo by a Monk 75 with Utsusemi: Ichi/Ni (potions or any strong regen items help but not required, evasion set and food recommended)
  • Duoable by: 58+ BST (recommended 59-60+ so there will not be mischarms very often) Use Esbats, Variable Hares, Uleguerand Tiger, and Glacier Eater (If you use worms, be sure to stand next to it until you are sure hate is on the worm).
