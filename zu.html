Zu

From Zenith XI Wiki

Ordinary level-20–23 Birds in Buburimu Peninsula.

Where and drops

Find them outdoors in Buburimu Peninsula. They are regular spawns, with no trigger item or quest required. They can link with nearby Zu, so pull one away from the group.

  • Drops: Bird Egg and Bird Feather.
  • Steal: Bird Feather.
  • Crystal: Fire; elemental weakness: Ice.
