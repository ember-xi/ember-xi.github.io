The Circle of Time

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is the third Bard Artifact Armor quest - otherwise known as AF3.

  Start NPC:   Mertaire - Lower Jeuno (I-8)
  Requirements:
   Bard 50+
  Items Needed:   None
  Title:   Paragon of Bard Excellence
  Repeatable:   Yes, by erasing AF memory
  Reward:   Choral Justaucorps

  Previous Quest:   The Requiem
  Next Quest:   None

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Mertaire in Lower Jeuno (I-8) who tells you to take the Star Ring obtained from The Requiem to an antique collector.
  • Talk to Imasuke in Port Jeuno (E-6) (near the entrance to Qufim Island). He says he can't read it and that you should bury it in Perennial Snow. The snow in question is located at (I-10) in Xarcabard. Bury it there and wait for the next day (JP Midnight).
  • After digging the ring up, return to Imasuke, who tells you the language is old San d'Orian and that you should find someone that can read it.
  • Talk to Chalvatot in Chateau d'Oraguille.
  • Chalvatot sends you to the Monastic Cavern. Head to (I-8) in the caverns. Clear the room and examine the altar to pop the NM Bugaboo.
  • The Orcish Warlord spawns in the same room and has True Sight.
  • Soloable by a 75 RDM/NIN.
  • You can simply pull the Orcish Warlord to the nearby zone so all other mobs despawn, then kill the Orc. Go back into the altar room and spawn and kill the Bugaboo.
  • After you defeat Bugaboo, check the altar again and go back to talk to Chalvatot to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Imasuke (Qufim Tunnel, Port Jeuno)
Summary:

Solve the riddle of the ring, and find the truth behind it.
