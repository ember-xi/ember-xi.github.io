Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Beastmen tend to be grouped into tiers of 10 levels each, with certain kinds of beastmen within those tiers.

Tier 8 is for beastmen whose levels range from 71 to 80. These are mostly seen in the Near East; some are Notorious Monsters. Gold Beastcoins can be stolen from the majority of these beastmen.
