Walkthrough

  • You must wait approximately 8 game days before Rycharde will give you this quest.
  • Rycharde will ask you to bring a piece of Dhalmel Meat and a Beehive Chip.
  • Dhalmel Meat drops from Dhalmels. Beehive Chips drop from Killer Bees in Tahrongi Canyon.
  • If you bring Rycharde the items after three game days he says he just got someone else to do it for him and will only reward you with 1000g and the old title of Purveyor In Training, rather than the full 1500g and the new title.

Game Description

Client: Rycharde (Sailors' Stay, Mhaura)
Summary:

Rycharde needs your help finding some ingredients. He needs a beehive chip and some dhalmel meat.
