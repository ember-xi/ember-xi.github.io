Makes pacts with Avatars: Carbuncle, then the six, then Fenrir. The avatar fights for you with Blood Pacts. Support, damage and healing.

EmberXI changes

  • Whispering Wind is available from level 38 and also removes status ailments (Blind, Poison, Paralysis, Disease, Petrification, Sleep, Silence, Slow).
  • Spring Water gives an area Refresh of 2 MP per tick.
  • Crimson Howl lasts 3 minutes.
  • Ecliptic Howl is unlocked at 43 and Ecliptic Growl at 54 (the two swapped levels).
  • Shining Ruby can overwrite itself.
  • Hastega matches the White Magic spell Haste, so the two overwrite each other.
  • The six level-75 Blood Pacts are learned from the Prime fights — see Prime Pacts.
  • Kukulcan's Staff can be equipped at level 40, with MP +30 and MP recovered while healing +3 — see Artifact Weapon.
  • The avatar quests need no Fame, allow Trusts, the NPC teleports you to the Protocrystal, and each fight repeats once a day — see Avatars.

Prime Pacts (level 75)

On EmberXI the big Blood Pacts are not merits. You learn each one by winning the avatar's Prime fight (the level 65+ fight that gives the Whisper) while you are SMN 75 and already have that avatar. The message === The Prime Pact === appears and the pact is yours. See Avatars.

Blood PactAvatarTypePrime fight
Meteor StrikeIfritFireTrial by Fire — Cloister of Flames
Heavenly StrikeShivaIceTrial by Ice — Cloister of Frost
Wind BladeGarudaWindTrial by Wind — Cloister of Gales
GeocrushTitanEarth (Stun)Trial by Earth — Cloister of Tremors
ThunderstormRamuhLightningTrial by Lightning — Cloister of Storms
Grand FallLeviathanWaterTrial by Water — Cloister of Tides
Mechanics: Avatar melee hits are slashing damage. Resting MP is 12 then +4 per tick, with +2 extra for SMN at 75. Magic Burst gets +35 Magic Accuracy and +35 Magic Attack on normal monsters (not NMs).
