Elemental Seal

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Enhances the accuracy of the user's next spell
  • Obtained: Black Mage Level 15
  • Recast Time: 10:00
  • Duration: 1 Spell or 60 seconds, whichever occurs first

Notes/Description

  • When activated, greatly increases the chance that user's next spell will not be resisted.
  • Very useful for landing emergency spells such as Sleep or Silence.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Elemental Seal" <me>
