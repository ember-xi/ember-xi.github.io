Hollow of Nine Teeth

Hollow of Nine Teeth
WhereGhoyu's Reverie — instance 12901, from Halvard
TypeEmberXI dungeon #1
LayoutThree sections sealed by maze doors; each door opens when the section is dead. Final boss behind the last seal.
RewardsHidden Coffer (gold): 5,000 gil + 3 Alexandrite · Ninetooth Coffer (blue): 20,000 gil + 5 Alexandrite on the first clear (3 after) + 50% chance of a missing Homam / Nashira piece
TrustsAllowed
DeathNo XP loss inside

The first EmberXI dungeon. Three sealed sections under Ghoyu's Reverie; kill every pack and boss in a section and its door opens. Ninetooth waits behind the last seal.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Halvard in Lower Jeuno → The gates → Nine Teeth. You arrive inside; a moogle greets you.
  2. Section 1: kill every pack (touch one and the whole pack comes) and its boss. The maze door opens.
  3. Section 2: the same. The door opens.
  4. Section 3: kill the packs; the last seal opens and Ninetooth waits behind it.
  5. Kill Ninetooth. The blue Ninetooth Coffer opens at the far wall; the Hidden Coffer (gold) can be found along the way.
  6. Talk to Kupitto to leave.
