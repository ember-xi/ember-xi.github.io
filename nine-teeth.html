Hollow of Nine Teeth

Hollow of Nine Teeth
WhereGhoyu's Reverie — instance from Halvard
TypeEmberXI dungeon #1
MonstersGrimjaw · Hollow Burrower · Hollow Eft · Hollow Stinger · Hollow Weaver · Pale Brood · Silkrot Spinner · Silkrot Matron · Ninetooth Hatchling · Ninetooth
NPCsKupitto · Hidden Coffer · Ninetooth Coffer
TrustsAllowed
DeathNo XP loss inside

The first EmberXI dungeon: a hollow under Ghoyu's Reverie ruled by Ninetooth. Fight through its brood to the coffers at the end.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Halvard in Lower Jeuno → The gates → Nine Teeth. You arrive inside the instance; Kupitto greets you.
  2. Clear the hollow: Burrowers, Efts, Stingers and Weavers first, then the Silkrot Spinners and their Matron.
  3. Grimjaw and the Ninetooth Hatchlings guard the deep chamber.
  4. Ninetooth is the boss. Kill it and open the Ninetooth Coffer. A Hidden Coffer may also appear.
  5. Talk to Kupitto to leave.
Mechanics: Full details of drops and coffer contents will be added from the server database.
