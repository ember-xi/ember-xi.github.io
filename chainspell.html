Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Allows rapid spellcasting.
  • Obtained: Red Mage Level 1
  • Recast Time: 2:00:00
  • Duration: 0:01:00

Notes/Description

  • Chainspell is a Red Mage's two hour special.
  • While active, all spells cast will have zero cast time other than the casting animation.
  • All spells cast while this ability is active will have their recast timers reduced to 0:00.
  • The activation of this ability does not cause any spells already recharging to immediately become available.
  • Commonly used to power out an emergency Warp or Escape.
  • Commonly used with Stun against dangerous Notorious Monsters.
  • Very effective when combined with proper timing of the ability Convert.
  • While active, spells can be interrupted.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja Chainspell <me>
  • Since Chainspell is a two hour special, some people prefer not to make a macro for it to prevent unintentional activation.
