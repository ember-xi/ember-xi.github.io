Before you begin

  • Finish your current nation’s Mission 5-2 and reach Rank 6.
  • Start Rise of the Zilart in Norg. Prepare access to Kazham, the jungles and the temple routes as each mission requires.
  • Complete RoZ in story order. Owning a teleport option or a map does not replace mission progress.

The final entry steps

  1. Obtain the Cerulean Crystal key item from Maryoh Comyujah during The Mithra and the Crystal. Reach the Hall of the Gods through Ro’Maeve.
  2. Examine the Cermet Gate and proceed to the Shimmering Circle. Finish the Circle’s scene to complete The Mithra and the Crystal and advance to The Gate of the Gods.
  3. Use the Shimmering Circle again as directed, proceed through the opened route and enter Ru’Aun Gardens. Finish its arrival scene.
  4. Confirm that you are in Ru’Aun Gardens and Ark Angels is now the active RoZ mission. You have reached Sky; defeating the Ark Angels is a later story objective.

Returning with Zenith Atlas

Atlas’s Sky camp route checks the required RoZ progress and a previous Ru’Aun Gardens visit. Make the first story entry normally; Atlas does not award the Cerulean Crystal or finish a mission. See Camp Travel Rules.
