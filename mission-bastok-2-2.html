Before you begin

Be allied with Bastok, have Rank 2, and finish the preceding required story steps. Raise Rank Points if the mission is not offered.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission and obtain one Lizard Egg, from a lizard drop or an available seller.
  2. Trade the egg to Alois in Metalworks (J-8).

Important notes

  • Optional for rank progression. The egg need not come specifically from Dangruf Wadi.
