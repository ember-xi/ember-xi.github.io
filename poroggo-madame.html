Notes:

  • Various spawn conditions depending on the location in Zhayolm Remnants.
    • Uses all standard and NM Poroggo abilities including:
      • Ancient Magic and Tier III -aga spells.
      • Providence followed immediately by an AoE enfeebling spell (Breakga, Bindga, Blindga, etc.) or single-target Death.
      • AoE Frog Chorus (Charm + Frog Transformation) on nearby alliance members (long range, at least 25')
    • First Floor
      • Spawned by reaching the sub-boss(Poroggo Gent?) without the use of any cells.Verification Needed
    • Second Floor
      • Spawned by trading 1 or more cells to the Socket.
      • Drops 2x as many cells as were used to spawn it.
    • Third Floor (North)
      • Spawned by clearing all Mamool Ja on the East/North path, then killing the Archaic Rampart
      • Both drops are 100%.
    • Third Floor (South)
      • Spawned by clearing all Mamool Ja on the West/South path, then killing the Archaic Rampart
    • Fourth Floor (North)
      • Spawned by reaching the floor in under 30 minutes.
      • All three drops are 100%.
    • Fourth Floor (South)
      • Spawned by reaching the floor in under 47 minutes.
    • Fifth Floor (North)
      • Spawned by completely clearing pathos off of three players.
      • Drops are random and rare (can have 0 drops or more than 1 drop).
    • Fifth Floor (South)
      • Spawned by completely clearing pathos off of one player.
    • Sixth Floor
      • Spawned by defeating four previous NMs (either Jakko + 3 Poroggo Madames, or 4 Poroggo Madames).
      • Drops are random and rare (can have 0 drops or more than 1 drop).
