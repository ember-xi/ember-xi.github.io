Notes:

  • Spawns at I-7 approximately every 9-12 hours. Requires that the Overgrown Rose there not be killed or aggroed usually for about 10 hours and then Overgrown Rose will despawn and quickly morph into the bigger Rose Garden. Any aggro or fighting of Overgrown Rose will reset the timer.
  • If Rose Garden is left up without being killed long enough, Voluptuous Vilma (retail link) will appear. That NM has been reported to have better odds of dropping Vilma's Ring.
  • Reported approximately 5% drop rate on Vilma's Ring.
  • Plantoid Auto Regen reported as ~1% of HP per tick during daytime regardless of being in open sunlight or brought down into a tunnel.
  • Prone to Paralyze.
  • Soloable by most jobs around level 65-70.
