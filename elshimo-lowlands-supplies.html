Elshimo Lowlands Supplies

From Zenith XI Wiki

Temporary supplies for the Yuhtunga Jungle outpost run.

Get and deliver the supplies

  1. While your nation controls Elshimo Lowlands, ask one of its city Conquest guards for a supply mission and choose Elshimo Lowlands.
  2. Travel to the outpost in Yuhtunga Jungle (G-11).
  3. Speak with your nation’s outpost overseer before the next weekly Conquest tally. The key item is delivered through conversation.

Completing the run earns Conquest Points and records the regional outpost teleport unlock; the teleport’s level requirement still applies. See Supply Run Mission.
