Before you begin

  • Complete CoP 6-4: One to be Feared.

Starting point

Arrival scene · Lufaise Meadows

Walkthrough

  1. Finish the Lufaise Meadows arrival scene and receive the Ducal Guard's Ring. If your inventory was full, free a slot and check the nearby ??? to collect the stashed ring.
  2. Enter Tavnazian Safehold for a scene.
  3. Visit Sealion's Den for another scene.
  4. Back in the Safehold, examine the basement Sewer Entrance (I-7) and the upper-floor Walnut Door (K-7). All three companion scenes are required.

Completion and reward

Progress to the next Chains of Promathia mission.
