Walkthrough

  • After your NPC is level 51, talk to Luto Mewrilah in Upper Jeuno (G-8) who instructs you to the hospital and speak with Monberaux (unequip your signal pearl before speaking with Monberaux).
  • Go back and speak with Luto Mewrilah, with your signal pearl unequipped. After the cutscene, you find out your friend has gone to Qufim Island.
  • Go to Qufim Island (E-6) and inspect the Giant Footprint for a cutscene and to spawn your NPC buddy and the NM Gigas named Ingaevon (level 53-55).
  • Once you have defeated the NM, speak with your NPC before doing /anything/ else or you can glitch this level cap quest in a way GMs are not able to reset. After your NPC disappears, examine the Giant Footprints and return to Luto Mewrilah for a cutscene.
  • Speak with Monberaux again to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Luto Mewrilah (Artisan Bridge, Upper Jeuno)
Summary:

Your adventuring fellow is in the hospital after being attacked by a monster in Qufim. You should go make sure your friend is not too badly hurt.
