Zircon Earring

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-49 earring for all jobs: Ice resistance +6 and Wind resistance +6.

Contents
  1. Craft it
  2. Purchase
  3. Sources

Craft it

Goldsmithing 65: Earth Crystal + 1 Gold Earring + 1 Zircon. The standard result is Zircon Earring; high-quality outcomes are Genius Earring or Genius Earring +1.

Purchase

Auction House → Armor → Earrings. A second recipe uses Gold Earring +1 at Goldsmithing 70.
