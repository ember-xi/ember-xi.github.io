Before you start

SCH 50+; Downward Helix complete; choose Gown from Loussaire.

Steps

  1. Search the Peiste area of Pashhow Marshlands (S) for Indescript Markings: (K-8), (K-9), (J-9), (K-10), (J-10), (I-10) or (H-10). Collect Peiste Dung; the active spot moves.
  2. At the stone pillars in Grauberg (S) (D-8), check Indescript Markings for a Sample of Grauberg Chert. Inspect around the pillars; the marker relocates after use.
  3. Return to Loussaire on Scholar with a free slot. Checking a marker removes Sneak. Retail reports disagree about rain/wind restrictions; the reviewed collection handler does not test weather.

Reward

Scholar's Gown (SCH 58).
