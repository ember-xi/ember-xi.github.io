Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Sabotenders, animate and conscious cacti native to the Altepa Desert, are well-known in adventurer circles for their merciless and fierce nature, especially when attacked. Their infamous ability, "1000 Needles," fires one-thousand of their incredible sharp needles, hitting everything within range; this ability has caused many a traveler through the desert no small amount of pain.

General Information

  • System: Plantoids
  • Weaknesses: Dark, Ice, Water
  • Immune: Terror
  • Traits: Resist Charm

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
1000 Needles: - 1,000 damage divided amongst all applicable targets (Party/Alliance of the target and their pets, fellowship NPCs, other players with hate, etc.) ignoring defense, but is absorbed or reduced by Stoneskin, Phalanx, and damage reduction gear.
Photosynthesis - Regen effect (Can only be used during daytime)
Needleshot - Single target physical attack
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
