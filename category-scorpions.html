General Information

  • System: Vermin
  • Weaknesses: Light, Ice
  • Immune: Innate resistance to Poison

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Death Scissors: Very high damage, single target
Mandible Bite: Single target damage
Poison Sting: Single target damage and Poison
Cold Breath: Cone Attack Ice damage and Bind
Numbing Breath: Cone Attack Damage and Paralysis
Earth Pounder: 15' AoE Dexterity Down, earth damage, wipes Utsusemi
Wild Rage: 15' AoE physical damage, absorbed by Utsusemi
Sharp Strike: 50% Attack Boost. 4 minute 15 second duration
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
