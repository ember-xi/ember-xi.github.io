Hot Shot

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Marksmanship weapon skill

  • Skill level: 5
  • Deals fire elemental damage to enemy.

Properties

  • Element: Fire
  • Skillchain properties: Liquefaction/Transfixion
  • Modifiers: AGI:30%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
0.50 0.75? 1.00

Levels

  • Hot Shot is only natively available to Rangers and Corsairs. While other jobs can obtain the required 5 Marksmanship skill, they must use one of the aforementioned jobs as their support job in order to gain access to Hot Shot.
  • Marksmanship skill level 5 is obtainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
