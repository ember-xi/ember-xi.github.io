Attacks:

  • Wire Cutter: Single Target, two hit physical damage.
  • Armor Buster: AoE target heavy magical damage.
  • Citadel Buster: AoE massive magical damage. Suggested that all alliance members except for the person with hate leave the room to avoid taking damage. Magic damage reduction gear significantly reduces the damage taken. A Blue Mage using Diffusion + Saline Coat, will also significantly reduce the damage taken. Only used when Proto-Ultima has less then 25% hp remaining. The Corsair Magus's Roll is also very good for this same reason. Stacking Saline Coat, Magus's Roll, Shell V, and Magic Defense Bonus gear will almost reduce all damage from Citadel Buster.
  • Dissipation: AoE Dispel used at 79% 59% 39% 19%. Important to Stun because it removes every buff from the people in range.
  • Antimatter: Single target light based magic damage.
  • Holy II: Single Target light based magic damage.
  • Equalizer: Heavy AoE physical damage. Does 600+ to normal melee. Possibly only used when hate is taken from behind.
  • Chemical Bomb: Frontal cone AoE Elegy & Slow II effects.
  • Nuclear Waste: AoE elemental resist reduction (-50).
  • Will be followed by one of the following attacks:
  • Cryojet: Frontal cone AoE Ice based magic damage with additional paralysis effect.
  • High-Tension Discharge: Frontal cone AoE thunder based magic damage with an additional stun effect.
  • Flame Thrower: Frontal cone AoE Fire based magic damage with additional plague effect.
  • Smoke Discharger: Frontal cone AoE Earth based magic damage with additional petrification effect.
  • Hydro Canon: Frontal cone AoE Water based magic damage with additional poison effect.
  • Turbofan: Frontal cone AoE Wind based magic damage with additional silence effect.


Notes:

  • Floor unlocked by trading a Cerulean Chip, Orchid Chip, and Silver Chip to the Matter Diffusion Module inside Temenos.
  • See Central Temenos - 4th Floor for battle strategy.
