Ranged Attack

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Determines the damage done by a Ranged Attack. Similar to attack, every 2 STR will give 1 Ranged Attack.

Formula:

Ranged Attack = {( 8 + Combat Skill + STR/2 + (+attack from gear) + (+attack trait/ability/minuet)) * berserk effect (if available)} * (1+ percentage increase from food )
