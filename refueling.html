Description

Type: Magical (Wind)

Stat Bonus: AGI+2
Jobs:

Timers

Casting Time: 1.5 seconds
Recast Time: 30 seconds
Duration: 5 minutes

Notes

  • Is 10% haste over a period of five minutes, as opposed to 15% haste over three minutes of the traditional Haste spell.
  • Overwritten by, and cannot overwrite Haste.
  • Overwrites, and cannot be overwritten by Hastega.
