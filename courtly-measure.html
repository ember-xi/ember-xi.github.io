Courtly Measure

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This effect is possible by equipping the Bard's Cannions or Bard's Cannions +1. It is activated when you do not have your Weapon drawn; and it grants the following:
STR -8, DEX -8, VIT -8, AGI -8, INT +8, MND +8, CHR +8

Note: While wearing Bard's Cannions +1, the negative effects of STR, DEX, VIT and AGI are not attributed to the character.
