  • Pet summoned by Jailer of Love. It summons them in groups of three.
  • While all other summoned pets of Jailer of Love have the Qn prefix, Ru'phuabo is the only one with the Ru prefix. Presumably, this is some indicator of a difference in difficulty. The Qn'phuabo has existed in the Al'Taieu .dat files since July 2005, but has not been activated in the game.
