Title: San d'Orian Royal Heir

Mission 9-2 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 9-1 Breaking Barriers.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Halver, then Perfaumand, Nachou and Arnau (cutscenes in the chateau and the cathedral).
  2. Enter King Ranperre's Tomb for a cutscene.
  3. Go to Fei'Yin → Qu'Bia Arena for the final Battlefield.
  4. Return to Halver for Rank 10 and the flag.
Mechanics: The last nation mission. Level 75 Battlefield with five Trusts. Rank 10 opens the Mog House in the other nations.
