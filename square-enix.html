Square Enix

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

English: Square Enix Japanese: スクウェア・エニックス (sukuwea enikkusu)

Square Enix (commonly abbreviated as SE) is a video game company best known for its role-playing games, one series which is known as "Final Fantasy".

The name came about because two companies "Square Co." Ltd and "Enix" merged on April 1, 2003. Enix officially took over Square and thus the name Square Enix. Yōichi Wada is the president of Square Enix.
