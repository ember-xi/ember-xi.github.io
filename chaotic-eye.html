Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Silences an enemy.

Description

Spell cost: 13 MP
Ecosystem: Beasts
Type: Magical (Wind)
Blue Magic Points: 2
Stat Bonus: AGI+1
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 32

Timers

Casting Time: 3 seconds
Recast Time: 10 seconds

Notes

  • This spell is a Gaze Attack. The target must be facing you for the spell to take effect.

Combines With...

You can use Chaotic Eye as part of a combo to create Conserve MP.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Conserve MP combo.

  • Zephyr Mantle
  • Frost Breath
  • Firespit

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Detonation, Fragmentation, and Light.

How to Obtain

  • Face away from the monster to prevent the silencing effect (you will still learn the spell).

Acquired From
