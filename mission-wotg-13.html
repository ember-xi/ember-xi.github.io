Before you begin

  • Complete Affairs of State.
  • Carry Count Borel's letter awarded by Affairs of State.

Starting point

Bulwark Gate · Sauromugue Champaign [S] · F-6

Walkthrough

  1. Examine the Bulwark Gate at (F-6), on the approach to Jeuno, with Count Borel's letter.
  2. Finish the long scene to receive the Underpass hatch key. Your next destination is Batallia Downs [S].

Completion and reward

Underpass hatch key.
