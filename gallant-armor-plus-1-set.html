Gallant Armor +1 Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This flexible metal armor set has unique designs of white colors and heavy plating to protect the Holy Knight from harm while causing enemies to hate this walking wall of a person. Providing excellent defense, extra magic and shield skill, adventurers and civilians alike should feel safe with a knight in this set around. For the Normal version, see Gallant Armor Set.
