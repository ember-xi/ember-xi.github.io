Triple Attack

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Occasionally attacks three times.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Occasionally causes character to attack three times in a single round.
    • If two weapons are Dual Wielded, this trait may activate on both attacks separately.
    • When a Hand-to-Hand weapon is equipped, this trait may activate on both punches separately.
    • May activate during weapon skills.
      • On multi-hit weapon skills, Triple Attack can occur twiceVerification Needed.
    • Base Triple Attack rate from Thief trait is 5%Verification Needed.
    • The exact formulae for calculating the effects of this trait are still unknown.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Triple Dagger
  • Assassin's Poulaines
  • Homam Corazza
  • Algol
  • Indra Katars
