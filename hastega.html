Hastega

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Hastega Hastes all allies in the area of effect.

Other Information

Note
The spell "Hastega" does not exist for adventurers to learn, except as a Blood Pact from Garuda, or by Blue Mages using Refueling with Diffusion, though many monsters are able to cast it on themselves.
