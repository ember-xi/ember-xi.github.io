Walkthrough

  1. Start Magicite and speak to Aldo for the Silver Bell.
  2. Speak to Paya-Sabya in Upper Jeuno (I-8) for the garden scene.
  3. Speak to Muckvix in his Lower Jeuno shop (H-10). With the garden scene complete, he grants the Yagudo Torch. This is a Magicite substep, not a separate accepted quest in your quest log.
  4. Follow the Castle Oztroja route on the Magicite page. Light the torch by the sealed door and enter the Altar Room; finish its scene and examine Magicite.
