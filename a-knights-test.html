A Squire's Test II—

Walkthrough

  • Speak with Balasiel once more. He reads you a riddle from the key item Book of Tasks that points to the guards of Victory Square.
  • Speak with Baunise in Southern San d'Oria (H-9), on the parapet over Westgate, for the key item Book of the West.
    • Enter the parapet at (F-8).
  • Speak with Cahaurme in Southern San d'Oria (J-9), on the parapet over Eastgate, for the key item Book of the East.
    • The books send you to Davoi for an item to give Balasiel.
  • Travel to Davoi. Bring Sneak and Invisible: Sneak for the water monsters, Invisible for the Orcs. Some Orcs aggro even at level 75.
  • Examine the Disused Well in Davoi (E-10) for the key item Knight's Soul.
    • Fastest route: drop into the water from the broken bridge at (I-8) and follow the river to (D-8).
    • Or head south from the entrance along the path at (J-8), through the camp to the path at (K-10), southwest to the water, follow it west until it bends north, then take the path south at (D-8).
    • Move southeast through that camp and south along the path to the well at (E-10). A NM (Blubbery Bulge) may stand near the well; usually it is only a Davoi Mush.
  • Return to Balasiel for your reward and the ability to become a Paladin.
