How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Attohwa Chasm

  • Talk to Jakaka in Attohwa Chasm (G-10). She will give you a Flaxen Pouch.
  • Climb the mountain of Parradamo Tor in Attohwa Chasm, and trade the pouch to the Cradle of Rebirth at the top (J-9), to receive the Parradamo Stones.
  • Return to Jakaka and trade him the Parradamo Stones to receive the Miasma Filter key item.
