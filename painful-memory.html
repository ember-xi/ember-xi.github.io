Painful Memory

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is the first Bard Artifact Armor quest - otherwise known as AF1.

  Start NPC:   Mertaire - Lower Jeuno (I-8)
  Requirements:
   Bard 40+
  Items Needed:   None
  Title:   None
  Repeatable:   Yes, by erasing AF memory
  Reward:   Paper Knife

  Previous Quest:   Path of the Bard
  Next Quest:   The Requiem

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Mertaire to activate the quest and receive Mertaire's Bracelet (key item).
  • Head to Ranguemont Pass and proceed into the room with the large stone door and the guards.
  • Go to E-5 in the north-western corner of the map.
  • Click on the "Waters of Oblivion" to spawn Tros.
  • Kill Tros and click again on the "Waters of Oblivion" for a cutscene to complete the quest.
  • If you have a higher level job, you can solo this NM easily. Just start with the quest with your Bard, then change to the high level job for the fight.
  • Your AF dagger will be given to you upon the completion of the cutscene.

Game Description

Client: Mertaire (Merry Minstrel Meadhouse, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

Take Mertaire's bracelet to Ranguemont Pass and immerse it in the Waters of Oblivion.
