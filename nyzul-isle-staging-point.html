Nyzul Isle Staging Point

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Nyzul Isle Staging Point is a Staging Point located within the Alzadaal Undersea Ruins. Maps of this and all other undersea areas can be gained by trading 3 Imperial Mythril Pieces to any of the Immortal Guards posted at each entrance to the Ruins.

Directions

To get there from Aht Urhgan Whitegate:

  • Having the maps for Alzadaal Undersea Ruins helps greatly. See above for details.
  • Get an Imperial Silver Piece either from the Auction House or Ugrihd (for 100 Imperial Standing).
  • Exit Whitegate via the tunnel at (H-7), or get a chocobo from Al Zahbi, to Bhaflau Thickets. Not Mamool Ja Staging Point.
  • Travel to (F-6) in Bhaflau Thickets. Note: You will need Sneak and Invisible if travelling on foot.
  • Trade the Imperial Silver Piece to Kamih Mapokhalam.
  • Once in Alzadaal Undersea Ruins continue until you see two teleporters.
  • Take the left teleporter. You will come to an area with two more teleporters, so take the left again (The brighter of the two).
  • On this much larger map, travel to (J-8) or (J-9) (either entrance is fine). There are Qiqirn along the way, but they do not aggro.
  • You will come to the Staging Point door. Enter and use the Runic Portal to return to Aht Urhgan Whitegate.

Connecting Areas

  • Caedarva Mire - Hediva Isle - Khimaira is found here.
  • Caedarva Mire - various places.
  • Bhaflau Thickets - Tandjana Islet.
  • Mount Zhayolm
