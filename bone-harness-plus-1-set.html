Bone Harness Plus 1 Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is the first full armor set with evasion bonuses on every piece. It is state-of-the-art for any tank as well as damage dealers who would like to avoid occasional attacks. The pieces are pricey for their level, so some players will enjoy a combination of NQ and HQ pieces of the bone armor set. This is the high quality version of the Bone Harness Set.
