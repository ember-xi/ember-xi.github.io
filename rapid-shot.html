Rapid Shot

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Occasionally reduces ranged attack delay.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Occasionally causes a ranged attack to fire instantly.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Hunter's Beret
  • Beater's Mantle
  • Scout's Jerkin
  • Mercenary's Dastanas

Rapid Shot

  • Obtained: Ranger Level 15
  • Obtained: Corsair Level 15
  • Possible: Blue Mage Level 38
