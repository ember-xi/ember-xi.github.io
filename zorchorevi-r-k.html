Zorchorevi, R.K.

From Zenith XI Wiki

San d’Oria’s Conquest overseer at the Elshimo Lowlands outpost.

Location

Yuhtunga Jungle — G-11.

Appears while San d’Oria controls Elshimo Lowlands. San d’Orian adventurers can obtain Signet, deliver Elshimo Lowlands Supplies for a Supply Run Mission, and use the guard’s applicable Conquest services. He also accepts Sheep Leather Missive to start the local Garrison.
