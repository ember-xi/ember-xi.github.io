Home Points

From Zenith XI Wiki

City crystals are available early; exploration unlocks ordinary destinations beyond the cities.

Unlock rules

  • 48 Home Points across San d’Oria, Bastok, Windurst and Jeuno are available from the start.
  • Other ordinary areas register on the first visit; recorded earlier visits count for existing characters.
  • The supported set contains 109 points through Wings of the Goddess. Adoulin points and the unimplemented Al Zahbi point are excluded.
  • Story-gated destinations still require the appropriate story progress and visits.

Story access

See Travel Access Requirements for expansion and story restrictions. Previously registered but ineligible story points are hidden without deleting their registration.
