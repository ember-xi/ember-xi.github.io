Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Improves power of magic spells.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
  • Involved in Calculating Magic Damage, specifically as part of the (100 + Magic Attack Bonus)/(100 + Magic Defense Bonus) ratio.
    • The job trait is available to certain jobs and the value of the trait stacks additively with Magic Attack Bonus gained from equipment.
    • This statistic affects all forms of magic damage, including spells, Quick Draw, and elemental weapon skill
      • See Calculating Magic Damage for the exact calculations.
    • Repeats of this trait do not stack. For example, a Level 20 RDM/BLM will not receive two Magic Attack Bonus I traits.
    • Despite the apparent pattern, RDMs do not gain Magic Attack Bonus III at level 60.
