Statistics

Mana Powder
This powder is infused with magical power. When it becomes airborne, it has the ability to restore the MP of anyone within a certain radius.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Effect: Restores 25% of MP to those within a 10-foot radius within party. (Ballista within own team)

Other Uses

Used in Quests: None

NPC Sell Price: ???~??? gil

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Ballista

  • Quarry

Salvage

  • Found in Armoury Crates that appear throughout Salvage.

Besieged

  • Given at the start of Besieged.
