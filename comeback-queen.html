Comeback Queen

From Zenith XI Wiki

Win the dance performance to receive Dancer's Casaque.

Contents
  1. Requirements
  2. Steps
  3. Sources

Requirements

Dancer level 50+; The Road to Divadom completed; change zones and wait for the next Vana’diel day.

Steps

  1. Speak to Laila in Upper Jeuno (G-7) as Dancer to obtain Wyatt’s Proposal.
  2. Take the proposal to Harmodios inside the music shop in Bastok Markets (K-10).
  3. Return to Laila, then talk to Rhea Myuliah to practice the audience minigame.
  4. After the next Vana’diel day, speak to Laila for the real performance. Choose dances promptly: children favor Jigs, young adults favor Waltzes, older adults favor Sambas.
  5. Keep at least four of seven spectators watching your troupe. Alternating Jigs and Waltzes targets the five children/young adults. Continue responding until the scene ends.
  6. Win to receive Dancer's Casaque. If you lose, zone and wait until the next Vana’diel day to retry.

Zenith availability: this is the original quest route. Completion on the current Zenith server has not been confirmed; a wiki entry does not install the quest.
