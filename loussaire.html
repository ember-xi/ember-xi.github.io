Loussaire

From Zenith XI Wiki

Scholar AF commissions in Bastok Markets (S).

Location: Bastok Markets (S) (G-10). Complete Downward Helix and speak on SCH 50+. Choose one piece, collect its two key items, and return on Scholar with inventory space.

PieceRequired key itemsGuide
Scholar's LoafersRafflesia Dreamspit + Drogarogan BonemealScholar's Loafers Quest
Scholar's PantsSlug Mucus + Djinn EmberScholar's Pants Quest
Scholar's GownPeiste Dung + Sample of Grauberg ChertScholar's Gown Quest

These are commissions rather than ordinary quest-log entries. Start the selected piece before searching for its markings. Historical retail guides require a zone change and a new game day between pieces; the reviewed Loussaire handler does not enforce that delay. Speak again after collection.
