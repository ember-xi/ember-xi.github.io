Home Point

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Large, glowing Crystals thoughout Vana'diel can be used to set the place where a character returns to upon death. These Home Point crystals do not move.

Additional ways to set your Home Point include 1) talking to military commanders at outposts.

As well as choosing to go there, after death, you can return to your Home Point by using some form of Warp, including the following:

  • Casting Warp or Warp II on yourself.
  • Having a Black Mage party member cast Warp II on you.
  • Using a Scroll of Instant Warp.
  • Using an Enchanted Item, such as a Warp Cudgel.

See also Blood Warp.
