Mercenary

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


A Mercenary is a character who has joined "Salaheem's Sentinels" by starting Aht Urhgan Mission 3: President Salaheem. Each Mercenary carries a Wildcat Badge Key Item to identify the character's Mercenary Rank.
