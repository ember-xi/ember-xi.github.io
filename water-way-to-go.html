Walkthrough

  • (If you've just completed the previous quest, Overnight Delivery, you need to zone before starting this quest)
  • Ohbiru-Dohbiru in the Rhinostery asks you to go to Giddeus and get him some water from the spring there.
  • Take the Rhinostery Canteen he gives you to Giddeus to spring (K-12) or (E-12) and fill it up for a Canteen of Giddeus Water and bring it back for your reward.

Game Description

Client: Ohbiru-Dohbiru (Rhinostery, Windurst Waters)
Summary:

Ohbiru-Dohburu is running out of the Giddeus water needed to raise Fluoro-flora. He wants you to take a Rhinostery canteen to the springs at the back off Giddeus in order to refill his supply.
