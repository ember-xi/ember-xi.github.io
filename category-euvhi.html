General Information

  • System: Lumorians
  • Weaknesses:
  • Immune:
  • Notes: Euvhi ("flowers") are found in two states, open and closed. They detect by true sound, at a longer range than normal sound aggro.
  • When closed, they wander around. They do not aggro, but they do link.
  • When open, they remain in one place. They aggro, but do not link. They hit harder as in closed state.
  • Open and Closed Euvhi both do not change between states when left alone, however, they will change states during combat, every minute or so.
