Rendezvous Point

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Used to talk to your adventuring fellow.

  1. Ru'Lude Gardens: G-10
  2. Windurst Waters (North): K-10/11
  3. Northern San d'Oria: L-10
  4. Port Bastok: J-13

Note also that there are Rendezvous Points in Promyvion. You find them as you are transported into the battle area, set on the ground close to the exit transporter.
