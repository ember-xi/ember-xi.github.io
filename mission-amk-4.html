Zenith availability

The route has been checked against references and available code; completion on Zenith has not yet been play-tested.

Before you begin

  • Complete AMK 3.
  • Mission must be active before gathering.
  • Pickaxes, Hatchets and Sickles; spare tools recommended.

Starting point

Inconspicuous Door · Upper Jeuno · H-8

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

Walkthrough

  1. Bring spare Pickaxes, Hatchets and Sickles. With this mission active, mine in an original or Zilart area until you obtain the Sturdy metal strip key item.
  2. Log in an original or Zilart area for the Piece of rugged tree bark; avoid Buburimu Peninsula for this objective. Harvest in an original or Zilart area for the Savory lamb roast key item.
  3. You may collect all three before returning. Examine the Upper Jeuno H-8 door repeatedly to deliver the strip, bark and roast in that order, watching each scene.
  4. These are gathering key items, including the roast; buying ordinary food does not fulfill the request.

Battle and progress notes

  • The items are random gathering results; several attempts or broken tools may be needed.

Completion and reward

Story progression.
