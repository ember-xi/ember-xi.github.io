Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Circle Protections are beneficial status effects that enhance a player's ferocity toward certain types of monsters until it wears off or is removed. Benefitting characters gain a "Killer" effect against a specific monster type.

How to remove the effect

  • This effect can be removed manually.

How the effect is inflicted/gained

Job Abilities

  • Dragoon - Ancient Circle (Dragons)
  • Samurai - Warding Circle (Demons)
  • Paladin - Holy Circle (Undead)
  • Dark Knight - Arcane Circle (Arcana)
  • Beastmaster - Killer Instinct (Gain the Killer Effect of the Beastmaster's current pet)
    • Though while does not have "Circle" in the name, Killer Instinct is similar to other Circle Protections.
