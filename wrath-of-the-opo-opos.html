Walkthrough

  • You must first have Zilart Mission 5 - Headstone Pilgrimage activated.
  • After completing your business for ZM5 at the Cermet Headstone, trade a Garnet to the headstone. You will get a cutscene.
    • Garnets can be obtained from various Treasure Chests, purchased at

  • At the end of the cutscene, you will receive the Opo-opo Necklace.
