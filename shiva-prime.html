Notes:

  • All versions have a very high magic detection range.
  • Higher Level Avatar Spawned by Quest: Waking the Beast
  • Uses Diamond Dust after 10 minutes, regardless of HP.
  • Special Attacks: Heavenly Strike, Rush, Blizzard IV, Double Slap, Sleepga, Frost Armor
  • Has a unique form of Heavenly Strike, which ignores shadows and deals ~1000 damage.
  • High Level Avatar Spawned by Quest: Trial by Ice
  • Uses Diamond Dust area attack at approximately 50% of health.
  • Special Attacks: Sleepga, Frost Armor
  • After June 5, 2007 update was seen to use Heavenly Strike, ignoring shadows, multiple times on Firesday, not at all on the following Earthsday. Any correlation should be confirmed.
  • Low Level Avatar Spawned by Quest: Trial Size Trial by Ice
