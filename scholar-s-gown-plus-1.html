Scholar's Gown +1

From Zenith XI Wiki

Body · SCH level 74 · Artifact +1.

Contents
  1. Materials
  2. How to obtain
  3. Sources

DEF 39; MP +18; INT +3; MND +3; enhances Dark Arts; MP recovered while healing +5.

Materials

  • Scholar's Gown
  • Electrum Stud ×1
  • Brilliantine ×1
  • Lynx Leather ×1
  • Ancient Beastcoin ×40

How to obtain

  1. Unlock Limbus access by reaching Garden of Antiquity (CoP8-1 / Sea).
  2. Deposit the required Ancient Beastcoins with Sagheera in Port Jeuno (I-8) first.
  3. Trade the original armor, Electrum Stud, Brilliantine and crafted material together.
  4. Return after the next Vana'diel midnight to collect the upgraded piece. Only one upgrade can be pending.

Recipe and completion timer checked against Zenith's reviewed base revision. Scholar Artifact Upgrades lists the whole set.
