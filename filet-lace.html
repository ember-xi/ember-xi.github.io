Filet Lace

From Zenith XI Wiki

Exclusive Temenos material required for every Dancer artifact +1 upgrade.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. How to use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Open Limbus item chests in Temenos. One verified farming route is the sixth-floor item chest in Temenos - Northern Tower. It is a possible reward, not guaranteed; opening a time or recovery chest does not substitute for the item chest. Other Temenos routes can also award this material.

How to use

Each Dancer AF +1 recipe requires one Filet Lace and one Gold Stud, along with the matching base artifact piece, a crafted material and Ancient Beastcoins. Bring the complete recipe to Sagheera in Port Jeuno. See Dancer Artifact and Relic Upgrades for the quantities and trade order. The material cannot be bought on the Auction House.
