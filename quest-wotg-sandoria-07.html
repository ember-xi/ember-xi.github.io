Before you begin

  • Complete In a Haze of Glory, leave the zone and wait until the next Vana’diel day.
  • For the intended story order, finish A Nation on the Brink and reach Crossroads of Time.
  • Bring multiple Hatchets for logging.

Starting point

Rholont · Southern San d'Oria (S) · E-7

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Rholont. Examine the nearby Tree Hollow for Long-life biscuits and the Well to the east for Flask of Kingdom water.
  2. Log in East Ronfaure (S) using Hatchets until you receive Ronfaure maple syrup. Return with all three key items to Rholont for Biscuit a la Rholont.
  3. Examine Felled Trees at western G-7 in Jugner Forest (S). In Vunkerl Inlet (S), examine Toppled Cresset I-8, then Toppled Cresset at F-8/F-9.
  4. Examine Underbrush at northeast F-13 for its scene, then again to spawn Madthrasher Zradbodd. Defeat it and immediately re-examine Underbrush before leaving the zone.
  5. Examine Shimmering Pondweed at northwest G-6 in Pashhow Marshlands (S) to finish.

Battle and progress notes

  • Madthrasher uses strong elemental and area spells. The native quest’s acceptance check omits the intended Crossroads gate; that omission is not a reason to skip the story order.

Completion and reward

Peiste Skin.

What this opens

  • Bonds That Never Die
