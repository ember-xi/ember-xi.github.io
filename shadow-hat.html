How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Abjuration

Obtained by trading the following items to Alphollon C Meriard at the cathedral in Northern San d'Oria.

  • Hadean Abjuration: Head (dropped from Helmwige's Chamber and Odin's Chamber)
  • Cursed Hat
