—A Squire's Test II

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Balasiel in Southern San d'Oria (F-7). He asks you to prove yourself by bringing a Revival Tree Root.
    • Balasiel is easy to miss: go to Watchdog Alley (F-6), find Celyddon, walk west into the stairwell, go up and take the first left across the wooden bridge (not the walkway). Follow the stairs up, down and around to Balasiel.
  • Get a Revival Tree Root (any source works: the Auction House under Materials → Alchemy, or drops from undead) and trade it to Balasiel.
    • Keep one free inventory slot before trading or you will lose the root without getting the Spatha.
    • You do not need to zone between the cutscene and the trade.
    • If you already meet the level, A Squire's Test II can be started right away without zoning.
