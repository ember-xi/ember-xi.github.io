Petras

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

How to Obtain

  • Only obtainable through Ballista and Brenner by /quarry command.
  • Virtually only a status effect or point not an inventory item.

Notes

  • Usage at Ballista: Need Gate Breach status to throw into a rook. Only 5 petras can be thrown at once. If you have more than five, you will keep the remaining petras and need to obtain Gate Breach again to throw the remaining petras.
  • Usage in Brenner: Obtained petras can be returned to a Pursuivant to heal HP and MP.
