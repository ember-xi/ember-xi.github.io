Before your first ride

Complete Chocobo’s Wounds with Brutus in Upper Jeuno (G-7). Its reward is the Chocobo License. Keep this license for stable rentals.

How to ride

Speak to Mairee or Couvoullie at the stables and choose the rental. The NPC shows the current fee and checks the normal rental requirements. Pay the fee to start your ride. Return to a stable when you need another rental.

Jeuno connections

Zenith Outpost and Zenith Guide are beside Home Point #1 in Upper Jeuno (F-5). Choose an outpost or an owned Gate Crystal destination.
