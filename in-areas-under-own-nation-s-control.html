Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Items with the effect of +stat 'In areas under own nation's control" will only take effect when, during conquest, the nation you currently are allied to is in control of the region you are in. This effect will take place in all zones within a region.

For example: If you are currently allied to Windurst, and Windurst controls the area of Elshimo Lowlands, then the effect of any armor's effects with 'In areas under own nation's control' will be in place when you are at Kazham, Norg, Sea Serpent Grotto, and Yuhtunga Jungle. Zoning into any of the areas will cause the effect to activate immediately.

Note that in order to obtain the benefits, you must have either Signet or Sanction active.

Furthermore, these benefits will still activate in certain areas that are not subject to conquest, regardless of which nation actually controls the area (if any). Examples of such areas are anywhere in the cities of San d'Oria, Bastok, Windurst, and Jeuno, Dynamis, and the Lumoria region (Sea). The benefits will never be active anywhere in the Aht Urhgan areas.
