Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Axe weapon skill

  • Skill level: 5
  • Delivers a two-hit attack. Damage varies with TP.
  • Will stack with Sneak Attack.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Detonation , Impaction
  • Modifiers: STR:30%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.00 1.50 2.00

Notes

Renkei type
Wind and Thunder.
Description
Two fold attack, damage varies with TP.
Animation
A regular swing followed by a backhand swing, accompanied by a blue/white swirling burst.
