Fighter's Armor Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

The Warrior's Artifact Armor exemplifies the gladiatorial showmanship of the profession. Constructed of darksteel and hardened leather, the design emphasizes freedom of movement and flashy appearance over practical defense. As such, while it is not always the preferred armor to charge into combat with, it is nonetheless well-suited to the versatility of its wearer, allowing for lightning-fast blows in quick succession, improved use of the shield for defensive purposes, and its flamboyant design baits foes into rushing right into the Warrior's waiting blades. Also see the HQ version, Fighter's Armor +1 Set.
