Notes:

  • Roams the entire zone. Alliances have 60 minutes to defeat it before the initial time limit expires.
  • Has over 27,000HP (as Benediction has been observed restoring that much).
  • It is susceptible to Bind, immune to Gravity, and very resistant to Stun.
  • Much like Absolute Virtue, the Apocalyptic Beast can use every jobs' two-hour ability (except the three newest jobs from ToAU). It will use them in sequence every 45-75 seconds (generally when previous wears off) based on the standard job listing (as shown under Status -> Job Levels on Main Menu). After going through all two-hours abilities, it will start over again.
  • Can use all Dragon TP abilities (except for Nullsong).
  • The two-hours abilities and Dragon TP moves can be disabled by killing the corresponding NM beastman or NM Dragon within the zone. The following list shows which NM to kill to disable which ability:
  • Mighty Strikes - Qu'Pho Bloodspiller
  • Hundred Fists - Hamfist Gukhbuk
  • Benediction - Gi'Bhe Fleshfeaster
  • Manafont - Flamecaller Zoeqdoq
  • Chainspell - Gosspix Blabberlips
  • Perfect Dodge - Va'Rhu Bodysnatcher
  • Invincible - Te'Zha Ironclad
  • Blood Weapon - Shamblix Rottenheart
  • Charm/Familiar - Woodnix Shrillwhistle
  • Soul Voice - Ree Nata the Melomanic

  • When it uses Chainspell, it will begin to bombard players with spells like Blindga, Paralyga, Bindga, Breakga, Sleepga, and Death.
  • When it uses Manafont, it will cast assorted tier 3 -aga nukes.
  • Summons 5 Dragon's Wyverns when it uses Call Wyvern.
  • Summons a Dragon's Avatar when it uses Astral Flow.
  • Has a chance to drop four 100-value currencies.
  • Click the ??? left behind after killing the Apocalyptic Beast to obtain the key item, for winning Dynamis - Buburimu.
  • Defeating it will grant a 60 minute time extension.
