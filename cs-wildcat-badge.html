badge from "Salaheem's Sentinels."

emblazoned in its center.

Assault

Promotion: Chief Sergeant;
Notes:
This badge indicates that your Mercenary Rank is Chief Sergeant.

Assault Missions

This rank grants access to the following assaults.

Name Assault Area Recommended Level Party Size Points Objective Description
Imperial Code Leujaoam Sanctum 70 3-6 1333 Retrieve the OGMA The Empire's mercury cipher (OGMA) has been stolen by a group of Far Eastern spies. With it, our enemies will be able to decrypt top-secret military orders, thus severely compromising Imperial safety. You are to intercept the enemy as they transport the device.
Marids in the Mist Mamool Ja Training Grounds 70 3-6 Neutralize the marids Our agents have informed us that the Mamool Ja have been training marids to use during sieges on the Empire. You are to locate these deadly beasts and capture or eliminate them before it's too late.
Egg Conservation Lebros Cavern 70 3-6 Drive out the hunters There have been reports that Qiqirn hunters have been collecting the eggs of our army's messenger apkallus. You are to join forces with the male apkallus in the area and eliminate the invading Qiqirn while protecting the females and their eggs.
Operation: Snake Eyes Periqia 70 3-6 Locate the generals Generals Karazahm and Umarid of the Imperial Army's Arrapago division have disappeared from camp headquarters while on a mission on Sharug Isle. There is a high possibility that the two have been kidnapped by members of the Undead Swarm.
Deserter Ilrusi Atoll 70 3-6 Locate the agents Agents of our Anti-Corsair Task Force have gone AWOL and are hiding somewhere in Ilrusi. Your mission is to swiftly disarm the deviants and take them into custody. All information on these criminals is highly classified and any disclosure of their identity is strictly prohibited.
