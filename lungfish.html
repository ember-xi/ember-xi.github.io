Statistics

Lungfish
This freshwater fish has the ability to
breathe out of water. It is found in the
waters of the Phanauet Channel.

Stackable: 12

Other Uses

Guild Points Value: 720 / 4,000 (5.56 items)

NPC Sell Price: 244 gil

How to Obtain

Auction House Category: Food > Fish (
 
 )

Best fishing setup: Halcyon Rod + Minnow at south landing > central landing route of Phanauet Channel (ferry leaves at 1:00). Lungfish may only be fished up from 2:23 to 3:10 while on the ferry. Albatross Ring effect is required to reel these in, as they will put up a fight and do not stay on the hook for long periods of time.
