Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

File:Status Plague.png Plague is a harmful status effect that reduces a character's TP and MP over time.

How to remove the effect

  • The White Magic spell Viruna will remove this effect.
  • Remedy Ointment will remove this effect.
  • Remedy will remove this effect. Verification Needed

How the effect is inflicted/gained

Spells

  • Blue Magic - Lowing

Job Abilities

  • Samurai - Blade Bash

Monster Abilities

  • Buffalo - Lowing
  • Bugard - Bone Crunch
  • Orobon - Vile Belch
  • Thinker - Stygian Vapor
  • Tiamat - Tebbad Wing
  • Phuabo - Spine Lash
  • Promathia - Pestilent Penance
