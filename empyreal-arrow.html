Empyreal Arrow

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Archery weapon skill

  • Skill level: 250
  • In order to obtain Empyreal Arrow, the quest From Saplings Grow must be completed.
  • Delivers a single-hit attack. Damage varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Fusion , Transfixion
  • Modifiers: STR:16%; AGI:25%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
2.00 2.75 3.00

Levels

  • Empyreal Arrow is only available to Rangers. While other jobs can obtain the required 250 Archery skill through the use of merits and special equipment, they will be unable to use Empyreal Arrow.
  • Archery skill level 250 is obtainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
