26 September 2026 — Connected level-75 reference

Added local job, equipment, acquisition, spell, monster, crafting and endgame reference pages with links between related articles. Existing Zenith mission walkthroughs and package notes remain. Removed later-expansion recipes from the native item reference and corrected retired equipment-quest prose.

25 September 2026 — Story mission walkthroughs

Added expansion mission pages, entry requirements, story branches, credited NPC images and route maps. The mission directory now links the Sky and Sea access routes. Known missing core battle steps are stated on the affected pages.

25 September 2026 — Area pages

Reorganized the area category into continent and region link groups. Zone articles now use a map and Zone Information panel, followed by quest, NPC and monster tables.

25 September 2026 — Game images

Added official images for the twenty jobs through Wings of the Goddess, NPC portraits, and monster images in area tables. Family images are placed in the Family column.

25 September 2026 — Weighted Stones

Added the acquisition walkthrough, permanent key item, target and Banishing Gate reference, with links from Garlaige Citadel and First Limit Break. Weighted Stones v1.0 corrects the hidden acquisition marker; an in-game check is pending.

Native Maat and Armsmith removal — v3.6.0

Zenith Armsmith is removed. Maat uses original acceptance and turn-ins. Automatic cap increases, the custom trial menu and Testimony bypass are removed. The first Limit Break keeps its three-kill missing-item guarantee. Previously unlocked caps, completed quests and earned items are preserved. Travel, camps, rings and Trusts remain.

Custom story quests retired — v3.5.0

Coastal Dispatch, Sandbound Repairs and The Broken Watch are retired. Their NPCs resume original interactions, with no story-material trades or new story rewards. Earned items and gil are kept. Valkurm Emperor, Dune Widow and Aquarius resume native spawning and drops. Trust, city-ring and Limit Break quests are unchanged.

Custom equipment chapters retired — v3.4.0

The eight early WAR/THF chapters and the later custom THF equipment quest are retired. Already earned items and augments are preserved. Equipment progression now points to original quests, AF, shops, crafting and monster drops. Native Phara/Brygid quests are verified without copying or replacing them. Trust, city-ring and national mission rewards remain unchanged.

23 September 2026 — Echad Ring retired

World Quests v3.3.1 removes Echad Ring from the final tutorial reward in all three starting cities. The installer removes only Tenzoki’s Echad Ring copies, with an inventory backup; other items and tutorial progress remain. No other balance changes are included. Install the package while the game server is stopped.

23 September 2026 — Jeuno travel

World Quests v3.3.0 adds Zenith Outpost and Zenith Guide beside Upper Jeuno Home Point #1 (F-5). Outpost rules match the starting cities. Personal chocobo summoning and its custom quest are retired; the Chocobo License and stable rentals remain. Tenzoki’s Chocobo Companion is removed from Mounts at login after installation.

23 September: San d’Oria mission walkthroughs

Expanded all 20 San d’Oria missions through Rank 10. Journey Abroad includes both city orders. Magicite includes its access quests and exchanges. Inventory materials and story key items have individual acquisition pages and working mission links. Corrected the Coming of Age wait to one minute and the Ranperre’s Final Rest deciphering step to a zone change, following the reviewed server scripts.

23 September: wiki tables and item acquisition

Shared page layout now places the information table above the walkthrough, with Contents alongside it. Quest lists use NPC, Quest and Reward. Item references include documented monster drops, synthesis recipes, NPC vendors and Auction House categories. Fixed market prices remain unchanged; native Price History and live in-game stock are explained.

23 September: World Quests v3.2.0

Three equipment side quests now use varied field objectives. Added A Shadow Reforged at Nanaa Mihgo and Three Nations, One Journey at the city ring guards. Current walkthroughs, exact rewards and game-map directions match the downloadable package; installing the package is still required.

23 September: complete quest rewards and directions

Quest, NPC and progression pages now share an explicit reward registry. Quest summaries identify the start and return NPC, game-map square, requirements and exact reward. Includes Silent Steps from World Quests v3.2.0: 8-minute Sneak + Invisible, 10-minute reuse. Retired central-menu directions are removed from current guides. This website update does not install the server package.

22 September: map locations

NPC, enemy and quest-marker directions now use the game map’s letter-number squares. EXP camp book locations also use map squares, with dungeon sections identified. World Quests v3.0.2 applies the same format to game messages.

22 September: World Quests v3.0.0

Specialist NPCs now own acceptance and rewards. Maat handles Limit Break; Gondebaud handles capacity and Uka; city representatives handle rings. Standalone challenges are retired and quest markers check the active objective. The package preserves progression and saved rewards. World Quests v3.0.2. Package checked offline; Windows installation and in-game verification are pending.

21 September: Progression v2.1.0 guide

The wiki now matches the delivered package: First Limit Break at 50 uses three monster items, with a once-only guarantee after three eligible kills per type; the following increases open automatically until 70. Added the unified quest menu, three story chains, all 24 NM marker locations and gifts, city rings, fourth WAR/THF chapters, and Uka/Monberaux guidance. Old hunt, exploration and Crystal Paths pages now explain retirement and retained rewards. This website update does not confirm installation on the game server.

Earlier release history

The entries below record older package states. Use the current guides above for the Progression menus.

19 September: Eco-Warrior package ready

Eco-Warrior v1.0 keeps the level-25 encounters, raises base gil to 10,000, adds an all-three-nations rotation and awards a saved +75% EXP bonus. Offline checks passed. Installation and in-game verification remain unconfirmed. Custom-quest fanfare playback is still pending.

19 September: Adventures v1.1 prepared

Added automatic 80-slot main Inventory, removed four bag-upgrade reminders, replaced the uniform oil/powder payouts with gil and explicit gift choices, and added three hunt-completion milestones. Prior completions retain their progress and can claim the new gifts once. Three features: job and level goals, sixteen optional hunt contracts and two automatic exploration routes. Added one Armsmith menu entry and Scout progress access. Offline LuaJIT checks covered saved counts, menu limits, reward retry/replay handling, routes and all twenty jobs. Twenty-seven installer scenarios covered fresh installs and v1.0 upgrades, supported text/encoding variants, failures and rollback. No live installation or in-game play is claimed. Full guide.

Valkurm Scout replacement beside the books

The runtime report confirmed one normal Scout at the previous location after player entry. At the player’s request, Scout By Books v1.0 now prepares a replacement at H-7, beside Field Manual and Survival Guide. A full map-server restart creates the replacement from the updated data. Quest state and Qufim are preserved. Placement was reviewed against the native books and pinned navigation surface; live-client appearance remains to be confirmed.

Scout source check — 19 September, 13:13 UTC

The follow-up collection contains both Zone.lua files and the four supporting Lua files. All hashes were verified. Both Scout initialization hooks are present; the Valkurm outpost coordinates remain unchanged. The callbacks passed an isolated LuaJIT composition check with mocked engine APIs. Map-server startup output and live NPC state are still needed; no cause, server fix or restored Scout visibility is claimed.
