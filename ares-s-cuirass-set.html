Ares's Cuirass Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A set of armor crafted from various incredibly rare and expensive items, as well as a total of 50 Imperial Gold Pieces. The Ares Plate Armor set is wearable by Warriors, Paladins, Dark Knights, and Dragoons only.

Number of Pieces: 5

Cost to store: Unknown. Storing may not be possible.
