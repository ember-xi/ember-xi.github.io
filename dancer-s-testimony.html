Dancer's Testimony

From Zenith XI Wiki

A monster drop used to enter the Dancer level-limit battle against Laila.

Contents
  1. Where to farm
  2. How to use
  3. Sources

Where to farm

MonsterLocationLevel
Amber QuadavNorth Gustaberg (S)59–63
Young QuadavGrauberg (S)61–63

Use these past-zone versions of the monsters. The testimony is a chance drop; allow inventory space and continue farming until it appears.

How to use

Follow A Furious Finale: Dancer level 66+, the previous level-limit quest and Comeback Queen are required. Trade the testimony to Laila in Upper Jeuno (G-7) to travel to the arena, then trade it to the Burning Circle for the battle. If your level limit is already unlocked through another job, this battle is not needed for leveling.
