Walkthrough

  • To obtain this quest, first go to any Promyvion location, enter, and return to Harith in the Grand Duke's Palace without erasing your memory at the large apparatus. He will then give you this quest.
  • Trade Harith one Recollection + 2,000 gil. Harith will immediately synth the appropriate bottle of anima for you:
  • Recollection of Pain --> Hysteroanima (No TP Abilities)
  • Recollection of Guilt --> Terroanima (Run in Terror)
  • Recollection of Fear --> Psychoanima (Paralyze)
  • Trade Harith one of the Recollections from the Notorious Monsters in Promyvion - Vahzl to receive the weapon/armor listed below. Unlike with the anima, you do not need to trade any gil to receive the item.
  • Recollection of Animosity --> Dia Wand
  • Recollection of Suffering --> Hamayumi
  • Recollection of Anxiety --> Stone Gorget
Note
If you give Harith the Recollection and gil to synth an item that you already possess, Harith will still carry out his task. However, he will not hand over the item because they are all . Harith will not accept any other Recollections until you dispose of your current item and take the one he's holding.

Game Description

Client: Harith (Grande Duke Palace, Ru'Lude Gardens)

The mysterious "society" that Harith works for is supposedly performing research on a strange life form known as the Empty.
