Notes:

  • Spawns around H-5 on map 4 in Castle Oztroja.
  • Respawn time: 21-24 hours
  • Uses Astral Flow. Yagudo Avatar has a very deadly Astral Flow.
  • You may avoid the most of the damage of Astral Flow by putting up the corresponding Barspell of the Yagudo's Elemental, because it will most likely summon an Avatar of the same element. (I.E.: Earth Elemental -> it will spawn Titan).
  • Assisted by Yagudo's Elemental
  • When engaging, he will say: "Kahk-ka-ka... You filthy, dim-witted heretics! You have damned yourselves by coming here."
  • When defeated, he will say: "Our lord, Tzee Xicu the Manifest! Even should our bodies be crushed and broken, may your soul endure into eternity..."
