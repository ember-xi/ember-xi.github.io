Walkthrough

Aht Urhgan Whitegate

  • Watch the cutscene at the Shaharat Teahouse that was triggered at the end of the last mission.
    • There will be several options through-out the cutscene. It does not matter which you choose, as you will have to choose them all eventually, anyway.

Aydeewa Subterrane

  • Both Sneak and Invisible are strongly recommended.
  • Zone into Aydeewa Subterrane from any entrance for a cutscene.
  • Take the #5 entrance from Wajaom Woodlands at G-7. Once inside, follow path A or B.
  • A) At the first intersection, continue South. You will reach a room with a small pond in which you will need Sneak, and Invisible, take the South exit in that room and continue until you reach the open area at E/F-9. Be careful, as there are many chigoes in that area. Sneak and Invisible advised. Turn West and walk/drop down a long ledge. You will now be on the 5th Map. Once you drop down the ledge, continue straight ahead until you find a targetable blank spot around the bottom right corner of grid (F-8).
  • B) To avoid chigoes, head north from small pond then go west at intersection to next room, use southern ramp at D-8 (now on 5th map). Continue straight ahead until you find a targetable blank spot around the bottom right corner of grid (F-8).
  • Inspect the blank spot for a cutscene, which will trigger Mission 21. Enjoy the cutscene as you will not get aggro during the cutscene, but will be susceptible when it is over.
