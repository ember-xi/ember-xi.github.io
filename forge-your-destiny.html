Forge Your Destiny

Forge Your Destiny
Starting NPCJaucribaix — Norg (K-8), upper level at the end of the hallway
Required FameNone
Level RestrictionLevel 30
Items NeededOriental Steel, Sacred Sprig, Hatchet, Bomb Steel, Sacred Branch
TitleNone
RepeatableNo
DescriptionA lump of bomb steel and a sacred branch are needed to forge a new katana. Ask Jaucribaix's pupils where to find them.
Rewards
  • Mumeito
  • Ability to become a Samurai
← Previous questNext quest →
——

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Jaucribaix in Norg (K-8). He asks for a Bomb Steel and a Sacred Branch.
  • Down the long steps talk to Aeka (I-8) for the Oriental Steel and Ranemaud (I-7) for the Sacred Sprig.
    • Lose them and you pay: Aeka wants a Darksteel Ore, Ranemaud a Platinum Ore and two Gold Ore.
  • Bomb Steel: go to Konschtat Highlands (D-8), enter the small cave and trade the Oriental Steel to the ??? to spawn Forger, a bomb around level 33. Defeat it and take the Bomb Steel it drops.
  • Sacred Branch: buy a Hatchet and go to the far east of The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah — an unmapped area north of (K-10) leads to (L-9). Trade the Hatchet to the ??? in the tree to spawn Guardian Treant. Defeat it, then trade the Sacred Sprig to the ??? for the Sacred Branch.
    • Go in daytime; the spot is hard to find at night.
  • Return to Norg and trade the Bomb Steel and Sacred Branch to Jaucribaix. Come back after three game days (about three real hours) and talk to him: Gilgamesh hands you the Mumeito and you can become a Samurai.
    • Keep the Mumeito — the Samurai AF weapon quest needs it (a replacement costs 30,000 gil from Ranemaud).
