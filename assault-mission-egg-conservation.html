Walkthrough

Defeat all the Qiqirn. Protect the female apkallu.

  • This was like a mini-besieged with the additional NPC helpers.
  • You start in a giant room with a lot of Apkallu - the bigger ones are the males and are going to help in the fight.
  • The smaller Apkallu are the females you're there to protect.
  • All of the Apkallu are buffable and curable.
  • The mission fails if all females are killed and you get points based on the number of females that are left at the end of the mission.
  • The Qiqirn Egglers wield daggers. They attack quickly with high accuracy.
    • They use Kibosh, which induces Amnesia, and Faze, which freezes you for several seconds.
  • Qiqirn start coming in from one of the sides of the room in teams of 1-2.
    • Once they're in range of the males, the males will engage as well.
    • After a little bit, another team of Qiqirn will join the attack from one of the remaining sides.
  • Once all Qiqirn are defeated there will be a short period to rest up, then another wave of Qiqirn comes. This continues for a few waves.
  • The Qiqirn come at a decent speed, so make sure you bring enough DD to get through each wave pretty quickly.
  • You can sleep them before the Apkallu attack them, or else the Apkallu will wake them up. It should be noted that they are highly resistant to sleep.
  • Another method is to have one or more people kite Qiqirn around the room, either to gather up males to attack them, or to get them away from the group, while other members of your party kill one or two Qiqirn.
  • Curing generates more hate than normal.
  • The male Apkallu will "faint" if they get below about 50% health and can't be healed/revived.
  • After a few waves of Qiqirn are defeated the Mission will be successful and the Rune of Release will spawn in the area you are in with the Apkallu.
