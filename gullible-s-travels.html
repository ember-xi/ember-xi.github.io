Notes

Game Description

Client: Magriffon (Kazham)
Summary:

Magriffon has given all his worldy possessions to save a pack of poor Opo-opos from starvation. Now he needs someone to save him from getting kicked out of the bed & breakfast before he has completed his "top-secret" mission.
