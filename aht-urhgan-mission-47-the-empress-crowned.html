Walkthrough

Continued cut scene from previous mission. The rewards for this mission are Imperial Standard and choice between Balrahn's Ring, Ulthalam's Ring, and Jalzahn's Ring. You may either choose your reward now or wait until a later time to choose it. (Picking a Ring now does not keep you from recieveing the reward from ToAU Mission 48.)

Game Description

You have been bestowed with the honor of crowning Empress Nashmeira II. There can be no higher acknowledgment of your achievements as a mercenary. Perhaps now President Naja will return your rightful reward...
