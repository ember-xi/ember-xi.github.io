Chocobo’s Wounds

From Zenith XI Wiki

Earn your Chocobo License by caring for the injured chocobo in Upper Jeuno.

Requirements

  • Main-job level 20 or higher to start.
  • 4 Gausebit Wildgrass. Gysahl Greens are not used for this license quest.

Walkthrough

  1. Take four Gausebit Wildgrass to Brutus at the Upper Jeuno chocobo stables (G-7).
  2. Speak to Brutus and agree to help. Check that Chocobo’s Wounds appears in your current Jeuno quests.
  3. Trade one grass to the NPC named Chocobo beside Brutus and Osker, not to Brutus.
  4. The first two progressing attempts refuse the food without consuming it. Attempts three through six consume one grass each. Complete the scene and wait between attempts.
  5. After the final scene, receive Chocobo License in Key Items and the title Chocobo Trainer.

Feeding interval

The reviewed base script waits 45 seconds after each feeding scene. An older era module changes the wait to the next Vana’diel day; whether that module is active on the running server has not been verified. An early trade does not progress the quest.

Do not trade all four grasses together. Six progressing attempts consume four grasses in total.

Reward

Chocobo License key item; Chocobo Trainer title.

After receiving your license

Speak to Mairee or Couvoullie at the Upper Jeuno chocobo stables (G-7) to rent a chocobo.

Walkthrough

  • You might get Chocobo on the Loose! first, instead of this quest. If you want to get Chocobo's Wounds but you don't want to finish Chocobo on the Loose!. To by-pass Chocobo on the Loose!, Talk to Brutus again, talk to Silver Tail, and than talk to Brutus once more. He should ask you a question if you want to ride a chocobo.
  • You will need 4 Gausebit Grasses. These can be obtained in Meriphataud Mountains from defeating Crane Flies or in Dangruf Wadi from Wadi Hares. They can also be bought from the Auction House.
  • Talking to Brutus starts the quest.
  • Trade a clump of Gausebit Grass to the Chocobo next to Osker. (It will not accept the grass.)
  • Come back the next game day (about 1 hour real life time).
  • Come back again for the third time and feed it. (This time you will be successful.)
  • Successfully feed it three more times (once daily) to complete the quest.
  • This quest takes approximately 6 hours real time.


Note:

  • You are not required to give the Gausebit Grass to the Chocobo in succession - i.e. you can skip several days between the feedings.

Quest Description

Client: Brutus (Chocobo Stables, Upper Jeuno)
Summary:

Help nurse the chocobo back to health. It seems to have suffered both mentally and physically. Gausebit wildgrass may do the trick.
