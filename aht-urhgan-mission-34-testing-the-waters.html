Walkthrough

Aht Urhgan Whitegate

  • Watch the cutscene that was triggered at the end of the last mission.

Caedarva Mire

  • If you have not gotten another Ephramadian Gold Coin since you used it up in Aht Urhgan Mission 15: The Black Coffin, you'll need to do that now:
    • Go to the cemetery at Caedarva Mire (Azouph Isle E-10) (out the west exit from Nashmau) and find a target point called "Jazaraat's Headstone" (beware of True Sight Imps).
    • Check the headstone to receive the Temporary Key Item: Ephramadian Gold Coin.

Arrapago Reef

  • Now head back to the Cutter BC entrance.
    • If you're at the headstone, just head south from there to the Alzadaal Undersea Ruins. You will need an Imperial Silver Piece or Remnants Permit to enter the ruins from here.
      • Go through the ruins and back out to Caedarva Mire (Dvucca Isle).
    • If you already had the key item, you can just take the Runic Portal to Dvucca Isle Staging Point.
    • In Dvucca, head West to Arrapago Reef and on to the ship "Black Coffin" for a cutscene. This cutscene will begin Mission 35.
