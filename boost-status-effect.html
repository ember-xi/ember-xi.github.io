Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Boost is a beneficial status effect that temporarily focuses a player's attacks until it wears off or is removed. Benefitting characters gain a small increase to attack, and it also give a bonus to a hidden stat that effects the damage of Chi Blast. Multiple Boost effects can be present at one time. However, if the effect wears off before being used, all stacked bonuses to attack (from the Boost effects) will be removed, not just the first one.

How to remove the effect

  • This effect will be removed after the next attack the player makes, regardless of whether he/she hits or not
  • This effect can be removed manually.
  • Speaking to any NPC will remove this effect

How the effect is inflicted/gained

Job Abilities

  • Monk - Boost

Monster Abilities

  • Hippogryph - Fantod
  • Trolls - Quake Stomp
  • Yovra - Fluorescence
