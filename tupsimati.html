Tupsimati

From Zenith XI Wiki

Staff · SCH 75 · Mythic weapon.

Level75 version: DMG62, Delay402, Accuracy+30, Magic Accuracy+10 and Magic Attack Bonus+20. Matching elemental weather reduces enmity; Omniscience activates its Mythic aftermath.

How to obtain

  1. Obtain Elder Staff from Nyzul Isle Investigation.
  2. Meet the Captain-rank and story requirements in An Imperial Heist, then begin the Mythic chain with your Scholar weapon.
  3. Continue Duties, Tasks, and Deeds, Forging a New Myth and Coming Full Circle. Follow those quest pages for the title checks, currencies, materials and final battle.

This page describes the level 75 weapon. Higher-level Trial of the Magians upgrades are outside this era guide. For only the weapon skill, use Unlocking a Myth (Scholar) instead.
