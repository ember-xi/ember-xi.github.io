Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description:

A silver Lance Corporal
 mercenary badge from "Salaheem's
Sentinels." A scintillant moat
carp is emblazoned in its center.

Used In Mission: Assault

Obtained: Promotion: Lance Corporal

Notes: This badge indicates that your Mercenary Rank is Lance Corporal.
