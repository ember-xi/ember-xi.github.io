Walkthrough

  • Once you complete The Clue, you will have to wait until Midnight JST before he will offer you this quest.
  • Rycharde now wants you to deliver his cuisine to his father Valgeir to once again try to show how his cooking skills to him. Rycharde will have your ferry fee paid for, so head off to Selbina.
  • Take the Mhauran Couscous key item to Valgeir.
  • Valgeir will tell you Rycharde still has a long way to go and will explain that cooking isn't about getting the most exotic ingredients or how it looks on the outside. It's what is the inside that counts.
  • Valgeir then hands you a Popoto item to give back to his son.
  • Take the Popoto back to Rycharde to complete the quest.
  • Once you give the Popoto to Rycharde, you can go back to Selbina again and talk to Valgeir to find out why he gave the Popoto to his son.

Game Description

Client: Rycharde (Sailors' Stay, Mhaura)
Summary:

Rycharde wants you to deliver a dish to Valgeir in Selbina, and report on how he liked it.
