Zvahl Chest Key

From Zenith XI Wiki

Opens a treasure chest in Castle Zvahl; it is different from the coffer key.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Fight Demon Pawn, Demon Knight, Demon Wizard or Demon Warlock in Castle Zvahl Baileys or Castle Zvahl Keep. Several other beastmen in the Keep also drop it.

Use

Trade to a Treasure Chest for treasure. With A Test of True Love active, the quest reward can take priority. For artifact coffers, obtain Zvahl Coffer Key instead.
