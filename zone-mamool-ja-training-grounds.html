Additional level-75 reference


Situated on the southern tip of the Mamool Ja Savagelands the training grounds are used by the Mamool Ja to train their warriors in new techniques as well as prepare for battle.

This area an Assault area. This area is only accessible if the Treasures of Aht Urhgan expansion is installed, The Road to Aht Urhgan quest is completed, and Aht Urhgan Mission 3: President Salaheem is completed.

A cage of Bhaflau Fireflies is given to all participating mercenaries who enter. They can be used to return to the Mamool Ja Staging Point.
