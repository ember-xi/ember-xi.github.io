Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Gives an edge against undead enemies.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Rate of activation for Paladin Job Trait is ~10%
  • Further Notes:
    • Occasionally causes undead type enemies to be intimidated.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Arcana Earring (2.5%)
  • Caballero Gauntlets (5%)
  • Tatami Shield
  • Exorcist Hose
  • Templar Mace (2%)
  • Esquire's Mantle
  • Flawless Ribbon

Food that Enhances this Trait

  • Garlic Cracker

Undead Killer

  • Obtained: Paladin Level 5
  • Obtained: Blue Mage Level 34
