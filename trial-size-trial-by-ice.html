Walkthrough

Notes
  • This is a solo battle.
  • There is a level 20 cap on this battle.
  • There is a 15-minute time limit.
  • You can only use Carbuncle.
  • Speak to Castilchat in Northern San d'Oria.
  • If you qualify, he will give you a Mini Tuning Fork of Ice.
  • This item is not a Key Item like the regular Tuning Forks, but is a Rare/Exclusive item that needs to go in your inventory.
  • When you are ready to go, return to Castilchat and trade him the Mini Tuning Fork of Ice (he does not keep the fork) and be warped to the cloister for the fight.
  • Trade the Mini Tuning Fork to the protocrystal to enter the battle.
  • Note that if you do not trade the Mini Tuning Fork and instead just select the protocrystal, you will enter the regular level 60+ party avatar battle if you have the regular tuning fork key item.
  • Try to fight Shiva Prime on Firesday and do not fight her on Iceday.
  • Defeat her for your reward.
  • Note: If you have never talked to the NPC before, talking to him multiple times might be required to obtain the key item.

Game Description

Client: Castilchat (Watchtower, Northern San d'Oria)
Summary:

The Rhinostery has developed a new "mini" tuning fork of ice. Ringing it upon the protocrystal in Fei'Yin will open the path to a one-on-one battle with the beast that awaits inside. However, only summoners are allowed to take up this challenge.
