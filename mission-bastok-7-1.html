Before you begin

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission and speak with Cid.
  2. Search Ro’Maeve for the mission ???. Possible squares include D-10, E-9, E-10, G-9, I-8, K-10, K-11, L-10 and L-7.
  3. Examine it and defeat the two Mokkurkalfi golems.
  4. Find and examine the ??? again for Reinforced Cermet, then return to Cid.

Important notes

  • Nearby enemies can detect magic. The ??? may move; obtain the key item before zoning.

Walkthrough

  • Trade in 4 stacks of crystals
  • Talk to the Gate Guard in Bastok.
  • Talk to Cid in Metalworks to get the mission.
  • Travel to Ro'Maeve and find the ??? in:
  • D-10
  • E-9
  • E-10
  • G-9
  • I-8
  • K-10
  • K-11
  • L-10
  • L-7
  • The ??? moves around about every 15 minutes or so.
  • Clear the area around the ???.
    • You may want to spawn the golems first before clearing the mobs, so that the ??? doesn't disappear while you clear the area.
  • When ready, target the ??? to spawn 2 NM Golems (Mokkurkalfi).
    • It is possible to pop them with Sneak active so that they don't aggro, even though Golems normally aggro by Sight (confirmed three times). You can then pull them with a ranged attack and fight them one at a time.
    • It is actually not necessary for the mission to defeat both NMs.
    • Seems susceptible to Slow but builds resistance to Paralyze and Blind.
    • Soloed by 75 WHM/BLM without consumables (depending on how well geared you are you may want to bring some Yagudo Drink, however). Recommend using Ice Spikes due to its building resistance to Paralyze spell.
  • Defeat them and check the ??? again and get the Key Item: Reinforced Cermet. The ??? maybe have changed location; if so, you will have to track it down again to get the Reinforced Cermet.
  • If you're KO'd while searching for the ??? after already killing the NMs, you will have to fight them all over again and repeat the process.
  • If you leave the zone before checking the ??? and after already killing the NMs, you will have to fight them all over again and repeat the process.
  • Go back and talk with Cid to get a cutscene.


Notes

  • The ??? moves as time goes, so it may be at somewhere different than the first ??? for the golem pop.
  • More than 2 parties of level 60+ is recommended when clearing weapons and fighting the NMs.
  • Soloable by either 75THF/37NIN or 75RDM/37BLM, if fought one at a time.
  • Very resistant to sleep, if not immune.
