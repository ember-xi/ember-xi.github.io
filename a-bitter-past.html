Walkthrough

  • Talk to Frescheque to start the quest. (3rd floor, Tavnazian Safehold at H-8)
  • Talk to Raminey. (3rd floor, Tavnazian Safehold at J-7)
  • Talk to Equette for an event. (2nd floor, Tavnazian Safehold at I-9)
  • Go to (I-8) near the lake in Lufaise Meadows and click on the ??? to spawn two Orc NMs:
  • Blackbone Frazdiz (Dark Knight)
  • Rainbringer Vjatvot (Black Mage)
  • Very easily solo'ed by a 70 RDM/BLM.
  • Incredibly easy for a 67 NIN/WAR.
  • Barely soloed by a 50 DRG/WHM with use of a signal pearl.
  • Defeat the NMs and select ??? again to get the Tiny Wristlet (key item).
  • Return to the Tavnazian Safehold and talk to Frescheque for a cutscene and to receive your reward.
  • Talk to Equette again for an additional cutscene.

Game Description

Client: Frescheque (Third floor, Tavnazian Safehold)
Summary:

Try to find some information on the fate of a little girl who was kidnapped in a forest near the safehold. Her father says that she was wearing a tiny wristlet that is similar to her mother's.
