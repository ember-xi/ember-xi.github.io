Restores HP for the target party member.

Description

Spell cost: 37 MP
Ecosystem: Beasts
Type: Magical (Light)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: HP+5
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 30

Timers

Casting Time: 2.5 seconds
Recast Time: 6 seconds

Notes

  • Only used by rabbits in CoP areas.
  • Roughly equivalent to Cure III but only targetable in party and has no upper cap for the amount of curing.
  • Affected by day/weather and Light Staff/Apollo's Staff.
  • 20' Maximum distance. - SE Patched - Distance 21'~ now

Combines With...

You can use Wild Carrot as part of a combo to create Resist Sleep.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Resist Sleep combo.

  • Pollen
  • Magic Fruit
  • Yawn
  • Exuviation
