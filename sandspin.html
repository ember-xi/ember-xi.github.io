Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Deals earth damage to enemies within range. Additional Effect: Accuracy Down.

Description

Spell cost: 10 MP
Ecosystem: Amorphs
Type: Magical (Earth)
Blue Magic Points: 2
Stat Bonus: VIT+1
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 1

Timers

Casting Time: 1.5 seconds
Recast Time: 9.75 seconds

Notes

  • Reduces Accuracy and Ranged Accuracy by 50 when this ability is used by worms.
  • Primarily modified by INT, INT: 20% secondary modifier.
  • Area of effect is centered around caster.
  • Cannot be resisted, similar to how Dia cannot be resisted.
  • Though the spell itself can not be resisted, the accuracy down still functions as an additional effect, and does not always stick.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Scission, Gravitation, Darkness
