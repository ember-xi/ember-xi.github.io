Double-Up

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Enhances an active Phantom Roll effect that is eligible for Double-Up.
  • Obtained: Corsair Level 5
  • Recast Time: 8 seconds
  • Duration: Instant

Information

  • After using Phantom Roll, the Double-Up icon appears on your screen (a die with a plus sign over it), signifying that the roll is eligible for Double-Up. Using Double-Up at this point will generate another random number from 1 to 6 and add it to the previous Phantom Roll total.
  • If the total comes to 12 or higher, the roll will Bust.
  • You can Double-Up as long as the Double-Up icon is displayed on the screen, which is up to 45 seconds after Phantom Roll was initially used; or until you Bust.
  • A Phantom Roll total of 11 creates the strongest effect.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Double-Up" <me>
