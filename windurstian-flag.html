Statistics

Windurstian flag[1][2]

Furnishing:

A Federation of Windurst flag.
Windurst's symbol, the Great Star Tree,
is depicted at the center.
The predominant green represents
the Windurstians' fondness of nature.<[3] STG: 1>

Moghancement: Windurst Conquest
Aura Strength: 9
Placement: Wall
Dimensions: —

Other Uses

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Also cannot be placed into Storage.

Mission

  • Windurst Mission 9-2: Moon Reading
