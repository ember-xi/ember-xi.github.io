Zovriace

From Zenith XI Wiki

A San d’Oria patrol scout who travels through West Ronfaure.

Location

West Ronfaure — moving between the La Theine Plateau exit at F-12 and Southern San d’Oria’s gate at I-6.

He patrols the road rather than standing at a fixed spot. Look along that route if you need to find him.
