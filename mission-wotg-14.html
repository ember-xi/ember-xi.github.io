Zenith availability

Reference walkthrough: the pinned server core contains story scenes but leaves the A Nation on the Brink instance in Everbloom Hollow unfinished or unconnected. This is not confirmed playable on Zenith.

Before you begin

  • Complete Borne by the Wind.
  • Carry the Underpass hatch key awarded by Borne by the Wind.

Starting point

Underpass Hatch · Batallia Downs [S] · J-9/K-9

Walkthrough

  1. Climb the rampart to the tower at the J-9/K-9 boundary and examine the Underpass Hatch for the scene.
  2. Examine it again with the Underpass hatch key to enter the Everbloom Hollow battle.
  3. Assist the three allied units against two beastman waves each: Quadav northwest, Orcs northeast, and Yagudo south. After all waves fall, defeat One-eyed Gwajboj in the central area. Finish all connected scenes after victory.
  4. After completion, examine the nearby ??? in Batallia Downs [S] for the Jeunoan Flag. If a retry consumes your key, return to the Bulwark Gate in Sauromugue Champaign [S] for a replacement.

Completion and reward

Battle of Jeuno Veteran title; Jeunoan Flag from the post-mission ???.
