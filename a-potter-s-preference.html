Walkthrough

  • Nereus asks you to bring him soil from Gusgen Mines.
  • Go to I-7 in Gusgen Mines, near a pond, and click on the ??? to obtain the Gusgen Clay.
  • Bring the Gusgen Clay back to Nereus to complete the quest.

Can repeated after eight real life hours.
