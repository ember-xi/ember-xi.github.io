Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Uses the ninja tool, "Uchitake". Deals fire damage to an enemy and lowers its resistance against water.

Description

Spell cost: Uchitake
Spell element: Fire
Magic skill: Ninjutsu
Jobs:

Timers

Recast Time: 60 seconds

How to Obtain

Obtained: Merit Point Group 2

Translation

"Katon" means release fire. Uchitake is a type of bamboo tube filled with explosive.
