Walkthrough

  • Talk to Tami at J-8 in Bastok Mines. She'll give you the key item Tami's Note to take to her husband.
  • Examine Tami's Note.
  • Take the note to Zelman; he asks you to go and get meat jerky for him. The note says you can get it at the Bat's Lair inn (Griselda at H-5 in Bastok Mines).
  • Go back to Tami and trade her the Meat Jerky (don't talk to her first) for the full reward.
Note
If you talk to Zelman before reading the note he just says he's busy. If you just talk to Tami (before trading the jerky to her) she will give you 10 gil. When you repeat the quest your only reward will be 10 gil (whether you give her Meat Jerky or not).

Game Description

Client: Tami (Near Residential Area, Bastok Mines)
Summary:

Hand Tami's note to her husband, Zelman, a miner in the Zeruhn Mines.
