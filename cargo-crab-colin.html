Notes:

  • Lottery Spawn from the Clipper around the riverbank at (H-7)-(H-8) on the same map as the Jammer Leeches.
  • Special: Additional Effect: Poison
  • Drops around 1200 gil, 1-2 Land Crab Meat, and 0-2 Crab Shells every time.
  • Killable by: Solo at level 50-55, level 40 Beastmaster.
  • One placeholder Clipper will appear in one of the two ponds on either side of the river. The Clippers respawn five game hours after the last one died. That statement is false. I'm not sure on the exact repop time, I've tried to time it, and sometimes the PH repops after 5 min and sometimes it takes 16. It doesn't seem to be a set repop time like it should be.
  • Cargo Crab Colin will pop on or near the bridge over the river. He also seems to appear slightly smaller than the regular crabs.
