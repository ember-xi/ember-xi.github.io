Runic Portal Use Permit

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Necessary to allow teleporting from the Runic Portal in the Chamber of Passage to a Staging Point for which you do not have an Assault Order.
Description
This permit authorizes the use of the runic portals in the Chamber of Passage.
How to obtain
You can buy it for 200 Imperial Standing from Sharin-Garin at (L-8) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate. Talk to him and choose "Using Runic Portals." then "Yes (200 points)."
Note
This is a single-use permit; a new permit must be purchased for each teleport.
