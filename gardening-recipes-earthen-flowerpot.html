Gardening Recipes/Earthen Flowerpot

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Placing an Earthen Flowerpot in your moghouse is a sign of a green thumbed Windurstian. A true gardener on the other hand will use all types of pots in his craft.


Following are the possible results when planting seeds in an Earthen Flowerpot. Results will vary depending on many variables, the following chart will show the commonly accepted "recipes". For now, the list is by no means complete, so please add any results you find yourself.
