Statistics

Daedalus Wing Temporary
When used, these man-made wings
instantly raise TP.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Medicine Effects:

  • Instantly increases TP by 100%

Other Uses

Used in Quest: None

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Besieged

  • Temporary Obtained if Kahah Hobichai is in Al Zahbi at the start of a Besieged.

Quests

  • Temporary Scouting the Ashu Talif

Ballista

  • Temporary Quarried in Ballista.
