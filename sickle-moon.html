Sickle Moon

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Great Sword weapon skill

  • Skill level: 200 (Paladin, Dark Knight only).
  • Delivers a two-hit attack. Damage varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Scission/Impaction
  • Modifiers: STR:20% ; AGI:20%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.50 2.00 2.75

Levels

  • Sickle Moon is only available to Dark Knights and Paladins. While other jobs can obtain the required 200 Great Sword skill, they will be unable to use Sickle Moon.
  • Great Sword skill level 200 is obtainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
Job Rating Level
Dark Knight A- 60
Warrior B+ 62
Paladin B 62
