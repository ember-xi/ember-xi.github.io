Zonpa-Zippa

From Zenith XI Wiki

A former Manustery minister and the creator of Windurst’s Cardians.

The present-day character appears in cutscenes during Doll of the Dead (Windurst 9-1), involving The Boyahda Tree. Follow that mission’s walkthrough; there is no ordinary town NPC to visit under this name. The Campaign-era character is a separate entry, Zonpa-Zippa (S).
