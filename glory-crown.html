Statistics

Glory Crown
[Head] All Races
A dark orichalcum crown set with
glittering painites. The message
"Gifted to the champion of Aht Urhgan
by Her Magnificence, Nashmeira II" is
inscribed on the inside of the crown.
Lv. 75 All Jobs

Other Uses

Used in Quest: Unknown

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Reward for completing the final ToAU mission

You earn this item from ToAU Mission 17, only to have it stolen from you in the same cutscene minutes later. You get it returned in ToAU Mission 48 as well as an itemized list of how much you owe the company.
