EmberXI keeps retail rules where they matter and removes the waiting. This page lists every rule that is different from retail, taken directly from the server settings and scripts.

Starting Out

  • New characters start with 5,000 gil and the level cap at 50.
  • Every new adventurer receives Apururu (UC) with the first trust from the tutorial: a healer from day one.
  • All four Mog House exits are open from the start.
  • Subjob quests from level 18; advanced job quests from level 30.
  • No trust permit is needed: any cipher you find can be used at once.

Levelling

  • Monster EXP ×2, quest EXP ×2.5, Fields of Valor / Grounds of Valor pages ×3, Records of Eminence ×3 (no weekly spark limit).
  • Skill-up chance ×3 for weapons, magic and crafting.
  • Level sync is on.
  • Limit Break: the retail quests with the item hunts removed.
  • At level 65 you receive the Dynamis pass (Shrouded Sand + Prismatic Hourglass) automatically. At 75 the Limit Breaker key item opens the merit menu.

Travel

  • Touch one Home Point crystal and every era Home Point unlocks. Same for Survival Guides.
  • The Runic Portal in Whitegate opens to everyone with all six staging points, no permit and nothing consumed.
  • The chocobo license quest gives you a Chocobo Whistle; Mapitoto in Upper Jeuno registers your chocobo and hands over the Trainer's Whistle and a Raptor. Mount notebooks cost 1,000 sparks at the Mount Trader.
  • The Mhaura ferry works on fixed windows (Selbina at hour 0, Al Zahbi at hour 4 of each cycle).

Battlefields

  • Every BCNM, ENM, KSNM and mission battlefield allows Trusts and six entrants.
  • Domenic in Lower Jeuno opens the BCNM/KSNM fights to everyone (no Beyond Infinity requirement).
  • Shami sells orbs for gil (five a day) next to the retail seal trade.

Combat tweaks

  • Resting MP: 12, then +4 per tick (+3 WHM, +2 BLM/SMN, +1 RDM at 75).
  • Magic Burst: +35 Magic Accuracy and +35 Magic Attack on normal monsters (not NMs).
  • Poison ticks for at least 4; Burn/Frost/Choke/Rasp/Shock/Drown last 120 s.
  • Chakra always clears the common ailments; Focus and Dodge last 3 minutes; Mug gives +5% crit on the target; Perfect Dodge +999 accuracy; Refueling 15%.
  • Goblin Bomb Toss is capped at 10 × the goblin's level.
  • Summoner: perpetuation relief −1 at 15 up to −5 at 75; see Summoner.

Login campaign

The login campaign never ends: every day pays points, and the shop includes ciphers (Zeid, Lion, Najelith) and mounts (Tiger, Bomb, Crawler, Red Crab, Fenrir, Omega).
