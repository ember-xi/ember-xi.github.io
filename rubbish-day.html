Walkthrough

  • After completing the Collect Tarut Cards quest, have Chululu test your compatability on three different days (don't have to wait an hour, just until the next day). The fourth time you speak to her, she will give you this quest.

-Currently on Eden, she DOES NOT make you wait the 3 game days, this may change in the future

  • Chululu asks you to take out the trash and will give you the Magic Trash (key item).
  • The Magic Trash needs to be taken to the Incinerator in Garlaige Citadel.
  • To get to the Incinerator, each person that needs to do the quest needs a Garlaige Key (Rare/Ex), which is dropped by Fallen Evacuee in Garlaige Citadel. You can find them in the smaller rooms on the first map. You cannot use a skeleton key as a thief to pick the lock.
  • After getting the key, proceed past the First Banishing Gate at I-9.
  • Next proceed through the Second Banishing Gate which is at G-10.
  • After you get through the 2nd Banishing Gate, go to I-7 on the map to the right to get to the Incinerator Room.
  • Speak with Mashira who will dump your trash for you.
  • Return to Chululu for your reward.
Note:
Consider completing the quest Making Amens! at the same time.
