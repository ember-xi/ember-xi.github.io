Walkthrough

  • Talk to Bluffnix in order to trigger the quest and see if you can actually get it.
  • Trade the Tiger Leather, Painite, Velvet Cloth, and Gold Ingot to him (these items can all be found at the AH and/or crafted).
  • He will upgrade your Gobbiebag from 40 spaces to 45!

Game Description

Client: Bluffnix (Muckvix's Junk Shop, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

Bluffnix promises to enlarge your pack even more if you bring him the following: some tiger leather, a gold ingot, a square of velvet cloth, and a painite.
