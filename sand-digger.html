Note

  • Sand Diggers can be found on map 7 in Quicksand Caves. On map 4 use exit D which requires the use of the weighted switch. On map 7 after another weighted switch drop down the hole at (I-8). 1 Galka, 2 hume/mithra/elvaan or 3 tarutaru will activate the switch. An Elvaan/Taru combo will also work. The correct zone from Western Altepa Desert is entrance 4 on this map.
    • To get to entrance 4 you have to take the path on the far left of the map. The area is hidden on the in-game map.
