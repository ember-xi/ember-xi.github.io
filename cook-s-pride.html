Walkthrough

  • Naruru is looking for a new Super Soup Pot and asks you to go to Windurst Waters and get a new one for her at the Culinarian's Guild.
  • Head to Windurst Waters and talk to Maysoon to complete the Hoist the Jelly, Roger quest and obtain a Super Soup Pot .

Game Description

Client: Naruru (Merchant's House, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

Naruru can't find her favorite cooking pot. Get her a new one from the Windurst Culinarians' Guild.
