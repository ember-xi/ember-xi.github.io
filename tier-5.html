Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Beastmen tend to be grouped into tiers of 10 levels each, with certain kinds of beastmen within those tiers.

Tier 5 is for beastmen whose levels range from 41 to 50. A number of these supply chest keys for their respective strongholds. Mythril Beastcoins can be stolen from the majority of these beastmen.
