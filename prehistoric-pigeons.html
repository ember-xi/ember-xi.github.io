Notes

  • All four use Greater Bird and Soul Voice special attacks.
  • When one is killed, the remaining ones are made more powerful. After two were killed, the remaining two both began to hit much faster (seemed to have NO delay), harder, and took less damage. Nin tanks not recommended.
  • All are susceptible to Sleep, Lullaby, and Bind. Resisted sleep (except with ES) after two of the birds were killed.
  • Can use 2 hour more than once.
  • Builds resistance to sleep quickly. Was only able to sleep them twice as BLM75/WHM37, before it was completely resisted all times except with elemental seal. Strategy was to weaken each one to 10% then finish all at once, yet without being able to sleep them it did not work well.
