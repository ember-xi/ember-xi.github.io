Lunar Cry

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Fenrir inflicts accuracy down and evasion down on target.

Technicals

Avatar: Fenrir (Dark)

Blood Pact: Ward

Cost: 41 MP

Level: 21

Description

Effect Duration: Unknown

Battle Application: N/A

Damage Type: N/A

Additional Notes:

  • Target's combined accuracy and evasion down will equal -32, the ratio of (-Accuracy):(-Evasion) varies depending on moon phase.
  • Full Moon indicates largest Accuracy reduction. New moon indicates largest Evasion reduction.

Macro Syntax

  • /pet "Lunar Cry" <t>

or

  • /ja "Lunar Cry" <t>
