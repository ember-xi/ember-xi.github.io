Requirements

This is one of the few quests that involve the Chateau d'Oraguille that is connected to Northern San d'Oria. In order to get into the Chateau d'Oraguille you must have either San d'Oria Rank:2 or be on Bastok Mission 2-3 or Windurst Mission 2-3 otherwise you will be unable to zone in and the guards outside will be quite rude to you. But once you have achieved one of the previous status in Rank and are able to get in you must also have San d'Oria Reputation 4 in order to actually flag this quest from Halver.

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Halver for a cutscene where King Destin desires Halver to find a suitable bride-to-be for Prince Trion. After consulting Prince Pieuje, Halver is sent off with a certain description of woman (face and race e.g.: mithra with wild red hair flowing over a headband - Mithra 6B of the character creation tool) that Trion prefers. Halver requests that you bring this person to him.
  • Find an adventurer with the description that the King gives you, group with them, and return for another cutscene.
  • You are rewarded with the Castor's Ring and the title "Royal Wedding Planner", and the fitting adventurer you brought to Halver is given Pollux's Ring and the title "Consort Candidate".
Note
  • The quality on these rings can be removed by synthing them with a Wind Crystal, and they can be signed by using a Cyclone Crystal. You don't get skillups by synthing those and there is no craft requirement. It's told that's a level 0 synth with 100% success rate.
  • You can cancel the quest and can reobtain the quest with a 99% chance of getting another description of a female adventurer. So when you can't find this specific char, cancel and get a new description.
  • After cancelling, you must wait until the next game day (after 24:00 on the clock) to restart the quest.
