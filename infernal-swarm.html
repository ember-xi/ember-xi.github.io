Notes

  • All bees have Pollen and Sharp Sting. Beelzebub's Pollen can heal for a couple thousand HP.
  • Beelzebub has Benediction and casts Cure V, Silencega and Paralyga.
  • Hell Fly can be charmed and slept, Beelzebub has a higher resistance to sleep.
