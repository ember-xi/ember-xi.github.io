Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Maelstrom: AoE water attack, added effect of Strength down
Whirlwind: AoE wind attack, added effect of Vitality down
Ink Jet: Cone Attack damage and Blind
Tentacle: Single target attack
Cross Attack: Single target attack, 2-hits
Regeneration: Regen
Hard Membrane: Evasion Boost
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
