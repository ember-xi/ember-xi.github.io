Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Katana weapon skill

  • Skill level: 225
  • Delivers a single-hit attack. Damage varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Gravitation
  • Modifiers: STR:30% ; DEX:30%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
2.50 2.75 3.00

Translation

天 is the kanji used for Blade: Ten, it can mean "sky", "the heavens", or "Heaven". Thus this could be "Blade: Heaven."
