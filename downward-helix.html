Before you start

Scholar level 50+; On Sabbatical complete. Zone and wait for the next Vana'diel day.

Steps

  1. Speak to Erlene in The Eldieme Necropolis (S) (J-8) as Scholar.
  2. Walk into Southern San d'Oria (S) from East Ronfaure (S) to trigger the scene. Teleporting directly into town does not replace this entrance.
  3. Return to Erlene and speak to her.
  4. Walk into Sauromugue Champaign (S) from Rolanberry Fields (S) for the next scene.
  5. Check Indescript Markings at the southeast edge of (J-7) for Ulbrecht's Mortarboard. If hidden by dusty weather, return when it clears.
  6. Return to Erlene for your bracers. Visit Loussaire for feet, legs and body; Seeing Blood-red is the headpiece quest.

Reward

Scholar's Bracers; unlocks Loussaire's three armor commissions.
