Walkthrough

  • Talk to Kubhe for a cutscene.
  • Exit from Aht Urhgan Whitegate to Wajaom Woodlands through the iron gate at (H-10), receive a cutscene, and return to Whitegate.
  • Speak to Kubhe for another cutscene.
  • Talk to Ratihb at the Shararat Teahouse for a cutscene.
  • Talk to Tehf Kimasnahya at (F-8) for a cutscene.
  • Return to Kubhe for a final cutscene and a reward of 1 bronze piece.

Game Description

Client: Kubhe Ijyuhla (Serpentking Square, Aht Urhgan Whitegate)
Summary:

The three volunteer soldiers Foudeel, Wahboud, and Bashraf have been acting strange lately. To help Kubhe find out what is really going on, you must first travel to the Wajaom Woodlands and train with Foudeel.
