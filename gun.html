Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Marksmanship is the skill involved in ranged attacks executed with crossbows and guns. Crossbows deal less damage than guns, but have the utility of being able to fire various Additional Effect bolts. Guns are high-damage high-delay weapons capable of incredible damage output.
