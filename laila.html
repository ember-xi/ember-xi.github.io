Laila

From Zenith XI Wiki

Leader of Troupe Brilioth and the starting contact for Dancer.

Contents
  1. Location
  2. Unlock Dancer
  3. Artifact armor
  4. Sources

Location

Upper Jeuno (G-7), beside Rhea Myuliah and Olgald.

Unlock Dancer

  1. Speak with her on a job of level 30 or above. Choose “I surely do.”, then “Never!” to start Lakeside Minuet.
  2. Talk with Rhea Myuliah next to her and follow the quest’s tavern and past-Jugner steps.
  3. Return to Laila after obtaining the Stardust Pebble to unlock Dancer.

Artifact armor

Return as Dancer for The Unfinished Waltz, then The Road to Divadom and Comeback Queen. The linked guides give the level gates, fights and rewards.
