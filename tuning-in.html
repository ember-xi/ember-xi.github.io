Walkthrough

  • Leepe-Hoppe requires three items:
  • Magicked Steel Ingots drop from Bugbear Servingmen, Bugbear Bondmen, Goblin Hammermen, Goblin Leadmen, Goblin Oilmen, and Goblin Tollmen in Oldton Movalpolos. It also drops from Bugbear Deathsmen and Bugbear Trashmen in Newton Movalpolos. A fairly common drop.
  • Spruce Lumber drops from Tauri in Phomiuna Aqueducts.
  • These check as Decent Challenge when you are capped at level 40.
  • Extra-fine Files drop from Fomor Warrior, Fomor Samurai, and Fomor Monk that spawn in the watchtowers and around the campfires at night (20:00-04:00) in Lufaise Meadows.
  • These spawn points are at (G-6), (H-8), (J-6), and (K-7).
  • Note: Fomors travel in parties, so all Fomors in the party will aggro if one of them aggros or is attacked.

Game Description

Client: Leepe-Hoppe (Rhinostery, Windurst Waters)
Summary:

A Mithran Tracker has demanded the construction of a new type of tuning fork so she can converse with the sleeping gods. Help Leepe-Hoppe gather the necessary materials.
