Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Defense Boost is a beneficial status effect that boosts defense until it wears off or is removed. Benefiting characters gain an increase to defense.

How to remove the effect

  • This effect can be dispelled.
  • This effect can be removed manually.
  • Acid Bolts replace this effect with their Defense Down effect, at least on Crabs and Crawlers.

How the effect is inflicted/gained

Spells

  • Blue Magic - Cocoon
  • Blue Magic - Reactor Cool

Job Abilities

  • Paladin - Rampart
  • Warrior - Defender
