Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Large dragonflies that are pervasive in any place with ample enough water to support them, these flies have developed a number of abilities, including a potent natural poison and a crude control of the water element. Their massive wings can be used by crafters to create various items, and the flies of Valkurm Dunes are sometimes hunted as a test for low-level adventurers looking for the parasitic Damselfly Worm for their sub-job quest.
