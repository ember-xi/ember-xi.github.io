Notes:

  • Respawn around (G-6) underground approximately every 21-24 hours.
  • Killable by: 18 characters level 65, 8-10 characters level 70, 6 characters level 75; Ninja or 74+ WAR/NIN tank recommended
  • Special Attacks: Draw In, Bad Breath, Sweet Breath, Impale, Drain Whip.
    • Bad Breath and Sweet Breath have an AOE range of 50'.
    • Impale resets the target's hate and inflicts severe Poison. Cannot be blinked by Utsusemi.
    • Drain Whip absorbs either HP, MP, or TP. Cannot be blinked by Utsusemi.
    • Draw In only occurs when Cassie's target is in a hallway
  • Sleepable using Sleep Bolts
