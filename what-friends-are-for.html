Walkthrough

  • Optional: Visit Nashmau and talk to Tsetseroon (G-6). No cutscene, he just says he's lonely.
  • Visit Aydeewa Subterrane (Map 3 G-10).
    • The easiest way to get to Map 3 (G-10) is to enter Aydeewa Subterrane from Wajaom Woodlands entrance (E-10)(marked as entrance 8 on the map).
      • Once inside follow the path south (do not drop off the ledge) until you come to a down slope with some worms. (You come to a T-junction where the path slopes down to the right or continues on, this is where you turn.) Take the right, then your next left. You will eventually come out into a large room.
  • Run through into the middle of the room and approach the stairs ruins (G-10) for a very short cutscene with NPC Wawaroon.
    • The cutscene will start before you reach the base of the stairs.
    • >>WARNING!!<< The cutscene WILL drop your Invisible effect, the moment the cutscene ends the chigoes nearby might aggro you. They attack incredibly fast, and will kill you very quickly if unprepared. Recommend Blink and Stoneskin (if possible) before entering the room with the stairs.
  • Return to Nashmau and talk to Tsetseroon again for a cutscene which flags the quest.
    • Trade him a Tin Ore and Cobalt Jellyfish to receive the Pot of Tsetseroon's Stew (Key Item).
  • Go back to Aydeewa Subterrane (Map 3 G-10) and check the ??? on the stairs for another cutscene with NPC Wawaroon.
    • NOTE: If you stand on the steps above the ??? before you drop Invisible, there's a good chance you won't aggro any chigoes.
  • After cutscene, you are given two options to examine Wawaroon's bag:
    • If you choose "I am no thief", you get nothing.
    • If you choose "Yes", you obtain the Map of Aydeewa Subterrane.
  • Return back to Nashmau and talk to Tsetseroon again for a final cutscene and reward.
    • If you chose "I am no thief" earlier, you obtain the Map of Aydeewa Subterrane.
    • If you chose "Yes" earlier, you obtain an Imperial Bronze Piece.
  • Note* The (E-10) Entrance is the easiest and fastest to use.

Game Description

Client: Tsetseroon (Junk Seller, Nashmau)
Summary:

Tsetseroon needs a cobalt jellyfish and a chunk of tin ore to make his special stew.
