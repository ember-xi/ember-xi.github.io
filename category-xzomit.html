General Information

  • System: Lumorians
  • Weaknesses:
  • Immune:
  • Often referred to as "squid".
  • They spawn in groups, with a large one leading some smaller ones. Follower Xzomit link with their leader anywhere in the zone, but do not link with other Xzomit. The large one in these packs can be aspired, but the smaller ones have no MP. These "leaders" also have 100% drop rate on Xzomit Organ.
  • Naturally Enhanced Movement Speed
