Notes

  • For reference, Gilagoge is the Spring Treant, Goga the Summer Treant, Ulagohvsdi the Autumn Treant, Gola the Winter Treant.
  • All use Treant special attacks.
  • They do not link when one is pulled.
  • When one uses it's 2 Hour ability, the others wake, in the order of: Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter.
  • All are susceptible to Bind, Sleep and Gravity, are extremely resistant to Lullaby, and immune to Charm. Resistance to bind, sleep, and gravity builds fast with repeated castings.
  • Gilagoge Tlugv uses only "Entangle" (cone Bind effect), Additional Effect: Dispel, and uses Benediction.
  • Goga Tlugvi uses only "Leafstorm" (AoE 2 shadow images or damage 600-900), Additional Effect: Stun, and uses 100 Fists.
  • Ulagohvsdi Tlugvi uses only "Pinecone Bomb (ranged single-target 1 shadow image or damage + sleep), Additional Effect: Paralyze, and uses Mijin Gakure.
  • Gola Tlugvi uses only "Drill Branch" (cone 2 shadow images or damage + blind), Additional Effect: Absorb TP, and uses Blood Weapon.
  • They definately build resistance to any elemental magic. An Elemental Seal Burst 2 did 0 dmg late in the fight
