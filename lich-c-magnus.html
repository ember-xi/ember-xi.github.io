Notes

  • As his name suggests, Lich C Magnus is significantly larger than regular Skeletons.
  • Spawned during the quest Blue Ribbon Blues by trading the Purple Ribbon to the Hume Bones at (M-10) of the second map.
  • There is about a 5 minute wait between spawns.
  • Is able to cast Ancient Magic, such as Quake.
