Combines With...

You can use Vertical Cleave as part of a combo to create Defense Bonus.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Defense Bonus combo.

  • Grand Slam
  • Terror Touch
  • Saline Coat

Notes

  • Slashing damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: STR: 50%.
  • Ignores shadows.
