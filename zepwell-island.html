Zepwell Island

From Zenith XI Wiki

The largest island in the Kuzotz region, southwest of the Quon continent.

Contents
  1. Areas
  2. Getting there
  3. Sources

Areas

  • Eastern Altepa Desert
  • Western Altepa Desert
  • Rabao
  • Quicksand Caves
  • Chamber of Oracles
  • Cloister of Tremors

Getting there

  1. From Bastok Mines, enter Zeruhn Mines.
  2. Take the entrance into Korroloka Tunnel.
  3. Follow the tunnel maps to its Eastern Altepa Desert exit.

Open the area pages for maps, connecting passages and quest locations. The island is part of Kuzotz; it is not a separate playable zone.
