Manustery

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

One of the five Ministries of Magic of Windurst. Located in the heart of Windurst Woods, the Manustery is the official department charged with the development of the Cardians ("Manustery" is a combination of the words "ministry" and "manual" -- working with the hands). Kopuro-Popuro, Kororo and Apururu work at the Manustery. The Manustery offers a number of quests:

  1. The All-New C-2000
  2. A Greeting Cardian
  3. Legendary Plan B
  4. The All-New C-3000
  5. Can Cardians Cry?
