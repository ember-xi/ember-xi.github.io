Rahal

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Quest NPC

Location: Chateau d'Oraguille (H-9) Royal Knight's Qtrs

Notes

  • Rahal S Lebrart is the leader of the Royal Knights. His past involves some shady characters, but his heart is pure. He is widely regarded for his knowledge of most things military (from the training of knights to ancient orders) and his awareness of the goings on within the nation's holdings.

  • Knight Stalker

Involved in Missions

  • San d'Oria Mission 8-2: Lightbringer
  • Aht Urhgan Mission 5: Confessions of Royalty

Involved in Quests

  • The Holy Crest
