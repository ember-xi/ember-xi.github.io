Convert MP to HP

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Summary

Convert MP to HP increases your maximum HP by a certain amount at the cost of an equal amount of maximum MP.

The key difference between this effect and equipment with MP -x HP +x is that the word 'converts' indicates the effect will not work if you have zero MP to begin with (i.e. Warriors, Monks, and other jobs with a zero MP total.
