Chocobo Egg

From Zenith XI Wiki

An egg used to begin raising your own chocobo.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. How to use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

  1. Check Dabih Jajalioh in Ru'Lude Gardens (F-9), down the steps near the Auction House. Her changing stock can include an egg for 1,040 gil.
  2. If no egg is offered, use the quest Chocobo on the Loose! or obtain one from chocobo breeding or an applicable Imperial Standing battlefield; see Chocobo Raising for those routes.

How to use

Trade the egg to a VCS trainer to start raising it. In Bastok, use Zopago in Bastok Mines (I-9). Return to that trainer for the care menu. Egg descriptions have several warmth variants.
