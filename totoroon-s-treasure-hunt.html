Walkthrough

  • Totoroon asks you to bring him "Sweets, with shooogary taste."
  • Trade Totoroon a Date, Pamamas or Kazham Pineapple.
  • He will send you to one of three places: Bhaflau Thickets, Wajaom Woodlands or Caedarva Mire.
    • You will be sent to Bhaflau Thickets or Wajaom Woodlands for trading Dates or Pamamas.
    • You will be sent to either Mount Zhayolm or Caedarva Mire for trading a Kazham Pineapple.

Bhaflau Thickets or Wajaom Woodlands

  • Find a harvesting spot in the appropriate zone.
  • You might harvest an item, or you might spawn the Berried Chigoe (NM) and some Chigoe's Nits.
  • Killing the NM is not required. But when you spawn the NM, it drops the quest item and the quest ends. You cant get any item by harvesting a 2nd time. Return to Totoroon who will ask for more sweets.

Caedarva Mire

  • If you are sent to Caedarva Mire, you will need to find a logging point.
  • You might log an appraisal item, or you might spawn Ravin Raven.

Mount Zhayolm

  • In Mount Zhayolm, you will need to find a mining point and use a Pickaxe to mine there.
  • You may spawn an Ancient Bombs (drops currently unknown), by doing so.

Game Description

Client: Totoroon (G-8, Nashmau)
Summary:

Totoroon has found a "soooper-doooper" treasure. He will tell you where it is hidden in exchange for something sweet.
