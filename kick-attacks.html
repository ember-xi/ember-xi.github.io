Kick Attacks

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Occasionally adds kick attacks.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • This trait is only active using hand-to-hand.
    • Occasionally adds an additional kick attack each combat round.
    • Kick Attacks II is thought to have a base rate of 10%; that is, for each combat round, there is a 10% chance of a Kick Attack occuring.
    • Merit upgrades of this trait affect the frequency of activation, at a rate of 1% per point added.
    • Equipment with the effect of "Kick Attacks +" is generally believed to increase the rate of kicks by that many percentage (i.e. +5 returns a 5% increase), although there is some evidence to suggest that a lower percentage is returned.
    • Equipment with the effect of "Kick Attacks +" will grant the trait if the job wearing the equipment doesn't already have the trait.
    • Kick damage is equal to base Hand to Hand damage (the damage of a punch with no weapon equipped). Damage can be increased by equipping footwear with the hidden "Kick Damage" effect, such as Kung Fu Shoes, Wulong Shoes, and Dune Boots.
