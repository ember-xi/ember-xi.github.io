Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Occasionally allows ranged attacks without consuming ammunition.
  • Job Traits are always active.


Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Scout's Beret
  • Scout's Beret +1
  • Volunteer's Earring
  • Kappa Necklace

Food that Enhances this Trait

Recycle

  • Obtained: Ranger Level 75 Merit Group 2

Notes

  • Each merit increases activation rate by 5%.
  • Does stack with Scout's Beret
