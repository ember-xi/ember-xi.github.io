Description

The Hohbiba-Mubiba's Wands of Wonder is a place to buy early level weapons. Hohbiba-Mubiba and Taniko-Maniko are on duty and sell more then just wands.

Location: Port Windurst H-8/9Shop type: Weapon Vendor
