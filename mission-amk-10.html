Zenith availability

The reviewed mission file does not establish this complete battlefield. Victory and progression on Zenith have not yet been confirmed.

Before you begin

  • Complete AMK 9.
  • Navaratna Talisman.

Starting point

Shimmering Circle · Chamber of Oracles

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. After the introductory scene, examine the Shimmering Circle again with the Navaratna Talisman to enter the battlefield.
  2. Control the three Goblin attendants while focusing on Nanaa Mihgo. Sleep can control the Goblins in the reference encounter.
  3. Prepare for Nanaa to call Bopa Greso and Cha Lebagta during the battle. Keep vulnerable party members out of her frontal attacks and maintain status-removal support.
  4. Defeat Nanaa to win, then watch the concluding scene and return to Upper Jeuno.

Completion and reward

Red coral key and story progression.
