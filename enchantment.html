Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

File:Enchantment (Status Effect).png Enchantments are a beneficial status effects that imbue the user of an item until it wears off or is removed. Benefitting characters gain various effects, depending on the source of their enchantment. The enchantment effect is separate from other status effects gained from enchanted items. A quick example: a Blink Band gives the user the effect of Shadow Images, but a Breath Mantle, when activated, gives the user an Enchantment Effect (HP +18 Enmity +3). Since those are different status effects, they will stack.

How to remove the effect

  • The duration of enchantments (non-instantaneous) are usually the same as their recast timer. (Mostly 30 minutes)
  • Some enchantments granted by equipment can be ended by removing the source piece of equipment, or by zoning.

How the effect is inflicted/gained

Items

  • Breath Mantle
  • High Breath Mantle
  • High Mana Wand
  • Mana Wand
