Contagion Transfer

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

An attack or ability that removes negative status effects and transfers them to the target(s). When used by a Giant Moa, it can also absorb negative status effects, confirmed with avatars. It can even absorb petrification from an avatar.


Monster Abilities

  • Giant Moa

Equipment with this Effect/Enchantment

  • Absorbing Shield
