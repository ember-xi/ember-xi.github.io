Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Description

Abiltiy Up is a beneficial status effect that raises an ability until it wears off or is removed. Benefiting characters can be affected by one Ability up effect per ability. The amount the ability is raised by is determined by the strength of the spell/ability that granted it.

How to remove the effect

  • These effects can be dispelled.
  • These effects can be removed manually.

How the effect is inflicted/gained

Spells

  • Black Magic - Absorb-STR, Absorb-DEX, Absorb-VIT, Absorb-AGI, Absorb-INT, Absorb-MND, Absorb-CHR.

Blood Pacts

  • Carbuncle - Glittering Ruby
  • Fenrir - Ecliptic Growl

Medicine

  • Agility Potion
  • Braver's Drink
  • Charisma Potion
  • Dexterity Potion
  • Intelligence Potion
  • Mind Potion
  • Strength Potion
  • Vitality Potion

Additional Effect

  • Power Staff - STR boost

Monster Abilities

  • Dhalmel - Whistle (AGI boost)
