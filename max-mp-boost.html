Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Increases maximum Magic Points.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Using a Stat Calculator with the all five races as a Summoner with no subjob, the increases per boosts were determined. All MP Boost traits add an additional 10 MP.
    • Review Discussion page for working studies

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Puppetry Tobe (Automaton: Max MP Boost)
