Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Sacrifices HP to damage enemies within range. Affects caster with Weakness.

Description

Spell cost: 100 MP
Ecosystem: Arcana
Type: Magical (Fire)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: STR+2
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 50

Timers

Casting Time: 3.25 seconds
Recast Time: 21 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Self-Destruct as part of a combo to create Auto Refresh.

Can be paired with some of the following spells to create the Auto Refresh trait. Please refer to the Auto Refresh section of the Blue Mage Job Traits page for details.

  • Stinking Gas
  • Cold Wave
  • Frightful Roar
  • Light of Penance
  • Voracious Trunk
  • Actinic Burst
  • Plasma Charge

Notes

  • Damage is based on total HP.
  • Reduce player HP to 1 point, and generate a average amount of hate.
  • Damage is not constant like the breath spells.
  • Weakness lasts as long as regular weakness from Raise.
  • Magic Attack Bonus does not seem to affect damage dealt. Verification Needed

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Liquefaction, Fusion, and Light.
