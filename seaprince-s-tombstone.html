Seaprince's Tombstone

From Zenith XI Wiki

Mythic trial and weapon hand-ins at the cemetery in Caedarva Mire (E-10).

Contents
  1. Route
  2. Hand-ins
  3. Sources

Route

Leave Nashmau through the west exit and travel to the cemetery.

Hand-ins

During Forging a New Myth, trade Tinnin's Fang, Sarameya's Hide, Tyger's Tail together for the battlefield entry key item. After winning, follow Coming Full Circle to trade and awaken the weapon.
