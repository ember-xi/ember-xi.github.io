Description

This shop sells a wide variety of lower level weaponry. Although their blades do not display the highest quality blacksmithing, still the steel is reliable, and many less experienced adventurers rely on Helbort's for their weapons.

Location: Southern San d'Oria K-7

Shop type: Weapons & Ammunition
