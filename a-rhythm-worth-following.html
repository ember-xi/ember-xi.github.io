Requirements

Main level 40+ and a Trust permit from any starting city.

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Gondebaud · Southern San d’Oria (L-6). Trust representative. Accept A Rhythm Worth Following.
  2. Go to Qufim Island outpost (F-6). Summon your Trusts before examining ???. Choose Wayward Weapon.
  3. Defeat Wayward Weapon within 10 minutes. It scales with your level, minimum 42. Stay alive, within 50 yalms and on the same main job used to start the fight.
  4. Return to Gondebaud · Southern San d’Oria (L-6). Trust representative. Collect your Trust reward.

Reward

Permanent Trust spell: Uka Totlihn. Learned directly, once per character. After acquisition, the companion can be used on your other jobs under normal Trust rules.

Retry

Losing does not erase acceptance. Return to the same ??? once the previous encounter clears and try again.
