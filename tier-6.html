Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Beastmen tend to be grouped into tiers of 10 levels each, with certain kinds of beastmen within those tiers.

Tier 6 is for beastmen whose levels range from 51 to 60. These are generally only found in strongholds, and those in Outland dungeons may drop chest/coffer keys. Mythril Beastcoins can be stolen from the majority of these beastmen.
