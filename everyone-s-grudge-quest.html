Everyone's Grudge (Quest)

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Magephaud - Norg I-8
  Requirements:
   Must have killed at least one Tonberry before.
  Reputation level 2
  Items Needed:   Gold Beastcoin x 3
  Key Item: Tonberry Key
  Title:   Honorary Doctorate, Majoring in Tonberries
  Repeatable:   Yes
  Reward:   Tonberry Hate Reset to 0
  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   None

Walkthrough

Not repeatable Part

  • Talk to Magephaud. He will offer to spill the beans about the dangerous Tonberry attack "Everyone's Grudge" if you trade him 3 Gold Beastcoins.
  • Gold Beastcoins drop off various high level Beastmen. Alternatively, you can buy them at the Auction House under Others -> Beast-made.
  • Trade your 3 Gold Beastcoins to Magephaud. He will give you the Key Item: Tonberry Key.

Repeatable Part

  • Go to the Temple of Uggalepih and go to (F-11).
  • Open the door with the key from Magephaud.
  • Talking to the Priest inside gives you the option of resetting your accumulated tonberry hate to zero for a price and thus minimizing the damage done by "Everyone's Grudge". The amount of gil needed to reset hate increases with the more hate you have. There is a hard cap of 6000g if you've accumulated a lot of hate. Verification Needed
  • You can come back here anytime without paying 3 Gold Beastcoins to Magephaud again because you keep the Key Item: Tonberry Key.

Game Description

Client:

Magephaud (Next to the central staircase, Norg)

Summary:

Bring back three gold beastcoins if you want to learn how to rid yourself of the Tonberries' rancor.
