Notes

  • To enter the battlefield, talk to Morangeart in the Tavnazian Safehold (G-10 on the main floor).
  • A character must have reached Chains of Promathia mission, chapter 2-5 or above, to receive access into Monarch Linn.
  • Morangeart will give you the Monarch Beard key item.
  • Proceed through Riverne - Site A01 or Riverne - Site B01 to Monarch Linn.
  • You will be able to choose one of the four ENM battles at the battlefield. Only one of the four battlefields in Monarch Linn can be completed every 5 Earth days.
  • Only the Watch Hippogryph must be defeated to win the ENM.
  • ¹A Guard Hippogryph will appear periodically (after every 25% HP of the Watch Hippogryph.) It has relatively low HP, thus a decent strategy to use is to save TP and immediately use weaponskills on the Guard Hippogryphs when they appear.
  • Both Hippogryphs use standard Hippogryph special attacks, are susceptible to Gravity and Bind, but immune to Charm.
  • The Watch Hippogryph has Additional Effect: Stun.
  • The Watch Hippogryph has about 5000 HP.
  • The Watch Hippogryph has very high evasion.
  • The Guard Hippogryph has strongly linked hate with the Watch Hippogryph.
  • The Guard Hippogryph has Additional Effect: Drain.
  • The Guard Hippogryph was believed to have 600 HP, however, this is false; it has at least 900 HP.
