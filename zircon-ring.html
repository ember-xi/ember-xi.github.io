Zircon Ring

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-54 ring for all jobs: INT +3 and Ice resistance +7.

How to obtain

Goldsmithing 75: Earth Crystal + one Gold Ring + one Zircon. Zircon Ring is the ordinary result; high-quality results are Genius Ring or Genius Ring +1. A second recipe uses Gold Ring +1 at Goldsmithing 80.

Buy the finished ring under Auction House → Armor → Rings.
