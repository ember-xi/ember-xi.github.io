Zinc Ore

From Zenith XI Wiki

Crafting ore also required by several early quests.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Uses
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Farm Tunnel Worm or Stone Eater in the Gustaberg areas. Other options include mining in Zeruhn Mines, Palborough Mines and Gusgen Mines, or buying from the Auction House under Materials → Goldsmithing. Teerth in Bastok Markets also stocks it when available.

Uses

Used for Brass Ingot, Paktong Ingot and Zinc Oxide. Quest uses include Shady Business, Beauty and the Galka and The Medicine Woman.
