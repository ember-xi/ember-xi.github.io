Mom, the Adventurer?

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Nbu Latteh - Bastok Markets (J-9)
  Requirements:
   Bastok Reputation 1
  Items Needed:   Copper Ring
  Title:   Ringbearer
  Repeatable:   Yes: see below
  Reward:   100g / 200g

  Previous Quest:   None.
  Next Quest:   The Signpost Marks the Spot

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Nbu Latteh; she wants a Copper Ring for her daughter, Roh Latteh, but only has a Fire Crystal which she gives you so you can synthesize one.
  • Obtain a Copper Ring. (It is not necessary to synthesize one yourself.)
  • Trade the Copper Ring to Roh Latteh; she gives you a key item Letter from Roh Latteh.
  • Do not peek at the letter. If you do you only get half the reward.
  • Talk to Nbu Latteh again to complete the quest.

Notes

  • You will receive 100 gil if you examined the letter, or 200 gil if you did not.
  • The quest is repeatable - to a point. You can repeat it every time you zone, until you attain Bastok reputation 2, whereupon speaking to Nbu Latteh begins The Signpost Marks the Spot.

Game Description

Client: Nbu Latteh (Gold Street, Bastok Markets)
Summary:

Bring Nbu Latteh's daughter a copper ring as a gift from her mother.
