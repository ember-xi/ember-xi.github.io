Quartermaster

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A Quartermaster is a member of a Party designated by the leader to receive all item drops automatically. Items dropped by monsters at the end of each battle will bypass the normal Treasure Pool and be deposited directly in the Quartermaster's Inventory.

Conditions under which an item will not be placed directly in the Quartermaster's inventory include:

  • The item is Rare and the player designated as Quartermaster already has another of the same item in their Inventory, Mog Safe, Storage or Mog Locker.
  • The inventory of the player designated as Quartermaster is currently full.
  • The player designated as Quartermaster is currently in a different zone.
