General Information

  • System: Vermin
  • Weaknesses:
  • Immune:
  • Will link with full-grown wamoura by sound.

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Cannonball: Physical damage (Only used when curled up)
Thermal Pulse: Fire damage and blindness
Heat Barrier: Blaze Spikes (~50/hit) and Enfire
Vitriolic Spray: Damage and Burn Status 30hp/tick
Amber Scutum: Defense Boost
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.

Other

  • Periodically switches between curled-up and open forms, even when not engaged. When curled up, special attacks are limited to Cannonball and Heat Barrier.
  • Certain Wamouracampa in Mount Zhayolm will morph into full-grown wamoura once alive for an extended period of time.
