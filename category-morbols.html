Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Bad Breath: Cone Attack that inflicts many debilitating status effects, including Slow, Poison, Silence, Paralyze, Weight, Bind and Blind
Vampiric Root: Single-target Drain, blocked by Utsusemi; Drains beneficial status effects and transfer them to the enemy.
Sweet Breath: Cone Attack Sleep
Impale: Single-target damage added effect Paralyze
Vampiric Lash: Single-target Drain, blocked by Utsusemi
In ToAU areas, Vampiric Lash will remove all beneficial status effects and transfer them to the enemy.
Extremely Bad Breath: Instant K.O. to targets within range. (used only by Evil Oscar and Cirrate Christelle)
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
