Mission 8-2 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 8-1 Coming of Age.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Halver (also Rahal, Curilla and Milchupain).
  2. Go to the Temple of Uggalepih (Yhoator Jungle) and examine the ??? for the Piece of a Broken Key after beating the guards.
  3. Return to Halver for Rank 9.
Mechanics: Tonberries are level 60-70 and use Everyone's Grudge, so do not kill many before the end. Use Sneak and Invisible.
