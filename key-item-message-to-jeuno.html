Finish Magicite by reporting to Nelcabrit at the San d’Oria embassy in Ru’Lude Gardens.

What to do with it

Story letter carried into the next San d’Oria mission, The Ruins of Fei’Yin.

Mission walkthrough

Magicite

Trading and storage

This is a key item. Find it under Key Items, not your ordinary inventory. It cannot be bought at the Auction House, crafted, traded to another player or placed in a bazaar.
