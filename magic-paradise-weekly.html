Magic Paradise Weekly

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

The Magic Paradise Weekly is operated by the Tarutaru Times Company. It is the only source of news for the citizens of Windurst.

Known Members

  • Naiko-Paneiko, Southern Windurst Waters (C-11), Editor
  • Hiwon-Biwon, Windurst Walls (K-8), reporter
  • Kyume-Romeh, Northern Windurst Waters (F-10), ace reporter
  • Umumu, Windurst Woods (I-6) , reporter
  • Yujuju, Port Windurst (M-6), reporter
