Judgment

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Club weapon skill

  • Skill level: 200
  • Delivers a single-hit attack. Damage varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Impaction
  • Modifiers: STR:32% MND:32%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
2.00 2.50 4.00

Levels

  • Judgment is only natively available to Paladins, White Mages, Warriors, Dark Knights, Blue Mages and Samurai. While other jobs can obtain the required 200 Club skill, they must use one of the aforementioned jobs as their support job in order to gain access to Judgment.
