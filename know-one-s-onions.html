Walkthrough

  • NOTE** If I Can Hear a Rainbow (SMN job quest) is active you will be able to complete this quest without getting the cutscene at House of the Hero but without the cutscene you will be UNABLE to activate the next quest Inspector's Gadget!
  • Speak with Kohlo-Lakolo at G-5 in Port Windurst. (You do NOT need to wait until the next conquest update after completing Truth, Justice, and the Onion Way! before initializing this quest. However, you will need to change zones before you can be offered this quest.)
  • Trade Kohlo-Lakolo four Wild Onions; there will be a cutscene and then you will receive the Scroll of Blaze Spikes.
  • Head to Windurst Walls and click on the door to the House of the Hero for a cutscene.
  • Go back and talk to Kohlo-Lakolo to finish the quest.

Game Description

Client: Kohlo-Lakolo (Warehouses, Port Windurst)
Summary:

There are reports that the Cat Burglar has been loitering around the Ghostly House in Windurst Walls. This looks like a job for the Star Onion Brigade! Gather four wild onions so Kohlo-Lakolo and his gang can create the perfect trap!
