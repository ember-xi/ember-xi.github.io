How to obtain it

Full purchase walkthrough. Talk to the NPC and purchase it through the dialogue.

Use

Examine a closed intersection gate in The Eldieme Necropolis. The gate opens briefly while you retain the key item. No Auction House purchase, crafting recipe or monster drop is involved.
