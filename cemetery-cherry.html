Notes:

  • Title Obtained: Mon Cherry
  • Spawns around (H-8) on the far map approximately every 60-72 hours, after all the Cherry Saplings have been killed.
  • Killable by: 18 characters level 59-65.
  • About 37500 HP.
  • Builds resistance to Gravity quickly, and to Bind moderately.
  • Special Attacks: Blood Weapon, Sleepga II, Graviga, Paralyga, Draw In
    • Pinecone Bomb doesn't give Sleep, but does ~600 damage.
    • Entangle is damage, Poison and Bind Also resets hate.
    • Leafstorm Dispels all effects and gives Slow instead of damage.
  • If Cemetery Cherry despawns without getting killed the Cherry Saplings will respawn 30min-60min later.
