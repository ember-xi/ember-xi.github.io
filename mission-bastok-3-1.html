Before you begin

Be allied with Bastok, have Rank 3, and finish the preceding required story steps. Raise Rank Points if the mission is not offered.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission and speak with Iron Eater at the President’s Office in Metalworks (J-8).
  2. Enter Beadeaux for the scene. Defeat 20 Copper Quadav there while the mission is active.
  3. Leave through the Pashhow Marshlands zone line and see the completion scene with Ayame.

Important notes

  • If the completion scene does not play, check the kill requirement. Use the ordinary exit rather than relying on a teleport to trigger it.

Walkthrough

  • After accepting mission, head to Iron Eater in the President's Office.
  • While you were away, the Iron Musketeers entered both mines to start up production, but the beastmen initiated an unprovoked attack. Mining production had begun to slowly start up again, but going had been slow since the workers have encountered strange chambers filled with worthless rocks. After these chambers were cleared, apparently the insane beastmen threw themselves on the waiting swords of the Iron Musketeers with no regard for their lives. The outraged Minister has ordered all chambers cleared at once and all beastmen slaughtered.
  • The fanatic beastmen, however, have taken a toll on the guards and you are instructed to help the Iron Musketeers give Bastok more "living space". As a loyal citizen of Bastok, you are ordered to head to the main Quadav stronghold in Beadeaux.
  • Travel to Beadeaux and kill 20 Copper Quadav vermin total (not per member).
  • You don't need to kill them all at once. The game will keep track of the total you and your party have killed.
  • Once you have killed 20, leave Beadeaux and as you zone into Pashhow Marshlands you will receive a cutscene. This cutscene will have Ayame, Naji, Hani, and Dalzakk in it.
  • Do not warp or teleport out of Beadeaux or you won't get the cutscene.
  • If you do warp out, you can return to Beadeaux and you will get the cutscene.
  • NOTE: You will also get a cutscene if you zone and have not killed enough, but you will only see Naji. He will comment about both you and him not killing enough.
  • After the cutscene, you will have finished the mission.


Game Description

Mission Orders
You are to join a surprise attack on the Quadav stronghold in Beadeaux. See Iron Eater in the President's Office for details.
