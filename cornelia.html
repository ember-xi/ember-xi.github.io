Cornelia

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Cutscene NPC

Location: Various cutscenes

Her room is behind the President (Bastok Metalworks: (K-8)) through Door: President's Office

Notes

The 20-year-old daughter of the current President Karst of Bastok, and granddaughter of Senator Derek Karst. Her late mother named her after the Mythril Musketeer Cornelia in the hopes that she would treat everyone equally and be loved by all.

  • Bastok Mission 5-1: Darkness Rising

  • Promathia Mission 3-1: Call of the Wyrmking
