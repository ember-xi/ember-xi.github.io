Chain Affinity

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Makes it possible for your next "physical" blue magic spell to be used in a skillchain. Effect varies with TP.
  • If a physical blue magic spell is cast while the Chain Affinity effect is active, all of the user's TP is consumed.
  • If a physical blue magic spell is not cast while the Chain Affinity effect is active, no TP is consumed.
  • Doubles the WSC of the Physical Blue Magic.
  • Obtained: Blue Mage Level 40
  • Recast Time: 2 minutes
  • Duration: 30 seconds
  • Does stack with both Sneak Attack and Trick Attack.

Also see: Blue Renkei
