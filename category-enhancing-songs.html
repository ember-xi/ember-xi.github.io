Enhancing Songs

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

One of the two classifications of Bard Songs. These are used on party members to enhance their performance in some form. Usually AoE, but there are exceptions.
All enhancing songs have a base duration of 120 seconds (needs confirmation), which can be increased with instrumental enhancements.
See Also: Songs, Enfeebling Songs
