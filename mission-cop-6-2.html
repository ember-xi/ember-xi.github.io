People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Approach the Grand Duke Palace for the scene, then speak to Pherimociel in the guard room (G-6).
  2. Travel to the Dilapidated Gate in Misareaux Coast (I-11). Prepare before examining it: Warder Aglaia, Warder Euphrosyne and Warder Thalia appear together.
  3. Defeat all three Warders. Concentrate damage on one at a time and control the others; their synchronized area attacks can hit the whole group.
  4. Examine the same gate again to register the victory and watch the concluding scene.

Battle and progress notes

  • This is a field encounter. The mission checks all three deaths before the gate advances.

Walkthrough

  • Head to Ru'Lude Gardens and approach the Grand Duke Palace for a cutscene. Then talk to Pherimociel at (G-6).
  • Head to Misareaux Coast and check the Dilapidated Gate at (I-11) to spawn three Spheroids NMs - Warder Aglaia, Warder Euphrosyne and Warder Thalia.
    • They have two special attacks that they all use together. If one of them is asleep at the time, they will use it when they wake up.
      • Reactive Armor: Shock Spikes
      • Electromagnetic Field: AoE ~90 damage, strips Utsusemi.
    • Sleeping the Spheroids and picking them off one at a time is a proven strategy.
      • They will gradually build up a Sleep resistance.
    • These NMs are known to be soloable by a 75 DRK, MNK, SMN, DRG, THF, BLU, BLM, NIN or RDM, but it is suggested to do this fight with a healer and a sleeper. A RDM can easily solo these Spheroids without Utsusemi; Phalanx and Stoneskin should be enough.
    • The NMs are susceptible to Lullaby, but build up resistance fast. Each casting of Lullaby lasts 3 seconds less than the previous one, and eventually they just resist entirely.
  • When the NMs are defeated, check the gate again for another cutscene.
