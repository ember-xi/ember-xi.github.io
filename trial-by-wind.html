—The Moonlit Path

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Agado-Pugado in Rabao (G-9). He gives you the Tuning fork of wind.
    • Under 65 he gives the mini fork instead — trade it back to him and he teleports you to the Cloister.
  • Talk to him again while you hold the fork. You are sent straight to the Cloister of Gales.
    • The Cloister is in Cape Teriggan at (F-5). Route on foot: Western Altepa Desert → Kuftal Tunnel (enter at H-4) → Cape Teriggan. Cape Teriggan Home Point #1 is outside it.
  • Summon your Trusts and your avatar, then examine the Wind Protocrystal and pick Trial by Wind.
  • Defeat Garuda Prime.
    • Wind damage heals her: no Aero spells, no wind weapon skills.
    • She uses Claw, Predator Claws, Aerial Armor, Hastega and Aero IV, and at about 50% HP her Astral Flow pact Aerial Blast — heavy AoE wind damage.
    • Best day: Iceday. Avoid Windsday. Keep Baraera up.
    • Bring Shiva: ice beats wind, and Rush hits hardest.
  • You receive the Whisper of Gales.
    • Going for Fenrir? Keep it and do not take a reward yet — see The Moonlit Path.
  • Return to Agado-Pugado and choose your reward.
    • Repeatable once a day (after midnight JST).
    • At SMN 75 with Garuda already learned, winning teaches Wind Blade, her level 75 Blood Pact.

EmberXI changes

  • No Fame requirement.
  • Trusts can be summoned inside the battlefield; the Prime fight needs level 65+.
  • Talking to the NPC while you hold the fork teleports you to the Cloister.
  • Repeatable once a day.
  • Under 65, summoners get a solo trial-size fight (level 20 cap, Carbuncle only).
