Timers

Combines With...

You can use Eyes On Me as part of a combo to create Magic Attack Bonus.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Magic Attack Bonus combo.

  • Sound Blast
  • Cursed Sphere
  • Memento Mori
  • Heat Breath
  • Reactor Cool
  • Magic Hammer
