Notes:

  • Archaic Gear spawns with only 1 gear. Archaic Gears is a similar monster that spawns with 3 gears clustered in a single *
  • There are 10 "Gear" that spawn on the 4th floor, both east and west side. Each of these gear warp upon aggro or if not killed within 60 seconds of pulling. Killing these gears typically results in a chest with high end items (revitalizers, megaelixers, etc). Killing all 10 gears before they warp causes the zone boss Long Bowed Chariot to lose strenth in the form of a Homing Missile that does considerably less damage without the hate loss. Killing 9 gears makes the hate reset single target. Killing 8 or less seems to have no effect.
