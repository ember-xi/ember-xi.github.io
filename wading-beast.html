Walkthrough

  • This mission requires a certain level of Rank Bar. Complete some repeatable missions or trade 1 crystal to a Conquest Overseer. Talk to a Bastok Gate Guard to receive the mission.
  • After receiving the mission, head to the Dangruf Wadi.
  • Kill lizards there until they drop a Lizard Egg, or simply buy one from the auction house.
  • Trade the egg to Senator Alois outside the President's office in the Metalworks.


Game Description

Mission Orders
Procure a lizard egg from monsters in the Dangruf Wadi. Deliver the egg to Senator Alois in the President's Office.
