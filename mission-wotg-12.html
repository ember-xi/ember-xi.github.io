Before you begin

  • Complete A Spoonful of Sugar.

Starting point

Radford or Velda-Galda · Bastok Markets [S] · H-6

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Radford in Bastok Markets [S] (H-6).
  2. Speak with Velda-Galda in Windurst Waters [S] (K-9). Either NPC may be visited first, but both scenes are required.
  3. After the second conversation, verify that Count Borel's letter appears in your key items.

Completion and reward

Count Borel's letter.
