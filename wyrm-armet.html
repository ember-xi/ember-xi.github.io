Enhances effect of wyvern's breath

  • Increases healing power of Healing Breath to the equivalent of Cure IV.
  • Increases the formula for calculating Elemental Breath from (Current Wyvern HP * 0.182) to (Current Wyvern HP * 0.212) Roughly an 11.5% increase.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
