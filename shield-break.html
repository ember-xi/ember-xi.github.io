Shield Break

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Great Axe weapon skill

  • Skill level: 5
  • Lowers enemy's Evasion. Duration of effect varies with TP.
  • Exact amount of -Evasion has been determined as up to -40 when unresisted.
  • Will stack with Sneak Attack.

Properties

  • Element: Ice
  • Skillchain: Impaction
  • Strong against: Crabs, Crawlers, Mandragora, Tonberry, Yagudo, Pugils, Lizards, Flies, Opo-opo, Beetles, Birds, Bees, Sea Monks, Scorpions, Spiders, Sabotenders.
  • Immune: Skeletons, Gigas, Tigers, Sheep, Bombs, Ghosts.
  • Modifiers: STR:20% ; VIT:20%
