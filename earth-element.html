Earth (Element)

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

File:Earth icon.JPG The element of Earth is strong against Lightning and weak against Wind. Associated with Scission, Gravitation, and Dark skillchains. Governed by the celestial avatar Titan.
