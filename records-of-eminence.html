Records of Eminence

From Zenith XI Wiki

RoE offers training and progression objectives with EXP and Sparks rewards. It is separate from the Rhapsodies of Vana’diel story (RoV).

Getting started

  1. Open Quests → Records of Eminence → Tutorial → Basics.
  2. Activate First Step Forward and speak to a Sparks NPC. Complete the dialogue to receive the Memorandoll and reward.
  3. Choose objectives for your current activities: combat, crystals, training and regional enemies. Return to your tutorial NPC as their training requests advance.
