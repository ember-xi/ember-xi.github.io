Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Elemental magics focus the pure energy of the elements into a destructive force. All of these magics deal elemental damage to your opponents.

These spells come in two forms;

  • Direct Damage (DD)
Example: Thunder, Blizzaga II, Freeze.
  • Elemental Debuffs
Example: Shock, Frost, Choke.


Elemental Magic Skill lowers the resistance rate and spell interruption rate for Elemental Magic spells. It does not affect the damage of direct damage spells; see Calculating Magic Damage for the relevant formulas.
