The Henge

The Henge
WhereReisenjima Henge — from Halvard
TypeEmberXI dungeon #2, Omen-style floors
FormatOne pillar arena, five floors in a row, 45 minutes
RewardA level 75 Salvage piece from the Henge Coffer
TrustsAllowed
DeathNo XP loss inside

Five floors fought in the same arena, one after another. Clear a floor and the next one comes down. At the end the Henge Coffer opens with a Salvage piece.

Floors

FloorMonstersRule
1Sweetwater Tigers and FliesClear the floor
2Omen Tigerlord and its packClear the floor
3Omen Doomed and a skeleton wallKill the Doomed only — the skeletons are just a wall
4One of the Scales (Fu, Gin, Kei, Kin, Kyou or Ou) with its escortDrawn each run; clear the floor
5The last floor bossClear it and the coffer opens
Mechanics: Talk to the Henge Keeper to start and to leave. The Treasure Portent marks the coffer. The 45-minute clock runs from floor 1.
