Command/autogroup

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Aliases: /ag
Usage: /autogroup [subcommand]
Description:

Turns the automatic party flag on/off.

Subcommands:
on Turn flag on.
off Turn flag off.

Toggle on and off when no subcommand
is specified.

Example: /autogroup on

Notes

/autogroup will automatically create a party with anyone in your area and/or region who is also using /autogroup and is within a few levels of you. This command will not prompt you if you want to join a party before forming it.
It will form when a mage and 3 others have the automatic party flag up. It will not form with 2 mages and 2 melee or 4 melee.
