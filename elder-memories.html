Elder Memories

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Start NPC Isacio - Selbina (G-10) or
Vera - Mhaura (G-10)
Requirements Level 18+
Items Needed Damselfly Worm
Magicked Skull
Crab Apron
or
Wild Rabbit Tail
Dhalmel Saliva
Bloody Robe
Repeatable No
Reward Ability to use Support Job


Walkthrough

  • Talk to Isacio (G-10) Selbina to activate quest Elder Memories or talk to Vera (G-10) Mhaura to activate quest The Old Lady.
    • Once one quest is active the other can never be activated.
    • Both quests have varied objectives but will result in the same reward.
  • If you talked to Isacio, obtain Damselfly Worm, Magicked Skull, and Crab Apron.
  • If you talked to Vera, obtain Wild Rabbit Tail, Dhalmel Saliva, and Bloody Robe.
  • Trade in the items to the respective NPCs to unlock Support Job access for player.
