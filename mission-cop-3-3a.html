Before you begin

  • Complete CoP 3-2.
  • The Road Forks active.

Starting point

Arrival scene · Northern San d'Oria

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Northern San d'Oria. Speak to Arnau in the cathedral at M-6, then Chasalvige in its manuscript room at L-6.
  2. Enter Carpenters' Landing from Jugner Forest E-6. Find Guilloud at H-10 and talk to him to spawn Overgrown Ivy.
  3. Defeat Overgrown Ivy, then speak to Guilloud again before leaving the zone.
  4. Visit Hinaree upstairs in Count Caffaule's Manor, Southern San d'Oria B-6. Her scene completes this branch.
  5. Finish the Windurst branch too, then report to Cid in Metalworks H-8.

Battle and progress notes

  • Overgrown Ivy can use Hundred Fists; prepare healing and a way to survive its burst.

Completion and reward

San d’Oria branch completed.
