Enlight

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Please be aware: this is likely not implemented on Eden at this time.

Adds light damage to your attacks.

How to Obtain

Currently the spell "Enlight" is only usable by means of enchanted weapons.
Enchanted weapons
  • Hallowed Sword
  • Sacred Lance
  • Sacred Sword
  • Sacred Degen
  • Sacred Mace
  • Sacred Maul
  • Sacred Wand

Also obtained during Harvest Festival 2008.
