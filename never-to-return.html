Walkthrough

  • Complete the Your Crystal Ball quest and have Kurou-Morou tell you your fortune on three separate game days, talking to him on a fourth day will trigger this quest.
  • Kurou-Morou tells you he has misplaced his Horn Hairpin and asks you to get him a new one.
  • Trade a Horn Hairpin to Kurou-Morou to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Kurou-Morou (Fortunetellers, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

Kurou-Morou has thrown his precious hairpin into the rubbish, so he needs a new one made.
