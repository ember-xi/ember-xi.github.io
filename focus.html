Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Enhances user's accuracy.
  • Obtained: Monk Level 25
  • Recast Time: 5:00
  • Duration: 2:00

Notes/Description

  • Upon usage, character's accuracy stat is raised 20 points.
  • If the Temple Crown is equipped, Focus will raise accuracy by 30 points.
  • If the Temple Crown is unequipped while Focus is active, the enhanced effect will continue.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Focus" <me>
  • Some people prefer to macro Focus in with other abilities to save space.
