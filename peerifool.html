Notes:

  • Spawned during the quest "One Good Deed?" by checking a ??? at (H-9).
  • Special Attacks: Mandragora special attacks and an Additional Effect of Sleep on normal attacks.
  • The additional effect very rarely lands, and while they do use Dream Flower repeatedly, it is relatively easy to evade.
  • Builds TP very quickly, and seems to build TP while sleeping.Verification Needed
  • One will always aggro when they are spawned, therefore they cannot be spawned and fought seperately by using Sneak.
  • Soloable by most 70+ jobs.
  • Somewhat challenging to defeat as a level 58 Red Mage (with a Black Mage subjob), but easy to defeat at level 70.
