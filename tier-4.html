Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Beastmen tend to be grouped into tiers of 10 levels each, with certain kinds of beastmen within those tiers.

Tier 4 is for beastmen whose levels range from 31 to 40. These are particularly prominent in the Middle Lands and Outlands. Silver Beastcoins can be stolen from the majority of these beastmen.
