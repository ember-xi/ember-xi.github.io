Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Grants your pet's Killer effect to party members within area of effect.
  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 75
  • Recast Time: 15:00
  • Duration: 1 minute

Notes/Description

  • Killer Instinct gives a bonus to party members (including yourself) the killer trait of your current pet. Ability is AoE, and grants approximately a 10% increase to the killer trait to all party members in range.

Macro Syntax

/ja "Killer Instinct" <me>
