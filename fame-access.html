Fame Access

From Zenith XI Wiki

Start Zenith’s reviewed level-75 quests without repeatedly farming Fame to meet their reputation requirement.

Status: Phase 2 installation and all 175 target files verified on 19 September 2026; in-game testing deferred. Details

How it works

  1. Visit the quest’s normal starting NPC.
  2. Meet its other requirements: level, job, preceding quests, story progress and any required items.
  3. Accept the quest and complete its original objectives.

What the change covers

Cleanup Phase 2 waives 182 reviewed quest-eligibility checks in 173 files for San d’Oria, Bastok, Windurst, Jeuno, Norg and the Selbina/Rabao quest set. This includes avatar-trial Fame checks.

Requirements that still matter

RequirementZenith behavior
Fame threshold in the reviewed quest setWaived.
Level and jobStill required; for example, a small avatar trial still requires SMN level 20.
Earlier quests and storyStill required.
Items and tradesYou still obtain and submit the required items.
Timers and retriesThe original non-Fame timers and retry conditions remain.
RewardsEarned by completing the quest; nothing is granted just by installing the patch.

Your real Fame

Fame points still increase normally. Reputation-reporting NPCs and merchant pricing use your real Fame level. The patch does not set maximum Fame or make every NPC dialogue identical.

If a quest will not start

Check its walkthrough for a preceding quest, level, job, story requirement, zoning step or timer. Quests outside the reviewed level-75 set are not promised automatic access. Report the NPC and quest name if a reviewed quest still explicitly blocks you for Fame.
