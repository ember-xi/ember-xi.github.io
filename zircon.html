Zircon

From Zenith XI Wiki

Gemstone used in Goldsmithing earrings and rings.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Uses and purchase
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Goldsmithing 20: use a Wind Crystal with one Translucent Rock. Zircon is the HQ2 result, so the ordinary result is not Zircon. Other sources include Clockwork Pod in Fei'Yin, Skull of Sloth in The Eldieme Necropolis, and treasure coffers in Quicksand Caves.

Uses and purchase

Used to craft Zircon Earring and Zircon Ring. Buy under Auction House → Materials → Goldsmithing, or check the Goldsmiths’ Guild stock in Bastok Markets.
