Rogue's Attire Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This set is created for Thieves. This lightweight green cloth armor excels at giving the thief a full, easy range of movement as well as enhancing their already proficient hiding abilities. They also provide the Thief with stats useful for their abilities and weaponskills, and a boost to their shield and parrying skill, in case they happen to find themselves in possession of some unwanted attention. For a fee you can store the set at any Armor Storer.


Number of Pieces: 5   Cost to store: 1000 gil   Storage marker: Rogue's Attire Claim Slip
