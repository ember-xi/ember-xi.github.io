Ziphius

From Zenith XI Wiki

A forced-spawn Pugil NM in Misareaux Coast that can drop Hospitaler Earring.

Spawn steps

  1. Bring six Slice of Carp to the Rafelous River in Misareaux Coast. Five ??? points are near H-5; the sixth is farther away near I-6.
  2. Bait the points at night. They appear from 22:00; trading before midnight is a safe common window across the source descriptions.
  3. Between 04:00 and 07:00, examine the baited points. Each check has a chance to produce Ziphius; the bait does not guarantee a spawn.
  4. Defeat it for a chance at Hospitaler Earring. Its hits have additional water damage.

The ??? disappear at 07:00. If the traps fail, bring fresh bait for another night.
