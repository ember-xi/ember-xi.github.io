How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Quest

Obtained by trading the following items obtained in Limbus and the original Artifact Armor to Sagheera in Port Jeuno:

  • Chameleon Yarn
  • Charcoal Cotton
  • Coeurl Leather
  • Ancient Beastcoin: 20
  • Hunter's Bracers
