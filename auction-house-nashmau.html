Description

Auction House located on the lower level of Nashmau near the exit to Caedarva Mire. Auctioneers here only sell items placed on the Auction Houses in Jeuno. You can also find delivery NPCs at this Auction House.

Location: Nashmau ( G - 6 )

Shop type: Auction House
