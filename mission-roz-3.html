Before you begin

  • Complete Welcome t’Norg.

Starting point

Jakoh Wahcondalo · Kazham · J-9

Walkthrough

  1. Travel to Kazham and speak with Jakoh Wahcondalo at J-9.
  2. Finish her cutscene and check your key items for the Sacrificial Chamber Key before leaving for the temple.

Battle and progress notes

  • Every character needing mission credit should complete this conversation; a party member’s key does not advance your mission.

Completion and reward

Sacrificial Chamber Key.

What this opens

  • The Temple of Uggalepih battlefield story step
