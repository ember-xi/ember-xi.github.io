Allied Forces of Altana

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

The Allied Forces of Altana was an alliance of The Kingdom of San d'Oria, The Republic of Bastok, The Federation of Windurst, and The Grand Duchy of Jeuno, in response to the aggression by the Beastman Confederate during the Crystal War. It was established by Archduke Kam'lanaut and the Armathrwn Society to unite the nations of the Middle Lands against impending devastation, under the banner of the goddess Altana.

The nations obtained aid from allies such as the Marquisate of Tavnazia and volunteers from the continent of Olzhirya, though the Empire of Aht Urhgan refused all requests for aid in the conflict. Some of the military divisions such as the Cobra Unit, Seventh Cohors, and Knights of the Iron Ram began to enlist volunteers and mercenaries to fill their ranks.

Despite heavy losses during the siege of Windurst, the Battle of Jeuno, and the catastrophic siege of Tavnazia, the tide began to turn and the individual beastmen forces began to lose confidence and abandoned the war effort. A group of champions stormed Castle Zvahl, and the leader of the Beastman Confederate, the Shadow Lord, was slain, effectively ending the great conflict.
