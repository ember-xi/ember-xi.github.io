Latent effect

  • Active when 500 or more Weapon Skill Points have been accumulated with this weapon.
  • Weapon Skill Points must be obtained while Heart Snatcher is equipped in main hand.
  • Latent effect will subsequently be active even when weapon is equipped in off-hand.
  • 6% critical hit rate only applies to Heart Snatcher and will not affect any other equipped weapon.
  • You can gain Weapon Skill Points (WSP) for this weapon while under the effects of Level Sync at ANY level.
