Notes:

  • Spawned by trading Relic Shield to ??? at (K-6)
  • Has ability to call all Tavnazian Sheep and Tavnazian Rams to link with it. They don't teleport, just walk from their spawns to wherever Amaltheia's target is.
    • The 22 sheep can be used for TP, and the 2 Rams can either be killed while kiting the NM or just kept asleep.
  • Immune to Slow, Elegy, Blind, Stun and Paralyze. Susceptible to Sleep and Gravity.
  • Magic: Uses Holy on random people in casting range, Cure V, Banishga III, Banish IV.
  • Special Attacks: Uses a Cone Attack Petrify ability called Petribreath that is similar to the Crawler ability Poison Breath
    • Seems to have a wider angle than the standard 45° arc for most Cone Attacks.
  • Can be defeated with an alliance of level 75 players using a kiting strategy.
