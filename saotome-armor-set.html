Saotome Armor Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

An armor set from the Dynamis zones, which the player may be rewarded with for defeating the mobs within. The Saotome set, as with all Relic Armor sets, share the same basic shape of it's regular AF counterpart, however the Relic Armor set has received a dramatic visual upgrade, with a black and gold color scheme replacing the orange coloring of the original set.

The set enhances attack and defense, giving both Dex, Accuracy and Attack boosts, alongside HP, Vitality and Enmity enhancements which help the Samurai to survive, and protect weaker characters. It also includes enhancements to the Samurai Job Abilities of Third Eye and Meditate, and the Samurai trait of Store TP.

Number of Pieces: 6

Cost to store: 2000g (excluding Saotome Koshi-Ate)
