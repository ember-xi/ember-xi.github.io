Walkthrough

  • Speak to Fochacha (I-9) to start the quest. She's a chef from Windurst, but her son has lost her latest supply of groceries to a pumpkin-headed bandit. Help Topok-Hippok track him down!
  • Talk to Qutiba (K-12) in the Shararat Teahouse. Qutiba's boss is the infamous pumpkinhead, luckily, Fochacha's son has you to hide behind... The bandit gives back half the groceries and leaves to pick up a shipment. Fochacha's son has an idea and runs off.
  • Talk to Fochacha (I-9) again to complete the quest. Her son returns with the goods, and she makes you a dish for you to try out. Pumpkinhead makes another appearance and gobbles up the goods before you get a taste. To make up for the loss, Fochacha gives you some coins she has left.

Game Description

Client: Fochacha (Serpentking Square, Aht Urhgan Whitegate)
Summary:

Fochacha's cooking supplies have been stolen! Her son has gone to retrieve them, but how is he faring? You might try looking for him at Shararat's Teahouse, where gourmet aficionados are known to reside...
