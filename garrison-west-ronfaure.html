Enemy Spawn Layout (North to South)

  • 1st Wave (3 party alliance)
    • Front row: Chasseur, Chasseur, Serjeant
    • Back row: Fighter, Fighter, Serjeant
  • 2nd Wave
    • Front row: Cursemaker, Chasseur, Serjeant
    • Back row: Cursemaker, Fighter, Serjeant
    • Two more spawns 10 sec after start of wave
  • 3rd Wave
  • 4th Wave
    • Two more Fighters at 10 sec
    • Two more Cursemakers at 20 sec
    • Orcish Fighterchief appears 30 sec after the start of wave, at the crossroads in front of the outpost
