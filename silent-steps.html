From Zenith XI Wiki

Earn a reusable Sneak Ring for quiet travel.

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to any one of the three city ring guards listed above. Choose Silent Steps, then Accept quest.
  2. Obtain 2 Beeswax and 1 Cotton Cloth. Bought, crafted or farmed materials all work. Check the Auction House catalogue for listings; stock is not guaranteed.
  3. Keep one free main-inventory slot. Trade exactly 2 Beeswax + 1 Cotton Cloth together to any of those guards, with no gil or extra items.
  4. Receive 1 Sneak Ring immediately. This is one shared quest, with one ring per character; changing city does not grant another.

Reward

1 Sneak Ring — Sneak + Invisible for 8 minutes; reuse every 10 minutes. Level 1, all jobs, either ring slot; reusable.

Use the ring

  1. Equip Sneak Ring in either ring slot and wait 5 seconds.
  2. Open Items and use the equipped ring. Activation takes 1 second. Equipping it alone does not activate it.
  3. Sneak and Invisible last up to 8 minutes. The next use is available 10 minutes after activation. Normal cancellation and detection rules still apply.

Already have a Sneak Ring?

Your existing ring receives the same server effects; no repeat quest is required. If you already own one, the guard keeps your materials and does not issue a duplicate.

Item description

The native item name is Sneak Ring. Its static description may still list only Sneak and the old 15-minute reuse; Zenith applies both effects and the 10-minute reuse on the server. This guide describes v3.1.1.
