Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Walkthrough

National Conquest Overseers of your current Allegiance offers this quest under the topic: "I want to go on on a supplies quest". You get the choice to deliver supplies of regions that are under your current nation's conquest-control. To see current Conquest standings across all regions, check your in-game menu Region Info, then choose the topic Conquest. You can only accept one supply mission per run and must wait for one Game Day to pass in order to accept another mission. After you get your key item, travel to the appropriate Regional Conquest Overseer and speak with the NPC. When you have spoken with one of the NPCs, the key item is considered 'delivered' and the mission is complete. You must complete the mission before the weekly conquest tally, or the Regional Conquest Overseer will deny the supply.


Reward

After delivering the supplies, you will be granted Conquest Points. The amount of points is determined by the "difficulty" of the run. You can see the difficulty by looking at the Outpost Teleporting list: Higher level requirements grant more Conquest Points. After completing the supply mission, you will be able to use the Outpost Teleporting system for the corresponding region if you are of the appropriate level, and the list of available Supply Quests will then indicate that you have already completed the run for that region by indicating "(Done)" next to the completed region.

Notes

  • Level requirements: none
     
  • This quest is repeatable.
     
  • Supply Quests are bound to your current nation. If you change your affiliated nation, all Supply Quests will be reset, and you must complete them again when your new nation has control over the region you wish to complete. Note - even if you complete a Supply Quest for a region, change to another nation, then change back to your original nation, you must still do the Supply Quest again in order to Outpost Warp in that region.
