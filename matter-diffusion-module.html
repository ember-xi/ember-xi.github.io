Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Location

The Matter Diffusion Module is the portal for entry into Temenos. It can be found in the area accessed from (H-4) in northern section of Al'Taieu (Holla section).

Entry

For all areas of Temenos, the following key items are required:

  • Cosmo-Cleanse
  • White Card

With these items, the following zones can be accessed:

  • Temenos - Western Tower
  • Temenos - Northern Tower
  • Temenos - Eastern Tower

However, by trading specific items obtained within Temenos to the module, additional areas may be accessed:

  • Central Temenos - Basement 1
  • Central Temenos - 1st Floor
  • Central Temenos - 2nd Floor
  • Central Temenos - 3rd Floor
  • Central Temenos - 4th Floor

Other Details

Near the Matter Diffusion Module a "Scanning Device" can be found that will tell you whether the desired area is currently occupied. If it is, it will also tell you the amount of time remaining for the people who are in that area.
