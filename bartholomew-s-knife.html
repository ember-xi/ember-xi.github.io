Additional effect: Water damage

  • This effect activates approximately 20% of the time and more frequently when fighting lower-level monsters.
  • Damage ranges between 8-80 damage, depending on the monster's level and water resistance.
  • The Additional Effect Water Damage deals no damage to Brigandish Blade.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Mini-Quest

  • This item is obtained by trading a Buccaneer's Knife to the ??? that appears at the south end of the room where Brigandish Blade is spawned (see screencap).
  • If the pillar is not ready to morph the knife, you will just receive the "Nothing happens." message.
  • If you fail you will need to steal a new knife to try again; a failed knife will never transform (verified 25/05/2020).
