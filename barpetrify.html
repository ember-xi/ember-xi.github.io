Enhances your resistance against petrification.

Description

Spell cost: 20 MP
Spell element: Wind
Magic skill: Enhancing Magic
Jobs:

  • Red Mage 43

Timers

Other Information

Note
This spell does not prevent players from being petrified. This spell only decreases the time that the negative status effect stays on the player. This spell's effectiveness, like any other barspell, is wholely dependant on your Enhancing Magic skill. Low enhancing magic will produce little to no results in increasing your resistances.
