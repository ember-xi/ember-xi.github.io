Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Grants the effect of your next support Blue Magic spell to party members within range.
  • Obtained: Blue Mage Level 75
  • Recast Time: 20 minutes. Additional merits decrease the recast time by 2 minutes and 30 seconds each.
  • Duration: 60 seconds, or until the next blue magic spell is cast.

Notes/Description

Only work with support spells. AoE Range is centered around the caster with a 8-9' radius.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Diffusion" <me>
