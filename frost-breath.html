Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Deals ice damage to enemies within a fan-shaped area originating from the caster. Additional effect: Paralysis.

Description

Spell cost: 136 MP
Ecosystem: Lizards
Type: Magical (Ice)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: INT-2
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 66

Timers

Casting Time: 6.5 seconds
Recast Time: 42.75 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Frost Breath as part of a combo to create Conserve MP.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Conserve MP combo.

  • Chaotic Eye
  • Zephyr Mantle
  • Firespit

Notes

  • The formula for this spell appears to be (Current HP / 3) + (your level / 0.625). For example, if you are level 66 and have 1200 HP, your damage would be 400+105.6, or 505-506. This is subject to Resists, however. Using it against Vermin or Magic Bursting it will not boost damage; it will only affect resist rates.
  • Is NOT affected by Magic Attack Bonus.
  • Ignores Magic Defense Bonus, but is subject to direct magic resistance (from Proto-Omega for example).

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Induration, Distortion, and Darkness.

How to Obtain

  • Only the raptors in Uleguerand Range will use this move. Raptors in other areas only use Flame Breath and Lightning Breath.

Acquired From

Mob Family: Raptor
Minimum Blue Mage Level To Acquire: 58 with Magus Jubbah equipped, 60 without Magus Jubbah
Name Level Zone
Nival Raptor 68-70 Uleguerand Range
