Notes

  • All have the Beetle special attacks.
  • Bisan is the green Beetle, Pilwiz is the brown Beetle. Bisan uses physical attacks and Beetle special attacks, Pilwiz uses magical attacks, such as Stonega II.
  • Trust magic cannot be used.
