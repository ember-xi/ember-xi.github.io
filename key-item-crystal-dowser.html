How to obtain

Received during The Secret Weapon victory scene; Rahal grants the quest copy again during Lightbringer.

What to do with it

For Lightbringer, you must speak to Rahal after the Great Hall scene even if you remember receiving this item earlier.

Mission walkthrough

The Secret Weapon

Trading and storage

This is a key item. Find it under Key Items, not your ordinary inventory. It cannot be bought at the Auction House, crafted, traded to another player or placed in a bazaar.

Native mission definition

Additional level-75 reference

Description: An instrument comprising a crystal attached to a string. Orcs used this to search for Lightbringer.
