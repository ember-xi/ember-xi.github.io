Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Beastmen tend to be grouped into tiers of 10 levels each, with certain kinds of beastmen within those tiers.

Tier 1 is for beastmen whose levels range from 1 to 10. These are the weakest beastmen, found in the low level beastmen camps. Beastcoins can be stolen from the majority of these beastmen.
