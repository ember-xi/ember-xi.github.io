Piercing Arrow

From Zenith XI Wiki
Contents
  1. Properties
  2. Notes
  3. Levels

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Archery weapon skill

  • Skill level: 40
  • Ignores enemy's defense. Amount ignored varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Induration , Transfixion
  • Modifiers: STR:16%; AGI:25%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.00 1.00 1.00

Notes

The amount of defence ignored is 0% with 100TP, 35% with 200TP and 50% with 300TP.

Levels

  • Piercing Arrow is only natively available to Rangers. While other jobs can obtain the required 40 Archery skill, they must use Ranger as their support job in order to gain access to Piercing Arrow.
  • Archery skill level 40 is obtainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
Job Rating Level
Ranger A- 13
Samurai C+ 14
Thief C- 14
Warrior D 15
Red Mage D 15
