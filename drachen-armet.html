Statistics

[Head] All Races

Lv. 60 DRG

Wyvern uses breaths more effectively

  • A Defensive wyvern will use Healing Breath when HP is at 50% or less.
  • A Multipurpose wyvern will use Healing Breath when HP is at 33% or less.
  • An Offensive wyvern will use Elemental Breaths to exploit a monster's weakness.
