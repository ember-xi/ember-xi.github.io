Slowga

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Slowga slows all enemies in the area of effect.

Other Information

Note:
The spell "Slowga" does not exist for adventurers to learn except as a Blood Pact from Leviathan, even though many monsters are able to use it.
Used By Mobs
  • Carmine Dobsonfly.
  • Ouryu in Promathia Mission 4-2.
  • Kam'lanaut in Zilart Mission 8 and Apocalypse Nigh.
  • Possible effect of the "Goblin Dice" ability used by Goblins in Dynamis - Jeuno.
  • Dynamis Lord in Dynamis - Xarcabard.
  • Opo-opo Monarch in the BCNM Royal Succession.
  • Anansi in the BCNM Come Into My Parlor.
  • Tartaruga Gigante in the BCNM The Hills Are Alive.
