Walkthrough

  • Kohlo-Lakolo asks you to talk to Pichichi, who directs you to Chamama in Windurst Waters (North, F-10, inside "Rarab Tail").
  • Chamama needs you to collect 4 balls of Saruta Cotton. These are dropped by Pygmaioi in Tahrongi Canyon.
  • Trade these to her and she will give you a Fake Moustache (key item) to take back to Kohlo-Lakolo.

Game Description

Client: Kohlo-Lakolo (Warehouses, Port Windurst)
Summary:

The Mana Orb the Star Onion Brigade "confiscated" from the Cat Burglar has gone missing. In order to track down the culprit, the gang will need the seven gadgets found in the Junior Investigator's toolkit! Pichichi will tell you what to bring.
