Notes

Wilhelm works for the The Brugaire Consortium, representing clients from the Near East. Trading him parts from Ultima and Omega (found in the chests that Proto-Ultima and Proto-Omega leave behind following their defeat) will cause his clients to reward you with parts of the Nashira and Homam armor sets.
Both sets of armor are Level 75. Rewards are as follows:

Nashira Set

Trade Items Obtained from Proto-Ultima
Usable By WHM, BLM, RDM, SMN, BLU

Part Armor
Ultima's Cerebrum Nashira Turban (head)
Ultima's Heart Nashira Manteel (body)
Ultima's Claw Nashira Gages (hands)
Ultima's Leg Nashira Seraweels (legs)
Ultima's Tail Nashira Crackows (feet)


Homam Set

Trade Items Obtained from Proto-Omega
Usable By THF, PLD, DRK, DRG, BLU
