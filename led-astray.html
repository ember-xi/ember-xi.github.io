Walkthrough

  • Talk to Mhasbaf in Aht Urhgan Whitegate at J-8.
  • Head to the lower section of J-8 and approach the pillar for a CS.
  • Talk to Tohka Telposkha at I-8; make sure to tell the Galka to go to Port Ephramad.
  • Repeat for Rubahah at F-9 and Cacaroon at G-11.
  • Go to I-11, slightly down the ramp beside the ferry dock, for a CS and the key item Letter from Bernahn.
  • Talk to Tataroon at G-8 in Nashmau for the final CS and your reward.
