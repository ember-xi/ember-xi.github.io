Dancer

From Zenith XI Wiki

A melee support job that uses TP for healing, travel dances and party support, with Steps and Finishing Moves for control.

Start here

Unlock with Lakeside Minuet. Build TP through melee attacks, use Steps for Finishing Moves, and reserve enough TP to heal. Reverse Flourish converts those moves back into TP.

Weapons and weapon skills

Use Dagger as your main weapon family. Cloak and Dagger unlocks Evisceration at job level 71+ and base dagger skill 230+. Unlocking a Myth (Dancer) uses Main Gauche to unlock Pyrrhic Kleos at 75.

The original level-75 Dancer special weapon is the Mythic dagger Terpsichore. Dancer cannot equip Mandau in the original rules; do not follow a Thief relic route expecting a Dancer weapon.
