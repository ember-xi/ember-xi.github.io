Vaghron

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Adventurer's Assistant

Location: Bastok Mines (H-9)

Notes

Offers Information on Monster Relationships:

What do you want? Oh, you think that because I'm just a kid you can walk over and talk to me for no good reason? True, I might look like a child now, but I know I was once a great warrior, and I can prove it. Do you want to see proof?

"Yes."

  • I thought so. Well...all right, here. But be careful! It's very, very valuable--it's a piece of my past life. A friend gave this to me over twenty years ago. I can't remember what it all means, though. I showed this to my friend Gumbah once, and his opinion was that this is a very valuable document that tells you each monster type's weakness. He said it will come in handy when I become an adventurer. You've been a good pupil. Come back anytime if you want to see it again. Well, at least until I leave on my first adventure...

"No."

  • I wasn't going to show you anyway. I know your type. Patting kids on the head and thinking how great life was when you were their age, right? Spare me, please.
