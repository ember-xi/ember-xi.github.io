Rhinostery Certificate

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description:This certificate with the signature of four researchers states you have been officially licensed by the Rhinostery. Now you should have clearance to the area behind the Priming Gate in Windurst Walls.

Obtained:Toraimarai Turmoil

Notes:Grants access to Toraimarai Canal via the Priming Gate in Windurst Walls.
