Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Guild Merchant

Notes

  • Open: 24 Hours
  • No longer shares stock with Visala, and prices are static.
  • Has much higher stock limits than Visala, but can be depleted.
  • Stock is replenished over time.
