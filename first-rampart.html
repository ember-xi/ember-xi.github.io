First Rampart

From Zenith XI Wiki

Floor-four Rampart in the original Zhayolm Remnants.

Contents
  1. Progression
  2. Fight and loot
  3. Sources

Progression

Choose this Rampart if your group entered Zhayolm Remnants on Firesday or Earthsday. The entry day determines the correct target, not the current day when you reach it. Defeating the correct target opens the route to floor five.

Fight and loot

It can summon a Rogue Marid. A possible drop is Zhayolm Card. Choose the right Rampart before attacking; the wrong choice can send the group around floor four again.
