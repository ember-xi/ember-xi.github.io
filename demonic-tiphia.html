Notes:

  • Lottery Spawn from the Wespe around G-6 to G-7 on the second map, approximately every 2 hours.
  • Killable by: 5 characters level 70+ Duo by 75 THF/NIN tank and RDM/NIN, Duo By 75 SAM/NIN and 51 BST/WHM.
  • Special Attacks: Resistant to Magic Damage, but is susceptible to Silence,Sleep and Paralyze
  • Casts Cure V (uses often whenever it drops below 50% health), Erase, Sleepga II; Paralyga; uses regular Bee special attacks, including Pollen for 3500-4000HP restored, so that it cures itself endlessly unless a massive amount of damage is done in a short period of time.
  • Can use Final sting.
  • Does have limited MP.
