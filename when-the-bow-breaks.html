When the Bow Breaks

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Start NPC: Gaweesh - Al Zahbi
Requirements:
 Treasures of Aht Urhgan
Ode to the Serpents started
Najelith present in Al Zahbi
Items Needed: Biyaada's Letter
Frayed Arrow
Title: Galeserpent Guardian
Repeatable: No
Reward: 500 Imperial Standing

Subquest of: Ode to the Serpents

Walkthrough

1. Talk to Gaweesh in Al Zahbi at G-8, who will tell you to speak with Najelith in Wajaom Woodlands during her off-duty hours.

2. Kill Aht Urhgan Attercops in Wajaom Woodlands or Bhaflau Thickets until you obtain the Frayed Arrow.
Note: The Frayed Arrow can also be purchased at the Auction House under Auction House Category: Others > Misc.

3. Head to the Giwahb Watchtower at F-5 in Wajaom Woodlands and trade the Frayed Arrow to the door there for a cutscene to complete this sidequest.

Note: Galeserpent General Najelith must be in the Commoner's Ward/Al Zahbi (G-7) for this quest to be accessible or completable. If she is being held prisoner talking to Gaweesh will not lead to a cutscene.

Game Description

Client:

Gaweesh - Al Zahbi

Summary:

Find the Galeserpent General in the Wajaom Woodlands, where she spends her spare time gazing on some object from her past...
