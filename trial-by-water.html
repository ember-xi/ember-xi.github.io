Trial by Water

Trial by Water
Starting NPCEdal-Tahdal — Norg (H-9), by the quay
Required FameNone
Level RestrictionLevel 65 (Prime) · under 65 the trial-size fight is offered instead
Items NeededTuning fork of water
Additional RequirementsSummoner main job for the trial-size fight
TitleHeir of the Great Water
RepeatableOnce a day
DescriptionAwaken the crystal in the Cloister of Tides and prove your worth to Leviathan.
Rewards
  • Leviathan (pact)
  • Leviathan's Rod
  • Water Belt
  • Water Ring
  • Eye of Nept (for the Evoker's Ring)
  • 10,000 gil

—The Moonlit Path

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Edal-Tahdal in Norg (H-9). He gives you the Tuning fork of water.
    • Under 65 he gives the mini fork instead — trade it back to him and he teleports you to the Cloister.
  • Talk to him again while you hold the fork. You are sent straight to the Cloister of Tides.
    • The Cloister sits in the Sea Serpent Grotto.
  • Summon your Trusts and your avatar, then examine the Water Protocrystal and pick Trial by Water.
  • Defeat Leviathan Prime.
    • Water damage heals him: no Water spells, no water weapon skills.
    • He uses Barracuda Dive, Spinning Dive, Tail Whip, Slowga and Water IV, and at about 50% HP his Astral Flow pact Tidal Wave — heavy AoE water damage.
    • Best day: Lightningday. Avoid Watersday. Keep Barwatera up.
    • Bring Ramuh: lightning beats water, and Chaotic Strike also stuns.
  • You receive the Whisper of Tides.
    • Going for Fenrir? Keep it and do not take a reward yet — see The Moonlit Path.
  • Return to Edal-Tahdal and choose your reward.
    • Repeatable once a day (after midnight JST).
    • At SMN 75 with Leviathan already learned, winning teaches Grand Fall, his level 75 Blood Pact.

EmberXI changes

  • No Fame requirement.
  • Trusts can be summoned inside the battlefield; the Prime fight needs level 65+.
  • Talking to the NPC while you hold the fork teleports you to the Cloister.
  • Repeatable once a day.
  • Under 65, summoners get a solo trial-size fight (level 20 cap, Carbuncle only).
