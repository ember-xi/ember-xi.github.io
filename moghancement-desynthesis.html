Moghancement: Desynthesis

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules













Moogle-powered assistance from your Mog House. Increases the chance of success when attempting desynthesis.

It is obtained through these Furnishings:

  • Acolyte's Grief
  • Alchemist's Tools
  • Ancient Blood
  • Book Holder
  • Dream Coffer
  • Dream Stocking
  • Dream Platter
  • Lines and Space
  • Tarutaru Stool
  • Thurible
  • Tsahyan Mask
  • Wastebasket
  • Walahra Burner

This is helpful when attempting Desynthesis. With this Moghancement you will have a greater chance of successfully desynthesizing merchandise. See Moghancements for more information.
