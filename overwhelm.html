Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Grants a damage bonus with weapon skills perfomed face-to-face with an enemy
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Increased damage bonus 5% each for the first 3 levels and 2% each for the last 2 levels.
  • 5% > 5% > 5% > 2% > 2% = 19% Total.
