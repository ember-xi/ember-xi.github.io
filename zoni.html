Zoni

From Zenith XI Wiki

Cooking soup with several small combat and recovery bonuses.

Contents
  1. Craft or buy
  2. Food effect
  3. Sources

Craft or buy

Cooking 27: Fire Crystal + one each of Rock Salt, Sticky Rice, Tokopekko Wildgrass, San d'Orian Carrot, Tiger Cod, Distilled Water, Lakerda, Ziz Meat. Zoni is the ordinary result; Zesty Zoni is the high-quality result. Buy under Auction House → Food → Meals → Soups.

Food effect

3 hours: HP +10, MP +10; STR, DEX, VIT and AGI +1; Accuracy, Ranged Accuracy, Attack, Ranged Attack and Evasion +1; resting HP and MP recovery +1.
