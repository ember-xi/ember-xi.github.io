True Will

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is the third Ninja Artifact Armor quest - otherwise known as AF3.

  Start NPC:   Ryoma - Norg (H-8)
  Requirements:
   Ninja 50+
  Items Needed:   Kuftal Coffer Key
  Title:   Paragon of Ninja Excellence
  Repeatable:   Yes, after AF complete
  Reward:   Ninja Chainmail

  Previous Quest:   I'll Take the Big Box
  Next Quest:   None

Walkthrough

  • Ryoma in Norg sends you to Yhoator Jungle.
  • Examine the ??? at (I-7) to spawn 3 NM Sahagin:
  • Kappa Akuso (Monk) Uses Hundred Fists.
  • Kappa Biwa (Bard)
  • Kappa Bonze (White Mage)
  • Note: Only Kappa Akuso needs to be defeated. Has true-hearing and seems to aggro magic. Recommended strategy is to spawn and run to Den of Rancor. Let Kappa Biwa and Kappa Bonze move back to the ???, and fight Kappa Akuso at the zone.
  • Killing Kappa Akuso or Kappa Biwa works. (Confirmed: Wednesday, May 29th, 2007) Killing Kappa Bonze also works. (Confirmed: Saturday, June 16th, 2007)
  • Killable by: RDM75/NIN37. Must fight right at zone and when Kappa Akuso uses Hundred Fists, zone into Den of Rancor.
  • Killable by: NIN75/WHM37. Zone into Den of Rancor to clear hate.
  • Killable by: WHM75/NIN37. Zone into Den of Rancor and then fight only Kappa Akuso. Zoned during Hundred Fists.
  • Killable by: 75BLM and 75WHM, using zone to clear hate. Alternatively a combination of 3+ 75 jobs to include tank and support.
  • Killable by: 75PLD/NIN. Use zone to clear hate, fight MNK until he two-hours then zone intro Den. Come back out with full MP/HP and solo monk to 0%.
  • Killable by: 75PLD/RDM: Using the zone to fight just Kappa, Chivalry is Reccomended, 2hr may be required for Hundred Fists.
  • Killable by: 75BST/WHM. Using the zone and fight only one Kappa method.
  • Killable by: 75MNK/NIN and 75BRD/WHM. Zoned everything, killed WHM, zoned MNK during 2hr, and killed it as well. Bard depop.
  • Killable by: 60NIN/WAR. Use zone to clear hate and fight one Kappa. Zone during MNK two-hour and resume after it wears. Helps to have an adventuring fellow set to healing at this level.
  • Take caution if Woodland Sage pops, it does aggro level 75's.
  • Kill them and examine ??? again to get an Old Trick Box (key item).
  • Take it back to Ryoma who then sends you to Rabao to talk with Leodarion.
  • Leodarion sends you to Kuftal Tunnel.
  • Obtain a Kuftal Coffer Key and open a Treasure Coffer to receive a Large Trick Box (key item). (Note: this can be done as any job; the Large Trick Box can be attained by picking the lock as THF.)
  • Bring it back to Leodarion to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Ryoma (Quay, Norg)
Summary:

The Sahagin have stolen Mitsunari's treasure and hid it somewhere in the Jungle on Elshimo Island. It is your job to find it.
