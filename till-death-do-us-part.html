Till Death Do Us Part

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Romilda - Port Bastok (I-7)
  Requirements:
   Bastok Reputation 3
  Items Needed:   Cotton Gloves
  Title:   Qiji's Rival
  Repeatable:   No
  Reward:   2000g

  Previous Quest:   Forever to Hold
  Next Quest:   None

Walkthrough

  • Zone after Forever to Hold quest.
  • Talk to Romilda.
  • Trade some Cotton Gloves to Romilda to complete the quest and receive your reward.

Game Description

Client: Romilda (Docks, Port Bastok)
Summary:

Romilda wants you to get her a pair of cotton gloves to give to her husband, Qiji, as a present.
