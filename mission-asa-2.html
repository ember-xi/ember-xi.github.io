Zenith availability

The route has been checked against references and available code; completion on Zenith has not yet been play-tested.

Before you begin

  • Complete ASA 1.
  • Travel on foot for the exit scene.

Starting point

Area exit · Windurst Woods

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

Walkthrough

  1. Leave Windurst Woods for East Sarutabaruta on foot. Alternatively, leave Windurst Waters or Port Windurst for West Sarutabaruta.
  2. Watch the scene and record which Enfeeblement Kit was requested: Poison, Blind, Sleep or Silence. Your assignment determines the potion needed for the next synthesis.

Completion and reward

Story progression.

What this opens

  • ASA 3
