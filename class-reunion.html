Walkthrough

  • Examine the House of the Hero in Windurst Walls (G-4) as SMN and obtain Carbuncle's Tear.
    • You must zone if you do this straight after the artifact weapon quest or any quest involving Carbuncle. The Mog House does not count as zoning.
    • If the door gives no cutscene, zone to Windurst Waters and back, then click it again.
  • From this point on the quest can be finished on any job.
  • Talk to Koru-Moru in Windurst Walls (E-7). He asks for 4 Astragalos.
    • Buy them from the Auction House under Ammunition, or the Boneworkers' Guild in Windurst Woods.
    • If you already have them, just trade the four to him.
  • Talk to Fuepepe at the Aurastery, Windurst Waters North (L-6).
    • Talk more than once if needed, until he mentions Uran-Mafran.
    • The ministry due north of the Windurst Waters Mog House.
  • Talk to Furakku-Norakku at the Optistery, Windurst Waters North (G-8).
  • Talk to Shantotto at her home in Windurst Walls (K-7). She mentions Sunny-Pabonny left for San d'Oria.
    • With high staff skill she may give the Blood and Glory cutscene instead — talk to her again.
  • Talk to Gulmama in Northern San d'Oria (E-7) for the Ice Pendulum.
    • The Tarutaru you pass on the way into the Watchtower.
    • The same Tarutaru who gives the Tuning fork of ice for the Shiva Prime fight.
  • Go to the Cloister of Frost in Fei'Yin (I-5).
    • You do not have to do the fight as Summoner.
    • Fei'Yin Home Point #2 is outside the Cloister.
    • The exit from the fight is on the first floor around (K-6).
  • Trade the Ice Pendulum to the Protocrystal and enter after the cutscene.
    • Buffs wear off on entering.
  • Defeat the six Dryads (Sapling type).
    • They attack as soon as you approach them at the top.
    • They can be slept with Sleepga; otherwise pull them down the ramp one at a time.
    • Trusts can be summoned inside.
  • Return to Koru-Moru in Windurst Walls (E-7) for your reward.
    • If you skipped Fuepepe or Furakku-Norakku, talk to them first or he will not hand over the Spats.
    • The Ice Pendulum can be tossed after the fight.
    • If you lose the fight, drop the Pendulum and talk to Gulmama for a new one.

EmberXI changes

  • No Fame requirement.
  • Trusts can be summoned inside the battlefield.
  • Artifact pieces can be upgraded to +1 by trading them to Brandt in Lower Jeuno with 4 Alexandrite.
