Notes:

  • Spotted at H-7 on the main map for Bhaflau Thickets. Also reported at the northern part of G-6.
  • Lottery spawn from Olden Treant. Killing the single treant that spawns in the northern part of G-6 can pop it, but take care during wind weather as an elemental spawns there.
  • Special Attacks: Additional Effect: Bind; High auto-regen during the daytime
  • Links to nearby saplings - be careful if pulling them to clear the area.
  • Apparently has TP regen during the day as well, so waiting until night to engage is strongly recommended.
  • Nails estimated to drop 20-25% of the time. Appears to be lower than 20-25% drop rate, currently 0-10 with TH4.
  • Soloable at night by well-equipped well-skilled RDM. Duoable by well-equipped ninja or most /nin and any mage that can cure.
