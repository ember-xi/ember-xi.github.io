Before you begin

  • Complete Headstone Pilgrimage and have all eight fragment key items.
  • The traditional route contains weight-operated doors: arrange sufficient player weight or a verified alternative access method.

Starting point

Ancient Door · Chamber of Oracles

Walkthrough

  1. In Western Altepa Desert, enter the off-map tunnel southwest of C-11. Follow it to the hidden D-12 entrance of Quicksand Caves.
  2. On Quicksand Caves map 4, open the pressure doors at I-9 and H-7. Drop at F-7 and follow the hidden route to the Chamber of Oracles exit at D-4.
  3. Prepare before entering the mission battlefield. Defeat Triarius V-VIII (BLM), Centurio V-III (PLD) and Princeps V-XI (RNG). Controlling the other two while removing the BLM reduces incoming spell pressure.
  4. After the victory scene, remain in the chamber: the next mission uses the eight pedestals here.

Battle and progress notes

  • Traditional door weight: one Galka, two Hume/Elvaan/Mithra, or three Tarutaru. Trusts are not a substitute for verifying the door mechanism. Echo Drops help against Silence.
  • The pinned native battlefield allows Trusts, up to six participants and 30 minutes, with the server maximum level as its cap. This is a code check, not a completed Zenith solo test.

Completion and reward

Story progression to The Chamber of Oracles.

Walkthrough

  • Form a 6 member party of 65~+; an alliance is optional, but note that the battle is 6 party members maximum.
    • Ensure everyone who needs ZM6 has the 8 elemental fragment key items. If their current ZM is "Through the Quicksand Caves" then they have all the fragments.
    • Magic users should bring Echo Drops for NM fight.
    • Also to avoid aggro and save time, bring Silent Oils or Sneak magic. There is no sight aggro in Quicksand Caves.
  • Travel to Western Altepa Desert head to C-11 and enter a tunnel.
  • Exit tunnel to hidden area of Western Altepa Desert and head down the ramp to D-12 and zone into Quicksand Caves
  • Inside Quicksand Caves (Sneak)...
    1. Travel towards I-9 and step on pad to open door.
    2. Then head West and step on pad @ H-7 to open next door. (NOTE: It will take a few players (pets and NPCs of any kind do NOT work) to open the 2 doors. You will need either 1 Galka, 3 Tarutaru, or 2 of any other combination.)
    3. Enter next room and drop down the hole in the floor @ F-7.
    4. This drops you into a hidden area; travel towards the Chamber of Oracles zone @ D-4.
  • Enter the Chamber of Oracles. The 6 party member BCNM consists of the following enemies fought in this order:
    1. Centurio V-III - PLD
    2. Triarius V-VIII - BLM
    3. Princeps V-XI - RNG
  • Elemental Seal + Sleepga (II) is a good way to start this battle off. They will all likely be buffed with Protect and/or Shell from the PLD so make sure to dispel these.
  • PLD should be the first target due to its Resist Sleep trait and ability to heal and buff itself and its comrades.
  • All enemies will use their appropriate Two Hour Ability. Beware of Eagle Eye Shot from the RNG, it hits hard enough to take out party members with little defense. Blink tanks may have trouble maintaining shadows against the RNG due to its fast rate of attacks. As in ZM4, the BLM is capable of being silenced, rendering its Manafont useless.
  • Buff upon Entering Shimmering Circle.
  • Upon defeating the 3 NM Anticans you will be appear in another area of the Chamber of Oracles; this is the start of "ZM7 - The Chamber of Oracles". Place the fragments in the pedestals for cutscene.


NOTE: If you are going to do more than one BCNM fight, you must zone out into Western Altepa Desert travel back (on foot) to Quicksand Caves for re-entry. In Western Altepa Desert, you will need Sneak spell/items for most mobs and Invisible spell/items for Manticores.

The zone to Quicksand Caves is right around the corner. You can easily zone back in to get to the Chamber of Oracles, without traipsing halfway across the desert.
