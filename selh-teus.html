Battle NPC Edit

Selh'teus fights alongside the party and Prishe in the battle for Dawn, participating in the battle against Promathia. He is required to stay alive for the battle to be won; if he should fall during battle, it is considered an instant loss. His job is currently unknown, and he uses some unusual abilities:

  • Luminous Lance - Light-based damage + Stun
  • Revelation - Light-based damage
  • Redemption
- Restores all HP and MP and increases TP to 300% for everybody within

range, including Selh'teus (used when his health gets low)
