Statistics

Canteen of Yagudo holy water
This canteen holds water that has been
blessed by a secret Yagudo ritual.
The Yagudo use this water to keep
the undead away.

Other Uses

Used in Quest: Prelude of Black and White

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
