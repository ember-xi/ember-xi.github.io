Statistics

Yellow Drop
A fruit-flavored candy. It is shaped
like an earth bead.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Medicine Effects: (All Races)

  • Vitality +5
  • Medicated (5 Minutes)

Other Uses

Used in Quests: None

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be sold on the Auction House or delivered, but can be traded and bazaared.
