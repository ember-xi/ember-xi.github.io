Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Abyss Blast: Single target Darkness based damage, with blind effect that is not absorbed by Utsusemi.
Undead Mold: Cone Attack damage and disease effect
Stinking Gas: Lowers VIT of players in range.
Whip Tongue: Single target damage and stun effect.
Acid Breath: Cone Attack STR down and Water(?) damage
Call of the Grave: AoE INT down
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
