Before you begin

  • Complete Storms of Fate, including the palace return scene.
  • View both Awakening epilogues (Norg and the Neptune’s Spire door).
  • Wait until the next Vana’diel day after finishing Storms of Fate, as required by the pinned native script.

Starting point

Esha’ntarl (palace entrance scene) · Ru'Lude Gardens · H-6

Era note: The reviewed Zenith modules list enabled custom/zenith only. The pinned base scripts therefore supply the waits described here; optional era modules that restore JST-midnight waits were not enabled in that snapshot. Live installation may differ.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Approach the Ducal Palace audience area to receive the Note Written by Esha’ntarl.
  2. Climb Promyvion - Holla to its fourth floor and examine the Memory Flux at J-10 for the Holla Sliver.
  3. Climb Promyvion - Dem to its fourth floor and examine the Memory Flux at K-4 for the Dem Sliver.
  4. Climb Promyvion - Mea to its fourth floor and examine the Memory Flux at K-6 for the Mea Sliver. The three zones may be visited in any order.
  5. Return to the palace entrance in Ru’Lude Gardens with all three slivers. Finish the scene; it consumes the slivers and starts the Apocalypse Nigh wait. Leave the zone afterward.

Battle and progress notes

  • Use the Promyvion floor maps and Memory Receptacle/Memory Stream ascent described in CoP’s opening missions. No spire boss battle is required for these slivers.
  • The pinned script uses a Vana’diel-day wait, not a weekly Conquest tally.

Completion and reward

Note Written by Esha’ntarl; quest completion after handing over the three slivers.

What this opens

  • Apocalypse Nigh after the next Vana’diel day and zoning

Walkthrough

After receiving Whisper of the Wyrmking and returning to Ru'Lude Garden Palace for cutscene, you must wait until the next Conquest update to activate this quest.

  • After the conquest tally walk back into the Ducal palace for a cutscene.
  • Head into the Neptune's Spire and enter the Tenshodo for a cutscene.
  • Walk into the palace in Ru'Lude Gardens for a cutscene and the Note Written by Esha'ntarl (key item).
  • Visit the three Promyvions and get the following key items at the Memory Fluxes on the 4th floor:
  • J-10 Promyvion - Holla - Promyvion - Holla Sliver
  • K-4 Promyvion - Dem - Promyvion - Dem Sliver
  • K-6 Promyvion - Mea - Promyvion - Mea Sliver
  • Return to the palace in Ru'Lude Gardens for a final cutscene with Esha'ntarl.

Game Description

Client: Esha'ntarl (Audience Chamber, Ru'Lude Gardens)
Summary:

Strange readings have been recorded from the Emptiness surrounding the mothercrystals. Travel to the crags of Holla, Dem, and Mea and investigate the coordinates where this phenomenon is occurring.
