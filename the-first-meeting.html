The First Meeting

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is the second Monk Artifact Armor quest - otherwise known as AF2.

  Start NPC:   Oggbi - Port Bastok (E-6)
  Requirements:
   Monk 50+
  Items Needed:   None
  Title:   None
  Repeatable:   Yes, after AF is complete.
  Reward:   Temple Gaiters

  Previous Quest:   Ghosts of the Past
  Next Quest:   True Strength and
  Borghertz's Striking Hands

Walkthrough

  • Oggbi in Port Bastok will ask you to accompany Dalzakk to Fei'Yin.
  • Zone into Qu'Bia Arena and upon exiting back into Fei'Yin you will trigger a cutscene and receive Letter from Dalzakk (key item).
  • After that, head to Davoi and look for a 'Hide Flap' on one of the tents at (F-7).
  • This triggers two NM Orcs named Bilopdop and Deloknok to spawn.
    • Soloable as 75THF/NIN (possibly other job combos).
    • Soloable as 75SMN (clear Orcs in the area, pop with Ramuh out, Thunderspark the NMs and train away. Check the Hide Flap again and let NMs depawn).
    • Immune to sleep even with Elemental Seal.
  • Kill them and investigate the 'Hide Flap' on the tent again to receive the key item San d'Orian Martial Arts Scroll.

Note: The 'Hide Flap' may have moved to another tent while you attack the NMs but will have re-popped within the same area. If this happens just examine the flap on the new tent to receive the key item. The second 'Hide Flap' spawns when you POP the two NMs, not when you KILL them, so if you have someone along who can take hate after the NMs are popped, it is not necessary to kill them. The NM fight can be solo at any level. Simply spawn the NMs with reraise on then die and wait for them to despawn, reraise then check the flaps without zoning.

  • Return to Oggbi to obtain the Temple Gaiters.

Game Description

Client: Oggbi (Steaming Sheep Restaurant, Port Bastok)
Summary:

You have been asked to head to Fei'Yin and help Dalzakk, one of Oggbi's students, with his task.
