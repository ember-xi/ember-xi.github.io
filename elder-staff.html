Elder Staff

From Zenith XI Wiki

Staff · SCH 75 · DMG55 · Delay402.

Obtain from notorious monsters in Nyzul Isle Investigation. On the documented floor 100 route, the player selecting the starting floor should be Scholar: the boss drops a weapon matching that player's job.

What to do with it

  • Equip it and start Unlocking a Myth (Scholar) to unlock Omniscience.
  • It is also the starting Scholar weapon used in the Tupsimati Mythic chain. Unlocking the weapon skill alone does not build the Mythic weapon.
