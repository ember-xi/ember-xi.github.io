Terpsichore

From Zenith XI Wiki

Dancer level-75 Mythic dagger: DMG 31, Delay 205.

Contents
  1. Benefits
  2. How to obtain
  3. Sources

Benefits

Improves Step accuracy and Step effects. Uses Pyrrhic Kleos to activate Aftermath: accuracy, attack, or occasional double attacks according to TP tier.

How to obtain

  1. Obtain Main Gauche from Nyzul Isle Investigation.
  2. Complete An Imperial Heist and Duties, Tasks, and Deeds.
  3. Finish Forging a New Myth, then awaken the dagger through Coming Full Circle.

Full requirements and materials: Mythic Weapon Acquisition at 75. This page covers the awakened level-75 weapon, not later upgrades.

Zenith status: original level-75 reference route. The complete weapon-building quest chain has not been verified as playable on Zenith. Do not assume that having these wiki pages means the NPC hand-ins or final battlefield are installed.
