Bucket of Divine Paint

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description: A bucket of special paint you found in the Garlaige Citadel. It is needed to restore the statue of Altana to Her former glory.

Used In Quest(s): Altana's Sorrow

Obtained: Found by clicking on the ??? in the first floor of Garlaige Citadel at (H-8) or (G-8). Must have the quest flagged.
