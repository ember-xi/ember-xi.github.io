Bat Hunt (San d'Oria 1-2)

← 1-1 Smash the Orcish Scouts · San d'Oria Missions · 1-3 Save the Children →

San d'Oria Mission 1-2
MissionBat Hunt
TypeRepeatable
Start NPCGate Guard (Endracion or Ambrotien in Southern San d'Oria; Grilau in Northern San d'Oria)
LocationKing Ranperre's Tomb
RequirementsRank 1 · 1-1 complete
Items NeededNone
Rewards200 Rank points

Mission 1-2 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 1-1 Smash the Orcish Scouts.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission from a Gate Guard.
  2. Go to King Ranperre's Tomb (entrance in East Ronfaure, J-9).
  3. Examine the Tombstone on the upper floor near the entrance. A cutscene plays.
  4. Return to any Gate Guard.
Mechanics: Bats are level 5-10 near the entrance, more at night. Keep the tank in front.
