Omniscience

From Zenith XI Wiki

Scholar Mythic staff weapon skill.

Deals darkness damage and can reduce the target's magic attack. It is associated with Tupsimati.

How to learn

Complete Unlocking a Myth (Scholar) using Elder Staff from Nyzul. This quest allows use with other staves on Scholar; finishing a Tupsimati is not required for the standalone unlock.
