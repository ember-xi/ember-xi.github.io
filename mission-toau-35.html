Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 34: Testing the Waters.
  • Percipient Eye key item.

Starting point

Rock Slab · Talacca Cove · F-6

Walkthrough

  1. From the arrival point, follow the northwest tunnel to the Rock Slab at F-6.
  2. Examine the slab and choose Legacy of the Lost.
  3. Fight Gessho within the 30-minute limit. Concentrate on the original when clones appear; the fight ends at 15% HP.
  4. Finish the victory scene and keep the Percipient Eye for the next mission.

Battle and progress notes

  • Native battlefield: Trusts allowed, maximum six participants. The key item is not consumed by this fight.
  • Native code contains clone handling but flags missing dialogue and potentially incomplete additional boss mechanics.

Completion and reward

Gessho's Mercy title.

What this opens

  • ToAU 36: Gaze of the Saboteur
