Statistics

Bhaflau Fireflies
This cage is filled with fireflies
that have an exceptionally strong
homing instinct.
They will fly straight home when
released.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Using this item allows a player to abandon an Assault mission and reappear back at the staging point. Abandoning the mission in this fashion makes it count as a loss to that player, even if the rest of his/her party goes on to complete the Assault on their own.

Other Uses

Used in Quests: None

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

This temporary item is given to each party member when entering the Mamool Ja Training Grounds for an Assault mission.
