Monarch Beard
Description:
A scale ripped from the jaw of a
mighty wyrm while it was
sleeping. Can be used to provoke
the fury of a dragon.
Obtained:
Morangeart - Tavnazian Safehold (G-10) after completion of PM 2-4 An Eternal Melody.
Notes:

Used to enter the Monarch Linn ENMs

  • Bad Seed
  • Fire in the Sky
  • Beloved of the Atlantes
  • Bugard in the Clouds

You can only obtain this key item once every 5 Earth Days.

Everyone in the party must have this key item to participate in the ENM.
