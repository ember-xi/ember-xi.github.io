Item Deliverer

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

These NPCs will deliver items or gil to the Delivery Box of PCs. You cannot send Rare/Exclusive or Exclusive items to other players via these NPCs.

Note: Most Exclusive items can be sent between characters on the same account. A list of Exclusive items that CANNOT be sent between characters on the same account can be found at this link.
