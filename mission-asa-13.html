Before you begin

  • Complete ASA 12.
  • Final introductory scene from ASA 12.
  • All sixteen tablets strongly recommended for level-75 attempts.

Starting point

Mahogany Door · Sacrificial Chamber

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. After the opening scene, examine the Mahogany Door again and choose Project: Shantottofication. The collected tablets are consumed on entry and supply their battle enhancements.
  2. Fight D. Shantotto and Shantotto with a plan to manage both targets. D. Shantotto uses scythe attacks and poison/paralysis; Shantotto uses staff attacks with stun, slow, silence and plague. Bring appropriate status-removal supplies.
  3. Near half health, either can trigger a rage phase that brings them together for skillchains. The raging target cannot be finished during that phase, even at 1% HP; prioritize survival until it ends.
  4. Defeat both, finish the scene and then return to Windurst Walls for the epilogue.

Battle and progress notes

  • The full tablet set raises attributes/resistances and triples HP/MP, with a special Reraise effect. It is not a permanent character bonus.

Completion and reward

Tonberry key and story progression.
