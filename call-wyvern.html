Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Summons a Wyvern to fight by your side.
  • Obtained: Dragoon Level 1
  • Recast Time: 0:20:00
  • Duration: Instant
  • Special: Only available if Dragoon is your main class.

Notes/Description

  • Call Wyvern is a Dragoon only 20 minute special ability.
  • A defeated Wyvern cannot be re-summoned until this ability's recast time is complete.
  • A wyvern can be re-summoned immediately if it has full health when dismissed.
  • Temporarily logging out of the game or mounting a Chocobo will cause a Wyvern to depart.
  • Update as of 12/13/05: Wyverns will now have the equivalent of the job trait Subtle Blow, reducing the amount of TP gained by opponents from the wyvern's attacks.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Call Wyvern" <me>
