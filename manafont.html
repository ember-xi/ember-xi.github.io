Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Eliminates the cost of magic spells.
  • Obtained: Black Mage Level 1
  • Recast Time: 2:00:00
  • Duration: 0:01:00

Notes/Description

  • Manafont is a Black Mage's two hour special.
  • When active, all spells can be cast at a cost of zero MP.
  • When active, the casting of spells cannot be interrupted by melee attacks. Stun, sleep, and movement can still interrupt the spells though.
  • Often used as emergency casting of healing magic when all mages are spent on MP.
  • Sometimes used as a means of ensuring the success of a Warp or Escape spell.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Manafont" <me>
  • Since Manafont is a two hour special, some people prefer not to make a macro for it to prevent unintentional activation.
  • Some people prefer to put two hour job abilities on a trigger macro. This means they use the macro syntax /ja "Manafont" <stpc>, which makes the macro user have to select a player character to use the job ability on, increasing the safety of the macro.
