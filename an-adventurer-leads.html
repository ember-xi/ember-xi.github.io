An Adventurer Leads

From Zenith XI Wiki

A Zenith journey that permanently increases your Trust allowance.

Requirements

  • Main job level 50 or higher.
  • A Trust permit from any starting city.
  • Complete Companions of the Road and collect its reward.

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Gondebaud at (L-6) in Southern San d’Oria. Open Gondebaud’s current capacity quest, read the requirements and choose Accept quest.
  2. Visit Crawlers’ Nest and Western Altepa Desert. Visits recorded before acceptance count.
  3. Defeat 15 enemies of level 45 or higher that award you EXP. Have a living Trust in your party and in the same zone. These kills must occur after acceptance.
  4. Return to Gondebaud and choose Claim reward. Talking to him alone does not grant the upgrade.

Reward

Your maximum allowance becomes five Trusts. The upgrade is permanent across jobs. The total party limit remains six, including real players.

Finding the areas

Use Zenith Atlas camp routes to find the zone entrances and books. A visit means entering the zone; there is no hidden quest marker. Kills may be in any area if they meet this quest’s enemy-level and EXP conditions.
