Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Magic Defense Down is a harmful status effect that disorients players until it wears off or is removed. Afflicted characters experienced reduced Magic Defense, thus taking more damage from Magic.

How to remove the effect

  • The White Magic spell Erase will remove this effect

How the effect is inflicted/gained

Monster Abilities

  • Trolls - Enervation
  • Flans - Boiling Point
