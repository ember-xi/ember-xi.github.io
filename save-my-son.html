Save My Son

Save My Son
Starting NPCDietmund — Lower Jeuno (G-11), Merchant's House door on the top floor
Required FameNone
Level RestrictionLevel 30
Items NeededNone
TitleNone
RepeatableNo
DescriptionTo cure Dietmund's son, go to Qufim Island, find the flower that only blooms at night, and bring back its roots.
Rewards
  • 2,100 gil
  • Beast Whistle
← Previous questNext quest →
—Path of the Beastmaster

Walkthrough

  • In Lower Jeuno go up the stairs at (G-11) and open the Merchant's House door for a cutscene with Dietmund.
  • Optional: talk to Shalott in the Chocobo Stables of Upper Jeuno (G-7) for a cutscene.
  • Go to Qufim Island (F-8) and find the path that runs down the cliff on the coast.
    • The Night Flowers bloom from about 21:30 to dawn.
    • Bring Sneak and Invisible: Banshees appear at night on the way, a Goblin Bounty Hunter patrols the cliff edge, and a Kraken may sit right on the flowers (sound aggro). Sneak is enough to touch the flowers.
  • Click the Night Flowers at the bottom of the path for a short cutscene.
  • Return to Lower Jeuno and open Dietmund's door again for your gil and the Beast Whistle.
