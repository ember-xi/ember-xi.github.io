Save My Son

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Start NPC: Dietmund - Lower Jeuno G-11
Requirements:
 Level: 30+
Items Needed: None
Title: Life Saver
Repeatable: No
Reward: 2,100g
Beast Whistle

Previous Quest: None
Next Quest: Path of the Beastmaster

Walkthrough

  • Click on the door in Lower Jeuno at G-11 (on the second floor) for a cut-scene.
  • Go to Qufim Island and follow a path down the cliff to F-8. Be warned that a Kraken spawns right on top of the flowers, so approach to a safe distance and wait till 22:00. After 22:00 (until the night ends) use sneak to approach safely and click on the Night Flowers for a cut-scene.
    • Note: The flower will spawn at 17:00, but will not be ready to harvest until 22:00 thru 00:00 (Midnight) Vana'diel time. After Midnight the flower will have lost it's magic and wilted, meaning you have a very short window to access the cutscene.
  • Note: If attempting this quest at lower levels, it is recommended to reach the path before 22:00 as Banshee's appear once night falls. Also note that the recently added Goblin Bounty Hunter runs down the path that leads to the Night Flowers and the Kraken so be extra careful.
  • After the cutscene, return to Lower Jeuno and click on Dietmund's door again (G-11).
  • His son will come out and give you your reward.
  • Next quest is the last step to get Beastmaster job.

Game Description

Client: Dietmund (Merchant's House, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

To cure Dietmund's son of his affliction, go to Qufim Island. Once there, find the flower that only blooms at night, and bring back its roots.
