Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A species of Empty, the memories of the souls that lie the mothercrystals. The Empty consist of monsters inside Promyvion and can be considered fairly powerful with stronger weapon skills than a normal enemy.

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Negative Whirl: AoE damage, Slow, strips Utsusemi
Winds of Promyvion: Erase
Empty Cutter: Single target damage, absorbed by Utsusemi
Spirit/Binary/Trinary Tap: Single target drain (up to) one, two or three status effects (including food and Utsusemi)
Spirit/Binary/Trinary Absorption: Single target HP Drain (100, 200, 300 HP?), absorbed by Utsusemi
Stygian Vapor: 8'(?) AoE Plague
Shadow Spread: AoE Curse and Sleep Notorious Monsters only
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
