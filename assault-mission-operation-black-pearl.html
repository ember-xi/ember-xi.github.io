Walkthrough

Defeat all enemies. Protect the NPCs.

  • This Assault is very much like the Mamool Ja Training Grounds Sergeant Major Assault Mission - Blitzkrieg.
  • You start at G-7.
  • The mobs will come from all four hallways.
  • The mobs are very weak and will come in multiple numbers.
  • If the guarding NPCs are alive, they will each report when a surge of enemies are approaching from their side.
  • These mobs cannot be slept with Sleep/Sleepga, even with elemental seal.
  • The objective is to not let a single enemy attack the Princess or her servant. (NPCs in the center of where you're defending)
  • The Wamouracampas and the Vulcanian Bombs can easily be defeated by a black mages GA II spell, or by a blue mage's Frypan or Body Slam.
    • Battle Dance works effectively as well, but won't deliver the 1 hit KO as the two previously mentioned spells.
  • Do take caution because the Bombs cast Flare.
  • Troll Combatants (MNK) are not defeated as easily. They are Even Match to a level 75.
  • One troll NM, Jorporbor the Hellraker (DRK), appears during the assault. Defeat him and the assault is won.
