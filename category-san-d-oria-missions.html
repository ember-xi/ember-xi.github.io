From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

These missions are available only to those currently in allegiance with San d'Oria


Note: Unlike expansion missions, most of these missions require rank points to undertake. All missions will award some rank points upon completion, but trading crystals is often requiring to unlock additional missions.

Types

  • Quest is an assignment to go somewhere (possibly dangerous) for a cutscene or to retrieve/deliver an item, but no fighting required.
  • Repeat is a mission that can be taken multiple times to gain rank points, and are usually optional.
  • Fight requires you to fight a Notorious Monster.
