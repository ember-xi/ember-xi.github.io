Notes

  • Osschaart has around 14500 HP/
  • Osschaart uses Ahriman special attacks, Draw In, and Charm on party members about every 30 seconds.
  • Osschaart uses Tier III-aga spells, these vary, watch for which one he is casting, and have someone throw up a bar-spell.
  • Osschart's initial charm target is random on Eden currently.
  • When it uses Charm on a new character, the old one is no longer charmed.
  • When it uses Charm on a Job with a Pet, the character will lose its pet and timers will not be reset.
  • Charm allows Osschaart to gain the Two-Hour Ability, Spells, and Job Traits of the charmed job, DISREGARDING TIMERS. Job Traits stolen are effective on Osschaart and the charmed even after multiple charms. (eg. PLD in party, and Thief was charmed and had no resist sleep trait nor gear with that trait; The Thief processed a "Resist!"(indicating Resist Sleep trait) for Sleep II from a fully merited RDM.)
  • Can Charm out of order if the Charm is stunned, but often just attempts the charm again.
  • It can summon Osschaart's Bat, Osschaart's Wyvern, Osschaart's Avatar, and Osschaart's Automaton. (Note: Osschaart only summons the pets if Osschaart copies a Job with a pet.) Pets are required to be killed if they pop, aside from the avatar.
  • Eyes On Me is capable of doing 800+ regularly. This ability is dark-based and CHR-modded. Absorb-CHR can lower the damage a bit, but it can still get dangerous if Osschaart steals Meikyo Shisui and uses it three times in a row.
  • Osschaart is susceptible to Sleep, Lullaby and Repose. Will build resistance to Lullaby, shortening sleep time considerably.
  • Osschaart is only susceptible to Silence through Elemental Seal.
  • It always uses charm immediately after Draw In.
  • It always charms party members in the same order. This order is random upon entering.
  • Effective methods of dealing with charmed members include sleeping the charmed member, then waking them once charm wears, or having the member to be charmed next unequip their weapon so that they will not be able to deal damage.
  • Osschaart can use two hour ability. Had a 75 NIN/WAR charmed, and within seconds Osschaart did Mijin Gakure and did 901 dmg to all members
