Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Restores HP for the target party member

Description

Spell cost: 72 MP
Ecosystem: Beasts
Type: Magical (Light)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: CHR+1 HP+5
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 58

Timers

Casting Time: 2.5 seconds
Recast Time: 6 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Magic Fruit as part of a combo to create Resist Sleep.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Resist Sleep combo.

  • Pollen
  • Wild Carrot
  • Yawn
  • Exuviation

Notes

  • Roughly equivalent to Cure IV but only targetable in party and a higher cure amount cap.
  • Enhanced by Light Staff/Apollo's Staff.
  • Heals 1 more for each additional point of VIT, and 2 more for each point of MND.
  • Has slightly increased potency from healing magic skill based on subjob.
  • 20' maximum casting distance
  • Heals ~539 with 150 MND with Light Staff/Apollo's Staff and WHM support job at 75 making it significantly better than Cure IV.
