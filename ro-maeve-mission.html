Walkthrough

  • (Optional) Talk to Aldo in the Tenshodo Headquarters inside the Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno to find out the fate of Verena.
  • Next head to Norg to talk to Gilgamesh. His librarian will tell of some ruins to the north of The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah that he thinks is what Eald'narche mentioned. You are to head there next.

Game Description

Mission Orders
The Gate of the Gods, Tu'Lia, has been restored by Eald'narche. What do his words "Ro'Maeve" and "blessed by the light" mean? What is Lion's next move?
