Riddle

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Were you looking for Ridill?
Reduces maximum MP of targets in an area of effect.

Description

  • Family: Manticores
  • Type: Enfeebling
  • Element: non-elemental
  • Can be dispelled: Erase - See Max MP Down for how to remove
  • Utsusemi/Blink absorb: Ignores shadows
  • Range: 15' radial

Notes:

  • Riddle reduces maximum MP by approximately 41.5%. MP lost this way are not restored when it wears off or is removed.
  • Reduction in maximum MP seems to vary - 75BLM MP reduced to 10%, 75PLD reduced to 56%.
  • Max MP can be reduced to 0 on rare occasions.
  • Appears to be non-elemental. Landed 100% of the time on all 8 Terrestrial Avatars during test.
