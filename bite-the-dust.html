Game Description

Client: Yazan (Harbor Way, Port Bastok)
Summary:

Yazan's business is in peril--vicious sand bats are disrupting the trade route between Selbina and Bastok. Hunt down those bats, and bring Yazan a sand bat fang as proof of your success.
