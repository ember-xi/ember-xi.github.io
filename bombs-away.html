Bombs Away!

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Start NPC Buffalostalker Dodzbraz - Uleguerand Range (F-9)
Items Needed Cluster Core x 2
Repeatable Yes
Reward Shu'Meyo Salt


Walkthrough

  • Trade Buffalostalker Dodzbraz, Uleguerand Range (F-9), two Cluster Cores to complete the quest.
The Shu'Meyo Salt is helpful in defeating the Snoll Tzar during Promathia Mission 5-3.

Game Description

Client
Buffalostalker Dodzbraz (Uleguerand Range)
Summary
Dodzbraz is an Orc that despises snolls. If you bring him two "cluster cores," Dodzbraz will give you something useful to use against them.
