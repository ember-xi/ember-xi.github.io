Mog Safe 2

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Mog Safe 2 is available to all players on Eden. Other key features include:

  • Any item in your inventory may be placed inside your Mog Safe 2.
  • Functions identically to Mog Safe 1.
  • Shares the same maximum storage value with Mog Safe 1.
  • Upgrades to Mog Safe 1 also upgrade Mog Safe 2.
