Silk Coat +1 Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A robe set for mages, crafted on a base of silk and velvet and embroidered with silver and gold. This set offers modest defense for all who can wear such light cloth, as well as a boost to mind and some elemental resistance. This is the HQ version of the Silk Coat Set.
