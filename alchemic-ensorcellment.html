Alchemic Ensorcellment

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This special alchemy ability allows you to enchant catalysts with the power of black magic.

  • Obtained via NPC: - Hemewmew, Bastok Mines (K-7)

Needed for Recipes

  • Brimsand - Alchemy (40 & 41)
  • Ethereal Vermilion Lacquer - Alchemy (25)
  • Invitriol - Alchemy (43)
  • Muting Potion - Alchemy (8)


Purchased From

Price: 40,000 Alchemy Guild Points
