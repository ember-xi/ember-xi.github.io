Emote/salute

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Aliases: None
Usage: /salute [subcommand]
Description: An emote command. The player performs one of the following salutes, dependent on the player's city of allegiance.
Example: /salute <t>

Notes

  • This command is an emote.
Emote text:
<NAME> salutes.
<NAME> salutes (the) <TARGET>.

San d'Oria: The player extends the right arm and presses it against his/her chest. The feet lock together.

Bastok: The player raises the right arm and holds it momentarily in front of them, parallel to their chest. The hand is clenched into a fist and the right foot moves forward slightly.

Windurst: The player raises both hands to chest level and brings them together slightly with the hands cupped to perform a namaste gesture.
