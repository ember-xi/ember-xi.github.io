Emote/dance1

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Aliases: (none)
Usage: /dance1 [motion]|[target]
Description:
Subcommands:
motion Triggers only the motion and not the descriptive text.
target Includes the target in the emote's descriptive text.
Example: /dance1
Usage Examples: /em performs a passionate samba.

/em performs a passionate samba for the Moogle. (Where Moogle is the /target).
