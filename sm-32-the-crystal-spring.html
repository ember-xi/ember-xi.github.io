The Crystal Spring (San d'Oria 3-2)

← 3-1 Infiltrate Davoi · San d'Oria Missions · 3-3 Appointment to Jeuno →

San d'Oria Mission 3-2
MissionThe Crystal Spring
TypeRepeatable
Start NPCGate Guard (Endracion or Ambrotien in Southern San d'Oria; Grilau in Northern San d'Oria)
LocationChateau d'Oraguille
RequirementsRank 3 · 3-1 complete
Items NeededCrystal Bass
Rewards400 Rank points

Mission 3-2 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 3-1 Infiltrate Davoi.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission from a Gate Guard.
  2. Get a Crystal Bass: fish it in Ordelle's Caves / Ranguemont Pass, or buy it from the Auction House.
  3. Trade it to Chalvatot in Chateau d'Oraguille.
  4. Return to a Gate Guard.
Mechanics: The Auction House on EmberXI stocks itself, so you will usually find a Crystal Bass for sale.
