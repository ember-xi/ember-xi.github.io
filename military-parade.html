Military Parade

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This ability is provided by some armor pieces that belong to bard:

  • Bard's Justaucorps
  • Bard's Justaucorps +1

The following status changes are activated when you have your weapon drawn and the armor equipped:

STR +8, DEX +8, VIT +8, AGI +8, INT -8, MND -8, CHR -8
