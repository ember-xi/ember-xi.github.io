Walkthrough

  • Watch the cutscene which was triggered at the end of the last mission.
  • Speak with Naja Salaheem another time.
You must be wearing appropriate clothes to receive this cutscene. She will display the various sets of acceptable armor.
Acceptable Armor is defined by color type. It must be one of these categories:
  1. Blue with Gold Trim
  2. Red with Gold Embroidery
  3. Pale Colors with Lace
  4. Deep Purple Armor
  5. Rich Crimson
  6. Cloth with a Pattern
  7. Indigo with Gold Embroidery
  8. Gorgeous Black
  • As long as you have a body piece obtained in Aht Urhgan (Jaridah Peti, for instance), she should let you go.
  • Additionally, you need to wear at least gloves, leg armor and boots, but they do not need to match the body piece.
