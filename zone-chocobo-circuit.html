Additional level-75 reference

Chocobo Raising hasn't been implemented yet.

This is an area that links with Al Zahbi and other cities. The circuit can be enjoyed by spectators wishing to make some fast gil with the introduction of chocobets. Registered Chocobos that have been raised by their owners can also be pitted against those of other players for gil or chocobucks. Chocobucks can be used to purchase special Chocobo Raising related items.

This area is only accessible if the Treasures of Aht Urhgan expansion is installed.
