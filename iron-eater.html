Iron Eater

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: NPC

Location: Metalworks, (J-8) guarding the Door to the President's Office.

Notes

  • He is a member of the The Mythril Musketeers and holds the rank of "Number Three." Iron Eater is his Hume-given name; his actual name is Pagdako. He plays a major role in Bastok Missions.
