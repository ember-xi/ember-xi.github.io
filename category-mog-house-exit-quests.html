Mog House Exit Quests

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Completing each of these quests will allow the player to exit their Mog House to any area of the city the quest was completed in.

Windurst: Flower Child

  • Item: Lilac
  • NPC: Ojha Rhawash (Residential Area, Windurst Walls)


Sandoria: Growing Flowers

  • Item: Marguerite
  • NPC: Kuu Mohzolhi (Across the street from the Phoenix Perch Inn, Northern San d'Oria)


Bastok: A Lady's Heart

  • Item: Amaryllis
  • NPC: Valah Molkot (Near Residential Area, Port Bastok)


Jeuno: Pretty Little Things

  • Item: Yellow Rock
  • NPC:Zona Shodhun (Residential Gate, Port Jeuno)


Aht Urhgan Whitegate: Keeping Notes

  • Items: Black Ink x 1 Parchment x 1
  • NPC: Ahkk Jharcham (Middle of Square, north of ship dock, Aht Urhgan Whitegate)
