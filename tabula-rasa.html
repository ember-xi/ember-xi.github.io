Tabula Rasa

From Zenith XI Wiki

Scholar level 1.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. How to use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Unlocked by reaching the listed Scholar level.

How to use

Activate when you need several stratagems in succession. During its short effect, stratagem use does not spend charges and both white and black magic receive the Grimoire benefits.

The historical special ability has a two-hour recast. Kaustra and Embrava were added after the level 75 era and are not included in this reference.
