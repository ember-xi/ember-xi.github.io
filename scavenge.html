Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Searches the ground around user for items.
  • Obtained: Ranger Level 10
  • Recast Time: 3:00
  • Duration: Instant

Notes/Description

  • Recovers a percentage of expended ammo, dependent on RNG level.
    • As of 4/20/13 this ability no longer gathers Fletchings, Arrowheads, or other crafting components. If used in a zone before you have used any ammunition, it will turn up nothing.
      • Ammo conserved using Unlimited Shot or Recycle do not count as expended ammo.
    • The formula seems to be floor(ammo expended * (RNGLevel/200 + Scavenge Effect bonus)).
      • Hunter's Socks enhance Scavenge Effect.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Scavenge" <me>
