Walkthrough

  • Watch the cutscene that was triggered to end the last mission. Abquhbah will give you several choices in the cutscene.
    • By choosing Gessho you will be given some information about his whereabouts.
    • By choosing Aphmau you will be asked to go to the palace. This will end the cutscene and continue the mission.
    • By choosing Lady Karababa you will be advised not to mention that name around Naja.
  • Unequip all weapons and examine the Imperial Whitegate door at (L-9) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate. This will trigger a cutscene and the next mission.

Mission Orders
