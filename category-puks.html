Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


General Information

  • System: Dragons
  • Weaknesses: Ice, Piercing.
  • Immune: Healed by Wind attacks.

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Zephyr Mantle: Blink with about 5-10 shadows
Wind Shear: 10' AoE damage and extreme knockback, absorbed by Utsusemi
Obfuscate: 10' AoE Flash
Crosswind: Cone Attack wind damage and knockback, ignores Utsusemi
Ill Wind: AoE wind damage and single buff Dispel, ignores Utsusemi. Only used inside Mamook and during Besieged.
White Wind: HP recovery on all nearby mobs centered on the user. Only used by Notorious Monsters and during Besieged.
Absorb Aero: Wind-based spells, wind-based weaponskills, added-effect wind damage (such as from Enaero or Sirocco Kukri), and Detonation skillchain damage will heal rather than harm a Puk.
TP Regen: On Lightsday and Windsday, and during Light or Wind Weather, Puks have the ability to regenerate TP, much like the Eruca of Mount Zhayolm.
Element TP Bonus: If the element corresponding to the elemental day of the in-game Vana'diel week is used on a puk, it will get 100% TP instantly. (e.g. Using Fire II on Firesday.)
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
