Boltmaker
Description:
This special woodworking ability allows you to bundle lumber together, making it easier to process more bolts with one crystal.
Notes:
Key item that is needed to make some items within Woodworking.

Needed for Recipes

  • Acid Bolt - Woodworking (16)
  • Blind Bolt - Woodworking (16)
  • Bloody Bolt - Woodworking (16)
  • Bronze Bolt - Woodworking (15)
  • Darksteel Bolt - Woodworking (62)
  • Holy Bolt - Woodworking (16)
  • Mythril Bolt - Woodworking (46)
  • Sleep Bolt - Woodworking (16)
  • Venom Bolt - Woodworking (16)

Purchased From

Price: 10,000 Woodworking Guild Points

  • Crystal: Wind

Woodworking (Lvl~16 for most bolt types, does not include Black, Darksteel and Mythril Bolts)
Yield: Misc Bolt x 99
HQ 1: Misc Bolt x 99 *
HQ 2: Misc Bolt x 99 *

  • Crystal: Wind
  • Crafting Tool: Boltmaker
  • 1 x Bundling Twine
  • 3 x Ash Lumber
  • 3 x Misc. Bolthead
  • When bundling twine is used, Key Item: Boltmaker will activate and synth all 3 sets of ingredients. This will yield 99 Misc. Bolts even if the HQ cutscene is observed. Thus a higher level Woodworker will not get as much benefit from Boltmaker because you can't create more than 99 Bolts in a synth, and a regular synth with Boltmaker already yields 99.
