Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Doubles the effectiveness of songs.
  • Obtained: Bard Level 1
  • Recast Time: 2:00:00
  • Duration: 0:03:00

Notes/Description

  • Soul Voice is a Bard's Two Hour Ability.
  • While active, the potency of both Enfeebling Songs and Enhancing Songs are doubled, and the accuracy of Enfeebling Songs is greatly increased.
  • Typical uses for Soul Voice:
  • Enhanced Effect of Carnage Elegy on a monster.
  • Enhanced Effect of Threnody or Foe Requiem on monster.
  • Enhanced Foe Lullaby on link or overly strong monster.
  • Double Minuet or Madrigal to maximize damage output.
  • Double Mage's Ballad for maximizing MP recovery.
  • Double March for approx +40% Haste gain.
  • Songs that are already active on a character prior to the activation of Soul Voice must be replayed to receive the effects of this ability.
  • Soul Voice primarily affects song potency, but not song duration. To increase song duration, Troubadour may be used. The effects of Soul Voice and Troubadour can be combined.
  • To extend the "duration" of Soul Voice, recast your chosen songs prior to Soul Voice wearing off. Even after Soul Voice wears off, the effects of your "supercharged" songs will remain, until the songs wear off or are recasted again.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Soul Voice" <me>
  • Since Soul Voice is a two hour special, some people prefer not to make a macro for it to prevent unintentional activation.
