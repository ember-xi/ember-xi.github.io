Walkthrough

(Requires a zone change after completing the previous quest "20 in Pirate Years".)

  • Speak with Ryoma in Norg who will give you the quest and tell you to track down Ensetsu in Port Bastok (I-5). (The remainder of this quest can be done on any job.)
  • Ensetsu will tell of a new player in the story, Leodarion, who resides in Rabao.
  • Obtain an Oak Pole and then head to Rabao.
  • Leodarion will ask you for the Oak Pole. Trade it him and then you must wait until the next Vana'diel day before you may speak to him again. He will then give you the Seance Staff (key item) and tell you to head to Mhaura.
  • Head to Mhaura and catch the ferry around midnight.
  • Once you have departed, head onto the deck and a NM ghost named Enagakure will be spawned.
  • Defeat Enagakure and Leodarion will meet you when the ship docks for a cutscene completing the quest.


Game Description

Client: Ryoma (Quay, Norg)
Summary:

The trick box you brought back to Norg has been identified as a fake. Your assistance is required in finding the real box. Start your search by pressing Ensetsu in Bastok for more information.
