Unlocking a Myth (Dancer)

From Zenith XI Wiki

Permanently unlock Pyrrhic Kleos without building Terpsichore.

Contents
  1. Requirements
  2. Steps
  3. Historical point requirement
  4. Sources

Requirements

Dancer level 75 and Main Gauche from Nyzul Isle Investigation.

Steps

  1. Equip Main Gauche in the main hand as Dancer. Talk to Zalsuhm in Muckvix’s Junk Shop, Lower Jeuno (H-9), and accept his offer. If you reject it, change zones and speak again.
  2. Keep Main Gauche in the main hand while earning Weapon Skill Points against experience-yielding enemies. A weapon skill gives one point; closing level-1/2/3 skillchains gives two/three/five.
  3. Reach the point requirement. Under era scaling, your current saved Nyzul floor determines the total below. The reviewed modern server base instead uses 250 unless its era override is enabled.
  4. Trade the dagger to Zalsuhm as Dancer. Once enough points are credited, the completion event permanently unlocks Pyrrhic Kleos. You retain the dagger.
