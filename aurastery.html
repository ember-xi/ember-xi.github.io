Description

One of the five Minsitries of Maic in Windurst, which emphasizes teaching ("Aurastery" is a combination of the words "ministry" and "aural" -- hearing. Apparently the students don't get much chance to say anything.) Located in Windurst Waters, the Aurastery acts as Windurst's School of Magic, teaching Windurstrian children the basics of magic. Pechiru-Mashiru, Moreno-Toeno, Fuepepe teach here. Koru-Moru is the principal. Among the notable students are Pakuu-Nakku, Chomoro-Kyotoro and Mashuu-Ajuu

Location: Windurst Waters K-6
