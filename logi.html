Notes:

  • Appears as one of the two random sets of mobs in Wing 1 Einherjar.
  • All Logi in zone will link regardless of distance.
  • Very high Resistance to Sleep.
  • Casts Firaga III, Flare, and Burn.
  • Uses Heat Wave. The Burn from Heat Wave is extremely potent.
  • After taking a certain amount of damage, Logis will double in size. During this time Logis appear completely immune to spells such as Stun.
  • Susceptible to Silence.
