The beastmen forces tend to be grouped into tiers of 10 levels each, with certain kinds of beastmen within those tiers.

This tier describes certain beastmen leaders, all notorious monsters with a 21-24 hour respawn period. A few are placeholders for an HNM.

Overlords

Sometimes, a high notorious monster will lottery spawn instead of ordinary beastman leader. They are the figureheads of the major beastmen forces, and may take days to spawn.

For the beastmen forces of the Near East, however, their purpose is slightly different. They drop 2 items each, a low amount of gil, and reward no experience points. They can also appear in the Commoner's Ward during level 5 or higher raids on Al Zahbi if they are up in their stronghold when their respective army begins to advance. If they are defeated, the beastmen will immediately call a retreat.
