Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Terror is a harmful status effect that makes the player immobile with fear until it wears off or is removed. Afflicted characters cannot move, attack, cast spells, or use abilities. It also freezes the character model.

How to remove the effect

  • Nothing can remove this effect except waiting for it to wear off (generally a short period of time).

How the effect is inflicted

Meritable Abilities

  • Beastmaster - Feral Howl

Spells

  • Blue Magic - Jettatura

Monster Abilities

  • Qiqirn - Faze
  • Hippogryphs - Jettatura
  • Wyrms - Absolute Terror
  • Brigandish Blade - Additional Effect on melee attacks
  • Biast - Additional Effect on melee attacks
  • Shii - Additional Effect on melee attacks
  • Alexander - Void of Repentance
