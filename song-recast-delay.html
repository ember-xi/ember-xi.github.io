Song Recast Delay

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is the time one must wait before a song is able to be sung again.

  • Sha'ir Gages reduce the recast delay for all songs by 2 seconds.
  • Sheikh Gages reduce the recast delay for all songs by 4 seconds.
  • Volunteer's Nails During besieged, reduces the recast for all songs by 4 seconds.
