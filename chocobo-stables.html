Chocobo Stables

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

The Chocobo Stables are places where you can rent a chocobo for riding and in some special locations raise your own chocobo.

Locations

Cities

There is a stable in each of the major cities and some even have multiple stables (Jeuno).

  • Southern San d'Oria (Has a VCS representative)
  • Windurst Woods (Has a VCS representative)
  • Bastok Mines (Has a VCS representative)
  • Lower Jeuno
  • Upper Jeuno
  • Port Jeuno
  • Kazham
  • Rabao
  • Norg
  • Al Zahbi

Other locations

There are nine "Chocogals" who offer rental-only services and they are located at the following locations:

  • Konschtat Highlands - Plaiaude (J-6)
  • Tahrongi Canyon - Pucotte (G-10)
  • La Theine Plateau - Coumaine (K-8)
  • Eastern Altepa Desert - Eulaclaire (G-7)
  • Yhoator Jungle - Paurelde (F-9)
  • Wajaom Woodlands - Watisa (G-8)
