Notes

  • Naji is an important part of the Bastok Missions.
  • He is a member of the Mythril Musketeers.
  • Ranks at number five in the Mythril Musketeer hierarchy. In spite of being among Bastok's five most elite soldiers, Naji is usually given menial tasks and is often the target of teasing from his comrades.
  • Their comments are referenced in Riding on the Clouds where his description is, "Bastok's number five 'doorman'."
  • Son of Yasin.
