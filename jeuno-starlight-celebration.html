Jeuno Starlight Celebration
Single by Naoshi Mizuta
Released December 7th, 2005
Recorded ???
Length 4:57
Label Square Enix

Jeuno -Starlight Celebration- was released in game on December 6, 2004. The song was made available via downloading it from iTunes, where it was made available on December 7, 2005. The song is also included on the Final Fantasy XI Unreleased Tracks which was released on March 28, 2007 in the premium box limited edition.

The music has been played in Port Jeuno, Lower Jeuno, and Upper Jeuno during the Starlight Celebration event since 2004, and can also be heard in Dynamis - Tavnazia.
