Walkthrough

Note* If you have RDM AF2 or AF3 quests "Enveloped in Darkness" or "Peace for the Spirit" open you will NOT be able to turn this quest in for the reward until the RDM AF quests are completed.

  • Talk to Curilla for a cutscene; agree twice to start the quest, and obtain Curilla's Bottle (key item).
  • Go through Yughott Grotto and exit at J-9 into a small open area of Horlais Peak.
  • Once in Horlais Peak, head south to the Hot Springs, and select it to fill the water bottle (which causes the description to change for the key item.)
  • Return to Chateau d'Oraguille and talk to Curilla to complete the quest.
Notes
The Norg quest Secret of the Damp Scroll also requires going to the Hot Springs, and can be done at the same time. Also, Curilla will not activate this quest if there are any RDM AF quests activated that involve her.
