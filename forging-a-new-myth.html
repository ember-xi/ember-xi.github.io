Forging a New Myth

From Zenith XI Wiki

Obtain the sealed Mythic weapon, then pass the Zahak and Balrahn trial.

Contents
  1. Requirements
  2. Before the battlefield
  3. Battlefield: 30 minutes
  4. Sources

Requirements

Duties, Tasks, and Deeds completed; Paparoon’s sealed invitation; all three trophies below. Use the job that can equip your Mythic.

Before the battlefield

  1. Examine the Imperial Whitegate palace door in Aht Urhgan Whitegate. Receive the sealed Mythic weapon and one Imperial Gold Piece.
  2. Collect the three tier-four Zeni Notorious Monsters trophies below. Trade them together to Seaprince's Tombstone at the cemetery in Caedarva Mire (E-10), reached from Nashmau’s west exit.
  3. Receive the Serpentking Zahak relief. Travel to the Nyzul Isle Staging Point and choose Forging a New Myth at the Runic Seal (I-9).

Battlefield: 30 minutes

  1. Equip the sealed Mythic in its proper weapon slot. RNG and COR need appropriate ammunition. Original battle rules do not permit Trusts.
  2. Against Zahak, land three weapon skills with the required weapon equipped to remove the three heads. Misses do not count; pet Blood Pacts do not replace weapon skills.
  3. When Balrahn appears, let him use your weapon’s Mythic skill first. This enables the trial’s Aftermath sequence, even if you already unlocked that skill.
  4. Use that Mythic weapon skill at 1000–1999 TP for Aftermath 1, at 2000–2999 TP for Aftermath 2, then at 3000 TP for Aftermath 3. Check the effect after each use. Old era guides call these 100%, 200% and 300%.
  5. If a higher Aftermath prevents a lower tier, remove and re-equip the weapon to clear it, then redo the missing tier. Do not rely on reducing Balrahn to zero HP to solve the trial.
  6. Complete the victory scene to receive the Serpentking Zahak relief shard. Continue to Coming Full Circle.

Failure consumes the entry relief; obtain and trade another full trophy set for a retry.

Zenith status: original level-75 reference route. The complete weapon-building quest chain has not been verified as playable on Zenith. Do not assume that having these wiki pages means the NPC hand-ins or final battlefield are installed.
