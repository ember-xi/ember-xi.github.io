Puppetry Attire Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

These strong coloured cloths in red and black reminds of a jester or a medieval showman costume and indeed it's for the puppetmaster. It's his main job to entertain the public with his Automaton. This set reflects the strong bondship between the puppetmaster and his automaton very well and provides a variety of Automaton enhancements and boosts. For a fee you can store the set at any Armor Storer.


Number of Pieces: 5   Cost to store: 1000 gil   Storage marker: Puppetry Attire Claim Slip
