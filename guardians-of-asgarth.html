Guardian of Asgarth

Each chamber in Einherjar contains a boss-type monster. These monsters are known as The Guardians of Asgarth.

Wing I

  • Hakenmann (Orobon)
  • Hildesvini (Marid)
  • Himinrjot (Buffalo)
  • Hraesvelg (Manticore)
  • Morbol Emperor (Morbol)
  • Nihhus (Wivre)

Wing II

  • Andhrimnir (Corse)
  • Ariri Samariri (Poroggo)
  • Balrahn (Soulflayers)
  • Hrungnir (Golem)
  • Mokkuralfi (Flan)
  • Tanngrisnir (Dahak)

Wing III

  • Dendainsonne
  • Freke (Cerberus)
  • Gorgimera (Khimaira)
  • Motosgnir (Dvergr)
  • Stoorworm (Hydra)
  • Vampyr Jarl (Vampyr)

Valgrind

  • Odin (Avatar)
