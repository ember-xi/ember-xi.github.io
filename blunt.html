Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Different attacks do different types of damage, and different enemies may be resistant or particularly vulnerable to certain damage types. The basic types are:

Damage Type Weapon Types
Blunt Hand-to-Hand, Club, Staff, Harlequin Frame, Stormwaker Frame
Slashing Axe, Great Axe, Great Sword, Sword, Scythe, Katana, Great Katana, Valoredge Frame
Piercing Dagger, Polearm, Archery, Marksmanship, Shuriken, Sharpshot Frame

Exceptions

Some weapons have a different damage type than usual for their category.

  • Rapier and Epee-type Swords do Piercing damage. Some examples: Joyeuse, Degen, Fleuret.
    • Bilbo-type Swords such as the Tuck use the same model as Rapiers but do Slashing damage.
  • The Bourdonasse Polearm does Blunt damage.
  • The Crude Sword Great Sword does Blunt damage.
  • The Honebami Great Katana does Blunt damage.
  • The Chiroptera Dagger Dagger does Slashing damage.
  • The Vampiric Claws Hand-to-Hand weapon does Slashing damage.
  • When using the Monk Job Ability "Formless Strikes", the damage type of melee strikes and kick attacks is nonphysical, nonelemental. This damage will not be subject to any of the damage-type specific bonuses or penalties below.

Despite having an uncharacteristic damage type when meleeing, these exceptions still use normal damage types during weapon skills. For example, when performing a weapon skill with Vampiric Claws or while using Formless Strikes and any Hand-to-Hand weapon, the damage type is Blunt.
