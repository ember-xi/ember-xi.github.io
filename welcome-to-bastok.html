Walkthrough

  • Talk to Powhatan; he's foolishly told someone coming into the port that he'll meet him wearing a shell shield when he doesn't have one.
  • Obtain a Shell Shield and equip it.
  • Talk with the clerk Bartolomeo in the Arrivals section of the Air Travel Agency. This triggers a cut-scene where an NPC named Evrain appears. You exchange greetings and then he finds his own way to the the tavern where Powhatan is waiting.
  • Talk to Powhatan again to complete the quest.
    • You keep the Shell Shield.

Game Description

Client: Powhatan (Steaming Sheep Restaurant, Port Bastok)
Summary:

Greet Powhattan's guest at the air travel agency. Wear a shell shield so he'll know who you are.
