Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Reduces delay for two-handed weapons when using Last Resort by 5% per merit.
  • Obtained: Dark Knight Level 75 Merit Group 2
  • Job Traits are always active.

Notes

  • The effect on delay is applied in the same way as Haste (not in a separate stage like for Dual Wield).
  • Although equivalent to Haste, the extra amount can break through the 70% cap.
