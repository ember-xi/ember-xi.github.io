Walkthrough

  • Talk to Ahaadah (G-6) (upper level) for a cutscene.
  • Go to (I-11), down the ramp and by 2 barrels under a platform for the next cutscene. (Stand under the shelter, to the right, as if you were a third barrel.)
  • Go to (I-12), upper level, at the end of the walk for another cutscene.
  • Take the (H-7) exit to Bhaflau Thickets
  • Find an ??? in the bushes in (I-8), and, when ready, check it to spawn the Plague Chigoe (NM).
    • Caution! Plague Chigoe (NM) has an added effect of Aspir (~1-8 MP/hit) and is approximately a level 75 mob who hits hard and very, very fast. It seems to have a very high rate of Triple Attack
  • Has approximately 4800 HP.
  • Defeat it and check the ??? again for a cutscene and the Map of Caedarva Mire.

Game Description

Client: Ahaadah (Philosopher's Alley, Aht Urhgan Whitegate)
Summary:

Alcander and Ahaadah want you to help Tinga-Matonga become a respectable citizen.
