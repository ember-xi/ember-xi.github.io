Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 21: Finders Keepers.
  • Access through the Gates of Halvung if using the overland route.

Starting point

Decorative Bronze Gate · Navukgo Execution Chamber

Walkthrough

  1. Reach Mount Zhayolm and travel toward Navukgo Execution Chamber at D-8. The overland Gates of Halvung route requires a Cast Metal Plate holder to open the gate.
  2. Enter Navukgo Execution Chamber for one scene, then examine the Decorative Bronze Gate for another.
  3. Examine the gate again, select Shield of Diplomacy, and defeat Khimaira 13 within 30 minutes.
  4. Keep Karababa alive during the battle; do not let her remain at critically low HP. Finish the victory scene.

Battle and progress notes

  • The native battlefield allows Trusts and up to six participants; this is a source check, not a live solo-clear test.
  • The battle script flags Karababa spell behavior as incomplete. Plan your own healing and damage rather than relying on the retail description of her spell support.

Completion and reward

Karababa's Bodyguard title.

What this opens

  • ToAU 23: Social Graces
