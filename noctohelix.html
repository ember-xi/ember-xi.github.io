Noctohelix

From Zenith XI Wiki

SCH 73 in the level 75 era · Dark damage over time · Elemental magic.

Contents
  1. Learn the spell
  2. Use
  3. Sources

Learn the spell

  1. Buy its scroll from Layton in The Eldieme Necropolis (S) (J-8), or the Auction House.
  2. Use the scroll at the required Scholar level.

Use

  1. Select Dark Arts. Use Voidstorm to provide matching weather when helpful.
  2. Cast Noctohelix on the enemy. It deals initial damage followed by repeated damage. Avoid applying it to a target your party needs to keep asleep.
  3. Use Modus Veritas after the Helix lands if you want to concentrate its damage into a shorter remaining period.

Historical learning level shown. Later retail moved Helix spells much earlier; this page does not assume Zenith has reverted those changes.
