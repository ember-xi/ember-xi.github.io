Zenith availability

Reference route: the native quest contains post-instance handlers, but the pinned Everbloom Hollow tree has only the doomvoid instance. The Young Behemoth mission instance was not present in the inspected base; completion on Zenith is not confirmed.

Before you begin

  • Complete The Price of Valor.
  • Bring Hatchets and leave inventory space for the quest’s supplied hatchet.

Starting point

Rholont · Southern San d'Oria (S) · E-7

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Rholont for Letter to Count Aurchiat, then examine Shimmering Pondweed at Pashhow Marshlands (S) G-6.
  2. In Jugner Forest (S), examine Overgrown Mushrooms beside the F-10 path. Receive the hatchet before continuing.
  3. Use Hatchets at Jugner Forest (S) Logging Points until you receive Length of Jugner Ivy. Return to the Overgrown Mushrooms for another scene.
  4. Examine Mossy Stump at southeast F-7 for its scene, then again to enter the Young Behemoth battlefield.
  5. Defeat Young Behemoth, finish the return scene and report to Rongelouts N Distaud at Southern San d’Oria (S) I-9.

Battle and progress notes

  • The reference battle allows six participants and 30 minutes. Failed attempts require a new Length of Jugner Ivy; logging success is not guaranteed on the first attempt.

Completion and reward

Behemoth Horn; reference title: Honorary Knight of the Cardinal Stag.

What this opens

  • Crossroads of Time progression
  • Songbirds in a Snowstorm after The Will of the World
