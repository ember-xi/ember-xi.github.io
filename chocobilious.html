Walkthrough

  • Talk to Kuoh Rhel to learn that a Chocobo is sick and she needs help getting the medicine to cure him.
  • Go talk to Tapoh Lihzeh at I-8 in Windurst Woods, then trade her the Papaka Grass.
  • Now go and talk to Kuoh Rhel again to let her know the medicine is on its way, and receive your reward.

Game Description

Client: Kuoh Rhel (Chocobo Stables, Windurst Woods)
Summary:

One of the Chocobos in the stables has taken ill. Kuoh Rhel has asked the Mithran apothecary to make her "secret cure-all medicament" for the sick bird, but when you go to pick it up, the apothecary says she is lacking one of the ingredients...
