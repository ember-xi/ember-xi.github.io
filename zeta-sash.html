Zeta Sash

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-75 waist equipment: DEF 6, Attack +10; an additional critical-hit bonus while in Salvage.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Jobs
  3. Sources

How to obtain

  1. Reach Second Lieutenant Assault rank.
  2. Complete Wake the Puppet in the Periqia Assault area.
  3. Obtain and appraise the ??? Sash reward for a chance at Zeta Sash.

Jobs

WAR, RDM, THF, PLD, DRK, BST, BRD, RNG, SAM, NIN, DRG, BLU, COR and DNC. Rare/Exclusive; cannot be bought at the Auction House.
