Notes

  • All have the Dhalmel special attacks.
  • Nenaunir can cast Diaga, Bind and Stone II.
  • All are immune to Charm and Lullaby.
  • Nenaunir is immune to Sleep and Silence.
  • Nenaunir's Wife is susceptible to Sleep, Silence, and Soporific.
  • All are susceptible to Bind and Gravity.
