Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Sythesis Image Support (SIS) is a beneficial status effect that focuses your mind on the task at hand until it wears off. Benefitting characters have an increase to one craft skill for a short period of time.

  • Regular image support raises your skill by 1
  • For all guilds except fishing, advanced image support raises your skill by 3.
  • For the fishing guild, advanced image support raises your skill by 2.
  • Advanced synthesis image support also costs a small amount of gil, dependant on your current craft rank. In Aht Urhgan areas, Advanced synthesis image support costs 1 Imperial Bronze Piece.
  • Guild-obtained image synthesis support belts give the equivalent bonus to craft skill as Advanced synthesis image support for that guild.

How to remove the effect

  • This effect cannot be removed manually.
  • This effect will wear off in the event of death.
  • This effect will be superceded by synthesis image support for any craftskill except the craftskill currently supported by the effect.
