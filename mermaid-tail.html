Statistics

Mermaid tail
This shriveled tail is often mistaken
for that of a mermaid. However, it is
simply the mummified remains of a
pugil, used by the Sahagin when
bartering with the pirates of Norg.

Other Uses

Guild Points Value: None

Used in Quests: Beastman Treasure (to obtain the Mermaid's Ring)

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
