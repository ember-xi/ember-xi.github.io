Walkthrough

  • Talk to Norejaie in the Lion Springs Tavern. She asks you to help with the extermination of a monster infesting Ordelle's Caves.
    • If you cannot get the quest Norejaie will tell you either:
      • Something about the food (Already completed Eco-Warrior this conquest period)
      • "...Can I have a moment of your time? Oh, but you look busy..." (Eco-Warrior from another nation is logged)
  • Collect or join an alliance of 15-18 people. See notes below for preparation.
  • Go to Ordelle's Caves using the entrance at the south end of the Ravine at F-7 in La Theine Plateau.
  • Talk to Rojaireaut to have him apply the ointment. This caps your level at 20.
    • Make sure everyone gets the ointment, if any alliance member does not receive it then the monster will not spawn.
  • From Rojaireaut take the path to the left (head east, through the puddle) and take the long corridor.
    • There will be Goblins at (I-4) that need to be defeated one at a time (nominate a puller) and Hognosed Bats and Goblins at (H-7), and more Goblins at (G-7).
  • The room here opens up at (G-7). Stay to the right wall & head into the south tunnel at (F-9)
  • At (F-10), the tunnel opens up into the room with the NM. Clear the room of mobs.
  • The ??? is located in the Northeast corner of the large room on a Stalagmite (G-10). The designated tank/puller should spawn the NM Necroplasm by touching the ???.
  • Defeat the Necroplasm. For this fight, only those with the level cap can assist in this fight. Anyone else trying to help will receive a message telling them they cannot.
  • Examine the ??? to receive the Key Item: Indigested Stalagmite.
  • After the battle, you may want to remove your level cap to cast Raise or Tele. To do this, you need to do one of the following:
    • Logout/Login (probably easiest)
    • Talk to Rojaireaut
    • Zone
  • Talk to Norejaie complete the quest.
Notes
  • You can only have one Eco-Warrior quest active at any time. This includes Eco-Warrior (Windurst), Eco-Warrior (San d'Oria) and Eco-Warrior (Bastok).
  • Eco-Warrior quests cannot be deactivated.
  • Only one Eco-Warrior quest can be completed once per Conquest Tally.
  • It will require a full alliance (15-18 players) to defeat Necroplasm. They must all have accepted the quest and should all have level at least 18.
  • There is a level cap so alliance members should all have armor that can be worn by a level 20.
  • Make sure everyone understands not to start the battle prematurely by checking the ??? before everyone is ready. Attempting to simultaneously battle the Necroplasm and the aggressive monsters that patrol the room will lead to the death of many, if not all, of the alliance members.

Game Description

Client: Norejaie (Lion Springs Tavern, Southern San d'Oria)
Summary:

Norejaie wants you to exterminate an ecological threat, and bring her evidence of the feat.
