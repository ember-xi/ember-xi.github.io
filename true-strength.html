de:Wahre Stärke This is the third Monk Artifact Armor quest - otherwise known as AF3.

  Start NPC:   Ayame - Metalworks (K-7)
  Requirements:
   Monk 50+
  Items Needed:   Yagudo Drink
  Title:   Paragon of Monk Excellence
  Repeatable:   Yes, after AF is complete.
  Reward:   Temple Hose

  Previous Quest:   The First Meeting
  Next Quest:   None

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Ayame in Metalworks.
  • She asks you to defeat a powerful Yagudo Huu Xalmo the Savage in Castle Oztroja and recover the Xalmo Feather as proof.
  • You will need to trade a Yagudo Drink to a ??? on map 4 H-8 of Castle Oztroja to spawn Huu Xalmo the Savage.
  • Return to Ayame and trade the Xalmo Feather to receive the Temple Hose.

Game Description

Client: Ayame (Cannonry, Metalworks)
Summary:

You must defeat a powerful Yagudo in Castle Oztroja. The Yagudo in question can be lured out with a bottle of Yagudo drink.
