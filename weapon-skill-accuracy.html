Weapon Skill Accuracy

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Weapon Skill Accuracy is a stat that is added to the regular accuracy stat when using weapon skills.

For Example: When wearing Abyss Cape (Accuracy +7, Weapon Skill Accuracy +7) a Dark Knight will gain +7 accuracy for their regular melee attacks, and +14 accuracy for their weapon skill attacks.
