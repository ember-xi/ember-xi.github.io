Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Flans are one of the few varieties of natural wildlife left on Mount Zhayolm. Although their bodies are pliable and gelatinous, flans also possess large, sharp eyes used in searching out prey, and a flexible mouth filled with fangs. Despite their outward appearance, flans are also known for being highly intelligent, and reports of flan using extraordinarily powerful magic are not uncommon. However, they are unskilled in suppressing their emotions, and a flan's mood tends to have an enormous effect on its actions. Flans live in groups that seem to be connected in some inexplicable manner... or so it is said, but not much is currently known due to the dangers of researching such formidable creatures.

General Information

  • System: Amorphs
  • Weaknesses: Fire
  • Immune: Physical attacks
  • Detects by Sight
  • Detects on Job Abilities
  • Possess Resist Silence.
  • Flan take more damage from magic than normal.
  • Will switch between different "modes" under unknown conditions.Verification Needed When in the "angry/spikey" mode, they will take more magical damage, and less physical damage, than usual. Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Boiling Point: Cone Attack Magic Defense Down
Amorphic Spikes: Single-target, 5 hit attack
Amplification: Magic Attack Bonus and Magic Defense Bonus
Synergism: AOE Drain from other nearby Puddings. This will wake any hurt flan.
Xenoglossia: Next spell will be cast instantly. Only used by Notorious Monsters.
Amorphic Scythe: Conic 2-hit physical attack. Only used by custards.
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
