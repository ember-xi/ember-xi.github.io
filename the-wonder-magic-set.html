Walkthrough

  • Talk to Panta-Putta, located at the merchant's house at G-10 in Lower Jeuno, for a cutscene.
  • Talk to him again after cutscene. He wants you to bring him the Wonder Magic Set.
  • Go to Port Jeuno and talk to Karl.You will then have to trade him a White Rock to obtain the Wonder Magic Set.
  • Trade the Wonder Magic Set to Panta-Putta to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Panta-Putta (Merchant's House, Lower Jeuno)
Summary:

You are to bring Panta the popular Wonder Magic Set. Don't say a word to his family, or else.
