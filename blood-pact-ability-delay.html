"Blood Pact" Ability Delay

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Avatars have a number of ablities called Blood Pacts. Like all ablities, Blood Pacts have a recast timer, which has a base recast of 60 seconds. This quality affects both Blood Pact: Rage and Blood Pact: Ward.
Every point in delay is one second, e.g. a delay by -2 means that blood pacts can be executed every 58 seconds.
