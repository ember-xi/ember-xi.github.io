Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

File:Copy Image (Status Effect).png Copy Image is a beneficial status effect that provides shadowy simulacrum to absorb enemy attacks until it wears off or is removed. Benefiting characters gain a number of copy images that absorb normal attacks, most spells, some monster abilities, and are more effective than the shadow images given by Blink. Once an image absorbs an attack, it is spent.


How to remove the effect

  • The effect can be manually removed.
  • The effect wears off on its own after 15 minutes has passed.
  • The effect expires once all copy images are gone.
    • Attacks, spells, and abilities can remove zero or more images at a time.
    • Previous to the September 14, 2004 update, Utsusemi was more resilient and consistent against enemy's attacks; Whirl of Rage, for example, always removed one copy image prior to the update. Post update, Utsusemi interacts with attacks by foes as follows:
Loses no copy image, and takes no damage
  • The caster successfully evades or parries a physical attack.
Loses one copy image
  • The caster is hit by a single physical hit or WS.
  • The caster is hit by a single-target spell.
Loses a variable number of copy images, and can take damage
  • The caster is hit by a multiple-attack WS, such as Jet Stream, or by a physical AoE attack, such as Condemnation or Whirl of Rage.
  • The number of shadows lost and the damage taken by the caster are determined by the potency of the attack as well as the player's Ninjutsu skill level, though to what extent these factors play are unknown.
Loses all copy images, and takes damage and/or becomes enfeebled
  • The caster is hit by an AoE magical WS such as Blood Saber, or an AoE spell such as Firaga.
  • The caster is hit by an AoE enfeebling spell like Breakga.
  • Note: Some spells (e.g. Diaga) and WS's (e.g. Negative Whirl) can strip copy images, damage, and enfeeble at the same time.
Loses no copy image, but takes damage
  • The caster is hit by a breath (e.g. Heat Breath), Self-Destruct type, or Throat Stab like attack.
Loses no copy image, but becomes enfeebled
  • The caster is hit by certain enfeebling WS's, such as Chaotic Eye or Jamming Wave.
  • Note that this does not apply to spells:
    • Single-target enfeebling spells are treated the same as any other single-target spell and will remove one copy image.
    • Multi-target spells such as Breakga will strip all copy images and enfeeble the caster.


How the effect is inflicted/gained

Spells

  • Ninjutsu
    • Utsusemi: Ichi: Gives the caster three copy images.
    • Utsusemi: Ni: Gives the caster four copy images if Ninja is currently the main job; gives three shadows instead if Ninja is the support job.
  • Notes:
    • Utsusemi: Ni overwrites both Utsusemi: Ni and Utsusemi: Ichi.
    • Utsusemi: Ichi overwrites Utsusemi: Ichi, but does not overwrite Utsusemi: Ni.
      • Casting the Ichi version while the Ni version is still on results in no effect; the player is left with however many copy images still on the Ni.
      • Players can manually cancel the effect of Ni before or during the casting of Ichi in order to successfully complete the casting of Ichi.
    • A player cannot have both the Copy Image and the Blink status effect at the same time.
      • Copy Image has higher priority than the Blink status effect. Both Utsusemi: Ichi and Utsusemi: Ni overwrite the Blink status effect, while Blink and similar spells (e.g. Aerial Armor, Zephyr Mantle) do not overwrite the Copy Image effect.
