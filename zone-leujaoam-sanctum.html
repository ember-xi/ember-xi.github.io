Additional level-75 reference


The Leujaoam Sanctum is a mysterious network of caves undrneath the Caedarva Mire on the south-western side of the Arrapago Islands. The Leujaoam Sanctum is guarded by the Aht Urhgan forces at the Azouph Isle Staging Point and is home to several types of unique flaura and fauna.

This area is an Assault area. This area is only accessible if the Treasures of Aht Urhgan expansion is installed, The Road to Aht Urhgan quest is completed, and Aht Urhgan Mission 3: President Salaheem is completed.

A cage of Azouph Fireflies is given to all participating mercenaries who enter. They can be used to return to the Azouph Isle Staging Point.
