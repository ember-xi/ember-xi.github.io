Statistics

Flaxen Pouch

Description: Jakaka gave you this bag and told you to travel to the top of Parradamo Tor. Are you supposed to collect something...?

Other Uses

Guild Points Value: N/A

Used in Quests: Boneyard Gully ENM

How to Obtain

Not Auctionable

Notes

  • To enter the battlefield, talk to Jakaka in the Attohwa Chasm (G-10).
  • Jakaka will give you the Flaxen Pouch.
  • At the J-9 on top of Parradamo Tor you will find the Cradle of Rebirth, trade the Flaxen Pouch to the Cradle of Rebirth to receive the Parradamo Stones.
  • Return to Jakaka and trade him the Parradamo Stones to receive the Miasma Filter key item.
