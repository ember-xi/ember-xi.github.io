Overview

Great Axes are two handed weapons primarily used by the Warrior job, although they can be, and sometimes are, used by a Dark Knight. They are very high damage and delay weapons, and their weaponskills are powerful.

Great Axe is a unique weapon class in that some of its weaponskills, such as Sturmwind or Keen Edge, are intended to simply produce large amounts of damage, while the series of "Break" weaponskills (such as Armor Break or Weapon Break) are intended to cripple the enemy's combat effectiveness while dealing moderate damage; the wielder essentially "enfeebles" the enemy with his or her weaponskill. This lends the Great Axe user great versatility in combat.
