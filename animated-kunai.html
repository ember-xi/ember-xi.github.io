Notes:

  • When pulled, the Animated Kunai spawns 4 Satellite Kunais which assist it during the fight.
  • After a short period of time has passed, the Animated Kunai will cast Warp and despawn. There is no known reliable method to prevent or delay this from happening, short of killing it before it attempts to Warp.
  • The Animated Kunai can use the weapon skill Blade: Metsu.
  • Demoniac Fragment drops 100% of the time if the 15 Demon Notorious Monsters in Dynamis - Xarcabard have already been defeated and it will not drop the item if they have not all been defeated.
  • Immune to: Stun, Sleep, Lullaby
