Zenith availability

Reference walkthrough. Completion is unconfirmed in the reviewed Zenith base: no dedicated quest handler or matching progression hook was found in the inspected zone/NPC files. A missing interaction may reflect unfinished content.

Before you begin

  • Complete The Long March North.
  • Bring a Bottled Pixie and any job Testimony; arrange access beyond Inner Horutoto Ruins’ Three Mage Gate.

Starting point

Zone-entry cutscene · Windurst Waters [S]

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Windurst Waters [S]. Collect the following three key items in any order.
  2. Irradiant strand: in present-day Inner Horutoto Ruins, examine Gate of Darkness (I-7, map 4). Enter from East Sarutabaruta J-7 and pass the Three Mage Gate using a Portal Charm or a WHM/BLM/RDM group; an already accessible Toraimarai Canal route also reaches this room.
  3. Scintillant strand: examine Fey Blossoms in Grauberg [S] (F-5) twice, then trade a Bottled Pixie between 18:00 and 06:00. Yagudo High Priests in Meriphataud Mountains [S] and Castle Oztroja [S] drop the bottle.
  4. Refulgent strand: examine the Writhing Flame in Castle Oztroja [S] (G-7, first-map north hallway), then trade any job Testimony.
  5. Speak to Velda-Galda in Windurst Waters [S] (K-9) three times to deliver the strands in separate scenes.
  6. Wait until the next Vana’diel day and speak to Velda-Galda again to collect the reward.

Battle and progress notes

  • The Testimony need not match your current job. The final potion depends on your current job.

Completion and reward

A job-dependent attribute potion; Karaha-Baruha’s Research Assistant title.

What this opens

  • Windurst route prerequisite for continuing Crossroads of Time.
  • Sins of the Mothers, after The Will of the World.
