Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Dodges the next attack.
  • Obtained: Samurai Level 15
  • Recast Time: :60
  • Duration: :30

Notes/Description

  • When active, user will anticipate and evade the next attack against him/her.
  • Also anticipates multi-hit attacks entirely.
  • Physical Area of Effect attacks will strip Third Eye, and the user of Third Eye will not evade the attack.
  • Combined with the Saotome Haidate, Third Eye has a chance to Counter an attack instead of simply evading it. It must be worn at the time of the attack, rather than the time Third Eye is used.

Effect with Seigan On

  • While Seigan is in effect, the recast time for the Third Eye job ability will be decreased by half.
  • The player may counterattack or keep the effect of Third Eye when the player evades an attack using the Third Eye job ability.
  • The chance that the player will keep the Third Eye effect even after evading will gradually decrease from the time the ability is used.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Third Eye" <me>
