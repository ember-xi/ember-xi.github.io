Walkthrough

  • After defeating the three Mammets-19 Epsilon (Mission 2-5), you will appear in South Gustaberg.
  • Head to Port Bastok and approach the Departures Entrance of the Air Travel Agency to receive a cutscene.
  • Go to the Metalworks in Bastok Markets and talk to Cid.
