Messenger from Beyond

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is the first White Mage Artifact Armor quest - otherwise known as AF1.

  Start NPC:   Narcheral - Northern San d'Oria (M-6)
  Requirements:
   White Mage 40+
  Items Needed:   Tavnazia Pass
  Title:   None
  Repeatable:   Yes, after AF is complete.
  Reward:   Blessed Hammer

  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   Prelude of Black and White

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Narcheral (M-6) upstairs in the cathedral in Northern San d'Oria and then head to Valkurm Dunes around B-8 (Secret Beach).
    • NOTE -- If you attempt to kill the NM and are defeated, you will have to wait until the next Vana'diel day before attempting again.
    • You can pop this NM on a different job, provided you receive the quest as White Mage
  • Wait until night (18:00-06:00) and a ??? will appear. The one on the wall near the Song Runes is the one you want. Click on it to spawn the NM Shadow Marchelute.
  • Defeat Marchelute and it will drop a Tavnazia Pass.
  • Trade the item to Narcheral to complete the quest.
    • You must be WHM when you trade the pass to him.

Game Description

Client: Narcheral (Cathedral, Northern San d'Oria)
Summary:

On the westernmost dune of Valkurm, a dark stalker lurks. Defeat it and bring back its Tavnazia pass.
