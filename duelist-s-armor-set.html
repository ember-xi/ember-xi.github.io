Duelist's Armor Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

An armor set from the Dynamis zones, which the player may be rewarded with for defeating the enemies within. The set maintains a red-and-black motif, however the color has shifted more towards blacks and grays, with the Legs, Feet and Hands primarily gray/black with hints of red. The body piece has a dark gray front with a gold emblem on the chest, and black and red stripes going down each arm. The headpiece bears a silver design on a black background, with a red rim.

The Duelist's set adds a slew of benefits for the Red Mage both offensively and defensively, complementing their label as a "Jack of all trades". The set gives balanced enhancements to MND, INT, Healing Magic Skill, Elemental Magic Skill (with a Magic Attack Bonus to go with it), Enhancing Magic Skill and perhaps most critically Enfeebling Magic Skill. A Magic Defense Bonus and an elemental resistance help protect the RDM from offensive magics, and an overall boost to "Spike" spells helps deter melee attacks. Finally, an enhancement to Fast Cast along with a Refresh effect round out the ensemble.

Number of Pieces: 5

Cost to store: 2000g
