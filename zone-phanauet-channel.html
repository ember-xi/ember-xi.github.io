Additional level-75 reference

The Carpenters' Landing Barge allows players to travel around this zone for a small fee.

This area is only accessible if the Chains of Promathia expansion is installed.

The full route of the Carpenters' Landing Barge is: South Landing -> Central Landing via Emfea Waterway -> South Landing via Newtpool -> North Landing -> Central Landing -> South Landing via Newtpool
