Solo Tips

  1. Trusts for 1→50: Kupipi (WHM) + Trion or Valaineral (tank) + Shantotto (BLM) + a damage dealer (Zeid / Lion).
  2. From 50: switch the tank to Curilla or Rahal, the healer to Apururu (Reraise), and add Ulmia (BRD) for Songs.
  3. Fight yellow/orange monsters (Even Match / Tough). Red is not worth the risk.
  4. Rest between fights: resting MP is fast on EmberXI (12, then +4 per tick).
  5. Change zone about every 8 levels; there is no point staying where everything checks blue.
