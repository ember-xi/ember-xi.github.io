Zephyr

From Zenith XI Wiki

A level-72 throwing ammunition item associated with Sky battles.

Obtain and use

  1. Defeat Byakko in Ru'Aun Gardens to obtain Zephyr.
  2. Equip it in the ammo slot. It has DMG 250 and Delay 276.
  3. Its special Sky use is a successful ranged hit against Seiryu to remove his additional elemental damage effect. It is consumed when thrown.
