Equipment

This is an Equipment slot where you equip your:

  • Bows for Archery
  • Crossbows and Guns for Marksmanship
  • Throwing weapons that are reuseable, such as Boomerangs and Chakrams
  • String Instruments
  • Wind Instruments
  • Fishing Gear such as Fishing Poles

Auction House

This is also a category at the Auction House that contains:

  • Bows for Archery
  • Crossbows and Guns for Marksmanship
  • Permanent throwing weapons such as Boomerangs and Chakrams

Instruments and Fishing Gear are found in other areas of the Auction House.
