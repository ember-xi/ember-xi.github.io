Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Start NPC: Fari-Wari - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (Shararat Teahouse)
Requirements:
 Treasures of Aht Urhgan
Rughadjeen present in Al Zahbi
Items Needed: ???
Title: No
Repeatable: No
Reward: 1 Imperial Gold Piece

Previous Quest: Saga of the Skyserpent
Next Quest: Soothing Waters

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Fari-Wari at the teahouse for another cutscene. Afterwards, you will obtain Biyaada's Letter. This will unlock the two subquests that must be completed to proceed:
    1. When the Bow Breaks
    2. Fist of the People
  2. After completing both quests, return to Fari-Wari for your reward.

Note: Skyserpent General Rughadjeen must be in the Commoner's Ward/Al Zahbi (G-7) for this quest to be accessible or completable. If he is being held prisoner talking to Fari-Wari will not lead to a cutscene. This quest can be started immediately after finishing the pre-requisite quest.

Game Description

Client:

Fari-Wari - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (Shararat Teahouse)

Summary:

Fari-Wari is working on the sequel to his masterpiece and has asked you to interview the adjutants to the Serpent Generals in Al Zahbi.
