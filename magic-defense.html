Magic Defense

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A hidden stat used to determine the damage taken from spells cast by monsters and other sources of magic damage. Bonus traits from the White Mage job can increase your magic defense, as can some equipment and the Shell line of spells.
