Zeni Notorious Monsters

From Zenith XI Wiki

A tiered endgame system: earn Zeni, buy a trigger, defeat the NM and exchange its trophy to unlock the next tier.

Progression steps

  1. Collect Zeni and speak to Sanraku in Aht Urhgan Whitegate E-8. Select the unique-fauna purchase menu.
  2. Buy a Tier I trigger and trade it to that NM’s ???. The trigger is consumed.
  3. Take the trophy back to Sanraku for a colored seal. Spend that seal plus Zeni on a Tier II trigger; repeat to reach Tier III.
  4. For Tier IV, collect all three different Tier III seals on the chosen path. Buying the higher trigger consumes the required seals.
  5. Tier IV trophies lead to the final Pandemonium Warden path; some are also required for Mythic Weapons.

Tier IV island access

Ask Ryo about islets, then buy the appropriate salt key item from Sanraku. Each participant needs their own salt; the classic price is 500 Zeni. Gather everyone on the island before spawning the boss.
