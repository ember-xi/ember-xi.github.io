Bubble Shower

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Deals Water damage to targets in area of effect. Additional effect: STR Down

Description

  • Family: Crab
  • Type: Magical
  • Can be dispelled: N/A
  • Utsusemi/Blink absorb: Ignores shadows
  • Range: Less than 15'
  • Notes: Bubble Shower is a "Breath Weapon", meaning its damage is proportional to the crab's remaining HP. Maximum damage from standard crab enemies is 200.
