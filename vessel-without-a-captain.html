Walkthrough

  • Head to Lower Jeuno and enter Neptune's Spire.
  • Click on the door near the back of the room (leading into Tenshodo HQ; it is unknown as to whether Tenshodo membership is needed) to trigger a cutscene.
  • Make your way to Ru'Lude Gardens and approach the palace stairs that lead up to the Audience Chamber doors to receive the final cutscene.
