Notes

  • Spawned by trading a Singed Buffalo to a ??? located on (H-8) of Sharug Isle (Map 3).
    • The Cyan Deep Salt Temporary Key Item is needed by everyone wishing to access the Islet.
    • The Cyan Deep Salt is lost after one use.
    • This is not a true battlefield. Members are not kicked from the islet after a fight ends. If your group plans on doing multiple runs, each person needs only oneCyan Deep Salt for access.
  • Islet is accessed from Caedarva Mire (H-6) (Map 4 - Hediva Isle) by using Cyan Deep Salt (purchased from Sanraku for 500 Zeni) on Engraved Tablet.
    • Fastest way is to use the Unity Concord warp (level 135) to Caedarva Mire. This will drop you right on the south of Hediva Isle.
    • From Aht Urhgan Whitegate, take the Runic Portal to Nyzul Isle staging point Alzadaal Undersea Ruins.
      • Then use the H-8 portal toward Silver Sea Remnants, take east portal, zone then out to Caedarva Mire - Hediva Isle.
    • Make your way to the top right corner of (H-7) Hediva Isle. Hidden behind some foliage is a tunnel that leads you off the map to the Engraved Tablet that warps you to Sharug Isle.
  • Getting the Singed Buffalo from Sanraku requires a Fallow-colored seal, a Taupe-colored Seal and a Sienna-colored Seal.
  • Uses Fulmination - below 50% health. (30' range, Strong Paralyze)
  • Uses Fossilizing Breath Breath - Cone Attack Petrify.
  • Uses Tenebrous Mist - AoE TP reset.
  • Uses Tourbillion - AoE damage, Knockback, and Defense Down. Can be absorbed by 3 shadows.
  • Uses Dreadstorm - AoE damage + Terror, strips Utsusemi.
  • Uses Thunderstrike - AoE damage + Stun, ignores Utsusemi
  • Builds resistance to stun.
  • Immune to slow and elegy.
  • Howls (visible animation) a few seconds before using Tourbillion, making it easy to avoid damage by casting your shadows in time to absorb all three hits.
  • Tyger possesses Alliance hate. If a party is dying, Tyger will attack anyone alive until it defeats all players in the alliance, before becoming unclaimed and non-aggressive.
