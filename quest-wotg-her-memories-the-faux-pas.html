Before you begin

  • Her Memories is the current Wings of the Goddess mission.
  • Start Her Memories: Homecoming Queen.

Starting point

Abioleget · Northern San d'Oria · M-7

Era note: September 2010 WotG memory chapter, released after the original level-75 cap.

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Abioleget in the cathedral at (M-7).
  2. Speak with Bertenont at (E-4) and finish the scene to receive the fragment.

Completion and reward

A tiny memory fragment toward Large memory fragment I.
