The Puppet Master

The Puppet Master
Starting NPCCarbuncle — House of the Hero, Windurst Walls (G-4)
Required FameNone
Level RestrictionLevel 40
Items NeededEarth Pendulum
Additional RequirementsSummoner main job to start · I Can Hear a Rainbow completed
TitleNone
RepeatableYes, see Vingijard in Lower Jeuno after the set is complete
DescriptionCarbuncle asks you to find whoever is draining the avatars' power from adventurers. The trail leads to Bastok, and from there to a beast sleeping under the Quicksand Caves.
Rewards
  • Kukulcan's Staff
← Previous questNext quest →
—Class Reunion

Walkthrough

  • Examine the House of the Hero in Windurst Walls (G-4) as SMN to speak to Carbuncle.
    • Zone first if you just finished another quest involving Carbuncle.
  • From this point on the quest can be finished on any job.
  • Talk to Juroro in Port Bastok (I-8) for a cutscene. Ildy-Goldy hands you the Earth Pendulum.
    • If you are on Trial by Earth, you may have to finish it first before he hands the Pendulum over.
  • Make your way to the Cloister of Tremors (C-8, Quicksand Caves map 1).
    • Enter Quicksand Caves from Eastern Altepa Desert (J-7), then take the one-way drops down twice.
    • Quicksand Caves Home Point #2 is right outside the Cloister.
    • As SMN, a trial-size tuning fork of earth teleports you there.
  • Trade the Earth Pendulum to the Earth Protocrystal and pick The Puppet Master.
    • 30 minutes, no level cap, and buffs cast outside do not wear off.
    • Trusts can be summoned inside.
  • Defeat Galgalim.
    • Hecteyes, level 56, about 6,000 HP.
    • It uses Fire Spikes, Poison and Paralyze.
    • It can be silenced, paralyzed and slowed, but never slept.
    • Easy for a level 70+ job; at 40 bring a tank and a healer Trust.
  • Talk to Juroro in Port Bastok again.
  • Talk to Koru-Moru in Windurst Walls (E-7) to finish the quest and receive the staff.
    • If he gives dialogue for another quest, talk to him again.
    • The Earth Pendulum can be tossed after the fight.

EmberXI changes

  • No Fame requirement.
  • Trusts can be summoned inside the battlefield.
  • Kukulcan's Staff is level 40 with MP +30 and MP recovered while healing +3.
  • Artifact pieces can be upgraded to +1 by trading them to Brandt in Lower Jeuno with 4 Alexandrite.
