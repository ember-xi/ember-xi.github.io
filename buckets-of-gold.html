Buckets of Gold

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Foss - Bastok Markets F-7
  Requirements:
   Bastok Reputation 1
  Items Needed:   Rusty Bucket x 5
  Title:   Bucket Fisher
  Repeatable:   Yes
  Reward:   300 gil

  Previous Quest:   None.
  Next Quest:   None.

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Foss and tell him you fish. He tells you that he will buy five rusty buckets from you.
  • These can be caught while fishing, or purchased from the auction house.
  • Once you have five, trade them to him to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Foss (Firewater Circle, Bastok Markets)
Summary:

Foss is willing to buy rusty buckets off you, if you happen to catch five of them.
