Before you begin

  • Complete Boy and the Beast.

Starting point

Rholont · Southern San d'Oria (S) · E-7

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Rholont for the rescue assignment.
  2. Reach the ??? under the wooden structure at H-10 in Jugner Forest (S). Examine it for a scene, prepare, and examine it again to spawn Cobraclaw Buchzvotch.
  3. Defeat Cobraclaw Buchzvotch and re-examine the ??? for the rescue scene.
  4. Report to Rholont in Southern San d’Oria (S). If desired, take the Military Scrip to Wyatt for its gil payment.

Battle and progress notes

  • To continue WotG’s main story, enter Southern San d’Oria (S) on foot from East Ronfaure (S) after completing this quest.

Completion and reward

Military Scrip; native redemption at Wyatt pays 20,147 gil.

What this opens

  • The Queen of the Dance progression
  • Perils of the Griffon after Purple, The New Black
