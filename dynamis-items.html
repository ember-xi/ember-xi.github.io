Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Dynamis Items is a term used to refer to sellable items that drop from the various Dynamis areas, some of which are used towards Stage 2 Relic Weapons.

There are several types of items, some exclusive to a particular Dynamis area. Can be stackable up to 12, or not at all.
