Assault Armband

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Key Item Description
This armband designates you as the leader of an Assault mission. It bears the symbol of a curved sword.
Obtained from Mission
Obtained from the NPC found at the enterance to an Assault area for a fee (paid in Imperial Standing). One member of a party doing an Assault Mission needs this item in order for the group to enter the Assault area.
