Weapon Bash

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Delivers an attack that can stun the target.
  • Obtained: Dark Knight Level 20
  • Cast Time: Instant
  • Recast Time: 5:00

Notes/Description

  • This ability requires a two-handed weapon to use.
  • Deals a very small amount of Blunt damage to the target.
  • The chance of it stunning the target is rather low but it has a decent chance of interrupting magic casting.
  • The target's level affects its success, you shouldn't rely on it to interrupt a Thundaga 3 if you have Stun up.
  • Unlike Stun it goes through shadows.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Weapon Bash" <t>
