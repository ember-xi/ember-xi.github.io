Walkthrough

  • Illu Bohjaa wants you to clear out the crawlers that have been eating up all of her food supplies.
  • She will reward you if you bring her either 3 Silk Threads or 3 Crawler Calculi.
  • They both drop off crawlers in Sarutabaruta.

Game Description

Client: Illu Bohjaa (Dhalmel Farm, Windurst Woods)
Summary: The dhalmel farmer wants you to cull the crawler pests that eat up all the dhalmel food. Bring her three silk threads or three crawler calculi as proof of your efforts in order to gain a reward.
