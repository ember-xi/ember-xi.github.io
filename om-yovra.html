Notes:

  • Can Charmga party members
  • Killable by very good party of 75s.
  • Can be killed by 4 people (NIN or PLD/NIN tank, BRD, 2 damage dealers who do not give the Yovra too much TP such as BLM, RDM or SMN).
  • Trio'd by skilled MNK/NIN, BLM/WHM, and a RDM/WHM.
  • Fighting Yovra above water gives them higher regen than fighting above land.
  • Uses an ability similar to a Monk's Boost. Unlike boost if it does not land a melee hit on someone, it's next boost will continue to stack its attack rating. With a very good blink tank, it is not uncommon for the unlucky person who pulls hate near the end of the fight, after many boosts, to get hit for 1000+ damage off a single melee hit. This ability can be stolen with Aura Steal.
