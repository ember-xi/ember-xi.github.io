Zenith availability

Reference walkthrough. Completion is unconfirmed in the reviewed Zenith base: no dedicated quest handler or matching progression hook was found in the inspected zone/NPC files. A missing interaction may reflect unfinished content.

Before you begin

  • Complete Knot Quite There.

Starting point

Rotih Moalghett · Fort Karugo-Narugo [S] · I-8, inner map

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Rotih Moalghett on the inner/Fivespires map.
  2. Enter Castle Oztroja [S] for a cutscene, then return to Rotih to obtain the Fort key.
  3. Examine the Colorful Door on the inner map at H-5, near the G-6 border, and enter Ghoyu’s Reverie.
  4. Defeat the Yagudo waves, including the final enemy Laa Yaku the Austere. Area attacks and crowd control help manage the groups.
  5. After victory returns you to West Sarutabaruta [S], enter Windurst Waters [S] for the ending.

Battle and progress notes

  • After a loss, obtain another Fort key from Rotih Moalghett.

Completion and reward

Mihgo Mithkabob; Friend of Lehko Habhoka title.

What this opens

  • Windurst route prerequisite for finishing Cait Sith and reaching The Queen of the Dance.
  • When One Man Is Not Enough, after Purple, The New Black.
