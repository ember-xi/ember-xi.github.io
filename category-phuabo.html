General Information

  • System: Lumorians
  • Weaknesses:
  • Immune:
  • Phuabo ("sharks") wander around underwater, where they cannot be targeted and do not appear on Wide Scan.
  • They aggro by true hearing and leap out of the water to attack, at which point they can be targeted.
  • Phuabo give 10% more experience than normal for a mob their level.
  • Phuabo have MP for Aspir.
  • Enhanced Movement Speed
