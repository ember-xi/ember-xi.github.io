Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Lowers Intelligence of enemies within range.

Description

Spell cost: 25 MP
Ecosystem: Birds
Type: Magical (Fire)
Blue Magic Points: 1
Stat Bonus: None
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 32

Timers

Casting Time: 4 seconds
Recast Time: 30 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Sound Blast as part of a combo to create Magic Attack Bonus.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Magic Attack Bonus combo.

  • Cursed Sphere
  • Eyes On Me
  • Memento Mori
  • Heat Breath
  • Reactor Cool
  • Magic Hammer

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Liquefaction, Fusion, and Light.
