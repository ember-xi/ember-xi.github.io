Qiqirn Mine (Monster)

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  • Counts as a notorious monster
  • Occasionally left behind by Qiqirn class mobs
  • Does not attack
  • Has less than 100 HP
  • Explodes when someone steps over it, doing major damage to anyone within an area of effect (whether or not they are in the party/alliance).
  • Can safely be detonated by attacking it with pets, avatars, or ranged attacks.
