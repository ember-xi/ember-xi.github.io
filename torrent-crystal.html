Statistics

Torrent Crystal
A crystal infused with water energy.

Stackable: 12

Note: This is a High Quality Crystal that can be used in place of a Water Crystal in order to add the crafter's "signature" to the item being made.

Other Uses

Guild Points Value: None

Used in Quests: None

Resale Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
