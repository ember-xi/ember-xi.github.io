General Information

  • System: Archaic Machines
  • Weaknesses: Ice
  • Resistances: Thunder
  • Immune:
  • Detection: True Sight, True Hearing, Magic
  • Special: Gears will also link with other archaic machinery and vice versa.Verification Needed
In the case of triple gears, a gear is a removed 50% and 25% health, while the gear ability "Restoral" will restore a gear.


==Special Attacks==a

Special Abilities
Artificial Gravity - AoE dmg (150-200) + Gravity, strips Utsusemi
Antigravity - AoE dmg (150-200) + Knockback, strips Utsusemi
Restoral - self heal 1300~ HP
Rail Cannon - ~500 damage, ignores Utsusemi (see note below regarding target behavior)
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
Note: The behavior of Rail Cannon depends on how many of the Gears are present in the cluster.
1 Gear: Rail Cannon is single target and ignores Utsusemi
2 Gears: Rail Cannon is directional (conal) AoE and ignores Utsusemi
3 Gears: Rail Cannon is AoE and strips Utsusemi
