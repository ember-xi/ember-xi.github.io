Walkthrough

Wait until your initial cutscene at the entrance of the Grand Ducal Palace has finished.

  • Speak with Pherimociel at (G-6) of Ru'Lude Gardens.
  • Then you must zone and wait one game day before speaking with Pherimociel for the next cutscene. (Choose "Ready!" to continue with the cutscene.)
  • At the end of the cutscene, you will be at the Leypoint in Wajaom Woodlands.

NOTE:

  • if you choose "Not Ready." you will have to zone out to get the CS again

Game Description

You have been asked to participate in the Wajaom Infiltration Operation. A high-speed airship is being prepared for this mission. Speak with Pherimociel when you are ready to depart.
