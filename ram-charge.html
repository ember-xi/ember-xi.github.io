Timers

Casting Time: 0.5 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Ram Charge as part of a combo to create Lizard Killer.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Lizard Killer combo.

  • Foot Kick
  • Claw Cyclone

Notes

  • Blunt damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: STR: 30%, MND: 50%.
  • Possible Knockback effect.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Skillchain Element(s): Fragmentation (can open/close Light with Fusion WSs and spells)
