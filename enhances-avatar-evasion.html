Enhances Avatar Evasion

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This Avatar statistic can be compared to the Evasion of playercharacters. It is checked against an enemy attacker's accuracy to determine the chance, that an avatar has got to avoid an attack.
Evasion is directly affected by evasion skill and AGI. It is the common understanding that every 2 points in Agility raise evasion by 2 points. Because evasion is a hidden status, it can only be analysed with the monster information by examining mobs with the ingame command "Check":

  • Low evasion occurs when your accuracy is twenty or greater than the monsers evasion.
  • High evasion occurs when your accuracy is twenty lower or less than the monsters evasion.
  • Within plus or minus 19 accuracy, neither will show.

For avatars there is no possible way to check it, but observations have shown that the wind based Garuda have the highest evasion and the earth based Titan the lowest.
