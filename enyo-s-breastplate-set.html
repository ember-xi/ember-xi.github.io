Enyo's Breastplate Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This very rare set of plate-type armor is one of three used to create Ares's Cuirass Set. See also Deimos's Cuirass Set and Phobos's Cuirass Set. All of its pieces are dropped in Salvage and, despite their purpose, are solid pieces of low-level equipment on their own.
