Gwinar

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Map Marker

Location: Port Bastok (I-12) (on your left as you exit Mog House)

Notes

Marks one's town map with the locations of various NPCs important to adventures:

  • Home Points
  • Item Depository
  • Melody Minstrels
  • Weather Reporters
  • Traveling Bards
  • Reputation
  • Immigration
  • Armor Depository
  • Teleportation
  • Pursuivant
  • Gold World Pass Vendor
