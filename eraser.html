Statistics

Eraser
A light-based automaton attachment.

Stackable: 12

Note: This is an Automaton Attachment:

Automaton Effect Req. Capacity
 Erases enfeebling and all maneuver effects  Light x 2

Maneuvers # Effects Removed
Light x 0 Will not be used.
Light x 1 1
Light x 2 2
Light x 3 3


  • A Light Maneuver must be active to use this attachment.
  • 30 second recast time.
  • Upon use, all maneuvers (regardless of element) will be removed.
  • The automaton can only target itself or the master with the Eraser, and only one person at a time.
  • It will always remove negative effects from itself, until completely clean, before removing effects from the master.
  • Removable statuses include: Paralyze, Blind, Poison, Silence, Sleep, Petrify, Curse, Disease, and all Eraseable ailments.
  • It will not activate for certain Erasable debuffs, such as from the special moves used by Fomors outside Aht Urhgan. It also cannot remove Doom or Bard songs. It cannot remove the "Gradual Petrify" effect caused by Peistes; however, it can remove the effect after the player has become fully petrified.
  • The Eraser cannot be used while the puppet is disabled by a status effect, such as Sleep or Petrify. Thus, these effects can only be removed from the master. It can, however, remove Silence from itself. This is because attachments are classified as job abilities, not spells.

*Warning: The above information may or may not be accurate for Eden.
