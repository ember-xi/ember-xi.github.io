Zenith Guide

From Zenith XI Wiki

Travel using owned Gate Crystals. The menu contains Present crystals, Past crystals, Travel help and Close. Each quest has its own NPC; see the quest directory for names, map squares and rewards.

Locations

CityZenith Guide / Atlas location
Southern San d’Oria(G-10), near Alaune and Home Point #1
Bastok Markets(D-11), beside Gulldago
Windurst Woods(K-10), beside Selele

Travel

  1. Speak to Zenith Guide at a location above.
  2. Choose Present crystals or Past crystals, then an unlocked destination.
  3. Choose Travel. Crystal requirements and travel fees apply.

Quest NPCs and rewards are handled by the specialists listed in the directory.
