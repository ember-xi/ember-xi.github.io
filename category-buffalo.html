Buffalo
Buffalo are large mammals that inhabit the Uleguerand Range. It is said the Orcs brought them in several hundred years ago as livestock, but they have since lost all traces of domestication.

Buffalo are well suited to the frigid climate, possessing a thick layer of subdermal fat and massive horns for breaking up the frozen ground. Despite their violent temperaments, they are sought after for their exquisite meat.
