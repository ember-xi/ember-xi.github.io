Water (Element)

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

File:Water icon.JPG The element of Water is strong against Fire and weak against Thunder. Associated with Reverberation, Distortion, and Dark skillchains. Governed by the celestial avatar Leviathan.
