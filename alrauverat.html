Notes

Notes for all characters
  • Casts Signet
  • Recharges Emperor Band, Empress Band or Chariot Band
  • Accepts traded Crystals for filling up the Mission-Rank bar of the character's appropriate Nation (the red bar at the character's profile)
  • Sells items for Conquest Points of the character's appropriate Nation (Items for Bastok / Items for San d'Oria / Items for Windurst) at certain conditions. It will be also possible to get some items of other Nations at the guards, if your current Allegiance ranks higher at the weekly Conquest results (for further information see under the items articles)
  • Explains the Conquest system by choosing some available questions.
