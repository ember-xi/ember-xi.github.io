Command/partycmd

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Aliases: /pcmd
Usage: /partycmd [subcommand]
Description: Executes various party commands.



Subcommand Description Example
add Invite targeted PC to your party. /pcmd add Bob
leave Leave your current party. /pcmd leave
kick [name] Kick a PC from your party. /pcmd kick Bob
breakup Dissolve your current party. /pcmd breakup
leader [name] Change leader of your current party. /pcmd leader Bob
looter [name] Change or activate the quartermaster for your current party. /pcmd looter Bob

Notes

When using the add subcommand, you must select a PC who is not in a party and who does not have any pending incoming or outgoing invitations.
To use the leave subcommand, you can be any member in the party.
If you use leave when you are party leader, you will drop from the party, and lead will be passed to the person been in the party the longest.
To use the kick or breakup subcommands, you must be a party leader.
If there are any items in the treasure pool, they will be lost if the party is dissolved with breakup.
The leader and looter commands are not listed when using /? pcmd. You must be the current leader of the party to use these commands.
