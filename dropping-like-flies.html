Notes

  • Houndflies only use Venom.
  • Houndflies are resistant to [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Horde_Lullaby Horde Lullaby], but can be slept with [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Sleep Sleep].
  • Huntfly uses [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Cursed_Sphere Cursed Sphere], and Somersault (single target damage).
  • Huntfly casts [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Aero Aero], [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Gravity Gravity], and [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Choke Choke].
  • Huntfly can be [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Weight Gravitied], [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Bind_(Status_Effect) Bound], and [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Silence_(Status_Effect) Silenced].
  • Huntfly cannot be slept.
  • Sleepga being level 31, it is unavailable for this fight. Diabolos' [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Nightmare Nightmare] should be able to land, but it has not been tested. [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Sheep_Song Sheep Song] seems to have no effect.
  • Houndflies have around 900HP, Huntfly has about 1800.
  • The Huntfly and Houndflies have shared hate. Actions taken against one will generate hate from the others at a reduced rate.
