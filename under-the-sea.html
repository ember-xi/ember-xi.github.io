Walkthrough

  • Talk to Yaya (H-9, Under the fisherman's guild) to start the quest.
  • Talk to Oswald (I-9 inside The Sheperd's Muster) who mentions he lost his favorite ring.
  • Talk to Jimaida (H-9) who tells you to go talk to Zaldon (H-9).
  • Zaldon mentions that Greedies like to swallow shiny things.
    • You will need to Fish up a Fat Greedie. A fishing skill of at least 10-12 is helpful.
    • The Fat Greedie is best caught with Sardine Balls and a small fishing rod such as a Halcyon Rod.
    • Lugworm also work fine and can be bought from Dohdjuma at G-10 Selbina.
  • Trade Zaldon the Fat Greedie to obtain the Key Item: Etched Ring.
    • It is not 100% chance, I went 1/6
  • Return to Oswald for your reward.

Game Description

Client: Oswald (Shepherd's Muster, Selbina)
Summary:

Oswald needs help retrieving his ring from the jaws of the ocean.
