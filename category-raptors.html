Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Foul Breath: Cone Attack Disease and Fire damage
Frost Breath: Cone Attack Paralysis and Ice damage
Chomp Rush: Threefold attack and 25% Slow
Thunderbolt: Cone Attack Lightning damage and Stun
Ripper Fang: Single target damage and Slow
Scythe Tail: Single target damage, Stun, and Knockback.
