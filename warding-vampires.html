Walkthrough

  • Maloquedil needs garlic to ward off the vampire living in the dungeon under the Chateau d'Oraguille.
  • Shaman Garlic drops off Orcish Cursemaker in Ghelsba Outpost.
  • Trade him two Shaman Garlic to receive your reward.

Game Description

Client: Maloquedil (Parade Grounds, Northern San d'Oria)
Summary:

Fetch two cloves of Orc garlic to keep vampires away.
