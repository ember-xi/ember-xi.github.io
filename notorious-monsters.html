Notorious Monsters

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Notorious Monsters, or Named Monsters, are enemies that check as Impossible to Gauge and drop a variety of useful items.

Some Notorious Monsters have True Sight or True Sound ability. Usually this applies to the highest tier beastmen in most beastmen families (these beastmen will have normal names and normal pops), but it can also apply to Boss Mobs in outdoor zones (almost all outdoor zones have them) and HNM.

For an easier reference to NM levels please see the Notorious Monsters: Level Guide section.

For a list of pop-type notorious monsters, please see the Pop NMs section.
