Zephyr Thread

From Zenith XI Wiki

Clothcraft tool consumed by bulk fletching recipes.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Buy from Meriri at the Weavers’ Guild in Windurst Woods or Taten-Bilten in Al Zahbi. Also listed under Auction House → Materials → Clothcraft.

Use

Used with the Fletching guild ability to make batches of fletchings. Choose the recipe for your feather type, such as Bird Fletchings, Yagudo Fletchings or Puk Fletchings.
