Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 26: Seal of the Serpent.

Starting point

Unlabeled target · Aydeewa Subterrane · F-8/G-8

Walkthrough

  1. Return through Wajaom Woodlands G-7 to Aydeewa Subterrane.
  2. Take the D-8 or E-9 connection to the lower tree-root area used in Teahouse Tumult.
  3. Examine the same blank target at F-8/G-8 and finish the scene with Rishfee.

Completion and reward

No item reward.

What this opens

  • ToAU 28: Bastion of Knowledge
