Jazaraat's Headstone

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This Headstone is the grave of the former captain of the Black Coffin - Jazaraat. It is located in a graveyard at E-10 on Azouph Isle in Caedarva Mire. After Mission 14 you can get there a new Ephramadian Gold Coin from here without fighting any NMs.
Involved in Mission: Aht Urhgan Mission 13: Lost Kingdom
