Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

Attack Down is a harmful status effect that disorients a player until it wears off or is removed. Afflicted characters experience reduced attack power.

How to remove the effect

  • The White Magic spell Erase will remove this effect.

How the effect is inflicted/gained

Spells

  • Blue Magic - Terror Touch

Monster Abilities

  • Bat Triplets - Sonic Boom
  • Worms - Gastric Bomb
  • Ghosts - Terror Touch
  • Wivres - Demoralizing Roar

Weapon Skills

  • Weapon Break
  • Full Break

Weapons

  • Amanomurakumo
  • Dreizack
