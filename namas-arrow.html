Namas Arrow

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Archery Weapon Skill

  • Skill Level: N/A
  • Description: Additional Effect: Temporarily improves Ranged Accuracy
  • Available only when equipped with the Relic Weapons Futatokoroto (Dynamis use only) or Yoichinoyumi.
  • These Relic Weapons are only available to Samurai and Rangers. As such, only these jobs may use this Weapon Skill.

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Light / Distortion
