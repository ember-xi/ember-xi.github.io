Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Deals ice damage that lowers Agility and gradually reduces HP of enemies within range.

Description

Spell cost: 37 MP
Ecosystem: Arcana
Type: Magical (Ice)
Blue Magic Points: 1
Stat Bonus: INT-1
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 52

Timers

Casting Time: 4 seconds
Recast Time: 60 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Cold Wave as part of a combo to create Auto Refresh.

Can be paired with some of the following spells to create the Auto Refresh trait. Please refer to the Auto Refresh section of the Blue Mage Job Traits page for details.

  • Stinking Gas
  • Frightful Roar
  • Self-Destruct
  • Light of Penance
  • Voracious Trunk
  • Actinic Burst
  • Plasma Charge

Notes

  • Frost damage over time is approximately 18 damage per tick.
  • AGI down effect starts very high (more than -35), and decays over time.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Induration, Distortion, and Darkness.
