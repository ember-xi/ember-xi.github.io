Description

Spell cost: 74 MP
Ecosystem: Dragon
Type: Physical (Blunt)
Blue Magic Points: 4
Stat Bonus: VIT+1, MP+5
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 62

Timers

Casting Time: 1 seconds
Recast Time: 27.75 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Body Slam as part of a combo to create Max HP Boost.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Max HP Boost combo.

  • Flying Hip Press
  • Frypan

Notes

  • Blunt damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: VIT: 40%.
  • Area of effect is centered around caster.
