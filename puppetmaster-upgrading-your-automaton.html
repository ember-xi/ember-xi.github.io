Upgrading Your Automaton

After you reach level 10, 20 and 30 you can get a new Automaton frame. Talk to Ghatsad (I-7) as a Puppetmaster in Aht Urhgan Whitegate. He will give you three options to choose from. After trading the items & payment, return (as PUP) after JST midnight to receive your new Automaton Head and Frame.

  • To equip your new frame you need to remove all attachments.

Costs

  • Level 10: 3x Imperial Silver Pieces
  • Level 20: 3x Imperial Mythril Pieces
  • Level 30: 1x Imperial Gold Piece

Enhances proficiency in Close Combat.
