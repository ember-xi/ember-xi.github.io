Starting point

??? · Qufim Island · G-6

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

Walkthrough

  1. Obtain a Seedspall Astrum from Yagudo in Meriphataud Mountains, near the Castle Oztroja approach at K-8.
  2. Obtain a Seedspall Luna from Quadav in Pashhow Marshlands, near the Beadeaux approach at K-10.
  3. Obtain a Seedspall Lux from Orcs in Jugner Forest, near the Davoi approach at G-11.
  4. Bring all three physical items to Qufim Island. Trade them together to the ??? at G-6 between the two stone pillars near the H-6 path, then watch the scene.
