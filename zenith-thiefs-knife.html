How to obtain

Original equipment quests starts at Nanaa Mihgo, Windurst Woods (J-3). The ordinary knife also drops from Sozu Rogberry, Temple of Uggalepih, Map 3 (G-8). The quest lets you upgrade that copy by trading it alone to Nanaa after your qualifying victory.

Auction House

Ordinary knife: Weapons → Daggers; check stock and Price History for the actual price. It is not in Zenith’s fixed stock-price catalogue. The quest augments are not included on ordinary market copies.
