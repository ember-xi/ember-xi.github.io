Walkthrough

  • You must first have completed Zilart Mission 7 - The Chamber of Oracles.
  • Once you have received the Key Item: Prismatic Fragment you can return to Sanctuary of Zi'Tah.
  • Check the Cermet Headstone. You will receive a cutscene and the Bat Earring.

Game Description

Client: None
Summary:

Dispel the troubled souls of those who perished while on their pilgrimages.
