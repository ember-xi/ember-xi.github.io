Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

File:Refresh (Status Effect).png Refresh is a beneficial status effect that refreshes a player's magic pool until it wears off or is removed. Benefitting characters gain magic points back while standing or sitting.

How to remove the effect

  • This effect can be dispelled.
  • Refresh spell gained from the Red Mage cast or food items can be manually removed (those that give the status icon pictured above). Those gained from Mage's Ballad, Mage's Ballad II, and Evoker's Roll cannot be manually removed (uses different status icons).

How the effect is inflicted/gained

Spells

  • Red Mage - Refresh (3mp/tick)
  • Song - Mage's Ballad (1mp/tick)
  • Song - Mage's Ballad II (2mp/tick)
  • Phantom Roll - Evoker's Roll (1-5mp/tick depending on conditions)

Consumables (Food)

  • Orange Juice (1mp/tick)
  • Apple Juice (1mp/tick)
  • Pineapple Juice (1mp/tick)
  • Grape Juice (2mp/tick)
  • Melon Juice (2mp/tick)
  • Vampire Juice (2mp/tick)
  • Yagudo Drink (2mp/tick)
  • Tomato Juice (1mp/tick)
  • Amrita (5mp/tick)

Auto-Refresh Gear

Effects gained from auto-refresh gear do not give the status icon, cannot be dispelled, and all give 1mp/tick.

  • Ares's Cuirass
  • Balrahn's Ring (in Assault and Salvage, only)
  • Black Cloak / Demon's Cloak
  • Cleric's Briault / Cleric's Briault +1
  • Dalmatica / Dalmatica +1
  • Duelist's Chapeau / Duelist's Chapeau +1
  • Gaudy Harness (as a latent effect)
  • Hercules' Ring (as a latent effect)
  • Hydra Doublet (as an enchantment)
  • Hydra Haubert (as an enchantment)
  • Marduk's Jubbah
  • Morrigan's Robe
  • Noble's Tunic / Aristocrat's Coat
  • Parade Gorget
  • Plastron / Plastron Plus 1
  • Sorcerer's Coat / Sorcerer's Coat +1
  • Vampire Cloak
  • Vermillion Cloak / Royal Cloak
  • Yigit Gomlek Set
  • Yinyang Robe

Auto-Refresh Job Traits

Effects gained from auto-refresh job traits do not give the status icon, cannot be dispelled, and all give 1mp/tick.

  • Summoner level 25
  • Paladin level 35
  • Blue Mage level 58 (under certain conditions)

Others

  • Sanction (1mp/tick, under certain conditions and at the cost of 100 Imperial Standing credits)
