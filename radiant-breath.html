Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Deals light damage to enemies within a fan-shaped area of effect originating from the caster. Additional effect: Slow and Silence.

Description

Spell cost: 116
Ecosystem: Dragons
Type: Magical (Light)
Blue Magic Points: 4
Stat Bonus: CHR+1, HP+5
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 54

Timers

Casting Time: 5.25 seconds
Recast Time: 33.75 seconds

Notes

  • The formula for this spell appears to be (Current HP / 5) + (your level / 0.75). For example, if you are level 54 and have 1000 HP, your damage would be 200+72, or 272. This is subject to Resists, however. Using it against Demons or Magic Bursting it does not boost damage; it only affects resist rates.
  • Is NOT affected by Magic Attack Bonus.
  • Ignores Magic Defense Bonus, but is subject to direct magic resistance (from Proto-Omega for example).

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Transfixion, Fusion, Light
