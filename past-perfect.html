Walkthrough

  • Talk to Evi, talk to Rafaela next to him, and then talk to Evi again.
  • Go to Konschtat Highlands and select the ??? near the windmills at G-8 to receive the Tattered Mission Orders (key item).
  • Return to Port Bastok and talk to Evi to deliver the orders and complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Evi (Harbor Way, Port Bastok)
Summary:

Bring Evi his old mission orders, buried near one of the windmills on the Konschtat Highlands.
