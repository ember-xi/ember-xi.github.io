Walkthrough

  • Talk to Horatius. He needs a Dangruf Stone for his gem collection and says they can be found in Dangruf Wadi, of course, but only on sunny days.
  • Go to Dangruf Wadi on a sunny day and at I/J-5 location target select the ???, to receive the stone.
  • Return to Horatius and trade him the Dangruf Stone for your reward.

Game Description

Client: Horatius (Trader's Home, Bastok Markets)
Summary:

Horatius wants you to bring him a rare stone that can only be found in the Dangruf Wadi.
