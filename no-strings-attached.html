No Strings Attached

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Shamarhaan - Bastok Markets (F-9)
  Requirements:
   One job at level 30+
  Items Needed:   None
  Title:   Proud Automaton Owner
  Repeatable:   No
  Reward:   Ability to become a Puppetmaster; Animator

  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   The Wayward Automaton

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Shamarhaan at in Bastok Markets near the fountain.
  • Talk to Iruki-Waraki at (K-9) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (on the top floor).
  • Talk to Ghatsad at (I-7) the Automaton Workshop (on the top floor).
  • Note that the area you need to travel to Arrapago Reef is also the same area for unlocking Corsair. So you may want to start that quest as well: Luck of the Draw.
  • Head to the first map of Illrusi Atoll, which is accessible from (I-6) of Caedarva Mire. There are several ways to get there:
    • The fastest way is to Escape from Illrusi Atoll Staging Point. This will put you very close to the (I-6) entrance.
    • If you don't have access to Escape, take the north dock ferry to Nashmau and go through North Gate to Caedarva Mire. Head NE/NW to the (I-6) zone to Arrapago Reef.
    • Alternatively, if you have access to Escape but not Illrusi Atoll Staging Point, you can walk from Nashmau's west gate from (F-10) to (E-9) Talacca Cove. Head generally north-east and you'll find a zone into Arrapago Reef. Escaping inside of Talacca Cove will put you very close to the (I-6) entrance.
  • Use caution around imps as they have True Sight.
  • Find the ??? at (H-10) on the first boat, hidden beneath the stairs, to receive a key item: Antique Automaton.
  • Return to Ghatsad (I-7) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate. Talk to him to give him the Antique Automaton. Wait for the next game day and talk to him again.
  • Return to Iruki-Waraki (K-9) to receive your Animator and to complete the quest. You will also be given a chance to pick your Automaton's name from a list.
  • Puppetmaster will not show up on your list of Job Levels until you zone.
  • A suggestion to try and obtain the Puppetmaster Job solo is to set your Home Point in Nashmau, then equip a Level 1 job, with a subjob which will provide Widescan. This will be useful in detecting Imps. You should be prepared by having a complete stack or more of Silent Oil and Prism Powder.

Game Description

Client: Iruki-Waraki (Way of the Devout, Aht Urhgan Whitegate)
Summary:

If you want to learn how to become a puppetmaster, you must bring an automaton to Iruki-Waraki.
