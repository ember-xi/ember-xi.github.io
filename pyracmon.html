Notes:

  • Has between 270 and 320HP (probably 300).
  • Appears during the Harvest Festival 2008 event.
  • Takes no damage from normal attacks; you need to kill bats to power of an Enlight effect that will harm him.
  • Uses a TP move called "Monocular Scowl" that will remove the Enlight effect.
    • To evade "Monocular Scowl" just turn your body, similar to Hecteyes Hex Eye.
  • Players must defeat Pyracmon and return to moogle before Enlight wears off in order to obtain a prize.
  • All players under the level restriction can attack him; however only the player or party that deals the killing blow will receive any stables neckwear.
  • Can load up to 4 neck pieces per kill
    • May drop any combination of the 3 neck pieces or all of one type
  • The following actions can take place with the proper parts on Puppetmaster's Automaton
    • Can be provoked via Strobe
    • Can be blinded via Flashbulb
    • Can be stunned via Shield Bash
