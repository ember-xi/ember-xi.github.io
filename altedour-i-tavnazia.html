Notes:

  • Spawned by Quest: Pieuje's Decision. The ??? has a 15 min repop timer.
  • Located on first map (J-10).
  • Has a lot of HP, but very low defense.
  • Uses Manafont.
  • Uses all black magic spells, including Dispel and Tier 3 and 4 nukes.
  • Has an En-Aspir effect on physical attacks that is very potent.
  • Resists Silence, Stun, and Sleep; can be Paralyzed, Slowed, Gravitied, and Flashed.
  • Will use Dimensional Death when at low health, sometimes doing it 2 to 3 times in a row.
  • When defeated says: "It is finally...over..Ahh... I can... I can see... Tavnazia... The land of wind ... and light... My... My home...!"
  • Killable by: Party of 3-6 level 75 characters, an alliance of 18 level 65 characters, duo with a Lv.75 NIN and WHM, solo by 75 SMN or 72 RDM/NIN.
