Sarameya's Hide

From Zenith XI Wiki

Tier-four ZNM trophy required for the Mythic battlefield.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Defeat Sarameya in Mount Zhayolm and obtain this trophy. Follow the enemy page for the pop item and Zeni Notorious Monsters progression.

Use

Keep it for the three-item trade in Forging a New Myth. Trading it toward a different ZNM objective will not also satisfy the Mythic hand-in.
