Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Deals earth damage to enemies within a fan-shaped area originating from the caster. Additional effect: Weight.

Description

Spell cost: 86 MP
Ecosystem: Beastmen
Type: Magical (Earth)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: VIT+2
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 46

Timers

Casting Time: 4.5 seconds
Recast Time: 29.25 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Magnetite Cloud as part of a combo to create Magic Defense Bonus.

Can be paired with the following spell to create Magic Defense Bonus combo.

  • Ice Break

Notes

  • The formula for this spell appears to be (Current HP / 6) + (your level / 1.875). For example, if you are level 46 and have 750 HP, your damage would be 125+24.5, or 149-150. This is subject to Resists, however. Magic Bursting it does not burst damage; it only affects the resist rate.
  • Is NOT affected by Magic Attack Bonus.
  • Ignores Magic Defense Bonus, but is subject to direct magic resistance (from Proto-Omega for example).

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Scission, Gravitation, and Darkness.
