Before you begin

  • Finish CoP Dawn’s post-battle epilogue and reward sequence; the pinned script checks Dawn Status 8.
  • Reach Riverne - Site #B01; take a Giant Scale for its displacement route.

Starting point

Esha’ntarl (palace entrance scene) · Ru'Lude Gardens · H-6

Era note: The reviewed Zenith modules list enabled custom/zenith only. The pinned base scripts therefore supply the waits described here; optional era modules that restore JST-midnight waits were not enabled in that snapshot. Live installation may differ.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Enter the Ducal Palace in Ru’Lude Gardens and accept Esha’ntarl’s request.
  2. Examine the Dilapidated Gate in Misareaux Coast for the quest scene, then enter Riverne - Site #B01.
  3. Use a Giant Scale on the required unstable displacement to reach the western E-7 island. Examine the green Unstable Displacement for its scene, then again to select Storms of Fate.
  4. Defeat Bahamut. Maintain magical protection and health before his flare attacks; keep support away from the tank and avoid drawing hate from behind him.
  5. After receiving Whisper of the Wyrmking, return to the Ru’Lude Gardens palace for the final scene. This return completes the quest and starts the wait for Shadows of the Departed.

Battle and progress notes

  • The native battlefield allows Trusts, up to 18 participants and 30 minutes. This does not establish that it is balanced for a single level-75 player.
  • Native progression waits until the next Vana’diel day after the return scene; Horizon’s guide describes a different real-world wait.

Completion and reward

Whisper of the Wyrmking; title: Conqueror of Fate.

What this opens

  • Shadows of the Departed, after both Awakening epilogues and the wait

Walkthrough

Please note that when you've begun Dawn for Chains of Promathia and Awakening for Rise of the Zilart, your in-game Mission tracker will stop updating your mission progress until The Last Verse is completed. It will show that you're still on Dawn and Awakening even if you've completed it, so while you're progressing through Storms of Fate, Shadows of the Departed and Apocalypse Nigh, you will need to keep track of your own mission progress.

  • Go to Ru'Lude Gardens, enter the Ducal Palace for a cutscene with Esha'ntarl.
  • Go to Riverne - Site #B01. You will receive a cutscene upon entering.
  • Head to the green Unstable Displacement on the west side of (E-7). This acts as a battlefield entrance similar to the mini-battlefield in Ghelsba Outpost.
    • The island this Unstable Displacement is on is only accessible through another, "normal" Unstable Displacement; one Giant Scale will thus be required to reach it.
  • Check the green Unstable Displacement once for a cutscene.
  • Check it again and select the battle "Storms of Fate" when ready.
    • The BCNM menu will not pop-up until everyone in the alliance has viewed the cutscene.

Boss Battle for "Storms of Fate"

You will enter the battlefield facing the Wyrmking Bahamut. Up to 18 people (one full Alliance) may enter the battlefield. This battle is uncapped. You are given 30 minutes to defeat him. HP/MP will be fully restored upon entry.

Bahamut has about 26000 HP. He casts the following spells:

  • Defensive: Protect V, Shell V, Stoneskin, Phalanx, Cure V
    • He is fully buffed at the beginning of the fight.
  • Offensive: Fire V, Flare II, Firaga IV, Silencega, Dispelga, Graviga

He uses the following special attacks:

  • Absolute Terror: AoE Terror, absorbed by Utsusemi
  • Trample: AoE damage (~200-500), Knockback and Bind, absorbed by Utsusemi
  • Sweeping Flail: Large AoE (range 20) damage (~100-300) and Knockback, absorbed by Utsusemi
    • Used when someone behind him gets hate
  • Touchdown: AoE wind damage (~300) and Knockback
  • Tempest Wing: Cone Attack wind(?) damage (~400)
  • Megaflare: Wide Cone Attack fire damage (~800).
    • He uses this every 10% HP he loses.
    • He insults the players before he uses it: "Children of Vana'diel, what do you think to accomplish by fighting for your lives?"
    • Don't bother stunning this, he'll just use it again when Stun wears off. Save your Stuns for his spells, particurlarly Firaga IV and Dispelga.
  • Gigaflare: Wide Cone Attack fire damage (~1200)
    • Used instead of Megaflare at 10% HP.
    • Before this he says "Contemplate on the good your death will bring as your mortal form is consumed by my brilliance!"

When Bahamut is defeated, you will receive the Key Item Whisper of the Wyrmking. Return to Ru'Lude Gardens and enter the palace again for a final cutscene, where you are told to assemble "an army of adventurers" and challenge Bahamut again...

(Note: The Key Item received from this quest is a required item for each Alliance member to have in order to enter the BCNM The Wyrmking Descends.)

Game Description

Client: Esha'ntarl (Audience Chamber, Ru'Lude Gardens)
Summary:

Bahamut returned to the heavens following the defeat of the Keeper of the Apocalypse, but recently he was witnessed descending upon Cape Riverne with an army of wyrms. Go to Bahamut and find out his intentions.
