Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

File:Silence (Status Effect).png Silence is a harmful status effect that makes the target unable to cast Magic of any type until it wears off or is removed.

It will not prevent players from speaking in any chat channel, nor will it stop abilities that would seem to rely on sound or voice, such as Whistle or Warcry.

How to remove the effect

  • The White Magic spell Silena will remove this effect. (Note that this spell cannot be cast if you yourself are silenced.)
  • Using a vial of Echo Drops, Remedy, or Remedy Ointment will remove this effect.

How the effect is inflicted

Spells

  • White Magic - Silence
  • Blue Magic - Chaotic Eye, Radiant Breath, Bad Breath

Weapon Skills

  • Scythe - Guillotine
  • Great Katana - Tachi: Gekko

Monster Abilities

  • Antica - Jamming Wave
  • Cockatrice - Sound Vacuum
  • Coeurls - Chaotic Eye
  • Corses - Silence Seal
  • Dhalmel - Cold Stare
  • Funguars - Silence Gas
  • Goobbues - Antiphase
  • Hippogryphs - Choke Breath
  • Imps - Deafening Tantara
  • Lizards - Brain Crush
  • Morbols - Bad Breath
  • Worms - Sound Vacuum
  • Wyverns - Radiant Breath
