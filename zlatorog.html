Zlatorog

From Zenith XI Wiki

The Ram boss on floor 2 of the original NW Apollyon Limbus route.

Contents
  1. Access
  2. Battle and rewards
  3. Sources

Access

Reach Sea, obtain Cosmo-Cleanse and a Red Card, then enter NW Apollyon from the Dem-side Al’Taieu entrance. Advance to floor 2.

Battle and rewards

Zlatorog repeatedly uses Mighty Strikes. Prepare defensive abilities and keep the healer ready; clear or avoid the nearby Mountain Buffalo. Ancient Beastcoin is a listed drop. Follow the NW Apollyon route for floor chests and the final Magenta Chip reward.
