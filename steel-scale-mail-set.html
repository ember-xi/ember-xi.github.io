Steel Scale Mail Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Another set of purely defensive armor, crafted mostly by bonding steel scales onto a sturdy leather base. Not as popular due to lack of stats. The high quality version of this set is the Steel Scale Mail +1 Set.
