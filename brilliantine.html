Brilliantine

From Zenith XI Wiki

Temenos material used for every Scholar artifact+1 piece.

Farm item chests in Temenos. Temenos - Western Tower has Scholar material opportunities on floors3 and 5; drops are not guaranteed.

Bring one Brilliantine and one Electrum Stud per piece to Sagheera with the original armor, crafted material and deposited coins shown in Scholar Artifact Upgrades.
