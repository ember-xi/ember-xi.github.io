Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


A large bird common throughout the Quon continent, these birds are commonly seen in grassy areas, where trees provide them nesting areas while brush provides a home to smaller creatures that they can prey upon.

Bird eggs are a fairly useful cooking ingredient, and their feathers can be used by crafters to create a few useful items. On occasion, bird feathers are used as decorative items, adorning various clothing items and providing a bit of flair to them.

General Information

  • System: Birds
  • Crystal: Fire
  • Weaknesses: Ice, Piercing
  • Immune:
  • Charmble: Yes

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Wing Cutter - Deals wind damage to enemies within area of effect.
Helldive - Damage varies with TP
Damnation Drive - Conal damage and Stun
Broadside Barrage - Damage, INT Down, and MND Down
Blindside Barrage - Damage, STR Down, and VIT Down
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
