Seeds

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Adventurers can acquire them around the world of Vana'diel, usually found amongst sapling type plantoids. They can then be cultivated with a flowerpot during the hobby of gardening. There are 2 types of seeds available:


Flowering Plants

  • Grain Seeds
  • Vegetable Seeds
  • Herb Seeds
  • Wildgrass Seeds

Trees

  • Fruit Seeds
  • Tree Cuttings
  • Tree Saplings
  • Cactus Stems
