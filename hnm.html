HNM

HNM
KingsBehemoth (Behemoth's Dominion) · Fafnir (Dragon's Aery) · Adamantoise (Valley of Sorrows)
SpawnEvery hour, timed
HQKing Behemoth / Nidhogg / Aspidochelone — popped with a 30% drop from the base king (Savory Shank / Sweet Tea / Red Pondweed)
OthersCerberus / Hydra / Khimaira: 1–2 hours (retail 48–72); Serket / Roc / Simurgh: 1–2 hours retail
TrustsAllowed

The HNMs on EmberXI keep their retail places but with short timers. The three kings are timed spawns (not forced pops), and their HQ versions pop from an item dropped by the base king.

Mechanics: Tiamat / Jormungand / Vrtra are full retail (100k HP) — out of scope for now.
