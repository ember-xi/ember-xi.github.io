HNM

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


High Notorious Monsters (HNMs) are a player-determined subset of Notorious Monsters that generally have long respawn timers, valuable drops, and fierce competition on claims. HNMs are typically camped by endgame linkshells designed around HNM camping, in order to have higher chances to claim and defeat these monsters. There is some debate on what monsters are considered HNMs, since it's a player-determined category. Back in 2005, for example, Elel was on the list of HNMs, but 2008-era campers would probably not consider this monster an HNM.
