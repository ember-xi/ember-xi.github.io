HNM

HNM
KingsBehemoth (Behemoth's Dominion) · Fafnir (Dragon's Aery) · Adamantoise (Valley of Sorrows)
SpawnEvery hour, timed
HQ kingsKing Behemoth / Nidhogg / Aspidochelone — popped with a 30% drop from the base king (Savory Shank / Sweet Tea / Red Pondweed)
OthersCerberus / Hydra / Khimaira / Serket / Roc / Simurgh: every 1–2 hours
TrustsAllowed

The HNMs spawn in their usual zones on short timers. The three kings are timed spawns (not forced pops), and their HQ versions pop from an item dropped by the base king.

Mechanics: Tiamat / Jormungand / Vrtra are unchanged (100k HP) — out of scope for now.
