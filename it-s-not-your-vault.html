Walkthrough

  • Speak to Keal at H-8, to start the quest.
  • Exit Norg (A) and make your way to the door marked (B).
  • Turn right and head to the door marked (C), then continue to door at (D).
  • You will now be in a room with a boarded floor. Check (examine) the ??? (E) in the room to receive the Sealed Iron Box (key item).
  • Return to Norg (or just get KO'd) and talk to Keal. He will give you Tonko: Ichi

Notes:

  • Every mob in this area senses by sound, so Silent Oils (or Sneak) are a must. Invisibility and deodorize are not necessary.
  • It wouldn't be a bad idea to do the quest The Sahagin's Stash at the same time you do this quest.
  • After the June 2007 patch the level cap for several quests was raised. It is suspected that you must now be level 20 or higher to complete this quest.
  • (As of November 2007, this quest was successfully flagged and completed using a Level 10 job)

Game Description

Client: Keal (Norg)
Summary:

Bring back a sealed iron box from the Sahagin-infested Norg vault.
