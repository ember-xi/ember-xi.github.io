Zenith availability

Reference walkthrough. Completion is unconfirmed in the reviewed Zenith base: no dedicated quest handler or matching progression hook was found in the inspected zone/NPC files. A missing interaction may reflect unfinished content.

Before you begin

  • Complete A Manifest Problem and Purple, The New Black; In the Name of the Father should be current or later.
  • Bring one Pipira.

Starting point

Dhea Prandoleh · Windurst Waters [S] · H-10

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Dhea Prandoleh, then examine the Sealed Entrance at the F-10/F-11 boundary in West Sarutabaruta [S].
  2. Return to Dhea for the conversation about fish.
  3. Trade one Pipira to that Sealed Entrance. When asked about giving more, decline to finish and receive the flowers.
  4. Enter Windurst Waters [S] to begin A Feast for Gnats.

Battle and progress notes

  • An active Snake on the Plains can take priority at the entrance. Complete or cancel that active assignment before retrying.
  • Other accepted fish change the scene length; one Pipira is sufficient.

Completion and reward

Twelve Red Roses.

What this opens

  • A Feast for Gnats
