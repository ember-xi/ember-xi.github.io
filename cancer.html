Notes:

  • Located around H-7 on the first map
  • Spawned randomly by trading a Quus to the ??? there. Mostly it seems to spawn a Robber Crab, and occasionally it will spawn Cancer.
  • The drop rate on the Arondight appears to be very low.
  • High resistance to physical damage, so magic damage is useful.
  • Killable by 6 level 65 players; solo by a 75RDM/37NIN
  • The ??? repops every 15 minutes after the spawned mob is either defeated or despawned (takes 3 minutes to despawn).
  • Cancer still has a chance to spawn the next time the ??? appears, regardless of whether the previous spawned mob was defeated/despawned.
  • Seems to have a high resistance to bind and sleep, suceptible to gravity.
  • Has an enwater effect that cannot be dispelled.
  • Drops 6,000 gil.
