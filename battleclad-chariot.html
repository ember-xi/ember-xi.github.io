Battleclad Chariot

From Zenith XI Wiki

The final boss on floor 7 of the original Zhayolm Remnants.

Contents
  1. Reach and defeat
  2. Armor drops
  3. Sources

Reach and defeat

  1. Enter Zhayolm Remnants through the original Salvage route. Follow the floor guide, restore your equipment and abilities with cells, and reach floor 7.
  2. Discoid splits a large magic-damage total among nearby targets. Keep the intended participants close to the main target so they share the damage; use Shell and Stoneskin.
  3. Discoid can be stunned, but repeated Stun attempts build resistance. Do not rely on an endless Stun chain.
  4. Win for the Star Charioteer title, required by An Imperial Heist.

Armor drops

Bodb's Slops, Ea's Crackows, Freyr's Gloves, Phobos's Cuisses, Tsukikazu Jinpachi, Tsukikazu Togi. These are level-25 components for the original Salvage armor upgrades; they are not the finished level-75 pieces.
