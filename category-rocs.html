Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


General Information

  • System: Birds
  • Weaknesses: Ice, Piercing
  • Resistances: Slashing
  • Immune:
  • Naturally Enhanced Movement Speed
  • Cannot be slept with Black Magic, but are susceptible to Lullaby.
  • Also known as Big or Greater Bird when it comes to a creature type.

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Feather Barrier: Evasion Boost
Gliding Spike: 1-hit damage + knockback
Scratch: 1-hit damage + blindness
Stormwind: 15' AoE wind based attack. Removes Blink and Utsusemi shadows
Triple Attack: 3-hit damage
Dread Dive: 1-hit damage + knockback. Used in place of Gliding Spike only by certain NMs
Blind Vortex: 1-hit damage + blindness. Used in place of Scratch only by certain NMs
Giga Scream: 3-hit damage. Used in place of Triple Attack only by certain NMs
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
