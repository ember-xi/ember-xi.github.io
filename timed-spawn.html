Timed Spawn

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A Notorious Monster is considered to be a Timed Spawn when its appearance in the game is restricted to certain times in the game world, or the creature is limited to appearing a certain time after it was previously killed.

Some examples of timed spawn mobs:

  • The Spook - Spawns every 20:00, 0:00 in King Ranperre's Tomb
  • Shikigami Weapon - Respawns 21 earth hours after being killed in Ro'Maeve
  • Fafnir/Nidhogg - Respawns 21-24 hours after being killed in Dragon's Aery
