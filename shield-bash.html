Shield Bash

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

ja:シールドバッシュ

Job Ability

  • Delivers an attack that can stun the target. Shield required.
  • Obtained: Paladin Level 15
  • Recast Time: 5:00
  • Duration: Instant

Notes/Description

  • This ability requires a shield to use.
  • Causes a small amount of damage to enemy.
  • Produces a large amount of enmity upon usage.
  • If Valor Gauntlets, Knightly Earring, and/or Aegis is equipped, this skill is enhanced.
  • If Guardian's Ring or Highlander's Targe are equiped the damage of Shield Bash is increased.
  • Chance of stunning reduces as the difference between the target and the player's level increases.
  • Unreliable for stopping enemy TP moves with long charge-up times and does not always seem to stop spells with long cast times.
  • A Puppetmaster's Valoredge Frame frame can use this job ability, the Analyzer, and Hammermill attatchments improve its use of the ability.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Shield Bash" <t>
  • /ja "Shield Bash" <stnpc>
