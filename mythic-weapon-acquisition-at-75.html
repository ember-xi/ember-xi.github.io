Choose the correct path

Unlocking a Myth unlocks a weapon skill. Building the finished weapon instead uses the four quests below. These are separate objectives.

Before starting

Complete the Treasures of Aht Urhgan story through Eternal Mercenary, earn Captain Wildcat Badge through Promotion: Captain, and obtain Runic Key from Nyzul floor 100. Acquire your job’s base weapon from Nyzul Isle Investigation.
