Walkthrough

  • Talk to Perih Vashai in Windurst Woods (K-7) for a cutscene. Talk again if she says something unrelated.
    • Optional: her guards Muhk Johldy and Kapeh Myohrye give more details.
  • Go to Sauromugue Champaign. Climb the cliff at (L-8) and head south to the cave at (L-10).
    • Three Sabertooth Tigers roam outside the cave. They do not aggro at level 40+; lower levels can ride a chocobo in or use Invisible.
  • Examine the Tiger Bones in the cave to spawn Old Sabertooth. Do not attack it. Stay at the back of the cave and wait about three to five minutes while it dies on its own.
    • If you kill it by mistake, wait five minutes and examine the bones again.
  • Once it dies, examine the Tiger Bones again for the key item Old Tiger's Fang.
  • Return to Perih Vashai for your reward.
