Walkthrough

  • Perih Vashai wants you to watch an old sabertooth die and bring her back his bones.
  • Go to Sauromugue Champaign (L-10), 3 Sabertooth Tigers wait outside the cave. You can ride a chocobo into the cave to avoid them, but bring along an Instant Warp Scroll if you do not want to fight them on the way out.
  • In the cave, find and touch the the bones to spawn Old Sabertooth.
  • Wait for Old Sabertooth to die naturally (~ 5 minutes). He will not aggro you if you stay at the back of the cave. If you accidentally kill him, just re-examine and he will pop again.
  • Examine the bones and bring the Old Tiger's Fang back to Perih to complete the quest.

Client: Perih Vashai (Vashai's residence, Windurst Woods)
Summary:In order to become a truly great hunter, one must be accepted by mother nature. In order for that to happen, one must gain the fang of a tiger, the fanged king. But it does not sound like any old fang will do...
