Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Calls forth your automaton.
  • Obtained: Puppetmaster Level 1
  • Recast Time: 0:20:00
  • Duration: Instant

More Information

  • Your automaton will disappear when you are affected by a level restriction, and must be resummoned using the Activate ability.
  • If Deactivate is used while the Automaton's HP is at 100%, Activate's recast timer will be reset to 0:00. (This is much like DRG's Dismiss and Call Wyvern abilities)

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Activate" <me>
