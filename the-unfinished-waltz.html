The Unfinished Waltz

From Zenith XI Wiki

Dancer artifact weapon quest: obtain War Hoop.

Contents
  1. Requirements
  2. Steps
  3. Next
  4. Sources

Requirements

Dancer level 40+; Lakeside Minuet completed. Remain on Dancer for the Grauberg events and fight.

Steps

  1. Talk to Laila, then Rhea Myuliah, in Upper Jeuno (G-7).
  2. Travel to Grauberg (S) and find the ??? among three close trees in Witchfire Glen (F-5). Use the past zone, not Abyssea.
  3. Examine ??? for the scene, then examine it again without leaving the zone to spawn Migratory Hippogryph. Defeat it.
  4. Examine ??? again to receive The Essence of Dance.
  5. Open your permanent key-item list and read The Essence of Dance.
  6. Speak to Laila as Dancer to receive War Hoop.

Next

Change zones and wait until the next Vana’diel day before starting The Road to Divadom.

Zenith availability: this is the original quest route. Completion on the current Zenith server has not been confirmed; a wiki entry does not install the quest.
