Star Onion Brigade

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


The Star Onion Brigade (S.O.B.) are a group of young Tarutaru and Mithra who wish to protect and help the citizens of Windurst. Their slogan is to fight for truth, justice and the onion way.

Uniform

  • Neck: Justice Badge
  • Neck: Promise Badge

Known Members

  • Kohlo-Lakolo, young Tarutaru, Port Windurst (G-5), he is the leader of the group.
  • Chomomo, young Tarutaru, Windurst Walls (H-3)
  • Gomada-Vulmada, young Tarutaru, Port Windurst (G-5)
  • Juna Moshal, young Mithra, Windurst Walls (H-3)
  • Papo-Hopo, young Tarutaru, Port Windurst (G-5)
  • Pichichi, young Tarutaru, Port Windurst (G-5), youngest member of the group.
  • Pyo Nzon, young Mithra, Port Windurst (G-5)
  • Yafa Yaa, young Mithra, Port Windurst (G-5)
