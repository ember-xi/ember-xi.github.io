Linkshell Distributor

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Linkshell Distributors are NPCs that only sell New Linkshells and Pendant Compasses. They can be found in the starting cities, and are open 12:00 - 20:00 and closed on Darksday.

Merchants

Price: 8,000 gil
