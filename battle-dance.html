Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Delivers an area attack. Additional effect: DEX Down. Duration of effect varies with TP.

Description

Spell cost: 12 MP
Ecosystem: Beastmen
Type: Physical (Slashing)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: DEX+2
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 12

Timers

Casting Time: 1 second
Recast Time: 10 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Battle Dance as part of a combo to create Attack Bonus.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Attack Bonus combo.

  • Uppercut
  • Death Scissors
  • Spinal Cleave
  • Temporal Shift

Notes

  • Slashing damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: STR: 30%.
  • Area of effect is centered around caster.
  • Additional effect of DEX down is not guaranteed.
  • Oddly enough, Battle Dance has a hidden effect that removes Third Eye(With/without Seigan) in Ballista. It will also strip any form of Blink, including Zephyr Mantle from Puks.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Skillchain Element(s): Lightning (can open Liquefaction or Detonation; can close Impaction or Fusion)
