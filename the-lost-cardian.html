Walkthrough

  • The Two of Swords has gone missing. Nobody knows where he has gone off to.
  • Teigero-Bangero and Panta-Putta will tell you they haven't seen it.
  • Speak to Monberaux in Upper Jeuno, who tells you he found the Two of Swords after he shut himself down because Panta-Putta broke his heart.
    • He gives you the Two of Swords Key Item, the reward and title.
    • You may have to talk to him several times, even up to 5 times and even if he repeats things he has already said relating to other quest/missions.
  • *Note: it is only after receiving this cut scene from Monberaux that the quest will appear in your current quest menu.
  • You must then take Two of Swords to Windurst, and deliver him to Apururu at the Manustery. This will lead you to The Kind Cardian quest.


  • If you are on CoP 3-5 Darkness Named Monberaux will not discuss this quest - he will only tell you "I wonder if there might be some connection between the boy and that young lady, Prishe."
  • If you are on Windurst Mission 8-2 you will be unable to deliver Two of Swords to Apururu.
