From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is the first Monk Artifact Armor quest - otherwise known as AF1.

  Start NPC:   Oggbi - Port Bastok (E-6)
  Requirements:
   Monk 40+
  Items Needed:   Pickaxe
  Title:   None
  Repeatable:   Yes, after AF is complete.
  Reward:   Beat Cesti

  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   The First Meeting

Walkthrough

  • After talking to Oggbi, head to Gusgen Mines and look for a ??? around G-7.
  • To get to the ??? in Gusgen Mines, go straight across the first map and down the stairs. Use the left hand door to enter the second map.
  • Go to the room in the northwest corner and follow the corridor that runs west from this room. This will take you down to a new map. Go to the second room.
  • The ??? is located on the left side of the room toward the back. There is another ??? on the left side at the back of a shed (this is not the one you want).
  • Use a pickaxe there and it will trigger the Wandering Ghost to spawn.
  • Kill it and it will drop the Miner's Pendant. Bring that back for your reward.


Game Description

Client: Oggbi (Steaming Sheep Restaurant, Port Bastok)
Summary:

You are to go to the Gusgen Mines and pass the same trial as Oggbi's former student, Cornelia. Use a pickaxe on a certain hole in the mines to lure the Wandering Ghost out. Defeat it and bring back its "miner's pendant."
