Statistics

Adamantoise Soup
A dish often served at banquets held
by the Quadav Adamantking. Its odor
prevents it from becoming a popular
dish in culinary circles.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Food Effects: (3 hours, All Races)

  • Strength -7
  • Dexterity -7
  • Vitality -7
  • Agility -7
  • Intelligence -7
  • Mind -7
  • Charisma -7

Other Uses

Guild Points Value: None

Used in Spawn: Kurrea

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs
