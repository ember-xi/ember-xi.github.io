Missions

Missions are tasks that advance the main story lines of Final Fantasy XI. Missions for the three starting cities reward higher ranks, gil, national flags and the ability to buy new items with Conquest Points. Missions from the expansions unlock previously inaccessible areas and special equipment. On EmberXI Trusts are allowed in every Battlefield and all Home Points are open, so the whole story can be played solo.
