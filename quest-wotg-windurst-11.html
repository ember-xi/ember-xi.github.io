Zenith availability

Reference walkthrough. Completion is unconfirmed in the reviewed Zenith base: no dedicated quest handler or matching progression hook was found in the inspected zone/NPC files. A missing interaction may reflect unfinished content.

Before you begin

  • Complete Howl from the Heavens and Darkness Descends; reach Adieu, Lilisette.
  • Each participant needs three Dorter Keys.

Starting point

Dhea Prandoleh · Windurst Waters [S] · H-10

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Dhea, examine the Mithran Bivouac in Meriphataud Mountains [S] (northeast I-8), then enter Castle Oztroja [S] for a scene.
  2. Farm three Dorter Keys per character from Yagudo Eradicators, Knight Templars, Prioresses or Prelates in Meriphataud Mountains [S] or Castle Oztroja [S].
  3. Climb to the castle’s second floor via J-8. On the connecting map, take I-9 or G-7 to the outdoor Iron Grille cells.
  4. Check each grille first. Trade a key only where a presence is detected. Free three prisoners to receive the Poet god’s key; empty cells consume keys without credit.
  5. Return to the connecting map, pass the G-8 door and G-9 passage, and climb to the top. Light a torch to open the Brass Door and reach the Collapsing Floor behind it.
  6. Check the floor for a scene, then again to fight Tzee Xicu the Manifest and two wind elementals. Defeat Tzee Xicu for completion.

Battle and progress notes

  • Defeating the elementals disables their Astral Flow attacks. Bring Echo Drops for silence.
  • For a retry, collect another Poet god’s key from the Mithran Bivouac, once per Vana’diel day.

Completion and reward

Hi-Reraiser; collect it at the Mithran Bivouac if inventory prevented receipt.

What this opens
