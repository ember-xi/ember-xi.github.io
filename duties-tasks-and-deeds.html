Duties, Tasks, and Deeds

From Zenith XI Wiki

Submit Alexandrite, two purchased relics, and a fresh record of all 50 Assaults.

Contents
  1. Requirements
  2. Steps
  3. Required totals
  4. Record by Assault area
  5. Sources

Requirements

An Imperial Heist completed; five free inventory slots for the Assault records.

Steps

  1. Speak to Paparoon beneath the archway in Nashmau (G-7). Read all assignment options and receive the five Assault records.
  2. Complete the four objectives below in any order. Turn in progress to Paparoon.
  3. For each Assault, win and trade the matching record to the Rune of Release before leaving. All 50 normal Assaults must be recorded after receiving these records; your earlier Captain clears are not a substitute.
  4. Return all five fully marked records and the two purchased items, and finish the Alexandrite total.
  5. Speak to Paparoon again for Paparoon’s sealed invitation. Continue to Forging a New Myth.
