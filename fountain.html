The Fountain

The Fountain — Halvard
WhereLower Jeuno, the fountain row — Halvard stands by the Forge Stone; Teodor and Brandt are a few steps away
MenuThe gates: Nine Teeth · The Henge · Ember Trials · Ambuscade Normal · Ambuscade Intense
The forgeOne Salvage-set piece per slot for 20 Alexandrite
AlsoPays out chest prizes refused for a full bag; Ambuscade win milestones
CurrencyPiece of Alexandrite

Halvard is the gatekeeper of EmberXI's own content. Talk to him for every dungeon and arena, and for the forge that turns Alexandrite into Salvage gear.

The gates

The forge

Pick a set, then a slot (head, body, hands, legs, feet). Each piece costs 20 Alexandrite. The forge never makes twins: if you already carry the piece, it refuses.

Where Alexandrite comes from

  • Ember Trials chests: 2–4 per run.
  • Ambuscade: 4 (Intense) or 2 (Normal) per win.
  • Halvard's milestones above.
