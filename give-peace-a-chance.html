Walkthrough

  • Aht Urhgan Whitegate: Talk to Mishhar (H-8), 2nd floor for first cutscene.
  • Wajaom Woodlands: Go to (K-7) in at night and click on the ??? (After 20:00 game time) for another cutscene. (NOTE: The ??? is located at the bottom-center of the (K-7) grid box and take care not to aggro the fomors.)


  • Aht Urhgan Whitegate: Go back to Mishhar (H-8) and you will get another cutscene.
  • Wajaom Woodlands: Travel to (E-12) and zone to Mamook.
  • Mamook (M-9): >>Caution!<< Mamool Ja detect by sight. At least two of them are in the vicinity of the ???; one in the tunnel and one just outside. Use Invisible spell or powder to approach the ???.
    • The ??? is located at the end of the tunnel (L-9). You must drop Invisible effect and click on the ??? to view the cutscene. You can't be attacked while you view the cutscene (so enjoy it) and it is a matter of luck whether the Mamool Ja will be facing you after the cutscene is finished.
  • Aht Urhgan Whitegate: Return to Mishhar for final cutscene and reward.

Game Description

Client: Mishhar (Serpentking Square, Aht Urhgan Whitegate)
Summary:

Mishhar wants you to find out what Isween is doing out in the Wajaom Woodlands at night.
