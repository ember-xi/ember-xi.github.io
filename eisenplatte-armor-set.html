Eisenplatte Armor Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

An imposing set of modestly decorated, yet solid, plate mail. An ideal set for any tank or heavy melee damage dealer looking to reduce the damage they take from hits. Aside from a solid defensive rating, this set also gives bonuses to vitality and agility, to help the wearer evade more hits, and take the hits that do connect more effectively. This armor is named in German meaning "Iron Plate".

Number of Pieces: 5


Cost to store: 400g (Eisenplatte Armor Claim Slip)

This set has a non-storable High Quality Version.
