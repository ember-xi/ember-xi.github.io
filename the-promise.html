Walkthrough

  • Talk to Kohlo-Lakolo to start the quest.
  • Talk to Pichichi's mom Chamama at the Rarab Tail Hostelry in the northern map of Windurst Waters at (F-10).
  • Chamama says she will make an Invisible Man Sticker (key item), but that you must get her a clump of Shoalweed first.
  • Shoalweed is Rare/Ex and can be found by killing Recluse Spiders in Kuftal Tunnel.
  • Trade her the Shoalweed for an Invisible Man Sticker, and return to Kohlo-Lakolo for your reward.


  • If you are rank 10 in Windurst (or have been and changed nation) and kill Fenrir you will get a key item: "Dark Mana Orb". When you have that go talk to Kohlo-Lakolo for a final cutscene that ties together the S.O.B. quests with the Windurst mission story line.

Game Description

Client: Kohlo-Lakolo (Warehouses, Port Windurst)
Summary:To help the Joker Cardian escape far away from Windurst, the Star Onion Brigade wants you to have Pichichi's mother, Chamama, make him an "Invisible Man Sticker."
