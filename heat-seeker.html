Statistics

Heat seeker
A thunder-based automaton attachment.

Stackable: 12

Note: This is an Automaton Attachment:

Automaton Effect Req. Capacity
 Analyzes enemy evasion patterns and gradually enhances accuracy  Lightning x 1
  • 0 Thunder Maneuvers: No effect
  • 1 Thunder Maneuver: Increases accuracy by 1 point per second
  • The Heat Seeker does not increase accuracy based upon previously missing a hit. Instead it increases accuracy at a linear rate and caps at approximately +30 accuracy.
  • The accuracy bonus does not initially wear off upon the expiration of all Thunder Maneuvers. It is possible that the accuracy bonus decays over time, however, but this is unknown at this time.
