Statistics

Other Uses

Resale Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Abjuration

Obtained by trading the following items to Alphollon C Meriard at the cathedral in Northern San d'Oria.

  • Neptunal Abjuration: Head (dropped from Fafnir and Odin)
  • Cursed Cap -1

Derivations

In Ancient Greece, a Hecatomb was a sacrifice to the gods of 100 cattle (hecaton = one hundred). Hecatombs were commonly offered to Greek gods such as Apollo, Athena, and Hera, during special religious ceremonies.
