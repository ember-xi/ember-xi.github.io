Rivals

   Bastok Reputation 3

  Repeatable:   No
  Reward:   Wolf Gorget

  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   None

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Detzo.
  • Obtain a Mythril Sallet and trade it to Detzo for the cut-scene and to receive your reward.
Note
You will have the Mythril Sallet again as soon the cut-scene is over, as well as as a Wolf Gorget.

Game Description

Client: Detzo (Ore Street, Bastok Mines)
Summary:

Lend Detzo a mythril sallet so he can win a contest against his rival.
