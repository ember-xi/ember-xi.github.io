Walkthrough

  • Speak with Rauteinot in Kazham. He will tell you the Mithra women do not listen to his sermons, but he has found a way to trick them. He asks you to retrieve a slab of Elshimo Marble from Yuhtunga Jungle.
    • Elshimo Marble is Rare/Ex and drops from Ivory Lizards in Yuhtunga Jungle.
  • Trade the Elshimo Marble to him and he will give you a Key Item called Rauteinot's Parcel.
  • Bring Rauteinot's Parcel to Mulaujeant in front of the Blacksmith's Guild in Northern San d'Oria.
  • Wait until midnight JP time and zone once, then talk to Mulaujeant again and he will give you a Key Item called Sublime Statue of the Goddess to give to Rauteinot.
  • Return to Rauteinot in Kazham and you will receive your reward, a scroll of Teleport-Yhoat.

Game Description

Client: Rauteinot (Kazham)
Summary:

First, find a slab of Elshimo marble in the Yuhtunga Jungle to help the young missionary realize his vision.
