A Taste of Honey

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:    Qutiba - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (K-12)
  Requirements:
    None
  Items Needed:    White Honey x3
  Title:    None
  Repeatable:    Yes
  Reward:    Irmik Helvasi and access to additional cooking recipes

  Previous Quest:    Vanishing Act
  Next Quest:    None

Walkthrough

  • Complete Vanishing Act quest; wait until after JST midnight, then talk to Qutiba for a cutscene.
  • Trade Qutiba three pots of White Honey for another cutscene and reward of Irmik Helvasi, the recipe to make it and another food recipe.
    • You may trade more White Honey if you forget the Irmik Helvasi recipe; you'll possibly get an additional food recipe also.

Game Description

Client: Qutiba (Shararat Teahouse, Aht Urhgan Whitegate)
Summary:

Qutiba wants you to bring her three pots of white honey so she can make the famous Near Eastern confectionery "irmik helvasi."
