Head Butt (Mandragora)

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Were you looking for the blue magic spell Head Butt, or Head Butt (Adamantoise)?
Deals damage to a single target. Additional effect: Stun

Description

  • Family: Mandragora
  • Type: Physical
  • Can be dispelled: N/A
  • Utsusemi/Blink absorb: 1 shadow
  • Range: Melee
  • Notes: This is not the learnable Blue Mage spell Head Butt. The correct version is used by Quadav enemies.
