Carbuncle Debacle

Carbuncle Debacle
Starting NPCCarbuncle — House of the Hero, Windurst Walls (G-4)
Required FameNone
Level RestrictionLevel 50
Items NeededLightning Pendulum · Daze-breaker Charm · Wind Pendulum
Additional RequirementsSummoner main job to start · Class Reunion completed
TitleParagon of Summoner Excellence
RepeatableYes, see Vingijard in Lower Jeuno after the set is complete
DescriptionCarbuncle calls for help again. Two more of the awakened crystals have spat out guardians, and Koru-Moru sends you after both.

Walkthrough

  • Examine the House of the Hero in Windurst Walls (G-4) as SMN to hear Carbuncle's cry for help.
    • Zone first if you just finished another quest involving Carbuncle.
  • Talk to Koru-Moru in Windurst Walls (E-7). The Rhinostery researcher he mentions is Ripapa.
  • Talk to Ripapa in Mhaura (I-9) for the Lightning Pendulum.
    • She is on the upper level, just east of the Porter Moogle.
    • If you are on Trial by Lightning you may have to finish it first, then talk to Koru-Moru again.
  • Go to the Cloister of Storms in The Boyahda Tree and trade the Lightning Pendulum to the Lightning Protocrystal.
    • Boyahda Tree Home Point #1 is outside the Cloister.
    • As SMN, a trial-size tuning fork of lightning teleports you there.
    • Buffs cast outside do not wear off on entering.
  • Defeat the Lightning Gremlin and the Thunder Gremlin.
    • The Lightning Gremlin is a Warrior; its hits drain TP.
    • The Thunder Gremlin is a Red Mage; its hits drain MP.
    • Both resist Sleep and Repose, and drain 1–40 per hit. Stoneskin absorbs the damage but not the drain, so shadows work better.
    • Kill the Thunder Gremlin first so your avatar keeps its MP.
  • Return to Koru-Moru — he gives you the Daze-breaker Charm.
  • Talk to Agado-Pugado in Rabao (G-9) for the Wind Pendulum.
  • Go to the Cloister of Gales in Cape Teriggan (F-5).
    • Route: Western Altepa Desert → Kuftal Tunnel (enter at H-4, cross to the north exit) → Cape Teriggan.
    • Cape Teriggan Home Point #1 is outside the Cloister.
  • Trade the Wind Pendulum to the Wind Protocrystal and defeat Ogmios.
    • A Manticore that spams Silencega and Dispelga — bring Echo Drops.
    • Keep mages back, away from his AoE TP moves, Riddle in particular.
  • Return to Koru-Moru in Windurst Walls (E-7) for the cutscene and the Evoker's Horn.
    • Both Pendulums can be tossed after the fights.
    • If you lose a fight, get a new Pendulum from the same NPC.

EmberXI changes

  • No Fame requirement.
  • Trusts can be summoned inside both battlefields.
  • Artifact pieces can be upgraded to +1 by trading them to Brandt in Lower Jeuno with 4 Alexandrite.
