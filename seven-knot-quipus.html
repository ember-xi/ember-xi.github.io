Statistics

Seven-knot Quipus
A knotted rope used by the Yagudo to
deliver orders. The type of knot
signifies the order (attack), while the
number of knots signifies the location
(Sarutabaruta).

Stackable: Not Stackable

Other Uses

Guild Points Value: None

Used in Quests: Garrison/West Sarutabaruta

Obtained from Desynthesis

  • None

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
