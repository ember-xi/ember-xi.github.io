Open a gate alone

Obtain Pouch of Weighted Stones at Map 1 (G-8), then select the closed gate. The key item is retained. Directions to the stones.

The reviewed door scripts open each gate for 15 seconds after a short delay. Continue through before it closes.

Pressure switches

The original method uses four players, one on each of the associated floor switches. When all four are pressed, the gate opens briefly.
