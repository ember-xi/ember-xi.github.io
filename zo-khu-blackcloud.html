Zo'Khu Blackcloud

From Zenith XI Wiki

A Black Mage Quadav NM, level 36–38, in Beadeaux.

Contents
  1. Find and defeat
  2. Notable loot
  3. Sources

Find and defeat

  1. Go to the upper level of Beadeaux around E-11/F-11.
  2. Kill the Zircon Quadav placeholder and watch subsequent respawns. The classic lottery window opens after about one hour; later retail shortened this, so do not apply the retail timer to an era server without checking.
  3. The NM uses Manafont. Interrupt dangerous spells and keep nearby Quadav out of the fight.

Notable loot

Marine Hat and Faerie Shield.
