Auto Refresh

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Gradually restores MP.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • While active, this trait restores 1 point of MP per tick.
    • Like all other Blue Magic Traits, the effect does not stack with Auto Refresh from Paladin or Summoner subjob.

Blue Magic Spells that Achieve this Trait

  • A combination of 8 Auto Refresh points from the table below is needed to achieve Auto Refresh.
