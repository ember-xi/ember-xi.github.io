Ramuh

Ramuh — Trial by Lightning
AvatarRamuh (lightning)
NPCRipapa — Mhaura (I-9), in front of the Sailors' Stay inn
RequirementPrime: level 65+. Trial-Size: Summoner under 65 with Carbuncle. No Fame needed.
Key ItemTuning Fork of Lightning → Whisper of Storms
BattlefieldCloister of Storms — behind the great waterfall in the Boyahda Tree (map 2, I-11). 30 minutes, no level cap, up to 6 players, Trusts allowed
Ramuh PrimeAbout 8,000 HP · weak to Earth · very long magic-detection range
Best day / AvoidEarthsday / Lightningday (and New Moon)
Bar-spellBarthundra
RepeatableOnce a day (after midnight JST)
TitleHeir of the Great Lightning
RewardsRamuh (pact) · Ramuh's Staff · Lightning Belt · Lightning Ring · Elder Branch · 10,000 gil

Ramuh sleeps in the Cloister of Storms, deep inside the Boyahda Tree. Two ways to win his pact: the Trial-Size fight under 65, or the Prime fight from 65 that also gives the Whisper of Storms for Fenrir.

Under 65: Trial-Size Trial by Lightning

  1. Talk to Ripapa in Mhaura (I-9) as SMN. You get the Mini Tuning Fork of Lightning.
  2. Trade the Mini Fork back to Ripapa. You are teleported to the Cloister of Storms.
  3. Trade the Mini Fork to the Lightning Protocrystal and enter.
  4. Summon Carbuncle, keep him in front, stay back and heal him, use the Blood Pact when it is ready.
  5. Talk to the crystal after the win. Ramuh is yours. You keep the Mini Fork.
Trial-Size rules: solo, level 20 cap, Carbuncle only, 15 minutes. Buffs cast outside are kept. Best day: Earthsday. Never on Lightningday.

65 and up: Trial by Lightning (Prime)

  1. Talk to Ripapa in Mhaura (I-9). You get the Tuning Fork of Lightning. If she does not give it at once, talk to her again.
  2. Talk to Ripapa again while you hold the fork. You are sent straight to the Cloister of Storms.
  3. Summon your Trusts and your avatar, then examine the Lightning Protocrystal and pick Trial by Lightning.
  4. Defeat Ramuh Prime. You receive the Whisper of Storms.
  5. Going for Fenrir? Keep the Whisper and do not return to Ripapa yet. Otherwise return to her and choose your reward.

Walking to the Cloister of Storms

  • Enter the Boyahda Tree from The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah at (K-12).
  • Follow the path to the first fork and take a left; take the next right and follow it to a large room with a huge tree in the middle.
  • Go northwest through that room and a second big room to (A-7) on map 1 and head up the tunnel. You come out at (E-6) on map 2 in another huge room.
  • Head south-east until you see the immense waterfall (I-11). Walk under it and down the passage behind it into the Cloister of Storms.
  • Boyahda Tree Home Point #1 is on the way — take it for later runs.

The Fight

  • Ramuh Prime uses Shock Strike, Thunderspark (AoE paralyze), Rolling Thunder and Lightning Armor.
  • At about 50% HP he uses Judgment Bolt, his Astral Flow pact: heavy AoE lightning damage. Keep everyone above half HP before that point.
  • Lightning damage heals him: no Thunder spells, no lightning weapon skills, no weapons with additional lightning damage.
  • Very long magic-detection range — buff outside the crystal, then enter.
  • The arena is a long path up a hill with a switchback: mages can stand above the fight and still reach it.
EmberXI solo tips: bring Titan (earth beats lightning; Mountain Buster, and Titan takes hits well). Keep Barthundra up, let the tank Trust hold Ramuh on the lower path, and stand up the slope with the healer.

Rewards

One per win. The Whisper is used up when you take a reward; win again (once a day) for the next.

Prime Pact (level 75)

Win this Prime fight as SMN 75 with Ramuh already learned and the message === The Prime Pact === appears: you learn Thunderstorm (the level 75 Blood Pact).

EmberXI changes

  • No Fame needed (Windurst Fame 6 gate removed).
  • Ripapa also runs the Trial-Size fight for summoners under 65 (mini fork, trade to teleport).
  • Talking to Ripapa while you hold the Tuning Fork of Lightning teleports you to the Cloister of Storms.
  • Trusts allowed in the Prime battlefield; Prime requires level 65+.
  • Repeatable once a day.
  • Prime Pact at SMN 75 (see above).
