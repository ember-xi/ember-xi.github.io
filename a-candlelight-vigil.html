A Candlelight Vigil

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Ilumida - Upper Jeuno (G-8)
  Requirements:
   Jeuno Reputation 4
  Items Needed:   Holy Candle (key item)
  Title:   Activist for Kindness
  Repeatable:   No
  Reward:   Flower Necklace

  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   Searching for the Right Words

Walkthrough

  • Ilumida asks you to obtain a candle from the Temple of the Goddess in memory of her late husband.
  • Head to the Temple of the Goddess right outside her house and speak with Rouliette.
  • Rouliette will ask you to complete the quest Candle-making.
  • After completing the quest Candle-making, talk to Ilumida to complete this quest.

Game Description

Client: Ilumida (House, Upper Jeuno)
Summary:

Ilumida wants to light a candle in memory of her husband. Bring her one from the Temple of the Goddess.
