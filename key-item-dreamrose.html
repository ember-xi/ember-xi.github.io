How to obtain

Examine the Dreamrose flowers at Western Altepa Desert (G-7), defeat Sabotender Enamorado, then examine the flowers again.

What to do with it

Return to Halver. The NM kill alone does not put the Dreamrose in your key items.

Mission walkthrough

Leaute's Last Wishes

Trading and storage

This is a key item. Find it under Key Items, not your ordinary inventory. It cannot be bought at the Auction House, crafted, traded to another player or placed in a bazaar.

Reference

Native mission definition

Additional level-75 reference

Description: In her will, the late Queen Leaute asked that this flower forever bloom in her chateau garden. The pollen of the dreamrose is said to cause hallucinations.
