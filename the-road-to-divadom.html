The Road to Divadom

From Zenith XI Wiki

Obtain Dancer's Tights and open the three commissioned artifact pieces.

Contents
  1. Requirements
  2. Steps
  3. Next
  4. Sources

Requirements

Dancer level 50+; The Unfinished Waltz completed; zone and next Vana’diel day after completion. Bring one Yagudo Glue.

Steps

  1. Talk to Laila in Upper Jeuno (G-7) as Dancer.
  2. Enter Jugner Forest (S) for a scene. Stay on Dancer.
  3. Examine Glowing Pebbles at Lake Mechieume (I-5).
  4. Trade one Yagudo Glue to Glowing Pebbles.
  5. Return to Laila as Dancer for Dancer's Tights.
  6. Talk to Olgald beside the dancers to open Matthias commissions for the tiara, bangles and shoes.

Next

After zoning and the next Vana’diel day, start Comeback Queen.

Zenith availability: this is the original quest route. Completion on the current Zenith server has not been confirmed; a wiki entry does not install the quest.
