Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Causes all attacks to drain enemy's HP.
  • Obtained: Dark Knight Level 1
  • Recast Time: 2:00:00
  • Duration: 0:00:30

Notes/Description

  • Blood Weapon is a Dark Knight's two hour special.
  • When active, all melee attacks will heal the Dark Knight by the same amount of damage they inflict.
  • It does not stack with Weapon Skills.
  • This ability has no effect on accuracy, as attacks made while Blood Weapon is active may still miss.
  • The Effects of Blood Weapon stack with the ability Jump and High Jump if a player chooses to sub Dragoon.Verification Needed
  • Most effectively used with Souleater to counteract HP lost.
  • This ability has no effect on Undead enemies.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Blood Weapon" <me>
  • Since Blood Weapon is a two hour special, some people prefer not to make a macro for it to prevent unintentional activation.
