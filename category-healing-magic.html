Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Spells that use the Healing Magic skill are all spells that help players recover from battle. These spells help players regain Hit Points, remove negative Status Effects, or recover from death/unconsciousness. Healing Magic also helps defend against spell interruptions for any spells in its category.

Healing Magic skill:
  • Determines the chance to successfully remove certain more powerful versions of status effects such as Doom or special Curses.
  • Increases the amount of Hit Points 'Cure' spells will recover, up to a "soft" cap, and increases the amount of damage done by cure spells to undead.
