Before you begin

  • Win The Celestial Nexus and finish its exit scenes.

Starting point

Norg entry; Neptune’s Spire door · Norg

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Norg and watch its post-victory scene with Gilgamesh.
  2. In Lower Jeuno, examine the Neptune’s Spire door for the Aldo epilogue. These two scenes can be viewed in either order.
  3. For the joint RoZ/CoP ending, complete CoP’s Dawn epilogue, then Storms of Fate, Shadows of the Departed and Apocalypse Nigh. The connecting quest pages give the full sequence.

Battle and progress notes

  • Awakening can remain the active mission after these two scenes. In the pinned implementation, Apocalypse Nigh’s earring selection completes Awakening and advances the log to The Last Verse.
  • The epilogue alone adds no extra boss fight. Sky access was already obtained during The Gate of the Gods.

Completion and reward

RoZ epilogue scenes; prerequisite progress for Shadows of the Departed.

What this opens

  • Joint RoZ/CoP ending quest chain, after its other prerequisites

Walkthrough

  • Zone into Norg for a cutscene with Gilgamesh.
  • Enter the Neptune's Spire in Lower Jeuno for a cutscene with Aldo.

Note: The last two cutscenes above can be done in any order. Be sure to complete both of these cutscenes before going to Ru'Lude Gardens for Shadows of the Departed. If you have trouble getting your cutscene in Ru'Lude Gardens, make sure you received the correct cutscenes for this mission.
