Notes:

  • Spawns at (I-5). Guards the Round Frigicite needed for Atop the Highest Mountains quest.
  • Respawns ~15 seconds after being killed.
  • To get the Round Frigicite, click on the ??? which spawns after Boreal Tiger dies.
  • Uses Draw In, and has increased attack speed and movement speed.
  • Has Counter.
  • Casts Firaga II, Flare and Blaze Spikes.
  • Casts Doom after 30 minutes.
  • Uses Mighty Strikes.
  • Very tough fight for a party of level 50 players.
