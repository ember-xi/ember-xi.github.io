Combat summoning

The installed Trust Combat package allows summoning while engaged or under aggro. Casting time and interruption still apply. Alliance, battlefield, zone and party-capacity restrictions remain.

Party capacity

Start with three Trusts. Complete Trust Journeys for four and then five. The party still holds six members in total; each real player uses a slot.

When a Trust falls

A fallen Trust has its own recovery timer. Read Trust Combat Recovery for the installed recovery rules.
