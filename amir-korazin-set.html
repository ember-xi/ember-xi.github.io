Amir Korazin Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A flexable and durable set of armor, forged in the Near Eastern land of Aht Urhgan, the Amir armor set is issued by the Empire to adventurers completing various Assault missions for the nation. Wearable by only a few of the heavier-armored jobs, the Amir gear provides the highest stat boosts in Attack, Evasion, and Accuracy, but also provides minor boosts to other stats. Also notable is the added resistance to magic and breath damage - 8% of damage from these sources, in fact - but only if the wearer equips the entire set.
