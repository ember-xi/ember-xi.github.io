Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Beastmen tend to be grouped into tiers of 10 levels each, with certain kinds of beastmen within those tiers.

Tier 3 is for beastmen whose levels range from 21 to 30. These are fairly widespread in the Middle Lands, especially goblins. Silver Beastcoins can be stolen from the majority of these beastmen.
