Mission 9-1 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 8-2 Lightbringer.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Halver.
  2. Collect three figures from the ???: Figure of Titan (Batallia Downs), Figure of Leviathan (Valley of Sorrows; the NM Suparna appears) and Figure of Garuda (Xarcabard).
  3. Return to Halver.
Mechanics: Suparna and its Fledglings in the valley are level 65-70. Pull them one at a time and use Sleepga.
