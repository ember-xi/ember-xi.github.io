Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Adds TP Bonus effect to Warcry.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • TP Bonus extends to other party members affected by Warcry, with the bonus reduced by 50% (Party members will receive a +5 TP Bonus per merit while affected by a Warcry with Savagery).
  • This bonus only affects Weapon Skills performed while Warcry is active.
  • Each additional Merit upgrade increases the TP Bonus by 10.
  • The TP Bonus effect works similarly to martial weapon TP bonus.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Warrior's Mask indirectly enhances this trait by extending the duration of Warcry by 10 seconds, providing a larger "window" for using your Weapon Skill.
