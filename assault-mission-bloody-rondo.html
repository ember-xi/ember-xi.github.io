Walkthrough

Defeat Count Dracula to complete the Assault.

Notes

  • Players charmed by Nocturnal Servitude are turned into bats, and can only be slept with Lullaby, Sheep Song, or Yawn. Bind also works well.
  • Count Dracula will create a Cursed Doppelganger of his current target when he uses Nosferatu's Kiss
  • Gravity gives Cursed Doppelganger a minor reduction to running speed.
  • Cursed Doppelganger does not have very much HP and can be killed quickly
  • If Count Dracula's TP move is stunned it will use another after stun wears.
  • Count Dracula is a Dark Knight, and will cast Drain-stat spells and tier 2 spells often
  • Makes kiting extremely easy
