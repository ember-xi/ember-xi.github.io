Red Cryptex

Statistics

Red Cryptex
A red stone used by the Orcs to
deliver orders. The red color signifies
"Ronfaure," and the number of nails
imbedded in its surface signifies
"Attack."

Other Uses

Guild Points Value: None

Used in Quests: Garrison/West Ronfaure

Obtained from Desynthesis

  • None

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
