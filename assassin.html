Assassin

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Enhances Trick Attack.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • When Trick Attack is used while this trait is active, the next attack made by user is guaranteed to be a critical and cannot miss as long as the ability's requirements are met.
    • Pre-Assassin Trick Attack's formula is:
      Damage = (Base Damage + Total AGI) x pDIF
    • Once Assassin is obtained the formula changes to reflect the crit hit bonus:
      Damage = (Base Damage + Total AGI) x Critical hit pDIF

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • None

Assassin

  • Obtained: Thief Level 60
