Clear Mind

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Increases the amount of MP recovered while resting.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Each upgrade of this trait increases the amount of MP Recovered While Healing.
    • Repeats of this trait do not stack.
      • For example, a Level 31 RDM/BLM will not receive Clear Mind I twice.
    • Standard recovery of MP per tick while resting without this trait is as follows: +12, +13, +14...

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Although no equipment directly enhances Clear Mind,
    there is equipment that increases your MP Recovered While Healing.
