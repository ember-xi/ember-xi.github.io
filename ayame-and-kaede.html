Ayame and Kaede

Ayame and Kaede
Starting NPCKaede / Ensetsu — Port Bastok (J-5 / I-5), the northernmost house
Required FameNone
Level RestrictionLevel 30
Items NeededKey items: Strangely Shaped Coral, Sealed Dagger
TitleNone
RepeatableNo
DescriptionEnsetsu wants a strangely shaped coral from the Korroloka Tunnel, then a dagger from Norg.
Rewards
  • Ability to become a Ninja
← Previous questNext quest →
——

Walkthrough

  • In Port Bastok talk to Kaede in the northernmost house (J-5) for a cutscene.
  • Talk to Kagetora inside Warehouse 2 (F-6).
  • Go back to the house and talk to Ensetsu (I-5). The quest now appears in your log.
  • Go to Korroloka Tunnel, Map 5 (K/L-8), and examine the ??? to spawn three Korroloka Leeches.
    • Clear the Thread Leeches nearby first — they link.
    • The leeches attack whoever touches the ???. Use Sneak if you are low level.
  • Kill the leeches and examine the ??? for the key item Strangely Shaped Coral.
    • If it says "nothing out of the ordinary", wait for the bodies to vanish and try again.
  • Return to Ensetsu. He sends you to Norg.
  • In Norg talk to Ryoma on the docks (H-8) for the key item Sealed Dagger.
  • Return the dagger to Ensetsu for the final cutscene.
