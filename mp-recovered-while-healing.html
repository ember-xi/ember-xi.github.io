MP Recovered While Healing

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

While resting, your character recovers a set number of Magic Points every 10 seconds(tick). Equipment with this trait will increase this set number of recovered MP. This is often colloquially referred to as HMP, or Healing MP.

Base MP recovery rate starts at 12. Each tick thereafter increase by one. The sequence goes as follows:

  • 1st tick: 12MP recovered (from 0 to 12 MP)
  • 2nd tick: 13MP recovered (from 12 to 25 MP)
  • 3rd tick: 14MP recovered (from 25 to 39 MP)
  • etc~

Abilities gained from jobs or equipment increase the base and/or the increment increase.

Example - Base character with Clear Mind I or MP Recovered While Healing +3:
(Possible ways for this include Level 15 Black Mage or any Level 1 character eating an Acorn Cookie)

  • 1st tick: 15MP recovered (from 0 to 15 MP)
  • 2nd tick: 16MP recovered (from 15 to 31 MP)
  • 3rd tick: 17MP recovered (from 31 to 48 MP)
  • etc~

Example - Base character with Clear Mind I and MP Recovered While Healing +3:
(Possible ways for this include Level 15 Black Mage eating an Acorn Cookie)

  • 1st tick: 18MP recovered (from 0 to 18 MP)
  • 2nd tick: 19MP recovered (from 18 to 37 MP)
  • 3rd tick: 20MP recovered (from 37 to 57 MP)
  • etc~

Example - Base character with Clear Mind III(+9HMP and +1 increase per increment):

  • 1st tick: 21MP recovered (from 0 to 21 MP)
  • 2nd tick: 23MP recovered (from 21 to 44 MP)
  • 3rd tick: 25MP recovered (from 44 to 69 MP)
  • etc~


All increments are cumulative. If you are interrupted while healing (i.e. from standing or being damaged) recovery is reset back to base. If you are healing, even at max HP and MP, and your max increases during this time (such as coming out of weakened status), the culmination applies, and you will not start healing at base.
