Vengeful Wrath

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Goraow - Bastok Mines (I-6)
  Requirements:
   Bastok Reputation 3
  Items Needed:   Quadav Helm
  Title:   Avenger
  Repeatable:   Yes
  Reward:   900g

  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   None

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Goraow.
  • Obtain a Quadav Helm and trade it to Goraow to receive your reward.

Notes

  • This quest can be repeated by continuing to turn in Quadav Helms; zoning between each repeat is not required.

Game Description

Client: Goraow (Ore Street Stairs, Bastok Mines)
Summary:

Kill some Quadav to avenge Goraow's fallen comrades, and bring back a Quadav Helm as proof of your deeds.
