Walkthrough

  • Talk to Tehf Kimasnahya (F-8) for a cutscene.
    • She will ask for a Vial of Luminous Water (Key Item).
  • Talk to Ekhu Pesshyadha (H-6) for a cutscene.
  • Talk to Zabahf (F-8).
  • Talk to Ekhu Pesshyadha again for the Vial of Luminous Water (Key Item).
  • Talk to Tehf Kimasnahya again for a cutscene.
  • Walk through the archway on the ground floor in (J-10) next to Hajaom for a cutscene.
    • The cutscene mentions fresh Orc livers and venom dust (Cantarella). (This is simply a reference to what item they are talking about. You do not actually need this for the completion of the quest itself.)
  • Talk to Tehf Kimasnahya again for a cutscene.
  • Wait until Japanese Midnight. Then talk to Tehf Kimasnahya one last time to finish the quest. You need to zone in order to trigger the final cutscene with her.

Game Description

Client: Tehf Kimasnahya (Balrahn Way, Aht Urhgan Whitegate)
Summary:

Tehf Kimasnahya wants you to get a vial of luminous water from Ekhu Pesshyadha.
