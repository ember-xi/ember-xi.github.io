Moghancement: Light
Description:
Moogle-powered assistance from your Mog House. Reduces the chances of losing materials when synthesis attempts utilizing a light crystal have failed.
Obtained:
It is obtained through these Furnishings:
  • 2 - Light Lamp
  • 3 - Adventuring Certificate
  • 4 - Dresser
  • 5 - White Jar
  • 6 - Elvaan F Mannequin
  • 6 - Elvaan M Mannequin
  • 6 - Galka Mannequin
  • 6 - Hume F Mannequin
  • 6 - Hume M Mannequin
  • 6 - Mithra Mannequin
  • 6 - Tarutaru F Mannequin
  • 6 - Tarutaru M Mannequin
  • 9 - Crystal Rose
Notes:
This is helpful when you're leveling a craft and using a lot of Light Crystals. When you fail, you will have a greater chance of not losing your materials. The stronger this Moghancement is, the less you will lose overall. See Moghancements for more information.
