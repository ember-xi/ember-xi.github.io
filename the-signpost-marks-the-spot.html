The Signpost Marks the Spot

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Nbu Latteh - Bastok Markets (J-9)
  Requirements:
   Bastok Reputation 2
  Items Needed:   None.
  Title:   Treasure Scavenger
  Repeatable:   No
  Reward:   Linen Robe

  Previous Quest:   Mom, the Adventurer?
  Next Quest:   None.

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Nbu Latteh.
  • Go to Konschtat Highlands and select the signpost at G-4/G-5 to receive the Painting of a Windmill (key item).
  • Return to Bastok, and talk to Roh Latteh in Bastok Mines to complete the quest and receive Linen Robe reward.

Game Description

Client: Nbu Latteh (Gold Street, Bastok Markets)
Summary:

Find the key item buried under one of the signposts on the Konschtat Highlands and bring it to Nbu Latteh's house.
