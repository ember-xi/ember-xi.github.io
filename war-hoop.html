War Hoop

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-40 Dancer artifact throwing weapon.

Contents
  1. How to get it
  2. Use
  3. Sources

How to get it

Complete The Unfinished Waltz. The quest includes a spawned Hippogryph fight in Grauberg (S) and requires reading The Essence of Dance before returning to Laila.

Use

This is a throwing weapon, not the dagger for your melee weapon skills.
