Parliament of Patriarchs

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

The Parliament of Patriarchs is the governing body for Windurst. Very little has been revealed about this organization. What has been revealed:

  • They are separate from the Ministers of Magic and the Star Sibyl.
  • Once per year they chose a Warlock Warlord to be the military leader.
  • Rumor has it that the Egg Hunt Egg-stravaganza is a game that a Windurstian hermit designed to gain favor with the Parliament. It is unknown if this is true, but many aspects of the game are similar to the traits needed to be a member of the Parliament.
  • The Parliment are guarded by an elite team of Beastmasters known as the Patriarch Protectors, led by Nyumomo.
