Additional Information

  • The experience point bonus takes effect via the Enchantment.
  • The "maximum bonus" value given is only an approximate number. The last bonus to experience points will always be the full 50% even if it brings the total bonus above 2,000 points.
  • This item can be recharged by trading it to one of your nation's conquest guards (including outpost guards) for 200 CP per charge.
  • You may only recharge the ring once per conquest tally.
  • You cannot purchase it if you already possess a similar item, such as a Chariot Band or Empress Band.
  • Using an Emperor Band does not disrupt Invisible like using most other items.


Tips for getting more than one use per day

  • The reuse timer is reset when the last charge is consumed. The ring can then be recharged and is ready to use. (Note: If you recharge the ring while the last charge is still in effect, the timer acts normally)
  • The effect of the ring pauses when you log off but the reuse timer does not.
