Voidstorm

From Zenith XI Wiki

SCH 53 in the level 75 era · Dark weather · Enhancing magic.

Contents
  1. Learn the spell
  2. Use
  3. Sources

Learn the spell

  1. Buy the scroll from Layton in The Eldieme Necropolis (S) (J-8), or buy it from the Auction House.
  2. Use the scroll at the required Scholar level.

Use

Gives one party member a single-weather effect of the listed element. It can support matching elemental magic and weather-sensitive equipment. Accession can spread the buff to nearby party members.

Pair it with Noctohelix or Klimaform. Stormsurge adds an attribute bonus after purchasing the merit trait.

This is the historical learning level. Later retail lowered some Storm levels; follow the scroll requirement shown in Zenith if it differs.
