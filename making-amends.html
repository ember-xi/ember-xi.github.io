Walkthrough

  • Hakkuru-Rinkuru in the Orastery has found that the Minister's wand has snapped in two! He asks that you bring him a block of Animal Glue so he can try to patch it up.
  • Bring Hakkuru-Rinkuru a block of Animal Glue to fix the minister's wand. He will pay you hush money of 1,500 gil.
  • If you are on Windurst Mission 6-2, you need to complete the BC with the Yagudo before you can activate the quest.

Game Description

Client: Hakkuru-Rinkuru (Orastery, Port Windurst)
Summary:

Hakkuru-Rinkuru has snapped Minister Ajido-Marujido's favorite wand in two! He wants you to find some "animal glue" so he can temporarily stick the wand together until he can obtain a replacement.
