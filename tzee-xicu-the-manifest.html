Notes:

  • Title Obtained: Deity Debunker
  • Located around (H-6) on map 5.
  • Lottery pop of the Yagudo Avatar which spawns every 21-24 hours - so a 3-5 day spawn. However it can spawn as early as 2 days from previous kill or as late as 10 days.
  • When she engages she says: "You are not here as sacrifices, are you? Could you possibly be committing this affront in the face of a deity? Very well, I will personally mete out your divine punishment, Kyah!"
  • When she is defeated she says: "You have...bested me...However, I...am...a god...I will never die...never rot...never fade...never..."
  • Uses Astral Flow, Additional Effect: Paralyze.
  • Killable by 6 level 75 players. Recommended to have 10+ though.
  • The drop rate of Summoner's Testimony is 100%.
  • Will drop either Daylight Dagger or Astral Signa but never both with Astral Signa being a very low chance of dropping.
