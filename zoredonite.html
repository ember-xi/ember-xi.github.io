Zoredonite

From Zenith XI Wiki

Uragnite notorious monster encountered aboard the Manaclipper.

Contents
  1. Find and fight
  2. Drops
  3. Sources

Find and fight

  1. Board the Manaclipper and watch the deck during the voyage. This NM appears naturally; it is not fished up.
  2. It can appear on any route and attacks level-75 characters. Claim it before it disappears.
  3. Prepare poison removal for Gas Shell and Venom Shell. It can use Invincible and cast Flash and cures.

Drops

Possible rewards include Nebimonite Belt, Zoredonite Ring, Healing Mace, Kosshin, Seashell.
