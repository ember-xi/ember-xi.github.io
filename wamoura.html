Notes

  • After a certain amount of time left alone, Wamoura Princes will "evolve" into adult Wamoura in Mount Zhayolm.
  • Adult Wamoura can be Aspir-ed.
  • Links with both Wamoura Princes and other Wamoura.
  • Wamoura Princes in Mount Zhayolm can lose hate if you Gravity and run far enough away. Adult Wamoura do not.
  • All adult Wamoura are both true sight, true sound, and magic detectors.
