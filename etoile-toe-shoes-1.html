Etoile Toe Shoes -1

From Zenith XI Wiki

Damaged relic material used to upgrade Etoile Toe Shoes.

Contents
  1. How to get it
  2. Use
  3. Sources

How to get it

Drops from Nightmare enemies in Dynamis - Qufim. Use the zone guide for access and enemy details.

Use

Combine with Etoile Toe Shoes, Holy Leather and 30 Ordelle Bronzepiece at Sagheera for Etoile Toe Shoes +1. This damaged item cannot be equipped.
