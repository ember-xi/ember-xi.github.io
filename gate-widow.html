Notes:

  • To reach the Gate Widow, defeat the Mad Bomber on the First floor of Bhaflau Remnants.
  • When Mad Bomber is defeated (it must not Self-Destruct), a Dormant Rampart NPC will appear.
  • Examining the rampart will warp you to a room with a Reactionary Rampart that summons a chigoe or Gate Widow at regular intervals.
  • The Gate Widow is a rare summon from the Reactionary Rampart on the 1st floor of Bhaflau Remnants.
  • It is common speculation that; if one Gate Widow spawns, there is a greater chance for more to spawn off one Reactionary Rampart.
