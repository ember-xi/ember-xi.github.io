Walkthrough

  • Talk to Ulamaal (K-12) for a cutscene (you must wait until after JST midnight after completing Delivering the Goods)
  • Talk to Fochacha in the Square (I-9) for another cutscene.
  • Visit the Alchemy Guild (F-5) and get a cutscene with Fochacha's son, Topok-Hippok, who says he needs a Rainbow Berry.
  • Obtain a few Sickles (in case of breaks) and head out to Wajaom Woodlands. Locate a Harvesting Point.
    • Some Harvesting Points can be found at (E-8/9 and H-10); don't restrict your search to this location. You may want to take a chocobo.
    • >>Caution!<< You must drop Invisible to harvest. Take care around sight aggro mobs.
    • Harvest from the point until you get a Key Item: Rainbow Berry.
  • Return to Ulamaal or Qutiba for final cutscene and reward of one Imperial Silver Piece.
  • After JST midnight, talk to Qutiba to start A Taste of Honey quest.

Game Description

Client: Ulamaal, Qutiba (Shararat's Teahouse, Aht Urhgan Whitegate)
Summary:

The leader of the "Epicureans" has been missing ever since saying he was going to the port. Where could he have gone...?
