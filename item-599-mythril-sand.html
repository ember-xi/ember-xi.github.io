Statistics

Mythril sand
This mythril sand is obtained by refining gravel from the mines. The purity is too low for it to have any market value.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Other Uses

Used in Missions:

  • Windurst Mission 2-3: The Three Kingdoms
  • San d'Oria Mission 2-3: Journey Abroad


NPC Sell Price: ~62 gil

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Special

Obtained by trading Mine Gravel to the Refiner in Palborough Mines.

Zenith market and additional sources

Mission acquisition and use

Make it using Palborough Mines’ ore refiner, not a synthesis crystal: put Mine Gravel into the third-floor input at (I-6), operate the lever, then use the output lever on the floor below. On San d’Oria’s Bastok-first route, trade one sand to Savae E Paleade in Metalworks (I-9).

Used in: 2-3 — Journey Abroad

Auction House

This item cannot be listed at the Auction House. Use the acquisition sources above.
