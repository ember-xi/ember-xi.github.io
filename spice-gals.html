Walkthrough

  • Rouva is located at (I-8) in Southern San d'Oria. Talk to her to start the quest.
  • You can get Rivernewort (key item) from a ??? in either Riverne - Site #A01 or Riverne - Site #B01.
    • In Riverne - Site #A01 the ??? is found in (E-8) on the island that connects to Monarch Linn.
      • You will need two Giant Scales to get there.
    • In Riverne - Site #B01 the ??? is found in (E-7) near the Spacial Displacement heading south toward Monarch Linn.
      • You will need one Giant Scale to get there.
  • Once you have the Rivernewort, return to Rouva for your reward.

Game Description

Client: Rouva (Victory Square, Southern San d'Oria)
Summary:

Rouva needs a sprig of the rare Rivernewort to spruce up her succulent suppers.
