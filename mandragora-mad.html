Walkthrough

  • Yoran-Oran needs at least one of the following:
  • Four-Leaf Mandragora Bud - Drops from Mandragora in Sarutabaruta and Pygmaioi in Tahrongi Canyon. Reward is 120 Gil.
  • Cornette - Drops from Mandragora in Sarutabaruta and Pygmaioi in Tahrongi Canyon. Reward is 200 Gil.
  • Yuhtunga Sulfur - Drops from Mandragora in Sarutabaruta and Pygmaioi in Tahrongi Canyon. Reward is 250 Gil.
  • Three-Leaf Mandragora Bud - Drops from Mandragora near Kazham. Reward is 1,200 gil
  • Snobby Letter - Drops from Mourioche in Boyahda Tree. Reward is 5,500 Gil.

Game Description

Client: Yoran-Oran (Manor, Windurst Walls near Heavens Tower)
Summary:

Yoran-Oran wants you to bring him unusual items that mandragoras drop, to help him in his research on the derivations of the species.
