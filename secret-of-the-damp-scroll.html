Walkthrough

  • First you need to acquire a Damp Scroll. This may be acquired one of two ways:
    • Fishing it up in the Sea Serpent Grotto; or
    • Buying it at the Auction House under Other -> Miscellaneous
  • With the Damp Scroll in your inventory, go and talk to Shivivi; she can be found wandering around (J-8), in Norg. Shivivi will tell you that the scroll is some "new fangled Ninjutsu" they made, but it needs to be dipped in the spring at Horlais Peak to be readable.
  • Head to Yughott Grotto and take the exit at (J-9) to Horlais Peak. Trade the scroll to the Hot Spring in the middle of the spring for your reward of a scroll of Jubaku: Ichi.
    • The quickest route is heading into Yughott Grotto from Ghelsba Outpost , exiting to Fort Ghelsba #3 then heading through Fort Ghelsba to Yughott Grotto #4.

Game Description

Client: Shivivi (Norg)
Summary:

Shivivi tells you to go to Horlais Peak to clear up a scroll and make it more readable.
