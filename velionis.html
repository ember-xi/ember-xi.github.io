Notes:

  • Spawned by trading Golden Teeth to a ??? at (F-11) in Arrapago Reef on the 1st map.
  • The ??? Respawns approximately 5 minutes after Velionis has been defeated.
  • Does not aggro.
  • The shortest route to the trigger point is through the Azouph Isle Staging Point, entering Arrapago Reef, and using the Lamian Fang Key to get to F-11.
  • The Survival Guide warp to Arrapago Reef also works, placing you at Map 1 (H-11). This works especially well when hunting Lil' Apkallu at the same time, as then only one Lamian Fang Key is needed to access both spawn points.
  • Does not possess any standard attacks. Will only cast Tier III and Tier II -ga Elemental Magic.
  • Uses standard skeleton TP moves, including Malediction (300-400 AoE Drain).
  • Immune to Silence and all forms of Sleep. Verification Needed
  • Possesses an innate, undispel-able Blaze Spikes effect that deal high damage (180-230 damage per swing). Appears to activate only when not casting a spell.
  • Possesses a potent Fast Cast effect that increases as its HP declines. At 10%, it becomes a near-Chainspell effect.
  • Will stop to cast every couple seconds, regardless of if anyone is in range or not.
  • Easily kited due to spellcasting.
