Reprisal

Increases chance of blocking with shield, and reflects portion of blocked damage back to attacker.

Notes

  • With Koenig Shield and capped enhancing, the effect seems to reflect around 55% damage.
  • Calculated before Phalanx, Stoneskin, Sentinel and Invincible. (Will reflect damage appropriate to the amount you would have taken without them.)
  • Does not overwrite spike spells.
  • Is overwritten by spike spells.
  • Bigger shields reflect more damage. Damage reflected appears to be 1/3 of the amount by which damage was reduced; further testing is still needed to corroborate.
  • Does not reflect damage from blocking physical-type TP moves.
  • The damage is considered Light-elemental magic damage.
    • Tested on Gurfurlur the Menacing, the damage healed him instead of hurting while magic ward is up.
