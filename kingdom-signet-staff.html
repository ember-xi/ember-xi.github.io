Enchantment: "Signet"

  • The Signet effect gained from this enchantment will overwrite Sanction.
  • The enchantment can only grant Signet to San d'Orian characters.
  • The enchantment can only be used in areas subject to Conquest.
  • The enchantment can only be used on characters in the caster's party.
  • Once every charge is used, it's not possible to recharge them. You will have to buy another.
