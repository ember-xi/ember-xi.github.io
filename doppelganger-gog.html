Notes:

  • He spawns in 3 different locations: J-6, I-7, or G-7. This is in the Lilac Tower which is at H-3 in East Sarutabaruta.
  • He spawns approximately every 8 minutes.
  • When it's time for him to spawn, either him, Doppelganger Dio, Bomb King, or a measly Balloon will pop. So it's random. Balloon will pop more frequently.
  • Killable solo at level 33.
  • Killed soloing RDM/BLM at level 31(with appropriate gear and food).
  • Killed solo as 31THF/15WAR(needed to use Perfect Dodge).
  • Killed solo as 30WAR/15THF (Started with Mighty Strikes).
  • Has aproximately 800 hit points. WARNING! may cast Dimensional Death for a hit 100+ HP.
