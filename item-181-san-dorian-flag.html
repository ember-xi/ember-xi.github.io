Furnishing:

The twin gryphons of house d'Oraguille
are emblazoned at the center.

Other Uses

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Also cannot be placed into Storage.

  • San d'Oria Mission 9-2: The Heir to the Light

Zenith market and additional sources

Mission acquisition and use

Speak to Halver after completing all of The Heir to the Light’s final scenes, including the Heavy Stone Door in King Ranperre’s Tomb. If your inventory was full, free a slot and speak to Halver again. This is the nation-story furnishing reward, not a monster drop.

Used in: 9-2 — The Heir to the Light
