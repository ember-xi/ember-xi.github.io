San d'Oria Conquest Points Items

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

All of these items are available from Temple Knights in San d'Oria:

  • Arpevion, T.K.
  • Aravoge, T.K.
  • Achantere, T.K.

as well as the Jeunoan Conquest Guards:

  • Morlepiche
  • Emitt
  • Alrauverat
  • Kochahy-Muwachahy

Items that are available when San d'Oria is in any Conquest Ranking are available from Temple Knights in Foreign nations - items available only when San d'Oria is in 1st or 2nd are not available from Temple Knights in Foreign nations:

  • Panoquieur, T.K.
  • Glarociquet, T.K.

Items that are available only during 1st or 2nd Conquest Ranking are indicated with a "1st" or "1st, 2nd" next to their name.
