New Player Guide to Milestones

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is a simple guide aimed at newer FFXI players, to see at which levels they can expect to hit the typical milestones. I did not write this guide and i take no credit for it, i have just transfered it over to this wiki. I apologize to the author that i do not know your name. If you would like to be acredited for this guide, please reach out to me and i will do so.

Level Milestone Requirements Notes
10+ Outpost Teleportation Outpost Teleportation Guide Will be reliant on your nation's conquest results.
18 Unlock Subjub Elder Memories If you collect a set of all six SJ items (both quests) afterwards, that will help unlock Aht Urhgan later
20+ Gain access to Kazham Kazham Airship Pass You can solo keys 25+ in some cases
20+ Ability to ride Chocobos Chocobo's Wounds Era accurate waiting time
30+ Access to Aht Urhgan areas/ToAU Missions The Road to Aht Urhgan Requires all 6 sub-job quest items
30+ Start Chains of Promathia Missions The Rites of Life Just zone into Delkfutt's Tower after reaching Rank 2
30+ Unlock Advanced Jobs Jobs
40+ Ability to use Airships to and from Jeuno Airship Pass You must clear the Rank 5 battle for your home nation.
40+ Start artifact armor quests Category:Artifact Armor Quest varies by job
50+ Open Gates of Halvung in Mount Zhayolm Cast Metal Plate You will need Sneak + Invisible for the trip
50+ Access to Toraimarai Canal Food for Thought This is for non-Windurstian players. It is a long quest chain starting with this quest
50+ Faster travel to and from Aht Urhgan zones Staging Point acquisition Need to complete The Road to Aht Urhgan first.
50+ Ability to warp back to Aht Urhgan for 300gil from home nation Lure of the Wildcat Do the quest that matches you nation. Need to complete The Road to Aht Urhgan first.
50+ Access to Den of Rancor from within Temple of Uggalepih Paintbrush of Souls
50 Raises level cap to 55 In Defiant Challenge
51+ Raises level cap to 60 Atop the Highest Mountains
56+ Raises level cap to 65 Whence Blows the Wind
60+ Start Zilart Missions The New Frontier After attaining nation Rank 6 you can start these missions. Most can be done with 3-4 players at 75
60+ Obtain a Moongate Pass to delve deeper into Ro'Maeve Moongate Pass Quest
61+ Raises level cap to 70 Riding on the Clouds
65+ Unlock Dynamis access The Shadow Lord After completing this mission, simply zone into Xarcabard to flag Dynamis
66+ Raises level cap to 75 Shattering Stars
71 Unlock Final Weapon Skill Varies by Job Check wiki on how to obtain your "Trial" weapon
