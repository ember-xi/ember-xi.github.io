Enhances "Maneuver" effect'

  • Increases base stats on automaton for each a maneuver active:
  • Fire Maneuver: STR
  • Ice Maneuver: INT
  • Wind Maneuver: AGI
  • Earth Maneuver: VIT
  • Thunder Maneuver: DEX
  • Water Maneuver: MND
  • Light Maneuver: CHR
  • Dark Maneuver: --
  • Maneuvers typically give +1 stat per maneuver. Puppetry Dastanas increase this to +2 per maneuver.


For example:
1x Fire Maneuver will add 2 STR
2x Fire Maneuver will add 4 STR
3x Fire Maneuver will add 6 STR.

How to Obtain

  • After flagging the Puppetmaster Blues quest, speak with Dhima Polevhia in Whitegate, (J-8)
  • She will ask for the following materials:
    • Rainbow Thread
    • Wamoura Cloth
    • Marid Leather
    • Platinum Sheet
  • After you trade those items to he, she will ask for 1 Imperial Mythril Piece.
  • Wait until JST midnight, and talk to her again to receive your reward.
