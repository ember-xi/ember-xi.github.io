Zenith availability

The route has been checked against references and available code; completion on Zenith has not yet been play-tested.

Before you begin

  • Complete AMK 5.
  • At least one orb key item; all four strongly recommended.
  • 5,000 gil for Shantotto.

Starting point

??? · Outer Horutoto Ruins

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Farm Cardians for the four orb key items: Swords, Cups, Batons and Coins. Dahlia Tower, entered at West Sarutabaruta F-11, contains all four families.
  2. Collecting all four on the initiating character removes the damage-type restrictions and reduces the encounter to five Custom Cardians. Each character needing credit should bring at least one orb.
  3. Enter the separate Outer Horutoto section from East Sarutabaruta H-3. Examine the ??? on the Ancient Magical Gizmo opposite the entrance to start the confrontation.
  4. Defeat every Custom Cardian without leaving the encounter area. The orbs are consumed; victory grants the Ripe starfruit.
  5. Return to Shantotto in Windurst Walls K-7 and agree to pay 5,000 gil. Finish her follow-up dialogue, then examine the Inconspicuous Door in Upper Jeuno H-8 to advance and receive the treasure-search destination.

Battle and progress notes

  • The reviewed code and wiki disagree on the Cups/Batons damage mapping; gathering all four avoids relying on that disputed mapping.

Completion and reward

Ripe starfruit and Peach coral key.
