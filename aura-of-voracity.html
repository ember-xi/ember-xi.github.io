Other Uses

NPC Sell Price: ???~??? gil

Trade an Aura of Voracity to Meret in Tavnazian Safehold to receive a Novia Earring. You must complete her friend Yurim's quest, In the Name of Science, before Meret will accept any trades.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
