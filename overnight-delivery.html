Walkthrough

  • Talk to Kenapa-Keppa in the Rhinostery until he gives you this quest. You must talk to him after 7:00 Vana'diel time. (if you just completed the Food for Thought quest, you will have to zone before receiving this quest).
  • Go to Mhaura and look for Kotan-Purutan. He appears near the ticket booth at (I-9).
  • Only during night hours, he will give you a Small Bag (key item) to take back. You then have to make it back to Kenapa-Keppa before dawn (7:00).
  • Talk to Kenapa again for your reward.
    • Note: This quest is much quicker with access to a Teleport-Mea.

Game Description

Client: Kenapa-Keppa (Rhinostery, Windurst Waters)
Summary:

You couldn't quite make out what Kenapa-Keppa was trying to say, but you guess he wants you to go pick up something from Mhaura. This doesn't look like your ordinary delivery job...?
