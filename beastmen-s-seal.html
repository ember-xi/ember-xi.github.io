Statistics

Beastmen's Seal
The beastmen use this seal as
a checkpoint pass. It is used by
pressing its surface onto a piece
of clay.

Stackable: 99

Other Uses

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

Used for: BCNMs

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Dropped From

  • There is a chance a Beastmen's Seal will drop from any mob that still gives you experience points (i.e. Easy Prey or higher), with around ~5-10% of one dropping.
  • Beastmen Treasures Quest
  • BCNM (varies)
