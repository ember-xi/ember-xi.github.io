Walkthrough

  • Helbort needs you to make a delivery for him.
  • Take the Weapons Order to Alexius (I-6) (by the lake) in Jugner Forest who will give you a Weapons Receipt.
  • Return for your reward.

Game Description

Client: Helbort (Helbort's Blades, Southern San d'Oria)
Summary:

Find the traveling merchant Alexius in Jugner Forest and place an order for some weapons.
