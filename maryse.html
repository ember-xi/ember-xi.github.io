Maryse

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Harvest Festival Witch NPC

Location: Patrols set route in Northern San d'Oria (E-5 > F-5 > E-6 > F-6) through Labourman's Way.

Notes

One of The Dark Lilies of the Harvest Festival event held each October since 2004. Follow Maryse without being seen to receive, from the Exorcist, a Witch Hat or Coven Hat.

Involved in Events

  • Harvest Festival 2005-present
