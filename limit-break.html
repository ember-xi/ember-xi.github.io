Quest progression

Level limitOriginal questObjective
50 → 55In Defiant ChallengeTrade Exoray Mold, Bomb Coal and Ancient Papyrus to Maat.
55 → 60Atop the Highest MountainsCollect the three Frigicite key items and return to Maat.
60 → 65Whence Blows the WindObtain the Orcish, Quadav and Yagudo Crests and return to Maat.
65 → 70Riding on the CloudsFollow Maat’s clues and trade Kindred’s Seals to the assigned NPCs.
70 → 75Shattering StarsFollow the original Testimony and battlefield quest, then return after victory.

Starting NPC

Maat · Ru’Lude Gardens (H-5). Native eligibility and dialogue determine the quest offered. Expansion jobs retain their own final-trial NPCs.

First quest assistance

First Limit Break hunting guide. Original drops remain. A missing item is granted after the third eligible kill of its reviewed monster type, once.

Original final battle

The Testimony requirement and original battlefield entry checks are restored. No custom Accept final trial, Travel to trial or Unlock level 75 buttons.

Existing progress

Previously unlocked limits, completed quests and earned items remain. Future increases use original quests; no automatic increases.

Additional level-75 reference

Limit Break Quests

  • LB1 or Genkai 1/In Defiant Challenge raises level cap from 50 to 55.
  • LB2 or Genkai 2/Atop the Highest Mountains raises level cap from 55 to 60.
  • LB3 or Genkai 3/Whence Blows the Wind raises level cap from 60 to 65.
  • LB4 or Genkai 4/Riding on the Clouds raises level cap from 65 to 70.
  • LB5 or Genkai 5/Shattering Stars raises level cap from 70 to 75.
