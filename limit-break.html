Limit Break

Limit Break
NPCMaat — Ru'Lude Gardens (H-5)
Levels50 → 55 → 60 → 65 → 70 → 75
QuestsLB1 In Defiant Challenge · LB2 Atop the Highest Mountains · LB3 Whence Blows the Wind · LB4 Riding on the Clouds · LB5 Shattering Stars
EmberXIAll item and key-item hunts are removed: Maat gives them to you on the spot. The Maat fight at 70 is retail.
TrustsAllowed in the Maat fight; subjob allowed; 30 minutes

On EmberXI the five Limit Break quests keep their retail structure, but Maat hands you every item and key item himself. You only travel to him and, at 70, fight him.

How it works

StepCapQuestWhat happens on EmberXI
150 → 55In Defiant ChallengeTalk to Maat at level 50. The Clump of Exoray Mold, Chunk of Bomb Coal and Piece of Ancient Papyrus appear in your bag. Trade them to Maat.
255 → 60Atop the Highest MountainsTalk to Maat. You receive the three Frigicite key items. Talk to him again.
360 → 65Whence Blows the WindTalk to Maat. You receive the Orcish, Quadav and Yagudo Crests. Talk to him again.
465 → 70Riding on the CloudsTalk to Maat. You receive the Scowling, Smiling, Spirited and Somber Stones. Talk to him again.
570 → 75Shattering StarsTalk to Maat. Your job's Testimony appears in your bag. Trade it to Maat and enter the Battlefield for your job (retail fight). Win = cap 75 + Scroll of Instant Warp.
Mechanics: The Maat fight is the only real test. Bring your five Trusts; the fight allows a subjob and lasts 30 minutes. If you lose, the Testimony is given again.
