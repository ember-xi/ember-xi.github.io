Blessed Briault Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A set of armor crafted from various expensive cloths and threads. The Blessed set, notable for its white and blue coloring, is wearable by White Mages only, and provides large enhancements to MND, MP, and also provides large Haste bonuses.

Number of Pieces: 4

Cost to store: Cannot store

This set has a non-storable High Quality Version.
