Additional Effect

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

The term "Additional Effect" is placed on certain attributes of a Weapon, Armor, or Ammunition that have a percentage chance of activating, usually while in combat. The frequency of the Additional Effect's activation, or proc rate, is based on many factors, the most common of which are the weapon and the level of the mob being attacked. However, it is generally not a good idea to rely on the Additional Effect of a weapon, as even the best non-relic Additional Effects proc fairly infrequently.

Legend

  1. Audited - Eden development team has investigated and determined the status of the following three definitions.
  2. Implemented - Eden development team indicates code is present, but may not be working correctly.
  3. Verified - Eden development team has verified the functionality and adjusted to be ToAU-era accurate. However, that does not mean further evidence will not be considered.
  4. Released - Functionality is live on Eden server. Should players find issue, report in GitHub please.

  • ? = unknown
