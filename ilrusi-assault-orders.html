Ilrusi Assault Orders

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Key Item Description
A document that authorizes you to take part in an Ilrusi Atoll Assault mission.
Obtained from Quest/Mission
Obtained when you accept one of the Assault Missions for Ilrusi Atoll:
  1. Golden Salvage
  2. Lamia No.13
  3. Extermination
  4. Demolition Duty
  5. Searat Salvation
