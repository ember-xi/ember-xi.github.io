Before you begin

  • Her Memories is the current Wings of the Goddess mission.
  • The Windurst past-allegiance branch of Her Memories.

Starting point

Miah Riyuh · Windurst Waters [S] · H-9

Era note: September 2010 WotG memory chapter, released after the original level-75 cap.

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Windurst Waters [S] from West Sarutabaruta [S] for a scene, then speak with Miah Riyuh at (H-9) on the northern map.
  2. Visit the three towers in West Sarutabaruta [S], at (J-8), (F-11), and (F-4). Examine each Sealed Entrance.
  3. Return to Miah Riyuh for the fragment.

Completion and reward

Large memory fragment IV.
