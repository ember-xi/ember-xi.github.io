Arrapago Islands

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Located to the north of Al Zahbi, the Arrapago Islands is a mist-shrouded archipelago dominated by marshlands and treacherous reefs.

Staging Points

  • The Azouph Isle Staging Point is located at (K-9) in Caedarva Mire (first map).
  • The Dvucca Isle Staging Point is located at (I-9) in Caedarva Mire (second map).
  • The Ilrusi Atoll Staging Point is located at (G-5) in Arrapago Reef (second map).
