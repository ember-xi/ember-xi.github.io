Sicklemoon Salt

From Zenith XI Wiki

A temporary key item for reaching the Tinnin battle island.

Obtain and use

  1. Ask Ryo about islets in Aht Urhgan Whitegate E-8.
  2. Buy Sicklemoon Salt from Sanraku for 500 Zeni under the classic rules. Each participant needs their own key item.
  3. Examine the Engraved Tablet in Wajaom Woodlands at H-13 to travel to the island.
  4. Gather the party before spawning Tinnin. The salt grants access; the boss still requires its separate trigger. See Zeni Notorious Monsters.
