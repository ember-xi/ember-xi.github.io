Ferry

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

The seas and waterways of Vana'diel are patrolled by various ships and barges, carrying adventurers and cargo from port to port. These ferries can provide alternative transport to areas that are normally difficult to get to. Whether you're headed to the beaches of Purgonorgo Isle or the docks of Nashmau, these ferries provide adventurers with the thrills that only a watercraft can provide. Shipping off by ferry is a unique opportunity to try out your fishing skills, or hope to chance upon a Notorious Monster. Some of these ships even have Fishing Guild representatives on board, to aid in your angling endeavors.
