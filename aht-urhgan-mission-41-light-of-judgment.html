Walkthrough

  • Speak to Rodin-Comidin at (I-7) (upper level) of Aht Urhgan Whitegate.
  • After you obtain the Nyzul Isle Route key item, head to Nyzul Isle Staging Point.

Notes

  • It is possible to sign up for a Nyzul Isle Assault mission to get to Nyzul Isle Staging Point without having to purchase a Runic Portal Use Permit.

Game Description

The Endymion has been blasted out of the sky by an intense beam of light... However, the mission must continue. Make your way to Aht Urhgan Whitegate and rendezvous with Rodin-Comidin.
