Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Additional effect: Slow. Duration of effect varies with TP.

Description

Spell cost: 6 MP
Ecosystem: Plantoids
Type: Physical (Blunt)
Blue Magic Points: 2
Stat Bonus: MND+1
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 4

Timers

Casting Time: .5 seconds
Recast Time: 7.25 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Sprout Smack as part of a combo to create Beast Killer.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Beast Killer combo.

  • Wild Oats
  • 1000 Needles

Notes

  • Blunt damage.
  • Secondary modifiers: VIT: 30%.
  • Additional effect of slow is approximately 15%.
  • Slow effect is not guaranteed even if the spell connects.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Skillchain Element(s): Water (can open Induration or Impaction; can close Reverberation or Fragmentation)
