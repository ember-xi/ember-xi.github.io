Before you begin

  • Complete CoP 7-2: Flames in the Darkness.

Starting point

Shaft Entrance · Mine Shaft #2716

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Reach Mine Shaft #2716. One established route is to trade a Snow Lily to Tarnotik in Oldton Movalpolos (K-10); eligibility begins during Three Paths, which you have already cleared.
  2. Examine the Shaft Entrance for the mission scene.
  3. Speak to Cid in the Metalworks (H-8). Wait for the next Vana'diel date, then speak to him again to complete the airship repairs and advance.

Battle and progress notes

  • The wait is a Vana'diel day change, not a real-world midnight. Snow Lily is an optional travel expense, not a mission turn-in.

Title: Prishe's Buddy; next mission.

Walkthrough

  • Head to Mine Shaft #2716 and check the Shaft Entrance for a cutscene.
  • Note: From North Gustaberg K-6, zone into Oldton Movalpolos E-13 where you will find Twinkbrix. Trade him a Snow Lily or play his dice game to earn a warp into Mine Shaft #2716.

(To play the dice game, you can trade him 1-10,000 gil. He will roll two 50-sided dice that must add up to a number between 2 and X, which is determined by how much gil you traded him. Once you win, trade him 2,000 gil)

  • Head to Metalworks and talk to Cid at H-8 for a cutscene.
  • Wait one Vana'diel day and talk to Cid again for a second cutscene.

Game Description

Mission Orders
Travel to the Moblin city of Movalpolos to find a path to the fifth and final mothercrystal.
