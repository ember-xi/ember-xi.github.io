Zunari Kabuto Plus 1

From Zenith XI Wiki

High-quality level-44 Samurai head armor with DEF 16.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Purchase
  3. Sources

How to obtain

High-quality result of the Zunari Kabuto recipe: Smithing 69 and Clothcraft 34; Fire Crystal + 2 Iron Sheets + 1 Copper Ingot + 1 Silk Thread + 1 Silk Cloth.

Purchase

Auction House → Armor → Head. The item’s displayed name is Zunari Kabuto +1.
