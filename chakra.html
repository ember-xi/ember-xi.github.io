Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Cures certain status effects and restores a small amount of HP to user.
  • Obtained: Monk Level 35
  • Recast Time: 5:00
  • Duration: Instant

Notes/Description

  • Restores an amount of HP to user equal to twice the character's VIT.
  • If the Temple Cyclas is equipped, this ability will restore an amount of HP to the user equal to three times the character's VIT.
  • If the Melee Gloves are equipped in addition to the Temple Cyclas, this ability will restore an amount of HP equal to 3.6 times the character's VIT.
  • Cures the user of poison and blind.
  • If the Temple Cyclas is equipped, this ability will also cure paralyze if successfully used.
  • If the Melee Gloves are equipped, this ability will also cure disease.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Chakra" <me>
