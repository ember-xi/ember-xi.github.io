Notes:

  • Spawned by trading a Goblin Drink to the Scrawled Writing, which spawns randomly at either (I-9), (I-11), or (G-13) in Oldton Movalpolos. The Scrawled Writing will respawn 15 minutes after Goblin Wolfman dies.
  • Has approximately 7,600 HP.
  • Uses Blood Weapon, and casts Stun (in one minute intervals), Drain, Aspir, and Absorb spells.
  • Attack speed and damage increase during Blood Weapon.
  • Hits hard, but has poor accuracy against most evasion-geared jobs and pets.
  • Susceptible to Bind, Gravity, Paralyze, Slow, and Silence.
  • Highly resistant or immune to Sleep, however Lullaby will stick.
  • Can be defeated by a party of six level 60 characters or two level 70 characters; Soloable by most jobs at level 75 and by Red Mage at level 60 (using solo kiting strategy).
  • Parade Gorget has a drop rate of roughly 25% to 30%.
