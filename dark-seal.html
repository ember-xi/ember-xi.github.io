Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Enhances the accuracy of your next dark magic spell.
  • Obtained: Dark Knight Level 75
  • Recast Time: 15 minutes - 2.5 minutes for every merit beyond the 1st
  • Duration: One spell

Notes/Description

  • This Job Ability only affects the resistance.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Dark Seal" <me>
