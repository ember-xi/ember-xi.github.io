Walkthrough

To Start Mission:

  • Head back to your hometown and rush to the San d'Orian court in the Chateau d'Oraguille for a cutscene.
  • After you arrive at the castle you will get a cutscene, but it's not over yet: you must speak with Halver before you can continue this mission. After speaking to Halver, he will award you the Key Item: New Fei'Yin Seal.
  • Note - this quest cannot be completed without the New Fei'Yin Seal.
  • Form a level 50 party. You may use anyone from any nation currently on Mission 5-1, as it is the same for all 3 nations.
    Everyone will need level 50 gear, as this is capped at level 50.
  • The location of Fei'Yin is in the northeast corner of Beaucedine Glacier. Upon entering Fei'Yin your party will encounter another cutscene involving Zeid.
  • Upon entering, head east towards (K-8). At (K-8) you will see 2 dolls. There is a cermet gate on the east wall. Click on this gate and you will zone into Qu'Bia Arena. While in the waiting area, prep up for the fight.
  • The majority of the mobs in Fei'Yin detect by sound, but there are several mobs that aggro to magic.
  • The boss is the Archlich Taber'quoan. Archlich Taber'quoan will spawn along with 2 Ancient Sorcerers. Ancient Warriors will also spawn from the hallway and enter the battelfield as the battle continues.
    • Concentrate on the Archlich and once he is dead you are done.
    • There is a level cap of 50 for the boss and only one party can enter at a time.
    • The Ancient Warriors only have 100 HP and can be taken out very easily w/ an AoE WS or having a BLM one shot them.
    • The Ancient Warriors will continue to spawn during the entire fight whether killed or not.
    • Archlich Taber'quoan is a BLM type skeleton with Sleepga II and Freeze, and will use the 2hr ability Manafont.
  • After defeating Archlich, a cutscene will take place. At the end of the cutscene, you will receive the Key Item Burnt Seal. Report back to Halver.
