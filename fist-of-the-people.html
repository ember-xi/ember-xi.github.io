Fist of the People

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Start NPC: Talhaal - Al Zahbi
Requirements:
 Treasures of Aht Urhgan
Ode to the Serpents started
Zazarg present in Al Zahbi
Items Needed: Biyaada's Letter
Rusty Medal
Title: Stoneserpent Shocktrooper
Repeatable: No
Reward: 500 Imperial Standing

Subquest of: Ode to the Serpents

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Talhaal in Al Zahbi at (H-6). He will tell you to meet him at the Shararat Teahouse later.
  2. Head back the Shararat Teahouse in Aht Urhgan Whitegate and talk to Fari-Wari again. Talhaal will show up to tell you the story of Zazarg. For the rest of his story he tells you to find the general himself.
  3. Obtain a Rusty Medal from a Wajaom Tiger in Wajaom Woodlands or Bhaflau Thickets.
    Note: The Rusty Medal can also be purchased at the Auction House under Auction House Category: Others > Misc.
  4. Trade it to the Leypoint in Wajaom Woodlands at (G-8) for the final Cutscene.

Note: Stoneserpent General Zazarg must be in the Commoner's Ward/Al Zahbi (G-7) for this quest to be accessible or completable. If he is being held prisoner talking to Talhaal will not lead to a cutscene.

Game Description

Client:

Talhaal - Al Zahbi

Summary:

Talhaal has agreed to tell you about the Stoneserpent General's deeds. Wait for him at the Shararat Teahouse.
