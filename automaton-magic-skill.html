Automaton Magic Skill

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This chart displays the earliest levels at which a Puppetmaster's automaton is capable of casting a spell. The column on the left shows the automaton magic skill required before the puppet can cast the spell. The columns on the right show the earliest level the magic skill is attainable, given various head/frame combinations. Spell availability is limited by the equipped head's casting AI as well, which is detailed in the spell availability sections of: Valoredge Head, Sharpshot Head, Harlequin Head, Stormwaker Head, Soulsoother Head and Spiritreaver Head.

Spell list is not up-to-date with the August 2007 update yet, and likely contains inconsistencies.
