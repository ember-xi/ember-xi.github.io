San d'Oria Mission 1-1
MissionSmash the Orcish Scouts
TypeRepeatable
Start NPCGate Guard (Endracion or Ambrotien in Southern San d'Oria; Grilau in Northern San d'Oria)
LocationGhelsba Outpost / West Ronfaure
RequirementsRank 1
Items NeededOrcish Axe
Rewards
150 Rank points

Mission 1-1 of the San d'Oria storyline.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to any Gate Guard and accept Smash the Orcish Scouts.
  2. Kill Orcs in West Ronfaure or Ghelsba Outpost until an Orcish Axe drops (normal drop, not 100%).
  3. Trade the Orcish Axe to any Gate Guard.
Mechanics: Orcs are level 3-8. Play the first 3 levels without Trusts, then call three (Kupipi + Trion + Shantotto).
