Zenith availability

The native instance and seven-kill victory condition are implemented. Its source flags a remaining issue: players are not locked safely during the entry cutscene and can aggro the nearby enemies. This is a known implementation issue, not proof of a successful live run.

Before you begin

  • Complete B-02: Fires of Discontent.
  • Complete WotG 2: Back to the Beginning; Cait Sith should be the current mission.
  • Bring 30 Goblin Chocolates or 5,000 gil. Every entrant needs a Mine Shaft Key.

Starting point

Gentle Tiger · Bastok Markets [S] · H-6

Walkthrough

  1. Speak with Gentle Tiger at H-6, Pagdako at H-9, then Blatherix at F-8 in Bastok Markets [S].
  2. Trade Blatherix exactly 30 Goblin Chocolates or 5,000 gil to receive the Mine Shaft Key.
  3. Enter Pashhow Marshlands [S] from Grauberg [S] for the required scene. Entering from another border does not substitute for this step.
  4. Examine the Corroded Door at F-5 in Pashhow Marshlands [S] and select Light in the Darkness to enter Ruhotz Silvermines.
  5. Defeat any seven of the ten Quadav. They begin close to the party; move clear before preparing, then pull manageable groups. Victory triggers once seven are defeated.
  6. After the exit scene, return to Gentle Tiger in Bastok Markets [S] for the Adaman Ingot.

Battle and progress notes

  • The native instance spawns nine Sapphirine Quadav and one Sapphire Quadav. The latter can use Benediction; killing it disperses the others temporarily.
  • A failed attempt can be retried with a replacement key from Blatherix for 10 Goblin Chocolates or 1,000 gil.
  • The initial next-day wait is a TODO in this quest’s source, so this guide does not require a real-world midnight wait. Completion sets the next quest’s game-day and zoning checks.

Completion and reward

Adaman Ingot.

What this opens

  • B-04: Burden of Suspicion, after the next Vana’diel day and a zone change
