Walkthrough

  • Obtain a Pickaxe and Mythril Pick.
  • Travel to Mount Zhayolm by way of Runic Portal or Halvung:
    • Chamber of Passage: Must have previously used the Runic Portal at the Halvung Staging Point and have a Runic Portal Use Permit.
    • via Bhaflau Thickets (F-8) to Halvung (N-5).
    • via Wajaom Woodlands (C-8) to Halvung (I-13).
      • The quickest way out of Halvung is to use the Escape spell which takes you to Mount Zhayolm (L-7).
      • Or you may travel to Halvung (J-7) and zone to Mount Zhayolm (L-7).
  • Find what looks like a manhole cover in east (L-7) of Mount Zhayolm. (NOTE: You should not need Sneak or Invisible in this immediate area.)
    • There is an unnamed targetable point on this object.
    • Check it for a cutscene with a Zurtermor (Troll) and Warsowok (Moblin) that starts the quest.
    • Trade a Pickaxe to the unnamed point for another cutscene.
    • Zone (L-7) into Halvung (just a short distance to the south)
    • Zone back to Mount Zhayolm and trade the Mythril Pick to the unnamed point for a final cutscene and reward.

NOTE: The Mythril Pick is used up by the quest, but you keep the Pickaxe.
