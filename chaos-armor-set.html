Chaos Armor Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

All races in Dark Knight Artifact Armor]] An image well-suited to the hellish glory of the Dark Knight, indeed; this is a suit of ornate black plate armor with distinctive demon motifs and an intimidating silhouette. Its baroque design lessens its protective practicality, but the Dark Knight is served well by its finely-crafted structure and weight distribution, allowing for maximum offensive potential. For a fee you can store the set at any Armor Storer.
