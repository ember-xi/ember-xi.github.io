Imperial Silk Cloth

From Zenith XI Wiki

Crafted cloth used by Matthias to make the Dancer’s Tiara.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Dancer use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Buy from the Auction House under Materials → Clothcraft, or craft it. The recipe produces one cloth:

  • Clothcraft 59.
  • Earth Crystal.
  • One Silk Thread, one Gold Thread and one Wamoura Silk.

Dancer use

Bring one cloth with the other listed materials to Matthias in Bastok Markets (J-9) after unlocking his artifact service. See Dancer's Tiara for the complete commission.
