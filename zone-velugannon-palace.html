Additional level-75 reference


Part of the floating island of Tu'lia, constructed ten thousand years ago by the Zilart as the "Gate of the Gods" this is said to be the very entrance to paradise itself. While the Shrine of Ru'Avitau makes up the centre of the island, Ve'Lugannon Palace comprises the interior of the areas that that make up the outer reaches of Tu'lia.
