Promotion: Lance Corporal

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Start NPC: Abquhbah - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-10) upstairs
Requirements: Acquire 25 Mercenary Rank points as Superior Private.
Items Needed:
Title: Lance Corporal
Repeatable: No
Reward: LC Wildcat Badge

Previous Quest: Promotion: Superior Private
Next Quest: Promotion: Corporal

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Abquhbah for a cutscene that starts the quest.
  • Then talk to Nafiwaa in the Alchemist's guild Aht Urhgan Whitegate (G-5) for another cutscene.
    • She will hand you 5 test tubes (key items).
  • Empty Test Tube #1
  • Empty Test Tube #2
  • Empty Test Tube #3
  • Empty Test Tube #4
  • Empty Test Tube #5
  • Fill each of the vials with water from ponds in Wajaom Woodlands and Bhaflau Thickets and the key items will change.
  • Test Tube #1 (H-11) Wajaom Woodlands
  • Test Tube #2 (G-11) Wajaom Woodlands
  • Test Tube #3 (I-7) Wajaom Woodlands
  • Test Tube #4 (J-8) Wajaom Woodlands
  • Test Tube #5 (G-7) Bhaflau Thickets
  • (There are aggressive monsters around the (H-11) pond)
  • Return to Nafiwaa for another cutscene.
    • The color of the liquid you mix for Nafiwaa depends on its concentration of Mythralline. You may receive an extra bonus from Naja when you receive your LC Wildcat Badge depending on the Mythralline concentration of the liquid:
      • Silver: No reward
      • Mythril:
      • Gold:
      • Platinum: 3-4 Imperial Mythril Pieces - 1 Imperial Gold Piece
      • Luminium: 2 Imperial Gold Pieces
  • If you check the tubes you will get the following:
    • Tube #1: Very Light
    • Tube #2: Same as Tube #4
    • Tube #3: Very Dense
    • Tube #4: Reaction is 3 times faster than Tube #1
    • Tube #5: Density is equal to 1/2 that of the highest concentration.
  • To get Luminium:
    • Select 'Start the Process' for the first menu.
    • Select Test Tube #3 3 times.
    • Select Test Tube #2 1 time.
    • Select Test Tube #4 1 time.
    • Select Test Tube #5 1 time.
    • Select 'Hand it to the Guild!'
  • Wait a game day and return to Abquhbah for another cutscene. (You will not be able to trigger this last cutscene until you zone out of Aht Urhgan Whitegate.)
    • Choose "Helping his fellow mercenaries." and receive your rank.

Game Description

Client: Naja Salaheem (Salaheem's Sentinels, Aht Urhgan Whitegate)
Summary: Falzum, your fellow mercenary, has been given the task of creating a batch of mythralline liquid. As his superior, it is your job to teach him how a mercenary carries out his duty.
