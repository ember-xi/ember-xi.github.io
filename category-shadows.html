Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

General Information

  • System: Undead
  • Weaknesses: Fire, Light
  • Immune: Drain, Aspir
  • Special: Shadows that use great swords link, while others in that tier do not. (This bug was introduced when they were changed from Dark Knights to Black Mages, in the June 2004 version update.) Verification Needed

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Dimensional Death: High damage, dark-based single target physical attack
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
