A Promise to San d’Oria

From Zenith XI Wiki

Defeat 3 Orcish Fodder in West Ronfaure.

Status: Guide matched to World Quests v3.2.0. Installation on the running server is not confirmed by the wiki. Details

Walkthrough

  1. At main level 10+, speak to Achantere, T.K. · Northern San d’Oria (C-8). At the western gate. Choose this city’s ring quest, then Accept quest. Any home nation may accept it.
  2. Defeat 3 Orcish Fodder in West Ronfaure (G-7)/(H-7) after accepting. Stay alive and within 50 yalms, on a level-10+ main job.
  3. Return to Achantere, T.K. · Northern San d’Oria (C-8). At the western gate. Choose Claim reward with one free inventory slot.

Reward

1 San d’Orian Ring — DEF +2, STR +2, MND +2, Accuracy +2 total. Once per character.

The three city quests are independent. An existing copy of the Rare ring prevents a duplicate reward. The city visit is recorded when you accept here.
