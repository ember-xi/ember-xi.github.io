Wake the Puppet

From Zenith XI Wiki

Second Lieutenant Assault in Periqia: return at least four of six automatons within 30 minutes.

Contents
  1. Start
  2. Steps
  3. Response table
  4. Rewards
  5. Sources

Start

Take orders from Lageegee in Aht Urhgan Whitegate and enter from Dvucca Isle Staging Point. Original party requirement: 3–6; recommended level 70.

Steps

  1. Search the four dead-end rooms north, south, east and west of Adeeha. Three rooms contain two automatons each.
  2. Target one and use /salute. Each player controls one puppet.
  3. Stay within 10 yalms and repeatedly /clap to move it toward you. Wights appear after activation; protect your puppet.
  4. If it stops to emote, answer using the table below.
  5. Beside Adeeha, use /goodbye. Its name turns green when successfully returned.
  6. Repeat for four puppets; then use the Rune of Release. Returning all six improves points.
