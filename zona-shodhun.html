Zona Shodhun

From Zenith XI Wiki

Gardening helper and the Jeuno Mog House exit quest NPC.

Contents
  1. Location
  2. Unlock Jeuno Mog House exits
  3. Sources

Location

Port Jeuno — E-8.

Unlock Jeuno Mog House exits

  1. Bring one Yellow Rock.
  2. Trade it to Zona Shodhun to complete Pretty Little Things.
  3. Your Jeuno Rent-a-Room can then exit to the different Jeuno districts.

She also explains Gardening.
