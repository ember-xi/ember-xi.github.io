Walkthrough

  • Extremely resistant to many debuffs
  • Can be Gravitied
  • Can be Shadowbound
  • Does NOT use Draw In
  • Can cast Ancient Magic, Sleepga and tier-3 -ga spells.
  • Has an extremely fast casting speed, very close to Chainspell speed. This casting speed is increased as he goes down in health.
  • Hellsnap: AoE Stun.
  • Hellclap: ?
  • Cackle: AoE Magic Attack down, Magic Acc down, and Magic Def down.
  • Thundris Shriek: AoE Damage (300-400) and Terrorize.
  • Necrobane: AoE Paralyze
  • Necropurge: AoE Curse
  • King Goldemar summons some items when he is aggroed. They will hover above and cannot be attacked until they are called down one at a time by the King. All can use Dire Whorl for minor physical (~100) AOE damage
  • Living Staves - Casts spells (including tier-3 -ga spells)
  • Cursed Axe - Melee damage (Constant Mighty Strikes)
  • Bloody Daggers - Melee damage (Constant Hundred Fists)
  • Magic Shields - Shield Bash (Does not use Regular attacks, Higher defense/hp)
  • Demonic Rod - Casts spells
  • Will intimidate players more as HP decreases. At the end of the battle, will intimidate more often.
