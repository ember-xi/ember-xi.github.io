Ziz Meat

From Zenith XI Wiki

Cooking ingredient obtained from Ziz in Mamook.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Uses
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Defeat Ziz or Zizzy Zillah in Mamook. It can also be purchased from players under Auction House → Food → Ingredients.

Uses

Original recipes include Zoni, Meat Mithkabob, Pet Food Epsilon Biscuit and Ic Pilav. Check each recipe for the crystal and remaining ingredients.
