Avatars

Avatars

RequirementsNo Fame needed for any avatar
TrustsAllowed in the Prime fights

Every avatar has two paths: a Trial-Size fight solo under 65, and a Prime fight with Trusts from 65 that gives the Whisper. At 75 the Prime fight also gives the big Blood Pact.

Under 65: Trial-Size

  1. Talk to the avatar's NPC as SMN. You get a Mini Tuning Fork.
  2. Trade the fork back to the NPC. You are teleported to the Protocrystal.
  3. Trade the fork to the crystal and enter the fight.
  4. Summon Carbuncle: keep him in front, stay back and heal him, and use the Blood Pact when it is ready.
  5. Talk to the crystal after the win. The avatar is yours.
Mechanics: The avatar spawns at level 20 like you. Cast the Bar-spell of its element before entering. Fight on the day of the element that beats it and avoid its own day.

65 and up: Prime Fights

  1. Talk to the avatar's NPC. You get the Tuning Fork (the avatar quest).
  2. Talk to the NPC again while you hold the fork. You are teleported to the Protocrystal.
  3. Summon your Trusts, then talk to the crystal. The fight is at your full level.
  4. Win = Whisper + title. Return to the NPC and choose: the avatar / the staff / the belt / the ring / a Light for the Evoker's Ring / 10,000 gil (once a day).
  5. If you are SMN 75 with the avatar learned, the message === The Prime Pact === appears and you learn the big Blood Pact.
Going for Fenrir? Do not return to the NPC for the reward: it takes the Whisper. Keep all six Whispers, get Fenrir first, then repeat the fights for the rewards.

Fenrir

The Moonlit Path
NPCLeepe-Hoppe — Windurst Waters (H-6), north side of the canal, in front of the Rhinostery
RequirementAll six Whispers at the same time (Trial-Size fights do not give Whispers). No Fame needed.
FightFenrir Prime — Full Moon Fountain (end of Toraimarai Canal), Trusts allowed, level 65+
RewardFenrir / Fenrir's Stone / Fenrir's Cape / Fenrir's Torque / Fenrir's Earring / Ancient's Key / 15,000 gil — one per win, repeatable once a day
  1. Hold the six Whispers: Flames, Frost, Gales, Tremors, Tides, Storms.
  2. Talk to Leepe-Hoppe in Windurst Waters (H-6). Accept The Moonlit Path.
  3. Talk to him again. He takes the six Whispers and gives you the Moon Bauble.
  4. Talk to him once more while you hold the Moon Bauble. You are teleported to the Full Moon Fountain.
  5. Summon your Trusts, then trade the Moon Bauble to the fountain. Fenrir Prime appears.
  6. Win = Whisper of the Moon. Return to Leepe-Hoppe and choose Fenrir.
Mechanics: Fenrir uses Lunar Cry (Accuracy and Evasion down) and Eclipse Bite. Bring Barblizzara off, Barsleep helps against Lunar Roar's Dispel; keep the tank Trust in front and heal from range.

Tips: which avatar to bring

Each Prime is weak to the element that beats it. Bring that avatar and use its Blood Pact: Rage on cooldown. Never use the Prime's own element (it heals it).

General: fight on the day of the element that beats the Prime (Watersday for Ifrit, Firesday for Shiva…) and never on its own day. Cast the Bar-spell of its element before you enter. Summon the avatar before you talk to the crystal so it comes in with you. Keep the tank Trust in front, stand at the back, and let the avatar and Trusts do the work.

Spirits

Spirits have no Blood Pacts and cannot be controlled; they cast on their own by element, your Summoning Magic skill, day and weather. Dark Spirit, for example: Bio, Poison, Blind, Sleep, Dispel, Drain, Aspir.
