Dvucca Isle Staging Point

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Dvucca Isle Staging Point is the Staging Point for Assault missions in Periqia.
It is located at (I-9) on Dvucca Isle, which is part of Caedarva Mire.

Directions

To get there from Aht Urhgan Whitegate take the ferry from (I-5) to Nashmau.

  • Note: You will need an imperial silver piece. 1 per person.
  • Once in Nashmau, take the west enterance to Caederva Mire.
  • Inside the Mire, go due west until you get to an NPC.
  • Trade that NPC your Silver piece to gain entry.
  • In the ruins, there are no mobs, go straight and use the teleporter.
  • Once you reappear, just go straight until you zone back into the Mire.
  • You will be at I-10 on Dvucca Isle in the Mire.
  • Talk to this NPC to get past.
  • Put Sneak and Invisible up after this.
  • Just hug the right wall. It is just a short walk away.[1]
