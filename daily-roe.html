Daily RoE Objectives

From Zenith XI Wiki

Optional daily objectives from the RoE package.

Objectives and base rewards

ObjectiveRequirementBase reward
Vanquish Multiple Enemies (D)30 enemies that award EXP100 Sparks, 500 EXP, Copper A.M.A.N. Voucher
Buff Allies (D)20 qualifying buffs300 Sparks, 1,500 EXP, Voucher
Heal for 500+ HP (D)30 qualifying heals of at least 500 HP each300 Sparks, 1,500 EXP, Voucher

Some objectives are unsuitable for a new character. Values above precede the first-completion bonus; the package awards triple Sparks on first completion.

Availability

The RoE package approves 677 definitions, including 14 timed-rotation definitions and an internal definition. They are not all available at the start. An unsupported objective title may remain visible in the client even when activation is rejected.
