Application

No mobs aggro on smell, however some follow solely on smell. For example:
  • Casting deodorize on someone being followed by a crawler will unlink the crawler.
  • Casting deodorize on someone who has pulled a crawler and linked one or more others will cause the linked mobs to unlink and drop aggro.
