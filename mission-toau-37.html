Starting point

Salaheem's Sentinels · Aht Urhgan Whitegate · I-10

Walkthrough

  1. Return to Whitegate and enter the second platform of Salaheem's Sentinels.
  2. Finish both linked scenes. The game moves your character within Whitegate between them; allow the second scene to finish.

Completion and reward

No item reward.
