General Information

  • System: Amorphs
  • Weaknesses: Fire
  • Resistances: -25% Slashing, -25% Piercing
  • High Resistances: -50% Water, -50% Blunt
  • Traits: Resist Charm III

There are indeed two different families of slimes, although their difference is very slight. Clots have 2 more attacks and are red in color; they also seem to take more Damage from weapons, but less from magic than slimes.

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Digest: Single target HP Drain.
Fluid Spread: 10' AoE damage, absorbed by Utsusemi.
Fluid Toss: Single target water damage.
HP Drain: HP Drain
Epoxy Spread: AoE Bind
Mucus Spread: AoE Slow.
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
