Save My Son—

Walkthrough

  • After Save My Son, talk to Brutus in the Chocobo Stables of Upper Jeuno (G-7) for a cutscene. You may need to talk to him more than once.
    • Do not "set the chocobo free" when he asks: that starts Chocobo on the Loose!. If it happens, talk to Brutus, then the boy by the steps caring for the chocobo, then Brutus again.
    • This quest does not show in your log until it is complete.
  • Zone once and Beastmaster appears in your job list.
