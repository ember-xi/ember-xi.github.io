Path of the Beastmaster

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Start NPC:   Brutus - Upper Jeuno (G-7)
  Requirements:
   Level: 30+
  Items Needed:   None
  Title:   Animal Trainer
  Repeatable:   No
  Reward:   Ability to choose Beastmaster Job

  Previous Quest:   Save My Son
  Next Quest:   Wings of Gold

Walkthrough

  • After completing the Save My Son quest, go talk to Brutus at the Chocobo Stables in Upper Jeuno.

Warning: When talking to Brutus, setting the chocobo free triggers the "Chocobo on the Loose!" quest!

Game Description

Client: Brutus (Chocobo Stables, Upper Jeuno)
Summary:

You have taken your first step. The path of the beastmaster lies before you.
