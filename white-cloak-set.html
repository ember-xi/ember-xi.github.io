White Cloak Set

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This white cloth armor set, created from linen and silk and intended for mages, gives a decent amount of defense but relatively few stat bonuses. The high quality version of this set is the White Cloak +1 Set.
