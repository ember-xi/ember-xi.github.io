Statistics

Martial Abjuration: Body
The inscription reads: "In accordance with the ancient martial pact, may the bane be lifted from thy forbidden artifact." This prayer is necessary in purifying a cursed cuirass.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Other Uses

Guild Points Value: None

Used in Quests:

Abjuration: Koenig Cuirass
Abjuration: Kaiser Cuirass


NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
