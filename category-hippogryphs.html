Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Fantod: Increases the strength of the Hippogryph's next attack, similar to the effect of Boost.
Jettatura: Frontal short-range AOE that causes Terror status to all targets in range facing the hippogryph.
Choke Breath: Frontal AOE. Damages all targets in range and causes Silence and Paralysis. Blockable with one to two Utsusemi shadows.
Nihility Song: AOE that Dispels a single beneficial status effects from all targets in range, including food effects.
Back Heel: Single target physical damage. Blockable with one Utsusemi shadow.
Hoof Volley: Critical single-target damage and Hate Reset. Can be used immediately following any of the above attacks. Easily Stunned. (NMs only)
Additional Effect:Weight
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
