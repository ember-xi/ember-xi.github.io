Saga of the Skyserpent

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Start NPC: Fari-Wari - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (Shararat Teahouse)
Requirements:
 Treasures of Aht Urhgan
Rughadjeen present in Al Zahbi
Items Needed: Lilac Ribbon
Title: Skyserpent Aggrandizer
Repeatable: No
Reward: Imperial Gold Piece, Imperial Standing (1000)

Previous Quest: None
Next Quest: Ode to the Serpents

Walkthrough

1. Talk to Fari-Wari to start quest (K-12).

1.a Optional Dialog from Biyaada in Al Zahbi (G-7) (On top of NW bridge).

2. Enter Halvung from the (C-8) Wajaom Woodlands entrance and go North to H-11, just past the Cast Bronze Gate (West around 13 meters) to get a Lilac Ribbon from the ???.

3. Return to Fari-Wari for a story of a Besieged battle against the Mamool Ja and the Trolls two years ago.

3.a Optional Dialog from Biyaada.

4. Return to Fari-Wari on the next game day for a cutscene and your reward.

Note: Skyserpent General Rughadjeen must be in the Commoner's Ward/Al Zahbi (G-7) for this quest to be accessible or completable. If he is being held prisoner talking to Fari-Wari will not lead to a cutscene.

Game Description

Client:

Biyaada - Al Zahbi, CS NPC Fari-Wari - Aht Urhgan Whitegate (Shararat Teahouse)

Summary:

Venture into Halvung to search for the Skyserpent General's stolen keepsake.
