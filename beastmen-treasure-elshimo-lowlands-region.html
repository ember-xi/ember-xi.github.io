Walkthrough

  • Peddlestox only appears on Windsday at 0:00 at (H-6) in Yuhtunga Jungle.
  • She will ask you to bring her four items. They are all Rare/Ex, and can be very rare drops. Note: This quest will not appear in your quest log.
    • Mermaid Head drop from Brook Sahagin in Sea Serpent Grotto.
    • Mermaid Body drops from Spring Sahagin in Sea Serpent Grotto.
    • Mermaid Hands drops from Creek Sahagin in Yuhtunga Jungle.
    • Mermaid Tail drops from Seww the Squidlimbed in Sea Serpent Grotto.
      • This NM is a Lottery Spawn from the Riparian Sahagin, at (F-7)-(G-7) past the Silver Beastcoin door.
  • When you bring the items back to Peddlestox, she will tell you the location of your Treasure Box. The location is different every time. Even when doing on two different characters at the same time, it can be in two different locations.
  • You must stand on the exact location of the ??? for it to appear.
  • The ??? will appear in the location between 20:00 and 4:00. (In at least one instance, the ??? did NOT disappear at 4:00 and I was able to use it after that timeframe.)
  • Use a Pickaxe on the ??? and you will randomly receive a treasure item, but not necessarily the item she said you could get. Possible treasure includes 3-5 of the following and 4000 to 5000 gil:
  • Mermaid's Ring
  • Silver Beastcoin
  • Mythril Beastcoin
  • Gold Beastcoin
  • Gold Nugget
  • Amber
  • Amethyst
  • Clear Topaz
  • Goshenite
  • Lapis Lazuli
  • Light Opal
  • Sardonyx
  • Tourmaline
  • Beastmen's Seal
  • Coral Fragment
  • Fruit Seeds
  • Vegetable Seeds
  • Grain Seeds
  • Black Rock
  • Blue Rock
  • Translucent Rock
  • Green Rock
  • Purple Rock
  • Red Rock
  • Yellow Rock
  • White Rock

Notes:

  • There are two possibilities in which the ??? will disappear:Verification Needed
    • 1: After you have traded all 4 required items to Peddlestox, the ??? will only appear once, and it will disappear after 4:00 (game time) if let unclaimed, thus, it will never appear again. (In at least one instance, the ??? did NOT disappear at 4:00 and I was able to use it after that timeframe.)
    • 2: After you have traded all 4 required items to Peddlestox, you have exited the related area/zone in which Peddlestox and the ??? reside at.
  • If you do not have the Elshimo Region map (available from Jeuno vendors) then Peddlestox will not give you the location of the beastman treasure, only a message about needing a map.
