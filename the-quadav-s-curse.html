Walkthrough

  • Talk to Corann inside one of the houses. He's got a phobia about going outside but he thinks that if you brought him something large to hide in it would help.
  • Taking a hint from the name of the quest, obtain a Quadav Backplate and trade it to Corann to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Corann (House, Port Bastok)
Summary:

Corann wants to shut himself away from the rest of the world. Find him an item to hide in -- something big enough to hide his whole body.
