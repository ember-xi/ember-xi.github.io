Walkthrough

  • After receiving the cut scene from Apururu ending The Lost Cardian this quest will be active. Go to the southwest tower in West Sarutabaruta (at F-11). Ten of Cups appears at J-8 on that map, which is a hidden area behind another hidden area behind a cracked wall.
  • Once you have the Ten of Cups (Card), take it back to the Manustery and trade it to Apururu.
  • Return to Lower Jeuno and talk to Panta-Putta (G-10) to finish the quest.

Game Description

Client: Panta-Putta (Infirmary Monberaux, Upper Jeuno)
Summary:

Panta wants you to take the Two of Swords to the Manustery in Windurst to restore the Cardian's magical power.
