Notes:

  • Involved in the quest: The Call of the Sea.
  • Spawned by clicking on a ??? in (K-12) when quest is active.
  • Has very high defense to magic and melee.
  • Defense is lowered while it's casting magic.
  • Uses Blind, Haste, Ice Spikes, Regen, Paralyze, Bio II and other Red Mage spells.
  • Some attacks have a Paralyze additional effect.
  • Killable by 6 level 52 players, and solo at 61+ depending on your class, soloable by a 60 RDM/BLM (20 minute fight though).
