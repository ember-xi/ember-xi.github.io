Solo Order

  1. Despot: kill Groundskeepers in Ru'Aun until it spawns (30% every 30 minutes on EmberXI) → Gem of the West.
  2. Steam Cleaner: kill Detectors (30% each) → Gem of the East. Zipacna / Faust / Mother Globe every hour. Aura Statues give Diorite 30% and Aura Pots give Ro'Maeve Water 30%.
  3. Collect gem + stone, go to the god's room and trade at the ???.
  4. The god gives a Seal and its retail drops (Abjurations and weapons).
  5. After all four → Kirin, who gives a guaranteed Abjuration on EmberXI.
Mechanics: Bring tank + healer + BRD + BLM + DD. Byakko and Suzaku need Barfira / Barblizzara. Kirin summons the four gods in phases; kill them one by one.
