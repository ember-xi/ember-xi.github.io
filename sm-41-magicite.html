Mission 4-1 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 3-3 Appointment to Jeuno.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Halver, then Nelcabrit in Ru'Lude Gardens for the Archducal Audience Permit. Talk to Aldo in Lower Jeuno (Tenshodo).
  2. Collect the three Magicite stones from the ??? at the end of each beastman stronghold:
  3. Orastone — Monastic Cavern (through Davoi).
  4. Optistone — Qulun Dome (through Beadeaux; a Silver Bell weakens the curse).
  5. Aurastone — Altar Room (through Castle Oztroja; a Yagudo Torch opens the door).
  6. Return to Nelcabrit for Rank 5 and the Airship Pass.
Mechanics: The longest nation mission. Each stronghold has level 30-40 monsters and NMs at the end. Four Trusts handle it solo; use Sneak and Invisible.
