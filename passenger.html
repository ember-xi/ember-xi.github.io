Passenger

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

NPC for the quest "A Discerning Eye". The passengers will be dressed similiarly to other passengers, but will always have at least one difference when it comes to armor.

One passenger sometimes quips "Isn't it weird how so many passengers look alike these days?"
