Walkthrough

  • Talk to Aquillina; she needs 4 flint stones to light her oven.
  • Trade 4 flint stones to Auqillina to complete the quest.

Note: This quest is repeatable, but can only be completed once every 15 (Earth time) minutes. This is based on completion by any player, not just yourself.

Game Description

Client: Aquillina (House, Bastok Markets)
Summary: Trade four flint stones to Aquillina so she can light her oven.
