Sahagin Key Quest

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This quest does not appear in your quest log.

Start NPC Gimb - Norg (H-9)
Requirements Airship pass for Kazham
Items Needed Gold Beastcoin
Silver Beastcoin
Norg Shell
3 Mythril Beastcoins (depending on luck)
Repeatable Yes
Reward Sahagin Key


Walkthrough

The Sahagin Key opens the Ornamented Door in Sea Serpent Grotto at (J-11).

Obtaining Your First Key

  • Talk to Gimb (H-9) in Norg (next to the Chocobo Rental)
  • Head into Sea Serpent Grotto. Trade a Gold Beastcoin to the "Gold Door" at (I-14) on map 1. You will not lose the item.
    • If you have not passed through this door before, examine it several times until you see a glitter of gold.
    • Apply some form of Sneak to avoid aggro.
  • Talk to Bou the Righteous (H-11).
    • If you leave Sea Serpent Grotto after this point (or accept Tractor), you will have to talk to Bou the Righteous again.
  • Return to the Gold Door.
  • Trade a Silver Beastcoin to the "Silver Door" at (N-14). You will not lose the item.
    • If you have not passed through this door before, examine it several times until you see a glitter of silver.
  • Talk to Hurr the Betrayer (H-3).
    • He will ask for a Norg Shell, and he will choose randomly between if he wants either 3 Mythril Beastcoins or a Gold Beastcoin.
  • Trade him the items he requests for a Sahagin Key.

Obtaining Subsequent Keys

  • Go through the Silver Door at (N-14).
  • Talk to Hurr the Betrayer (H-3).
    • He will ask for a Norg Shell, along with either 3 Mythril Beastcoins or a Gold Beastcoin.
  • Trade him the items he requests for a Sahagin Key.
