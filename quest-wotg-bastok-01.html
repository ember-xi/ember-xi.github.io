Before you begin

  • Complete one Campaign enlistment quest: The Fighting Fourth, Steamed Rams, or Snake on the Plains.
  • Bring one Gnole Claw for the later trade.

Starting point

Zone boundary from Bastok Markets [S] · North Gustaberg [S] · J-9

Walkthrough

  1. Exit Bastok Markets [S] into North Gustaberg [S] for the opening scene and Clump of Animal Hair key item.
  2. Speak with Engelhart outside the music shop in Bastok Markets [S], K-10.
  3. Return to North Gustaberg [S] and examine the ??? at F-8 beside Drachenfall, on the right side of the waterfall.
  4. Speak to Leadavox at J-6 in Vunkerl Inlet [S], then trade her one Gnole Claw to obtain the Xhifhut key item.
  5. Return to Engelhart to finish, receive 10,000 gil, and obtain the Warning Letter. Leave and re-enter the zone before starting the next quest.

Battle and progress notes

  • This story quest is open after any nation’s first enlistment; it does not itself change Campaign allegiance.
  • Gnole Claws come from gnoles and may also be available through the Auction House. Keep the Warning Letter for later story progression.

Completion and reward

10,000 gil; Warning Letter key item.

What this opens

  • B-02: Fires of Discontent
