Notes

The younger sister of Aldo, head of the Tenshodo. She has an affinity for the beastmen races and is friends with local Goblins living in Jeuno, especially Fickblix, which creates tension with her brother. However, her natural affinity has also caught the attention of Lord Eald'narche, who became interested in this trait......
