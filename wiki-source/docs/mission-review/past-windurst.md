# Windurst [S] story quest walkthroughs

Deliverable: `windurst-past-quests.json`, generated by `author.py`.

Contains the optional Windurst Campaign enlistment (W-00) and all twelve national story quests (W-01–W-12). Every article has original actionable prose, prerequisites, starting location, complete progression steps, warnings relevant to completion, reward, unlocks, exact zone names, reference links and pinned code links. No Site or game files were edited.

## Native scope and uncertainty

Pinned base: LandSandBoat `f45ab5c0aa3607b4b8fb694da58434e4dd29a2e4`.

The full native tree at `../job-balance/melee/tree.json` contains 22,628 entries and is not truncated. Dedicated quest handlers exist for W-00–W-03 and were read in full. The tree has no dedicated later Windurst national quest handlers or named later Windurst battlefield handlers.

To check legacy implementations, inspected the available Zone, DefaultActions and NPC files in Windurst Waters [S], West Sarutabaruta [S], Fort Karugo-Narugo [S], Ghoyu's Reverie, Castle Oztroja [S] and Meriphataud Mountains [S]. The later quest constants and relevant named battle items did not appear as progression hooks in this inspected set. The extra 24-path fetch list is in `native/inspected-paths.json`; earlier Windurst/West Saruta source files are also in `native/` and the WotG agent's native directory.

This is not a complete runtime implementation audit. Accordingly W-04–W-12 say **reference walkthrough; completion unconfirmed in the reviewed base**, not unplayable. No Trust eligibility, uncapped solo success or Zenith runtime completion is asserted. The early native handlers establish source support, not a gameplay test.

Important verified native behavior:

- W-00 changes Campaign allegiance to Windurst. First-time shoes and ribbon are conditional on having no existing allegiance. W-01 also accepts either other nation's completed enlistment, so users do not need to switch allegiance to start Windurst's story.
- W-01 checks the Small starfruit and lower Acolyte Hostel door; it has no Inky Black Yagudo Feather gate.
- W-02 requires rechecking the ??? after War Lynx, then reporting to Dhea, and sets `needToZone(true)` at completion.
- W-03 requires entry into Southern San d'Oria [S] from East Ronfaure [S]; a direct town warp does not meet its native trigger.
- Later main-story quest completion predicates exist in `scripts/missions/wotg/helpers.lua`, but those alone do not prove that the national quests themselves are implemented. Its day-wait TODOs are not treated as implemented waits.
