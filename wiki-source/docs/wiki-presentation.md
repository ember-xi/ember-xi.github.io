# Wiki presentation

User direction, confirmed 25 September 2026: all Zenith XI website additions follow the established Eden/Horizon wiki organization. The website stays entirely English.

Use traditional article headings, linked category groups, infoboxes, maps and data tables. Area categories follow continents → regions → linked zone articles. Zone articles use Description, Table of Contents, Connections, Map and Zone Information, then quest, NPC and monster sections.

Do not add dashboard counters, statistic tiles, new filter toolbars, marketing summaries or interface explanations to wiki content. Use the existing global wiki search. Preserve existing article URLs and useful game information. Keep source attribution in References and document Zenith differences in the relevant article text.

Reference structures: https://horizonffxi.wiki/Category:Areas and https://edenxi.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Areas .
