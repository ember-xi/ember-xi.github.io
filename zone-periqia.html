Additional level-75 reference


Located along the western expanse of the Arrapago Islands this network of caverns and niches is littered with hiding places of those who wish to keep their operations quiet. Surely among the crates and barrels hidden from prying eyes scattered throughout there must be something of value...

This area an Assault area. This area is only accessible if the Treasures of Aht Urhgan expansion is installed, The Road to Aht Urhgan quest is completed, and Aht Urhgan Mission 3: President Salaheem is completed.

A cage of Dvucca Fireflies is given to all participating mercenaries who enter. They can be used to return to the Dvucca Isle Staging Point.
