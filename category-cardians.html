General Information

The specialty of the Federation of Windurst these automaton guards were created by Zonpa-Zippa and are handled by the Manustery. Since their creation Cardians have become a permanent fixture of Windurstian life, used to patrol, guard and scout Cardian guards are valued resource of the Windurstian Military, however rumours persist of Cardians going rogue and attacking their masters.


  • System: Arcana
  • Weaknesses:
  • Immune:


The rogue Cardians are broken up into four sub-groups based on their families that will determine their job type. Coins are Red Mage type mobs, Swords are Paladins, Batons are Black Mages, and Cups are White Mage mobs.
