Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Garuda grants party members Haste within area of effect.

Technicals

Avatar: Garuda (Wind)

Blood Pact: Ward

Cost: 112 MP

Level: 48

Description

Effect Duration: 1:30

Battle Application: Party buff

Damage Type: N/A

Additional Notes: Each summoning magic skill level over the current skill level cap will add three seconds to the duration. Grants a 15% haste bonus. When Blood Pact: Ward and Rage were added, the haste bonus was increased from its previous value of 10%. At any level, with capped skill and +30 skill gear/merits, Hastega caps at 3:00 duration.

Macro Syntax

  • /pet "Hastega" <me>

or

  • /ja "Hastega" <me>
