  Previous Quest:   None.

Walkthrough

  • Speak with Chamama in the Rarab Tail Hostelry at (F-10) in Windurst Waters. If she doesn't give you the quest, speak to Kogri-Nargri next to her, and then Chamama again. 
  • She will ask you to find her a replacement stone.
  • Kill Crawlers in East Sarutabaruta and West Sarutabaruta until one drops a smooth stone.
  • Trade the smooth stone to Chamama.
  • One of two things may happen:
  • She will tell you that the stone is no good. You will have to bring her another stone to get your reward and complete the quest.
  • She will take the stone and give you your reward.

Game Description

Client: Chamama (Rarab Tail Hostelry, Windurst Waters)
Summary:

The weighting stone used to make the Rarab Tail Hostelry's specialty dish, "Rarab Tail Pickles", was broken.
 
Chamama wants you to find her a replacement stone that is just the right size, shape, and weight.
