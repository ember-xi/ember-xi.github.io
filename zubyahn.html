Zubyahn

From Zenith XI Wiki

A Whitegate NPC involved in Five Seconds of Fame.

Location

Aht Urhgan Whitegate — K-12, Shararat Teahouse.

During Five Seconds of Fame, speak with him between 05:00 and 07:00 Vana’diel time for the teahouse recording scene, after the required conversation with Ratihb. Follow the quest for the precise recording timing.
