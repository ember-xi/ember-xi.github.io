MissionJourney Abroad
TypeQuest
Start NPCHalver — Chateau d'Oraguille
LocationBastok (Metalworks) · Windurst (Windurst Woods)
RequirementsRank 2 · 2-2 complete
Items NeededNone
RewardsRank 3 · 3,000 gil · Adventurer's Certificate · Title: Certified Adventurer

Mission 2-3 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 2-2 The Davoi Report.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission from a Gate Guard, then talk to Halver in Chateau d'Oraguille for the Letter to the Consuls.
  2. Visit the consulate in Metalworks (Bastok) and talk to the consul (Chantain / Savae E Paleade). Then visit the consulate in Windurst Woods (Erpolant / Mourices).
  3. Return to Halver for Rank 3.
Mechanics: No fighting. On EmberXI all Home Points are open, so you can teleport straight to Bastok and Windurst.
