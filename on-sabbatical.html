Before you start

Scholar level 40+; A Little Knowledge complete.

Steps

  1. Talk to Erlene in The Eldieme Necropolis (S) (J-8) on Scholar to receive Ulbrecht's Sealed Letter.
  2. Speak to Gentle Tiger in Bastok Markets (S) (H-6), by the Metalworks entrance. If another quest scene takes priority, talk again.
  3. Check Indescript Markings on the northern edge of (E-11) in Pashhow Marshlands (S) for Schultz's Sealed Letter. Nearby Peistes detect by sight; approach carefully.
  4. Return to Erlene for the scroll. For Downward Helix, reach SCH 50 and wait until the next Vana'diel day.

Reward

Klimaform scroll, usable at SCH 46.
