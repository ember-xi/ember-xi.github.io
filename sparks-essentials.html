Buying and learning a Trust

  1. Complete First Step Forward and receive the Memorandoll to use the shop.
  2. Speak to a Sparks NPC, choose Trust Ciphers, select your companion and confirm Buy one. Keep an inventory slot free.
  3. After obtaining any starting-city Trust permit, trade the Cipher by itself to Gondebaud, Clarion Star or Wetata.
  4. The companion is learned permanently. Summon it from Magic → Trust under the normal Zenith rules.

The shop will not charge you again if you already learned that Trust or own its Cipher. If item delivery fails, the payment is returned or saved as credit until there is room in your balance. The Sparks cap is 99,999; the separate weekly spending limit still applies if enabled.

Voucher exchange

  1. Trade Copper A.M.A.N. Vouchers to a Sparks NPC to store them.
  2. Choose Voucher exchange and select Sparks or Imperial Standing.

Imperial Standing costs a voucher, not Sparks. It retains all its ordinary uses and requires Treasures of Aht Urhgan to be enabled. This exchange does not unlock Runic Portals or waive their access, permit or rank requirements.
