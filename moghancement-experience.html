Moghancement: Experience

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description
"Moogle-powered assistance from your Mog House. Reduces the amount of experience lost when KO'd."
Notes
This will decrease the amount of Experience Points that are lost when you're killed. It is confirmed that with this moghancement you will retain at least 5% of the XP you would have lost upon death. It is also confirmed that stronger Mog House elemental energy does not independently increase the amount of XP retained. Unconfirmed reports indicate that having additional beds can increase the amount of XP retained upon death or that the different beds yield different savings.

Moghancement: Experience does have an effect on Raise spells and Home Pointing, the effect of the Moghancement reduces the initial loss in EXP upon death. Source: http://wiki.ffxiclopedia.org/2004_-_%2804/22/2004%29_Update_Details

See Moghancements for more information.
It is obtained through these Furnishings:
  • Bronze Bed
  • Chocobo Bedding
  • Mahogany Bed
  • Marble Bed
  • Miniature Airship
  • Noble's Bed
  • Oak Bed
  • Royal Bed
  • Simple Bed
  • Amir Bed
