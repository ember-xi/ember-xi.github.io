Trial by Earth

Required FameNone

Items NeededTuning fork of earth

—The Moonlit Path

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Juroro in Port Bastok (I-8). He gives you the Tuning fork of earth.
  • Talk to him again while you hold the fork. You are sent straight to the Cloister of Tremors.
    • The Cloister is at (C-8) on Quicksand Caves map 1. On foot: enter the caves from Eastern Altepa Desert (J-7) and take the one-way drops down twice. Quicksand Caves Home Point #2 is outside it.
  • Summon your Trusts and your avatar, then examine the Earth Protocrystal and pick Trial by Earth.
  • Defeat Titan Prime.
    • Earth damage heals him: no Stone spells, no earth weapon skills.
    • He uses Rock Throw, Mountain Buster, Earthen Ward and Stone IV, and at about 50% HP his Astral Flow pact Earthen Fury — heavy AoE earth damage.
    • Best day: Windsday. Avoid Earthsday. Keep Barstonra up.
    • Bring Garuda: wind beats earth, and Predator Claws hits hardest.
  • You receive the Whisper of Tremors.
    • Going for Fenrir? Keep it and do not take a reward yet — see The Moonlit Path.
  • Return to Juroro and choose your reward.
    • Repeatable once a day (after midnight JST).
    • At SMN 75 with Titan already learned, winning teaches Geocrush, his level 75 Blood Pact.
    • Juroro also runs the artifact quest The Puppet Master; being on this trial can hold that quest back until you finish it.

EmberXI changes

  • No Fame requirement (the Bastok Fame gate was removed).
  • Trusts can be summoned inside the battlefield; the Prime fight needs level 65+.
  • Talking to Juroro while you hold the fork teleports you to the Cloister.
  • Repeatable once a day.
  • Under 65, summoners get a solo trial-size fight from Ferrol in Port Bastok (level 20 cap, Carbuncle only).
