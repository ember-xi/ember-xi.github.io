Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Leviathan restores hit points to party members within area of effect and cures some status ailments.

Technicals

Avatar: Leviathan (Water)

Blood Pact: Ward

Cost: 99 MP

Level: 47

Description

Effect Duration: Instantaneous

Battle Application: N/A

Damage Type: N/A

Additional Notes:

  • Reported to cure hit points equal to (47 + Level*3)
  • Removes blind, poison, paralysis, disease, petrification, sleep, silence, and sometimes slow.

Macro Syntax

  • /pet "Spring Water" <me>

or

  • /ja "Spring Water" <me>
