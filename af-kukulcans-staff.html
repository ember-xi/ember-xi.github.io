Kukulcan's Staff

Kukulcan's Staff
TypeArtifact Weapon (Staff)
Level40 · Summoner
StatsMP +30 · MP recovered while healing +3
QuestThe Puppet Master (SMN AF1)
StartHouse of the Hero — Windurst Walls (G-4), as Summoner main job
RequirementSummoner 40+, quest I Can Hear a Rainbow completed (Carbuncle)
BattlefieldCloister of Tremors (C-8, Quicksand Caves map 1) · 30 minutes · uncapped · buffs do not wear · Trusts allowed
BossGalgalim — Hecteyes, level 56, about 6,000 HP
RewardKukulcan's Staff
Next questClass Reunion (Evoker's Spats)

The first Summoner artifact piece. Carbuncle asks you to hunt down whoever is draining the avatars' power, and the trail leads to a Hecteyes sleeping under the Quicksand Caves.

Walkthrough

  1. As Summoner main job, examine the House of the Hero in Windurst Walls (G-4) to speak to Carbuncle. From here on any job can finish the quest.
  2. Talk to Juroro in Port Bastok (I-8) for a cutscene. Ildy-Goldy hands you the Earth Pendulum.
  3. Enter Quicksand Caves from Eastern Altepa Desert (J-7). Inside, follow the one-way drops down twice and make your way to (C-8) on map 1, the Cloister of Tremors. Quicksand Caves Home Point #2 sits right outside it — take it for later runs.
  4. Summon your Trusts, then trade the Earth Pendulum to the Earth Protocrystal and pick The Puppet Master.
  5. Defeat Galgalim.
  6. Talk to Juroro in Port Bastok again.
  7. Talk to Koru-Moru in Windurst Walls (E-7) to finish the quest and receive the staff. If he gives dialogue for another quest, talk to him again.
  8. The Earth Pendulum can be dropped once the fight is done.

The Fight

  • Galgalim is a Hecteyes, level 56, around 6,000 HP. 30 minutes, no level cap, and buffs cast outside stay on.
  • It uses Fire Spikes, Poison and Paralyze.
  • It can be silenced, paralyzed and slowed, but it cannot be slept — no Sleep, Sleepga or Repose will hold.
  • Easy for a level 70+ job, and easy with Trusts at 40 if you bring a tank and a healer.

EmberXI changes

  • Equippable at level 40 instead of 41.
  • MP +30 instead of MP +20, and MP recovered while healing +3.
  • Trusts can be summoned inside the battlefield.
  • No Fame requirement anywhere in the quest.
