Notes:

  • Lottery Spawn from one of the two possible Tundra Tigers around the tower at F-7, approximately every hour. Can often be up to 6 hours. The other tiger is shared pop with a Living Statue, and is not the placeholder for Nue.
  • Only the Tundra Tigers are a lottery for it, not the Goblin's Tiger from the Goblin Trader in that area.
  • The Tundra Tiger have a 5 minute timed spawn. Monks camping Nue for Fistful of Fury can use the job ability Chakra to time this.
  • To give an idea of the drop rate without treasure hunter, the fang dropped after 7 attempts at Nue.
  • Killable by: 2 characters level 50+, soloable 55+.
