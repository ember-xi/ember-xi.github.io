Zungutt

From Zenith XI Wiki

Melody Minstrel for Rabao cutscene replays.

Location

Rabao — E-8.

Speak with him to replay previously viewed Rabao scenes for a small fee. The replay list includes scenes from The Mithra and the Crystal, Trial by Wind, Chasing Dreams, The Search for Goldmane, I'll Take the Big Box, True Will and Carbuncle Debacle. Replaying a scene does not advance its quest.
