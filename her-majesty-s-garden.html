Requirements

This is one of the few quests that involve the Chateau d'Oraguille that is connected to Northern San d'Oria. In order to get into the Chateau d'Oraguille you must have either San d'Oria Rank:2 or be on or have completed Bastok Mission 2-3 or Windurst Mission 2-3 in the respective nations. Otherwise, you will be unable to zone in and the guards outside will be quite rude to you. But once you have achieved one of the previous status in Rank and are able to get in you must also have San d'Oria Reputation:4 in order to actually flag this quest from Chalvatot.

Walkthrough

  • Examine Queen Leaute's Grave in Chateau d'Oraguille.
  • Talk to Chalvatot, who is nearby. He will seem upset and not willing to help if you are not of the San d'Oria allegiance.
  • Talk to Chalvatot again and receive a cutscene(if you do not get the cutscene you must zone out of Chateau d'Oraguille and back in.) Accept the Quest -Default Selection is No. So make sure you're not spamming enter to skip through the dialogue.
  • Trade Chalvatot a Derfland Humus to complete the quest.
  • You will have obtained a Key Item: Map of the Northlands Area. Congratulations!

Game Description

Client: Chalvatot (Queen Leaute's Garden, Chateau d'Oraguille)
Summary:

Bring some Derfland humus to use as fertilizer so that flowers might grow in the late queen's garden.
