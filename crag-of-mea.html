Crag of Mea

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Location

The Crag of Mea is located in Tahrongi Canyon.
This Crag also contains an entrance to Promyvion - Mea via the Hall of Transference, one may enter by examining the Shattered Telepoint located on a platorm outside of the Crag.

Teleportation

You can teleport here by having the spell Teleport-Mea cast on you.

  • You can obtain the key item Mea Gate Crystal by clicking on the Teleport pad at the Crag of Mea.
  • Mea Gate Crystal must be in your Key Items for the Teleport-Mea spell to affect you.
