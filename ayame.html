Ayame

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: NPC

Location: Metalworks (K-7), on the top level in the Cannonry

Notes

  • A member of the Mythril Musketeers. She is "Number 4". Plays a key role in multiple Bastok Missions.

Starts Quests

  • A Question of Faith
  • Out of the Depths
  • Return to the Depths
  • True Strength (MNK AF3)

Involved in Missions

  • Bastok Mission 2-1: The Crystal Line
  • Bastok Mission 3-1: The Four Musketeers
  • Bastok Mission 6-2: The Pirates' Cove
  • Bastok Mission 9-2: Where Two Paths Converge
  • Promathia Mission 1-3: The Mothercrystals
  • Promathia Mission 3-1: Call of the Wyrmking
  • Aht Urhgan Mission 38: Stirrings of War

Involved in Quests

  • Faded Promises
  • Lure of the Wildcat (Bastok)
