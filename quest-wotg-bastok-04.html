Before you begin

  • Complete B-03: Light in the Darkness.
  • Wait until the next Vana’diel day and change zones after that completion.

Starting point

Gentle Tiger · Bastok Markets [S] · H-6

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Gentle Tiger at H-6, then Engelhart at K-10 in Bastok Markets [S].
  2. Enter The Eldieme Necropolis [S]. On map 1, reach the southern room at G-9 and drop through its middle pit.
  3. On map 2, take the eastern passage to map 3. Reach the northern room at I-7 and examine the Sarcophagus on its eastern side.
  4. Return to Gentle Tiger to complete the investigation.
  5. Speak to Blatherix at F-8 to collect the associated Elixir and prepare the next quest’s timer. Keep that Elixir for The Truth Lies Hid.

Battle and progress notes

  • Orcs guard the Sarcophagus room. Bring a way to avoid or handle aggro and a way home.
  • The quest completion grants the title. The Elixir comes from Blatherix afterward; that conversation is necessary to open Storm on the Horizon in the audited base.

Completion and reward

Assistant Detective title; Elixir from the follow-up conversation with Blatherix.

What this opens

  • The Bastok route’s requirement for progressing WotG 3: Cait Sith to WotG 4: The Queen of the Dance
  • B-05: Storm on the Horizon
