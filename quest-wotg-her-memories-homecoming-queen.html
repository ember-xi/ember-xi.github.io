Before you begin

  • Her Memories is the current Wings of the Goddess mission.

Starting point

Zone entrance · Southern San d'Oria

Era note: September 2010 WotG memory chapter, released after the original level-75 cap.

Walkthrough

  1. Walk into present-day Southern San d'Oria from East Ronfaure to start the memory scene.
  2. Complete the three child quests in any order: Old Bean, The Faux Pas and Grave Resolve.
  3. Their three tiny fragments combine automatically into Large memory fragment I; no additional hand-in is required.

Completion and reward

Large memory fragment I.
