Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is moogle and he is present in your Mog House and Rent-a-Rooms as a housekeeper. He can:

  • Change your Main Job and Support Job
  • Check your Delivery Box
  • Access your Mog Safes 1 and 2, Storage, and Mog Wardrobes 1 and 2
  • Change the Layout of your Furniture
  • Manage your Gardening.


These services are only available at the five major nations of San d'Oria, Bastok, Windurst, Jeuno and Aht Urhgan. In smaller towns, you'll need to speak with Nomad Moogles. Speaking to him will also confirm the status of your Moghancement auras.

  • Moogles in the Wild
  • The Moogle's Picnic!
