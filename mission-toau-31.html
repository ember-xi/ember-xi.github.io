Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 30: Prevalence of Pirates.
  • Periqia Assault Area Entry Permit key item; consumed on entry.

Starting point

Runic Seal · Caedarva Mire · I-9

Walkthrough

  1. Return to Dvucca Isle Staging Point from the Cutter.
  2. Examine the Runic Seal and choose Shades of Vengeance to enter Periqia. Each character on this mission needs the entry permit.
  3. Go to the Lamiae gathered around H-9. Pull carefully and defeat K23H1-LAMIA until the objective completes; the required count varies.
  4. Finish within 30 minutes and allow the exit scene in Caedarva Mire to advance the mission.

Battle and progress notes

  • If you fail, wait until the next Vana'diel day and talk to Nahshib at Dvucca Isle for a replacement permit. This mission instance is separate from ordinary ranked Assault orders.

Completion and reward

Nashmeira's Mercenary title.

What this opens

  • ToAU 32: In the Blood
