Avatar Magical Accuracy

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Merit Point Enhancement

  • Category: Job-specific - Group 1 (Summoner)
  • Limits: 5 Levels (Merit Point progression: 1>2>3>4>5 = 15 points total)
  • Description: Each level of this enhancement increases Magic Accuracy for all of your summoned Avatars by 3 per level, with a maximum bonus of +15 at level 5.
  • Further Notes:
  • Physical abilities (e.g. Camisado, Crescent Fang, Predator Claws, etc.) are not affected by this enhancement. See Avatar Physical Accuracy
  • Avatar Magic Accuracy is also enhanced by the addition of Summoning Magic skill exceeding the Summoner's current natural cap.
  • It is unclear if this also affects summoned Spirits' spells.Verification Needed
