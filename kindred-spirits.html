Notes

  • Demons have the Demon special attacks.
  • Duke Amduscias has Flood, Bind, Stonega III.
  • Count Andromalius uses Absorb spells.
  • All are susceptible to Gravity and Bind; Lullaby and Sleep work very rarely.
