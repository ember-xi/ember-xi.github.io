Trust Ciphers

From Zenith XI Wiki

A Cipher is exchanged for a permanent Trust spell.

Contents
  1. How to exchange
  2. Zenith sources
  3. NPC map locations

How to exchange

  1. Complete your first Trust quest and obtain its permit.
  2. Take one Cipher to Gondebaud, Clarion Star or Wetata.
  3. Trade the Cipher by itself and complete the dialogue.
  4. Open Magic → Trust to summon the learned companion in a permitted area.

Zenith sources

  • RoE Trust walkthrough: all five Ciphers
  • Permanent Sparks Cipher selection

Retail campaign listings do not establish availability on Zenith. Apururu’s quest teaches her spell directly; it does not award a Cipher.
