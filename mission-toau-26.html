Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 25: Playing the Part.
  • Empty Main and Sub equipment slots.

Starting point

Imperial Whitegate · Aht Urhgan Whitegate · L-8/L-9

Walkthrough

  1. Empty Main and Sub equipment slots, including shields or a second weapon.
  2. Examine the Imperial Whitegate and finish the audience.

Battle and progress notes

  • The palace-approved body armor check from Guests of the Empire is not applied to this audience. Ranged and Ammo slots can remain equipped.

Completion and reward

No item reward.

What this opens

  • ToAU 27: Misplaced Nobility
