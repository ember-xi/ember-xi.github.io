Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Enhances defense and covers you with magical ice spikes. Enemies that hit you take ice damage.

Description

Spell cost: 28 MP
Ecosystem: Luminions
Type: Magical (Ice)
Blue Magic Points: 5
Stat Bonus: INT+3 MND+3
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 74

Timers

Casting Time: 3 seconds
Recast Time: 60 seconds
Duration: 120 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Reactor Cool as part of a combo to create Magic Attack Bonus.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Magic Attack Bonus combo.

  • Sound Blast
  • Cursed Sphere
  • Eyes On Me
  • Memento Mori
  • Heat Breath
  • Magic Hammer

Notes

  • Adds approximately +12% Defense.
  • Defense up effect does NOT stack with Cocoon, and will overwrite the 50% increase from Cocoon. However, reapplying Cocoon will give you the 50% defense boost again, while maintaining the Ice Spikes from Reactor Cool.
