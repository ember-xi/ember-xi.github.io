Asuran Fists

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Hand-to-Hand weapon skill

  • Skill level: 250 (Monks and Puppetmasters only.)
  • In order to obtain Asuran Fists, the quest The Walls of Your Mind must be completed.
  • Delivers an eight-hit attack. Accuracy varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Gravitation / Liquefaction
  • Modifiers: STR:10% ; VIT:10%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.00 1.00 1.00

Levels

  • Asuran Fists is only available to Monks and Puppetmasters. While other jobs can obtain the required 250 Hand-to-Hand skill through the use of merits and special equipment, they will be unable to use Asuran Fists.
  • While Monks may obtain skill level 250 earlier than level 71, Asuran Fists must be quested once base skill level 250 (without merits) and job level 71 is reached (requires the ability to wield Knuckles of Trials).
  • Puppetmasters require level 75 to obtain skill level 250. Unconfirmed if merits allow the quest to be flagged earlier than 75.
