Pirates

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Sometimes arrive on the ferry between Selbina and Mhaura. The Pirates will usually spawn in rainy or foggy weather, but can appear in any weather. When the pirate boat pulls alongside, some humes will walk onto the deck and begin summoning skeletons on the ferry's deck. The pirates you will face are the skeleton mobs, not the pirate humes on the other ship.

The skeletons that appear on the deck of the ferry behave like other skeletons. They will aggro like other skeleton mobs, including blood aggro; the safest place to be if you do not wish to fight the skeletons is below deck, although going up the stairs that lead to the cockpit of the ferry will keep you safer than staying on the deck itself.

Beware, if one of the pirate summoners is wearing what appears to be a Vermillion Cloak, one of the NM pirates, Silverhook or Blackbeard, will spawn in the attack. These NMs are much more difficult than the normal pirates, and must be fought with care.

Mobs that spawn on the deck during a pirate attack:

  • Crossbones
  • Ship Wight

(If one of the pirate-summoners wears a Vermillion Cloak)

  • Blackbeard (NM)
  • Silverhook (NM)
