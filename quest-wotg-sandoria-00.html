Before you begin

  • Access the Crystal War through Cavernous Maws.
  • For a first enlistment, obtain the Red recommendation letter from Randecque at Garlaige Citadel (S), I-6 on map 2.
  • Do not have another nation’s recruitment quest currently active.

Starting point

Mainchelite · Southern San d'Oria (S) · I-9

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Mainchelite and accept the investigation. First-time recruits surrender the Red recommendation letter.
  2. In East Ronfaure (S), examine the ??? at southeast H-8 in the dirt for the Charred propeller.
  3. Examine the waterfall ??? at I-8 where the river divides for the Oxidized plate, and the ??? beside a tree/bush in northwest J-7 for the Piece of shattered lumber.
  4. Return to Mainchelite with all three key items to finish enlistment.

Battle and progress notes

  • This quest sets Campaign allegiance to San d’Oria. Completing any one nation’s recruitment permits the later national story quests; changing allegiance is unnecessary just to follow the Griffon story.

Completion and reward

Title: Knight of the Iron Ram. First-time enlistment also awards Sprinter’s Shoes and Bronze Ribbon of Service.

What this opens

  • Campaign enlistment
  • Gifts of the Griffon
