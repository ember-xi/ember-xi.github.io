General Information

  • System: Dragons; Black wyverns: +Undead
  • Weaknesses: Darkness
  • Immune: Light

Naturally enhanced movement speed

Wyverns are distinctly different from wyrm and dragon in both behavior and appearance. Wyverns are much smaller in size, tend to be gold in color, and have very good sight; wyverns can see in front of them and from the side for a very long distance. The notable difference between a wyvern and a wyrm is the fact wyverns rarely fly.

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Dispelling Wind: 10' AoE Dispel
Dread Shriek: 10' AoE Paralysis
Deadly Drive: Single attack. Potential one-shotter, but not as likely as Fang Rush.
Fang Rush: Single target three hit attack. Has strong potential to one-shot anyone.
Wind Wall: Gives Evasion Boost
Radiant Breath: Light-based Breath Attack that inflicts Slow and Silence
Tail Crush: Single-target physical damage that inflicts Poison
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
