Zeruhn Soot

From Zenith XI Wiki

Quest material dropped by the original low-level monsters in Zeruhn Mines.

Contents
  1. How to obtain
  2. Quest use
  3. Sources

How to obtain

Defeat Ding Bats, Tunnel Worm, Mouse Bat, River Crab, Leech in Zeruhn Mines. It is also tradable through Auction House → Others → Miscellaneous.

Quest use

Collect soot for Minesweeper. Open that quest for the NPC, required amount and reward.
