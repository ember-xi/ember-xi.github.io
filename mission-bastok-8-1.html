Before you begin

Be allied with Bastok, have Rank 8, and finish the preceding required story steps. Raise Rank Points if the mission is not offered.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission and speak with Iron Eater.
  2. Enter Quicksand Caves from Western Altepa Desert (G-5). Pass the weighted door at (H-8) and inspect the Galka Statue at (G-11).
  3. Defeat the three Antica, then examine the statue again.
  4. Use the separate Western Altepa entrance at (D-12), reached through C-11 / D-11. Pass the eastern weighted doors toward map 6.
  5. Examine the ??? before the mural at map 6 (H-8), then return to Iron Eater.

Important notes

  • Arrange enough player weight to open the doors; do not assume Trusts operate the sensors.

Walkthrough

  • Receive the mission from the Bastok Gate Guard and then talk to Iron Eater.
  • Travel to Western Altepa Desert (G-5) and enter Quicksand Caves.
  • Head to (H-8) and pass through the Weight Detection device [works with 1 Galka, 2 Hume/Elvaan/Mithra, or 3 Tarutaru]
  • Head south onto a new map and pass another Weight Detection device (H-10). The ??? (Galka Statue) you need is just to the south located at (G-11).
  • Clear the room first, then spawn the 3 NM:
  • Triarius IV-XIV (Black Mage)
  • Princeps IV-XLV (Paladin)
  • Centurio IV-VII (Warrior)
  • It is recomended that you kill them in the order listed above with 12 level 60+ alliance or 6 level 72+ party.
  • All these 3 are about ~lvl 60 NM Antica.
  • After you win, everyone should touch the ??? to get a cutscene.
  • Next Teleport-Altep/Escape and zone back into Western Altepa Desert, and zone into Quicksand Caves located in the furthest SW corner of Western Altepa Desert (D-12).
  • Head to (K-8) on this map and pass a Weight Detection device.
  • Head east into a new map and pass another Weight Detection device (G-8).
  • The ??? is located just to the east (H-8). Touch it for another cutscene.
  • Return to Iron Eater in the Metalworks for a final cutscene.


Game Description

Mission Orders
The president has ordered a full-scale investigation of Zepwell Island. You are to travel to the Quicksand Caves located in the central Altepa Desert. Report to Iron Eater for a briefing.
