Qu'Hau Spring

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Location: Ro'Maeve (H-6)

Note

  • Only full during a Full Moon, between the hours of 0:00 and 3:00, and during clear weather (Foggy weather doesn't have an onscreen icon).

Involved in Quests:

  • Divine Might

Involved in Missions:

  • Vain (Windurst Mission 8-1)
  • Moon Reading (Windurst Mission 9-2)
