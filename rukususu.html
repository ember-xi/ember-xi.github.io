Rukususu

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Cutscene NPC

Location: Fei'Yin (F-6) (Behind a locked door, with a golem type mob)

Notes

Rukususu is the Rhinostery minister. She spends much of her time doing field research, particularly concerning the fate of her mentor Iru-Kuiru. In the English version of FFXI, she speaks some French but mostly English with a heavy French accent.

Involved in Missions

  • Windurst Mission 5-1: The Final Seal
  • Windurst Mission 7-2: Awakening of the Gods
  • Windurst Mission 8-2: The Jester Who'd Be King
