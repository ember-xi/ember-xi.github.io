General Information

  • System: Unclassified
  • Weaknesses: Dynamis statues do show weakness to Lightning spells, lowering resistance to lightning does produce higher damage results from Black Magic Tier II+ spells. The statues are slow moving and have trouble casting while in motion, so its essential for kiters to stay mobile and away from group to prevent ga spells from falling upon them. Ninja Ni de-buffs also land with fair frequency so wisely used by Ninja kiters to inhibit abilities of statues from reaching full strength.
  • Immune: 100% resistance to any form of Sleep magic.
  • Types:
  • Statues with Red Eyes do not grant any bonus upon defeat.
  • Statues with Blue Eyes restore full HP to those in the area of effect upon defeat.
  • Statues with Green Eyes restore full MP to those in the area of effect upon defeat.

Special Attacks

Special Abilities Original and Zilart Areas Promathia Areas
Seismostomp: AoE damage and Stun, absorbed by Utsusemi
Lead Breath: Cone Attack Weight (Quadav statues only)
Numbing Glare: Gaze Attack Paralysis (Orc statues only)
Tormentful Glare: Gaze Attack Curse (Yagudo statues only)
Torpid Glare: Gaze Attack Sleep (Goblin statues only)
Quadav statues do not cast anything.
Goblin statues cast White Mage spells such as Protectra IV, Haste and Stona
Orc statues cast Red Mage spells such as Thunder III, Bio III, Regen and Gravity.
Yagudo statues are the most dangerous. Avatar Icons cast -ga III nukes, while Manifest Icons cast -ga debuffs such as Dispelga, Paralyga and Sleepga II.
Dynamis statues have a very high TP gain rate, even minimal melee attacks will result in the statue using a TP move, these moves are typically always AOE and cause very high damage to anyone within range. Statues should never be meleed, even in the most dire situations.
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
