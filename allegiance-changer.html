Allegiance Changer

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

You can talk to an Allegiance Changer NPC in your new nation to switch nations.

  • San d'Oria: Beriphaule (Northern San d'Oria C-9)
  • Bastok: Mythily (Metalworks J-8)
  • Windurst: Rakano-Marukano (Heavens Tower)

Cost to change Allegiance when the new nation is in:

  • 1st Place in Conquest - 40,000g
  • 2nd Place in Conquest - 12,000g
  • 3rd Place in Conquest - 4,000g

Notes:

  • The first time you switch to a nation, you have Rank 1 and 0 Conquest Points in that nation.
    • If you return to it later, you get your previous Rank and Conquest Points back.
  • Any rewards from missions done in other nations are retained when you switch (i.e. you will keep your Airship Pass, etc.)
  • All outpost teleports are lost; you will need to redo the supply quests if you return to your previous nation.
  • If you have a mission active from your current nation, you will not be able to change allegiances.
    • If you are Rank 5 in any nation, even if you do not have an active mission, you will not be able to change allegiances. Instead, you will be told your nation is in trouble and your application can not be accepted.
