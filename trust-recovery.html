Trust Combat Recovery

From Zenith XI Wiki

A fallen Trust has a 60-second recovery timer during battle. The installed Recovery v1.1 source clears that timer when the whole party is out of combat.

Status: Recovery v1.1 source and installation record verified; updated in-game behavior not yet confirmed. Details

During battle

Wait up to 60 seconds before summoning the same fallen companion again. You may summon a different Trust if other requirements are met. Dismissing a living Trust during combat to replace it immediately is blocked. Casting and interruption rules still apply. Nothing is summoned automatically.

After battle

The recovery check includes players, Trusts, pets and remaining aggro. Once the whole party is out of combat, you can attempt to summon the fallen companion without waiting out the remaining minute. You do not have to wait for an enemy corpse to disappear.

Killing one enemy while another still has aggro does not end the fight for this check. Party, zone, battlefield and Trust-slot requirements still apply.

Version history

Combat v1.0 kept the full minute even after a fight ended. The 19 September audit found the Recovery v1.1 source and installation record. The updated out-of-combat behavior still needs an in-game check.
