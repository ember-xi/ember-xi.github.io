Zuber

From Zenith XI Wiki

The Marble Bridge Eatery’s attendant.

Location

Upper Jeuno — F-7, Marble Bridge Eatery.

He provides the Marble Bridge Coaster key item, which reports whether you meet the eatery’s entry restriction when entering Upper Jeuno. He also appears in Save the Clock Tower and Dawn (Promathia 8-4).
