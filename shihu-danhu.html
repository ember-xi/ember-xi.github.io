Notes

  • He will warp you for free to an area near Jeuno. The actual destination is random:
    • Ru'Lude Gardens (H-9)
    • Batallia Downs (K-8)
    • Rolanberry Fields (J-6)
    • Sauromugue Champaign (F-5)
    • Qufim Island (I-9)
  • However, if the Astral Candescence is lost, his list expands to include the above and several, less desirable destinations:
    • Batallia Downs (D-7)
    • Rolanberry Fields (J-13)
    • Sauromugue Champaign (L-7)
    • Qufim Island (F-8)


  • This NPC just like the other NPCs of Al Zahbi can be taken prisoner after a Besieged event. He must then be rescued. His guarded cell is H-8 of Arrapago Reef. This can be accomplished by going to the appropriate prison area of the beastmen who captured him, then unlocking him from his prison.
  • Shihu-Danhu's older brother is Kaduru-Haiduru and he will warp you to Ru'Lude Gardens or home city for an Imperial Silver Piece. He is never taken as a prisoner, and can also take you to your home nation after using his service a few times. Using Shihu-Danhu will make you "reek of amateur sorcery," preventing you from using his brother's service for one game day.
