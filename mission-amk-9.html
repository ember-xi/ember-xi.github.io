Zenith availability

The route has been checked against references and available code; completion on Zenith has not yet been play-tested.

Before you begin

  • Complete AMK 8.
  • Map of the Quicksand Caves to follow the assigned markers.
  • Sufficient player weight to open the weight doors. The later-retail Loadstone shortcut is an option only if available on Zenith.

Starting point

Goblin Geologist · Quicksand Caves · J-11

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

Walkthrough

  1. Enter Quicksand Caves through the hidden Western Altepa Desert D-12 entrance and speak to the Goblin Geologist at J-11.
  2. Use your map markers to find the nine assigned ??? sites. Examine each for a stone; track the ones collected because markers may remain visible.
  3. Other marked sections may require the Western Altepa G-5/J-9 or Eastern Altepa H-10/K-7 entrances. Collect Surya, Chandra, Mangala, Budha, Brihaspati, Shukra, Shani, Rahu and Ketu.
  4. Return all nine key items to the geologist for the Navaratna Talisman.
  5. From the geologist's section, pass the I-9 and H-7 weight doors, drop down and head toward D-4 to reach the Chamber of Oracles. Examine the Shimmering Circle for the story scene.

Battle and progress notes

  • Trusts do not substitute for player weight on the standard weight-door route. Stone sites themselves are not behind those doors.

Completion and reward

Navaratna Talisman.
