Borghertz's Calling Hands

Borghertz's Calling Hands
Starting NPCGuslam — Durable Shields, Upper Jeuno (H-8), as Summoner main job
Required FameNone
Level RestrictionLevel 50
Items NeededGrotto Coffer Key (Gusgen Mines) · 1,000 gil for the first AF quest only
Additional RequirementsClass Reunion started · no other Borghertz's Hands quest active
Key ItemsOld gauntlets · Shadow flames
TitleNone
RepeatableYes, see Vingijard in Lower Jeuno after the set is complete
DescriptionGauntlets wrought by Borghertz, the legendary craftsman, lie hidden somewhere in Vana'diel.
Rewards
  • Evoker's Bracers
  • Evoker's Doublet (coffer)
  • Evoker's Pigaches (coffer)

Class Reunion—

Walkthrough

  • Talk to Guslam at the Durable Shields shop, Upper Jeuno (H-8), on Summoner to start the quest.
    • You only need to be Summoner to accept the quest, not to open the gauntlet coffer.
    • Finish any other Borghertz's Hands quest first.
  • Open a coffer in Gusgen Mines and take the Old gauntlets.
    • Use a Grotto Coffer Key, or pick the lock as a Thief. The key also drops from mobs in the zone.
    • Coffers spawn around (H-7), (I-9), (H-8), (G-6), (F-6), (G-10), (F-9), (D-9) and (E-11).
  • Return to Guslam in Upper Jeuno. He cannot repair them.
    • If this is your first artifact gauntlet quest: talk to Deadly Minnow inside the same shop, then Yin Pocanakhu in the Tenshodo area of Lower Jeuno (J-8) and trade 1,000 gil.
  • Go to Port Jeuno and examine the ??? on the crates near the Auction House and the Duty-Free Shop (H-8) for a cutscene.
  • Travel to Castle Zvahl Baileys (F-8) on map 1 and touch one of the torches to spawn Dark Spark.
    • Kill Dark Spark, then touch the same torch again for the Shadow flames.
    • Party members who also need the key item can touch the torch — the fight is done once for everyone.
  • Return to Port Jeuno and examine the ??? again. The cutscene ends the quest and gives you the Evoker's Bracers.
  • Two more pieces are in coffers, on Summoner main job:
    • Evoker's Doublet — a coffer in the Temple of Uggalepih.
    • Evoker's Pigaches — a coffer in Toraimarai Canal.
    • The quest only needs to be active or completed; the coffers are not part of finishing it.

EmberXI changes

  • No Fame requirement.
  • Artifact pieces can be upgraded to +1 by trading them to Brandt in Lower Jeuno with 4 Alexandrite.
