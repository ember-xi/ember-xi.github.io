Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Gives party members within area of effect the effect of "Stoneskin."

Description

Spell cost: 99 MP
Ecosystem: Beastmen
Type: Magical (Earth)
Blue Magic Points: 3
Stat Bonus: VIT+1
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 67

Timers

Casting Time: 7 seconds
Recast Time: 1 minute 30 seconds
Duration: 5 minutes

Notes

  • Self-target only, with AoE effect centered around caster.
  • The formula to calculate the damage reduced seems to be: 2/3 * Blue Magic Skill.
  • Stoneskin and Rampart overwrite Diamondhide.
  • Diamondhide does not overwrite Stoneskin or the Magic Shield effect from Rampart and gives a "has no effect" error.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

None

How to Obtain

Acquired From

Mob Family: Troll
Minimum Blue Mage Level To Acquire: 58 with Magus Jubbah equipped, 61 without Magus Jubbah
Name Level Zone
Troll Gemologist 71-71 Halvung
Troll Shieldbearer 71-73 Bhaflau Thickets
Woodtroll Warrior 72-72 Wajaom Woodlands
Woodtroll Dark Knight 72-72 Wajaom Woodlands

Minimum Setup

  • You will need a 75 job that can solo like Ninja or Red Mage to help.
  • 3 level 66 Blue Mage are capable to safely trio the trolls in Wajaom Woodlands.
