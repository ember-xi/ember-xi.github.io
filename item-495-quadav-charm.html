Mission acquisition and use

Dropped by De’Vyu Headhunter on Beadeaux’s upper level around (I-9). Accept Sattal-Mansal’s Beadeaux requests after Aldo’s Silver Bell, then trade the charm by itself at Lower Jeuno (J-8) for the Coruscant Rosary.
