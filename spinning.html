Spinning

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description: This special weaving ability allows you to work fiber with a spindle, making it easier to process more thread with one crystal.

Obtained: Bought from Clothcraft Guild.

Notes: Key item that is needed to make some items within Clothcrafting.
