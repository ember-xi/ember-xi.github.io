San d'Oria Mission 7-1
MissionPrestige of the Papsque
TypeFight
Start NPCGate Guard (Endracion or Ambrotien in Southern San d'Oria; Grilau in Northern San d'Oria)
LocationWest Ronfaure
RequirementsRank 7
Items NeededNone
Rewards1,000 Rank points

Mission 7-1 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 6-2 Ranperre's Final Rest.

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission from a Gate Guard.
  2. Go to West Ronfaure near Ghelsba. The NM Marauder Dvogzog appears.
  3. Kill it and take the Ancient San d'Orian Tablet.
  4. Return to a Gate Guard.
Mechanics: A level ~60 NM in the starting zone. Easy with five Trusts.
