  Previous Quest:   Hat in Hand
  Next Quest:   None.

Walkthrough

  • Baren-Moren asks you to fetch him some feathers. You need to get three Giant Bird Feathers, which drop from Tabar Beaks and Axe Beaks in the Meriphataud Mountains and Sauromugue Champaign.
  • You must zone out between trades

Game Description

Client: Baren-Moren (Hat Shop, Windurst Waters)
Summary:

Baren-Moren has been requested by a certain lady to make an extra-luxurious hat. He wants your help in collecting the giant bird feathers he needs to finish off his masterpiece.
