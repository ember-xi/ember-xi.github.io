Chocobo Renter

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

These NPCs will offer you the option to ride a chocobo for varying amounts of gil. Some will also allow you to participate in City to City Chocobo Races.

All NPCs will allow you to trade a Chocobo Ticket to ride a Chocobo. However, only the NPCs in the San d'Oria, Bastok, and Windurst will allow you to trade a Chocopass.
