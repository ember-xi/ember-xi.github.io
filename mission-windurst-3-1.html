Before you begin

Be allied with Windurst, have Rank 3, and finish the preceding required story steps. Raise Rank Points if the mission is not offered.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Accept the mission, speak with Kupipi for the Starway Stairway Bauble, then meet Rhy Epocan upstairs in Heavens Tower.
  2. Speak with Hakkuru-Rinkuru at the Port Windurst Orastery (E-7).
  3. Reach the lever door on Castle Oztroja’s first map (I-8). Personally operate the trap lever and fall for the mission scene.
  4. Return to Rhy Epocan.

Important notes

  • Another player triggering the fall does not substitute for your own mission interaction.

Walkthrough


Accept the Mission

  • Accept the Mission (turn in some crystals if they don't offer it immediately).
  • Go to Heavens Tower and talk to Kupipi. She will give you access to the upstairs regions of the Tower.
  • Go through the doors to the left of Kupipi and go all the way to the very top (third) floor.
  • At the top, speak to Rhy Epocan for a cut-scene.
  • [Optional] Several NPC will offer exposition about the event in the cut-scene.
  • [Optional] Foo Beibo states that the Orastery Minister returned to the first floor via the portal.
  • [Optional] The glowing blue light is a portal that will take you to the bottom floor)
  • [Optional] In the Clerical Office, Kupipi mentions that the Orastery Minister may have returned to the Orastery.
  • Go to the Orastery in Port Windurst. Talk to Hakkuru-Rinkuru at E-7.

Castle Oztroja

  • Set your Home Point in Windurst. Close to Heavens Tower is recommended since you'll be returning here.
    • This is a good time to also do the Onion Rings quest.
  • Go out to Castle Oztroja. At level 30, the mobs will mostly be Easy Prey, but Sneak/Invis is still a good idea just because there are so many of them. And it's highly recommended that you bring a Scroll of Instant Warp/Warp Cudgel to get out easily.
  • Go to the door at I-8. You have to pull the 2 levers until a trap door opens beneath you and a cut-scene will begin. You must stand between the 2 levers to trigger the cut-scene and drop down.
    • If the mobs still aggro you here, just be patient and wait for all the Yagudo to face away from you. Then cancel Invisible and hit the lever.
    • NOTE: If you are doing the mission with multiple people, each person will need to hit the lever and drop down in order to trigger the cut-scene.
  • After the cut-scene you'll be in I-8 on this map (the small sliver on the right side).
    • If you're doing the Onion Rings quest, this is where the ??? that you get the ring from is located.
  • Either make your way out of the Castle, or use your Warp if you brought one.

Return to Windurst

  • Go back to Heavens Tower and talk to Rhy Epocan for a cut-scene which completes the Mission.
  • [Optional] Several Sybyl-Guards offer exposition about the Yagudo.

Game Description


Mission Orders
On the command of Semih Lafihna, captain of the Sibyl Guards, you are requested to track down the Minister of the Orastery, Ajido-Marujido, and discover his true intentions. Inquire at the Orastery if you lose track of him.
