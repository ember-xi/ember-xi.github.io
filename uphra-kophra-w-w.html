Notes

Appears, when Elshimo Lowlands is under weekly conquest-control by Windurst.

Notes for characters only with Windurst as current Allegiance
  • Casts Signet
  • Choice of setting Home Point for free
  • Recharges Empress Band or Chariot Band
  • Accepts Elshimo Lowlands Supplies for finishing Supply Quest
  • Accepts Garrison starting item Sheep Leather Missive
Notes for characters only without Windurst as current Allegiance
  • Choice of setting Home Point for a fee - higher prices for higher Allegiance Ranks (Rank 10: 5600 gil)

For further information see Conquest Overseer.
