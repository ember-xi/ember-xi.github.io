Notes

  • This battle has an unusual time limit, only 3 minutes.
  • The Waughroon Kid can gain TP very fast and can kill players fairly fast with a combination of physical attacks and [./https://ffxiclopedia.fandom.com/wiki/Goobbue Goobbue] specials like Big Blow and Counterstance.
