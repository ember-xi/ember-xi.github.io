Argute Pants +1

From Zenith XI Wiki

Legs · SCH level 75 · Relic +1.

Contents
  1. Materials
  2. How to obtain
  3. Sources

DEF 28; HP +17; MP +17; INT +6; Dark Magic skill +7; Enmity -3.

Materials

  • Argute Pants
  • Argute Pants -1 from Dynamis - Tavnazia
  • Ether Leather ×1
  • 1 Byne Bill ×28

How to obtain

  1. Reach Sea / Garden of Antiquity to use Sagheera in Port Jeuno (I-8).
  2. Trade the base armor, damaged -1 armor, crafted material and 28 One Byne Bills together.
  3. Wait until the next Vana'diel midnight, then return for the +1 armor. Only one Sagheera upgrade may be pending.
