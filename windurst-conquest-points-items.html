Windurst Conquest Points Items

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

All of these items are available from War Warlocks in Windurst:

  • Milma-Hapilma, W.W.
  • Puroiko-Maiko, W.W.
  • Harara, W.W.

as well as the Jeunoan Conquest Guards:

  • Morlepiche
  • Emitt
  • Alrauverat
  • Kochahy-Muwachahy

Items that are available when Windurst is in any Conquest Ranking are available from War Warlocks in Foreign nations - items available only when Windurst is in 1st or 2nd are not available from War Warlocks in Foreign nations:

  • Chapal-Afal, W.W.
  • Lexun-Marixun, W.W.

Items that are available only during 1st or 2nd Conquest Ranking are indicated with a "1st" or "1st, 2nd" next to their name.
