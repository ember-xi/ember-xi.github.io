Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A type of Empty, the memories of the souls that lie the mothercrystals. The Empty consist of monsters inside Promyvion and can be considerd fairly powerful with stronger weapon skills than a normal enemy.

General Information

  • System: Empty
  • Weaknesses:
  • Immune:

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Empty Seed: 20' AoE physical damage and Knockback, damage absorbed by Utsusemi
Stray Spawn: Spawns a single Stray monster; goes up when it does this. There is no readying animation or TP required. There is also no text involved.
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
