Spirits Within

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Sword weapon skill

  • Skill level: 175
  • Delivers an unavoidable attack. Damage varies with HP and TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Modifiers: HP (Damage is calculated by TP Multiplier times current HP)
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
4/32 6/32 15/32
  • Special Properties: Unlike normal Weapon Skills, Spirits Within is unaffected by the target's Defense and Evasion, including the abilities Invincible and Perfect Dodge. Some Notorious Monsters may take less than the normal amount of damage.
  • Damage modifiers do not apply in Besieged.
  • Does not interrupt Skillchain .
