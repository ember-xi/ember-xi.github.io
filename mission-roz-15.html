Before you begin

  • Obtain all five Ark Angel shards and advance beyond Ark Angels.

Starting point

Oaken Door · Norg · K-8

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Return to Norg and examine the Oaken Door at K-8 for the mission conversation.
  2. Travel back to Sky and enter The Shrine of Ru’Avitau. Finish the arrival scene to activate The Celestial Nexus.

Battle and progress notes

  • The pinned mission requires the Oaken Door scene before the shrine arrival. Speaking only to Gilgamesh’s normal dialogue does not set that door-scene flag.
  • If you used Divine Might, collect its earring at the shrine Blank Target as a separate quest reward.

Completion and reward

Story progression; no separate item reward.

Walkthrough

  • If you have not received your earring from Divine Might Enter Shrine of Ru'Avitau from H-9 in Ru'Aun Gardens. The mission log should change.
  • Now go to Norg and talk to Gilgamesh.
  • Then go to Lower Jeuno and talk to Aldo in Tenshodo HQ J-8 for a cutscene.
  • Go back to Ru'Aun Gardens.
  • Enter Shrine of Ru'Avitau again from the (H-8) entrance for a cutscene with Lion.

Game Description

Mission Orders
The door deep within the Shrine of Ru'Avitau is sealed with some form of supernatural power. Could this be the work of Eald'narche?
