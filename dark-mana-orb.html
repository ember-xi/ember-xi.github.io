Dark Mana Orb

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description: A Mana Orb that the Manustery is in Charge of maintaining. It hasn't been filled with magic, so it doesn't glow.

Used In Mission: Windurst Mission 1-2

Notes:

  • Windurst Mission 1-2: There are six total Dark Mana Orbs,

named "First dark Mana Orb", "Second dark Mana Orb", etc. Each has the same description and is used in the same mission. These are lost upon their usages in the Mission..
