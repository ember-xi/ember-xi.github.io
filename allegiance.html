Allegiance

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Allegiance is the nation that you are currently loyal to in order to participate in Missions. Allegiance is also known as Home Nation.

  • There are three nations that players may assign an Allegiance with: Bastok, Windurst, and San d'Oria.
  • Upon starting a new character, the home nation selected is also the allegiance that is assigned.
  • Players have the ability of changing Allegiance by speaking with an Allegiance Changer.
  • Upon switching allegiance, the player's completed supply quests will be erased causing them to lose all Outpost Teleportation areas. They are not regained by switching back.
  • Conquest Points are not erased when switching allegiance, they are retained should the player decide to switch back.
