Zenith availability

The pinned quest marks Orcish Bloodletter as awaiting verification/implementation and deliberately assigns an excessively high level. Treat the fight as an unresolved base-server issue; a normal level-75 clear is not promised.

Before you begin

  • Complete Bonds That Never Die, leave the zone and wait until the next Vana’diel day.
  • Complete The Will of the World so Fate in Haze is active or later.
  • Bring a fishing rod, a Flint Stone and inventory room for bait.

Starting point

Rholont · Southern San d'Oria (S) · E-7

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Rholont, then Daigraffeaux at I-11. Enter Beaucedine Glacier (S) for the arrival scene.
  2. Examine Colossal Footprint behind the I-7 tower. At the G-10 lake, examine Rocky Perch repeatedly for several Goliath Worms.
  3. Equip your rod and Goliath Worm bait. Fish up the key items: Paladin Lobster at G-10, Scutum Crab at J-7 and Lance Fish at H-9.
  4. Return to Colossal Footprint. Trade the Flint Stone to Charred Firewood immediately north of the I-7 tower, two terrain levels above it.
  5. Examine Compressed Snow by the H-10 tower for a scene, then again to spawn Orcish Bloodletter. Defeat it and re-examine the snow for the ending.

Battle and progress notes

  • The quest fishing targets are key items. Extra worms cover unsuccessful attempts.

Completion and reward

Icarus Wing.

What this opens

  • Blood of Heroes
