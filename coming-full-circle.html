Coming Full Circle

From Zenith XI Wiki

Finish the level-75 Mythic and close the imperial quest chain.

Contents
  1. Requirements
  2. Steps
  3. Sources

Requirements

Forging a New Myth cleared; Serpentking Zahak relief shard and the sealed Mythic weapon.

Steps

  1. Return to Seaprince's Tombstone in Caedarva Mire (E-10). Examine it with the Mythic equipped for the scene.
  2. Unequip and trade the sealed weapon to the tombstone. Receive the awakened level-75 weapon.
  3. Keep the awakened weapon in your main inventory. Unequip weapons and examine the Imperial Whitegate palace door for the next scene.
  4. Return to Salaheem’s Sentinels and approach Naja Salaheem to finish. Receive three Imperial Silver Pieces.

This is the end of the level-75 weapon route. The separate Unlocking a Myth quest is how you permanently use its weapon skill with other suitable weapons.

Zenith status: original level-75 reference route. The complete weapon-building quest chain has not been verified as playable on Zenith. Do not assume that having these wiki pages means the NPC hand-ins or final battlefield are installed.
