Widescan

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Scans the area for monsters, revealing them on the map.
  • Job Traits are always active.
  • Further Notes:
    • Each upgrade of this trait increases the area of the scan.
    • The upgrades are unofficial and are not reflected in your abilities menu.
    • See the Wide Scan Guide for additional information.

Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • None

Widescan I

  • Range: 50'
  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 1

Widescan II

  • Range: 150'

  • Obtained: Beastmaster Level 20
