Notes

Notes for characters only with Windurst as current Allegiance
  • Casts Signet
  • Recharges Emperor Band, Empress Band or Chariot Band
  • Accepts traded Crystals for filling up the Windurst Mission-Rank bar (the red bar at the character's profile)
  • Sells items for Conquest Points (Items for Windurst) at certain conditions. It will be also possible to get some items of other Nations (Items for Bastok / Items for San d'Oria) at the guards, if your current Allegiance ranks higher at the weekly Conquest results (for further information see under the items articles)
  • Starts Supply Run Missions and offers a list of already delivered supplies
  • Starts an Expeditionary Force by giving an E.F. region insignia to you
  • Explains the Conquest system by choosing some available questions.
Notes for characters only without Windurst as current Allegiance
  • Doesn't offer any function

For further information see Conquest Overseer.
