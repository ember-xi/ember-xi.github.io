Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

File:Defense Down (Status Effect).png Defense Down is a harmful status effect that disorients players until it wears off or is removed. Afflicted characters experience reduced Defense, sometimes significantly reduced (by a couple hundred points). Defense Down is a wind-based effect.

How to remove the effect

  • The White Magic spell Erase will remove this effect.
  • The Blue Magic spell Cocoon may overwrite Defense Down with its own Defense Boost.
  • The Paladin ability Rampart may overwrite Defense Down with its own Defense Boost.
