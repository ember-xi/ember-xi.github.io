Nightmare

Name Location Notes
Vanguard Eye Appears on 2nd and 3rd floor, in the inner area. Spawns Hydra Corps on 2nd floor, and Dark Kindred & Taurus on 3rd floor.
Nightmare Cluster Appears on the 3rd floor, on the downward ramp. Spawns 2 clones when aggroed.
Nightmare Hornet Spawns in each hallway with two clones between the ring and the inner area. Uses Frenzy Pollen which speeds up its attacks similar to Hundred Fists.
Nightmare Leech North side of 2nd floor, on the inner downward ramp. Spawns 2-3 clones when aggroed.
Nightmare Makara South side of 2nd floor, on the inner downward ramp. Spawns 2-3 clones when aggroed.
Nightmare Taurus Spawns on 3rd floor from Vanguard Eyes. Drops various Hydra Armor equipment.
Nightmare Antlion Hidden in 1 of 4 locations on the 3rd floor ring. NM - Grants 30 minute time extension, trigger to remove SJ Restriction.
Nightmare Bugard Spawns at Phomiuna Aqueducts entrance. NM - Grants 15 minute time extension.
Nightmare Worm Hidden in 1 of 5 locations on the 2nd floor ring. NM - Trigger to remove SJ Restriction.
SJ Restriction NMs can be killed in ANY order.

Notes

It is important to get the entire group inside as fast as possible upon activating the hourglass due to the 15 minute starting time limit.

The level 70 Hydra armor pieces drop from Nightmare Tauri.
Level 74 relic armor from any Hydra on the 2nd floor. Unlike Hydra in Dynamis - Beaucedine, these can actually be slept.
Level 75 relic armor drops from any Kindred on the 3rd floor.
Relic body armor -1 drops from Nightmare Leeches on the 2nd floor.
Relic body armor -1 drops from Nightmare Clusters on the 3rd floor.
Relic leg armor -1 drops from Nightmare Hornets on either floor, and Nightmare Makara on the 2nd floor.

Both the Nightmare Worm and Nightmare Antlion must be defeated to unlock support jobs.

Megaboss: If one Umbral Diabolos is aggro'd during the run through the tunnels, pulling the mega boss at the end of the NPC's hallway will NOT cause the other Umbral Diabolos npc's to despawn. All alliance members must sneak by the NPCs undetected to fight the boss with only one Diabolos suit popped.
