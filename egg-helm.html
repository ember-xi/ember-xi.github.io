Chocobo Digging Bonus

  • If worn while digging, you will find all sorts of egg related items. The possible finds include: Sairui-Ran, Bird Egg, Hard-Boiled Egg, Soft-Boiled Egg, and Colored Egg.
  • Allows you to dig beyond the 100 item fatigue limit, but the only items you will dig up after this point are the egg related items at a very low success rate. Egg items obtained before the 100 item limit count towards the fatigue limit as normal.
