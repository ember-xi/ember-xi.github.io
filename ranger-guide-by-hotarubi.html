Abilities!

Job Abilities

Eagle Eye Shot

This is Ranger's 2-hour job special ability and one of the most straightforward in the game. It instantly deals a huge spike of physical damage to a single target based on your ranged weapon and ranged attack parameters. This attack can and will miss its target. It generates the same amount of TP as any other ranged attack. EES makes for a pretty good "oh, shit!" button in dire party situations where the tank is about to die and you want to change the monster's attention to you with its high enmity generation (or just get the mob dead faster). Other than unplanned usage, it fits well into huge burst combos for battlefield or mission fights like Meditate -> Meditate -> Slug -> Barrage -> EES -> Slug -> Icarus Wing -> Slug which is pretty much how your Maat fight will end in 20 seconds.

Sharpshot (5)

Can be used every 5 minutes for a temporary 1-minute ranged accuracy boost of +40. Sharpshot is mostly just going to be used in conjunction with Barrage (explained a few paragraphs down) since they share the exact same cooldown and have great synergy. Other than as a combo with barrage, the +40 ranged accuracy can be nice. Later on in end game once you optimize your builds to maximize accuracy, the +40 accuracy will have little effect so if you want to be a lil' cheeky you can add a conditional line in Ashitacast to swap out 40 points worth of R.accuracy pieces for STR and R.attack pieces while Sharpshot is active (for non-barrage shots) and the effects of the ability can be felt a bit more as a raw damage increase. Think of it as a bit of an aftermath effect on your barrages 8) Ashitacast below... the elseif is very important if you keep the accuracy during Barrage.

<midranged>
    <if buffactive="Barrage"> <equip set="barrageSet" /> </if>
    <elseif buffactive="Sharpshot"> <equip set="minus40AccSet" /> </elseif>
</midranged>


Scavenge (10)

Scavenge returns a percentage of the ammunition you've used since you've either (1) last changed zones, (2) last used Scavenge or (3) last swapped your ammunition. When you first acquire this ability, it will refund 5% of ammunition used and at level 75 it will refund 37%. The scaling on the levels in between is linear; you can simply divide your job level by 2 (i.e. check your subjob level) to see what the refund rate is. Scavenge Effect can be merited for an additional 25% increase and Hunter's Socks can be macro'd or Ashitacasted in for an additional 5% increase, making the maximum return on this ability an impressive 67%, cutting the cost on ammo down to just one-third at level 75. Just remember to Scavenge frequently -- you get no returns if you forget to use it!

Camouflage (20)

This ability turns you invisible for a minute or two with a five-minute cooldown. There's not much to say about Camouflage in a world with easy access to infinite uptime invisibilities via Prism Powders and Tonko; I guess it might save you a buck here or there. The only notable advantage over other invisibility sources is that the application for Camouflage is instantaneous so there might be a very rare situation where cutting that half-second will save you in a pinch if you have the quick draw on your ctrl+J shortcut since no sane person is going to make a macro for this thing.

Accuracy Bonus (10/30/50/70)

This one is a... wait for it... bonus to your accuracy. At levels 10/30/50/70 you will get +10/+22/+35/+48 melee accuracy and ranged accuracy. The melee accuracy part is what makes Ranger a contender as a subjob on melee damage-dealing jobs in the early 20's for EXP. The ranged accuracy is quite nice and what I would call an absolute necessity to make the job work via more reliable Slug Shots and Sidewinders (which have accuracy penalties). The melee accuracy is not really used although it might catch you a bit of extra TP here and there on your dagger/axe hits, who knows. Its only real application is (was?) for Ranger builds that used multi-hit melee weapons (Kraken Club or Mercurial Kris) to quickly gain TP for Slug Shot/Sidewinder spam which I believe are mostly obsolete due to the distance penalty being introduced into Eden -- more on that at in the Formulas section at the end of the article.
