Moghancement: Gardening

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is one of many Moghancements that can be obtained by placing certain Furnishings inside of the Mog House. Moghancement:Gardening reduces the chance of plants withering and dying whilst Gardening. Placing multiple furnishings with this effect will increase it's strength, but by how much is currently unknown.

The effect can be obtained via the following Furnishings:

  • Bastokan Tree
  • Beverage Barrel
  • Bureau
  • Caisson
  • Elshimo Palm
  • Freshwater Aquarium
  • Kadomatsu
  • Maple Table
  • Oak Table
  • San d'Orian Tree
  • Scimitar Cactus
  • Tarutaru Desk
  • Water Cask
  • Workbench
  • Windurstian Tree
