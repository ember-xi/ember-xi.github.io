Why a Food Guide?

File:Food icon.png

Food in FFXI is a confusing thing, and using the wrong food is one of the most common mistakes made by new and old players alike. This guide aims to make your food choices simpler by explaining how food works, what foods you should carry around, and point out common trap foods that FFXI players sometimes use.

How does Food work?

Food typically has percentage based modifiers attached to it, for example Rice Dumpling has Attack+20%. Percentage based modifiers take your flat Attack value (Attack from Gear, Traits, Minuet, anything that is a flat number) and multiplies it by that percentage. What this means is that Chaos Roll, Berserk, Warcry, Last Resort, or any other percentage based attack modifier will not interact at all with your food. The formula for food math is:

Food Attack Bonus = (Base Attack + Gear + Minuet) x Food Attack Modifier

Using Rice Dumpling (+20% Attack) for example

Food Attack Bonus = (75 + 10 + 15) x 1.20% = (100 x 1.20%) = 120 Attack Total, with 20 gained from the food.

Given Rice Dumplings max out at 45 gained attack, we now know we are gaining 20 attack, and still have room to gain 25 more attack before cap.

Low Level Physical DPS

TLDR: Rice Dumplings are the go-to best food you can use for its high attack percentage, bonus accuracy, and if applicable ranged attack. For those on a budget Sausage is almost as good and purchasable from a vendor in Bastok for only 140g each. If you're specifically using ranged attacks then Flounder Meuniere can provide a mix of Ranged Accuracy and Ranged Attack that can compete with Rice Dumplings at lower level ranges.

Rice Dumpling: Caps at 225 base Attack and 150 base Ranged Attack. This food offers a great mix of Attack, Ranged Attack, and Accuracy which pairs very well with low level play. It's relatively expensive for low level food, but is basically unbeatable in quality. Since it caps higher in attack/ratk you can use this food for longer. This starts maxing out in the mid to late 30s depending on job.

Sausage: Caps at 111 base attack and ranged attack. Sausage is bought from an NPC in Bastok for 140g each, depending on conquest results. It has very high Attack+% but a very low maximum attack of 30. This is absolutely amazing starter food for new players on a budget that still want to perform. The big differentiation between this and Meat Jerky is it gives ranged attack.

Meat Jerky: Caps at 137 base attack. This is another very cheap NPC bought food, and every nation has access to it for around 108g. It's weaker than Sausage just straight up, but is at least pretty close in power level as long as you don't use ranged attacks.

Flounder Meuniere: Caps at 178 base ranged attack. A good option for specifically ranged attack users, such as arrow burns or rangers in general. Given the very low attack bonus, this does heavily lose out in bonus ranged attack to Rice Dumplings, but the +7.5% hit rate with ranged weapons is pretty powerful.

High Level Physical DPS

TLDR: Sushi has entered the chat, starting around level 40, sushi begins to offer significant accuracy bonuses and become viable. The general rule of thumb is if your pre-food accuracy is below 75%, use sushi, if not use a Meat food to buff your attack. I would always recommend running a parser program to monitor your accuracy and adjust food on the fly.

Sole Sushi, Squid Sushi, Bream Sushi, Crab Sushi: All sushi products give relatively the same amount of accuracy, except Crab Sushi, and the differentiation between these are what base stat modifiers they give and how expensive they are.

  • Sole Sushi is the most expensive, and gives STR which is generally the most useful secondary stat.
  • Squid Sushi is less expensive than Sole, and gives AGI.
  • Bream Sushi is less expensive than Squid, and gives VIT. Bream also gives a very small amount more accuracy than Sole/Squid.
  • Crab Sushi is significantly less expensive than Bream, but gives less accuracy and barely any other stats.

Meat Mithkabobs: Caps at 272 base attack. This is the staple attack food of FFXI, it's very affordable and still gives a respectable amount of attack. This food is very useful for fighting Colibri that steal your food, to use while farming gil, or really any event where performance doesn't matter that much but you still want to use food.

Coeurl Subs: Caps at 373 base attack/ranged attack. Coeurl Sub is my staple food of choice, it offers a good mix of both a medium attack percentage and medium attack cap, making it relatively easy for any job to get max value out of a Coeurl Sub. The main issue of this food is the medium cap means that certain jobs, especially well geared 2h jobs in their WS gear, can have just too much attack for this food and be way over the cap. The high quality version of this food helps with that, and in most circumstances Coeurl Sub +1 is going to be better than than say a Bison Steak because of the higher percentage attack gained.

Yellow Curry: Caps at 357 base attack/ranged attack. As of 2025 this item has been updated and launched into meta relevance. It now has different stats depending on how many party members you have. While solo, this food is basically as strong as a Coeurl Sub, a good food but nothing to stand out from others. In a group this food moves to Attack+22% and Max 85, which confidently moves it ahead of most attack foods in the game. The high +22% means that most 2h DD jobs can easily cap out at 85 attack on this food, which confidently beats other options like Coeurl Sub +1 and Bison Steak. I personally think this should be the new staple food 3h food of Eden and will carry this around at all times now.

Carbonara: Caps at 361 base attack. Carbonara is a sleeper overpowered food, it still gives an alright amount of attack but has two extra stats on it that make it very powerful. Store TP+6 allows many jobs, when properly min/maxed, to get to 1000 TP faster thus increasing their damage a ton. HP+14% gives you a huge amount of extra HP padding so you don't hit the ground as often, a dead DD does no damage and Carbonara helps keep you alive. One thing worth thinking about is many jobs can gain significant Attack out of using Carbonara by swapping their gear around with the bonus Store TP, this could make Carbonara actually give more attack than competitor food.

Red Curry: Caps at 653 attack/ranged attack. This is the king of attack food, and commands a kingly price because of it. It provides the highest Attack+% of any good food, so it will generally always be the highest amount of attack possible. Given the max attack on this food is so high, you likely won't ever come close to actually capping out on the 150 attack bonus. This food scales very well if you are receiving external attack buffs like Valor Minuets.

Niche Foods

Be careful with these, these are relatively expensive foods that in many situations are not great and will just make you spend more gil than necessary for the same results.

Hydra Kofte: Caps at 750 attack/ranged attack. This is another expensive attack food with a high cap but medium percentage. The 150 Attack Cap on this food looks super enticing, much like Red Curry, but it only giving +20% Attack means you will once again be hard pressed to make this item great. You do get a nice little Defense Bonus out of this food, which gives it a niche as a hybrid defense/offense food. However at 80k for each Kofte on Eden currently, it's hard to say this food is really worth the cost.

Hedgehog Pie: Caps at 500 attack/ranged attack. +18% attack is just a horrible percentage, requiring massive amounts of base attack to fuel this food. This food is similar to Bison Steak/Marbled Steak, but significantly more expensive at around 27k per. For that up charge you get 5 accuracy (+2.5% hit rate) and 55 HP. Personally I don't see high value in this item, but if you are getting double Minuets, can use accuracy, and want a food a small bit better than Marbled Steak at a significant mark up, I can see a niche use for it.

Marbled Steak: Caps at 528 attack/ranged attack. +18% attack is just a horrible percentage, requiring massive amounts of base attack to fuel this food. I would only use it when you have double Minuets and are a well geared, maybe even relic weapon geared, 2 handed DD job. If you don't have Minuets, you won't even be able to get any extra attack out of this over Bison Steak so it truly is worthless for that. If you do have double Minuets, this is more than likely always giving more attack than Yellow Curry, so it isn't bad for that.

What Shouldn't I Eat as a Physical DPS

Jack O Lantern. Don't do it. The Accuracy+10 on this item will always provide exactly +5% hit rate, which is just not anywhere close to as good as using the other low level foods listed. Most Meat foods will make you hit nearly 50% harder per swing at low levels, which is significantly better than hitting 5% more of the time.

Hellsteak: Caps at 806 attack/ranged attack. It's just way too much attack necessary to make use of this thing, it's all the same drawbacks of Bison Steak but costs an astronomical 60k per steak. The only situation this item really makes sense is some sort of Soul Voice Minuetx2 zerg, but also do you even need more attack to cap in that situation? Take into consideration that most jobs will be sitting in the low 400 attack in their TP set.

Bison Steak: Caps at 500 attack/ranged attack. +18% attack is just a horrible percentage, requiring massive amounts of base attack to fuel this food. I would only use it when you have double Minuets and are a well geared, maybe even relic weapon geared, 2 handed DD job. Yellow Curry will essentially never lose to this food without Minuets.

Drinks

Drinks are an entirely different buff and can be used alongside food. Useful for any job that has mp and doesn't have access to refresh. To make maximum use of drinks in a party you'll have to get cooking to at least 30 for Pineapple Juice. Juices don't stack, but the materials do

Pineapple Juice: 30 cooking to craft. 1mp/tick for 4 minutes (80mp total). Costs ~120g each

Melon Juice: 40 cooking. 2mp/tick for 2:15 (90 total). Costs ~600g each

Yagudo Drink: 60 cooking. 2mp/tick for 3:00 (120 total). Costs ~1000g each

Observations

Food is very valuable. Aside from your weapon, food is often the single biggest contribution to the party. Always eat something, even if it's just a sausage or kabob.

At low levels sushi's % based approach offers almost no bonus. Eat attack food for maximum output(It's cheaper too).

Carbonara can be super powerful for jobs where the Store tp takes a swing off your attack rounds.

Red Curry gives more attack then Hedgehog Pie starting at 396 base attack.

Meat Mithkabobs have a IRL and in game shirt because they are dope.
