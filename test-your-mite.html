Notes

  • To enter the battlefield, talk to Ghebi Damomohe in Lower Jeuno (Merchant for the Neptune's Spire at I-7).
  • Obtain a Florid Stone from the mobs in Pso'Xja.
  • Trade the Florid Stone to Ghebi Damomohe; if you have reached Chains of Promathia mission, chapter 3-5 or above, and received access into The Shrouded Maw, she will give you the Astral Covenant key item.
  • Proceed through the level 40 capped section of Pso'Xja to The Shrouded Maw.
  • You will have the option of entering the ENM battle at the battlefield.
  • Pasuk uses Additional Effect: Absorb TP, and a breath attack that does Gravity and Slow.
  • Pasuk also casts the spell Drain.
  • Pasuk is immune to Charm.
  • Pasuk's conic slow + gravity attack (Viscid Secretion) will overwrite haste. Bringing a white mage with erase is recommended.
  • 2 NIN's along with 1 or 2 WHM's can win reasonably reliably.
  • RDM, WHM, MNK, DRK - Got it to about 70% life. I'd say it may be immune to Silence.
