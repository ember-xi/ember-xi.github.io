400 Rank points

Mission 5-1 of the San d'Oria storyline. Comes after 4-1 Magicite.

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Halver in Chateau d'Oraguille for the New Fei'Yin Seal.
  2. Go to Fei'Yin (from Beaucedine Glacier) and walk to the end of the temple: Qu'Bia Arena. Enter the Battlefield (Rank 5 Mission) and beat the guardians.
  3. After the win the seal becomes the Burnt Seal.
  4. Return to Halver and hand in the Burnt Seal.
Mechanics: Trusts are allowed in the Battlefield on EmberXI. Fei'Yin monsters are level 40-50 (Ghosts and Dolls).
