Before you begin

  • Complete Another World.
  • One Lilac.

Starting point

Weathered Gravestone · Batallia Downs · I-10

Walkthrough

  1. Obtain one Lilac and reach the Weathered Gravestone at (I-10) in present-day Batallia Downs.
  2. Examine the gravestone for the scene, then trade the Lilac to it for the next scene. The flower is consumed.

Battle and progress notes

  • The pinned core has an incomplete main-story battle at mission 14; later story handlers do not establish that this chapter is currently reachable on Zenith.

Story progression.
