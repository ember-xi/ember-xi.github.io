Quests of Note

  • If you cannot access a specific quest, repeat quest Mandragora-Mad to level up fame.
  • See Quick Reputation Guide for alternative options to level fame.
  • Fame can be checked at NPC Zabirego-Hajigo (F-10) Windurst Waters North.
  • See Windurst Reputation for determining what level your fame is at.
