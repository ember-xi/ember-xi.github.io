Poseaulloie

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Special Event NPC

Location: Patrols set route in Northern San d'Oria I-8 > J-8 > I-9 > J-9 through the Parade Ground shown on the map, north of the exit to South San d'Oria.

Notes

  • One of The Dark Lilies of the Harvest Festival event held each October since 2004. Follow Poseaulloie without being seen to receive, from the Exorcist, a Witch Hat or Coven Hat.
  • Thought to be the Dark Kindred's Shadowhand from the Crystal War when the The Dark Lilies were enslaved by the Shadow Lord.

Involved in Events

  • Harvest Festival 2005-present
