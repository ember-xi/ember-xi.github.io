Quests of Note

  • If you cannot access a specific quest, repeat quest Only the Best to level up fame.
  • See Quick Reputation Guide for alternative options to level fame.
  • Fame can be checked at NPC Namonutice (K-6) Southern San d'Oria.
  • See San d'Oria Reputation for determining what level your fame is at.
