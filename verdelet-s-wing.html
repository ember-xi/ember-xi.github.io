Statistics

Verdelet's wing
The detached, fleshy wing of the imp,
Verdelet. Either by some latent magic, or
simply remaining nerves, it twitches
periodically.

Other Uses

Resale Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.
ZNM: Trade to Sanraku to obtain an Amber Seal.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
