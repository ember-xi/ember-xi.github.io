Survival Guides

From Zenith XI Wiki

Ordinary Survival Guide destinations are available from the start under Travel v1.1.

Fees and requirements

The base fee is 1,000 gil or 50 Tabs before original discounts. The free tutorial trip follows its own conditions. Special regions retain access requirements.

Tutorial visits

You must still examine the requested city and field books in the tutorial’s order. Automatic destination access does not complete that training step. See Tutorial Quests.
