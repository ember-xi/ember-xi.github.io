Before you begin

  • Complete ACP 2.
  • Access to the raised ledges through Beaucedine Glacier, Crawlers' Nest and Garlaige Citadel.

Starting point

??? · Qufim Island · G-6

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

Walkthrough

  1. Defeat Goblins in Batallia Downs until you receive the bowl of bland Goblin salad key item. Reach the raised F-5 ledge through Beaucedine Glacier, examine its ??? and defeat Vegnix Greenthumb for Seedspall Roseum.
  2. Defeat Goblins in Rolanberry Fields for the jug of greasy Goblin juice. Reach the raised D-11 ledge through Crawlers' Nest, examine its ??? and defeat Chuglix Berrypaws for Seedspall Caerulum.
  3. Defeat Goblins in Sauromugue Champaign for the chunk of smoked Goblin grub. Reach the I-9/J-10 cliff through Garlaige Citadel, examine its ??? and defeat Dribblix Greasemaw for Seedspall Viridis.
  4. Each participant seeking credit must hold the corresponding food key item when that NM dies. After obtaining all three seedspall key items, return to the Qufim G-6 ??? for the scene.
