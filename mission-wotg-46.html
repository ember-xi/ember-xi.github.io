Zenith availability

Reference walkthrough: the pinned server core contains story scenes but leaves the When Wills Collide battlefield in Walk of Echoes unfinished or unconnected. This is not confirmed playable on Zenith.

Before you begin

  • Complete Time Slips Away.
  • Carry the Bottled punch bug awarded by Time Slips Away.

Starting point

Veridical Conflux · Grauberg [S] · F-5

Era note: Later WotG chapter, released after the level cap began rising above 75. Included for story continuity; this guide does not claim a tested level-75 balance.

Walkthrough

  1. Examine the Veridical Conflux (F-5) to enter the mission section of Walk of Echoes.
  2. Go north up the stairs and examine the Ornate Door for a scene. Examine it again with the Bottled punch bug to enter the battle.
  3. Defeat Portia, Ragelise, Larzos and the Fourth Spitewarden. Finish the victory scene.
  4. If the entry item is lost, trade one Punch Bug to the Veridical Conflux in Grauberg [S] to receive another Bottled punch bug.

Battle and progress notes

  • The pinned core has an incomplete main-story battle at mission 14; later story handlers do not establish that this chapter is currently reachable on Zenith.

Completion and reward

Vanquisher of Spite title.
