Other maps:

These maps are all obtained in various unusual ways that do not fall into one of the above categories.

Map of Norg

Complete Zilart mission 1, The New Frontier

Map of Promyvion - Holla

1. Obtain Teal Memosphere in Promyvion - Holla (low drop rate from Thinker, high drop rate from NM Cerebrator).

2. Trade Teal Memosphere to appropriate ??? on third or fourth floor. Note that there are two third floors depending on which memory receptacle you use on the second floor. One has a map ??? spawn point, one does not.

Map of Promyvion - Dem

1. Obtain Beryl Memosphere in Promyvion - Dem (low drop rate from Gorger, high drop rate from NM Satiator).

2. Trade Beryl Memosphere to appropriate ??? on third or fourth floor. Note that there are two third floors depending on which memory receptacle you use on the second floor. One has a map ??? spawn point, one does not.

Map of Promyvion - Mea

1. Obtain Indigo Memosphere in Promyvion - Mea (low drop rate from Craver, high drop rate from NM Coveter).

2. Trade Indigo Memosphere to appropriate ??? on third or fourth floor. Note that there are two third floors depending on which memory receptacle you use on the second floor. One has a map ??? spawn point, one does not.

Map of Promyvion - Vahzl

1. Obtain White Memosphere in Promyvion - Vahzl (low drop rate from Thinker, Gorger, Craver; high drop rate from NMs Deviator, Provoker, Wailer).

2. Trade White Memosphere to appropriate ??? on third or fifth floor.

Map of the Aqueducts

Check correct ??? in one of the four Phomiuna Aqueducts storage rooms (F-7, F-9, J-7, J-9, on the third map).

Map of Al'Taieu

Talk to all 20 of the Quasilumin NPCs in Al'Taieu, then talk to any one of them again.

Map of Hu'Xzoi

Talk to all 10 of the Quasilumin NPCs in the Grand Palace of Hu'Xzoi, then talk to any one of them again.

Map of Ru'Hmet

Talk to all 10 of the Quasilumin NPCs in the Garden of Ru'Hmet, then talk to any one of them again.

Map of Dio. Abdhaljs-Ghelsba

Enter Diorama Abdhaljs-Ghelsba for the first time, and you will receive the map after zoning in.

Map of Abdh. Isle-Purgonorgo

Enter Abdhaljs Isle-Purgonorgo for the first time, and you will receive the map after zoning in.

Map of Mamook

Purchase for 1000 Imperial Standing in Aht Urhgan.

Map of Halvung

Purchase for 1000 Imperial Standing in Aht Urhgan.

Map of Arrapago Reef

Purchase for 1000 Imperial Standing in Aht Urhgan.

Map of Alzadaal Ruins

Trade three Imperial Mythril Pieces to the NPC at Bhaflau Thickets (F-6) who allows entry to Alzadaal.

Map of the Chocobo Circuit

Make your way to the center of the Chocobo Circuit, click ??? on central desk, choose to take a map.
