Fletching

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description:This special weaving ability allows you to sew feathers with zephyr thread, making it easier to process more fletching with one crystal.

Obtained :Purchased from Clothcraft Guild.

Notes: Key item that is needed to make some items within Clothcrafting.

Needed for Recipes

  • Chocobo Fletchings - Clothcraft (2)
  • Yagudo Fletchings - Clothcraft (22)
  • Bird Fletchings - Clothcraft (42)
  • Insect Fletchings - Clothcraft (52)
  • Black Chocobo Fletchings - Clothcraft (72)
  • Giant Bird Fletchings - Clothcraft (93)
