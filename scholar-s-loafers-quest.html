Before you start

SCH 50+; Downward Helix complete; choose Loafers from Loussaire in Bastok Markets (S) (G-10).

Steps

  1. In Fort Karugo-Narugo (S), search outdoor map 1 for Indescript Markings at (I-9), (I-10), (H-8), (G-7), (H-5) or (G-5). Check the active spot for Rafflesia Dreamspit; it moves after collection.
  2. In Meriphataud Mountains (S), check the markings at southwest (E-7), on the south side of Drogaroga's Spine, for Drogarogan Bonemeal. Historical appearance window: 07:00–17:00 game time.
  3. Return to Loussaire on Scholar with a free inventory slot. Checking these markings removes Sneak; renew protection before moving on.

Reward

Scholar's Loafers (SCH 54).
