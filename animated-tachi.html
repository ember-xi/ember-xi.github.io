Notes:

  • When pulled, the Animated Tachi spawns 4 Satellite Tachi which assist it during the fight.
  • After a short period of time has passed, the Animated Tachi will cast Warp and despawn. The only way to prevent this is by attacking it with the stage 4 relic Great Katana: Totsukanotsurugi.
  • The Animated Tachi can use the weapon skill Tachi: Kaiten.
  • Divine Fragment drops 100% of the time if the 15 Demon Notorious Monsters in Dynamis - Xarcabard have already been defeated and it will not drop the item if they have not all been defeated.
  • Immune to: Stun, Sleep, Lullaby
