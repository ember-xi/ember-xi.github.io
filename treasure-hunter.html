Job Trait Overview

  • Game Description: Increases chance that treasure will be dropped by enemies.
  • Job Traits are always active.


Equipment that Enhances this Trait

  • Thief's Knife
  • Assassin's Armlets
  • Assassin's Armlets +1

Treasure Hunter

  • Obtained: Thief Level 15

Treasure Hunter II

  • Obtained: Thief Level 45


How Treasure Hunter Works

In 2020, SE finally released information explaining how Treasure Hunter works. All items are placed into one of 7 categories, ranging from 'Very Common' to 'Ultra Rare'. Each category has a drop percentage associated and Treasure Hunter increases the amount for each category, as per the table below.

TH Level Very Common Common UnCommon Rare Very Rare Super Rare Ultra Rare
0 24% 15% 10% 5% 1% 0.5% 0.1%
1 48% 30% 12% 6% 1.5% 0.75% 0.2%
2 56% 40% 15% 7% 2% 1% 0.3%
3 60% 42.5% 16.5% 7.5% 2.25% 1.2% 0.35%
4 64% 45% 18% 8% 2.5% 1.4% 0.4%

For further reading, please see [1], halfway down the page.

Note

  • It's widely regarded that a player needs to be on a mobs hate list for Treasure Hunter to be active.


Further Reading

Just Released 03NOV2007 from the Altana Festival in Osaka:

Q: Do multiple instances of Treasure Hunter in a single party increase the chances of a drop? When exactly does the effect of Treasure Hunter take place?

A: When more than one member has Treasure Hunter, the player with the strongest version will have theirs take effect for the party. The effect comes into play as soon as the Thief generates enmity from the target enemy. Even if the Thief dies, the effect remains.

Myths & Needed Testing

There are many myths regarding Treasure Hunter. Here is a list of some of them. If these were proven to be true then this page should be edited to reflect that.

  • Despite rumors to the contrary, so long as a thief is in the party, and in the same zone, Treasure Hunter is always active for all mob kills, regardless of who lands the killing blow. This has been revealed in many interviews with SE representatives. No action, or lack of action, on the part of the thief will affect this trait, short of equipping gear that Enhances "Treasure Hunter" effect.
  • There are rumors that a Four-Leaf Mandragora Bud, Wild Rabbit Tail and Lucky Egg all enhance "Treasure Hunter". These are unconfirmed in large scale tests, however smaller tests do seem to show positive results. Verification Needed
  • There have been several tests that reveal "Treasure Hunter +1" items add approximately 1% to the drop rate of any given item. Verification Needed
