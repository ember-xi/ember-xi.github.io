Before you begin

  • Complete ASA 7.
  • Slashing, blunt, piercing melee, ranged and suitable elemental magic damage.
  • Nighttime access for the Zi'Tah NMs.

Starting point

Ensorcelled Door · Ro'Maeve · B-10

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Find the Ensorcelled Door at B-10. Gather all six luminous fragment key items from the following NMs; bring the required damage types.
  2. In Ro'Maeve: Lode Golem at E-9/E-10 takes Blizzard, Water or Stone magic (blue fragment); Fired Urn at K-9/K-10 takes slashing damage (purple); Steely Weapon at G-10/G-11 takes ranged attacks (yellow).
  3. In The Sanctuary of Zi'Tah, search at night, 17:00–07:00: Holey Horror at H-8 takes piercing melee damage (green); Skeleton Scuffler at F-8 takes blunt damage (red); Blest Bones near F-7 takes Thunder, Fire or Wind magic (beige).
  4. Check your key items after each victory. Once you hold all six, return to the Ensorcelled Door and examine it for the story scene.

Completion and reward

Bomb key and story progression.
