Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Enhances user's next attack.
  • Obtained: Monk Level 5
  • Recast Time: 0:15
  • Duration: 3:00

Notes/Description

  • Upon usage, character's attack is raised by 12.5%.
  • Wears off upon next attack, or when user speaks to a NPC.
  • Does not wear when the user counters an attack.
  • Usage while engaged in combat will slightly delay the user's next attack. (Note that using any ability for any job causes a one second delay.)
  • Boosting multiple times without attacking will further increase attack.
  • If no attack is made within three minutes of a user's first Boost, the entire effect wears off.
  • Adds to the user an amount of enmity equal to one sixth of a Provoke.
  • Directly increases the damage output of Chi Blast.
  • If the Temple Gloves are equipped, all properties of Boost are enhanced, making the attack rise by 18.75%.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Boost" <me>
  • Some people prefer to use one macro to Boost multiple times:
    • /ja "Boost" <me>
    • /wait 15
    • /ja "Boost" <me>
    • /wait 15
    • /ja "Boost" <me>
    • /recast "Boost"
