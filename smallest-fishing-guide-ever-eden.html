Pre-Guide Notes:

  • Halcyon Rod is the only rod you will need, until Level 44. Composite Rod from 44-100
  • Levels 0-32 are sorted Sandy / Bastok / Windy. After level 32, Sandy and Basty will need to travel outside of their starting zones.
  • Access to Windurst required to uncap fishing every 10 levels.
  • Windurstians can level to 69 without leaving Sarutabaruta.
  • Make sure to do gobbie bag quests around fishing 30-45, its well worth it.
    • Fame quest requires about 80k to hit rank 9. (Only The Best-Selbina)
      • 60 inv slots cost about 280k total (Dec 2021)
