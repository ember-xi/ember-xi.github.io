Reading rewards

“Choose 1” means one item from the listed choices, plus the listed gil. Equipment augments are additional stats unless the row explicitly says total. All map locations use the game’s letter-number squares.

Travel and EXP

Zenith Guide handles crystal travel; Zenith Atlas handles camps. Zenith Scout records equipment reports.
