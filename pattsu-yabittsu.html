Pattsu-Yabittsu

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Type: Warp NPC

Location: Windurst Woods (K-12)

Notes

A teleport NPC. He is located at the lower-left of K-12 on your map. He will teleport you for free to Tonana (Windurst Waters, G-8).
