How to Obtain

Cannot be sold on the Auction House or delivered, but can be traded and bazaared.
 

Trade a Sin of Insolence to Meret in Tavnazian Safehold. You must complete her friend Yurim's quest, In the Name of Science, before Meret will accept any trades.
