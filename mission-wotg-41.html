Zenith availability

Reference chapter: the four-fragment completion check exists, but the pinned source does not contain a Verdure Footfalls handler for the Windurst branch. Earlier main-story battle gaps also block normal progression.

Before you begin

  • Complete Edge of Existence.
  • A Lilac, a Philosopher's Stone, Rice Vinegar, Ground Wasabi and Holy Basil are used by the subquests.

Starting point

Memory quest NPCs · Southern San d'Oria

Era note: Later WotG chapter, released after the level cap began rising above 75. Included for story continuity; this guide does not claim a tested level-75 balance.

Walkthrough

  1. Collect Large memory fragments I–IV through the linked subquests; their order is flexible. Start Of Malign Maladies early to run its day wait while doing the other routes.
  2. For fragment I, start Homecoming Queen by walking from East Ronfaure into Southern San d'Oria, then complete Old Bean, The Faux Pas, and Grave Resolve.
  3. For fragment II, complete Of Malign Maladies. For fragment III, complete Operation Cupid.
  4. For fragment IV, complete the branch associated with your past allegiance: Carnelian Footfalls, Azure Footfalls, or Verdure Footfalls. You need one national fragment, not all three. Keep your allegiance unchanged during this stage.
  5. Once all four large fragments are present, the next mission is assigned automatically.

Battle and progress notes

  • The pinned core has an incomplete main-story battle at mission 14; later story handlers do not establish that this chapter is currently reachable on Zenith.

Completion and reward

Four Large memory fragments; progress to Forget Me Not.
