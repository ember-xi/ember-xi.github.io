Zenith availability

Reference reward guide. Claiming this final reward on Zenith has not yet been verified.

Before you begin

  • Complete AMK 14.
  • Final battlefield victory and its reward key.

Starting point

Treasure Coffer · Lower Jeuno · J-8

Era note: 2009 level-75-era add-on. Modern retail shortcuts and item-level solo claims are not assumed on Zenith.

Walkthrough

  1. Return to the Tenshodo headquarters in Neptune's Spire at J-8 and examine the Treasure Coffer.
  2. Claim the final head-armor reward: Champion's Galea, Anwig Salade or Selenian Cap. Review its augment selections before confirming your choice.
  3. The Fin. entry marks the end of the story and can remain in the current mission log.

Completion and reward

One selected, augmented add-on head armor.
