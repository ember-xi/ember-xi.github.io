Steel Cyclone

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Great Axe weapon skill

  • Skill level: 240
  • In order to obtain Steel Cyclone, the quest The Weight of Your Limits must be completed.
  • Delivers a single-hit attack. Damage varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Distortion/Detonation
  • Modifiers: STR:50% ; VIT:50%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.50 1.75 3.00
  • +50% Increased attack power.

Levels

  • While Warriors may obtain skill level 240 earlier than level 71, Steel Cyclone must be quested once base skill level 240 (without merits) and job level 71 is reached (requires the ability to wield Axe of Trials).
  • Great Axe skill level 240 is obtainable by the following jobs at these corresponding levels:
Job Rating Level
Warrior A+ 68
Dark Knight B- 75
