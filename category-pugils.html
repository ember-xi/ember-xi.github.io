Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Pugils are a large, common fish native to nearly every body of water throughout Vana'diel. Their bodies are covered in durable scales, and their sharp teeth can provide painful wounds to any adventurer careless enough to be bit by them. It uses water to both defend itself and attack with, and can also use its strong snout as a crude lance to impale adversaries.

General Information

  • System: Aquans
  • Weaknesses: Ice, Thunder
  • Immune: Water
  • Detect by Sound

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Screwdriver: - Deals critical damage. Chance of critical hit varies with TP.
Water Wall: - Pugil gains Defense Boost
Water Shield: - Pugil gains Evasion Boost
Aqua Ball: - AOE water damage centered on primary target, with STR Down
Intimidate: - Cone Attack Slow
Splash Breath: - Cone Attack water damage
Recoil Dive: - Similar to Screwdriver
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
