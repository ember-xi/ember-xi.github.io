Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Great Katana weapon skill

  • Skill level: 250
  • In order to obtain Tachi: Kasha, the quest The Potential Within must be completed.
  • Paralyzes target. Damage varies with TP.

Properties

  • Element: None
  • Skillchain properties: Fusion / Compression
  • Modifiers: STR:75%
  • Attack Modifier:65%
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
1.75 2.00 2.50

Notes

Tachi: Kasha has a main effect of Fusion, and a secondary effect of Compression. This means that when performed after a Fragmentation Skillchain, the result will be a high-level Light Skillchain. When performed after a Distortion (Ice- & Water-based) Skillchain or weapon skill, the result will be a Fusion Skillchain. When performed after a Detonation (Wind) - based weapon skill, the result will be a Gravitation (Dark + Earth) Skillchain. When performed after an Induration (Ice) or Transfixion (Light) - based weapon skill, the result will be a Compression (low-level Dark) skillchain.

Levels

  • While Samurai may obtain skill level 250 earlier than level 71, Tachi: Kasha must be quested once base skill level 250 (without merits) and job level 71 is reached (requires the ability to wield Tachi of Trials).
