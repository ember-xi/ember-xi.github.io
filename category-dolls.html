General Information

  • System: Arcana
  • Weaknesses: Lightning
  • Immune: Resist Sleep
  • Detect by Magic

Special Attacks

Special Abilities
Meltdown: Self Destruct AoE Fire damage.
Typhoon: 10' AoE physical damage and knockback.
Gravity Field: AoE Slow (weaker than Haste).
Panzerfaust: ("Armored Fist") Single-target damage and knockback.
Panzerschreck: Single-target damage.
Blitzstrahl ("Lightning Bolt"): Single target damage and Stun.
Kartstrahl: Single-target damage and Sleep, sleep ignores Utsusemi.
Berserk: Warcry.
Note: Notorious Monsters in this family may use all of the above and/or additional unique special abilities.
