Main Job

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

A character's Main Job (Current Job) is the primary job he/she has chosen to use, and the only one of that character's jobs which will gain or lose any Experience Points.

After one of a character's jobs has reached level 18, he/she can do a quest to unlock the ability to choose a Support Job to complement his/her Main Job. (See Support Job for more info.)

A character can change his/her Main Job (and Support Job, if unlocked) in the following ways:

  1. By talking with the Moogle in the Mog House,
  2. Via the Mog House menu, or
  3. By talking with a Nomad Moogle.
