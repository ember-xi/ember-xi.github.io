Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Description

File:Bind (Status Effect).png Bind is a harmful status effect that roots the player to the ground until it wears off or is removed. Afflicted characters are unable to move but they may still attack and cast spells.

How to remove the effect

  • The White Magic spell Erase will remove this effect.
  • Taking damage may make the effect wear off sooner.
  • Note: If a player is bound and attempts to walk/run in a direction and makes their character "blink" via changing visible armor, they will move a short distance. A player may continue "blinking" to continue moving. This technique may be useful when the Bind status cannot be removed directly.

How the effect is inflicted

Spells

  • Black Magic - Bind
  • Blue Magic- Blastbomb, Light of Penance, Ice Break

Job Abilities

  • Ranger - Shadowbind

Weapon Skills

  • Dagger - Shadowstitch

Monster Abilities

  • Ahriman - Binding Wave
  • Antlions - Sandpit
  • Manticores - Tail Smash
  • Morbols - Bad Breath
  • Scorpions - Cold Breath
  • Treants - Entangle
  • Uragnites - Suctorial Tentacle
  • Orcs - Slam Dunk
