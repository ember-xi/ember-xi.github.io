Walkthrough

  • Speak with Mashuu-Ajuu who will complain about Pakuu-Nakku snoring in class.
  • Talk to Pakuu-Nakku to start the quest. It won't ask you if you want to accept the quest, it will just appear in your current quest list as "Let Sleeping Dogs Lie".
  • Talk to Maabu-Sonbu at the Orastery (Port Windurst E-7) who will eventually suggest that you try "a bunch of blazing peppers" to wake up the kid. According to him these peppers grow in the Pashhow Marshlands and you need a sickle to harvest them.
  • Go to Pashhow Marshlands and harvest at the (E-5) ??? to obtain the Blazing Peppers, or buy them from

  • Trade the Blazing Peppers to Pakuu-Nakku, who will wake up halfway. Mashuu-Ajuu suggests some sort of spell.
  • Go back to speak with Maabu-Sonbu who sends you to talk with Pechiru-Mashiru (inside the eastern building of the Aurastery in Windurst Waters).
    • Note: Talking to Maabu-Sonbu is optional; all it does is add more dialogue to the quest.
  • Pechiru-Mashiru gives you a magic phrase to say. Go back to Pakuu-Nakku and talk to him. You will get a choice of 3 things to say. Pick "The automaton butler did it!"
  • Go back to Pechiru-Mashiru who will give you a Hypno Staff.

Game Description

Client: Paku-Nakku (Aurastery, Windurst Waters)
Summary:

Paku-Nakku keeps falling asleep in class. It seems as though something happened during the class excursion to the Orastery. Find out the cause of his sleepiness and wake him up, before he fails the grade.
