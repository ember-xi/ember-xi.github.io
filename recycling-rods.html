Walkthrough

  • Talk to Keshab-Menjab to start the quest. He asks you to bring him a broken rod, but it has to be one "busted clean in two."
  • Travel to Bibiki Bay - Purgonorgo Isle and fish with a Composite Fishing Rod and Minnow until you catch the NM Lancet Jagil.
  • Kill the Lancet Jagil and obtain the Cleanly Snapped Rod (Rare/Ex).
  • Return to Mhaura and trade the rod to Keshab-Menjab to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Keshab-Menjab (Outside Orlando's Antiques, Mhaura)
Summary:

You've been asked to help bring in cleanly snapped rods for recycling. The recycled rods are supposedly sold somewhere around Bibiki Bay.
