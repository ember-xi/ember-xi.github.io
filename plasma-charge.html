Covers you with magical lightning spikes. Enemies that hit you take lightning damage.

Description

Spell cost: 24 MP
Ecosystem: Luminians
Type: Magical (Lightning)
Blue Magic Points: 5
Stat Bonus: STR+3 DEX+3
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 75

Timers

Casting Time: 3 seconds
Recast Time: 60 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Plasma Charge as part of a combo to create Auto Refresh.

Can be paired with some of the following spells to create the Auto Refresh trait. Please refer to the Auto Refresh section of the Blue Mage Job Traits page for details.

  • Stinking Gas
  • Cold Wave
  • Frightful Roar
  • Self-Destruct
  • Light of Penance
  • Voracious Trunk
  • Actinic Burst
