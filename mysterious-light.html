Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Deals wind damage to enemies within range. Additional effect: Weight.

Description

Spell cost: 73 MP
Ecosystem: Arcana
Type: Magical (Wind)
Blue Magic Points: 4
Stat Bonus: AGI+3
Jobs:

  • Blue Mage Level 40

Timers

Casting Time: 3.75 seconds
Recast Time: 24.5 seconds

Combines With...

You can use Mysterious Light as part of a combo to create Max MP Boost.

Can be paired with any of the following spells to create Max MP Boost combo.

  • Metallic Body
  • Hecatomb Wave

Notes

  • Primarily modified by CHR, CHR 30% secondary modifier.
  • Additional effect of gravity is not guaranteed, even if spell damage is not resisted.

Skillchain/Magic Burst Info

Magic Bursts on: Detonation, Fragmentation, Light
