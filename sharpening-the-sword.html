Sharpening the Sword

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

This is the first Paladin Artifact Armor quest - otherwise known as AF1.

  Start NPC:   Ailbeche - Northern San d'Oria (J-8)
  Requirements:
   Paladin 40+
  Items Needed:   None
  Title:   None
  Repeatable:   Yes, after AF is complete.
  Reward:   Honor Sword

  Previous Quest:   Father and Son and A Knight's Test
  Next Quest:   A Boy's Dream

Walkthrough

  • Complete the Father and Son quest and make sure to give the Willow Fishing Rod back to the son.
  • Talk to Ailbeche again. "Make sure you get the "Ordelle Whetstone" dialogue.
  • Talk to Sobane in (D-6) of Southern San d'Oria. Be sure to talk to her twice if you get a cutscene the first time - the cutscene is the beginning of the quest Signed in Blood and not part of this quest.
  • Head to Ordelle's Caves, entering from F-6 then zone at F-7 from La Theine Plateau, hang left until you change maps, keep left until you get to G-8 then take the path east which you will see a few Dung Beetle, there are two paths so be sure to take the (H-9) one which has stairs, finally look for a stalagmite around (H-10) and don't fall in the hole by mistake(if you do take path north and hang left to find stairs again).
  • Spawn and kill the NM Polevik, a lvl 45 Earth Elemental. (Note: Can be killed on any job 55+, as long as you have the quest. High defense like regular elementals) <---- Found to be untrue as a PLD56/WAR28 couldn't solo. Retail wiki suggests "Should be level 65+ to solo the fight."
  • DONT FORGET TO Touch the stalagmite again to receive the Ordelle Whetstone.
  • Talk to Ailbeche to complete the quest.

Game Description

Client: Ailbeche (Parade Grounds, Northern San d'Oria)
Summary:

The boy needs a whetstone to sharpen an old blade. Find one for him in Ordelle's Caves.
