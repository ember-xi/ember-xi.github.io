Notes:

  • Spawns around (F-8) of the first map.
  • The NM uses Firespit very frequently, with no need to wait for TP. He does not use any other of the Mamool Ja special attacks, but is able to cast Ninjutsu and use Mijin Gakure.
  • When the NM casts Utsusemi, the shadow images appear as additional monsters with the same name. Up to four shadows can appear this way.
    • The shadow copies have about 1,000 HP. Two Thundaga III or one Astral Flow will be enough to clear them out.
    • The shadows use Firespit in sync with each other. They seem to be able to do it without the NM and at different targets from each other. They Firespit more often than the NM.
  • Both the NM and his shadows use Mijin Gakure constantly, however, it deals less damage than a Mijin Gakure from a NM would normally deal.
  • Having high fire resistance and magic defense is very important in this battle, as fire damage is being dealt constantly. Having Shell IV, Barfira, and Fire Carol are very helpful, especially in addition to fire resistance gear.
  • The NM and his shadows are immune to Stun, Silence, and Sleep.
  • Defeating this NM lowers the Mamool Ja Savages' military force by 1.
  • He can detect you through the Mamool Ja costume.
