Tips From The Trade

  • Take your level, add 10 levels above, add your gear and you SiS and that is your target range.

Ex if you are level 14 and you have +4 in gear and +2 from SiS then your target range is 24 - 30.
You can go higher or lower than this level range but this is your target level "sweet spot".

  • Get Advanced Synthesis Support every chance you get, it lasts for 2 hours and only costs 30 gil per rank so at it's max rank it only costs 300 gil, you can afford that by simply selling one Rusty Bucket to an NPC for 51 gil, so there is no excuse for never having it.
  • Aim for the fish which is the safest and furthermost from your current level. (+4 gear) (+2 ASiS) (+10 fishing rod*(varies)) meaning you should be aiming for fish around 15 levels approximately above your current level.
  • Keep reading the fish messages, if it doesn't say "good" doesn't matter if it's an item, a mob or another fish don't reel it in, it could risk breaking your line or worst your rod.

Exotic Fish Route

Legendary Rods - Lu Shang's Rod and The Ebisu

Are these rods worth the time and effort to get them? That's up to you, but If your goal is to get fishing to 100 then you do not need a Lu Shang's Fishing Rod.

However, if your goal is to get an Ebisu Fishing Rod then you MUST get a Lu Shang's Fishing Rod.

There are 102 fish in each area that you can catch; you can catch 90 of those with a regular rod. However, if you're going to catch those last 12 fish, you must have one of the legendary fishing rods.

There are a couple of ways to get a Lu Shang's Fishing Rod:

  • buy one off someone or from the AH, you should expect to spend a between 1M to 4M.
  • Complete the quest The Rivalry/The Competition. Each quest requires that you catch and turn in 10k fish.
