Walkthrough

NOTE: You must zone after completing the previous quest before you can start this quest. Quest cannot be started if on Windurst Mission 2-1 or Windurst Mission 7-1

  • Tosuka-Porika informs you that The Library of Magic wants you to help retrieve its overdue library books.
  • In this case, a Hume by the name of Orn has checked out the book "Art for Everyone", but has failed to return it by the due date.
  • After you accept he tells you to talk to Furakku-Norakku, behind the counter in the same room, who gives you an "Overdue Book Notification" (key item) to give to Orn.
  • Orn can be found in Hostelry Room #2 above the Timbre Timbers Tavern (F-10).
  • He tells you about how some yagudo waylaid him and how he had to throw things at them to escape, including the book. Orn asks you to request for an extension for him. Speak to him again and he will tell you he heard that the yagudo store their stolen items in the Treasure Room of Giddeus.
  • If you want you can ask Furakku-Norakku for an extension, but it is not necessary.
  • Head to Giddeus and go down one level through a hidden burrow at G-8.
  • Head to G-6 Treasure Room once you dropped down.
  • There is a Yagudo "in" the Treasure Room named Quu Bokye who wants a silver beastcoin.
  • Trade him the silver beastcoin and he will give you "Art For Everyone". (note: there is a yagudo npc "guarding" the entrance to the Treasure Room named Uu Zhoumo, just select the door and open it; ignore the npc)
  • Head back to Windurst Waters and talk to Furakku-Norakku to complete the quest.

The Library of Magic wants you to help retrieve its overdue library books. In this case, a Hume the name of Orn has checked out the book "Art for Everyone," but has failed to return it by the due date.
