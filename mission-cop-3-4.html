  • Zone into Lower Jeuno to get a cutscene.
    • NOTE: Don't zone in from the Mog house, it will not play the needed cutscene.
  • Go to Lower Jeuno and inspect the door of the Tenshodo Headquarters at the Neptune's Spire to get another cutscene.
