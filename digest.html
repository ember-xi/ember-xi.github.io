Steals an enemy's HP. Ineffective against undead.

Description

Spell cost: 20 MP
Ecosystem: Amorphs
Type: Magical (Dark)
Blue Magic Points: 2
Stat Bonus: HP-5, MP+5
Amount Drained is affected by Blue magic skill, formula is not known.
Dark Staff bonus applies to this spell.
AF Helm affects only resist rates, does not increase damage.
