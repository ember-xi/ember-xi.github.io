Statistics

Bastokan Flag

[1][2]Furnishing:

A Republic of Bastok flag.
Bastok's symbols, a water wheel and
chimneys, are depicted at the center.
The blue represents the rivers of
Bastok, the city's main power source.<[3] STG: 1>

Moghancement: Bastok Conquest
Aura Strength: 9
Placement: Wall
Dimensions: ---

Other Uses

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
 

Also cannot be placed into Storage.

Mission

  • Bastok Mission 9-2: Where Two Paths Converge
