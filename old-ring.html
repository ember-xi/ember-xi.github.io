Old Ring
Description:
An old ring found in Castle Oztroja. It seems to be engraved with the seal of the Optistery in Windurst.
Used In Quest(s):
Onion Rings
Obtained:
Found in Castle Oztroja by falling through the trap door (J-8) and checking the blank target (escape hole) in the first room you fall into.
