Walkthrough

  • Watch the cutscenes in Jade Sepulcher that followed the end of the battle.
  • Take the Dvucca Isle Staging Point to Caedarva Mire, and head to the Arrapago Reef entrance around F/G-9.
  • You will get a cutscene upon zoning in.
  • Afterwards, approach the "Cutter," which is on the ship connected to the docks at H-8 for a cutscene(entrance to Arrapago Reef #3).
    • Exact same area as the ToAU Mission 15 - Black Coffin.
  • The beginning of this cutscene triggers Mission 31.
