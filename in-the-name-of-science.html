Walkthrough

  • Talk with Yurim to start the quest.
  • Decide which of the final rewards you wish to obtain (see chart below).
  • Trade Yurim the proper "base" item and colored chip.
  • Once the selection is made, you must obtain the proper items to get the reward.
  • Talk further with Yurim, for information on her research.
  • Trade an Apple Pie to Yurim (optional), for more hints on how to finish the research steps.
  • Go to Al'Taieu and obtain the necessary items as shown in the below chart.
  • Return to the Tavnazian Safehold and trade all the organs (all at once) to Yurim to receive your reward and complete the quest.
