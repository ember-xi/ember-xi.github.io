Arrival and hazards

Arrive beside Scout and Atlas at the Qufim outpost, near the Field Manual. Hunt worms and crabs outside; avoid giants and night undead.

Zenith NPCs

Zenith Scout handles active equipment-quest field steps. Zenith Atlas handles camp travel. They are placed near the outpost staging point in the prepared placement packages.

Scout location guide

Zenith Scout: current placement package, marked maps and source evidence. Scout By Books v1.0 places the Valkurm Scout beside Field Manual and Survival Guide at H-7 after installation and restart. Qufim remains at the F-6 staging area.

Additional level-75 reference



An island to the north of Jeuno, Qufim Island is home to those Gigas who are unable to survive the depths of Delkfutt's Tower. Often crowded with adventurers during the day due to its proximity to Jeuno, at night a large number of undead roam around forcing many less experienced adventurers to seek safe cover.
