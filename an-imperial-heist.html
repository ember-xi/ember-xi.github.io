An Imperial Heist

From Zenith XI Wiki

Register a Nyzul weapon and prove the required eight boss victories.

Contents
  1. Requirements
  2. Steps
  3. Required victories
  4. Sources

Requirements

Treasures of Aht Urhgan story completed; Captain Wildcat Badge; Runic Key; your chosen job’s Nyzul/Vigil weapon.

Steps

  1. Enter Salaheem’s Sentinels in Aht Urhgan Whitegate (I-10) for the opening scene.
  2. Talk to Abquhbah and trade the base weapon for the Mythic you intend to build. He requires the Runic Key.
  3. Acquire the eight titles listed below. Previously earned titles count; they need not be your currently displayed title.
  4. Return to Naja Salaheem after all objectives are credited. Continue to Duties, Tasks, and Deeds.
