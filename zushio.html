Zushio

From Zenith XI Wiki

Level-40 Ninja katana: original-era DMG 23, delay 238 and STR +2.

Contents

How to obtain

Complete 20 in Pirate Years, the first Ninja artifact quest. The quest also awards Anju. Open the quest for its prerequisite, NPC and fight route.

Equipment

Rare/Exclusive quest weapon. The Horizon-specific increase to DMG 25 is not assumed for Zenith.
