Before you begin

  • Finish Steamed Rams, The Fighting Fourth, or Snake on the Plains.
  • Have inventory space for seven Plumes d’Or.

Starting point

Louxiard · Southern San d'Oria (S) · G-7

Walkthrough

  1. Speak to Louxiard. Leave Southern San d’Oria (S), return, and speak to him again.
  2. Speak to Rholont at E-7 to accept the delivery task and receive seven Plumes d’Or.
  3. Trade one plume each to Machionage C-6, Louxiard G-7, Illeuse H-9, Rongelouts N Distaud I-9, Sabiliont I-11, Elnonde K-9 and Loillie K-9.
  4. Reach Loillie’s upper ledge using the stairs at L-8. After all seven deliveries, return to Rholont for the reward.

Battle and progress notes

  • You can do this story while allied to Bastok or Windurst. Preserve the plumes; native replacement of lost plumes can require a Conquest-tally wait.

Completion and reward

Deathstone.

What this opens

  • Claws of the Griffon after the next Vana’diel day
