Puts an enemy to sleep.

Description

Spell cost: 26 MP
Spell element: Light
Magic skill: Divine Magic
Jobs:

  • White Mage Level 48
  • When unresisted, lasts 90 seconds or 37.5 game minutes.
  • Unlike other sleep spells, Repose is light-based Divine Magic and can affect undead.
  • Repose will overwrite Sleep, Horde Lullaby and Foe Lullaby.
  • Repose will not overwrite Sleep II nor will Sleep II overwrite Repose.

Timers

Casting Time: 4.5 seconds
Recast Time: 30 seconds
