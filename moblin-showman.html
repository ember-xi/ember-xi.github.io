Moblin Showman

Type:NPC

Location:Newton Movalpolos (I-9)

Notes

  • Trade an Air Tank to this NPC to pop the Bugbear Matman.
  • The quickest and easiest way to get to him is to teleport to Mineshaft #2716 and then turn around and zone out. Bring some Firesand just in case.
    • Zone in to Oldton Movalpolis from North Gustaberg (K-6) to find Twinkbrix at (E-13). Alternatively, you can zone in from Gusgen Mines and run across the zone.
    • If you do not have the temporary item Shaft Gate Operating Dial, you can gamble with Twinkbrix to attain it (see his page for information on gambling).
    • If you have the temporary item Shaft Gate Operating Dial, trade Twinkbrix 2,000 gil to be teleported to Mineshaft #2716.
    • Turn around and zone out. DO NOT go into the ENM.
  • Travel to (J-8) to find a gate. The gate has two different positions, and will either allows passage to the left (south) or right (north).
    • If the gate permits you to travel left (south), proceed to south.
    • If the gate does not permit you to travel left, turn around to find a Furnace Hatch behind you. Trade Firesand to the Furnace Hatch to manipulate the gate.
      • If you did not bring Firesand, some of the level 75-79 enemies in the area can drop it (Moblin Roadman, Moblin Engineman, Goblin Marksman, and Goblin Headman).
      • The gate may also automatically switch at 2:00 in game time.
  • Continue south from the gate. Drop down the ledge at (I-9)/(J-9) to find the Moblin Showman.
