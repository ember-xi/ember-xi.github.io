Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Erases one roll or bust effect. Targets self-cast effect with the longest remaining duration.
  • Obtained: Corsair Level 75
  • Recast Time: 15 Minutes
  • Duration: Instant

Notes/Description

  • Each further merit decreases recast time by 2:30
  • Will remove any Double-Up effect as well, if one is active
  • Will remove Bust effects before roll effects.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Fold" <me>
