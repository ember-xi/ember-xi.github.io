Level 75 reference · Zenith rules


Armor Depositories are NPCs that will store full sets of certain armor for you (either 4 or 5 pieces, depending on the set).

Armor Depositories

Name Location
Caruvinda Lower Jeuno (J-6)
Tesch Garanjy Windurst Woods (H-11)
Taulenne Northern San d'Oria (G-8)
Sinon Bastok Markets (H-9)
Ratonne Tavnazian Safehold (F-8) (Main level)
Hajawaa Aht Urhgan Whitegate (H-8)

How to Use

Trade your armor to the NPC and receive an "armor set" claim slip in return (a key item).

You will be charged a fee (depending on the armor set) when you pick-up your armor later on.

Restrictions

  • Only certain sets of armor can be stored.
  • The whole set must be stored, not just pieces.
  • High Quality armor can't be stored.
  • Signatures and Augments on armor will be lost once put in storage.
