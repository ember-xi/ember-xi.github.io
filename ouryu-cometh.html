Notes

  • The Cloud Evoker is taken into Riverne - Site #A01, which is an area capped at Level 40, and traded to the green Spatial Displacement at I-9. This opens up the BCNM to a full alliance, who then enter through the displacement into a non-level capped area.
  • Only Ouryu must be defeated to win this BCNM.
  • Elementals (one Water, one Earth) will spawn inside the BCNM area, build resistance to Sleep, and will respawn quickly if killed.
  • Ouryu's special attacks include: Wyrm special attacks, Draw In, Invincible, Absolute Terror (AoE Terror), Ochre Blast, Touchdown, Stonega III, Stoneskin, Break, Breakga, Bai Wing (~250 AoE damage, gives Slow effect to all hit by it)
  • Ouryu is susceptible to: Sleep, Lullaby, Silence, Gravity
  • The four Ziryu have special attacks of: Worm special attacks, Stonega III, Quake
  • Ziryu are susceptible to: Sleep, Silence, Gravity, Bind, Charm, Lullaby, Elegy
  • Ouryu lands for two minutes, then flies for two minutes. The Mistmelt item works on him in this fight, just as it does in the mission.
  • You may enter as long as you have access to Monarch Linn (COP Mission 2-5) Source
