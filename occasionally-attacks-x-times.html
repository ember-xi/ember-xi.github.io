Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Introduction

Some weapons have the possibility of attacking more than one time per round. For most weapons the "occasionally" means that the additional attacks are executed 30% to 50% of the time.

Notes

  • This ability acts like, but does not stack with, the Double Attack or Triple Attack job traits. It is believed that the weapon effect is determined first, and, if executed, the job traits will not execute. If the weapon effect does not execute then the job traits will have the possibility of executing.
    • Jailer/Virtue Stone weapons can proc with a double/triple attack, resulting in an extra attack. Jailer/Virtue Stone weapons and Double/Triple attack can only proc on the first hit of the attack.
  • This ability is active regardless of Dual Wield. It will work regardless of whether the weapon is placed in the main or the offhand.
  • This ability cannot activate during weapon skills.
