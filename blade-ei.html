Katana weapon skill

  • Skill level: 175
  • Delivers a dark elemental attack.

Properties

  • Element: Dark
  • Skillchain properties: Gravitation
  • Modifiers: Unknown
  • Damage Multipliers by TP:
100%TP 200%TP 300%TP
Unknown Unknown Unknown

Notes

While Blade: Ei isn't very impressive, it is one of the weapon skills that ignores shadow effects (Utsusemi & Blink).

Translation

"Ei" means shadow in Japanese. So it is Blade: Shadow.
