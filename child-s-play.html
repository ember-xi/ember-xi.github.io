Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

  Requirements:
   Started the quest The Wonder Magic Set

  Repeatable:   No.

  Previous Quest:   None
  Next Quest:   The Wonder Magic Set (related quest)

Walkthrough

  • Karl, a kid who is standing near the stairs down to the chocobo stables, will give a Wonder Magic Set to you if you bring him a White Rock.
  • Trade the White Rock to Karl and he will give you the Wonder Magic Set.

Game Description

Client: Karl (Port Jeuno)
Summary:

Karl will trade you a Wonder Magic Set for a white rock.
