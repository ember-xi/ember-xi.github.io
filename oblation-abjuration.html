Oblation Abjuration
The inscription reads:

"In accordance with the ancient
pact, may the bane be lifted from
thy forbidden extract."

Necessary in purifying the cursed soup.

Stackable: Not Stackable

Other Uses

Used in Quest: Abjuration: Ambrosia

NPC Sell Price: Cannot be sold to NPCs.

How to Obtain

Cannot be auctioned, traded, or bazaared, but can be delivered to a character on the same account.
