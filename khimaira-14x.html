Notes:

  • Much weaker version of Khimaira and uses all of its corresponding moves.
  • Dreadstorm - AoE Terror, 200~500 damage.
  • Fossilising Breath - Targeted Cone Attack. Additional effect: Petrification.
  • Plague Swipe - Backwards Cone Attack damage and Bio. (Only used when hate is pulled from behind.)
  • Tenebrous Mist - AoE TP reset.
  • Thunderstrike - AoE Stun, 600 damage
  • Tourbillion - Three-hit physical damage AoE knockback, with Defense Down. 200-1200 damage, depending on Defense and how many hits connect. Can be evaded, or absorbed by 3 shadows.
  • Fulmination - Moderate AoE Paralyze, Stun, 100~700 damage.
  • If Martial Maestro Megomak is alive, it can use this move anytime during the fight, even when his HP is above 25%, but only when Martial Maestro Megomak rolls an 8.
  • If Martial Maestro Megomak is dead, it will not use this move until it has 25% or less, at which time it will use this move as a regular TP attack.
  • Uses Draw In
