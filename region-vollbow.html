Additional level-75 reference

Desolate, sand-filled lands make up a majority of the Vollbow region. The lands were traversed more freely in old times, but a mysterious disaster during the Great War caused the lands to be separated from the nearby Zulkheim region.

Outpost

The outpost of this region is located in Cape Teriggan at G-8
