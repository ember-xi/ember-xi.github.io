Before you begin

  • Complete ToAU 16: Ghosts of the Past.
  • An approved body piece; nonempty Hands, Legs and Feet slots; empty Main and Sub slots at the palace.

Starting point

Naja Salaheem · Aht Urhgan Whitegate · I-10

People in this mission

Walkthrough

  1. Talk to Naja, then equip a palace-approved body piece plus separate equipment in Hands, Legs and Feet.
  2. Speak with Naja while dressed correctly and obtain her approval.
  3. Keep that armor equipped and empty both Main and Sub slots, including any shield.
  4. Examine the Imperial Whitegate at L-8/L-9 and finish the palace audience. Leave an inventory slot for the reward.

Battle and progress notes

  • Accessible examples include Brigandine (level 45, suitable jobs), Holy Breastplate (WHM 40), Justaucorps (58) and Haubergeon (59). Normal and listed HQ variants are accepted. Head equipment is optional.
  • Completing this story gate satisfies the mission prerequisite for Salvage. Salvage still has its own level, currency and entry requirements.

Completion and reward

One Imperial Mythril Piece; Ovjang's Errand Runner title.

What this opens

  • Salvage story prerequisite
  • ToAU 18: Passing Glory
