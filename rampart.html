Level 75 reference · Zenith rules

Job Ability

  • Grants a Magic Shield effect and enhances defense for party members within area of effect.
  • Obtained: Paladin Level 62
  • Recast Time: 5:00
  • Duration: 0:30

Notes/Description

  • This ability raises the defense of all party members within the area of effect.
  • Magic Shield effect seems to be 100~ damage at 75, further testing is required.
    • NOTE: Recent tests have shown that the amount of damage absorbed by Rampart's magic Stoneskin is in fact variable. The currently accepted formula for damage absorbed seems to be VIT*2.
  • Can overwrite Defense Down status from some mobs (i.e., Dhalmel's Sonic Wave ability), but conversely can be overwritten itself by Defense Down.
  • Adds an amount of enmity to user comparable to that of a Provoke.
  • If Valor Coronet is equipped, duration of this ability increases to 0:45.

Macro Syntax

  • /ja "Rampart" <me>
