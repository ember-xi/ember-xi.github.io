Walkthrough

  • Talk to Milazahn for Cut Scene.
  • Talk to Cacaroon (G-11) in Aht Urhgan Whitegate for cutscene.
  • Trade 1000g for an additional cutscene.
  • Go to Wajaom Woodlands (D-12) and zone into Mamook.
  • Go to (E-8) and check the Viscous Liquid for a cutscene. Note the Poroggo are true sound aggro.
  • Check it again to spawn Mamool Ja (NM).
    • A BST can easily solo the NM using one of the Brei (Slimes) just to the south.
    • A RDM or BLM can solo the NM using their highest Blizzard spell and Sleep. As a RDM75 it only took 8 Blizzard IIIs.
    • A Summoner can easily solo the NM using Fenrir.
  • Kill it and examine the Viscous Liquid again for another cutscene.
    • Examine the Viscous Liquid and get a cutscene after killing the NM. If you don't, you will have to kill the NM again when you come back.
  • Return to Milazahn in Aht Urhgan Whitegate for final cutscene.

Game Description

Client: Milazahn (Balrhan Way, Aht Urhgan Whitegate)
Summary:

The ferocious "Two Horn the Savage" has been wreaking havoc around the Empire, but nobody knows the creature's identity or its whereabouts. You are to lead an investigation into the facts surrounding this frightening beast.
