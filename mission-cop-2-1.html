Before you begin

  • Complete CoP 1-3.

Starting point

Arrival cutscene · Lufaise Meadows

Walkthrough

  1. Finish the scene after your third Promyvion victory.
  2. Travel west across Lufaise Meadows to either Safehold entrance, E-8 or F-10. Use suitable stealth around the field monsters.
  3. Enter Tavnazian Safehold and finish the arrival scene.

Battle and progress notes

  • Visit the Safehold before leaving the archipelago; this establishes the story destination for the next mission.

Completion and reward

Story progression.

Walkthrough

  • After completing all 3 Promyvions, you will be teleported to Lufaise Meadows and a cutscene will start.
  • Your Mysterious Amulet will be stolen.
  • After the cutscene, proceed west to the Tavnazian Safehold. Orcs and Gigas will aggro to sight as well as link, and Bugards will aggro to sound.
  • Once you zone into the Safehold, another cutscene will begin.

Game Description

Mission Orders
You have woken up in some unknown land, only to find your amulet has been stolen by a group of three Tarutaru. You think they may have run off somewhere to the west.
