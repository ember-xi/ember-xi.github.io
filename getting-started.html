First steps

  1. Choose one of the six starting jobs. Open Equipment and equip your starting gear.
  2. Get Signet from a city guard. Speak to Alaune in Southern San d’Oria, Gulldago in Bastok Markets, or Selele in Windurst Woods to begin tutorial training.
  3. Trade your Adventurer Coupon to an adventurer assistant. Prepare space for the starter rewards.
  4. Activate First Step Forward in Records of Eminence and speak to the local Sparks NPC. Select objectives appropriate to your hunting area.
  5. At level 5, start your city’s Trust quest and complete it to receive a Trust permit.
  6. From level 10, use Zenith Atlas for camps. Start equipment quests at WAR15 or THF20; City Rings start at level 10; story adventures start at level 20.

Carry more from the beginning

80-slot Inventory is included in Adventures v1.1. Existing characters receive it at their next login after installation.

Your first limit break

At level 50, collect three items from named monsters and trade them to Maat in Ru’Lude Gardens. The following increases open automatically until the final trial at 70.
