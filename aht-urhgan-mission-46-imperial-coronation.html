Walkthrough

  • You must be wearing appropriate clothes to receive this cutscene.
  • Remove your weapon and while wearing your appropriate clothing, touch the Imperial Whitegate door at (L-9) of Aht Urhgan Whitegate for a cut scene.
  • Note: you must wear hands/legs/feet otherwise the cutseen will not trigger

Game Description

You have been chosen to accompany President Naja to the Empress's coronation. Once you are properly attired, make your way to the palace.
